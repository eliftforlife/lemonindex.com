How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

    Questions

    Quick answers

    Is the Tesla Model S reliable?

    The Tesla Model S drew about 4 times the average car's complaints (model years 2012–2020). Its EV ranking score is 679 for model years 2021–2024, #19 of 22 electric cars (100 is the average car).

    What are the most common Tesla Model S problems?

    In owner complaints to NHTSA, the areas named most are automatic emergency braking (24%), steering (17%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints that name each area.

    How many recalls does the Tesla Model S have?

    29 recalls for model years 2021-2024. Recall repairs are free at any dealer.

    How many fire complaints does the Tesla Model S have?

    2 fire complaints (2.5 per 100,000 sold); the average for all EVs is 0.8.

    What is the Tesla Model S's range?

    The EPA rates the 2025 Tesla Model S at 312–410 miles of range, and about $600–$800 a year to charge (15,000 miles at national average prices).

    How to read these numbers

    Complaints filed with NHTSA by the end of the year after the model year. Sales are Kelley Blue Book estimates for every brand (Tesla doesn't report U.S. sales by model). EV scores cover model years 2021–2024 and count complaints filed by the end of the year after the model year, so brand-new EVs like the Cybertruck get a complete window. The main Lemon Index covers each model's own years and counts complaints from each car's first 3 years. Different years and windows, so an EV that has both shows two different numbers. Sales source: Kelley Blue Book EV sales reports (estimates).
