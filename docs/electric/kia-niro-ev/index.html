Recalls

5 recalls for model years 2022-2024, 1 mentioning fire. The newest:

  • back over prevention Jun 2025
    The circuit board for the rearview camera may become damaged and cause the rearview camera image not to display. NHTSA 25V426000
  • seats Jan 2025
    The floor wiring assembly beneath the front passenger seat may become damaged, which can prevent the front air bags and seat belts from deploying properly or cause an unintended side curtain air bag deployment. NHTSA 25V024000
  • electrical May 2024
    The high voltage battery safety plug may overheat and melt, which can cause a loss of drive power or fire. Fire risk. NHTSA 24V358000
  • transmission & drivetrain Apr 2024
    The differential gear bolts in the gear drive unit may not be tightened properly, which can result in a loss of drive power or cause the differential to lock up. NHTSA 24V271000
  • transmission & drivetrain Jan 2024
    One of the drive shafts may have been improperly heat-treated, allowing it to break under load and resulting in a loss of drive power. NHTSA 24V057000
