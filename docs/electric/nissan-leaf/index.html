Is the Nissan Leaf reliable?

The Nissan Leaf drew a bit more than the average car's complaints: #1 of 22 electric cars. 100 is the average car; lower is better.

What are the most common Nissan Leaf problems?

In owner complaints to NHTSA, the areas named most are automatic emergency braking (23%), electrical (20%) and brakes (20%). That is the share of complaints that name each area.

How many recalls does the Nissan Leaf have?

5 recalls for model years 2021-2024, 1 mentioning fire. Recall repairs are free at any dealer.

How many fire complaints does the Nissan Leaf have?

0 fire complaints (0.0 per 100,000 sold); the average for all EVs is 0.8.

What is the Nissan Leaf's range?

How to read these numbers

Complaints filed with NHTSA by the end of the year after the model year. Sales are Kelley Blue Book estimates for every brand (Tesla doesn't report U.S. sales by model). EV scores cover model years 2021–2024 and count complaints filed by the end of the year after the model year, so brand-new EVs like the Cybertruck get a complete window. The main Lemon Index covers each model's own years and counts complaints from each car's first 3 years. Different years and windows, so an EV that has both shows two different numbers. Sales source: Kelley Blue Book EV sales reports (estimates).
