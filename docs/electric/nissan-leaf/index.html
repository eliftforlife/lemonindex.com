Recalls

5 recalls for model years 2021-2024, 1 mentioning fire. The newest:

  • electrical Oct 2025
    The lithium-ion battery may overheat during Level 3 charging. Fire risk. NHTSA 25V655000
  • back over prevention Feb 2024
    Damage to the camera harness can cause distortion or loss of the rearview camera display image. NHTSA 24V071000
  • speed control Jul 2023
    The vehicle may accelerate unintentionally if the driving mode is changed ("D" to "B"; e-Pedal "On"; or "ECO" mode) after disengaging the cruise control. NHTSA 23V494000
  • brakes May 2023
    The right-hand brake tubes may have been damaged during production and could corrode, possibly causing a hole in the brake line and a brake fluid leak. NHTSA 23V362000
