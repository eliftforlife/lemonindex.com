Recalls

33 recalls for model years 2021-2024. The newest:

  • lights Aug 2026
    The headlight low beams may be too bright and exceed the maximum light output. NHTSA 26V507000
  • back over prevention May 2026
    The rearview camera image may be delayed when the vehicle is placed in reverse. NHTSA 26V283000
  • steering Feb 2025
    The printed circuit board for the electronic power steering assist may experience an overstress condition, causing a loss of power steering assist when the vehicle reaches a stop and then accelerates again. NHTSA 25V092000
  • back over prevention Jan 2025
    The computer circuit board may short, resulting in the loss of the rearview camera image. NHTSA 25V002000
  • tires Dec 2024
    The tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) warning light may not remain illuminated between drive cycles, failing to warn the driver of low tire pressure. NHTSA 24V935000
  • seats Nov 2024
    The weld attaching the seat recliner mechanism to the front seat backs may fail. NHTSA 24V886000
  • latches/locks/linkages Jul 2024
    The hood latch assembly may fail to detect an unlatched hood condition after the hood has been opened. NHTSA 24V554000
  • seat belts May 2024
    In the event of an unbelted driver, the seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended. NHTSA 24V376000
