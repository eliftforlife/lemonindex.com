Tesla Autopilot crash reports: what the government data shows

Since 2021, automakers have reported 5,042 crashes in which a driver-assist system like Autopilot or Super Cruise was in use. Tesla filed 88% of them.

5,042crash reports, 2019-2026
88%from Tesla
65of 75 reported deaths were in a Tesla
88%of Tesla reports came from the car itself

Why that isn't a safety ranking

  1. Teslas report themselves. 88% of Tesla's reports came from the car's own data connection. For other brands it was 37%: most of them hear about a crash only if the owner complains or sues. A brand that doesn't get the data can't report it.
  2. No miles. The reports don't say how many miles each system drove. Tesla has the most cars with driver assist on the road, and owners use it a lot. More use means more crashes even at the same rate.
  3. Reported, not proven. A report means the system was on within 30 seconds of the crash. It doesn't say the system caused it. NHTSA itself warns against comparing brands with these reports.
