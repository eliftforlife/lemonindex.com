Recalls

5 recalls for model years 2022-2024, 1 mentioning fire. The newest:

  • electrical Jul 2026
    The high voltage battery cells may contain misaligned electrodes, which can lead to a fire while parked or driving. Fire risk. NHTSA 26V431000
  • electrical Nov 2024
    The Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU) may become damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery, which can result in a loss of drive power. NHTSA 24V867000
  • electrical Mar 2024
    The Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU) may become damaged and stop charging the 12-Volt battery, which can result in a loss of drive power. NHTSA 24V200000

    One of the drive shafts may have been improperly heat-treated, allowing it to break under load and resulting in a loss of drive power. NHTSA 24V057000

    A software error in the Shifter Control Unit (SCU) may disengage the parking mechanism, which can allow the vehicle to rollaway. NHTSA 22V322000
