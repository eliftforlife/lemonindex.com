Recalls

7 recalls for model years 2021-2023, 5 mentioning fire. The newest:

  • electrical Oct 2024
    The installation of advanced diagnostic software may have failed. Fire risk. NHTSA 24V812000
  • electrical Jun 2024
    The advanced diagnostic software may have been incorrectly installed and fail to detect defective battery modules, possibly resulting in a battery fire. Fire risk. NHTSA 24V481000
  • body structure Dec 2023
    This recall includes certain vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under NHTSA recall number 22V-930. Fire risk. NHTSA 23V845000
  • airbags Aug 2023
    The instrument panel may be missing the perforation on the underside of the panel cover that allows the passenger-side air bag to deploy properly. NHTSA 23V567000
  • body structure Dec 2022
    After a crash with seat belt pretensioner deployment, the pretensioner exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar, causing a fire. Fire risk. NHTSA 22V930000
  • electrical Aug 2021
    The high voltage battery could catch fire when charged to full or nearly full capacity. Fire risk. NHTSA 21V650000
  • airbags Jul 2021
    One of the two fasteners that secure the driver's frontal air bag to the steering wheel may be missing or improperly tightened. NHTSA 21V517000
