Electric cars · Problems

The most common electric car problems.

The #1 EV complaint isn't the battery. It's automatic emergency braking: 41% of EV complaints, against 10% for other cars. Owners describe the car braking hard for nothing.

One complaint can name several areas, so the shares add up to more than 100%. Engines and transmissions sit near the top for gas cars and barely show up for EVs.

Without the driver-assist complaints

Take out complaints about automatic emergency braking, lane assist, blind-spot and backup sensors, and EVs still draw more: 117 per 100,000 sold against 43 for other cars. The Tesla Model 3 and Model Y move from the bottom half to near the top.
