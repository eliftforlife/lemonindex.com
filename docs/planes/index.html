How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each plane and version, for an A&P mechanic or maintenance shop at $130/hour unless you change the rate), checked against what owners reported paying. Overhauls, paint and other big jobs are one all-in price. Confidence says how well owner prices back the number: High = many owner prices that agree, Medium = some owner prices, Low = an estimate with few or no owner prices behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Owner prices are dollar amounts owners said in YouTube videos (annuals, overhauls, parachute repacks), not checked invoices. On twins, engine and propeller jobs are priced per engine. Where this plane's own owner prices are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and condition.

Counts follow fleet size (the Cessna 172 is the most common plane in the U.S.), so compare the shares, not the totals. Per-plane rates would need the FAA registry, so we don't show them. Source: NTSB aviation accident database, U.S. accidents 2008–2025.
