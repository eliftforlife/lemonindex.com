Free VIN decoderCheck any VIN.

Decode it, then see that car's recalls and problems.

It's on the dashboard by the windshield, the door frame, or your registration. We don't store it.

Buying? Ask the seller for the VIN before you go see the car. Honest sellers share it; it's also in most online listings and on the windshield.

No VIN yet?

Pick the car and year. You'll see every recall for it, newest first: a recall from the last 12 months has had the least time to be fixed, so it's the one most likely still open on a used car.

In Canada? Recalls there are run by Transport Canada: search Transport Canada recalls or check a VIN on the maker's Canadian recall site.
