Where the numbers come from

Owner complaints come from NHTSA, the U.S. government's vehicle safety agency, which publishes them for free. The score and the transmission matching are ours. See exactly how the index works.

Not included: car prices, sales figures and complaint text (other sources own those). Please don't present the data as an official rating, and don't suggest we endorse you. Using it somewhere big, or want a custom cut? Tell us. We'd love to see it.
