Is one open on your Durango?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Dodge Durango have?

72 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 138.

Which Dodge Durango year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 11 campaigns. 2013: 11, 2012: 10, 2006: 10.

What are most Dodge Durango recalls about?

Electrical: 14 of the 72 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (10) and airbags (9).

What is the newest Dodge Durango recall?

Body structure, filed Aug 2026 (NHTSA 26V528000): The rear spoiler may not be aligned properly with the roof, possibly resulting in damage to the spoiler during liftgate use.

What is the biggest Dodge Durango recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Dodge Durango has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Dodge dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
