Is one open on your Grand Caravan?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Dodge Grand Caravan have?

51 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 120.

Which Dodge Grand Caravan year has the most recalls?

The 2005, with 11 campaigns. 2002: 11, 2008: 10, 2009: 9.

What are most Dodge Grand Caravan recalls about?

Airbags: 7 of the 51 campaigns. Then electrical (7) and equipment (6).

What is the newest Dodge Grand Caravan recall?

Equipment adaptive/mobility, filed Dec 2025 (NHTSA 25V876000): Please refer to Rollx's recall report for specific model year information.

What is the biggest Dodge Grand Caravan recall?

NHTSA 20V396000 (airbags, Jul 2020) covers 925,239 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles may have a loose or missing brand emblem in the center of the steering wheel.

How do I check if my Dodge Grand Caravan has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Dodge dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
