Is one open on your XF?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Jaguar XF have?

31 recall campaigns across model years 2009–2022, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 55.

Which Jaguar XF year has the most recalls?

The 2013, with 12 campaigns. 2010: 10, 2014: 7, 2009: 6.

What are most Jaguar XF recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 9 of the 31 campaigns. Then airbags (6) and electrical (3).

What is the newest Jaguar XF recall?

Seat belts, filed Jul 2022 (NHTSA 22V524000): The driver and front passenger seat belt pretensioners may be damaged, which can cause the seat belts to not properly restrain occupants.

What is the biggest Jaguar XF recall?

NHTSA 16V373000 (airbags, May 2016) covers 20,107 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An inflator rupture may result in metal fragments striking the vehicle occupants resulting in serious injury or death.

How do I check if my Jaguar XF has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Jaguar dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
