Is one open on your Mazda?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Mazda have?

133 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 12 Mazda models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Mazda has the most recalls?

The Mazda6, with 31 campaigns. Then the Mazda3 (30), the Tribute (22), the MPV (16). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Mazda recall?

NHTSA 16V644000 (body structure, Sep 2016): 580,455 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the CX-5, Mazda3, Mazda5 among the models we track. On the affected vehicles, the lift gate support struts may corrode, possibly causing the struts to break and/or the lift gate to drop unexpectedly.

What is the newest Mazda recall?

Filed Sep 2025 for the CX-90 (NHTSA 25V568000): The fuel gauge in the instrument cluster may display an inaccurate fuel reading.

How do I check a Mazda for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mazda dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
