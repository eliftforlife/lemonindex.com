Is one open on your Tribute?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mazda Tribute have?

22 recall campaigns across model years 2001–2011, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 60.

Which Mazda Tribute year has the most recalls?

The 2001, with 15 campaigns. 2002: 9, 2003: 8, 2004: 7.

What are most Mazda Tribute recalls about?

Brakes: 3 of the 22 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (2) and speed control (2).

What is the newest Mazda Tribute recall?

Fuel system, gasoline, filed Oct 2016 (NHTSA 16V788000): On vehicles with a 3.0L engine, the Fuel Delivery Module (FDM) may crack, causing a fuel leak.

What is the biggest Mazda Tribute recall?

NHTSA 12V357000 (engine, Jul 2012) covers 217,500 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Inadequate clearance between the engine cover and the speed control cable connector could result in a stuck throttle when the accelerator pedal is fully or almost-fully depressed.

How do I check if my Mazda Tribute has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mazda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
