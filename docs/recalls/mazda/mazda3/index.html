Is one open on your Mazda3?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mazda3 have?

30 recall campaigns across model years 2004–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 58.

Which Mazda3 year has the most recalls?

The 2019, with 6 campaigns. 2016: 6, 2018: 4, 2015: 4.

What are most Mazda3 recalls about?

Airbags: 4 of the 30 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (4) and electrical (3).

What is the newest Mazda3 recall?

Airbags, filed May 2025 (NHTSA 25V357000): The Sophisticated Air Bag Sensor (SAS) units may set an error code and deactivate the air bags, due to a low battery condition.

What is the biggest Mazda3 recall?

NHTSA 16V644000 (body structure, Sep 2016) covers 580,455 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. On the affected vehicles, the lift gate support struts may corrode, possibly causing the struts to break and/or the lift gate to drop unexpectedly.

How do I check if my Mazda3 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mazda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
