Is one open on your CX-9?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-9 have?

14 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2023, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 48.

Which Mazda CX-9 year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 7 campaigns. 2010: 6, 2008: 6, 2007: 6.

What are most Mazda CX-9 recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 14 campaigns. Then electrical (2) and seats (2).

What is the newest Mazda CX-9 recall?

Fuel system, gasoline, filed Nov 2021 (NHTSA 21V875000): The impeller inside the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and deform, potentially causing the fuel pump to fail.

What is the biggest Mazda CX-9 recall?

NHTSA 17V429000 (airbags, Jun 2017) covers 205,377 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are equipped with certain air bag inflators assembled as part of the passenger frontal air bag modules, and used as original equipment or replacement equipment.

How do I check if my Mazda CX-9 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mazda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
