Is one open on your Q7?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Audi Q7 have?

27 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 54.

Which Audi Q7 year has the most recalls?

The 2017, with 7 campaigns. 2020: 5, 2019: 4, 2011: 4.

What are most Audi Q7 recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 7 of the 27 campaigns. Then airbags (5) and back over prevention (2).

What is the newest Audi Q7 recall?

Back over prevention, filed Dec 2025 (NHTSA 25V900000): Please see the recall report for a complete list of models.

What is the biggest Audi Q7 recall?

NHTSA 25V900000 (back over prevention, Dec 2025) covers 356,894 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Please see the recall report for a complete list of models.

How do I check if my Audi Q7 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Audi dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
