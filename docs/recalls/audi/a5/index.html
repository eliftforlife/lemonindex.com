Is one open on your A5?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Audi A5 have?

22 recall campaigns across model years 2008–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 44.

Which Audi A5 year has the most recalls?

The 2018, with 6 campaigns. 2021: 5, 2020: 4, 2019: 3.

What are most Audi A5 recalls about?

Airbags: 6 of the 22 campaigns. Then seat belts (4) and back over prevention (2).

What is the newest Audi A5 recall?

Seat belts, filed Dec 2025 (NHTSA 25V899000): The automatic locking retractor may fail to engage when a child seat is installed using the seat belt.

What is the biggest Audi A5 recall?

NHTSA 16V078000 (airbags, Feb 2016) covers 659,493 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Upon deployment of the driver's frontal air bag, excessive internal pressure may cause the inflator to rupture.

How do I check if my Audi A5 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Audi dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
