Is one open on your A3?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Audi A3 have?

31 recall campaigns across model years 2006–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 70.

Which Audi A3 year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 7 campaigns. 2015: 7, 2016: 6, 2018: 5.

What are most Audi A3 recalls about?

Airbags: 10 of the 31 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (4) and equipment (3).

What is the newest Audi A3 recall?

Airbags, filed Apr 2024 (NHTSA 24V251000): An electrical connection in the front passenger seat for the occupant detection system (ODS) control module may loosen and deactivate the front passenger air bag.

What is the biggest Audi A3 recall?

NHTSA 22V815000 (tires, Oct 2022) covers 296,044 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The tire pressure monitoring system may not detect an air pressure loss in adequate time when deflating occurs among all four tires simultaneously.

How do I check if my Audi A3 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Audi dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
