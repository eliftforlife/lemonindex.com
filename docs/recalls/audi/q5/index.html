Is one open on your Q5?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Audi Q5 have?

24 recall campaigns across model years 2017–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 40.

Which Audi Q5 year has the most recalls?

The 2021, with 8 campaigns. 2020: 6, 2018: 6, 2023: 5.

What are most Audi Q5 recalls about?

Electrical: 4 of the 24 campaigns. Then engine (4) and seat belts (3).

What is the newest Audi Q5 recall?

Seat belts, filed Apr 2026 (NHTSA 26V266000): The driver and front passenger seat belts may have been manufactured incorrectly, and fail to properly restrain occupants during a crash.
