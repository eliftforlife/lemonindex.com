Is one open on your Pontiac?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Pontiac have?

133 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 13 Pontiac models we track, model years 1993–2010. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Pontiac has the most recalls?

The Pontiac Grand Prix, with 22 campaigns. Then the Montana (22), the Vibe (22), the Sunfire (21). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Pontiac recall?

NHTSA 14V400000 (electrical, Jul 2014): 5,877,718 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Grand Am, Grand Prix among the models we track. If the key is not in the run position, the air bags may not deploy if the vehicle is involved in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.

What is the newest Pontiac recall?

Filed Jan 2020 for the Vibe (NHTSA 20V039000): The replacement air bag may not unfold as designed during inflation in high temperature conditions, possibly resulting in the air bag not inflating properly.

How do I check a Pontiac for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Pontiac dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
