Is one open on your Lancer?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mitsubishi Lancer have?

22 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2017, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 49.

Which Mitsubishi Lancer year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 9 campaigns. 2010: 7, 2008: 7, 2006: 7.

What are most Mitsubishi Lancer recalls about?
