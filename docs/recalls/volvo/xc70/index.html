Is one open on your XC70?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volvo XC70 have?

23 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2016, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 60.

Which Volvo XC70 year has the most recalls?

The 2008, with 8 campaigns. 2001: 6, 2010: 5, 2009: 5.

What are most Volvo XC70 recalls about?

Electrical: 5 of the 23 campaigns. Then equipment (5) and airbags (3).

What is the newest Volvo XC70 recall?

Airbags, filed Oct 2021 (NHTSA 21V800000): The driver's air bag inflator may explode during deployment, due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

What is the biggest Volvo XC70 recall?

NHTSA 20V378000 (seat belts, Jun 2020) covers 308,260 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The flexible steel cable that connects the seat belt to the front outboard seating positions may fatigue over time, causing the seat belt to not secure the occupant in the event of a crash.

How do I check if my Volvo XC70 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volvo dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from

Source: NHTSA recall campaigns (api.nhtsa.gov), matched to the model names owners use in NHTSA complaints. Vehicle counts and the "park it" warnings come from NHTSA's recall file, which starts in 2010, so older recalls have no count. A campaign's count covers every make and model in it.
