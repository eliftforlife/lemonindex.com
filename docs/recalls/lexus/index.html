Is one open on your Lexus?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Lexus have?

62 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 4 Lexus models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Lexus has the most recalls?

The Lexus RX, with 30 campaigns. Then the IS (21), the ES (20), the LS (18). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Lexus recall?

NHTSA 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline, Jan 2020): 1,830,752 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the ES, IS, LS, RX among the models we track. Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) recalled certain 2018-2019 4Runner, Highlander, Camry, Land Cruiser, Sequoia, Sienna, Tacoma and Tundra, and Lexus RC 300, RC 350, GS 350, GX 460, IS 300, LC 500, LS 500,…

What is the newest Lexus recall?

Filed Nov 2026 for the IS (NHTSA 26V748000): The inflators may rupture due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

How do I check a Lexus for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Lexus dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
