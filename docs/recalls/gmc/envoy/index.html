Is one open on your Envoy?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the GMC Envoy have?

28 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2009, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 46.

Which GMC Envoy year has the most recalls?

The 2002, with 16 campaigns. 2003: 10, 2006: 9, 2007: 5.

What are most GMC Envoy recalls about?

Lights: 4 of the 28 campaigns. Then visibility (4) and airbags (3).

What is the newest GMC Envoy recall?

Visibility, filed Sep 2015 (NHTSA 15V599000): Fluid may enter into the driver's door master power window switch module, causing corrosion that could result in a short in the circuit board, causing window switches to become inoperative.

What is the biggest GMC Envoy recall?

NHTSA 15V519000 (lights, Aug 2015) covers 429,170 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, the headlamp driver module (HDM) may overheat and fail, causing the headlamps and daytime running lights to not illuminate.

How do I check if my GMC Envoy has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any GMC dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
