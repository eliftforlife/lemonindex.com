Is one open on your GMC?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does GMC have?

239 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 9 GMC models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which GMC has the most recalls?

The GMC Sierra, with 97 campaigns. Then the Yukon (70), the Acadia (37), the Yukon XL (32). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest GMC recall?

NHTSA 16V651000 (airbags, Sep 2016): 3,640,162 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Sierra, Yukon, Yukon XL among the models we track. In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

What is the newest GMC recall?

Filed Sep 2026 for the Sierra (NHTSA 26V611000): The left-side or right-side roof rail air bag (RRAB) inflator end cap may detach from the inflator or the inflator sidewall may rupture.

How do I check a GMC for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any GMC dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
