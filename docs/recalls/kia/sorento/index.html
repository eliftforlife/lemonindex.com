Is one open on your Sorento?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Kia Sorento have?

40 recall campaigns across model years 2003–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 76.

Which Kia Sorento year has the most recalls?

The 2016, with 8 campaigns. 2011: 8, 2022: 6, 2014: 6.

What are most Kia Sorento recalls about?

Electrical: 6 of the 40 campaigns. Then engine (6) and transmission & drivetrain (5).

What is the newest Kia Sorento recall?

Electrical, filed Jan 2026 (NHTSA 26V046000): Due to a software error, the instrument panel display may fail.

What is the biggest Kia Sorento recall?

NHTSA 23V652000 (brakes, Sep 2023) covers 1,730,192 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may experience an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

How do I check if my Kia Sorento has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Kia dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
