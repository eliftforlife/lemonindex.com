Is one open on your Kia?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Kia have?

161 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 12 Kia models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Kia has the most recalls?

The Kia Sorento, with 40 campaigns. Then the Optima (31), the Sportage (31), the Sedona (28). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Kia recall?

NHTSA 23V652000 (brakes, Sep 2023): 1,730,192 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Forte, Optima, Rio, Sorento, Soul, Sportage among the models we track. The Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may experience an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

What is the newest Kia recall?

Filed Jul 2026 for the Telluride (NHTSA 26V430000): The front power seat motor may overheat due to a stuck power seat slide knob or an improper recall 24V407 repair, which can result in a fire while parked or driving.

How do I check a Kia for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Kia dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
