Is one open on your Sedona?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Kia Sedona have?

28 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 73.

Which Kia Sedona year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 7 campaigns. 2022: 6, 2011: 6, 2007: 6.

What are most Kia Sedona recalls about?

Body structure: 3 of the 28 campaigns. Then electrical (3) and fuel system, gasoline (3).

What is the newest Kia Sedona recall?

Fuel system, gasoline, filed Apr 2026 (NHTSA 26V232000): Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the fuel pipe and fuel rail.

What is the biggest Kia Sedona recall?

NHTSA 13V114000 (lights, Apr 2013) covers 980,377 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Originally, in April 2013, Kia recalled 623,658 model year 2007-2010 Rondo and Sportage, model year 2007-2011 Sorento, model year 2007 Sedona, model year 2010-2011 Soul, and model year 2011 Optima vehicles.

How do I check if my Kia Sedona has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Kia dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
