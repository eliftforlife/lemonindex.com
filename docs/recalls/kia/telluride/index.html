Is one open on your Telluride?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Kia Telluride have?

17 recall campaigns across model years 2020–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 33.

Which Kia Telluride year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 7 campaigns. 2020: 7, 2023: 6, 2024: 5.

What are most Kia Telluride recalls about?

Electrical: 4 of the 17 campaigns. Then seats (3) and airbags (2).

What is the newest Kia Telluride recall?

Seats, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V430000): The front power seat motor may overheat due to a stuck power seat slide knob or an improper recall 24V407 repair, which can result in a fire while parked or driving.

What is the biggest Kia Telluride recall?

NHTSA 26V430000 (seats, Jul 2026) covers 462,869 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The front power seat motor may overheat due to a stuck power seat slide knob or an improper recall 24V407 repair, which can result in a fire while parked or driving.

How do I check if my Kia Telluride has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Kia dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
