Is one open on your BMW?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does BMW have?

222 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 5 BMW models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which BMW has the most recalls?

The BMW X5, with 104 campaigns. Then the 3 Series (68), the X3 (65), the 5 Series (57). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest BMW recall?

NHTSA 22V119000 (engine, Mar 2022): 917,106 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the 5 Series, X3, X5 among the models we track. An electrical short can cause the PCV valve heater to overheat, increasing the risk of a fire while driving or, soon after driving when parked.

What is the newest BMW recall?

Filed Nov 2026 for the 3 Series, 5 Series, X1, X3, X5 (NHTSA 26V733000): An improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit.

How do I check a BMW for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any BMW dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
