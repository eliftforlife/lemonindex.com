Is one open on your X3?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the BMW X3 have?

65 recall campaigns across model years 2004–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 140.

Which BMW X3 year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 16 campaigns. 2018: 11, 2021: 10, 2019: 10.

What are most BMW X3 recalls about?

Airbags: 13 of the 65 campaigns. Then electrical (8) and engine (8).

What is the newest BMW X3 recall?

Engine, filed Nov 2026 (NHTSA 26V733000): An improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit.

What is the biggest BMW X3 recall?

NHTSA 22V119000 (engine, Mar 2022) covers 917,106 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An electrical short can cause the PCV valve heater to overheat, increasing the risk of a fire while driving or, soon after driving when parked.

How do I check if my BMW X3 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any BMW dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
