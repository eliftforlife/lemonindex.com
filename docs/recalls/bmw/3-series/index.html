Is one open on your 3 Series?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the BMW 3 Series have?

68 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 157.

Which BMW 3 Series year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 14 campaigns. 2014: 13, 2019: 9, 2021: 8.

What are most BMW 3 Series recalls about?

Airbags: 16 of the 68 campaigns. Then engine (11) and electrical (9).

What is the newest BMW 3 Series recall?

Engine, filed Nov 2026 (NHTSA 26V733000): An improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit.

What is the biggest BMW 3 Series recall?

NHTSA 17V683000 (engine, Oct 2017) covers 740,561 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The heater for the positive crankcase ventilation (PCV) valve may short circuit.

How do I check if my BMW 3 Series has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any BMW dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
