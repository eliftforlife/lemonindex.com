How many recalls does the BMW X5 have?

104 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 197.

The 2010, with 19 campaigns. 2009: 16, 2011: 15, 2019: 13.

What are most BMW X5 recalls about?

Airbags: 19 of the 104 campaigns. Then engine (14) and brakes (11).

What is the newest BMW X5 recall?

Engine, filed Nov 2026 (NHTSA 26V733000): An improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit.

NHTSA 22V119000 (engine, Mar 2022) covers 917,106 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An electrical short can cause the PCV valve heater to overheat, increasing the risk of a fire while driving or, soon after driving when parked.
