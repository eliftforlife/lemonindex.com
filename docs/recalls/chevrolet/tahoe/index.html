Is one open on your Tahoe?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Tahoe have?

75 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 142.

Which Chevrolet Tahoe year has the most recalls?

The 2021, with 16 campaigns. 2015: 14, 2000: 10, 2016: 8.

What are most Chevrolet Tahoe recalls about?

Airbags: 11 of the 75 campaigns. Then brakes (10) and seat belts (9).

What is the newest Chevrolet Tahoe recall?

Wheels, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V304000): Incorrect bolts may have been installed on the left and right front wheel hubs, which can result in the bolts loosening or deforming over time.

What is the biggest Chevrolet Tahoe recall?

NHTSA 16V651000 (airbags, Sep 2016) covers 3,640,162 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

How do I check if my Chevrolet Tahoe has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
