Is one open on your Silverado?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Silverado have?

95 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 212.

Which Chevrolet Silverado year has the most recalls?

What are most Chevrolet Silverado recalls about?

Airbags: 20 of the 95 campaigns. Then brakes (15) and lights (12).

What is the newest Chevrolet Silverado recall?

Airbags, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V611000): The left-side or right-side roof rail air bag (RRAB) inflator end cap may detach from the inflator or the inflator sidewall may rupture.

What is the biggest Chevrolet Silverado recall?

NHTSA 16V651000 (airbags, Sep 2016) covers 3,640,162 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

How do I check if my Chevrolet Silverado has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
