Is one open on your Chevrolet?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Chevrolet have?

481 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 30 Chevrolet models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Chevrolet has the most recalls?

The Chevrolet Silverado, with 95 campaigns. Then the Tahoe (75), the Suburban (73), the Malibu (54). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Chevrolet recall?

NHTSA 14V400000 (electrical, Jul 2014): 5,877,718 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Impala, Malibu, Monte Carlo among the models we track. If the key is not in the run position, the air bags may not deploy if the vehicle is involved in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.

What is the newest Chevrolet recall?

Filed Sep 2026 for the Silverado (NHTSA 26V611000): The left-side or right-side roof rail air bag (RRAB) inflator end cap may detach from the inflator or the inflator sidewall may rupture.

How do I check a Chevrolet for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
