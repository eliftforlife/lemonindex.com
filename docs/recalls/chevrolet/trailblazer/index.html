Is one open on your Trailblazer?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Trailblazer have?

31 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 46.

Which Chevrolet Trailblazer year has the most recalls?

The 2002, with 13 campaigns. 2003: 9, 2006: 8, 2007: 5.

What are most Chevrolet Trailblazer recalls about?

Lights: 4 of the 31 campaigns. Then visibility (4) and airbags (3).

What is the newest Chevrolet Trailblazer recall?

Back over prevention, filed Aug 2026 (NHTSA 26V539000): The rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image.

What is the biggest Chevrolet Trailblazer recall?

NHTSA 15V519000 (lights, Aug 2015) covers 429,170 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, the headlamp driver module (HDM) may overheat and fail, causing the headlamps and daytime running lights to not illuminate.

How do I check if my Chevrolet Trailblazer has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
