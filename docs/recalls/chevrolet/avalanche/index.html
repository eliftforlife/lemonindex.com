Is one open on your Avalanche?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Avalanche have?

25 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2013, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 52.

Which Chevrolet Avalanche year has the most recalls?

The 2004, with 7 campaigns. 2003: 7, 2011: 5, 2009: 5.

What are most Chevrolet Avalanche recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 25 campaigns. Then brakes (3) and electrical (3).

What is the newest Chevrolet Avalanche recall?

Airbags, filed Feb 2021 (NHTSA 21V052000): An inflator explosion may result in sharp metal fragments striking the driver or other occupants, resulting in serious injury or death.

What is the biggest Chevrolet Avalanche recall?

NHTSA 21V050000 (airbags, Feb 2021) covers 2,641,272 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An inflator explosion may result in sharp metal fragments striking the driver or other occupants, resulting in serious injury or death.

How do I check if my Chevrolet Avalanche has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
