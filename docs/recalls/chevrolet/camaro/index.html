Is one open on your Camaro?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Camaro have?

18 recall campaigns across model years 2010–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 36.

Which Chevrolet Camaro year has the most recalls?

The 2012, with 5 campaigns. 2021: 4, 2014: 4, 2013: 4.

What are most Chevrolet Camaro recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 18 campaigns. Then electrical (4) and steering (3).

What is the newest Chevrolet Camaro recall?

Steering, filed Mar 2025 (NHTSA 25V175000): The electric power steering (EPS) assist may fail.

What is the biggest Chevrolet Camaro recall?

NHTSA 14V346000 (electrical, Jun 2014) covers 464,712 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, the driver may accidentally hit the ignition key with their knee, unintentionally knocking the key out of the run position, turning off the engine.

How do I check if my Chevrolet Camaro has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chevrolet dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
