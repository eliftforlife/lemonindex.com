Is one open on your Wrangler?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Wrangler have?

82 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 212.

Which Jeep Wrangler year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 15 campaigns. 2021: 15, 2024: 14, 2023: 14.

What are most Jeep Wrangler recalls about?

Electrical: 14 of the 82 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (12) and airbags (10).

What is the newest Jeep Wrangler recall?

Steering, filed Jun 2026 (NHTSA 26V363000): The electric hydraulic power steering pump wiring may overheat and cause a vehicle fire, even when the vehicle is parked with the ignition in the "Off" position.

What is the biggest Jeep Wrangler recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Jeep Wrangler has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Jeep dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
