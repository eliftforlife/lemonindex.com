Is one open on your Liberty?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Liberty have?

23 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2012, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 38.

Which Jeep Liberty year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 10 campaigns. 2005: 8, 2007: 7, 2008: 5.

What are most Jeep Liberty recalls about?

Suspension: 5 of the 23 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (4) and brakes (3).

What is the newest Jeep Liberty recall?

Suspension, filed May 2018 (NHTSA 18V278000): The rear suspension lower control arms can fracture due to excessive corrosion, causing a loss of vehicle control.

What is the biggest Jeep Liberty recall?

NHTSA 13V252000 (fuel system, gasoline, Jun 2013) covers 1,560,000 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The fuel tanks in these vehicles are at risk of failure and leakage in certain rear impacts.

How do I check if my Jeep Liberty has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Jeep dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
