Is one open on your Grand Cherokee?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

123 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 268.

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 24 campaigns. 2023: 23, 2014: 19, 2024: 16.

What are most Jeep Grand Cherokee recalls about?

Electrical: 22 of the 123 campaigns. Then brakes (12) and airbags (11).

What is the newest Jeep Grand Cherokee recall?

Suspension, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V562000): The rear coil springs may be incorrectly installed and detach from the vehicle while driving.

What is the biggest Jeep Grand Cherokee recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Jeep Grand Cherokee has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Jeep dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
