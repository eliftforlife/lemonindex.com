Is one open on your Compass?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Compass have?

26 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 37.

Which Jeep Compass year has the most recalls?

The 2018, with 6 campaigns. 2022: 4, 2017: 4, 2012: 4.

What are most Jeep Compass recalls about?

Electrical: 4 of the 26 campaigns. Then engine (3) and equipment (3).

What is the newest Jeep Compass recall?

Back over prevention, filed Jun 2024 (NHTSA 24V436000): The radio software may prevent the rearview image from displaying.

What is the biggest Jeep Compass recall?

NHTSA 16V668000 (seat belts, Sep 2016) covers 1,435,625 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The Occupant Restraint Control (OCR) module may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags, seat belt pretensioners, and side air bags from deploying in the event of a crash.

How do I check if my Jeep Compass has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Jeep dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
