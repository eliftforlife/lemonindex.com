Is one open on your XT5?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac XT5 have?

15 recall campaigns across model years 2017–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 19.

Which Cadillac XT5 year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 5 campaigns. 2023: 4, 2024: 3, 2022: 3.

What are most Cadillac XT5 recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 3 of the 15 campaigns. Then airbags (2) and tires (2).

What is the newest Cadillac XT5 recall?

Electrical, filed Jun 2024 (NHTSA 24V459000): The instrument panel may not illuminate when the hazard warning lights, high beams, or turn signals are activated.

What is the biggest Cadillac XT5 recall?

NHTSA 22V709000 (back over prevention, Sep 2022) covers 95,231 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The rearview camera coaxial cable connectors may have been crimped improperly, causing the rearview camera to fail or function intermittently.

How do I check if my Cadillac XT5 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Cadillac dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
