Is one open on your Escalade?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac Escalade have?

57 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 91.

Which Cadillac Escalade year has the most recalls?

The 2021, with 11 campaigns. 2015: 9, 2003: 7, 2011: 6.

What are most Cadillac Escalade recalls about?

Airbags: 11 of the 57 campaigns. Then electrical (5) and fuel system, gasoline (5).

What is the newest Cadillac Escalade recall?

Airbags, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V329000): The front-driver air bag inflator may rupture during deployment.

What is the biggest Cadillac Escalade recall?

NHTSA 16V651000 (airbags, Sep 2016) covers 3,640,162 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

How do I check if my Cadillac Escalade has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Cadillac dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
