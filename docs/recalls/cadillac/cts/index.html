Is one open on your CTS?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac CTS have?

27 recall campaigns across model years 2003–2019, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 61.

Which Cadillac CTS year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 6 campaigns. 2007: 6, 2006: 6, 2005: 6.

What are most Cadillac CTS recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 27 campaigns. Then steering (4) and brakes (3).

What is the newest Cadillac CTS recall?

Airbags, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V329000): The front-driver air bag inflator may rupture during deployment.

What is the biggest Cadillac CTS recall?

NHTSA 10V240000 (electrical, Jun 2010) covers 1,365,070 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. How do I check if my Cadillac CTS has an open recall?
