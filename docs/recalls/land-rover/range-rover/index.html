Is one open on your Range Rover?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Land Rover Range Rover have?

59 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 129.

Which Land Rover Range Rover year has the most recalls?

The 2016, with 13 campaigns. 2023: 11, 2024: 10, 2022: 10.

What are most Land Rover Range Rover recalls about?

Airbags: 10 of the 59 campaigns. Then latches/locks/linkages (6) and back over prevention (5).

What is the newest Land Rover Range Rover recall?

Back over prevention, filed Oct 2026 (NHTSA 26V678000): Water may not be able to drain away from the rearview camera due to insufficient drain holes, which may result in damage to the rearview camera.

What is the biggest Land Rover Range Rover recall?

NHTSA 26V389000 (airbags, Jun 2026) covers 250,857 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The driver’s air bag clockspring connector may corrode, which can cause the air bag not to deploy as intended.

How do I check if my Land Rover Range Rover has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Land Rover dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
