Is one open on your Discovery?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Land Rover Discovery have?

42 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 89.

Which Land Rover Discovery year has the most recalls?

The 2000, with 11 campaigns. 2001: 7, 2023: 6, 2022: 6.

What are most Land Rover Discovery recalls about?

Engine: 7 of the 42 campaigns. Then electrical (6) and fuel system, gasoline (3).

What is the newest Land Rover Discovery recall?

Electrical, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V599000): The DC-DC converter may malfunction due to an electrical overload that can occur during high power demand.

What is the biggest Land Rover Discovery recall?

NHTSA 26V389000 (airbags, Jun 2026) covers 250,857 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The driver’s air bag clockspring connector may corrode, which can cause the air bag not to deploy as intended.

How do I check if my Land Rover Discovery has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Land Rover dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
