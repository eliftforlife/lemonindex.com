Is one open on your Range Rover Sport?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Land Rover Range Rover Sport have?

39 recall campaigns across model years 2006–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 68.

Which Land Rover Range Rover Sport year has the most recalls?

The 2016, with 11 campaigns. 2015: 9, 2020: 8, 2014: 8.

What are most Land Rover Range Rover Sport recalls about?

Electrical: 5 of the 39 campaigns. Then latches/locks/linkages (5) and airbags (4).

What is the newest Land Rover Range Rover Sport recall?

Electrical, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V599000): The DC-DC converter may malfunction due to an electrical overload that can occur during high power demand.

What is the biggest Land Rover Range Rover Sport recall?

NHTSA 26V248000 (electrical, Apr 2026) covers 170,169 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Insufficient electrical load balancing during times of high demand can lead to a current overload condition and malfunction of the DCDC converter.

How do I check if my Land Rover Range Rover Sport has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Land Rover dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
