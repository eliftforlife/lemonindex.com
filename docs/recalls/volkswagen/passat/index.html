Is one open on your Passat?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Passat have?

27 recall campaigns across model years 2012–2021, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 57.

Which Volkswagen Passat year has the most recalls?

The 2012, with 11 campaigns. 2014: 10, 2013: 9, 2017: 7.

What are most Volkswagen Passat recalls about?

Airbags: 8 of the 27 campaigns. Then brakes (4) and unknown or other (4).

What is the newest Volkswagen Passat recall?

Airbags, filed Oct 2024 (NHTSA 24V834000): The driver's side frontal air bag inflator may explode due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

What is the biggest Volkswagen Passat recall?

NHTSA 19V389000 (lights, May 2019) covers 662,185 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The blocking cap disabling the adjustment of the headlight's horizontal aim may not have been installed.

How do I check if my Volkswagen Passat has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volkswagen dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
