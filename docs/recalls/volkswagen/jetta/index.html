Is one open on your Jetta?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Jetta have?

58 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 143.

Which Volkswagen Jetta year has the most recalls?

The 2019, with 11 campaigns. 2011: 10, 2015: 8, 2013: 8.

What are most Volkswagen Jetta recalls about?

Electrical: 7 of the 58 campaigns. Then lights (7) and fuel system, gasoline (6).

What is the newest Volkswagen Jetta recall?

Electrical, filed Mar 2026 (NHTSA 26V185000): The instrument panel may fail to display warning lights or gauges.

What is the biggest Volkswagen Jetta recall?

NHTSA 19V615000 (electrical, Aug 2019) covers 679,027 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. A build-up of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may enable the key to be removed from the ignition when the transmission shift lever is not in "Park.".

How do I check if my Volkswagen Jetta has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volkswagen dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
