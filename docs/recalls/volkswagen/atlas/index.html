Is one open on your Atlas?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Atlas have?

47 recall campaigns across model years 2018–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 83.

Which Volkswagen Atlas year has the most recalls?

The 2021, with 17 campaigns. 2018: 17, 2019: 12, 2020: 11.

What are most Volkswagen Atlas recalls about?

Airbags: 8 of the 47 campaigns. Then back over prevention (5) and brakes (5).

What is the newest Volkswagen Atlas recall?

Steering, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V590000): One of the bolts that attaches the steering rack to the subframe may corrode and break.

What is the biggest Volkswagen Atlas recall?

NHTSA 19V389000 (lights, May 2019) covers 662,185 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The blocking cap disabling the adjustment of the headlight's horizontal aim may not have been installed.

How do I check if my Volkswagen Atlas has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volkswagen dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
