Is one open on your Tiguan?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Tiguan have?

38 recall campaigns across model years 2011–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 60.

Which Volkswagen Tiguan year has the most recalls?

The 2018, with 14 campaigns. 2022: 5, 2021: 5, 2019: 5.

What are most Volkswagen Tiguan recalls about?

Airbags: 6 of the 38 campaigns. Then back over prevention (4) and suspension (4).

What is the newest Volkswagen Tiguan recall?

Steering, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V590000): One of the bolts that attaches the steering rack to the subframe may corrode and break.

What is the biggest Volkswagen Tiguan recall?

NHTSA 15V483000 (airbags, Aug 2015) covers 415,825 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In the affected vehicles, debris may contaminate the air bag clock spring, a spiral wound, flat cable that keeps the air bag powered while the steering wheel is being turned.

How do I check if my Volkswagen Tiguan has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volkswagen dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
