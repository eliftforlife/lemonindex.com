Is one open on your Golf?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Golf have?

44 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2021, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 119.

Which Volkswagen Golf year has the most recalls?

The 2015, with 12 campaigns. 2016: 9, 2001: 9, 2000: 8.

What are most Volkswagen Golf recalls about?

Airbags: 7 of the 44 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (6) and lights (5).

What is the newest Volkswagen Golf recall?

Fuel system, gasoline, filed Feb 2024 (NHTSA 24V110000): Due to a problem with a suction jet pump seal inside the fuel tank, fuel may flow into the evaporative emissions (EVAP) system and possibly leak out of the charcoal canister.

What is the biggest Volkswagen Golf recall?

NHTSA 19V615000 (electrical, Aug 2019) covers 679,027 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. A build-up of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may enable the key to be removed from the ignition when the transmission shift lever is not in "Park.".

How do I check if my Volkswagen Golf has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Volkswagen dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
