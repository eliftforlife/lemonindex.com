Is one open on your Buick?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Buick have?

110 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 10 Buick models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Buick has the most recalls?

The Buick Enclave, with 28 campaigns. Then the LaCrosse (25), the Regal (17), the Encore (13). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Buick recall?

NHTSA 16V651000 (airbags, Sep 2016): 3,640,162 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Encore, LaCrosse among the models we track. In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

What is the newest Buick recall?

Filed Feb 2026 for the Regal (NHTSA 26V113000): The rear suspension toe links may have received improper corrosion protection, which can result in rear toe link fracture.

How do I check a Buick for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Buick dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
