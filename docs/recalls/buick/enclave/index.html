Is one open on your Enclave?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Buick Enclave have?

28 recall campaigns across model years 2008–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 65.

Which Buick Enclave year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 8 campaigns. 2014: 7, 2020: 6, 2012: 6.

What are most Buick Enclave recalls about?

Airbags: 8 of the 28 campaigns. Then electrical (4) and seat belts (3).

What is the newest Buick Enclave recall?

Airbags, filed May 2023 (NHTSA 23V334000): The driver's air bag inflator may explode during deployment, due to a manufacturing defect.

What is the biggest Buick Enclave recall?

NHTSA 10V240000 (electrical, Jun 2010) covers 1,365,070 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign.
