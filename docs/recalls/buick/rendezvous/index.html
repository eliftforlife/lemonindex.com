Is one open on your Rendezvous?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Buick Rendezvous have?

10 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2007, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 18.

Which Buick Rendezvous year has the most recalls?

The 2003, with 7 campaigns. 2004: 5, 2002: 3, 2005: 2.

What are most Buick Rendezvous recalls about?

Airbags: 3 of the 10 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (2) and electrical (1).

What is the newest Buick Rendezvous recall?

Fuel system, gasoline, filed Aug 2007 (NHTSA 07V349000): ON CERTAIN VEHICLES, VARIATIONS IN THE MANUFACTURING PROCESS OR EXPOSURE TO HIGH AMBIENT TEMPERATURES MAY LEAD TO FRACTURE OF THE FUEL TANK INLET CHECK VALVE (ICV).

How do I check if my Buick Rendezvous has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Buick dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
