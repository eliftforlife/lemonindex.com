Is one open on your pickup?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ram pickup have?

122 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 249.

Which Ram pickup year has the most recalls?

The 2019, with 30 campaigns. 2015: 18, 2016: 17, 2014: 17.

What are most Ram pickup recalls about?

Airbags: 20 of the 122 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (17) and steering (13).

What is the newest Ram pickup recall?

Back over prevention, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V560000): A radio software error may cause the rearview camera image not to display.

What is the biggest Ram pickup recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Ram pickup has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ram dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
