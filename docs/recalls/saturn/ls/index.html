Is one open on your LS?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Saturn LS have?

12 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2002, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 16.

Which Saturn LS year has the most recalls?

The 2001, with 7 campaigns. 2000: 6, 2002: 3.

What are most Saturn LS recalls about?

Airbags: 3 of the 12 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (2) and lights (2).

What is the newest Saturn LS recall?

Engine, filed Nov 2007 (NHTSA 07V519000): ON CERTAIN PASSENGER VEHICLES EQUIPPED WITH A 2.2L 4-CYLINDER ENGINE, LINKS IN THE ENGINE'S TIMING CHAIN CAN SEPARATE.

How do I check if my Saturn LS has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Saturn dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
