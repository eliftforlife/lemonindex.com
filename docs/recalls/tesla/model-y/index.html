Is one open on your Model Y?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Tesla Model Y have?

36 recall campaigns across model years 2020–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 92.

Which Tesla Model Y year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 24 campaigns. 2021: 23, 2022: 20, 2023: 17.

What are most Tesla Model Y recalls about?

Electrical: 8 of the 36 campaigns. Then automatic emergency braking (4) and back over prevention (4).

What is the newest Tesla Model Y recall?

Lights, filed Aug 2026 (NHTSA 26V507000): The headlight low beams may be too bright and exceed the maximum light output.

What is the biggest Tesla Model Y recall?

NHTSA 24V051000 (electrical, Jan 2024) covers 2,193,869 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An incorrect font size is displayed on the instrument panel for the Brake, Park, and Antilock Brake System (ABS) warning lights.

How do I check if my Tesla Model Y has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Tesla dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
