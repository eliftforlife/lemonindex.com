Is one open on your Forester?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Subaru Forester have?

38 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 105.

Which Subaru Forester year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 18 campaigns. 2010: 17, 2011: 16, 2012: 15.

What are most Subaru Forester recalls about?

Airbags: 14 of the 38 campaigns. Then engine (4) and equipment (3).

What is the newest Subaru Forester recall?

Equipment, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V436000): These vehicles were manufactured with an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) on the certification label.

What is the biggest Subaru Forester recall?

NHTSA 19V149000 (lights, Feb 2019) covers 1,303,530 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Exposure to certain contaminants may cause the brake light switch to malfunction, preventing the brake lights from illuminating and also preventing keyless ignition vehicles from starting and CVT/automatic transmissions…

How do I check if my Subaru Forester has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Subaru dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
