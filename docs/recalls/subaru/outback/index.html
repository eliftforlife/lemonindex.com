Is one open on your Outback?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Subaru Outback have?

68 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 189.

Which Subaru Outback year has the most recalls?

The 2010, with 18 campaigns. 2012: 16, 2011: 16, 2003: 15.

What are most Subaru Outback recalls about?

Airbags: 20 of the 68 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (10) and brakes (5).

What is the newest Subaru Outback recall?

Airbags, filed Mar 2024 (NHTSA 24V227000): A capacitor in the sensors for the Occupant Detection System (ODS) may crack and short circuit, which can prevent the front passenger air bag from deploying in a crash.

What is the biggest Subaru Outback recall?

NHTSA 12V602000 (lights, Dec 2012) covers 633,842 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Up to 78,650 of these vehicles may have been equipped with accessory puddle lights that, when illuminated, brighten the area under the doors of the vehicle.

How do I check if my Subaru Outback has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Subaru dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
