Is one open on your Navigator?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln Navigator have?

65 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 125.

Which Lincoln Navigator year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 14 campaigns. 2020: 13, 2021: 12, 2018: 12.

What are most Lincoln Navigator recalls about?

Visibility: 8 of the 65 campaigns. Then electrical (7) and back over prevention (6).

What is the newest Lincoln Navigator recall?

Seats, filed Oct 2026 (NHTSA 26V691000): The Rear Audio Control Module (RACM) may inadvertently command the chauffeur seat feature, causing the front passenger seat to adjust forward, rearward, or recline unexpectedly without occupant input.

What is the biggest Lincoln Navigator recall?

NHTSA 26V104000 (electrical, Feb 2026) covers 4,381,878 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. When towing a trailer, the integrated trailer module (ITRM) may lose communication with the vehicle, possibly causing a loss of brake and turn signal lights, or a loss of brake function.

How do I check if my Lincoln Navigator has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Lincoln dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
