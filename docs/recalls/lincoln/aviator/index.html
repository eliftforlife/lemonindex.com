Is one open on your Aviator?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln Aviator have?

51 recall campaigns across model years 2003–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 120.

Which Lincoln Aviator year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 30 campaigns. 2021: 28, 2022: 22, 2023: 16.

What are most Lincoln Aviator recalls about?

Back over prevention: 11 of the 51 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (7) and seats (5).

What is the newest Lincoln Aviator recall?

Seats, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V470000): An unoccupied driver's seat may recline unintentionally during certain scenarios, such as remote unlock or remote start.

What is the biggest Lincoln Aviator recall?

NHTSA 26V123000 (back over prevention, Mar 2026) covers 889,950 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The image on the center display may flip or invert, resulting in an incorrectly displayed rearview image when the vehicle is placed in reverse.

How do I check if my Lincoln Aviator has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Lincoln dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
