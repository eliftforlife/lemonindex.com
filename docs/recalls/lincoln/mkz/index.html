Is one open on your MKZ?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln MKZ have?

35 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 92.

Which Lincoln MKZ year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 14 campaigns. 2013: 13, 2015: 11, 2010: 9.

What are most Lincoln MKZ recalls about?

Airbags: 9 of the 35 campaigns. Then latches/locks/linkages (5) and steering (4).

What is the newest Lincoln MKZ recall?

Back over prevention, filed Oct 2025 (NHTSA 25V695000): The rearview camera may display a distorted, intermittent, or blank image when the vehicle is in reverse.

What is the biggest Lincoln MKZ recall?

NHTSA 21V158000 (airbags, Mar 2021) covers 2,602,668 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The driver's side air bag inflator may explode due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

How do I check if my Lincoln MKZ has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Lincoln dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
