Is one open on your Ford?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Ford have?

851 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 26 Ford models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Ford has the most recalls?

The Ford F-Series, with 157 campaigns. Then the Explorer (120), the E-Series (116), the Escape (101). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Ford recall?

NHTSA 26V104000 (electrical, Feb 2026): 4,381,878 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Expedition, F-Series, Maverick, Ranger among the models we track. When towing a trailer, the integrated trailer module (ITRM) may lose communication with the vehicle, possibly causing a loss of brake and turn signal lights, or a loss of brake function.

What is the newest Ford recall?

Filed Nov 2026 for the Maverick (NHTSA 26V724000): The 12V positive battery cable terminal may become loose or detach, which can cause a loss of electrical systems (including power steering assist, power brake assist, and exterior lighting) or a loss of drive power.

How do I check a Ford for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
