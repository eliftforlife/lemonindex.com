Is one open on your Transit?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Transit have?

63 recall campaigns across model years 2015–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 146.

Which Ford Transit year has the most recalls?

The 2015, with 26 campaigns. 2016: 21, 2023: 19, 2017: 18.

What are most Ford Transit recalls about?

Seats: 11 of the 63 campaigns. Then back over prevention (10) and transmission & drivetrain (7).

What is the newest Ford Transit recall?

Body structure, filed Feb 2026 (NHTSA 26V061000): The engine crossmember may not be secured properly, which can cause the engine to shift and possibly result in a loss of drive power or brake failure.

What is the biggest Ford Transit recall?

NHTSA 25V572000 (back over prevention, Sep 2025) covers 1,456,417 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The rearview camera may display a distorted, inverted, or blank image when the vehicle is in reverse.

How do I check if my Ford Transit has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
