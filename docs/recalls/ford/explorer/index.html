Is one open on your Explorer?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Explorer have?

120 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 277.

Which Ford Explorer year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 33 campaigns. 2021: 26, 2022: 24, 2025: 20.

What are most Ford Explorer recalls about?

Transmission & drivetrain: 15 of the 120 campaigns. Then back over prevention (13) and fuel system, gasoline (12).

What is the newest Ford Explorer recall?

Seats, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V470000): An unoccupied driver's seat may recline unintentionally during certain scenarios, such as remote unlock or remote start.

What is the biggest Ford Explorer recall?

NHTSA 24V031000 (body structure, Jan 2024) covers 1,889,110 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The A-pillar trim retention clips may not be properly engaged, allowing the trim to detach.

How do I check if my Ford Explorer has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
