Is one open on your E-Series?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford E-Series have?

116 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2023, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 298.

Which Ford E-Series year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 43 campaigns. 2007: 36, 2008: 34, 2009: 27.

What are most Ford E-Series recalls about?

Equipment adaptive/mobility: 59 of the 116 campaigns. Then equipment (11) and fuel system, gasoline (8).

What is the newest Ford E-Series recall?

Engine, filed Nov 2025 (NHTSA 25V793000): The engine cover may become excessively hot.

What is the biggest Ford E-Series recall?

NHTSA 17V472000 (seat belts, Jul 2017) covers 94,605 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Improperly manufactured bolts may have been used to install certain seats, seatbacks, seatbelt buckles, or seatbelt anchors on these vehicles.

How do I check if my Ford E-Series has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
