Is one open on your Bronco?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Bronco have?

47 recall campaigns across model years 2021–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 96.

Which Ford Bronco year has the most recalls?

The 2022, with 23 campaigns. 2021: 20, 2025: 19, 2023: 18.

What are most Ford Bronco recalls about?

Back over prevention: 8 of the 47 campaigns. Then suspension (5) and engine (4).

What is the newest Ford Bronco recall?

Engine, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V468000): The engine compartment wiring harness may become damaged and short circuit.

What is the biggest Ford Bronco recall?

NHTSA 25V315000 (back over prevention, May 2025) covers 1,076,138 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. A software error may cause the rearview camera image to delay, freeze, or not display when the vehicle is in reverse.

How do I check if my Ford Bronco has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
