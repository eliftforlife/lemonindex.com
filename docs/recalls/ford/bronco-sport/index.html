Is one open on your Bronco Sport?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Bronco Sport have?

29 recall campaigns across model years 2021–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 60.

Which Ford Bronco Sport year has the most recalls?

The 2021, with 16 campaigns. 2022: 15, 2023: 14, 2024: 8.

What are most Ford Bronco Sport recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 6 of the 29 campaigns. Then electrical (5) and engine (3).

What is the newest Ford Bronco Sport recall?

Suspension, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V340000): The front lower control arm ball joints may have been incorrectly installed or incorrectly repaired at the vehicle assembly plant, allowing the control arm to disconnect from the front wheel knuckle.

What is the biggest Ford Bronco Sport recall?

NHTSA 25V467000 (fuel system, gasoline, Jul 2025) covers 694,271 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. A fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.

How do I check if my Ford Bronco Sport has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
