Is one open on your Escape?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Escape have?

101 recall campaigns across model years 2001–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 226.

Which Ford Escape year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 24 campaigns. 2022: 21, 2021: 20, 2013: 18.

What are most Ford Escape recalls about?

Electrical: 15 of the 101 campaigns. Then engine (12) and fuel system, gasoline (12).

What is the newest Ford Escape recall?

Visibility, filed Jun 2026 (NHTSA 26V374000): The power-operated windows may exert excessive force when encountering an object, before they automatically reverse.

What is the biggest Ford Escape recall?

NHTSA 22V413000 (transmission & drivetrain, Jun 2022) covers 2,925,968 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

How do I check if my Ford Escape has an open recall?
