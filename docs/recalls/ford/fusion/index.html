Is one open on your Fusion?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Fusion have?

49 recall campaigns across model years 2006–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 128.

Which Ford Fusion year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 19 campaigns. 2013: 19, 2016: 13, 2015: 13.

What are most Ford Fusion recalls about?

Airbags: 9 of the 49 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (8) and latches/locks/linkages (5).

What is the newest Ford Fusion recall?

Transmission & drivetrain, filed Jun 2026 (NHTSA 26V376000): The clutch may fracture, resulting in damage to the transmission assembly and possibly a transmission fluid leak.

What is the biggest Ford Fusion recall?

NHTSA 22V413000 (transmission & drivetrain, Jun 2022) covers 2,925,968 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

How do I check if my Ford Fusion has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
