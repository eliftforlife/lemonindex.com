Is one open on your Expedition?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Expedition have?

79 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 154.

Which Ford Expedition year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 18 campaigns. 2018: 14, 2022: 12, 2021: 12.

What are most Ford Expedition recalls about?

Lights: 9 of the 79 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (8) and visibility (7).

What is the newest Ford Expedition recall?

Lights, filed Sep 2026 (NHTSA 26V606000): The headlight assemblies may contain a contaminated LED chip, which can result in a loss of daytime running lights, parking lights, high beams or low beams.

What is the biggest Ford Expedition recall?

NHTSA 26V104000 (electrical, Feb 2026) covers 4,381,878 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. When towing a trailer, the integrated trailer module (ITRM) may lose communication with the vehicle, possibly causing a loss of brake and turn signal lights, or a loss of brake function.

How do I check if my Ford Expedition has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
