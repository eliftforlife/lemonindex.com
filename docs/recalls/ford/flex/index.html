Is one open on your Flex?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Flex have?

14 recall campaigns across model years 2009–2019, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 34.

Which Ford Flex year has the most recalls?

The 2013, with 6 campaigns. 2019: 4, 2017: 4, 2015: 4.

What are most Ford Flex recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 3 of the 14 campaigns. Then back over prevention (2) and airbags (1).

What is the newest Ford Flex recall?

Body structure, filed Oct 2025 (NHTSA 25V732000): The driver and front passenger B-Pillar door trim may detach while driving.

What is the biggest Ford Flex recall?

NHTSA 25V695000 (back over prevention, Oct 2025) covers 1,448,655 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The rearview camera may display a distorted, intermittent, or blank image when the vehicle is in reverse.

How do I check if my Ford Flex has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
