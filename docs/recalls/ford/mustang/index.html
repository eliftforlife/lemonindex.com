Is one open on your Mustang?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Ford Mustang have?

73 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 186.

Which Ford Mustang year has the most recalls?

The 2024, with 12 campaigns. 2015: 11, 2006: 10, 2005: 10.

What are most Ford Mustang recalls about?

Airbags: 11 of the 73 campaigns. Then electrical (8) and lights (8).

What is the newest Ford Mustang recall?

Electrical, filed Aug 2026 (NHTSA 26V547000): The engine compartment wiring harness ground connections may fracture and result in a loss of drive power, or a loss of function in the windshield washing system, headlights, air conditioning, or engine cooling fan.

What is the biggest Ford Mustang recall?

NHTSA 20V331000 (latches/locks/linkages, Jun 2020) covers 2,147,323 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles were previously recalled and repaired under recalls 15V-246, 16V-643 or 17V-210, however the repair may not have been completed properly.

How do I check if my Ford Mustang has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Ford dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
