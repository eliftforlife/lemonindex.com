Is one open on your Mountaineer?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mercury Mountaineer have?

15 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2010, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 25.

Which Mercury Mountaineer year has the most recalls?

The 2000, with 8 campaigns. 2002: 4, 2001: 4, 2008: 2.

What are most Mercury Mountaineer recalls about?

Speed control: 3 of the 15 campaigns. Then visibility (3) and seats (2).

What is the newest Mercury Mountaineer recall?

Seats, filed Apr 2010 (NHTSA 10V161000): FORD IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2010 FUSION, EXPLORER, EXPLORER SPORT TRAC, AND MERCURY MILAN AND MOUNTAINEER VEHICLES MANUFACTURED BETWEEN DECEMBER 15, 2009 THROUGH FEBRUARY 3, 2010 EQUIPPED WITH FRONT SEAT MANUAL…

What is the biggest Mercury Mountaineer recall?

NHTSA 10V161000 (seats, Apr 2010) covers 33,256 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. FORD IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2010 FUSION, EXPLORER, EXPLORER SPORT TRAC, AND MERCURY MILAN AND MOUNTAINEER VEHICLES MANUFACTURED BETWEEN DECEMBER 15, 2009 THROUGH FEBRUARY 3, 2010 EQUIPPED WITH FRONT SEAT MANUAL…

How do I check if my Mercury Mountaineer has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mercury dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
