Is one open on your Hyundai?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Hyundai have?

225 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 12 Hyundai models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Hyundai has the most recalls?

The Hyundai Sonata, with 61 campaigns. Then the Santa Fe (58), the Elantra (50), the Tucson (35). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Hyundai recall?

NHTSA 13V113000 (lights, Apr 2013): 1,712,336 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Accent, Azera, Elantra, Genesis, Santa Fe, Sonata, Tiburon, Tucson, Veracruz among the models we track. Originally, in April 2013, Hyundai recalled 1,059,824 model year 2007-2009 Accent and Tucson, model year 2007-2010 Elantra, model year 2007-2011 Santa Fe, model year 2008-2009 Veracruz, model year 2010-2011 Genesis…

What is the newest Hyundai recall?

Filed Nov 2026 for the Veloster (NHTSA 26V728000): A software error in the air bag control unit (ACU) may improperly deactivate the air bag system and seat belt pretensioners.

How do I check a Hyundai for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Hyundai dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
