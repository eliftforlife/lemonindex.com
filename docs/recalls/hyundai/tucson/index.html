Is one open on your Tucson?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Tucson have?

35 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 66.

Which Hyundai Tucson year has the most recalls?

The 2005, with 8 campaigns. 2025: 7, 2016: 5, 2011: 5.

What are most Hyundai Tucson recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 35 campaigns. Then electrical (5) and lights (5).

What is the newest Hyundai Tucson recall?

Electrical, filed Jun 2026 (NHTSA 26V400000): Due to a software error, the instrument panel display may fail.

What is the biggest Hyundai Tucson recall?

NHTSA 13V113000 (lights, Apr 2013) covers 1,712,336 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Originally, in April 2013, Hyundai recalled 1,059,824 model year 2007-2009 Accent and Tucson, model year 2007-2010 Elantra, model year 2007-2011 Santa Fe, model year 2008-2009 Veracruz, model year 2010-2011 Genesis Coupe…

How do I check if my Hyundai Tucson has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Hyundai dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
