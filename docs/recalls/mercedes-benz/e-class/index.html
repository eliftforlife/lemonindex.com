Is one open on your E-Class?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz E-Class have?

63 recall campaigns across model years 2001–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 128.

Which Mercedes-Benz E-Class year has the most recalls?

The 2017, with 20 campaigns. 2018: 10, 2019: 9, 2014: 8.

What are most Mercedes-Benz E-Class recalls about?

Airbags: 12 of the 63 campaigns. Then electrical (11) and fuel system, gasoline (6).

What is the newest Mercedes-Benz E-Class recall?

Electrical, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V281000): The infotainment control unit may reset while the vehicle is in motion and cause the instrument panel display to go blank.

What is the biggest Mercedes-Benz E-Class recall?

NHTSA 21V058000 (electrical, Feb 2021) covers 1,292,258 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Please refer to the Part 573 report for specific vehicle model details.

How do I check if my Mercedes-Benz E-Class has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mercedes-Benz dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
