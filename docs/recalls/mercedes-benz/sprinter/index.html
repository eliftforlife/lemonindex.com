Is one open on your Sprinter?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter have?

94 recall campaigns across model years 2010–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 172.

Which Mercedes-Benz Sprinter year has the most recalls?

The 2019, with 42 campaigns. 2020: 22, 2021: 18, 2016: 16.

What are most Mercedes-Benz Sprinter recalls about?

Body structure: 15 of the 94 campaigns. Then electrical (12) and equipment (12).

What is the newest Mercedes-Benz Sprinter recall?

Body structure, filed Oct 2025 (NHTSA 25V725000): The right longitudinal frame member may not be reinforced properly, decreasing the structural integrity of the third-row seats.

What is the biggest Mercedes-Benz Sprinter recall?

NHTSA 16V077000 (airbags, Feb 2016) covers 136,361 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Upon deployment of the driver's frontal air bag, excessive internal pressure may cause the inflator to rupture.

How do I check if my Mercedes-Benz Sprinter has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mercedes-Benz dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
