Is one open on your CLA?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz CLA have?

24 recall campaigns across model years 2013–2021, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 61.

Which Mercedes-Benz CLA year has the most recalls?

The 2020, with 11 campaigns. 2017: 10, 2018: 9, 2016: 8.

What are most Mercedes-Benz CLA recalls about?

Airbags: 5 of the 24 campaigns. Then electrical (5) and brakes (3).

What is the newest Mercedes-Benz CLA recall?

Latches/locks/linkages, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V481000): Please refer to MBUSA's recall report for specific model information.

What is the biggest Mercedes-Benz CLA recall?

NHTSA 21V058000 (electrical, Feb 2021) covers 1,292,258 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Please refer to the Part 573 report for specific vehicle model details.

How do I check if my Mercedes-Benz CLA has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Mercedes-Benz dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
