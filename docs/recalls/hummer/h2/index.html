Is one open on your H2?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Hummer H2 have?

12 recall campaigns across model years 2003–2009, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 23.

Which Hummer H2 year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 5 campaigns. 2003: 5, 2004: 4, 2008: 3.

What are most Hummer H2 recalls about?

Brakes: 2 of the 12 campaigns. Then electrical (2) and steering (2).
