Is one open on your Avalon?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Avalon have?

32 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2022, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 81.

Which Toyota Avalon year has the most recalls?

The 2010, with 7 campaigns. 2009: 7, 2006: 7, 2005: 7.

What are most Toyota Avalon recalls about?

Airbags: 9 of the 32 campaigns. Then electrical (3) and equipment (3).

What is the newest Toyota Avalon recall?

Airbags, filed Dec 2023 (NHTSA 23V865000): A short circuit may develop in the Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor, preventing the front passenger air bag from deploying.

What is the biggest Toyota Avalon recall?

NHTSA 20V024000 (airbags, Jan 2020) covers 2,891,976 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. During certain crashes, the air bag electronic control unit (ECU) may malfunction, possibly disabling the deployment of the air bags and/or seat belt pretensioners.

How do I check if my Toyota Avalon has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
