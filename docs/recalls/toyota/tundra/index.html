Is one open on your Tundra?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Tundra have?

84 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 240.

Which Toyota Tundra year has the most recalls?

The 2010, with 16 campaigns. 2005: 15, 2023: 14, 2022: 14.

What are most Toyota Tundra recalls about?

Equipment: 17 of the 84 campaigns. Then airbags (11) and lights (8).

What is the newest Toyota Tundra recall?

Airbags, filed Nov 2026 (NHTSA 26V748000): The inflators may rupture due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

What is the biggest Toyota Tundra recall?

NHTSA 12V491000 (visibility, Oct 2012) covers 2,519,424 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The power window master switch assemblies in some of these vehicles were built using a less precise process for lubricating the internal components of the switch assemblies.

How do I check if my Toyota Tundra has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
