Is one open on your RAV4?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota RAV4 have?

53 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 143.

Which Toyota RAV4 year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 15 campaigns. 2010: 14, 2008: 14, 2007: 14.

What are most Toyota RAV4 recalls about?

Airbags: 11 of the 53 campaigns. Then equipment (8) and electrical (4).

What is the newest Toyota RAV4 recall?

Seats, filed Apr 2026 (NHTSA 26V256000): The front seat brackets for the seat rails may be welded improperly.

What is the biggest Toyota RAV4 recall?

NHTSA 12V491000 (visibility, Oct 2012) covers 2,519,424 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The power window master switch assemblies in some of these vehicles were built using a less precise process for lubricating the internal components of the switch assemblies.

How do I check if my Toyota RAV4 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
