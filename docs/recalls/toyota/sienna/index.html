Is one open on your Sienna?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Sienna have?

49 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 147.

Which Toyota Sienna year has the most recalls?

The 2011, with 14 campaigns. 2005: 12, 2006: 11, 2017: 10.

What are most Toyota Sienna recalls about?

Equipment: 10 of the 49 campaigns. Then airbags (8) and body structure (7).

What is the newest Toyota Sienna recall?

Equipment adaptive/mobility, filed Dec 2025 (NHTSA 25V876000): Please refer to Rollx's recall report for specific model year information.

What is the biggest Toyota Sienna recall?

NHTSA 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline, Jan 2020) covers 1,830,752 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) recalled certain 2018-2019 4Runner, Highlander, Camry, Land Cruiser, Sequoia, Sienna, Tacoma and Tundra, and Lexus RC 300, RC 350, GS 350, GX 460, IS 300, LC 500, LS 500,…

How do I check if my Toyota Sienna has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
