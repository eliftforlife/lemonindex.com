Is one open on your Yaris?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Yaris have?

21 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2018, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 56.

Which Toyota Yaris year has the most recalls?

The 2009, with 10 campaigns. 2008: 10, 2007: 10, 2010: 9.

What are most Toyota Yaris recalls about?

Airbags: 7 of the 21 campaigns. Then equipment (4) and suspension (2).

What is the newest Toyota Yaris recall?

Airbags, filed Oct 2019 (NHTSA 19V741000): These vehicles had their driver or passenger frontal air bag inflators previously replaced under a prior recall using inflators of the same design.

What is the biggest Toyota Yaris recall?

NHTSA 12V491000 (visibility, Oct 2012) covers 2,519,424 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The power window master switch assemblies in some of these vehicles were built using a less precise process for lubricating the internal components of the switch assemblies.

How do I check if my Toyota Yaris has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
