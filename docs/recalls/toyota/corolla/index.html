Is one open on your Corolla?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Corolla have?

56 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 151.

Which Toyota Corolla year has the most recalls?

The 2010, with 17 campaigns. 2004: 14, 2003: 14, 2009: 13.

What are most Toyota Corolla recalls about?

Airbags: 18 of the 56 campaigns. Then lights (9) and equipment (7).

What is the newest Toyota Corolla recall?

Airbags, filed Nov 2026 (NHTSA 26V748000): The inflators may rupture due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

What is the biggest Toyota Corolla recall?

NHTSA 20V024000 (airbags, Jan 2020) covers 2,891,976 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. During certain crashes, the air bag electronic control unit (ECU) may malfunction, possibly disabling the deployment of the air bags and/or seat belt pretensioners.

How do I check if my Toyota Corolla has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
