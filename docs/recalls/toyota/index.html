Is one open on your Toyota?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Toyota have?

326 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 17 Toyota models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Toyota has the most recalls?

The Toyota Tundra, with 82 campaigns. Then the Tacoma (59), the Camry (57), the Highlander (56). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Toyota recall?

NHTSA 20V024000 (airbags, Jan 2020): 2,891,976 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Avalon, Corolla, Matrix among the models we track. During certain crashes, the air bag electronic control unit (ECU) may malfunction, possibly disabling the deployment of the air bags and/or seat belt pretensioners.

What is the newest Toyota recall?

Filed Sep 2026 for the Tundra (NHTSA 26V571000): The load carrying capacity modification label may display inaccurate added weight values.

How do I check a Toyota for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Toyota dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
