Is one open on your 500?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Fiat 500 have?

12 recall campaigns across model years 2012–2024, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 32.

Which Fiat 500 year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 7 campaigns. 2015: 6, 2016: 5, 2013: 4.

What are most Fiat 500 recalls about?

Transmission & drivetrain: 4 of the 12 campaigns. Then electrical (3) and airbags (1).

What is the newest Fiat 500 recall?

Lights, filed Oct 2025 (NHTSA 25V715000): The headlight horizontal aim adjustment is not covered as intended, which can result in improperly adjusted headlights.

What is the biggest Fiat 500 recall?

NHTSA 24V510000 (airbags, Jul 2024) covers 337,128 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. A seat belt buckle switch sensor may be improperly connected, preventing the front seat air bag from deploying as intended.

How do I check if my Fiat 500 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Fiat dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
