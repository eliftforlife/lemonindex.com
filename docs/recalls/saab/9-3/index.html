Is one open on your 9-3?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Saab 9-3 have?

15 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2011, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 45.

Which Saab 9-3 year has the most recalls?

The 2004, with 6 campaigns. 2003: 5, 2011: 4, 2010: 4.

What are most Saab 9-3 recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 4 of the 15 campaigns. Then seat belts (3) and airbags (2).

What is the newest Saab 9-3 recall?

Airbags, filed Feb 2016 (NHTSA 16V063000): Upon deployment of the driver's frontal air bag, excessive internal pressure may cause the inflator to rupture.

What is the biggest Saab 9-3 recall?

NHTSA 16V063000 (airbags, Feb 2016) covers 113,716 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Upon deployment of the driver's frontal air bag, excessive internal pressure may cause the inflator to rupture.

How do I check if my Saab 9-3 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Saab dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
