Is one open on your Cayenne?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Porsche Cayenne have?

32 recall campaigns across model years 2003–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 77.

Which Porsche Cayenne year has the most recalls?

The 2004, with 9 campaigns. 2003: 9, 2019: 6, 2021: 5.

What are most Porsche Cayenne recalls about?

Suspension: 5 of the 32 campaigns. Then electrical (4) and fuel system, gasoline (4).

What is the newest Porsche Cayenne recall?

Back over prevention, filed Dec 2025 (NHTSA 25V896000): The rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse.

What is the biggest Porsche Cayenne recall?

NHTSA 22V656000 (lights, Aug 2022) covers 222,858 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Please see the recall report for a complete list of all affected models and model years.

How do I check if my Porsche Cayenne has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Porsche dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from
