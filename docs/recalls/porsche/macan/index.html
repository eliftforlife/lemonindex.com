Is one open on your Macan?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Porsche Macan have?

12 recall campaigns across model years 2015–2023, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 28.

Which Porsche Macan year has the most recalls?

The 2015, with 7 campaigns. 2017: 6, 2016: 6, 2018: 5.

What are most Porsche Macan recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 3 of the 12 campaigns. Then airbags (2) and suspension (2).

What is the newest Porsche Macan recall?

Seat belts, filed Jun 2023 (NHTSA 23V443000): The screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame on the outboard rear seats may not have been tightened properly.

What is the biggest Porsche Macan recall?

NHTSA 22V656000 (lights, Aug 2022) covers 222,858 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Please see the recall report for a complete list of all affected models and model years.

How do I check if my Porsche Macan has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Porsche dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
