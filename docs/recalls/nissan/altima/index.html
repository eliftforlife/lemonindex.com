Is one open on your Altima?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Altima have?

57 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 113.

Which Nissan Altima year has the most recalls?

The 2013, with 12 campaigns. 2002: 12, 2015: 10, 2003: 10.

What are most Nissan Altima recalls about?

Airbags: 10 of the 57 campaigns. Then lights (8) and engine (7).

What is the newest Nissan Altima recall?

Airbags, filed Aug 2026 (NHTSA 26V526000): The front passenger air bag and front passenger knee air bag may have been installed with incorrect mounting fasteners, which can cause the air bags not to deploy as intended.

What is the biggest Nissan Altima recall?

NHTSA 16V244000 (airbags, Apr 2016) covers 3,296,947 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In these vehicles, the front seat passenger Occupant Classification System (OCS) may incorrectly classify an adult passenger as a child or classify the seat as empty despite it being occupied.

How do I check if my Nissan Altima has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
