Is one open on your Pathfinder?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Pathfinder have?

37 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 74.

Which Nissan Pathfinder year has the most recalls?

The 2014, with 11 campaigns. 2013: 11, 2022: 5, 2015: 5.

What are most Nissan Pathfinder recalls about?

Airbags: 7 of the 37 campaigns. Then brakes (6) and seats (4).

What is the newest Nissan Pathfinder recall?

Brakes, filed Mar 2025 (NHTSA 25V173000): One or both front brake caliper assemblies may have reduced strength and could break.

What is the biggest Nissan Pathfinder recall?

NHTSA 16V244000 (airbags, Apr 2016) covers 3,296,947 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. In these vehicles, the front seat passenger Occupant Classification System (OCS) may incorrectly classify an adult passenger as a child or classify the seat as empty despite it being occupied.

How do I check if my Nissan Pathfinder has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
