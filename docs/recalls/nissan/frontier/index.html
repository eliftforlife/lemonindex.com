Is one open on your Frontier?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Frontier have?

33 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 61.

Which Nissan Frontier year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 7 campaigns. 2022: 6, 2010: 6, 2008: 6.

What are most Nissan Frontier recalls about?

Back over prevention: 4 of the 33 campaigns. Then equipment (4) and suspension (4).

What is the newest Nissan Frontier recall?

Latches/locks/linkages, filed Jan 2026 (NHTSA 26V023000): The door strikers may have been improperly welded and break, allowing the door to open while moving.

What is the biggest Nissan Frontier recall?

NHTSA 19V654000 (back over prevention, Sep 2019) covers 1,228,830 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Additionally included are 2019 Nissan GT-R and Taxi and Infiniti QX50, QX60, Q70, Q70L vehicles.

How do I check if my Nissan Frontier has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
