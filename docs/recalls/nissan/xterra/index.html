Is one open on your Xterra?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Xterra have?

24 recall campaigns across model years 2002–2015, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 48.

Which Nissan Xterra year has the most recalls?

The 2008, with 8 campaigns. 2010: 6, 2005: 6, 2002: 6.

What are most Nissan Xterra recalls about?

Equipment: 4 of the 24 campaigns. Then suspension (4) and airbags (3).

What is the newest Nissan Xterra recall?

Airbags, filed Feb 2023 (NHTSA 23V067000): The resin emblem on the driver's air bag cover may develop cracks and detach during air bag deployment.

What is the biggest Nissan Xterra recall?

NHTSA 10V517000 (engine, Oct 2010) covers 747,480 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. NISSAN IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2004-2006 ARMADA, TITAN, INFINITI QX56 AND MODEL YEAR 2005-2006 FRONTIER, PATHFINDER AND XTERRA VEHICLES.

How do I check if my Nissan Xterra has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
