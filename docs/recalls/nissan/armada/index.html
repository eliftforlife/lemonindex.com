Is one open on your Armada?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Armada have?

21 recall campaigns across model years 2004–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 51.

Which Nissan Armada year has the most recalls?

The 2008, with 7 campaigns. 2010: 5, 2006: 5, 2005: 5.

What are most Nissan Armada recalls about?

Equipment: 4 of the 21 campaigns. Then airbags (3) and back over prevention (2).

What is the newest Nissan Armada recall?

Equipment, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V455000): The Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) listed on the certification label is incorrect, which can allow the vehicle to be overloaded.

What is the biggest Nissan Armada recall?

NHTSA 19V654000 (back over prevention, Sep 2019) covers 1,228,830 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Additionally included are 2019 Nissan GT-R and Taxi and Infiniti QX50, QX60, Q70, Q70L vehicles.

How do I check if my Nissan Armada has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
