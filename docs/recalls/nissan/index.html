Is one open on your Nissan?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Nissan have?

268 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 16 Nissan models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Nissan has the most recalls?

The Nissan Altima, with 57 campaigns. Then the Sentra (50), the Rogue (44), the Pathfinder (37). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Nissan recall?

NHTSA 16V244000 (airbags, Apr 2016): 3,296,947 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Altima, Maxima, Murano, Pathfinder, Rogue, Sentra among the models we track. In these vehicles, the front seat passenger Occupant Classification System (OCS) may incorrectly classify an adult passenger as a child or classify the seat as empty despite it being occupied.

What is the newest Nissan recall?

Filed Aug 2026 for the Altima (NHTSA 26V526000): The front passenger air bag and front passenger knee air bag may have been installed with incorrect mounting fasteners, which can cause the air bags not to deploy as intended.

How do I check a Nissan for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
