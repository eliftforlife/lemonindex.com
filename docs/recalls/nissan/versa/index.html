Is one open on your Versa?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Versa have?

30 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 71.

Which Nissan Versa year has the most recalls?

The 2012, with 11 campaigns. 2007: 9, 2010: 8, 2009: 8.

What are most Nissan Versa recalls about?

Airbags: 13 of the 30 campaigns. Then electrical (2) and steering (2).

What is the newest Nissan Versa recall?

Airbags, filed Feb 2024 (NHTSA 24V154000): Due to a manufacturing issue, the driver's air bag inflator may absorb moisture, and cause the air bag to deploy improperly.

What is the biggest Nissan Versa recall?

NHTSA 19V654000 (back over prevention, Sep 2019) covers 1,228,830 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. Additionally included are 2019 Nissan GT-R and Taxi and Infiniti QX50, QX60, Q70, Q70L vehicles.

How do I check if my Nissan Versa has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Nissan dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
