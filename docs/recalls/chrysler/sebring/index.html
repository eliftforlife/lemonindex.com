Is one open on your Sebring?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chrysler Sebring have?

36 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2011, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 68.

Which Chrysler Sebring year has the most recalls?

The 2001, with 13 campaigns. 2004: 8, 2000: 8, 2007: 7.

What are most Chrysler Sebring recalls about?

Fuel system, gasoline: 5 of the 36 campaigns. Then airbags (4) and brakes (3).

What is the newest Chrysler Sebring recall?

Seat belts, filed Sep 2016 (NHTSA 16V668000): The Occupant Restraint Control (OCR) module may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags, seat belt pretensioners, and side air bags from deploying in the event of a crash.

What is the biggest Chrysler Sebring recall?

NHTSA 16V668000 (seat belts, Sep 2016) covers 1,435,625 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. The Occupant Restraint Control (OCR) module may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags, seat belt pretensioners, and side air bags from deploying in the event of a crash.

How do I check if my Chrysler Sebring has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chrysler dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.

Where this data comes from

Source: NHTSA recall campaigns (api.nhtsa.gov), matched to the model names owners use in NHTSA complaints. Vehicle counts and the "park it" warnings come from NHTSA's recall file, which starts in 2010, so older recalls have no count. A campaign's count covers every make and model in it.
