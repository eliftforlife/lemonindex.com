Is one open on your 300?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chrysler 300 have?

38 recall campaigns across model years 2005–2023, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 84.

Which Chrysler 300 year has the most recalls?

The 2012, with 9 campaigns. 2014: 8, 2011: 7, 2010: 7.

What are most Chrysler 300 recalls about?

Electrical: 9 of the 38 campaigns. Then transmission & drivetrain (8) and airbags (7).

What is the newest Chrysler 300 recall?

Airbags, filed Mar 2024 (NHTSA 24V198000): The right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.

What is the biggest Chrysler 300 recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Chrysler 300 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chrysler dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
