Is one open on your 200?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Chrysler 200 have?

16 recall campaigns across model years 2011–2018, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 24.

Which Chrysler 200 year has the most recalls?

The 2015, with 8 campaigns. 2013: 5, 2012: 4, 2011: 3.

What are most Chrysler 200 recalls about?

Electrical: 4 of the 16 campaigns. Then engine (2) and steering (2).

What is the newest Chrysler 200 recall?

Electrical, filed May 2018 (NHTSA 18V332000): These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

What is the biggest Chrysler 200 recall?

NHTSA 18V332000 (electrical, May 2018) covers 4,815,661 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

How do I check if my Chrysler 200 has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Chrysler dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
