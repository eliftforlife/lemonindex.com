Is one open on your Honda?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does Honda have?

269 NHTSA recall campaigns on the 12 Honda models we track, model years 2000–2025. A campaign that covers several models or years counts once.

Which Honda has the most recalls?

The Honda Accord, with 83 campaigns. Then the Odyssey (77), the Civic (74), the CR-V (72). Models with more model years on sale have had more time to collect recalls.

What is the biggest Honda recall?

NHTSA 15V320000 (airbags, May 2015): 6,281,043 vehicles in the whole campaign, on the Accord, CR-V, Civic, Element, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline among the models we track. What is the newest Honda recall?

Filed Oct 2026 for the Pilot (NHTSA 26V721000): The software for the electric power steering (EPS) assist may cause the EPS system to enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

How do I check a Honda for recalls by VIN?

Type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Honda dealer: that shows whether a recall is still open on that car. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
