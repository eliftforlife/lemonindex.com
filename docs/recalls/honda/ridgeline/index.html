Is one open on your Ridgeline?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda Ridgeline have?

42 recall campaigns across model years 2006–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 147.

Which Honda Ridgeline year has the most recalls?

The 2006, with 14 campaigns. 2019: 12, 2012: 12, 2011: 11.

What are most Honda Ridgeline recalls about?

Airbags: 16 of the 42 campaigns. Then body structure (5) and fuel system, gasoline (4).
