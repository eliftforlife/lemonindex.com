Is one open on your CR-V?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda CR-V have?

72 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 206.

Which Honda CR-V year has the most recalls?

The 2002, with 16 campaigns. 2010: 15, 2005: 14, 2003: 14.

What are most Honda CR-V recalls about?

Airbags: 28 of the 72 campaigns. Then electrical (7) and fuel system, gasoline (7).

What is the newest Honda CR-V recall?

Tires, filed Oct 2026 (NHTSA 26V663000): When using the tire repair kit, pressure may build within the sealant bottle, causing the bottle cap to detach.

What is the biggest Honda CR-V recall?

NHTSA 15V320000 (airbags, May 2015) covers 6,281,043 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. How do I check if my Honda CR-V has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Honda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
