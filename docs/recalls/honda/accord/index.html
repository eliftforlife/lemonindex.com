Is one open on your Accord?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda Accord have?

83 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 267.

Which Honda Accord year has the most recalls?

The 2005, with 24 campaigns. 2003: 24, 2004: 22, 2000: 21.

What are most Honda Accord recalls about?

Airbags: 27 of the 83 campaigns. Then lights (15) and electrical (7).

Tires, filed Oct 2026 (NHTSA 26V663000): When using the tire repair kit, pressure may build within the sealant bottle, causing the bottle cap to detach.

How do I check if my Honda Accord has an open recall?
