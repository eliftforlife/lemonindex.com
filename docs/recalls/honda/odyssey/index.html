Is one open on your Odyssey?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda Odyssey have?

77 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 186.

Which Honda Odyssey year has the most recalls?

The 2019, with 20 campaigns. 2018: 16, 2003: 15, 2020: 14.

What are most Honda Odyssey recalls about?

Airbags: 20 of the 77 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (9) and electrical (6).

What is the newest Honda Odyssey recall?

Back over prevention, filed Jul 2026 (NHTSA 26V423000): Water may enter into the rearview camera, which can cause the rearview camera image to fail to display when the vehicle is in reverse.

What is the biggest Honda Odyssey recall?

NHTSA 15V320000 (airbags, May 2015) covers 6,281,043 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. How do I check if my Honda Odyssey has an open recall?
