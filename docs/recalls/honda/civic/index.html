Is one open on your Civic?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda Civic have?

74 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 228.

Which Honda Civic year has the most recalls?

The 2001, with 27 campaigns. 2003: 24, 2002: 24, 2000: 18.

What are most Honda Civic recalls about?

Airbags: 21 of the 74 campaigns. Then lights (17) and fuel system, gasoline (8).

What is the newest Honda Civic recall?

Airbags, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V332000): The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

What is the biggest Honda Civic recall?

NHTSA 15V320000 (airbags, May 2015) covers 6,281,043 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. How do I check if my Honda Civic has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Honda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
