Is one open on your Fit?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Honda Fit have?

34 recall campaigns across model years 2007–2020, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 93.

Which Honda Fit year has the most recalls?

The 2010, with 12 campaigns. 2012: 11, 2011: 11, 2009: 11.

What are most Honda Fit recalls about?

Airbags: 18 of the 34 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (3) and transmission & drivetrain (3).

What is the newest Honda Fit recall?

Airbags, filed May 2026 (NHTSA 26V332000): The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

What is the biggest Honda Fit recall?

NHTSA 16V346000 (airbags, May 2016) covers 2,719,386 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. An inflator rupture may result in metal fragments striking the vehicle occupants resulting in serious injury or death.

How do I check if my Honda Fit has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Honda dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
