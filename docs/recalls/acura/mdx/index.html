Is one open on your MDX?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Acura MDX have?

46 recall campaigns across model years 2001–2025, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 131.

Which Acura MDX year has the most recalls?

The 2005, with 13 campaigns. 2003: 13, 2006: 11, 2018: 10.

What are most Acura MDX recalls about?

Airbags: 16 of the 46 campaigns. Then fuel system, gasoline (5) and brakes (4).

What is the newest Acura MDX recall?

Steering, filed Oct 2026 (NHTSA 26V721000): The software for the electric power steering (EPS) assist may cause the EPS system to enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.
