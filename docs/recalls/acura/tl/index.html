Is one open on your TL?

A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car. NHTSA's public recall data works by make, model and year, so these lists cover every car of that model year, not one VIN. Whether a recall was already fixed on a particular car is in the automaker's records: type the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any dealer of that brand. The repair is free.

Questions

How many recalls does the Acura TL have?

23 recall campaigns across model years 2000–2014, from NHTSA. Many campaigns cover several model years; counted once for every model year they cover (the way each year's page counts them), that's 63.

Which Acura TL year has the most recalls?

The 2005, with 7 campaigns. 2002: 7, 2006: 6, 2004: 6.

What are most Acura TL recalls about?

Airbags: 9 of the 23 campaigns. Then electrical (2) and seat belts (2).

What is the newest Acura TL recall?

Airbags, filed Jan 2020 (NHTSA 20V027000): These vehicles may have received a replacement driver frontal air bag module as part of a vehicle repair.

What is the biggest Acura TL recall?

NHTSA 15V320000 (airbags, May 2015) covers 6,281,043 vehicles in all, every model in that campaign. How do I check if my Acura TL has an open recall?

Use its VIN: type it at nhtsa.gov/recalls or ask any Acura dealer. Our VIN check decodes it and lists every recall for that model year. Recall repairs are free.
