Questions

What is the Peugeot 107 MOT pass rate?

62.1% of Peugeot 107 cars first registered 2005–2014 passed their MOT first time in 2024, from 113,678 tests. Cars first registered in the same years passed 61.8%, so the Peugeot 107 is 0.3 points better than cars its age.

Which Peugeot 107 years have the best MOT pass rate?

Against cars the same age: first registered in 2014, 74.0% (+3.4 points); first registered in 2013, 70.5% (+3.1 points); first registered in 2012, 67.3% (+2.5 points).

Which Peugeot 107 years should I be careful with?

The years furthest below cars the same age: first registered in 2005, 50.0% (−6.8 points); first registered in 2006, 51.7% (−4.7 points); first registered in 2008, 53.4% (−3.7 points).

Why do Peugeot 107 cars fail their MOT?

The top areas: lights and electrics (19.1% of tests), brakes (12.6% of tests), suspension (9.6% of tests). The single most common item is sidelight not working (5.7% of tests).

How many miles has a Peugeot 107 usually done at its MOT?

72,084 miles on average at the 2024 test, across first registration years 2005–2014, against 95,698 for cars the same age.

Does the MOT pass rate tell me if a Peugeot 107 is reliable?

Not on its own. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. Wear items like tyres, bulbs and wipers depend on the owner as much as the car. Comparing the Peugeot 107 with cars first registered in the same years takes age out of it, but always check the MOT history and service record of the exact car.

How to read these numbers

How to read this. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. It counts what a tester found that day, and some of it (tyres, bulbs, wipers) is down to how a car was looked after, not how it was built. So we only compare a model with cars first registered in the same year, and we show the test count and the reasons cars failed. A low MOT pass rate is not proof that a model is unreliable.

Method. Every class 4 (cars and light vans) normal MOT test carried out in Great Britain in 2024 with a result of pass, fail or PRS (fixed at the test centre within an hour), one row per test. First-time pass rate = tests passed outright, out of all of them. PRS counts as a fail because the car arrived failing (this is DVSA's own “initial failure rate”, turned around). Trim and engine names are merged into one model (“FIESTA ZETEC” and “FIESTA” are both the Fiesta). Year = year of first registration in the UK, not the year the car was built. A year needs 1,000+ tests to be shown. Vans, pickups and van-based people carriers are compared with other vans. Northern Ireland isn't in this data. The Peugeot 107 here merges these DVSA model names: 107, 107 URBAN MOVE, 107 URBAN, 107 VERVE, 107 VERVE SPORT, 107 URBAN LITE.
