Questions

Questions

What is the Peugeot Partner MOT pass rate?

62.3% of Peugeot Partner vans first registered 2004–2021 passed their MOT first time in 2024, from 151,337 tests. Vans first registered in the same years passed 66.4%, so the Peugeot Partner is 4.0 points worse than vans its age.

Which Peugeot Partner years have the best MOT pass rate?

Against cars the same age: first registered in 2012, 60.4% (+2.6 points).

Which Peugeot Partner years should I be careful with?

The years furthest below cars the same age: first registered in 2018, 65.5% (−8.1 points); first registered in 2017, 62.3% (−7.9 points); first registered in 2016, 60.9% (−6.5 points).

Why do Peugeot Partner vans fail their MOT?

The top areas: lights and electrics (22.8% of tests), suspension (10.0% of tests), brakes (7.5% of tests). The single most common item is sidelight not working (7.4% of tests).

How many miles has a Peugeot Partner usually done at its MOT?

93,248 miles on average at the 2024 test, across first registration years 2004–2021, against 94,208 for vans the same age.

Does the MOT pass rate tell me if a Peugeot Partner is reliable?

Not on its own. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. Wear items like tyres, bulbs and wipers depend on the owner as much as the car. Comparing the Peugeot Partner with vans first registered in the same years takes age out of it, but always check the MOT history and service record of the exact car.

How to read these numbers

How to read this. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. It counts what a tester found that day, and some of it (tyres, bulbs, wipers) is down to how a car was looked after, not how it was built. So we only compare a model with cars first registered in the same year, and we show the test count and the reasons cars failed. A low MOT pass rate is not proof that a model is unreliable.

Method. Every class 4 (cars and light vans) normal MOT test carried out in Great Britain in 2024 with a result of pass, fail or PRS (fixed at the test centre within an hour), one row per test. First-time pass rate = tests passed outright, out of all of them. PRS counts as a fail because the car arrived failing (this is DVSA's own “initial failure rate”, turned around). Trim and engine names are merged into one model (“FIESTA ZETEC” and “FIESTA” are both the Fiesta). Year = year of first registration in the UK, not the year the car was built. A year needs 1,000+ tests to be shown. Vans, pickups and van-based people carriers are compared with other vans. Northern Ireland isn't in this data. The Peugeot Partner here merges these DVSA model names: PARTNER, PARTNER PROFESSIONAL PREM BHDI, PARTNER PROFESSIONAL L1 P-TECH, PARTNER ASPHALT PREMIUM BHDI A, PARTNER GRIP PREMIUM BLUEHDI, PARTNER PRO PREMIUM BHDI AUTO….
