Questions

Which used cars have the best MOT pass rate in the UK?

Against cars the same age, among models with 50,000+ tests in 2024: the Mazda MX-5 (+10.4 points), the Audi TT (+9.6 points), the Land Rover Range Rover (+8.4 points), the BMW 5 Series (+8.0 points), the Audi A6 (+7.7 points).

Which cars fail their MOT most?

Against cars the same age, among models with 50,000+ tests: the Citroen DS3 (−15.5 points), the Renault Clio (−12.0 points), the Renault Megane (−11.6 points), the Citroen C4 (−10.9 points), the Dacia Sandero (−10.4 points). An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. A low pass rate is not proof that a model is unreliable.

Is the Toyota Avensis good on MOTs?

Yes, against cars its age. 66.7% of Avensis cars passed first time in 58,469 tests, 3.2 points better than cars first registered in the same years, even though they averaged 121,258 miles at the test (same-age cars: 92,297).

Why compare cars with cars the same age?

Because age matters more than anything. Cars first registered in 2021 passed 88.8% of their 2024 MOTs first time; cars first registered in 2004 passed 56.7%. A model sold mostly as new cars would look good next to one whose cars are mostly old, so each model is compared with every car first registered in the same years.

What do cars fail their MOT on most?

Across cars first registered 2004–2021: lights and electrics (10.2% of tests), suspension (8.4% of tests), tyres (6.6% of tests), brakes (6.3% of tests).

Where does this data come from?

DVSA's anonymised MOT tests and results: every MOT test carried out in Great Britain in 2024, published free under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The scores are our own analysis, not DVSA's.

How to read this. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. It counts what a tester found that day, and some of it (tyres, bulbs, wipers) is down to how a car was looked after, not how it was built. So we only compare a model with cars first registered in the same year, and we show the test count and the reasons cars failed. A low MOT pass rate is not proof that a model is unreliable.

Method. Every class 4 (cars and light vans) normal MOT test carried out in Great Britain in 2024 with a result of pass, fail or PRS (fixed at the test centre within an hour), one row per test. First-time pass rate = tests passed outright, out of all of them. PRS counts as a fail because the car arrived failing (this is DVSA's own “initial failure rate”, turned around). Trim and engine names are merged into one model (“FIESTA ZETEC” and “FIESTA” are both the Fiesta). Year = year of first registration in the UK, not the year the car was built. A year needs 1,000+ tests to be shown. Vans, pickups and van-based people carriers are compared with other vans. Northern Ireland isn't in this data.
