Questions

Questions

What is the Citroen C3 MOT pass rate?

65.0% of Citroen C3 cars first registered 2004–2021 passed their MOT first time in 2024, from 190,937 tests. Cars first registered in the same years passed 71.1%, so the Citroen C3 is 6.1 points worse than cars its age.

Which Citroen C3 years have the best MOT pass rate?

Against cars the same age: first registered in 2021, 90.8% (+2.0 points).

Which Citroen C3 years should I be careful with?

The years furthest below cars the same age: first registered in 2012, 54.9% (−9.9 points); first registered in 2011, 52.8% (−9.5 points); first registered in 2010, 51.5% (−8.4 points).

Why do Citroen C3 cars fail their MOT?

The top areas: lights and electrics (17.0% of tests), suspension (10.5% of tests), body, chassis and underside (7.8% of tests). The single most common item is brake light not working (4.5% of tests).

How many miles has a Citroen C3 usually done at its MOT?

62,698 miles on average at the 2024 test, across first registration years 2004–2021, against 74,698 for cars the same age.

Does the MOT pass rate tell me if a Citroen C3 is reliable?

Not on its own. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. Wear items like tyres, bulbs and wipers depend on the owner as much as the car. Comparing the Citroen C3 with cars first registered in the same years takes age out of it, but always check the MOT history and service record of the exact car.

How to read these numbers

How to read this. An MOT shows the car's condition on the test day, not how it will run tomorrow. It counts what a tester found that day, and some of it (tyres, bulbs, wipers) is down to how a car was looked after, not how it was built. So we only compare a model with cars first registered in the same year, and we show the test count and the reasons cars failed. A low MOT pass rate is not proof that a model is unreliable.

Method. Every class 4 (cars and light vans) normal MOT test carried out in Great Britain in 2024 with a result of pass, fail or PRS (fixed at the test centre within an hour), one row per test. First-time pass rate = tests passed outright, out of all of them. PRS counts as a fail because the car arrived failing (this is DVSA's own “initial failure rate”, turned around). Trim and engine names are merged into one model (“FIESTA ZETEC” and “FIESTA” are both the Fiesta). Year = year of first registration in the UK, not the year the car was built. A year needs 1,000+ tests to be shown. Vans, pickups and van-based people carriers are compared with other vans. Northern Ireland isn't in this data. The Citroen C3 here merges these DVSA model names: C3, C3 SHINE PURETECH S/S, C3 FLAIR + PURETECH S/S, C3 SHINE + PURETECH S/S, C3 FLAIR + PURETECH S/S AUTO, C3 SHINE + PURETECH S/S AUTO….
