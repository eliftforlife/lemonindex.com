The Lemon Index

Car complaint statistics.

906,476 U.S. owner complaints to the government, broken down by system, brand and model.

Key numbers

  • 906,476 owner complaints are in our dataset for the 126 models we track by model year.
  • Owners reported 46,629 crashes, 16,882 fires, 29,556 injuries and 906 deaths in those complaints. These are owner reports, not verified counts.
  • Owners reported 31,672 vehicles towed.
  • We track 3,645 different recall campaigns across those models.
  • The most reliable brand by owner complaints is Lexus, with a Lemon Index of 30. The highest is Tesla at 322. 100 is the average car.
  • The most reliable popular model is the Toyota 4Runner at 26. The highest is the Tesla Model S at 430.
  • The Ford EcoSport drew the most check engine light complaints per vehicle sold, at 139.9 per 100,000.
