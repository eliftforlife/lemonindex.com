The Cars Owners Complained About Most in November 2026 (So Far)

NHTSA received 1,837 vehicle owner complaints from November 1 to 9, about 204 a day (October: 212 a day).

Month to date. Complaints NHTSA received November 1 to 9, 2026 (NHTSA's public file runs a few days behind). This page updates as more of the month comes in; the full-month version publishes in early December.

Key findings

1,837owner complaints

204a day (October: 212)

95reported a crash

31reported a fire

  • NHTSA received 1,837 vehicle owner complaints from November 1 to 9, about 204 a day (October: 212 a day).
  • The Ford F-Series drew the most: 67 complaints, most often about its transmission & drivetrain.
  • The most common problem area was engine (27.2% of complaints, vs 24.3% in October).
  • Among newer cars, the 2025 Honda Odyssey drew the most complaints per car sold: 14.7 per 100,000 sold.
  • NHTSA received 4 new vehicle recalls covering 80,459 vehicles; the largest: Hyundai Motor America, 37,901 vehicles (air bags).

How we counted

Every vehicle complaint in NHTSA's public owner-complaint file that NHTSA received in the month, counted once per complaint (its ODI number), grouped by model across all model years, with the site's own make and model matching (a Ram 1500 and a Ram 2500 are both a Ram pickup). Per-car-sold numbers divide one model year's complaints by its U.S. sales. Recalls are the vehicle recalls NHTSA received that month, for car brands we cover.

Complaints are owners' own reports, not confirmed defects, and most owners never file one. This is not the Lemon Index score, which uses each car's first 3 years: how the index works.
