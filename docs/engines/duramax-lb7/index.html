Quick answers

Is the Duramax LB7 reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 592 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 7% name the engine, #10 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 72 (12%) mention an injector, against 1.2% for its peers.

What trucks have the Duramax LB7?

In our data: the Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD/3500HD and GMC Sierra 2500HD/3500HD, model years 2001–2004, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common Duramax LB7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government: brakes, fuel system, electrical.

How many recalls name the Duramax LB7?

1 NHTSA recall on these trucks names it.
