Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of cars with this engine name much more often than owners of the other 50 everyday engines we track (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • The DEF (AdBlue) system and NOx sensors. 35 of 316 owner complaints (11%) mention the DEF (AdBlue) system or a NOx sensor, against a median of under 0.1% for the other 50 everyday engines we track.

    I have received an engine code 4bcc " passive tank fill sensor" for my 2011 bmw x5d. this is in the the emission system def tank. this appears from my research that it is a frequent problem.

    2011 owner · NHTSA complaint 11269390
  • The EGR and the particulate filter. 49 of 316 owner complaints (16%) mention the EGR or the diesel particulate filter, against a median of under 0.1% for the other 50 everyday engines we track.

    The EGR system in my 2013 BMW x5d has failed. There is a recall notice for the EGR system on the 2014 BMW x5d with the same engine and exhaust system related to a fire warning. It would seem to make sense to include the my car in the recall under the circumstances.

    2013 owner · NHTSA complaint 11706109

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: the turbochargers (6 of 316, 1.9%, peers under 0.1%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

Recalls that name this engine

  • 18V336000 May 2018 Engine And Engine Cooling X5 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013

    BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2009-2013 X5 xDrive 35d diesel vehicles. The engine idler pulley bolt may loosen over time and break.

    Fix. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the idler pulley bolt with an improved part, free of charge. The recall began March 11, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

  • 13V339000 Aug 2013 Fuel System, Diesel X5 2009, 2010

    BMW is recalling certain model year 2009-2010 X5 xDrive35d vehicles equipped with a diesel engine and manufactured from June 13, 2009, through November 9, 2009. The fuel filter heater, which is used for warming up the diesel fuel during vehicle operation at low outside temperatures, could experience an electrical overload condition and become permanently activated.

    Fix. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel filter heater, free of charge. The safety recall began on September 26, 2013. Owners may contact BMW customer relations at 1-800-525-7417.

  • 11V441000 Aug 2011 Fuel System, Diesel X5 2009

    Bmw is recalling certain model year 2009 x5 xdrive35d vehicles equipped with a diesel engine and manufactured from october 1, 2008, through june 12, 2009. The fuel filter heater, which is used for warming up the diesel fuel during vehicle operation at low outside temperatures, could experience an electrical overload condition and become permanently activated.

    Fix. Bmw will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel filter heater free of charge. The safety recall began on october 11, 2011. Owners may contact bmw customer relations and services at 1-800-525-7417.

  • 11V332000 Jun 2011 Engine And Engine Cooling X5 2011

    Bmw is recalling certain model year 2011, x5 diesel sports activity vehicles (sav) manufactured from march 31, 2011, through may 19, 2011. Due to a component manufacturing error at the supplier, the welds in the belt tensioner could have incipient cracks. Over time, this could lead a malfunction of the belt tensioner.

    Fix. Dealers will replace the engine belt tensioner free of charge. The safety recall is expected to begin during august 2011. Owners may contact bmw customer relations and services at 1-800-525-7417, or via email at customerrelations@bmwusa.com.

Quick answers

Is the BMW M57 3.0 diesel reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car score. Of the 316 complaints owners filed about cars with it, 40% name the engine, #10 of the 51 everyday engines we track (1 = the most). 35 (11%) mention the DEF (AdBlue) system or a NOx sensor, against under 0.1% for the other engines. 49 (16%) mention the EGR or the diesel particulate filter, against under 0.1% for the other engines.

What cars have the BMW M57 3.0 diesel?

In our data: the BMW X5 and BMW 3 Series, model years 2009–2013, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common M57 diesel problems?

In owner complaints to the government: engine, airbags, fuel system.

How many recalls name the M57 diesel?

4 NHTSA recalls on these cars name it.

Where this data comes from

Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under each car's own NHTSA names (the same ones its car pages count), all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN with NHTSA's vPIC decoder (displacement, cylinders, engine model, turbo, hybrid) and the engine maker's model years; a VIN that doesn't decode to exactly one engine is left out. Older cars have had more years to collect complaints, and popular cars draw more because more were sold. Source: NHTSA owner complaints (FLAT_CMPL + COMPLAINTS_RECEIVED_2025-2026), received through 20261115; engines: NHTSA vPIC VIN decoder (batch); NHTSA recalls API. Engine names and model years: BMW.
