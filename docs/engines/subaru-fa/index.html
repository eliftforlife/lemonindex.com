How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Known issues on record

  • Valve spring recall (Recall). On early 2013 FR-S cars, valve springs can fracture, which can make the engine run badly or stall. Fix: Dealer replaces the valve springs for free (NHTSA 18V-772). 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (ZN6 GR86).
  • Engine failures after the valve spring fix (Owner-reported, disputed). Some owners reported power loss, knocking or a dead engine after the recall repair. Excess sealant getting into the oil is a theory, not a confirmed cause. 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZN6 GR86).
  • Fuel pump recall (Recall). On some 2018–2019 Toyota 86s, the low-pressure fuel pump in the tank can fail and stall the engine while driving. Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-587). 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (ZN6 GR86).
  • Oil starvation and bearing failure (Owner-reported, disputed). Some early GR86s lost rod bearings; one dealer teardown blamed excess factory sealant blocking the oil pickup. How many engines are affected is not known. Fix: Toyota rebuilt that car under warranty after first refusing. 14 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZN8 GR86).
  • Oil pressure drops on track (Owner-reported). An owner's data logging on one BRZ and one GR86 showed big oil pressure drops in hard right-hand corners on track. 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZN8 GR86).
  • Valve spring recall (2013) (Recall). On 2013 BRZs, valve springs could break, making the engine run badly or stall. Some owners told The Drive their engines failed soon after the recall work. Fix: Dealer replaces the valve springs for free (NHTSA 18V-772). 32 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZC6 BRZ).
  • Fuel pump recall (2018–2019) (Recall). On some 2018–2019 BRZs, the low-pressure fuel pump can fail, which can stall the engine while driving. Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-587). 27 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZC6 BRZ).
  • Coil packs overheat (early cars) (Parts seller). A parts seller says coil packs on early BRZs tend to overheat, causing misfires and faster spark plug wear. Fix: Replace with the newest coil pack revision. 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (ZC6 BRZ).
  • RTV sealant and oil starvation (disputed) (Lawsuit claim, disputed). A 2024 class action claims excess factory RTV sealant can flake into the oil and starve 2013–2023 BRZ engines. It is an unproven claim; no bulletin or recall has been issued. 6 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZC6 BRZ).
  • Oil pressure drops on track (disputed) (Owner-reported). One owner's logging on a 2022 BRZ and GR86 showed oil pressure falling from 62 to 28 psi in hard right-hand track corners. Subaru and Toyota did not comment. Fix: Owners suggest a full oil level, an oil cooler or a pan baffle for track use. 3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZD8 BRZ).
  • RTV sealant in the oil pickup (disputed) (Owner-reported / lawsuit claim). In 2022 a GR86 owner found RTV sealant in the oil pickup after a bearing failure; BRZ owners reported similar buildup. A 2024 class action makes the same claim. No bulletin or recall followed. 7 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ZD8 BRZ).
  • No engine recall (Recall). NHTSA lists no engine recall for 2022–2025 BRZs; the one recall (2022) covers rear turn signals.

Quick answers

What cars have the Subaru FA20 / FA24 boxer engine?

In the US: the Scion FR-S, Subaru BRZ, Toyota 86, Toyota GR86, model years 2013–2026. See the version table for each one.
