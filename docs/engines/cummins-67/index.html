Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of trucks with this engine name much more often than owners of other diesel truck generations (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • The DPF / EGR / DEF system. 164 of 7,667 owner complaints (2.1%) mention the DPF, DEF or regen system, against a median of 0.1% for the other diesel truck generations we track.

    DPF regeneration frequency. Truck began exhaust regeneration at frequent intervals. Truck produced check engine light for "regen frequency P2459". Problem verified by dealer. Cause of the issue is unknown.

    2022 owner · NHTSA complaint 11689162
  • The turbo. 129 of 7,667 owner complaints (1.7%) mention the turbo, against a median of 1.0% for the other diesel truck generations we track.

    Heater grid fell of got into motor had to replace the motor and fuel lines, injectors, and turbo. Cost me over 30K cash. My wife withdrew from her retirement so we could afford to replace the motor. Then I had to take a job and move to be able to pay the truck off.

    2016 owner · NHTSA complaint 11775735

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: the EGR system (51 of 7,667, 0.7%, peers 0.3%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

When problems show up

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 17,000 and 84,000 miles; the middle report was at 44,000 miles (2,929 complaints with a mileage).

Quick answers

Is the Cummins 6.7 (Ram) reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 7,667 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 11% name the engine, #6 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 164 (2.1%) mention the DPF, DEF or regen system, against 0.1% for its peers. 129 (1.7%) mention the turbo, against 1.0% for its peers.

What trucks have the Cummins 6.7 (Ram)?

In our data: the Ram 2500/3500 (Heavy Duty), model years 2007–2026, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 6.7 Cummins problems?

In owner complaints to the government: brakes, steering, electrical.

How many recalls name the 6.7 Cummins?

9 NHTSA recalls on these trucks name it.
