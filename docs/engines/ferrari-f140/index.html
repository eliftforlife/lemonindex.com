Quick answers

What cars have the Ferrari F140 engine?

In the US: the Ferrari FF / GTC4Lusso, Ferrari F12berlinetta, Ferrari 812 Superfast, model years 2012–2024. See the version table for each one.

Is the Ferrari F140 reliable?

Mostly yes. YouTube owner tone 54/100, #39 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the top third); 1 engine-related recall on record. Not enough owner complaints to judge it per car: supercars sell in small numbers. Its cars had 9 recalls (1 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the Ferrari F140 make?

651–819 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.

How to read this chart

Not enough complaints to chart. Owners filed 3 complaints with the government that we can tie to this engine's cars, 2012–2024, about any part of the car. Supercars sell in small numbers, so we show YouTube, recalls and the automaker's repair notes instead.

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2023 (58) and least about the 2012 (48); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
