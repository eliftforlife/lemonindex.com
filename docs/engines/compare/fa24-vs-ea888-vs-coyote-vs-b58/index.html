Quick answers

Which is more reliable: the FA24F 2.4 turbo, EA888 2.0 TSI, 5.0 Coyote or the B58?

Safest bet on our data: the BMW B58. Its YouTube owner tone is the best of the 2 rated here (59/100, #9 of 99 engines with a YouTube study), and it has 2 engine recalls on record. The FA24F 2.4 turbo isn't rated: no YouTube study yet. The EA888 2.0 TSI isn't rated: no YouTube study yet.

How is the pick made?

By YouTube owner tone, the one number every engine page measures the same way (praise vs trouble words in the most-watched videos, 0-100). Owner complaints to NHTSA are filed by car, not engine, and follow how many were sold, so they are shown but not used to pick. Recalls and known issues come from our performance pages, each with its source.
