BMW

BMW engines, ranked by owner complaints.

The M54 draws the fewest owner complaints per car, the N52 the most. Tap an engine for every year.

Rank = owner complaints per car sold (the Lemon Index: complaints about any part of the car, per car sold, 100 = average car, lower is better), averaged over the model years where this was the only engine in a model we have sales for. Engines with no such year are listed after, unranked. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are (0–100), averaged over the scored years; it is not part of the rank. None of these is an engine failure rate.
