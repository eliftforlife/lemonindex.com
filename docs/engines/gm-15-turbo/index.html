Recalls that name this engine

  • 19V642000 Sep 2019 Electrical System Malibu 2018

    General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Malibu vehicles equipped with 1.5L turbo engines. An error in the Engine Control Module (ECM) software may result in the fuel injectors being disabled.

    Fix. GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the ECM software, free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2019. Owners may contact GM customer service at 1-800-630-2438. GM's number for this recall is N192221960.

Quick answers

Is the GM 1.5 Turbo (LFV / LYX) reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car score. Of the 4,477 complaints owners filed about cars with it, 33% name the engine, #15 of the 51 everyday engines we track (1 = the most).

What cars have the GM 1.5 Turbo (LFV / LYX)?

In our data: the Chevrolet Malibu, Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain, model years 2016–2025, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

How we count

How to read this chart

Counts of complaints filed so far. Older cars have had longer to collect them, and popular cars draw more because more were sold: no one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car rate. Compare the shares below instead.

Where this data comes from

Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under each car's own NHTSA names (the same ones its car pages count), all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN with NHTSA's vPIC decoder (displacement, cylinders, engine model, turbo, hybrid) and the engine maker's model years; a VIN that doesn't decode to exactly one engine is left out. Older cars have had more years to collect complaints, and popular cars draw more because more were sold. Source: NHTSA owner complaints (FLAT_CMPL + COMPLAINTS_RECEIVED_2025-2026), received through 20261115; engines: NHTSA vPIC VIN decoder (batch); NHTSA recalls API. Engine names and model years: GM.
