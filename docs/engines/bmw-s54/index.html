Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where this was its only engine that year; a gray dot means the model shared engines, so the number isn't this engine's alone. How we count

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Known issues on record

  • Rod bearings (BMW service actions). BMW replaced oil pumps and rod bearings on 2001–2003 cars under two service actions and extended engine coverage to 6 years / 100,000 miles, long expired. Bearings are still the S54’s best-known weak spot. Fix: Oil analysis, or preventive bearings (ECS Tuning: about every 100k miles). 3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (E46 M3).
  • VANOS (Owner-reported). Worn VANOS hub tabs and sprocket bolts that back out. BMWBLOG calls it the most common S54 issue. Fix: VANOS rebuild with updated hubs. 7 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (E46 M3).
  • SMG gearbox (Owner complaints). Owners filed complaints about the optional SMG automated manual: surging and replaced units. 26 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (E46 M3).

Sources: BMW NA SI B11 02 03 (Campaign 356): E46 M3 oil pump and rod bearings, BMW NA SI B11 04 04: E46 M3 connecting rod bearings (via Euroconnex), BMWBLOG: BMW S54 engine, everything you need to know (2022), ECS Tuning: most common BMW E46 M3 problems, NHTSA owner complaints and recalls. Quick answers

What cars have the BMW S54 engine?

In the US: the M3, model years 2001–2006. See the version table for each one.

Is the S54 reliable?

Mostly, with known issues to check. YouTube owner tone 46/100, #89 of 141 engines with a YouTube study (in the middle third); no engine-related recall on record, 3 known issues listed below. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2003 (54). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the S54 make?

BMW rated it at 333 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.
