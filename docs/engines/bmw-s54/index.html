BMW S54 engine: reliability by year, specs and the cars that have it.

The BMW S54 is BMW's 3.2 L naturally aspirated inline-6, made 2000–2008. In the US it was used in the M3 from model years 2001 to 2006, rated 333 hp. 1 owner complaints to the government name the S54 in their own words. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2003 (54). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate.

Owner complaints in (a) and (b) cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine: see the top complaint areas.
