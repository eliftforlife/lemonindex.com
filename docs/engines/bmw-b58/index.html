BMW B58 engine: reliability by year, specs and the cars that have it.

The BMW B58 is BMW's 3.0 L single twin-scroll turbo inline-6, made 2015–present. In the US it was used in the 2 Series, 3 Series, 4 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, Z4 from model years 2016 to 2026, rated 322–382 hp. 54 owner complaints to the government name the B58 in their own words. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2020 (57). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate.

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints in (a) and (b) cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine: see the top complaint areas. Complaints naming "B58" = complaints whose own text mentions the code, about a model and year that had it. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube says = how positive the videos are, 0–100 (higher = more positive), from video titles and transcripts, counted only where the title names the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos; "naming" = how many titles name that year. Under 3 videos' worth shows "not enough videos".

Quick answers

What cars have the BMW B58 engine?

In the US: the 2 Series, 3 Series, 4 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, Z4, model years 2016–2026. See the version table for each one.

Is the B58 reliable?

The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2020 (57). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the B58 make?

BMW rated it at 322 to 382 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.
