Reliability by year

Known issues on record

  • Brake-assist recall (engine software, 2020) (Recall). On some 2020 M340i and M340i xDrive cars, the engine software could cut brake assist during engine start. Full mechanical braking stays available. Fix: Dealer updates the engine software for free (NHTSA 21V-598). 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (G20 340i).
  • Oil filter housing leaks (Parts seller). The plastic housing and its gaskets can warp with heat and leak oil or coolant, often around 60,000–100,000 miles. Fix: Replace the housing and gaskets. 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (F30 340i).
  • Coolant loss (Owner-reported). Owners report coolant leaks. A parts seller names the water pump seal and the heat management module as common sources. 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (G20 340i).
  • Valve cover and PCV (Owner-reported). Valve cover gasket leaks and PCV valve faults are the most-reported B58 problems on owner forums, per SlashGear. 3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (G20 340i).
  • Brake-assist recall (engine software) (Recall). On about 13,000 2020–2021 Supras, engine software could damage the part that powers brake assist on certain starts, lengthening stops. Fix: Dealer updates the engine computer for free (NHTSA 21V-598).

Sources: Toyota dealer notice, Safety Recall 21TA04 / NHTSA 21V-598 (Aug 2021), FCP Euro: BMW B58 engine parts and common problems, SlashGear: Common problems with BMW's B58 engines, according to owners (Oct 2024). The same list as on the 340i page, the GR Supra page.

Quick answers

What cars have the BMW B58 engine?

In the US: the M240i, 3 Series, 4 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, Z4, model years 2016–2026. See the version table for each one.

Is the B58 reliable?

Mostly, if the recalls were done. YouTube owner tone 59/100, #9 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the top third); 2 engine-related recalls on record, 5 known issues listed below. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2020 (57). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the B58 make?

BMW rated it at 255 to 382 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.

How to read this chart

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2023 (62) and least about the 2016 (52); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
