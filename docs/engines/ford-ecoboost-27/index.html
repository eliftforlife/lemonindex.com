Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of trucks with this engine name much more often than owners of other F-150 engines (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • Oil leaks (the oil pan on early trucks). 44 of 2,480 owner complaints (1.8%) mention an oil leak, against a median of 0.3% for the other F-150 engines we track.

    Oil pan leaking, front oil pan leaking on RH. Valve covers failed for updated oil pan. RTD seal leak. Seen that this is a current recall. But my VIN isn't showing this recall. But I'm having this issue.

    2017 owner · NHTSA complaint 11630708
  • Coolant leaks. 39 of 2,480 owner complaints (1.6%) mention coolant loss or overheating, against a median of 1.0% for the other F-150 engines we track.

    Daytime Running Lamps, vertical beam on driver side does not function after 4 years. Research shows it's likely melting the headlight assembly components during overheating and burnout.

    2021 owner · NHTSA complaint 11735305

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: cam phaser rattle at cold start (5 of 2,480, 0.2%, peers 1.1%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

When problems show up

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 14,000 and 80,000 miles; the middle report was at 42,000 miles (985 complaints with a mileage).

Recalls that name this engine 1

  • 24V635000 Aug 2024 Engine And Engine Cooling

    Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Bronco, F-150, Edge, Explorer, Lincoln Nautilus, and Lincoln Aviator vehicles equipped with either a 2.7L or 3.0L Nano EcoBoost engine. The engine intake valves may break while driving, which can result in engine failure and a loss of drive power.

    Fix. Dealers will perform an engine cycle test and replace the engine as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 3, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S55.

Quick answers

Is the Ford 2.7 EcoBoost reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 2,480 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 18% name the engine, #3 of 4 F-150 engines we track (1 = the most). 44 (1.8%) mention an oil leak, against 0.3% for its peers. 39 (1.6%) mention coolant loss or overheating, against 1.0% for its peers.

What trucks have the Ford 2.7 EcoBoost?

In our data: the Ford F-150, model years 2015–2026, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 2.7 EcoBoost problems?

In owner complaints to the government: transmission & drivetrain, engine, electrical.
