Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where this was its only engine that year; a gray dot means the model shared engines, so the number isn't this engine's alone. How we count

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Known issues on record

  • A 2017 recall covered a crankcase-ventilation valve heater that could short-circuit (about 740,000 BMW six-cylinder cars).
  • A 2023 recall covered VANOS bolts that could loosen or break.
  • The block is magnesium-aluminum; magnesium is more sensitive to corrosion and to creep at high temperature.

Quick answers

What cars have the BMW N52 engine?

In the US: the 3 Series, 5 Series, X3, X5, Z4, model years 2006–2013. See the version table for each one.

Is the N52 reliable?

The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2011 (799). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the N52 make?

BMW rated it at 215 to 241 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.
