Quick answers

What cars have the BMW S68 4.4 twin-turbo V8 engine?

In the US: the BMW XM, BMW M5 (G90), model years 2023–2026. See the version table for each one.

Is the BMW S68 4.4 twin-turbo V8 reliable?

Mostly yes. YouTube owner tone 55/100, #28 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the top third); 1 engine-related recall on record. Not enough owner complaints to judge it per car: the government files most performance versions under their base model. Its cars had 9 recalls (1 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.

How to read this chart

Not enough complaints to chart. Owners filed 12 complaints with the government that we can tie to this engine's cars, 2023–2026, about any part of the car. Performance versions are mostly filed under their base model (a Shelby GT500 is filed as a Mustang), so we only count complaints whose model name or the owner's own words tie them to this engine, and lead with YouTube, recalls and the automaker's repair notes.

Why the car page shows more: the X5 M page counts 0 complaints and 0 recall campaigns for 2024–2026, everything NHTSA files as "X5 M", other engines included. This page counts only the 12 complaints and 9 recalls we can tie to the BMW S68 4.4 twin-turbo V8 by model name or the owner's own words.

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2023 (56) and least about the 2024 (53); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
