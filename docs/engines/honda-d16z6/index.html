Less liked than mostRanks in the bottom third of 178 engines on YouTube. 1 engine recall on record. 2 known issues below.

Known issues on record

  • Theft target (Outlet report). The NICB's count of cars stolen in 2010 put the 1995 Honda Civic second in the US. That count covers every Civic body style. 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (EJ Civic Coupe).
  • Automatic shifter clip (recall) (Recall). Honda recalled 1992 to 1994 Civics with the automatic gearbox because a clip on the shift cable could come off, so the lever could show the wrong gear. Dealers fitted a better clip. Fix: Check that recall 94V063000 was done. 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (EJ Civic Coupe).

Specs by generation

Honda D16Z6: EJ Civic Coupe, 1993–1995

  • Layout: Inline-4, SOHC 16-valve with VTEC
  • Output: 125 hp at 6,600 rpm, 106 lb-ft at 5,200 rpm
  • Size: 1,590 cc, 75 x 90 mm bore x stroke
  • VTEC: Switches to the high-lift cam at about 4,800 rpm
  • Redline: 7,200 rpm

Quick answers

What cars have the Honda D16Z6 engine?

On this site so far: the Honda Civic Coupe, model years 1993–1995. It is used in other cars we haven't covered yet.

Is the Honda D16Z6 reliable?

Not one of the safer bets on our data. YouTube owner tone 44/100, #130 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the bottom third); 1 engine-related recall on record, 2 known issues listed below. Owner complaints are filed by car, not engine, and cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate.

How much horsepower does the Honda D16Z6 make?

125 hp in the versions on this page (factory figures).
