Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of trucks with this engine name much more often than owners of other diesel truck generations (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • The CP4.2 high-pressure fuel pump. 83 of 779 owner complaints (11%) mention the CP4 or the high-pressure fuel pump, against a median of 0.3% for the other diesel truck generations we track.

Quick answers

Is the Duramax LML reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 779 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 13% name the engine, #5 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 83 (11%) mention the CP4 or the high-pressure fuel pump, against 0.3% for its peers. 33 (4.2%) mention the DPF, DEF or regen system, against 0.1% for its peers.

What trucks have the Duramax LML?

In our data: the Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD/3500HD and GMC Sierra 2500HD/3500HD, model years 2011–2016, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common Duramax LML problems?

In owner complaints to the government: fuel system, electrical, steering.

How many recalls name the Duramax LML?

0 NHTSA recalls on these trucks name it.
