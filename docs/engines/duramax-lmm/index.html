Quick answers

Is the Duramax LMM reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 385 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 8% name the engine, #9 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most).

What trucks have the Duramax LMM?

In our data: the Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD/3500HD and GMC Sierra 2500HD/3500HD, model years 2007–2010, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common Duramax LMM problems?

In owner complaints to the government: airbags, electrical, engine.
