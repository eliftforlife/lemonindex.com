How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or, for the Corvette, when the owner's own words name the Z06, ZR1 or E-Ray. They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Quick answers

What cars have the Bugatti W16 engine?

In the US: the Bugatti Veyron, Bugatti Chiron, model years 2010–2024. See the version table for each one.

Is the Bugatti W16 reliable?

Not enough owner complaints to judge it per car: supercars sell in small numbers. Its cars had 10 recalls (2 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.
