When problems show up

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,500 and 100,000 miles; the middle report was at 56,000 miles (1,524 complaints with a mileage).

Recalls that name this engine 2

  • 05V270000 Jun 2005 Electrical System

    Certain pickup trucks, sport utility vehicles and vans equipped with 6.0l diesel engines may experience stalling without warning while driving and may or may not restart.

    Fix. Dealers will upgrade the fuel injection control module (ficm) wire harness or replaced, and/or have a new injection control pressure (icp)) sensor connector installed free of charge. The recall began on july 22, 2005. Owners may contact ford at 1-800-392-3673.

  • 04V327000 Jul 2004 Electrical System

    On certain pickup trucks and sport utility vehicles equipped with 6.0l diesel engines, the engine ground stud that attaches the negative battery terminal eyelet to the engine block may be loose.

    Fix. Dealers will replace the engine ground stud and the radio suppression strap and adjacent components will be inspected for damage and replaced, if necessary. The recall began on july 23, 2004. Owners should contact ford at 1-800-392-3673.

Quick answers

Is the Ford 6.0 Power Stroke reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 1,708 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 34% name the engine, #1 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 52 (3.0%) mention a head gasket, against under 0.1% for its peers. 150 (8.8%) mention the EGR system, against 0.3% for its peers.

What trucks have the Ford 6.0 Power Stroke?

In our data: the Ford Super Duty (F-250/F-350), model years 2003–2007, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 6.0 Power Stroke problems?

In owner complaints to the government: engine, steering, fuel system.

How many recalls name the 6.0 Power Stroke?

2 NHTSA recalls on these trucks name it.
