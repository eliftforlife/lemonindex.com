VW EA888 2.0 TSI engine: known problems and recalls.

Volkswagen's 2.0-liter inline-4, turbo. This page covers the versions we have sourced so far: the Volkswagen Golf R, model years 2015–2025. The engine is used in other cars too; those aren't covered here yet.

Known issues on record

  • Turbo wastegate sticks (class settlement) (Maker bulletin). Rust can seize the turbo wastegate rod on 2015–2019 Golf Rs, setting code P2563 or P00AF. VW denies a defect but settled a class action. Fix: VW pays 50% of a turbo repair for 8.5 years or 85,000 miles (VWP-25-16); TSB 21-25-01 first tries freeing the rod.
  • Fuel hose recall (2018) (Recall). On some 2018 Golf Rs, the fuel hose connector in the engine bay can come loose and leak fuel, raising fire risk. Fix: Dealer secures the fuel line connection for free (NHTSA 18V-864). 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (Mk7 / Mk7.5 Golf R).
  • Coolant leaks at the water pump (Owner-reported). A few owners told NHTSA of coolant leaks that dealers traced to the water pump and thermostat housing. 3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (Mk7 / Mk7.5 Golf R).
  • Engine cover recall (2022) (Recall). Hard acceleration could loosen the engine cover on 2022 Golf Rs; it can touch hot parts and melt, raising fire risk. Fix: Dealer removes the cover, then fits an updated one for free (NHTSA 22V-163).
  • Coolant hose recall (2022–2023) (Recall). The radiator may not be fixed to the intercooler correctly, which can damage a coolant hose and let the engine overheat. Fix: Dealer inspects the radiator and replaces damaged parts for free (NHTSA 22V-707).
  • Thermostat fault code P00B7 (Maker bulletin). Some 2022–2024 Golf Rs set P00B7 (low coolant flow) and the check-engine light. VW says not to replace parts first. Fix: Dealer updates the engine computer software (TSB 01-25-05).

Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of cars with this engine name much more often than owners of the other 38 everyday engines we track (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • Oil consumption. 272 of 7,443 owner complaints (3.7%) mention burning or losing oil, against a median of 0.5% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    Car burns excessive oil (1 quart per 1000 miles). Smoke comes from tail pipes. Had PCV replaced. Engine blocked scoped and valve guide #3 is bad. VW has not recalled this common issue and does NOT have the parts available to fix (on 6+ month back order).

    2019 owner · NHTSA complaint 11773791
  • The timing chain tensioner. 254 of 7,443 owner complaints (3.4%) mention the timing chain, against a median of 0.1% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    Timing chain issues, motor oil circulation problems, that Volkswagen has recalls out for 2.0 cc turbo 2013.

    2013 owner · NHTSA complaint 11673096
  • Water pump and thermostat housing leaks. 169 of 7,443 owner complaints (2.3%) mention the water pump, against a median of 0.2% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    my initial water pump from Audi was replaced via recall. then I recently changed the thermostat hose and my water pump started leaking again. can you please look into these faulty water pumps?

    2017 owner · NHTSA complaint 11763714

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: carbon buildup (27 of 7,443, 0.4%, peers under 0.1%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

5 recalls on these cars name the engine

  • 20V153000 Mar 2020 Electrical System A6 2019, 2020

    Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Audi A6 vehicles equipped with mild-hybrid technology and 2.0TFSI engines. Moisture may enter the starter generator and cause an electrical short circuit.

    Fix. Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the starter generator, free of charge. The recall began by May 22, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 27H2.

  • 18V229000 Apr 2018 Engine And Engine Cooling A4 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016; Q5 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017; A6 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015

    Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2013-2017 Audi A5 Cabriolet, A5 Sedan and Audi Q5 vehicles, 2012-2015 Audi A6 vehicles and 2013-2016 Audi A4 Sedan and A4 allroad vehicles. These vehicles, equipped with 2.0l Turbo FSI engines, have an electric coolant pump that can either become blocked with debris from the cooling system causing it to overheat or can short-circuit from moisture within the pump.

    Fix. Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the pumps, free of charge. The recall began September 14, 2018. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number's for this recall is 19N3/19N4. Note: This recall is in additional to the coolant pump software update applied as a remedy under recall 17V002.

  • 17V280000 Apr 2017 Equipment A3 2015

    Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2015 Audi A3 1.8T FWD S tronic and 2.0T Quattro S tronic vehicles. The safety certification label has an incorrect vehicle production date. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."

    Fix. Audi will notify owners, and dealers will install an overlay label containing the correct vehicle production date, free of charge. The recall June 23, 2017. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 51F5.

  • 17V002000 Jan 2017 Engine And Engine Cooling A4 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016; Q5 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017; A6 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015

    Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2013-2017 Audi A5, A5 Cabriolet and Q5 vehicles, 2013-2016 Audi Allroad and Audi A4 vehicles and 2012-2015 Audi A6 vehicles. These vehicles, equipped with 2.0l Turbo FSI engines, have an electric coolant pump that can be blocked with debris from the cooling system, resulting in the pump overheating.

    Fix. Audi will notify owners, and dealers will update the software so that the power supply to the coolant pump is deactivated if the pump becomes blocked with debris, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin February 20, 2017. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 19M1.

  • 08V235000 May 2008 Engine And Engine Cooling Tiguan 2009

    Volkswagen is recalling 4,079 my 2008 passat and 2,500 my 2009 tiguan passenger vehicles equipped with 2.0t fsi ulev ii engines. These vehicles have an engine control module (ecm) containing software that may not properly control engine idle with the air conditioning turned on. In rare cases, the ecm may unexpectedly increase engine rpm.

    Fix. Dealers will inspect and update the ecm software free of charge. The recall began on may 30, 2008. Owners may contact volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298.

Quick answers

What cars have the VW EA888 2.0 TSI engine?

On this site so far: the Volkswagen Golf R, model years 2015–2025. It is used in other cars we haven't covered yet.

Is the VW EA888 2.0 TSI reliable?

We can't rate it yet: there's no YouTube owner study for it, and owner complaints are filed by car, not engine. It has 3 engine-related recalls on record, 6 known issues listed below. Owner complaints are filed by car, not engine, and cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate.

How much horsepower does the VW EA888 2.0 TSI make?

292 to 328 hp in the versions on this page (factory figures).
