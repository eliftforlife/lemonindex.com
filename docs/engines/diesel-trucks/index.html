Diesel truck engines compared.

12 heavy-duty diesel generations in the Ram 2500/3500, Ford Super Duty and Silverado / Sierra HD, matched to 24,426 owner complaints by VIN. The 6.0 Power Stroke draws the biggest share of engine complaints (34%), the 5.9 Cummins the smallest (4%).

Engines to check hardest

  • 7.3 Power Stroke: stalling (often the cam sensor), 242 of 2,049 complaints (12%) against a 7.8% median.

Quick answers

Which diesel truck engine has the most engine complaints?

By VIN-matched owner complaints, the Ford 6.0 Power Stroke: 34% of its complaints name the engine. A share, not a failure rate.

Which diesel truck engine has the fewest engine complaints?

The Cummins 5.9 (Ram): 4% of its complaints name the engine.
