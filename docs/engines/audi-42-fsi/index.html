How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Known issues on record

  • Carbon buildup on intake valves (Parts seller). Direct injection means fuel never washes the intake valves, so carbon builds up. Signs are weak acceleration, misfires and higher fuel use. Fix: Pull the intake manifold and clean the valves; one shop lists $1,295 and up for an RS 5.
  • Injector failure (Owner-reported). An owner FAQ reports injectors that fail suddenly and can flood a cylinder; one car threw a cylinder 3 misfire at about 62,000 miles. Fix: Replace the injectors; that owner replaced all eight.
  • PCV valve diaphragms (Owner-reported). The two PCV valves use rubber diaphragms that can tear, per an owner FAQ.

Sources: 034Motorsport: carbon cleaning for direct-injected Audi/VW engines, Audi Revolution forum: RS5 maintenance and reliability FAQ (B8, 2019–2020). The same list as on the RS 5 page.

Quick answers

What cars have the Audi 4.2 FSI V8 engine?

In the US: the Audi R8 / R8 Spyder, model years 2010–2015. See the version table for each one.

Is the Audi 4.2 FSI V8 reliable?

Mostly yes. YouTube owner tone 54/100, #33 of 99 engines with a YouTube study (in the top third); no engine-related recall on record, 3 known issues listed below. Not enough owner complaints to judge it per car: supercars sell in small numbers. Have any used car inspected.

414 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.
