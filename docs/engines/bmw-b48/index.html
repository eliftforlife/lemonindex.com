Quick answers

What cars have the BMW B48 / B46 engine?

In the US: the 2 Series, 3 Series, 4 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, X1, X2, X3, X4, Z4, model years 2016–2026. See the version table for each one.

Is the B48 reliable?

Mostly yes. YouTube owner tone 57/100, #16 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the top third); no engine-related recall on record, 1 known issue listed below. Where this was the only engine in the model, the 2017 had the fewest owner complaints per car (Index 71) and the 2023 the most (Index 280), counting complaints about any part of the car. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the B48 make?

BMW rated it at 228 to 312 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.

How to read this chart

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2023 (60) and least about the 2018 (52); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

Owners and YouTube disagree: owner complaints favor the 2017, YouTube favors the 2023.

Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
