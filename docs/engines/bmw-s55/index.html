Reliability by year

Known issues on record

  • Crank hub (Outlet + parts seller). The stock crank hub is not keyed and can slip on the crank, throwing off cam timing. BMWBLOG calls it rare but serious; parts sellers say it is common, so how often it happens is disputed. Fix: An aftermarket keyed or pinned crank hub is the usual fix. 13 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (F80 M3, F82 M4).
  • Oil leaks (Outlet). Gaskets wear with age. BMWBLOG names the valve cover, oil pan and oil filter housing gaskets as common leak points. Fix: Replace the leaking gasket.
  • Carbon buildup (Outlet). Direct injection lets carbon collect on the intake valves over time, which can cause rough idle and misfires. Fix: Clean the intake valves at intervals. 1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (F82 M4).
  • Carbon on intake valves (Owner-reported). Direct injection lets carbon build on the intake valves: rough idle and misfires. Fix: Walnut-blast the valves.
  • Gasket oil leaks (Owner-reported). Valve cover, oil pan and oil filter housing gaskets leak with age. Fix: Replace the gaskets.

Sources: BMWBLOG: BMW S55 engine overview (2023), Studio RSR: S55 crank hub fix (F80 M3 / F82 M4). The same list as on the M2 page, the M3 page, the M4 page.

Quick answers

What cars have the BMW S55 engine?

In the US: the 2 Series, M4, M3, model years 2015–2021. See the version table for each one.

Is the S55 reliable?

Mostly, with known issues to check. YouTube owner tone 50/100, #82 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the middle third); no engine-related recall on record, 5 known issues listed below. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2018 (9). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the S55 make?

BMW rated it at 405 to 493 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.

How to read this chart

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2020 (55) and least about the 2016 (47); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
