Known issues on record

  • Oil use and blue smoke (Outlet report). evo says some EK9s had a hunger for oil that not all owners kept up with, and to treat puffs of blue smoke as a sign the engine needs serious care. JDM Buy Sell suggests a compression or leak-down test.
  • Timing belt history (Outlet report). The B16B has belt-driven cams. JDM Buy Sell’s buyer’s guide says to ask for dated proof of the last belt change and have a mechanic inspect the car.
  • Swapped or wrong engine (Outlet report). JDM Buy Sell says to confirm the chassis code reads EK9, the engine is a real B16B and the car still has its helical limited-slip differential.

Specs by generation

Honda B16B: EK9 Civic Type R, 1997–2000

  • Layout: Inline-4, DOHC with VTEC, belt-driven cams; VTEC switches over at 6,100 rpm
  • Output: 185 PS (182 hp) at 8,200 rpm, 160 Nm (118 lb-ft) at 7,500 rpm
  • Bore x stroke: 81 x 77.4 mm, 1,595 cc, 10.8:1 compression
  • Redline: 8,400 rpm (rev limit 8,600 rpm)
  • Output per liter: About 114 bhp per liter, with no turbo
  • Build: Hand-ported; uses the taller Integra Type R block, in effect a short-stroke B18C Type R engine

Quick answers

What cars have the Honda B16B engine?

On this site so far: the Honda Civic Type R, model years 1997–2000. It is used in other cars we haven't covered yet.

Is the Honda B16B reliable?

Mostly yes. YouTube owner tone 56/100, #22 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the top third); no engine-related recall on record, 3 known issues listed below. Owner complaints are filed by car, not engine, and cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate.

How much horsepower does the Honda B16B make?

182 hp in the versions on this page (factory figures).
