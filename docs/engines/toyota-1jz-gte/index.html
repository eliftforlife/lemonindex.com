Toyota 1JZ-GTE engine: known problems and recalls.

Toyota's 2.5-liter inline-6, turbo, in the Toyota Chaser.

Owners are mixed on itRanks in the middle third of 178 engines on YouTube. 1 engine recall on record. 4 known issues below.

Known issues on record

  • Ceramic turbine wheels (early engines) (Outlet report). Wikipedia says the first twin-turbo engines use ceramic turbine wheels that can delaminate at high turbo speed and heat. This applies to the early engines, not the single-turbo JZX100 version.
  • Blow-by valve and turbo seals (early engines) (Recall). On early engines a faulty one-way valve on the intake cam cover let blow-by gas into the intake, and oil vapor reaching the turbos wore their seals early. Wikipedia says Toyota recalled these engines in Japan, but exports were not covered. Fix: Fixed on later engines.
  • Worn clutch on manual cars (Outlet report). JDMBUYSELL’s buyer’s guide quotes Unique Cars: on late five-speed manual Chasers, a hard-driving former owner may have burned out the clutch. Test drive for slip and judder.
  • Rust (Outlet report). JDMBUYSELL says Japan salts its roads, so a Japanese car is not proof of a dry one. Check the underbody, sills, arches and floors.

Specs by generation

Toyota 1JZ-GTE: X100 (JZX100) Chaser, 1996–2001

  • Layout: Inline-6, DOHC 24-valve, two belt-driven cams
  • Output: 280 PS (276 hp) at 6,200 rpm, 379 Nm (280 lb-ft) at 2,400 rpm in the VVT-i single-turbo version
  • Bore x stroke: 86 x 71.5 mm, 2,492 cc
  • Turbos: Two CT12A turbos at launch in 1990; one CT15B turbo with VVT-i from about 1996, as in the JZX100
  • Compression: 8.5:1 twin-turbo; 9.0:1 single-turbo VVT-i

Quick answers

What cars have the Toyota 1JZ-GTE engine?

On this site so far: the Toyota Chaser, model years 1996–2001. It is used in other cars we haven't covered yet.

Is the Toyota 1JZ-GTE reliable?

Mostly, with known issues to check. YouTube owner tone 49/100, #90 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the middle third); 1 engine-related recall on record, 4 known issues listed below. Owner complaints are filed by car, not engine, and cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate.

How much horsepower does the Toyota 1JZ-GTE make?

276 hp in the versions on this page (factory figures).
