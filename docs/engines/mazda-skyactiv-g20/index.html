How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

2 of them are about the engine, fuel, cooling or powertrain. They come first.

  • 21V875000 Fuel System, Gasoline · Mazda MX-5 2018–19

    The impeller inside the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and deform, potentially causing the fuel pump to fail.

  • 19V072000 Power Train · Mazda MX-5 2016–18

    Due to incorrect programming of the Transmission Control Module (TCM), certain conditions may cause the vehicle to unexpectedly downshift and abruptly decelerate.

  • 25V336000 Traction Control System · Mazda MX-5 2024–25

    The Traction Control System (TCS) and Dynamic Stability Control (DSC) indicator light may fail to illuminate as intended due to a software error.

  • 24V695000 Air Bags · Mazda MX-5 2016–23

    A software error in the air bag control module may cause the front air bags to deploy with excessive force during a crash.

  • 19V496000 Structure · Mazda MX-5 2016

Known issues on record

  • No known engine pattern (Owner-reported). Few NHTSA complaints for 2016–2025 MX-5s involve the engine; most are about the manual gearbox and an air bag recall.
  • Fuel pump recall (Recall). On some 2018–2019 MX-5s, the low-pressure fuel pump impeller can crack and deform, so the pump can fail and the engine stall. Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-875). 8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ND Miata).
  • Manual gearbox failures (transmission) (Owner-reported). Owners report third gear that grinds or won't engage, and some outright failures, mostly 2016 and 2022–2024 cars. No recall; rates unverified. 8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ND Miata).
  • Automatic downshift recall (transmission) (Recall). On some 2016–2019 automatics, transmission software could cause an unexpected downshift and sudden slowing. Fix: Dealer reprograms the transmission computer for free (NHTSA 19V-072). 4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (ND Miata).

Sources: NHTSA owner complaints and recalls, NHTSA recall 21V-875: low-pressure fuel pump, 2018–2019 MX-5 and other Mazdas (Dec 2021), Torque News: ND MX-5 Miata owner reports third-gear transmission issues (Mar 2025), NHTSA recall 19V-072: transmission control software, 2016–2019 MX-5 automatic (Jan 2019).
