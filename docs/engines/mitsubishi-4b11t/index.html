Reliability by year

Known issues on record

  • Drive belt and pulley recall (Recall). On 2008-2011 cars with 4B1 engines, the accessory pulleys can wear and let the drive belt come off. That can drain the battery and stall the engine. Fix: Dealer fits a rubber belt and replaces worn pulleys for free (NHTSA 14V-562). 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (CZ4A (Evo X) Evo).
  • Fuel return pipe recall (Recall). On 2008-2009 Evos, vibration at certain engine speeds can crack the fuel return pipe where it is fixed, which can leak fuel and start a fire. Fix: Dealer replaces the pipe and adds two brackets for free (NHTSA 09V-349). 5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (CZ4A (Evo X) Evo).
  • No engine failure pattern (Owner-reported (NHTSA)). NHTSA complaints on 2008-2015 Evos center on the AYC/ACD hydraulic pump and the clutch, not the engine. Only a handful report engine failures.

Sources: NHTSA recall 14V-562: drive belt and pulleys, 4B1 engines 2008-2011 (Dec 2014), NHTSA recall 09V-349: fuel return pipe, 2008-2009 Lancer Evolution and Ralliart (Aug 2009), NHTSA complaints: 2008 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution, NHTSA recall 13V-446: clutch master cylinder, 2008-2013 Lancer Evolution (Sep 2013). Quick answers

What cars have the Mitsubishi 4B11T 2.0 turbo engine?

In the US: the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution X, model years 2008–2015. See the version table for each one.

Is the Mitsubishi 4B11T 2.0 turbo reliable?

Not one of the safer bets on our data. YouTube owner tone 45/100, #119 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the bottom third); 4 engine-related recalls on record, 2 known issues listed below. Owners filed 116 complaints we can tie to it, about any part of the car. Its cars had 10 recalls (4 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the Mitsubishi 4B11T 2.0 turbo make?

287–291 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.

How to read this chart

Why the car page shows more: the Evo page counts 93 complaints and 10 recall campaigns for 2008–2015, everything NHTSA files as "Lancer Evolution", other engines included. This page counts only the 116 complaints and 10 recalls we can tie to the Mitsubishi 4B11T 2.0 turbo by model name or the owner's own words.

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2011 (47) and least about the 2009 (44); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
