Reliability by year

YouTube tone: 48 (2000). 0-100, higher = better; years not listed had none.

Known issues on record

  • Timing chain tensioner rattle (Owner-reported). A rattle at startup usually means the timing chain tensioner needs replacing, per SlashGear and PistonHeads buyer's guides. Fix: Replace the timing chain tensioner.
  • Oil use (Owner-reported). Some engines use up to a liter of oil every 1,000 miles while others use almost none. If the level runs low, the computer blocks VTEC. 2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (AP1 S2000).

Sources: SlashGear: 12 things you should know before buying a Honda S2000 (Mar 2023), PistonHeads: Honda S2000 buying guide, powertrain (Jul 2014). The same list as on the S2000 page.

Quick answers

What cars have the Honda F20C / F22C1 engine?

In the US: the Honda S2000, model years 2000–2009. See the version table for each one.

Is the Honda F20C / F22C1 reliable?

Mostly, with known issues to check. YouTube owner tone 48/100, #94 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the middle third); no engine-related recall on record, 2 known issues listed below. Owners filed 107 complaints we can tie to it, about any part of the car. Its cars had 5 recalls (0 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the Honda F20C / F22C1 make?

240 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.

How to read this chart

Why the car page shows more: the S2000 page counts 107 complaints and 2 recall campaigns for 2000–2009, everything NHTSA files as "S2000", other engines included. This page counts only the 107 complaints and 5 recalls we can tie to the Honda F20C / F22C1 by model name or the owner's own words.

How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
