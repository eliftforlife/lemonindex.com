Quick answers

Is the Duramax LLY reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 374 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 7% name the engine, #11 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 16 (4.3%) mention coolant loss or overheating, against 0.8% for its peers. 22 (5.9%) mention an injector, against 1.2% for its peers.

What trucks have the Duramax LLY?

In our data: the Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD/3500HD and GMC Sierra 2500HD/3500HD, model years 2004–2006, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common Duramax LLY problems?

In owner complaints to the government: brakes, electrical, fuel system.

How many recalls name the Duramax LLY?

0 NHTSA recalls on these trucks name it.

How to read this chart
