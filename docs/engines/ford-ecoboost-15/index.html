Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of cars with this engine name much more often than owners of the other 38 everyday engines we track (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • Coolant loss into the cylinders. 2,893 of 5,622 owner complaints (51%) mention coolant loss or overheating, against a median of 1.2% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    Coolant intrusion into the engine causing overheating issue that resulted in $4000 of repair costs and is still not fixed. While driving, the warning light came on to pull over immediately. I was at an intersection at a stop sign. Had to get it towed to the service shop.

    2018 owner · NHTSA complaint 11776538
  • Head gaskets. 261 of 5,622 owner complaints (4.6%) mention a head gasket, against a median of 0.1% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    When I originally attempted to start the car, white smoke was pouring from the exhaust pipe. We came to the conclusion that my head gasket on the car blew. We came to this conclusion because it was misfiring and when you try to open the coolant lid there’s suction.

    2019 owner · NHTSA complaint 11755101
  • Misfires. 805 of 5,622 owner complaints (14%) mention a misfire, against a median of 0.6% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

    Misfire in cylinder 2. Diagnosed as coolant intrusion. Block and header replaced.

    2019 owner · NHTSA complaint 11775546

Recalls that name this engine 2

  • 25V343000 May 2025 Electrical System Escape 2023, 2024, 2025

    Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Lincoln Nautilus, 2023-2025 Escape, 2023 Lincoln Corsair, 2025 Lincoln Corsair, Maverick, and Bronco Sport vehicles equipped with a 1.5L or 2.0L MPC engine and an engine block heater. The engine block heater may overheat when the vehicle is parked with the block heater plugged in.

    Fix. Dealers will replace the block heater element and inspect the electrical cord, replacing it if necessary, free of charge. Owners will also have a alternative option to replace engine block heater element with a threaded blanking plug coupled, and remove the block heater electrical cord. Owner notification letters were mailed June 9, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.

  • 22V859000 Nov 2022 Fuel System, Gasoline Escape 2023

    Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Escape and 2021-2023 Bronco Sport vehicles equipped with 1.5L engines. A fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.

    Fix. Dealers will update the engine control software to include fuel injector leak detection and install a drain tube, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed January 9, 2023. Remedy parts are expected to be available in 1st Quarter of 2023. Owner notification letters were mailed February 28, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service

Quick answers

Is the Ford 1.5 EcoBoost reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car score. Of the 5,622 complaints owners filed about cars with it, 66% name the engine, #1 of the 39 everyday engines we track (1 = the most). 2,893 (51%) mention coolant loss or overheating, against 1.2% for the other engines. 261 (4.6%) mention a head gasket, against 0.1% for the other engines.

What cars have the Ford 1.5 EcoBoost?

In our data: the Ford Escape and Ford Fusion, model years 2014–2025, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 1.5 EcoBoost problems?

In owner complaints to the government: engine, transmission & drivetrain, electrical.
