In everyday cars, matched by VIN

Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of cars with this engine name much more often than owners of the other 38 everyday engines we track (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • The VVT-i oil line and other oil leaks. 127 of 8,680 owner complaints (1.5%) mention an oil leak, against a median of 0.5% for the other 38 everyday engines we track.

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: water pump leaks (41 of 8,680, 0.5%, peers 0.2%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Quick answers

What cars have the Toyota 2GR-FE engine?

In the US: the Lotus Evora / Emira, model years 2010–2025. See the version table for each one.

Is the Toyota 2GR-FE reliable?

Mostly, with known issues to check. YouTube owner tone 53/100, #49 of 103 engines with a YouTube study (in the middle third); 3 engine-related recalls on record. Not enough owner complaints to judge it per car: supercars sell in small numbers. Its cars had 6 recalls (3 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the Toyota 2GR-FE make?

276–416 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.
