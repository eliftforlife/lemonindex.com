How we count: owner complaints only where the car's name and year leave no doubt; YouTube tone from videos that name this engine or its cars. How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

Known issues on record

  • High-pressure fuel pump recall (Recall). On some 2018 LC 500s (and RC F, GS F), a damper in one of the two high-pressure fuel pumps could crack the pump cover and leak fuel. Fix: Dealer replaces the high-pressure fuel pumps for free (NHTSA 18V-107). 4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (USC10 RC F).
  • In-tank fuel pump recall (Recall). The low-pressure fuel pump in the tank of some 2018–2020 LC 500 and LC 500h cars can fail, which can stall the engine while driving. Fix: Dealer fits an improved fuel pump for free (NHTSA 20V-012 and 20V-682). 9 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (URZ100 LC 500, USC10 RC F).
  • Water pump seepage vs. real leak (Maker bulletin). A Lexus bulletin covering 2018–2021 LC 500, LC 500 convertible and LC 500h says dry coolant residue at the pump is normal; wet residue or drips is a real leak. Fix: Replace the water pump only for an active, wet leak.
  • Valley plate coolant leak (Owner-reported). Sealant under the valley plate can fail with heat cycles and leak coolant. Guides tie it mainly to older IS F engines past about 8 years and 80,000 miles, not the LC. Fix: Reseal the valley plate; Jalopnik puts it at about $1,500 to over $3,000.

Quick answers

What cars have the Lexus 2UR-GSE 5.0 V8 engine?

In the US: the Lexus IS F, Lexus RC F, Lexus GS F, Lexus LC 500, Lexus IS 500, model years 2008–2026. See the version table for each one.

Is the Lexus 2UR-GSE 5.0 V8 reliable?

Mostly, if the recalls were done. YouTube owner tone 60/100, #7 of 99 engines with a YouTube study (in the top third); 3 engine-related recalls on record, 4 known issues listed below. Owners filed 31 complaints we can tie to it, about any part of the car. Its cars had 10 recalls (3 about the engine, fuel or powertrain). Have any used car inspected.
