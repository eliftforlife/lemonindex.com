How we count

Owner complaints (NHTSA) are filed by model and year, not engine. We count one for this engine only when the model name and year had no other engine (e.g. "488 GTB", "GT-R"), or when the owner's own words name a version that had only this engine that year (Z06, ZR1, Hellcat, GT500 ...). They cover the whole car (wheels, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine, and none of them is a failure rate. We chart them only for engines with 30 or more, over 3 or more years. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts (praise vs trouble words, the title counts 3 times), weighted by views; a year needs at least 3 videos' worth. A video counts for this engine when its title names the engine or a car that only had this engine in the years it names; a video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of that car, so neighboring years share most of their videos.

NHTSA manufacturer communications (technical service bulletins), matched by model name and year like the complaints, engine-related ones only. A repair note is not a recall: ask a seller whether it was done.

Known issues on record

  • Intercooler pump failure (Enthusiast outlet). The pump that cools the supercharger's intercooler fails often. Intake air heats up and the engine cuts boost. Fix: Replace it with the updated Bosch 010 pump.
  • Supercharger coupler (Parts seller). The plastic coupler that drives the supercharger can crack and break, usually around 80,000–120,000 miles, causing a sudden loss of boost. Fix: Replace it with a billet aluminum coupler.
  • Oil leaks (Enthusiast outlet). The rear main seal and valve cover gaskets harden with age and leak. Fix: Replace the rear main seal before the leak gets bad.

Sources: 8020 Automotive: Mercedes M113 V8 engine problems, reliability and specs, Euro Premium Parts: Mercedes-Benz M113 engine guide, common problems (2026). The same list as on the G63 page.

Quick answers

What cars have the Mercedes-AMG M113K 5.4 supercharged V8 engine?

In the US: the Mercedes-AMG SL55, Mercedes-AMG CL55, Mercedes-AMG S55, Mercedes-AMG E55, Mercedes-AMG G55, Mercedes-AMG CLS55, model years 2003–2011. See the version table for each one.

Is the Mercedes-AMG M113K 5.4 supercharged V8 reliable?

Not one of the safer bets on our data. YouTube owner tone 44/100, #341 of 409 engines with a YouTube study (in the bottom third); no engine-related recall on record, 3 known issues listed below. Owners filed 380 complaints we can tie to it, about any part of the car. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the Mercedes-AMG M113K 5.4 supercharged V8 make?

469–493 hp in factory figures, depending on the car.
