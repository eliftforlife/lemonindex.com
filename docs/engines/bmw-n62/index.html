BMW N62 engine: reliability by year, specs and the cars that have it.

The BMW N62 is BMW's 4.4 L / 4.8 L naturally aspirated V8, made 2001–2010. In the US it was used in the 5 Series, 7 Series, X5 from model years 2002 to 2010, rated 315–362 hp. 7 owner complaints to the government name the N62 in their own words. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2006 (296). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate.

The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2006 (296). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate.

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2008 (47) and least about the 2005 (45); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints in (a) and (b) cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine: see the top complaint areas. Complaints naming "N62" = complaints whose own text mentions the code, about a model and year that had it. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube says = how positive the videos are, 0–100 (higher = more positive), from video titles and transcripts, counted only where the title names the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos; "naming" = how many titles name that year. Under 3 videos' worth shows "not enough videos".

Quick answers

What cars have the BMW N62 engine?

In the US: the 5 Series, 7 Series, X5, model years 2002–2010. See the version table for each one.

Is the N62 reliable?

The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2006 (296). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the N62 make?

BMW rated it at 315 to 362 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.
