Quick answers

What cars have the BMW N62 engine?

In the US: the 5 Series, 7 Series, X5, model years 2002–2010. See the version table for each one.

Is the N62 reliable?

Not one of the safer bets on our data. YouTube owner tone 43/100, #136 of 178 engine pages with a YouTube study (in the bottom third); no engine-related recall on record. The most owner complaints (about any part of the car) filed under version names that used this engine were for the 2006 (296). Counts follow sales, so this is not a failure rate. Government complaints are filed by model and year, not engine, and they cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate. We only credit a number to this engine where the version name or the model year leaves no doubt. Have any used car inspected.

How much horsepower does the N62 make?

BMW rated it at 315 to 362 hp in US versions, depending on the car and year.

How to read this chart

YouTube videos are most positive about the 2006 (46) and least about the 2003 (35); neighboring years share many of the same videos, so small gaps mean little.

Owner score = the whole car's Lemon Index where

How each number is matched to the engine: (a) complaints filed under a version name that used only this engine that year (e.g. "335I"); (b) the model had only this engine that year, so its Lemon Index (complaints per car sold vs the average car) applies to every car with this engine; "all engines combined" = the model had several engines under one name, so its number is not this engine's alone. Owner complaints cover the whole car (brakes, air bags, electrics ...), not only the engine. None of these is a failure rate. YouTube tone = how positive the videos are, 0–100, from titles and transcripts that name the engine or a version that used it. A video whose title names no model year counts toward every year of its generation, so neighboring years share most of their videos.
