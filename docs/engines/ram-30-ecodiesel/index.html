Quick answers

Is the Ram / Jeep 3.0 EcoDiesel V6 reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car score. Of the 2,874 complaints owners filed about cars with it, 50% name the engine, #6 of the 51 everyday engines we track (1 = the most). 142 (4.9%) say the engine knocked, seized or failed, against 2.7% for the other engines.

What cars have the Ram / Jeep 3.0 EcoDiesel V6?

In our data: the Ram pickup, Jeep Grand Cherokee, Jeep Wrangler and Jeep Gladiator, model years 2014–2023, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 3.0 EcoDiesel problems?

In owner complaints to the government: engine, fuel system, transmission & drivetrain.

How many recalls name the 3.0 EcoDiesel?

7 NHTSA recalls on these cars name it.

How we count

How to read this chart

Counts of complaints filed so far. Older cars have had longer to collect them, and popular cars draw more because more were sold: no one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car rate. Compare the shares below instead.

Where this data comes from

Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under each car's own NHTSA names (the same ones its car pages count), all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN with NHTSA's vPIC decoder (displacement, cylinders, engine model, turbo, hybrid) and the engine maker's model years; a VIN that doesn't decode to exactly one engine is left out. Older cars have had more years to collect complaints, and popular cars draw more because more were sold. Source: NHTSA owner complaints (FLAT_CMPL + COMPLAINTS_RECEIVED_2025-2026), received through 20261115; engines: NHTSA vPIC VIN decoder (batch); NHTSA recalls API. Engine names and model years: Chrysler.
