Mitsubishi 6G72 3.0 V6 engine: known problems and recalls.

Mitsubishi's 3.0-liter V6, no turbo. This page covers the versions we have sourced so far: the Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder, model years 2001–2005. The engine is used in other cars too; those aren't covered here yet.

Known issues on record

  • Fuel return hose recall (Recall). On some 2003-2004 Eclipses, Spyders and Galants, a restricted fitting on the fuel pump can build too much fuel pressure and make the return hose leak. Leaking fuel near an ignition source can start a fire. A second 2004 recall covered a badly molded fitting. Fix: Dealer checks fuel pressure and modifies the pump (NHTSA 03V-442), or replaces the pump (NHTSA 04V-312). 3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (3G Spyder (D5xA) Eclipse Spyder).
  • Crank sensor wiring recall (2001) (Recall). On some 2001 Eclipses, Spyders and Galants, the crankshaft position sensor harness can rub on a heat shield and short out. The engine can then die while driving. Fix: Dealer re-routes the harness for free (NHTSA 00V-299). 9 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (3G Spyder (D5xA) Eclipse Spyder).
  • Transmission cooler hose recall (2001) (Recall). On 2001 Spyders with automatics (and 2000-2001 Eclipses and 1999-2001 Galants), a badly made transmission cooler hose can leak fluid. Fluid on the exhaust can smoke. Fix: Dealer replaces the cooler hose assembly (NHTSA 01V-011).
  • No engine failure pattern (Owner-reported (NHTSA)). NHTSA has 79 owner complaints on 2001-2005 Spyders. Most are about the body, wheels, tires and air bags; only a handful mention the engine.

Specs by generation

Mitsubishi 6G72: 3G Spyder (D5xA) Eclipse Spyder, 2001–2005

  • Layout: V6, SOHC 24-valve, cast-iron block, aluminum heads
  • Output: 210 hp at 5,750 rpm, 205 lb-ft at 3,750 rpm (2003-2005)
  • Earlier output: 200 hp at 5,500 rpm, 205 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm (2001-2002)
  • Bore x stroke: 91.1 x 76.0 mm; 10.0:1 compression (9.0:1 before 2003)
  • Redline: 6,200 rpm

Quick answers

What cars have the Mitsubishi 6G72 3.0 V6 engine?

On this site so far: the Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder, model years 2001–2005. It is used in other cars we haven't covered yet.

Is the Mitsubishi 6G72 3.0 V6 reliable?

We can't rate it yet: there's no YouTube owner study for it, and owner complaints are filed by car, not engine. It has 3 engine-related recalls on record, 3 known issues listed below. Owner complaints are filed by car, not engine, and cover the whole car, so none of these numbers is an engine failure rate.

How much horsepower does the Mitsubishi 6G72 3.0 V6 make?

200 to 210 hp in the versions on this page (factory figures).
