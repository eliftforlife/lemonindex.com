Quick answers

Is the Toyota 1GR-FE 4.0 V6 reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-car score. Of the 5,757 complaints owners filed about cars with it, 7% name the engine, #38 of the 39 everyday engines we track (1 = the most).

What cars have the Toyota 1GR-FE 4.0 V6?

In our data: the Toyota Tacoma, Toyota 4Runner, Toyota FJ Cruiser and Toyota Tundra, model years 2003–2024, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 1GR-FE problems?

In owner complaints to the government: body structure, suspension, airbags.

How many recalls name the 1GR-FE?
