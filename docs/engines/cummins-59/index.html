Known issues, checked against owner complaints

Problems owners of trucks with this engine name much more often than owners of other diesel truck generations (at least 1.5× the median, and 10+ complaints):

  • Injectors. 81 of 3,245 owner complaints (2.5%) mention an injector, against a median of 1.2% for the other diesel truck generations we track.

    2007 dodge diesel 3500 truck fuel filter system doesn't work will not stop any thing and kills injectors.

    2007 owner · NHTSA complaint 10294487

Also often talked about, but not standing out in complaints to NHTSA: stalling and power loss (lift pump, VP44 pump on 1999–2002) (163 of 3,245, 5.0%, peers 7.8%). That doesn't mean it never happens, only that owners rarely file it with the government.

Quick answers

Is the Cummins 5.9 (Ram) reliable?

No one reports sales by engine, so there's no per-truck score. Of the 3,245 complaints owners filed about trucks with it, 4% name the engine, #12 of 12 diesel truck engine generations we track (1 = the most). 81 (2.5%) mention an injector, against 1.2% for its peers.

What trucks have the Cummins 5.9 (Ram)?

In our data: the Ram 2500/3500 (Heavy Duty), model years 1999–2007, matched by the VIN in each owner complaint.

What are the most common 5.9 Cummins problems?

In owner complaints to the government: steering, airbags, transmission & drivetrain.

How many recalls name the 5.9 Cummins?

0 NHTSA recalls on these trucks name it.
