Corolla GT-S (US) AE86 · 1985–1987

112horsepower

1.6-liter 4A-GE twin-cam, 112 hp in the US GT-S (130 PS in Japan), 5-speed manual, rear-drive. Built in Japan May 1983 to July 1987.

Why it's famous

  • Initial DAnime

    In the Initial D manga (1995–2013) and anime, Takumi Fujiwara drives his father’s AE86 Sprinter Trueno GT-APEX on early-morning tofu deliveries, then races it on mountain passes. The series kept the car famous with a new generation of fans.

  • The Drift KingDrifting

    Keiichi Tsuchiya learned car control on Japan’s mountain passes in an AE86 Sprinter Trueno and became known as the Drift King. He helped turn drifting into a real motorsport and was a consultant on Initial D. In 2002, Katsuhiro Ueo won the D1 Grand Prix title in an AE86.

  • Touring car titlesRacing

    Chris Hodgetts won the British Saloon Car Championship in an AE86 in 1986 and again in 1987. The AE86 also won the 1986 European Touring Car Championship manufacturers’ title in its class.

  • The 86 nameName

    Toyota says the 86 and GR86 are named in tribute to the AE86, called the hachi-roku (8-6) in Japan and the Corolla GT-S in the US.

Sold new in the US. Sold new in the US as the rear-drive Corolla Sport coupe and liftback for model years 1984 to 1987. The SR5 had the single-cam 4A-C from the start. The twin-cam GT-S, the US version of the Levin and Trueno, arrived for the 1985 model year. Japan built the AE86 from May 1983 to July 1987.

The engine: Toyota 4A-GE

1.6-liter inline-4, DOHC 16-valve, naturally aspirated

LayoutInline-4, DOHC 16-valve, cams driven by a timing belt, forged crankshaft
Output112 hp at 6,600 rpm, 97 lb-ft at 4,800 rpm in the US GT-S; 130 PS (128 hp) at 6,600 rpm in Japan
Size1,587 cc, 81 x 77 mm bore x stroke, 9.4:1 compression
InductionFuel injection with T-VIS, butterfly valves in the intake runners that open at about 4,200 rpm; airflow meter on US cars, pressure (MAP) sensor in Japan
Parts todayToyota’s GR Heritage Parts program has remade some AE86 parts, including new cylinder blocks and heads

Known issues

  • Head gasketOutlet report

    DSPORT magazine says the 4A-GE’s head gaskets are prone to failure. Goodwood’s buyer’s guide still calls the twin-cam mechanically tough.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (1984–1987)
  • Timing belt historyOutlet report

    Both camshafts are driven by a timing belt. JDM Buy Sell’s buyer’s guide says to ask for dated belt records and to treat the belt as due if there are none.

    Fix: Replace the timing belt if there are no dated records.

  • Fake or swapped twin-camsOutlet report

    Goodwood warns about single-cam AE85s passed off as twin-cam AE86s, especially when bought unseen from an importer. JDM Buy Sell says a 4A-GE fitted to an AE85 does not make it an AE86, so check the chassis code and papers against the engine.
