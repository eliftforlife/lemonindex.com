Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the Nissan 240SX SE?

The S14 240SX SE makes 155 hp and 160 lb-ft. Motor Trend measured 0–60 mph in 8.3 seconds.

What engine does the Nissan 240SX (S14) have?

The S14 240SX (1995–1998) uses the Nissan KA24DE, a 2.4-liter inline-4, DOHC, naturally aspirated.

Which Fast and Furious movie is the 240SX in?

The Fast and the Furious (2001): Letty’s 1997 240SX, painted purple (it looks magenta on screen). It runs in the Race Wars drag race, and the film cars had SR20DET engines swapped in.

Was the Nissan 240SX (S14) sold in the US?

Yes. Sold new in the US for model years 1995-1998. US cars had the 2.4-liter KA24DE, not the Japanese Silvia’s SR20DET turbo.
