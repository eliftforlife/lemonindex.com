Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the Toyota Chaser Tourer V?

The X100 (JZX100) Chaser Tourer V makes 276 hp and 280 lb-ft.

What engine does the Toyota Chaser (JZX100) have?

The X100 (JZX100) Chaser (1996–2001) uses the Toyota 1JZ-GTE, a 2.5-liter inline-6, turbo.

Why is the Chaser famous?

D1 Grand Prix champion: Daigo Saito won the 2016 D1 Grand Prix title in a JZX100 Chaser. His first D1 title, in 2008, came in the Mark II version of the same car. The drift sedan: Wikipedia says the Chaser became famous in drifting, mostly because it is front-engined with rear-wheel drive, and many X90 and X100 cars were built into drift cars. Jalopnik says the drift crowd took to it for its JZ engine and rear-wheel drive. Japanese touring car champion: X100 Chasers raced in the Japanese Touring Car Championship in 1997 and 1998. In 1998 the car won the title with Masanori Sekiya and the TOM’S team.

Was the Toyota Chaser (JZX100) sold in the US?

No. Never sold new in the US. JZX100 Chasers began to become legal to import under the 25-year rule in 2021, each car once it is 25 years old by its month of build.
