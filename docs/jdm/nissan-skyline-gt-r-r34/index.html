The engine: Nissan RB26DETT

2.6-liter inline-6, twin-turbo

LayoutInline-6, DOHC 24-valve, iron block, aluminum head, belt-driven cams
Output280 PS (276 hp) at 6,800 rpm, 392 Nm (289 lb-ft) at 4,400 rpm in the R34
Bore x stroke86 x 73.7 mm, 2,568 cc
TurbosTwo turbos in parallel with ceramic turbine wheels; the R34 got ball-bearing units
ThrottlesSix individual throttle bodies instead of one

Known issues

  • Coil packs and misfiresOutlet report

    PistonHeads lists failing coil packs as an R34 weak spot, and names plugs, coil packs and airflow meters as the usual causes of poor running on the R33.

  • Split vacuum linesOutlet report

    PistonHeads lists splitting vacuum lines as one of the R34 engine weak spots.

    Fix: Replace the vacuum lines.

  • Ceramic turbine wheelsOutlet report

    The stock turbos have ceramic turbine wheels, which Wikipedia says are weak at high boost. PistonHeads warns that overspinning a turbo can break the wheel off its shaft, and that a noisy turbo points to worn bearings.

  • Oil pushed past the turbosOutlet report

    PistonHeads says crankcase pressure can push oil past the turbos into the intake, and too much oil in the head can starve the sump and spin a bearing. It says to change the oil at least every 6,000 miles.
