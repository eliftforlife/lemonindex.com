Known issues

  • Oil burning on hard-driven carsOutlet report

    Hagerty's buyer's guide says many of these high-revving engines were driven hard and can burn oil. Look for tailpipe smoke after a long idle and check the dipstick.

  • Timing belt and water pumpOutlet report

    The owners and experts in Hagerty's buyer's guide say the timing belt and water pump should be replaced together every 90,000 miles.

    Fix: Replace the timing belt and water pump together at 90,000 miles.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (1994–2001)
  • Theft and swapped enginesOutlet report

    For a time the 1999 Integra was the most stolen car in America, per Hagerty, and the 1994 Integra was eighth on the NICB's 2010 list. Some GS-R and Type R cars have had their VTEC engines stolen or swapped for non-VTEC ones.

Quick answers

How fast is the Acura Integra GS-R?

The DC2 Integra GS-R makes 170 hp and 128 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 6.9 seconds.

What engine does the Acura Integra GS-R (third generation) have?

The DC2 Integra (1994–2001) uses the Honda B18C1, a 1.8-liter inline-4, DOHC VTEC, naturally aspirated.
