Known issues: Mazda 13B-REW

  • Apex and side seals (low compression)Outlet report

    PistonHeads says a compression test shows whether the apex and side seals are wearing out. Readings of 7 to 8 bar are good; lower is a concern, and a low reading means a rebuild is likely.

    Fix: Rebuild the engine.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (1993–1995)
  • Vacuum hoses (the rat's nest)Outlet report

    The sequential turbos are run by a rat's nest of 67 vacuum hoses, says Driving Line. Sports Car Market says engine heat made the hoses shrivel up and fail.

    Fix: Replace the vacuum hoses with strong synthetic rubber ones.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (1993–1995)
  • Coolant seals and air separator tankOutlet report

    PistonHeads says steam from the exhaust at start-up points to coolant seals going brittle with age and heat. The air separator tank can split and leak coolant.

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (1993–1995)

Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the Mazda RX-7?

The FD (FD3S) RX-7 makes 255 hp and 217 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.3 seconds. Top speed: 159 mph.

What engine does the Mazda RX-7 (FD) have?

The FD (FD3S) RX-7 (1993–1995) uses the Mazda 13B-REW, a 1.3-liter twin-rotor Wankel, sequential twin-turbo.

Which Fast and Furious movie is the RX-7 in?

The Fast and the Furious (2001): Dom drives a red 1993 RX-7 to the opening street race. The film used a privately owned car, repainted red so it would stand out. 2 Fast 2 Furious (2003): Orange Julius races an orange RX-7. One of the first film's RX-7s was reused for it with a new body kit and graphics.

Was the Mazda RX-7 (FD) sold in the US?

Yes. Sold new in the US for the 1993 to 1995 model years; fewer than 14,000 were sold here. Japanese-market FDs were built until 2002 and can be imported once they are 25 years old.
