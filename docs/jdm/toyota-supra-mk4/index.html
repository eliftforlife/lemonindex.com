Known issues

  • Head gasketOutlet report

    The RAC calls blown head gaskets an early Supra bugbear. Signs are slow coolant loss, gurgling behind the dash and a temperature that climbs on hard runs.

    Fix: Replace the head gasket.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (1993–1998)
  • Turbo smokeOutlet report

    The RAC rates the turbos as fairly tough but says to walk away from a car blowing white smoke from the exhaust.

  • Rear differential noiseOutlet report

    Autocar says the diff is very strong but could clonk even when new, mostly going from reverse into first. A whine under load is the bad sign.

  • Tailgate rustOutlet report

    Autocar says the base of the tailgate, just below the rear window, is where Mk IV bodies rust.

Quick answers

How fast is the Toyota Supra Turbo?

The A80 Supra Turbo makes 320 hp and 315 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.6 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (electronically limited).

What engine does the Toyota Supra (Mk IV) have?

The A80 Supra (1993–1998) uses the Toyota 2JZ-GTE, a 3.0-liter inline-6, sequential twin-turbo.

Which Fast and Furious movie is the Supra in?

The Fast and the Furious (2001): Brian brings a run-down orange 1994 Supra to Dom's garage, the crew rebuilds it, and at the end he hands Dom the keys. 2 Fast 2 Furious (2003): Slap Jack drives a gold Supra Turbo. It is the same car Brian had in the first film, redone for the sequel.

Was the Toyota Supra (Mk IV) sold in the US?

Yes. Sold new in the US for the 1993 to 1998 model years. The Turbo was not sold in California-emissions states for 1998.
