Civic Type R EK9 · 1997–2000

182horsepower
140mph top speed

1.6-liter B16B DOHC VTEC, 185 PS (182 hp) at 8,200 rpm and an 8,400 rpm redline, 5-speed manual, front-drive. Japan only.

Why it's famous

  • The first Civic Type RName

    Launched in Japan in August 1997, the EK9 was the first Civic to wear the Type R badge. Every Civic Type R since traces back to it.

  • Initial DAnime

    In Initial D, Daiki Ninomiya of the Todo School drives an EK9 Civic Type R. The school’s Spoon Sports EK9 is the pride of its Honda fleet.

  • 114 bhp per liter, no turboRecords

    The 1.6-liter B16B makes 185 PS, about 114 bhp per liter by evo’s count. Wikipedia calls it one of the highest outputs per liter of all time for an engine without a turbo or supercharger.

  • A race car for the roadName

    Honda gave it a hand-ported engine, a seam-welded body for stiffness, a helical limited-slip differential, red Recaro seats and a titanium gear knob. Its signature color is Championship White.

  • Gran TurismoGamesNot fully confirmed

    Hagerty UK says the EK9 name stands tall with Gran Turismo fans.

Never sold new in the US. Never sold new in the US; the EK9 was sold only in Japan. A car becomes legal to import under the 25-year rule once it is 25 years old from its date of manufacture, so the first EK9s (on sale from August 1997) began to qualify in 2022.

The engine: Honda B16B

1.6-liter inline-4, DOHC VTEC, naturally aspirated

LayoutInline-4, DOHC with VTEC, belt-driven cams; VTEC switches over at 6,100 rpm
Output185 PS (182 hp) at 8,200 rpm, 160 Nm (118 lb-ft) at 7,500 rpm

Redline8,400 rpm (rev limit 8,600 rpm)

Known issues

  • Oil use and blue smokeOutlet report

    evo says some EK9s had a hunger for oil that not all owners kept up with, and to treat puffs of blue smoke as a sign the engine needs serious care. JDM Buy Sell suggests a compression or leak-down test.

  • Timing belt historyOutlet report

    The B16B has belt-driven cams. JDM Buy Sell’s buyer’s guide says to ask for dated proof of the last belt change and have a mechanic inspect the car.

  • Swapped or wrong engineOutlet report

    JDM Buy Sell says to confirm the chassis code reads EK9, the engine is a real B16B and the car still has its helical limited-slip differential.

Quick answers

How fast is the Honda Civic Type R?

The EK9 Civic Type R makes 182 hp and 118 lb-ft. Top speed: 140 mph (225 km/h per Wikipedia; evo estimates 135 mph).

What engine does the Honda Civic Type R (EK9) have?

The EK9 Civic Type R (1997–2000) uses the Honda B16B, a 1.6-liter inline-4, DOHC VTEC, naturally aspirated.
