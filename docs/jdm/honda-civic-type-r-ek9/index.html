Quick answers

How fast is the Honda Civic Type R?

The EK9 Civic Type R makes 182 hp and 118 lb-ft. Top speed: 140 mph (225 km/h per Wikipedia; evo estimates 135 mph).

What engine does the Honda Civic Type R (EK9) have?

The EK9 Civic Type R (1997–2000) uses the Honda B16B, a 1.6-liter inline-4, DOHC VTEC, naturally aspirated.

Why is the Civic Type R famous?

The first Civic Type R: Launched in Japan in August 1997, the EK9 was the first Civic to wear the Type R badge. Every Civic Type R since traces back to it. Initial D: In Initial D, Daiki Ninomiya of the Todo School drives an EK9 Civic Type R. The school’s Spoon Sports EK9 is the pride of its Honda fleet. 114 bhp per liter, no turbo: The 1.6-liter B16B makes 185 PS, about 114 bhp per liter by evo’s count. Wikipedia calls it one of the highest outputs per liter of all time for an engine without a turbo or supercharger.

Was the Honda Civic Type R (EK9) sold in the US?

No. Never sold new in the US; the EK9 was sold only in Japan. A car becomes legal to import under the 25-year rule once it is 25 years old from its date of manufacture, so the first EK9s (on sale from August 1997) began to qualify in 2022.
