Known issues

  • Timing belt (interference engine)Outlet report

    Hagerty says the belt needs replacing every 90,000 miles. The C engines are interference designs, so a snapped belt can let valves hit pistons.

    Fix: Replace the timing belt on schedule.

  • Gearbox snap ring (1991–1992)Outlet report

    Hagerty says some 1991–1992 five-speed cars had bad transmission countershaft snap rings. Ask for proof it was fixed.

    Fix: Replace the snap ring.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (1991–1996)
  • Early ABS unitOutlet report

    Hagerty says some early cars had problems with the ABS control unit.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (1991–1996)
  • Coolant reservoir leaksOutlet report

    Hagerty says the coolant reservoirs had some leak issues.

Quick answers

How fast is the Acura NSX?

The NA2 NSX makes 290 hp and 224 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.8 seconds.

What engine does the Acura NSX (first generation) have?

The NA2 NSX (1997–2005) uses the Honda C32B, a 3.2-liter V6, VTEC, naturally aspirated. Older generations: NA1: Honda C30A.

Which Fast and Furious movie is the NSX in?

2 Fast 2 Furious (2003): Tej Parker's car in the film is a first-generation NSX.

Was the Acura NSX (first generation) sold in the US?

Yes. Sold new in the US as the Acura NSX for the 1991 to 2005 model years.
