Questions

Which Japanese cars are in The Fast and the Furious?

In the 2001 film: Brian O'Conner: Toyota Supra (Mk IV), Dominic Toretto: Mazda RX-7 (FD), Brian O'Conner: Mitsubishi Eclipse (2nd gen), Leon: Nissan Skyline GT-R (R33), Letty: Nissan 240SX (S14), Vince: Nissan Maxima (A32), Johnny Tran: Honda S2000, Edwin: Acura Integra GS-R (third generation), Mia Toretto: Acura Integra GS-R (third generation), Dom Toretto's crew: Honda Civic Coupe (fifth generation).

Which Japanese cars are in 2 Fast 2 Furious?

In the 2003 film: Brian O'Conner: Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34), Brian O'Conner: Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VII, Roman Pearce: Mitsubishi Eclipse Spyder GTS, Suki: Honda S2000, Slap Jack: Toyota Supra (Mk IV), Orange Julius: Mazda RX-7 (FD), Tej Parker: Acura NSX (first generation).

Which of these cars were never sold in the US?

Nissan Skyline GT-R (R33): Never sold new in the US. R33 GT-Rs became legal to import under the 25-year rule from January 2020, each car once it is 25 years old by its month of build. Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34): Never sold new in the US. The first R34 GT-Rs became legal to import under the 25-year rule in January 2024, each car once it is 25 years old by its month of build. Before that, a few came in under the Show or Display exemption. Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VII: Never sold new in the US; the US got its first Evo, the Evo VIII, in 2003. Cars become legal to import under the 25-year rule once 25 years old by build month, so the first Evo VIIs (on sale in Japan from February 2001) qualify from early 2026.

What engine does the Fast and Furious Supra have?

The Mk IV Supra Turbo was built with the Toyota 2JZ-GTE, a 3.0-liter inline-6, sequential twin-turbo, rated 320 hp in the US. The film cars were modified; our numbers are for the stock car.
