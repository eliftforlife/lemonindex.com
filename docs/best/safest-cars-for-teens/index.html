Quick answers

What is the safest used car for a teenager?

By NHTSA crash ratings and owner complaints, the 2018 Volkswagen Jetta ranks first: a 5-star NHTSA overall safety rating for every version NHTSA rated that year and a Lemon Index of 8 (100 is the average car, lower is better).

Why only 2012 and newer?

Electronic stability control, which helps stop skids and rollovers, has been required on every new U.S. passenger vehicle since the 2012 model year (federal standard FMVSS No. 126). Older cars may not have it.

Does this list include prices?

Not yet. We only publish a price when we can cite where it came from, and we don't have a free used-price source for every model year. For budget picks, see the lists of the best used cars under $10,000, $15,000 and $20,000.

How did you pick these cars?

Model years 2012 to 2022 (stability control standard, and 3 full years of owner complaints); a 5-star NHTSA overall crash rating for at least one version; a Lemon Index of 110 or lower (about average or better); no sports or muscle cars (IIHS advises new drivers to stay away from high horsepower) and no cargo vans. Sources and method

The safest used cars for a teen driver, by our data: the 2018 Volkswagen Jetta, the 2022 Nissan Murano and the 2021 Toyota Prius. Each has a 5-star NHTSA crash rating, standard stability control (every 2012 and newer car) and fewer owner complaints than the average car. Sources: NHTSA 5-Star Safety Ratings (overall rating, per model year and version, api.nhtsa.gov); NHTSA owner complaints filed within 3 years of each model year, per vehicle sold; published U.S. sales. Stability control: FMVSS No. 126. 494 model years earned 5 stars for every version tested. Complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect. This is not a safety rating of its own: it combines NHTSA's crash tests with our complaint score.
