Quick answers

What is the most reliable minivan?

By owner complaints per vehicle sold, model years 2010 to 2022, the Toyota Sienna ranks first with a Lemon Index of 57.

Which minivans get the most complaints?

The Chrysler Pacifica and Chrysler Town & Country drew the most complaints per vehicle sold among popular minivans.

Lemon Index: NHTSA owner complaints filed in a model year's first 3 years, per vehicle sold, against the average car. Model years 2010 to 2022, models with 150,000 or more sold. Complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.
