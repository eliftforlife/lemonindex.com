Sources and method

The most reliable trucks by owner complaints per vehicle sold are the Nissan Frontier, Toyota Tacoma and Chevrolet Silverado. A score of 100 is the average car, and lower is better. Lemon Index: NHTSA owner complaints filed in a model year's first 3 years, per vehicle sold, against the average car. Model years 2010 to 2022, models with 150,000 or more sold. Complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.
