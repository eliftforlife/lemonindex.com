Quick answers

What are the best used cars under $5,000?

In 80 YouTube videos on the best used cars under $5,000, the most-picked are the Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla and Toyota Camry. Of the 15 picks below, the BMW 3 Series has the fewest owner complaints.

Where do these picks come from?

From 80 YouTube buyer's guide videos with 23.8M views. A pick counts when the model is in the title or named in the video.

Does this list show prices?

No. These are buyer-guide picks, not prices: check the exact model year and price before you buy.
