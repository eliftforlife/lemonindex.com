Quick answers

What is the best car to buy used?

By the Lemon Score, model years 2010 to 2022, the Honda Fit ranks first with 73 out of 100.

What is the most reliable car?

By owner complaints per vehicle sold alone (the Complaint Score), the Lexus ES has the fewest: a Complaint Score of 27, where 100 is the average car.

Which cars score lowest?

The Chrysler 200 and Ford Fusion have the lowest Lemon Scores among popular cars.

Sources and method

The best cars to buy by the Lemon Score are the Honda Fit, Mazda3 and Toyota Corolla: reliability and safety, cost to own, what YouTube owners say and resale value, out of 100 (higher is better). Lemon Score: reliability and safety 40% (owner complaints per car sold, how serious, recalls, NHTSA crash tests), cost to own 25% (EPA fuel, estimated repairs and insurance), YouTube owners 20%, resale 15% (AI price estimates). Ranked: models with all 4 parts, 150,000 or more sold. How the Lemon Score works.
