Quick answers

What is the most reliable SUV?

By owner complaints per vehicle sold, model years 2010 to 2022, the Toyota 4Runner ranks first with a Lemon Index of 26.

Which SUVs get the most complaints?

The Tesla Model Y and Jeep Cherokee drew the most complaints per vehicle sold among popular SUVs.
