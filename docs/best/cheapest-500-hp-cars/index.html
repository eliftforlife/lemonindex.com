Quick answers

What is the cheapest car with 500 horsepower?

The 2007 Mercedes ML63 AMG: 503 hp for about $8,000–11,500 from a private seller (Lemon Index AI estimate, November 2026). Next are the 2010 Range Rover Sport Supercharged (about $8,500–12,000) and the 2007 Mercedes E63 AMG (about $10,500–14,500).

What is the cheapest 500 hp sedan?

The 2007 Mercedes E63 AMG: 507 hp for about $10,500–14,500.

What is the cheapest 500 hp SUV?

The 2007 Mercedes ML63 AMG: 503 hp for about $8,000–11,500.

What is the cheapest 500 hp coupe?

The 2006 BMW M6: 500+ hp for about $16,000–21,500.

What is the cheapest electric car with 500 horsepower?

The 2015 Tesla Model S P85D: 691 hp combined for about $15,500–20,500. Tesla doesn't publish horsepower; that is the combined motor output Wikipedia lists.

Where do the prices come from?

They are Lemon Index estimates made with AI for November 2026, not listings or an appraisal: a fair private-seller price for the 500 hp version itself, an average car with a clean title at the miles most cars of that year have. Fast cars swing with miles, modifications and how hard they were driven, so check real listings and get an inspection before you buy.

Sources and method

Prices: Lemon Index AI estimates (November 2026) of a fair private-seller price for the 500 hp version itself, at the model year's typical miles, average condition, clean title. Not listings or an appraisal. Where our price table prices that exact version (like the X5 M), the list uses it. Where it only prices the regular model (an ML350, not an ML63 AMG), we estimated the fast version on its own.

Ranking: each car's cheapest model year with 500 hp or more, by the middle of its price range, one entry per car (its cheapest generation). Cars, SUVs, trucks, electric cars and supercars all count.

YouTube score: how the most-watched YouTube videos about that generation talk about it, out of 100 (performance cars only).

Horsepower: factory ratings from the maker's specs, press releases and Wikipedia, the same sources as our car pages. A + is the lowest rating that car's engine had (Wikipedia), so every car of those years makes at least that. Tesla doesn't publish horsepower: its numbers are the combined motor outputs Wikipedia lists.
