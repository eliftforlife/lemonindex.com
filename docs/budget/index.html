Why years and not one model year?

Each pick is a run of model years from one generation, so you can search for any of them near you. Every year in the run scores as well as or better than the average car.

Where do the prices come from?

They are Lemon Index estimates made with AI for November 2026, not listings or an appraisal: a fair private-seller and dealer price for an average car with a clean title at the mileage most cars of those years have. Check real listings and get an inspection before you buy.

How are the picks ranked?

Mostly by reliability: the Lemon Index of those years (owner complaints to NHTSA in the first 3 years, per car sold; 100 is the average car, lower is better). Then by how well YouTube buyer guides and owner reviews rate those years, and a little extra for a longer run of good years. Supercars have too few complaints to score, so they are ranked by YouTube buyer score and price only.
