Quick answers

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 155; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Avalanche year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (42%), steering (10%) and transmission & drivetrain (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche have?

7 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche get?

14 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 19,000 and 55,562 miles. The middle report was at 41,000 miles, from 86 complaints.

Does the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Chevrolet Avalanche have?

5.3L 8-cyl.
