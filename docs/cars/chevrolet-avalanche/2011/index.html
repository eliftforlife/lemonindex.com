How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Chevrolet Avalanche reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Avalanche year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2011 Chevrolet Avalanche problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (41%), body structure (35%) and speed control (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V054000 (airbags), 21V050000 (airbags) and 14V827000 (electrical) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Chevy sent dealers 1558 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Loss of high speed gmlan communications, intermittent no crank, ip gage fluctuation,…”?
