Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Avalanche reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2002, 2003 and 2004.

Is the Chevrolet Avalanche a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2002-2012: Lemon Index 129; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2002 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Avalanche years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2011, 2007. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Avalanche years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2002 (worse than average, Lemon Index 198), 2004 (worse than average, Lemon Index 155), 2003 (worse than average, Lemon Index 130). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Avalanche have?

52 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2003 (7), 2004 (7), 2002 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Avalanche recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Avalanche problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, body structure and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Chevrolet Avalanche years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Avalanche, 2002 to 2013, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2009. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
