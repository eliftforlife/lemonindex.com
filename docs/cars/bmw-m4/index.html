Known issues

  • Crank hubOutlet + parts seller

    The stock crank hub is not keyed and can slip on the crank, throwing off cam timing. BMWBLOG calls it rare but serious; parts sellers say it is common, so how often it happens is disputed.

    Fix: An aftermarket keyed or pinned crank hub is the usual fix.

    8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2020)
  • Oil leaksOutlet

    Gaskets wear with age. BMWBLOG names the valve cover, oil pan and oil filter housing gaskets as common leak points.

    Fix: Replace the leaking gasket.

  • Carbon buildupOutlet

    Direct injection lets carbon collect on the intake valves over time, which can cause rough idle and misfires.

    Fix: Clean the intake valves at intervals.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2015–2020)

Quick answers

How fast is the BMW M4 Competition?

The G82 M4 Competition makes 503 hp and 479 lb-ft. The maker quotes 0–60 mph in 3.8 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (limited; 180 with M Driver’s Package).

What engine does the BMW M4 have?

The G82 M4 (2021–2025) uses the BMW S58, a 3.0-liter inline-6, twin-turbo. Older generations: F82: BMW S55.

Which BMW M4 engine do YouTube owners like most?

BMW S58 56/100, BMW S55 51/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
