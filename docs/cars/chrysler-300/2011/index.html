Quick answers

Is the 2011 Chrysler 300 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 169; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 300 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (64%), engine (12%) and lights (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Chrysler 300 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Chrysler 300 get?

18 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
