Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chrysler 300 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 145; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 300 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (56%), engine (16%) and airbags (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chrysler 300 have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chrysler 300 get?

17 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Chrysler 300 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Chrysler 300 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 42,100 and 83,800 miles. The middle report was at 65,000 miles, from 300 complaints.

Does the 2012 Chrysler 300 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Chrysler 300 cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Chrysler 300 have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Chrysler 300 year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2017.
