Quick answers

Is the 2005 Chrysler 300 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 123; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 300 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2005 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (18%), suspension (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Chrysler 300 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Chrysler 300 get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Chrysler 300 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 36,400 and 116,000 miles. The middle report was at 76,000 miles, from 765 complaints.

Does the 2005 Chrysler 300 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Chrysler 300 cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Chrysler 300 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.

What Chrysler 300 year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2017.
