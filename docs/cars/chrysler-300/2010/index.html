Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chrysler 300 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 32; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), engine (14%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chrysler 300 get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Chrysler 300 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 47,000 and 97,000 miles. The middle report was at 70,200 miles, from 63 complaints.

Does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Chrysler 300 cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.
