Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chrysler 300 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 32; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), airbags (14%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chrysler 300 get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Chrysler 300 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 47,000 and 97,000 miles (76,000–156,000 km) (the middle half of the 63 complaints, of all 123 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Chrysler 300 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $5,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Chrysler 300 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Chrysler 300 year than the 2010?

Not nearby: the 2010 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Chrysler 300 years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V018000 (airbags), 18V021000 (airbags) and 16V352000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Chrysler sent dealers 81 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Drivers And/Or Passenger Front Power Door Locks And Windows Inoperative U0200 Lost Communication”?
