Quick answers

Is the 2006 Chrysler 300 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 94; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 300 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2006 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (19%), engine (16%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Chrysler 300 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Chrysler 300 get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
