Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 300 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Chrysler 300 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 123; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Chrysler 300 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2016, 2017. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Chrysler 300 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2013, 2011.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 300 have?

84 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2012 (9), 2014 (8), 2005 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler 300 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
