Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 300 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Chrysler 300 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 123; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Chrysler 300 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2016, 2017. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Chrysler 300 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 236), 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 231), 2011 (worse than average, Lemon Index 169, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 300 have?

84 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2012 (9), 2014 (8), 2005 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Chrysler 300 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chrysler 300 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 13 Chrysler 300 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Chrysler 300, 2005 to 2023, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
