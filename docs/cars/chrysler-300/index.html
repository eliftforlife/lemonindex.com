Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 300 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints. Best year: 2010. Avoid 2011, 2013 and 2014.

Is the Chrysler 300 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 123; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Chrysler 300 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2016, 2017.

What Chrysler 300 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2013, 2011.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 300 have?

77 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2012 (9), 2014 (8), 2005 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler 300 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
