How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Chrysler 300 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 55; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chrysler 300 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2008 Chrysler 300 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (29%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Chrysler 300 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Chrysler 300 get?

18 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Chrysler 300 problems show up?

How much does the 2008 Chrysler 300 cost in fuel a year?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V352000 (airbags), 15V313000 (airbags) and 14V567000 (electrical) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Chrysler sent dealers 81 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Drivers And/Or Passenger Front Power Door Locks And Windows Inoperative U0200 Lost Communication”?
