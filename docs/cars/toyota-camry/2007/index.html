Quick answers

Is the 2007 Toyota Camry reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 252; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Camry year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2007 Toyota Camry problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (39%), transmission & drivetrain (22%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Toyota Camry have?

10 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Toyota Camry get?

21 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Toyota Camry problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 32,300 and 104,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,000 miles, from 2,776 complaints.

Does the 2007 Toyota Camry have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Toyota Camry cost in fuel a year?

About $1900 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Toyota Camry have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Camry year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2017 and 2016.
