Questions to ask the seller

7 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 16V396000 (seats), 15V689000 (visibility) and 14V743000 (seats) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done? (149 owners named a brakes repair bill; the middle one was $2,600.)
  6. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (55 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $2,000.)
  7. Toyota sent dealers 402 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “2gr-fe engine oil leak from front timing cover”?

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Toyota Camry reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 252; 100 is average, lower is better). 2007 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about half as many complaints as the average car. The Toyota Camry year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2007 Toyota Camry problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (39%), transmission & drivetrain (22%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Toyota Camry have?

10 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Toyota Camry get?

21 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Toyota Camry problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 32,000 and 104,000 miles (52,000–167,000 km) (the middle half of the 2,776 complaints, of all 3,615 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Toyota Camry have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Toyota Camry cost in fuel a year?

About $1,900 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Toyota Camry have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Camry year is better than the 2007?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.
