Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Camry reliable?

Yes, very. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2007, 2009 and 2018.

Is the Toyota Camry a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 49; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2007 the most.

What are the best Toyota Camry years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2017, 2016. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Camry years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2007 (avoid, Lemon Index 252, the first year of a new generation), 2009 (worse than average, Lemon Index 172), 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 118, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Camry have?

111 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2007 (10), 2009 (10), 2010 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Camry recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Camry problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Toyota Camry repairs cost?

The repairs Toyota Camry owners reported paying for most: ABS module replacement (middle bill $2,600, 76 reports), brake repair (middle bill $958, 40 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $4,600, 29 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 29 Toyota Camry years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 1,182 YouTube videos about the Toyota Camry, 1997 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
