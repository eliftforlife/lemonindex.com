Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Camry reliable?

Yes, very. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2018.

Is the Toyota Camry a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 49; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Toyota Camry years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2017, 2016. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Camry years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 118, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 82), 2012 (better than average, Lemon Index 66, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Camry have?

111 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2007 (10), 2009 (10), 2010 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Camry problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
