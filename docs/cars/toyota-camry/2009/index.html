2009 Toyota Camry.

The 2009 Toyota Camry is worse than average by owner complaints: 496 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 172, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about speed control, it has 10 recalls, and it gets 23 to 34 mpg combined. The best Toyota Camry year is 2022.

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Toyota Camry reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 172; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Camry year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2009 Toyota Camry problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (48%), brakes (8%) and suspension (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Toyota Camry have?

10 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Toyota Camry get?

23 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Toyota Camry problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 23,000 and 93,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,900 miles, from 1,031 complaints.

Does the 2009 Toyota Camry have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Toyota Camry cost in fuel a year?

About $1900 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Toyota Camry have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Camry year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2017 and 2016.
