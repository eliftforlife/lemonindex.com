Toyota Camry transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2007 (242 complaints), 2018 (171 complaints), 2019 (100 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2017, 2024.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Toyota Camry years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Toyota Camry years are 2007, 2018, 2019.

What are common Toyota Camry transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, hard or jerky shifting, shudder.

Which Toyota Camry years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Toyota Camry years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2017, 2024.
