Quick answers

Is the 2014 Toyota Camry reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Camry year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2014 Toyota Camry problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (19%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Toyota Camry have?

3 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Toyota Camry get?

25 to 40 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Toyota Camry safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Toyota Camry problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 8,000 and 78,000 miles. The middle report was at 36,000 miles, from 321 complaints.

Does the 2014 Toyota Camry have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Toyota Camry cost in fuel a year?

About $1600 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Toyota Camry have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Camry year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2017 and 2016.
