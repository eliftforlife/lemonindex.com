When problems show up

Half of the Mazda CX-5 complaints that listed a mileage came before 30,900 miles. Most came between 10,000 miles and 46,600 miles.

The top problem area, lights, usually showed up between 31,000 miles and 55,500 miles.

Recalls: 5

  • lights 2020
    The LED Daytime Running Lights (DRL) may fail while driving. NHTSA 20V063000
  • body structure 2016
    On the affected vehicles, the lift gate support struts may corrode, possibly causing the struts to break and/or the lift gate to drop unexpectedly. NHTSA 16V644000
  • steering 2016
    The lower mounting nuts and bolts that attach the front strut assembly to the steering knuckle may loosen, allowing the steering knuckle to separate. NHTSA 16V203000
  • fuel system, gasoline 2016
    The affected vehicles have a fuel filler pipe that may rupture in the event of a crash. NHTSA 16V064000
  • visibility 2016
    The affected windshields have a bracket mounted on the inside to hold a laser sensor or LIDAR device for the Smart City Brake Support system. NHTSA 16E002000
