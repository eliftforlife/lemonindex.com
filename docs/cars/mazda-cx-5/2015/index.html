Quick answers

Is the 2015 Mazda CX-5 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 79; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda CX-5 year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2015 Mazda CX-5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (28%), speed control (12%) and brakes (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Mazda CX-5 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Mazda CX-5 get?

26 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
