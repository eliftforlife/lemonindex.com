Quick answers

Is the 2019 Mazda CX-5 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda CX-5 year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Mazda CX-5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (18%), engine (16%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 have?

2 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 get?

24 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Mazda CX-5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Mazda CX-5 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 2,500 and 38,000 miles. The middle report was at 17,000 miles, from 43 complaints.

Does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $3350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 have?

2.2L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Mazda CX-5 year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2017.
