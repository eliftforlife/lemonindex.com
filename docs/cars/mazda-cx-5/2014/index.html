Recalls 4

  • body structure 2016

    On the affected vehicles, the lift gate support struts may corrode, possibly causing the struts to break and/or the lift gate to drop unexpectedly.

  • steering 2016

    The lower mounting nuts and bolts that attach the front strut assembly to the steering knuckle may loosen, allowing the steering knuckle to separate.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2016

    The affected vehicles have a fuel filler pipe that may rupture in the event of a crash.
