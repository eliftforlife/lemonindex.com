Quick answers

Is the 2023 Mazda CX-5 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.9× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 34; 100 is average). The Mazda CX-5 year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2023 Mazda CX-5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (17%), engine (16%) and lights (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Mazda CX-5 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Mazda CX-5 get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Mazda CX-5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Mazda CX-5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Mazda CX-5 cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Mazda CX-5 have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Mazda CX-5 year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (4.2× fewer complaints than average).

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
