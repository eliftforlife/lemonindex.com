Ford Focus transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2014 (1,441 complaints), 2012 (1,286 complaints), 2013 (974 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2011, 2010, 2018.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Ford Focus years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Focus years are 2014, 2012, 2013.

What are common Ford Focus transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving.

Which Ford Focus years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Focus years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2011, 2010, 2018.
