Quick answers

Is the 2013 Ford Focus reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 314; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Focus year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2013 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (61%), engine (18%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Ford Focus have?

12 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
