The 2008 Ford Focus is one of the most reliable years by owner complaints: 72 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 37, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about tires, it has 2 recalls, and it gets 28 mpg combined. The best Ford Focus year is 2011.

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Ford Focus reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 37; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Focus year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2008 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (19%), doors, locks & latches (15%) and suspension (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Ford Focus have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Ford Focus get?

28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Ford Focus problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 32,000 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 61,600 miles, from 199 complaints.

Does the 2008 Ford Focus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Ford Focus cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Ford Focus have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Ford Focus year is better than the 2008?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2010 and 2015.
