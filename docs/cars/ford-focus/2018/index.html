When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 34,000 and 80,000 miles (56,000–129,000 km) (the middle half of the 423 complaints, of all 837 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles (161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: transmission & drivetrain mostly 30,000–83,000 miles (48,000–134,000 km); engine mostly 33,000–71,000 miles (52,000–114,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V369000 (fuel system, gasoline), 26V376000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 26V011000 (equipment) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into timing belt failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (120 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $2,000.)
  7. Ford sent dealers 107 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “MIL On with DTC's P0300 and/or P0316 store in Powertrain Control”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Ford Focus reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 167; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Focus year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2018 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (39%), fuel or electric drive system (26%) and engine (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Ford Focus have?

7 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2018 Ford Focus safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Ford Focus problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 34,000 and 80,000 miles (56,000–129,000 km) (the middle half of the 423 complaints, of all 837 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2018 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Ford Focus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 39% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Ford Focus cost in fuel a year?

About $700 to $3,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Ford Focus year is better than the 2018?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).
