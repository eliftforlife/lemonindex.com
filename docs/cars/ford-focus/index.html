Quick answers.

Is the Ford Focus reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2001, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Ford Focus a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2018: Complaint Score 227; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Ford Focus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2008, 2006. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Ford Focus years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012 (avoid, Complaint Score 413, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (avoid, Complaint Score 380), 2001 (avoid, Complaint Score 353). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Focus have?

100 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2001 (14), 2013 (12), 2014 (12). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Focus recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Focus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Focus repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Focus owners reported paying for most: clutch replacement (middle bill $2,100, 88 reports), steering rack replacement (middle bill $2,000, 66 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $700, 34 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 19 Ford Focus years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 588 YouTube videos about the Ford Focus, 2000 to 2018, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2012. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
