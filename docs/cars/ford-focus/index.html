Quick answers.

Is the Ford Focus reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2012, 2013 and 2014.

Is the Ford Focus a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2018: Lemon Index 227; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Ford Focus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2010. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Ford Focus years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 413, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 380), 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 314). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Focus have?

100 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2001 (14), 2013 (12), 2014 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Focus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
