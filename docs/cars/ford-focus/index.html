Quick answers.

Is the Ford Focus reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.3× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2001, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Ford Focus a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2018: Lemon Index 227; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Ford Focus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2008, 2006.

What Ford Focus years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012, 2014, 2001.

How many recalls does the Ford Focus have?

100 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2001 (14), 2013 (12), 2014 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Focus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
