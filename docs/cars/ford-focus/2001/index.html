2001 Ford Focus: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Ford Focus drew 3.5× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 991 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 264,414 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Focus complaints that listed a mileage came before 58,000 miles. Most came between 37,000 miles and 93,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 41,300 miles and 94,100 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
