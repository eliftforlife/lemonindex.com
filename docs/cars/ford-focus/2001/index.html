  • Slight engine vibration at idle, most noticeable when engaged in reverse

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2001 Ford Focus reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 353; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Focus year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2001 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), engine (18%) and fuel system, gasoline (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Ford Focus have?

14 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Ford Focus get?

24 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
