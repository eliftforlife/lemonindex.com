Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford Focus reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 195; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Focus year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2016 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (52%), engine (21%) and fuel or ev drive system (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford Focus have?

10 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
