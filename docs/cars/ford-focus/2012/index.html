Quick answers

Is the 2012 Ford Focus reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 413; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Focus year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2012 Ford Focus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (54%), steering (17%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Ford Focus have?

8 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2012 Ford Focus safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Ford Focus problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 38,000 and 96,000 miles. The middle report was at 67,000 miles, from 3,125 complaints.

Does the 2012 Ford Focus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 54% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Ford Focus cost in fuel a year?

About $700 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Ford Focus year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2010 and 2015.
