2006 Ford Focus: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Ford Focus drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 65 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 177,006 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Focus complaints that listed a mileage came before 59,900 miles. Most came between 32,500 miles and 94,400 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 37,600 miles and 79,600 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
