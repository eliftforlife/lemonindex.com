Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Armada reliable?

Mostly. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2004, 2005 and 2006.

Is the Nissan Armada a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2019: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2004 the most.

What are the best Nissan Armada years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2018. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Armada years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2004 (avoid, Lemon Index 390), 2005 (avoid, Lemon Index 216), 2006 (worse than average, Lemon Index 135). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Armada have?

51 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2008 (7), 2005 (5), 2006 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Nissan Armada recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Nissan Armada problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, electrical and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Nissan Armada repairs cost?

The repairs Nissan Armada owners reported paying for most: brake booster replacement (middle bill $991, 28 reports), brake repair (middle bill $400, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 16 Nissan Armada years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Nissan Armada, 2004 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
