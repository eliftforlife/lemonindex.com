Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Palisade reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.5× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Even its best year, 2022, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2020 and 2021.

Is the Hyundai Palisade a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2020-2022: Lemon Index 251; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Hyundai Palisade years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2021, 2020.

What Hyundai Palisade years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2021, 2022.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Palisade have?

29 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2021 (6), 2020 (5), 2022 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Palisade problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, seat belts and wipers & visibility most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
