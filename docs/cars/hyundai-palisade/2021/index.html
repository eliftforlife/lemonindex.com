Quick answers

Is the 2021 Hyundai Palisade reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 247; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Palisade year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2021 Hyundai Palisade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (19%), seat belts (17%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Hyundai Palisade have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Hyundai Palisade get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
