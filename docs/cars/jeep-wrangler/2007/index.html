2007 Jeep Wrangler: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Jeep Wrangler drew 7.3× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 775 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 119,243 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Jeep Wrangler complaints that listed a mileage came before 40,000 miles. Most came between 16,400 miles and 67,000 miles.

The top problem area, fuel system, gasoline, usually showed up between 25,500 miles and 51,000 miles.
