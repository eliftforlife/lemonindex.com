Quick answers

Is the 2006 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 163; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Wrangler year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2006 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (18%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and fuel system, gasoline (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Jeep Wrangler have?

3 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Jeep Wrangler get?

14 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Jeep Wrangler problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 20,000 and 53,700 miles. The middle report was at 36,000 miles, from 624 complaints.

Does the 2006 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Jeep Wrangler have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Wrangler year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2017 and 2011.
