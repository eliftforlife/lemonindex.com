When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 18,000 and 125,000 miles (28,000–201,000 km) (the middle half of the 80 complaints, of all 108 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles (241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: body structure mostly 60,000–148,000 miles (97,000–238,000 km); suspension mostly 44,000–150,000 miles (71,000–241,000 km).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 06E026000 (lights), 06E022000 (brakes) and 06E011000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (14 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $600.)
  5. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced? (7 owners named a suspension repair bill; the middle one was $800.)
  6. Jeep sent dealers 43 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Automatic transmission fluid usage atf+4”?

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 69; 100 is average, lower is better). 2002 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.9× the average car's complaints. The Jeep Wrangler year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2002 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (24%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and tires (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Jeep Wrangler have?

6 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Jeep Wrangler get?

14 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Jeep Wrangler problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 18,000 and 125,000 miles (28,000–201,000 km) (the middle half of the 80 complaints, of all 108 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2002 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2002 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2002 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2002 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $3,800 to $4,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Jeep Wrangler have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Jeep Wrangler year than the 2002?

Not nearby: the 2002 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Jeep Wrangler years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest.
