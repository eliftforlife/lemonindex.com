Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Wrangler reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.9× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2015. Avoid 2007, 2008 and 2018.

Is the Jeep Wrangler a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 195; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2015 and the worst is 2007.

What are the best Jeep Wrangler years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2002, 2017.

What Jeep Wrangler years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2007, 2008, 2018.

How many recalls does the Jeep Wrangler have?

212 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (15), 2022 (15), 2018 (14). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Wrangler problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
