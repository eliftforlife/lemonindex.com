Quick answers

Is the 2016 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 85; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Wrangler year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2016 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (22%), airbags (21%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Jeep Wrangler have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Jeep Wrangler get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
