Jeep Wrangler transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2008 (125 complaints), 2007 (96 complaints), 2000 (66 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2015, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Jeep Wrangler years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Jeep Wrangler years are 2008, 2007, 2000.

What are common Jeep Wrangler transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, slipping, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Jeep Wrangler years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Jeep Wrangler years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2015, 2017.
