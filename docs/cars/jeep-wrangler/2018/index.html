Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V676000 (back over prevention), 24V652000 (electrical) and 23V116000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 14 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Jeep sent dealers 717 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Air Suspension Components Star Parts Catalog has been updated to show individual service parts…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10162561: “Flash: Powertrain Control Module (PCM) Updates This bulletin involves reprogramming the PCM with the latest available…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10166588: “Radio and Back-up Camera Enhancements This bulletin involves updating the radio software level to 1848.24”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 523; 100 is average, lower is better). The Jeep Wrangler year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2018 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (73%), suspension (22%) and electrical (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Jeep Wrangler have?

14 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Jeep Wrangler get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2018 Jeep Wrangler problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,100 and 16,000 miles (the middle half of the 1,200 complaints, of all 1,827 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 6% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Jeep Wrangler have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Wrangler year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (1.6× fewer complaints than average).
