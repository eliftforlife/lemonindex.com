Quick answers

Is the 2011 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 84; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Wrangler year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2011 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (18%), steering (18%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Jeep Wrangler have?

11 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Jeep Wrangler get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2011 Jeep Wrangler problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 80,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,500 miles, from 366 complaints.

Does the 2011 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Jeep Wrangler have?

3.8L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Wrangler year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2017 and 2011.
