2021 Jeep Wrangler.

The 2021 Jeep Wrangler is one of the worst years by owner complaints: 663 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 336, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about electrical, it has 15 recalls, and it gets 14 to 25 mpg combined. The best Jeep Wrangler year is 2015.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 336; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Wrangler year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2021 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), steering (29%) and engine (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Jeep Wrangler have?

15 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Jeep Wrangler get?

14 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2021 Jeep Wrangler problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 6,400 and 37,200 miles. The middle report was at 20,000 miles, from 124 complaints.

Does the 2021 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Jeep Wrangler have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Jeep Wrangler year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2017 and 2011.
