Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V363000 (steering), 26V327000 (back over prevention) and 25V766000 (engine) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (10 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $750.)
  6. Jeep sent dealers 311 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Turbocharger Kit Please note many parts of the Turbo Assembly can be serviced separately and…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Jeep Wrangler reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 79; 100 is average). 2025 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.9× the average car's complaints. The Jeep Wrangler year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2025 Jeep Wrangler problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (59%), engine (36%) and fuel system (30%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Jeep Wrangler have?

6 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Jeep Wrangler get?

14 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2025 Jeep Wrangler have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Jeep Wrangler cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $5,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Jeep Wrangler have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Jeep Wrangler year is better than the 2025?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car).
