Quick answers

Is the 2022 Tesla Model Y reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 272; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (86%), speed control (17%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Tesla Model Y have?

20 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
