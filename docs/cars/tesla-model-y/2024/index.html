Quick answers

Is the 2024 Tesla Model Y reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 94; 100 is average). The best Tesla Model Y year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2024 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (53%), backup camera (21%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Tesla Model Y have?

5 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
