Tesla Model Y transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2020 (3 complaints), 2021 (7 complaints), 2022 (5 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2024, 2023, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Tesla Model Y years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Tesla Model Y years are 2020, 2021, 2022.

What are common Tesla Model Y transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, hard or jerky shifting, rolled away or park trouble.

Which Tesla Model Y years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Tesla Model Y years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2023, 2022.
