Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V507000 (lights), 26V283000 (back over prevention) and 24V935000 (tires) done? It has 24 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into fsd collisions in reduced roadway visibility conditions. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (28 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  7. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (10 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $3,100.)

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Tesla Model Y reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 298; 100 is average, lower is better). The Tesla Model Y year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (56%), brakes (16%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Tesla Model Y have?

24 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2020 Tesla Model Y safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Tesla Model Y problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,000 and 22,000 miles (1,600–35,000 km) (the middle half of the 52 complaints, of all 282 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Tesla Model Y have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Tesla Model Y cost in fuel a year?

About $650 to $700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Tesla Model Y year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about 3 times the average car's complaints).
