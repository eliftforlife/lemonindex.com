Quick answers

Is the 2020 Tesla Model Y reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 298; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Tesla Model Y year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (56%), brakes (16%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Tesla Model Y have?

24 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
