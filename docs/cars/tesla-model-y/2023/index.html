When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,000 and 26,000 miles (3,200–42,000 km) (the middle half of the 82 complaints, of all 1,182 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K, 100K and 150K miles (97K, 161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: automatic emergency braking mostly 1,000–5,000 miles (1,600–8,000 km); electrical mostly 1,000–15,000 miles (1,600–24,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V507000 (lights), 26V283000 (back over prevention) and 25V092000 (steering) done? It has 17 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into fsd collisions in reduced roadway visibility conditions. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (28 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  7. Tesla sent dealers 91 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some Model 3 and Model Y vehicles, the steering wheel cover material may separate from the…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2023 Tesla Model Y reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about 3 times the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 258; 100 is average). The Tesla Model Y year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2023 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (54%), steering (18%) and speed control (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Tesla Model Y have?

17 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2023 Tesla Model Y safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 Tesla Model Y problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,000 and 26,000 miles (3,200–42,000 km) (the middle half of the 82 complaints, of all 1,182 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2023 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2023 Tesla Model Y have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Tesla Model Y cost in fuel a year?

About $600 to $700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.
