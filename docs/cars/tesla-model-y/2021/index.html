Quick answers

Is the 2021 Tesla Model Y reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 533; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Tesla Model Y year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2021 Tesla Model Y problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (90%), brakes (19%) and speed control (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Tesla Model Y have?

23 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2021 Tesla Model Y safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Tesla Model Y problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 600 and 27,100 miles. The middle report was at 3,800 miles, from 100 complaints.

Does the 2021 Tesla Model Y have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Tesla Model Y cost in fuel a year?

About $600 to $700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Tesla Model Y year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2021.
