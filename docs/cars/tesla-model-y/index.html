Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Tesla Model Y reliable?

Not really. It draws 3.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2022, still drew 2.7× the average.

Is the Tesla Model Y a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2020-2022: Lemon Index 359; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2021 the most.

What are the best Tesla Model Y years to buy?

Only 3 Tesla Model Y years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2022 drew the fewest complaints (about 3 times the average car's complaints).

What Tesla Model Y years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Tesla Model Y years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Tesla Model Y have?

92 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2020 (24), 2021 (23), 2022 (20). Each year's page lists them, and every Tesla Model Y recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Tesla Model Y problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name automatic emergency braking, speed control and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Tesla Model Y years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Two scores, two windows: Lemon Index 359 for model years 2020–2022 (complaints in each car's first 3 years) on this page; EV score 344 for model years 2021–2024 (complaints filed by the end of the year after the model year) on its EV page. Different model years and windows, so the numbers differ.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 197 YouTube videos about the Tesla Model Y, 2020 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
