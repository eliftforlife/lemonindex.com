Quick answers.

Is the Tesla Model Y reliable?

Not really. It draws 3.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Even its best year, 2022, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2020 and 2021.

Is the Tesla Model Y a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2020-2022: Lemon Index 359; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2021.

What are the best Tesla Model Y years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2020, 2021.

What Tesla Model Y years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2021, 2020, 2022.

How many recalls does the Tesla Model Y have?

92 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2020 (24), 2021 (23), 2022 (20). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Tesla Model Y problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name automatic emergency braking, speed control and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
