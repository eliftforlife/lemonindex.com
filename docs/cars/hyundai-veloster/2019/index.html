2019 Hyundai Veloster Problems, recalls and years to avoid

All 10 yearsCompare

Is the 2019 Hyundai Veloster reliable? Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 71; 100 is average, lower is better). 2019 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Hyundai Veloster year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2014.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Hyundai Veloster reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 71; 100 is average, lower is better). 2019 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Hyundai Veloster year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2014.

What are the most common 2019 Hyundai Veloster problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (47%), engine (24%) and wipers & visibility (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Hyundai Veloster have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Hyundai Veloster get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2019 Hyundai Veloster have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Hyundai Veloster cost in fuel a year?

About $2,000 to $3,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Hyundai Veloster have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Hyundai Veloster year than the 2019?

Not nearby: the 2019 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Hyundai Veloster years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V301000 (engine) and 19V311000 (latches/locks/linkages) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  5. Hyundai sent dealers 144 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “If you are servicing an applicable vehicle with a ?Check Engine?”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 399 YouTube videos about the Hyundai Veloster, 2012 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
