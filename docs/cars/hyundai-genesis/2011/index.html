How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Hyundai Genesis reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Genesis year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 Hyundai Genesis problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (32%), brakes (16%) and lights (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Hyundai Genesis have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Hyundai Genesis get?

20 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2011 Hyundai Genesis problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 6,000 and 59,000 miles (9,700–95,000 km) (the middle half of the 22 complaints, of all 25 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2011 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2011 Hyundai Genesis have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2011 Hyundai Genesis cost in fuel a year?

About $2,850 to $3,750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Hyundai Genesis have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.8L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

What Hyundai Genesis year is better than the 2011?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V713000 (lights), 13V489000 (brakes) and 13V113000 (lights) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
