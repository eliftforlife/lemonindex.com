Quick answers

Is the 2013 Hyundai Genesis reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 152; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Genesis year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2013 Hyundai Genesis problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (34%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Hyundai Genesis have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Hyundai Genesis get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2013 Hyundai Genesis problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,200 and 28,000 miles (3,500–45,000 km) (the middle half of the 55 complaints, of all 62 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2013 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2013 Hyundai Genesis have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Hyundai Genesis cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $3,950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Hyundai Genesis have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.8L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Hyundai Genesis year is better than the 2013?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car).
