Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Genesis reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2012, 2013 and 2015.

Is the Hyundai Genesis a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2016: Lemon Index 123; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Genesis years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2011, 2016. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Hyundai Genesis years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012 (worse than average, Lemon Index 165), 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 152), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 147). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Genesis have?

22 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2016 (4), 2009 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Hyundai Genesis recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Hyundai Genesis problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and lights most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
