How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Hyundai Genesis reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 74; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Genesis year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2016 Hyundai Genesis problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), electrical (26%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Hyundai Genesis have?

4 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Hyundai Genesis get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Hyundai Genesis safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2016 Hyundai Genesis problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 3,200 and 18,000 miles (5,100–30,000 km) (the middle half of the 22 complaints, of all 27 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2016 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2016 Hyundai Genesis have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Hyundai Genesis cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Hyundai Genesis have?

3.8L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Hyundai Genesis year than the 2016?

Not nearby: the 2016 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Hyundai Genesis years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V664000 (seats), 24V107000 (electrical) and 21V160000 (brakes) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into hyundai and kia abs module fires. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 83 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “If you are servicing a vehicle with a ?Check Engine light?”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 362 YouTube videos about the Hyundai Genesis, 2009 to 2016, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2016. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
