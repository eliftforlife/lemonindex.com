Known issues

  • Ringland and piston failureParts seller / enthusiast outlet

    Detonation can crack a piston ring land, causing low compression, blow-by and oil burning. Parts sellers say it happens on stock cars too, more often with bad tunes, low octane or full throttle at low rpm.

    Fix: Rebuild with new pistons; Subaru switched to stronger pistons for 2019.

  • Bearing failure from oil starvationOwner-reported

    SlashGear, citing a YouTube mechanic, names oil starvation of the second and third bearings as the main EJ257 problem, which can end in bearing failure.

  • Head gasket leaksOutlet

    Head gasket failures happen on the EJ257 but less often than on other EJ engines, and are more likely with a higher-boost tune, per SlashGear.

  • Oil consumptionEnthusiast outlet

    A cracked ring land lets combustion gas pressurize the crankcase, which burns oil. Rising oil use can be an early warning, so check the level often.

Known issues

  • Ringland and piston failureParts seller / enthusiast outlet

    Detonation can crack a piston ring land, causing low compression, blow-by and oil burning. Parts sellers say it happens on stock cars too, more often with bad tunes, low octane or full throttle at low rpm.

  • Bearing failure from oil starvationOwner-reported

    SlashGear, citing a YouTube mechanic, names oil starvation of the second and third bearings as the main EJ257 problem, which can end in bearing failure.

    10 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2014)
  • Head gasket leaksOutlet

    Head gasket failures happen on the EJ257 but less often than on other EJ engines, and are more likely with a higher-boost tune, per SlashGear.

    A cracked ring land lets combustion gas pressurize the crankcase, which burns oil. Rising oil use can be an early warning, so check the level often.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2014)
  • Secondary air pump relay recallRecall

    On turbo 2008–2014 Imprezas, including the STI, the relay for the secondary air injection pump can fail and keep the pump running until it overheats, raising fire risk.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the relay for free (NHTSA 16V-738, Subaru WTM-73).

    7 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2014)
