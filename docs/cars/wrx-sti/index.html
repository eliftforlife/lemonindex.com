Quick answers

How fast is the Subaru WRX STI?

The VA WRX STI makes 310 hp and 290 lb-ft.

What engine does the Subaru WRX STI have?

The VA STI (2015–2021) uses the Subaru EJ257, a 2.5-liter flat-4, turbo. Older generations: GD: Subaru EJ257; GR / GV: Subaru EJ257.

Why is the Subaru WRX STI famous?

World Rally champion: Subaru won the World Rally Championship makers' title three years in a row, 1995 to 1997, with the Impreza. Colin McRae won the drivers' title in 1995, Richard Burns in 2001 and Petter Solberg in 2003. Blue paint, gold wheels: The rally team's blue and yellow look came from its sponsor, State Express 555, starting in 1993. That blue became World Rally Blue, and a blue STI on gold wheels still points back to those rally cars. The STI name: STI stands for Subaru Tecnica International, the motorsport arm Subaru set up in 1988. The first Impreza with the STi badge went on sale in Japan in 1994 as a rally homologation car.
