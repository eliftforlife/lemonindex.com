Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Commander reliable?

Not really. It draws 4.6× the average car's complaints. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2008, still drew 2.4× the average.

Is the Jeep Commander a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2006-2008: Lemon Index 459; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2008 drew the fewest complaints and 2006 the most.

What are the best Jeep Commander years to buy?

Only 3 Jeep Commander years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2008 drew the fewest complaints (about twice the average car's complaints).

What Jeep Commander years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Jeep Commander years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Jeep Commander have?

24 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2006 (6), 2007 (6), 2008 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Commander problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
