How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Lincoln MKC reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 27; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2019 Lincoln MKC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (57%), electrical (29%) and body structure (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Lincoln MKC have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Lincoln MKC get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Lincoln MKC safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2019 Lincoln MKC have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Lincoln MKC cost in fuel a year?

About $2,600 to $3,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Lincoln MKC have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.3L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Lincoln MKC year than the 2019?

Not nearby: the 2019 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Lincoln MKC years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V685000 (equipment), 25V572000 (back over prevention) and 23V378000 (engine) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Lincoln sent dealers 99 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “For 2010-2019 vehicles equipped with turbocharged engines, when diagnosing and/or replacing…”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Lincoln MKC years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 196 YouTube videos about the Lincoln MKC, 2015 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
