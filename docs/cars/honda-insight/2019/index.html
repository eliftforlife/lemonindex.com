When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,800 and 23,000 miles (4,500–37,000 km) (the middle half of the 25 complaints, of all 42 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K, 100K and 150K miles (97K, 161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags), 23V858000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 23V158000 (seat belts) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into inadvertent automatic emergency braking. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Honda Insight reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 114; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Insight year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2019 Honda Insight problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (24%), electrical (19%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Honda Insight have?

8 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Honda Insight get?

48 to 52 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Honda Insight safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Honda Insight problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,800 and 23,000 miles (4,500–37,000 km) (the middle half of the 25 complaints, of all 42 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Honda Insight have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Honda Insight cost in fuel a year?

About $1,150 to $1,250 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Honda Insight have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Honda Insight year is better than the 2019?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car).
