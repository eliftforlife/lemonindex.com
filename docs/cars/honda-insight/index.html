Quick answers.

Is the Honda Insight reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2010.

Is the Honda Insight a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2019: Lemon Index 91; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints and 2019 the most.

What are the best Honda Insight years to buy?

Only 2 Honda Insight years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2010 drew the fewest complaints (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car).

What Honda Insight years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Honda Insight years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Honda Insight have?

62 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2010 (10), 2011 (10), 2012 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Insight problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name automatic emergency braking, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
