Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Golf reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.7× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2014. Avoid 2001, 2002 and 2019.

Is the Volkswagen Golf a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2021: Lemon Index 170; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2019.

What are the best Volkswagen Golf years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2020, 2013.

What Volkswagen Golf years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2019, 2002, 2001.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Golf have?

119 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2015 (12), 2001 (9), 2016 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Volkswagen Golf problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
