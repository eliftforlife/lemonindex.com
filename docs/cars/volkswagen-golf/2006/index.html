Quick answers

Is the 2006 Volkswagen Golf reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 145; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Golf year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2006 Volkswagen Golf problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (31%), fuel system, gasoline (10%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Volkswagen Golf have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Volkswagen Golf get?

24 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
