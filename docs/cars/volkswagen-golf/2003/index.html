2003 Volkswagen Golf: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Volkswagen Golf drew 1.5× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 41 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 29,342 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Golf complaints that listed a mileage came before 43,000 miles. Most came between 19,700 miles and 70,500 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 19,000 miles and 88,600 miles.
