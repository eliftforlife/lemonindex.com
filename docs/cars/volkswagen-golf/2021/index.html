How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Volkswagen Golf reliable?

We can't score it: only 9,253 were sold in the U.S. in 2021 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2021 Volkswagen Golf problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), brakes (29%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Volkswagen Golf have?

2 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Volkswagen Golf get?

32 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Volkswagen Golf safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2021 Volkswagen Golf have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Volkswagen Golf cost in fuel a year?

About $1,950 to $2,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Volkswagen Golf have?

1.4L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Golf year is better than the 2021?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22V514000 (back over prevention) and 20V716000 (back over prevention) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work? (8 owners named a fuel system repair bill; the middle one was $3,300.)

How the YouTube ranks work

Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
