Quick answers

Is the 2002 Volkswagen Golf reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 344; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Golf year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2002 Volkswagen Golf problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), airbags (22%) and wheels (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Volkswagen Golf have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Volkswagen Golf get?

23 to 38 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Volkswagen Golf problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,900 and 62,500 miles. The middle report was at 26,500 miles, from 135 complaints.

Does the 2002 Volkswagen Golf have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2002 Volkswagen Golf cost in fuel a year?

About $2500 to $2850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Volkswagen Golf have?

1.9L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Golf year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2020 and 2013.
