2000 Volkswagen Golf: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Volkswagen Golf drew 2.2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 70 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 28,124 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Golf complaints that listed a mileage came before 60,000 miles. Most came between 37,500 miles and 85,500 miles.

The top problem area, visibility, usually showed up between 18,500 miles and 68,200 miles.

Before you buy one
