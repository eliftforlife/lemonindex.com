Volkswagen Golf transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (69 complaints), 2008 (40 complaints), 2007 (41 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2016, 2014.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Volkswagen Golf years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Volkswagen Golf years are 2019, 2008, 2007.

What are common Volkswagen Golf transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation.

Which Volkswagen Golf years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Volkswagen Golf years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2016, 2014.
