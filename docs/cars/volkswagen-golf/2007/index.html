2007 Volkswagen Golf: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Volkswagen Golf drew 1.5× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 54 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 41,844 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Golf complaints that listed a mileage came before 32,400 miles. Most came between 12,000 miles and 72,800 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 15,800 miles and 74,200 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
