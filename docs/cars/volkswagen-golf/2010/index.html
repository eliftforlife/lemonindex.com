Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 1176 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Malfunction light is on and there is noise with the engine running rough
  • In some vehicles, the warning light for engine may be on and the engine power has failed
  • Certain model year vehicles are experiencing engine not starting, making a rattling noise

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Volkswagen Golf reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 196; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Golf year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2010 Volkswagen Golf problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, diesel (31%), engine (25%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Volkswagen Golf have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Volkswagen Golf get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Volkswagen Golf problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,000 and 73,000 miles. The middle report was at 40,000 miles, from 103 complaints.

Does the 2010 Volkswagen Golf have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Volkswagen Golf cost in fuel a year?

About $2450 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Volkswagen Golf have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 5-cyl.

What Volkswagen Golf year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2020 and 2013.
