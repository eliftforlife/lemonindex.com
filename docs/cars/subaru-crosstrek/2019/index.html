When problems show up

Half of the Subaru Crosstrek complaints that listed a mileage came before 10,500 miles. Most came between 4,300 miles and 19,000 miles.

The top problem area, wipers & visibility, usually showed up between 4,900 miles and 16,900 miles.

Recalls: 7

  • engine 2019
    The aluminum Positive Crankcase Ventilation (PCV) valve can separate, allowing the separated components to enter the engine, resulting in a loss of power while driving. NHTSA 19V856000
  • steering 2019
    A connector inside the Electronic Power Steering (EPS) unit may short circuit resulting in a loss of electric power steering assistance. NHTSA 19V065000
  • electrical 2019
    The Engine Control Module (ECM) may continue to power the ignition coil after the engine is shut off, which could result in a short circuit and blown fuse. NHTSA 19V743000
  • visibility 2019
    The driver's side rear-door window glass may be improperly tempered. NHTSA 19V404000
  • seat belts 2020
    The seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended. NHTSA 20V189000
  • suspension 2021
    The rear stabilizer bracket bolts may loosen and detach. NHTSA 21V263000

Recall work is free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. See all 7 at nhtsa.gov.
