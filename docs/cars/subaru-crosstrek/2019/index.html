Quick answers

Is the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 167; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Crosstrek year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Subaru Crosstrek problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (48%), electrical (15%) and engine (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek have?

7 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek get?

25 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Subaru Crosstrek problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 4,300 and 19,000 miles. The middle report was at 10,500 miles, from 179 complaints.

Does the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 5% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Subaru Crosstrek have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Crosstrek year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2013 and 2014.
