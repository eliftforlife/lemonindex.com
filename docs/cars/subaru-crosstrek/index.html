Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Crosstrek reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.6× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2019.

Is the Subaru Crosstrek a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2019 the most.

What are the best Subaru Crosstrek years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2013, 2014. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Crosstrek years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2019.

How many recalls does the Subaru Crosstrek have?

23 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2019 (7), 2018 (5), 2013 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Crosstrek problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
