Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Crosstrek reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2019.

Is the Subaru Crosstrek a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2019 the most.

What are the best Subaru Crosstrek years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2013, 2014. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Crosstrek years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 167), 2018 (about average, Lemon Index 90, the first year of a new generation), 2021 (better than average, Lemon Index 71). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Crosstrek have?

23 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2019 (7), 2018 (5), 2013 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Subaru Crosstrek recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Subaru Crosstrek problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 12 Subaru Crosstrek years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 594 YouTube videos about the Subaru Crosstrek, 2013 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
