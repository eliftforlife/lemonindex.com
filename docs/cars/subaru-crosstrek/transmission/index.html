Quick answers

Which Subaru Crosstrek years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Subaru Crosstrek years are 2013, 2014, 2015.

What are common Subaru Crosstrek transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation.

Which Subaru Crosstrek years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Subaru Crosstrek years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2022, 2023.
