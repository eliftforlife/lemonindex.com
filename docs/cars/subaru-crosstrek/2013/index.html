Quick answers

Is the 2013 Subaru Crosstrek reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 28; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Crosstrek year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2013 Subaru Crosstrek problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (24%), steering (19%) and engine (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Subaru Crosstrek have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Subaru Crosstrek get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
