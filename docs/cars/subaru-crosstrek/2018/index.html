How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The Subaru Crosstrek year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2018 Subaru Crosstrek problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (43%), engine (17%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek get?

25 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2018 Subaru Crosstrek problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,600 and 35,000 miles (9,000–56,000 km) (the middle half of the 117 complaints, of all 195 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 to $2,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Subaru Crosstrek have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Crosstrek year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V263000 (suspension), 21V264000 (electrical) and 19V743000 (electrical) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Subaru sent dealers 523 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This bulletin announces part information for new Active Valve Control System (AVCS) oil…”?
