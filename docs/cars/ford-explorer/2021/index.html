2021 Ford Explorer.

The 2021 Ford Explorer is about average by owner complaints: 239 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 112, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about transmission & drivetrain, it has 26 recalls, and it gets 19 to 27 mpg combined. The best Ford Explorer year is 2008.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Ford Explorer reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 112; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Explorer year by this measure since 2010 is 2024.

What are the most common 2021 Ford Explorer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (32%), backup camera (15%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Ford Explorer have?

26 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Ford Explorer get?

19 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Ford Explorer safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Ford Explorer problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 73,000 miles. The middle report was at 44,000 miles, from 109 complaints.

Does the 2021 Ford Explorer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 32% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Ford Explorer cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Ford Explorer have?

2.3L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl.

What Ford Explorer year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2018 and 2010.
