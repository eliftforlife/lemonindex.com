Quick answers

Is the 2008 Ford Explorer reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 29; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Explorer year by this measure since 2010 is 2024.

What are the most common 2008 Ford Explorer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (15%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and seats (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Ford Explorer have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Ford Explorer get?

15 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Ford Explorer problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,500 and 102,700 miles. The middle report was at 77,000 miles, from 129 complaints.

Does the 2008 Ford Explorer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2008 Ford Explorer cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Ford Explorer have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

What Ford Explorer year is better than the 2008?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2018 and 2010.
