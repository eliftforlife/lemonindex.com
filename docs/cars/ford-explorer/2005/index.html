2005 Ford Explorer: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Ford Explorer drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 100 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 239,788 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Explorer complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 47,800 miles and 96,000 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 60,000 miles and 100,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
