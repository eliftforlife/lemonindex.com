Quick answers

Is the 2006 Ford Explorer reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 149; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Explorer year by this measure since 2010 is 2024.

What are the most common 2006 Ford Explorer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (44%), engine (30%) and electrical (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Ford Explorer have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Ford Explorer get?

15 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Ford Explorer problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 35,000 and 83,000 miles. The middle report was at 55,000 miles, from 546 complaints.

Does the 2006 Ford Explorer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 44% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Ford Explorer cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Ford Explorer have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

What Ford Explorer year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2018 and 2010.
