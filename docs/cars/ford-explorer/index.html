Questions6 quick answers

Quick answers.

Is the Ford Explorer reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Ford Explorer a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 178; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best Ford Explorer years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2018, 2010. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Ford Explorer years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 407), 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 267), 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 248, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Explorer have?

277 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2020 (33), 2021 (26), 2022 (24). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Explorer recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Explorer problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chartColors, ranks and what counts

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 31 Ford Explorer years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work986 videos read · favorite year: 2024

YouTube rank: we read 986 YouTube videos about the Ford Explorer, 1995 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
