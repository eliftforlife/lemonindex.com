Quick answers.

Is the Ford Explorer reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2019. Avoid 2015, 2016 and 2020.

Is the Ford Explorer a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 178; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2008 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best Ford Explorer years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2008, 2019, 2007.

What Ford Explorer years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016, 2015, 2020.

How many recalls does the Ford Explorer have?

277 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2020 (33), 2021 (26), 2022 (24). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Explorer problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
