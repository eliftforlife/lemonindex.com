Ford Explorer transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2002 (1,273 complaints), 2020 (364 complaints), 2006 (267 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2024, 2025, 2023.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2025 2025

    The transmission may be improperly secured due to loose cross member bolts.

  • 2025 2025

    The transmission valve body may have been machined incorrectly, which can cause reverse gear failure, or unexpected forward movement in reverse or neutral gear.

  • 2025 2025

    The powertrain control module (PCM) may reset while driving, which can damage the park system or cause an engine stall.

  • 2024 2026

    An unintentional powertrain control module (PCM) reset may occur while the vehicle is in motion and result in park system damage.

  • 2023 2023

    The transmission may contain a loose bolt which could prevent the transmission from engaging the park gear, although the gear shifter position may indicate that the vehicle has been shifted to "PARK.".

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Explorer years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Explorer years are 2002, 2020, 2006.

What are common Ford Explorer transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, slipping, transmission replaced.

Which Ford Explorer years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Explorer years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2025, 2023.
