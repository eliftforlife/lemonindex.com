Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford Explorer reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 267; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Explorer year by this measure since 2010 is 2024.

What are the most common 2015 Ford Explorer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (20%), steering (16%) and fuel or ev drive system (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford Explorer have?

9 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford Explorer get?

18 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
