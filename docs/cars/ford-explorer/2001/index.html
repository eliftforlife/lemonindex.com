Quick answers

Is the 2001 Ford Explorer reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 55; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Explorer year by this measure since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2001 Ford Explorer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (23%), suspension (13%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Ford Explorer have?

12 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Ford Explorer get?

15 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
