Known issues

  • No known patternOwner-reported

    No recurring 1LR-GUE failure is documented. PistonHeads found little on known problems, and a Japanese owner reported eight trouble-free years.

  • Clutch wear from hard launchesOwner-reported

    The ASG is an automated manual with a single-plate clutch. PistonHeads warns that launches without launch control can burn it.

Quick answers

How fast is the Lexus LFA?

The LFA10 LFA makes 552 hp and 354 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.6 seconds. Top speed: 202 mph.

What engine does the Lexus LFA have?

The LFA10 LFA (2012) uses the Lexus 1LR-GUE, a 4.8-liter V-10, naturally aspirated.
