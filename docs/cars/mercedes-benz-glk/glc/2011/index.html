Quick answers

Is the 2011 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 14.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 7; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2011 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (50%) and brakes (50%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
