Quick answers

Is the 2020 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2020 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (20%), backup camera (14%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

16 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC get?

24 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
