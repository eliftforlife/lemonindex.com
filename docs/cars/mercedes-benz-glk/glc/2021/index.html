Quick answers

Is the 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), fuel or ev drive system (19%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

7 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
