Quick answers

Is the 2010 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2010 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (35%), body structure (18%) and tires (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
