Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.7× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2013. Avoid 2016.

Is the Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 60; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2013.

What Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

86 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2020 (16), 2016 (12), 2017 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
