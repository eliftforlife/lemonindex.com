Quick answers

Which Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years are 2010, 2018, 2014.

What are common Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, shudder.

Which Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2023, 2012.
