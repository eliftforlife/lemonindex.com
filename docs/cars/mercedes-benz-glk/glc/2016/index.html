Quick answers

Is the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 151; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (35%), engine (26%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC have?

12 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLK/GLC get?

24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
