Quick answers

Is the 2005 GMC Yukon reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 58; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Yukon year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2005 GMC Yukon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), transmission & drivetrain (26%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 GMC Yukon have?

5 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 GMC Yukon get?

13 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
