Quick answers

Is the 2013 GMC Yukon reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 54; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Yukon year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2013 GMC Yukon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (21%), body structure (11%) and seats (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 GMC Yukon have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 GMC Yukon get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 GMC Yukon safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 GMC Yukon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,500 and 91,800 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 96 complaints.

Does the 2013 GMC Yukon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 5% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 GMC Yukon cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $4300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 GMC Yukon have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Yukon year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2020 and 2019.
