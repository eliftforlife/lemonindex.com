Quick answers

Is the 2021 GMC Yukon reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 97; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Yukon year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2021 GMC Yukon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (30%), fuel or ev drive system (18%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 GMC Yukon have?

13 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 GMC Yukon get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 GMC Yukon safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 GMC Yukon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 24,000 and 94,600 miles. The middle report was at 69,000 miles, from 71 complaints.

Does the 2021 GMC Yukon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 GMC Yukon cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 GMC Yukon have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Yukon year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2020 and 2019.
