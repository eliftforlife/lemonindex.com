Quick answers.

Is the GMC Yukon reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2015 and 2016.

Is the GMC Yukon a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 94; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best GMC Yukon years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2020, 2019. 2012 drew the fewest.

What GMC Yukon years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 516, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 236), 2017 (about average, Lemon Index 109). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the GMC Yukon have?

130 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (13), 2001 (10), 2015 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Yukon problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and lights most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
