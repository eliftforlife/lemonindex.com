Quick answers.

Is the GMC Yukon reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car. Best year: 2012. Avoid 2000, 2015 and 2016.

Is the GMC Yukon a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 94; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best GMC Yukon years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2020, 2019.

What GMC Yukon years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015, 2016, 2000.

How many recalls does the GMC Yukon have?

130 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (13), 2001 (10), 2015 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Yukon problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and lights most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
