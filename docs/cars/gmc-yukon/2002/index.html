2002 GMC Yukon: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 GMC Yukon drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 83 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 76,488 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Yukon complaints that listed a mileage came before 79,000 miles. Most came between 43,900 miles and 131,000 miles.

The top problem area, brakes, usually showed up between 107,000 miles and 145,200 miles.
