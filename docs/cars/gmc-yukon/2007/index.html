2007 GMC Yukon: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 GMC Yukon drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 61 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 63,428 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Yukon complaints that listed a mileage came before 88,000 miles. Most came between 60,000 miles and 126,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 75,000 miles and 150,000 miles.
