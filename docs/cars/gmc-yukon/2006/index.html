Problems GMC told dealers about

  • Harsh 1-2 shift
  • Investigation of extension seal leaks on 4l60e automatic transmission
  • 4l60-e/4l65-e automatic transmission/torque converter clutch (tcc) shudder, water in transmission

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2006 GMC Yukon reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 6 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 GMC Yukon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (33%), transmission & drivetrain (33%) and body structure (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 GMC Yukon have?

3 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 GMC Yukon get?

13 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 GMC Yukon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 49,800 and 127,300 miles. The middle report was at 82,500 miles, from 38 complaints.

Does the 2006 GMC Yukon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 33% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 GMC Yukon cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 GMC Yukon have?

4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What GMC Yukon year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2020 and 2019.
