Quick answers

Is the 2023 GMC Yukon reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.4× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 243; 100 is average). The best GMC Yukon year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2023 GMC Yukon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (65%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 GMC Yukon have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 GMC Yukon get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 GMC Yukon safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 GMC Yukon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 18,700 and 44,400 miles. The middle report was at 34,000 miles, from 29 complaints.

Does the 2023 GMC Yukon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 GMC Yukon cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 GMC Yukon have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Yukon year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2020 and 2019.
