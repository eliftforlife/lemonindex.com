Quick answers

Is the 2019 Audi A4 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 17; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2019 Audi A4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (40%), automatic emergency braking (40%) and lane assist (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Audi A4 have?

1 safety recall (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Audi A4 get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
