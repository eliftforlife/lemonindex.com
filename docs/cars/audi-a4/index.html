Quick answers.

Is the Audi A4 reliable?

Yes. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2019.

Is the Audi A4 a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2016-2021: Lemon Index 45; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Audi A4 years to buy?

Only 4 Audi A4 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2019 drew the fewest complaints (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

What Audi A4 years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Audi A4 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Audi A4 have?

21 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2017 (6), 2018 (4), 2021 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Audi A4 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Audi A4 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, airbags and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Audi A4 repairs cost?

The repairs Audi A4 owners reported paying for most: ABS module replacement (middle bill $1,300, 14 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $9,400, 8 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $800, 8 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 7 Audi A4 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 392 YouTube videos about the Audi A4, 2009 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
