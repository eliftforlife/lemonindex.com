Quick answers.

Is the Audi A4 a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2016-2021: Lemon Index 45; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Audi A4 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2016, 2018.

What Audi A4 years should you stay away from?

No Audi A4 year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Audi A4 have?

14 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2017 (6), 2018 (4), 2016 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi A4 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
