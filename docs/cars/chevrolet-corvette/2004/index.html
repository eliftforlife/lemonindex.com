Quick answers

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Corvette year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2004 Chevrolet Corvette problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (33%), steering (33%) and transmission & drivetrain (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette have?

4 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette cost in fuel a year?

About $3750 to $3950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Chevrolet Corvette have?

5.7L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Corvette year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2015.
