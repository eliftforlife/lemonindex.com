Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 276; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Corvette year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2021.

What are the most common 2000 Chevrolet Corvette problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (65%), engine (9%) and electrical (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette have?

4 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Chevrolet Corvette problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 15,000 and 32,000 miles (24,000–52,000 km) (the middle half of the 25 complaints, of all 95 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2000 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette cost in fuel a year?

About $3,950 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Chevrolet Corvette have?

5.7L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Corvette year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2003 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average). Across every year, 2021 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 09E025000 (lights), 09E012000 (lights) and 04V060000 (steering) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 359 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Condition in which the accessory (serpentine) drive belt tensioner pulley fails”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 782 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Corvette, 1997 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
