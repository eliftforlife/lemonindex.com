How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette reliable?

We can't score it: only 14,132 were sold in the U.S. in 2012 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 11 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2012 Chevrolet Corvette problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (55%), seat belts (18%) and body structure (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette have?

1 safety recall (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette get?

17 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette cost in fuel a year?

About $3,150 to $4,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Chevrolet Corvette have?

6.2L 8-cyl, 7.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Corvette year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2021 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 11V491000 (body structure) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Do all the seat belts latch and retract?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 1380 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The transmission is slipping or has no forward/reverse movement”?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
