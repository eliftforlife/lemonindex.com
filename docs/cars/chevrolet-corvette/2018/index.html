Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 25V175000 (steering) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Chevy sent dealers 1010 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “18038 - Service Update”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette reliable?

We can't score it: only 18,789 were sold in the U.S. in 2018 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 45 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2018 Chevrolet Corvette problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wheels (89%), brakes (4%) and body structure (2%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette have?

1 safety recall (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette get?

16 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2018 Chevrolet Corvette problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,700 and 17,000 miles (9,200–27,000 km) (the middle half of the 41 complaints, of all 45 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 2% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette cost in fuel a year?

About $3,950 to $4,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Chevrolet Corvette have?

6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Corvette year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2021 drew the fewest.
