Known issues: BMW N55

  • Cracked charge pipeParts seller

    The factory plastic charge pipe gets brittle from heat. It can crack and the car loses boost.

    Fix: Replace it, often with an aluminum pipe.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2018)
  • Electric water pumpOutlet

    The electric water pump can quit with little warning. Owners report overheating and limp mode.

    Fix: Replace the pump.

  • Oil leaks from gasketsOutlet

    The oil filter housing gasket and valve cover gasket are common leak points as they age.

    Fix: Replace the leaking gasket.

  • High-pressure fuel pumpOutlet

    The high-pressure fuel pump can fail. Signs are long cranking, rough running, low power and a check engine light.

Known issues: BMW S55

  • Crank hubOutlet + parts seller

    The stock crank hub is not keyed and can slip on the crank, throwing off cam timing. BMWBLOG calls it rare but serious; parts sellers say it is common, so how often it happens is disputed.

    Fix: An aftermarket keyed or pinned crank hub is the usual fix.

  • Oil leaksOutlet

    Gaskets wear with age. BMWBLOG names the valve cover, oil pan and oil filter housing gaskets as common leak points.

    Fix: Replace the leaking gasket.

  • Carbon buildupOutlet

    Direct injection lets carbon collect on the intake valves over time, which can cause rough idle and misfires.

    Fix: Clean the intake valves at intervals.

Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the BMW M2 (2025 update)?

The G87 M2 (2025 update) makes 473 hp and 443 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.5 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (limited; 177 with M Driver’s Package).

What engine does the BMW M2 have?

The G87 M2 (2023–) uses the BMW S58, a 3.0-liter inline-6, twin-turbo. Older generations: F87: BMW N55; F87 Competition: BMW S55.

Which BMW M2 engine do YouTube owners like most?

BMW S58 56/100, BMW N55 50/100, BMW S55 50/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
