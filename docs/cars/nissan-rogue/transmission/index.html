Nissan Rogue transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2013 (241 complaints), 2008 (133 complaints), 2011 (151 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2025, 2024.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Nissan Rogue years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Nissan Rogue years are 2013, 2008, 2011.

What are common Nissan Rogue transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, transmission replaced, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Nissan Rogue years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Nissan Rogue years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2025, 2024.
