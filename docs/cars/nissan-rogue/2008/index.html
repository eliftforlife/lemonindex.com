Quick answers

Is the 2008 Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 76; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Rogue year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2008 Nissan Rogue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (57%), airbags (14%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Nissan Rogue have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Nissan Rogue get?

23 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
