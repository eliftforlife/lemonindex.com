2008 Nissan Rogue: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Nissan Rogue drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 70 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 92,861 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Rogue complaints that listed a mileage came before 81,900 miles. Most came between 49,200 miles and 115,200 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 52,000 miles and 121,000 miles.
