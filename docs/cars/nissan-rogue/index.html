Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the Nissan Rogue a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 61; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Nissan Rogue years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2020, 2011. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Rogue years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (about average, Lemon Index 104), 2014 (better than average, Lemon Index 70, the first year of a new generation), 2022 (better than average, Lemon Index 63). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Rogue have?

82 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2014 (10), 2021 (10), 2015 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Rogue problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
