Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the Nissan Rogue a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 61; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Nissan Rogue years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2020, 2011. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Rogue years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (about average, Lemon Index 104), 2008 (better than average, Lemon Index 76), 2014 (better than average, Lemon Index 70, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Rogue have?

82 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2014 (10), 2021 (10), 2015 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Nissan Rogue recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Nissan Rogue problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Nissan Rogue repairs cost?

The repairs Nissan Rogue owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $4,100, 57 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $8,000, 6 reports), wiring harness repair (middle bill $4,200, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 18 Nissan Rogue years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 597 YouTube videos about the Nissan Rogue, 2008 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
