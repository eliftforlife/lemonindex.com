Quick answers

Is the 2010 Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 48; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Rogue year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2010 Nissan Rogue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (48%), speed control (23%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Nissan Rogue have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Nissan Rogue get?

23 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Nissan Rogue problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 45,000 and 103,200 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 230 complaints.

Does the 2010 Nissan Rogue have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 48% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Nissan Rogue cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Nissan Rogue have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Rogue year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2020 and 2011.
