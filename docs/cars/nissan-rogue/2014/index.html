Quick answers

Is the 2014 Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The Nissan Rogue year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2014 Nissan Rogue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system (26%), airbags (23%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Nissan Rogue have?

10 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Nissan Rogue get?

24 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Nissan Rogue safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Nissan Rogue problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 19,000 and 88,000 miles (30,000–141,000 km) (the middle half of the 276 complaints, of all 440 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2014 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Nissan Rogue have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Nissan Rogue cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $2,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Nissan Rogue have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Rogue year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V093000 (electrical), 22V024000 (electrical) and 16V244000 (airbags) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any shudder, whine or slipping from the CVT? Ask for CVT fluid service records.
  5. Nissan sent dealers 332 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Altima, sentra, and rogue; cvt fail-safe condition service information this bulletin has been…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 597 YouTube videos about the Nissan Rogue, 2008 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Complaint Score counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
