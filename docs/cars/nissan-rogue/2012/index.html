Quick answers

Is the 2012 Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2012 Nissan Rogue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (37%), speed control (12%) and tires (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Nissan Rogue have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Nissan Rogue get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
