Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V093000 (electrical), 22V875000 (electrical) and 21V839000 (engine) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into false automatic emergency braking. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any shudder, whine or slipping from the CVT? Ask for CVT fluid service records.
  5. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (17 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $2,000.)
  7. Nissan sent dealers 263 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “CLICKING NOISE FROM FRONT OR REAR AXLE DURING TAKE-OFF/ACCELERATION This bulletin has been…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether a transmission special coverage or warranty extension applies to this car. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10211969: “Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT) Class Action Owner Notification On March 23, 2022, the Court presiding over a…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10109240: “CVT / TCM CALIBRATION DATA "WRITE" PROCEDURE This bulletin does not apply to Pathfinder Hybrid, Murano Hybrid or Rogue…”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10120563: “2017 ROGUE SPORT; MIL ON WITH DTC P050B STORED IF YOU CONFIRM - The MIL is ON with P050B stored”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Nissan Rogue reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The Nissan Rogue year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2017 Nissan Rogue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (29%), transmission & drivetrain (12%) and automatic emergency braking (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Nissan Rogue have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Nissan Rogue get?

27 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Nissan Rogue safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Nissan Rogue problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 10,000 and 58,000 miles (16,000–93,000 km) (the middle half of the 300 complaints, of all 600 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2017 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2017 Nissan Rogue have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Nissan Rogue cost in fuel a year?

About $1,900 to $2,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Nissan Rogue have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Rogue year is better than the 2017?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.
