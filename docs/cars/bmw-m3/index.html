BMW S54 engine page →

Known issues

  • Rod bearingsBMW service actions

    BMW replaced oil pumps and rod bearings on 2001–2003 cars under two service actions and extended engine coverage to 6 years / 100,000 miles, long expired. Bearings are still the S54’s best-known weak spot.

    Fix: Oil analysis, or preventive bearings (ECS Tuning: about every 100k miles).

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2001–2006)
  • VANOSOwner-reported

    Worn VANOS hub tabs and sprocket bolts that back out. BMWBLOG calls it the most common S54 issue.

    Fix: VANOS rebuild with updated hubs.

    7 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2001–2006)
  • SMG gearboxOwner complaints

    Owners filed complaints about the optional SMG automated manual: surging and replaced units.

    26 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2001–2006)

BMW S65 engine page →

Known issues

  • Rod bearingsOwner-reported

    Tight bearing clearances plus hard revving on cold oil wear the bearings early. A spun bearing can end the engine.

    Fix: Preventive bearings around 60,000–80,000 miles (BMWBLOG). Warm the oil first.

    30 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2013)
  • Throttle actuatorsOwner-reported

    The two actuators that drive the throttle bodies wear out inside and trigger limp mode (codes 2B15, 2B16, 2B21).

    Fix: Rebuilt or new actuators, often both at once.

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2013)
  • Idle control actuatorBMW warranty extension

    BMW extended coverage on the idle control actuator to 10 years / 120,000 miles for 2013 coupes and convertibles. Not a recall.

  • M DCT gearboxOwner complaints

    The M DCT is the gearbox E9x owners name most in complaints, mostly on 2008–2009 cars. A 2008 recall (08V595000) covered about 2,500 DCT cars.

    50 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2013)

BMW S55 engine page →

Known issues

  • Crank hubOwner-reported, disputed

    The crank hub is pressed on, not keyed. Under high torque, mostly on tuned cars, it can slip and knock the timing out. BMWBLOG calls it rare but serious.

    Fix: Aftermarket keyed or pinned hub.

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2018)
  • Carbon on intake valvesOwner-reported

    Direct injection lets carbon build on the intake valves: rough idle and misfires.

    Fix: Walnut-blast the valves.

  • Gasket oil leaksOwner-reported

    Valve cover, oil pan and oil filter housing gaskets leak with age.

    Fix: Replace the gaskets.

How fast is the BMW M3 Competition?

The G80 M3 Competition makes 503 hp and 479 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.5 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (limited).

What engine does the BMW M3 have?

The G80 M3 (2021–2025) uses the BMW S58, a 3.0-liter inline-6, twin-turbo. Older generations: E46: BMW S54; E9x: BMW S65; F80: BMW S55.
