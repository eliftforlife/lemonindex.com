Known issues

  • Intake manifold coolant leaksClass action

    The composite intake's coolant crossover can crack and leak, which can overheat the engine. Class actions covered some 1997–2001 Mustangs.

    Fix: Replace the intake manifold.

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2004)
  • Spark plug threadsOwner-reported

    Spark plug threads in the aluminum heads can be stripped or too shallow, and a plug can work loose and blow out.

    Fix: Ford's approved out-of-warranty repair is a thread insert kit.

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2004)

Quick answers

How fast is the Ford Mustang GT?

The S650 Mustang GT makes 480 hp and 415 lb-ft.

What engine does the Ford Mustang GT have?

The S650 Mustang GT (2024–) uses the Ford Coyote 5.0, a 5.0-liter V8, DOHC 4-valve. Older generations: SN95 (New Edge): Ford 4.6 Modular (2-valve); S197: Ford 4.6 Modular (3-valve); S197 (5.0): Ford Coyote 5.0; S550: Ford Coyote 5.0.
