Known issues

  • Oil filter housing leaksParts seller

    The plastic housing and its gaskets can warp with heat and leak oil or coolant, often around 60,000–100,000 miles.

    Fix: Replace the housing and gaskets.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2016–2018)
  • Coolant lossOwner-reported

    Owners report coolant leaks. A parts seller names the water pump seal and the heat management module as common sources.

  • Valve cover and PCVOwner-reported

    Valve cover gasket leaks and PCV valve faults are the most-reported B58 problems on owner forums, per SlashGear.

Known issues

  • Brake-assist recall (engine software, 2020)Recall

    On some 2020 M340i and M340i xDrive cars, the engine software could cut brake assist during engine start. Full mechanical braking stays available.

    Fix: Dealer updates the engine software for free (NHTSA 21V-598).

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2020–)
  • Oil filter housing leaksParts seller

    The plastic housing and its gaskets can warp with heat and leak oil or coolant, often around 60,000–100,000 miles.

    Fix: Replace the housing and gaskets.

  • Coolant lossOwner-reported

    Owners report coolant leaks. A parts seller names the water pump seal and the heat management module as common sources.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2020–)

Quick answers

How fast is the BMW M340i?

The G20 M340i makes 382 hp and 369 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.8 seconds. Top speed: 130 mph (155 mph with performance tires).

What engine does the BMW M340i / 340i have?

The G20 340i (2020–) uses the BMW B58, a 3.0-liter inline-6, turbo. Older generations: F30: BMW B58.
