Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Buick LaCrosse reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2015.

Is the Buick LaCrosse a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 65; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2015 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Buick LaCrosse years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015, 2013, 2014. 2015 drew the fewest.

What Buick LaCrosse years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012 (about average, Lemon Index 88), 2010 (about average, Lemon Index 85), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 71, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Buick LaCrosse have?

48 safety recalls across the 14 model years we track. Most: 2005 (5), 2012 (5), 2013 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Buick LaCrosse recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Buick LaCrosse problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 12 Buick LaCrosse years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 595 YouTube videos about the Buick LaCrosse, 2005 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
