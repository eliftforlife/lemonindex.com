Quick answers.

Is the Buick LaCrosse reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.5× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2015.

Is the Buick LaCrosse a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 65; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2015 and the worst is 2005.

What are the best Buick LaCrosse years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2013, 2014.

How many recalls does the Buick LaCrosse have?

48 safety recalls across the 14 model years we track. Most: 2005 (5), 2012 (5), 2013 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Buick LaCrosse problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
