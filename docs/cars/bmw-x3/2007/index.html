Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22V119000 (engine), 20V017000 (airbags) and 17V683000 (engine) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (6 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,100.)
  7. BMW sent dealers 74 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Repairing transfer case actuator (vtg servo-motor)”?

Problems BMW told dealers about

BMW sent dealers 74 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Repairing transfer case actuator (vtg servo-motor)
  • Shudder from the Driveline on Vehicles with xDrive

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Recalls 5

  • Engine · recall issued 2022

    An electrical short can cause the PCV valve heater to overheat, increasing the risk of a fire while driving or, soon after driving when parked.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2020

    An inflator explosion may result in sharp metal fragments striking the driver or other occupants resulting in serious injury or death.

  • Engine · recall issued 2017

    The heater for the positive crankcase ventilation (PCV) valve may short circuit.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2017

Quick answers

Is the 2007 BMW X3 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 149; 100 is average, lower is better). 2007 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. The BMW X3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2007 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (87%), fuel system, gasoline (5%) and speed control (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 BMW X3 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 BMW X3 get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 BMW X3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 40,000 and 110,000 miles (65,000–176,000 km) (the middle half of the 152 complaints, of all 258 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 87% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 BMW X3 have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2007?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).
