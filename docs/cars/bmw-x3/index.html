Quick answers.

Is the BMW X3 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the BMW X3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best BMW X3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2018, 2014. 2012 drew the fewest.

What BMW X3 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 90), 2019 (about average, Lemon Index 85), 2020 (better than average, Lemon Index 77). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the BMW X3 have?

135 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2020 (16), 2018 (11), 2019 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common BMW X3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
