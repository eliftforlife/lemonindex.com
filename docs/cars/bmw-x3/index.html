Quick answers.

Is the BMW X3 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.6× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2018. Avoid 2004 and 2007.

Is the BMW X3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2004.

What are the best BMW X3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2018, 2014.

What BMW X3 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2004, 2007.

How many recalls does the BMW X3 have?

118 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2020 (16), 2018 (11), 2019 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common BMW X3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
