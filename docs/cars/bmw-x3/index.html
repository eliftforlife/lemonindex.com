Few problems. Blown turbo at 140k was the most expensive @$4500 at independent shop. Other things seem normal like front strut replacement and transfer case service. Brakes have been great but I use PowerStop brand.

Annoying stuff: Cigarette lighter fuses. Delaminated jockey box. Parking sensors don't like water. Rubber door handles break down and get gooey; bought aftermarket inserts to cover them. Very fun and engaging car to drive. Hasn't cost me anything near what I thought it was going to.

Quick answers.

Is the BMW X3 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2004 and 2007.

Is the BMW X3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2004 the most.

What are the best BMW X3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2018, 2014. 2012 drew the fewest.

What BMW X3 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2004 (worse than average, Complaint Score 158), 2007 (worse than average, Complaint Score 149), 2015 (about average, Complaint Score 90). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the BMW X3 have?

140 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2020 (16), 2018 (11), 2019 (10). Each year's page lists them, and every BMW X3 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common BMW X3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do BMW X3 repairs cost?

The repairs BMW X3 owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $11,200, 11 reports), timing chain replacement (middle bill $3,700, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 20 BMW X3 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 589 YouTube videos about the BMW X3, 2004 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
