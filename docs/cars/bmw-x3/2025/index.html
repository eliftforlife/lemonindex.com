Quick answers

Is the 2025 BMW X3 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.2× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 82; 100 is average). The best BMW X3 year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2025 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), automatic emergency braking (32%) and steering (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 BMW X3 have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 BMW X3 get?

27 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 BMW X3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2750 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 BMW X3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 4-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2018 and 2014.
