Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2025 BMW X3 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn somewhat fewer complaints than the average car per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 82; 100 is average). The BMW X3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2025 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), automatic emergency braking (21%) and steering (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 BMW X3 have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 BMW X3 get?

27 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 BMW X3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2025 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $2,950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 BMW X3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 4-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2025?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V524000 (steering), 25V857000 (steering) and 25V247000 (brakes) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. BMW sent dealers 126 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “When turning left, a creaking noise may be heard from the EPS when near the steering wheel end…”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 19 BMW X3 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 589 YouTube videos about the BMW X3, 2004 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
