Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2004 BMW X3 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 158; 100 is average, lower is better). 2004 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. The BMW X3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2004 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (13%), airbags (11%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 BMW X3 have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 BMW X3 get?

17 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 BMW X3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 99,000 miles (33,000–159,000 km) (the middle half of the 106 complaints, of all 138 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2004 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2004 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2004 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $4,400 to $4,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 BMW X3 have?

2.5L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2004?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 08V384000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. BMW sent dealers 60 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Repairing transfer case actuator (vtg servo-motor)”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 589 YouTube videos about the BMW X3, 2004 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
