Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V056000 (electrical), 25V106000 (latches/locks/linkages) and 24V534000 (body structure) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. BMW sent dealers 55 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “?Water leaking into the vehicle from the headliner/microphone area”?

Quick answers

Is the 2023 BMW X3 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 15 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), body structure (20%) and electrical (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 BMW X3 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 BMW X3 get?

17 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 BMW X3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,200 to $4,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 BMW X3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
