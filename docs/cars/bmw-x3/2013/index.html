Quick answers

Is the 2013 BMW X3 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X3 year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2013 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), brakes (30%) and electrical (26%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 BMW X3 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 BMW X3 get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2013 BMW X3 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 60,000 and 97,800 miles. The middle report was at 84,500 miles, from 160 complaints.

Does the 2013 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $3300 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 BMW X3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2018 and 2014.
