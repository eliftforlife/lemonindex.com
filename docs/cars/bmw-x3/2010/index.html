Quick answers

Is the 2010 BMW X3 reliable?

We can't score it: only 6,075 were sold in the U.S. in 2010 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 4 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2010 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (50%), visibility (25%) and body structure (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 BMW X3 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 BMW X3 get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2010 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 BMW X3 have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2018 and 2014.
