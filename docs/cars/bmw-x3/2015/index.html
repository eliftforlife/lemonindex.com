How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 BMW X3 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The BMW X3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (21%), brakes (17%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 BMW X3 have?

8 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 BMW X3 get?

21 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 BMW X3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2015 BMW X3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 17,000 and 88,000 miles (27,000–141,000 km) (the middle half of the 53 complaints, of all 105 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2015 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2015 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2015 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,150 to $3,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 BMW X3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2015?

Nearby, the 2012 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V733000 (engine), 24V608000 (engine) and 21V907000 (fuel system, diesel) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into high pressure fuel pump failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. BMW sent dealers 304 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Fuel gauge fails to show correct level of fuel when filling fuel tank but would update itself”?
