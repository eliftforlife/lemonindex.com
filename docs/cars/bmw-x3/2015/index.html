Quick answers

Is the 2015 BMW X3 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X3 year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (21%), electrical (17%) and speed control (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 BMW X3 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 BMW X3 get?

21 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
