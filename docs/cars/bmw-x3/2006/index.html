Problems BMW told dealers about

BMW sent dealers 70 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Check engine lamp illuminated and/or vehicle overheats
  • Engine electrical systems
  • Service engine soon lamp or check gas cap warning illuminated while driving

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2006 BMW X3 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 5 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 BMW X3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (20%), brakes (electric) (20%) and equipment & accessories (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 BMW X3 have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 BMW X3 get?

17 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 BMW X3 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 55,000 and 100,500 miles. The middle report was at 76,000 miles, from 110 complaints.

Does the 2006 BMW X3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2006 BMW X3 cost in fuel a year?

About $4400 to $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 BMW X3 have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X3 year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2018 and 2014.
