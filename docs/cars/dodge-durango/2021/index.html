Quick answers

Is the 2021 Dodge Durango reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 149; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Durango year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2021 Dodge Durango problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (31%), electrical (19%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Dodge Durango have?

8 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Dodge Durango get?

13 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Dodge Durango safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Dodge Durango problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,700 and 57,300 miles. The middle report was at 31,200 miles, from 30 complaints.

Does the 2021 Dodge Durango have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Dodge Durango cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $6150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Dodge Durango have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Durango year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2022 and 2019.
