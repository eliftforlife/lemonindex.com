2004 Dodge Durango: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Dodge Durango drew 2.3× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 273 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 137,148 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Durango complaints that listed a mileage came before 94,000 miles. Most came between 49,800 miles and 125,900 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 100,000 miles and 142,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
