When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 43,000 and 100,000 miles (the middle half of the 471 complaints, of all 640 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: electrical mostly 49,000–100,000 miles; engine mostly 50,000–104,000 miles.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V813000 (electrical), 17V572000 (brakes) and 17V435000 (electrical) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Dodge sent dealers 207 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Squeal Noise From Front Of Vehicle When Braking This bulletin involves verifying the front…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10042853: “General motors: on some 2012 vehicles, the powertrain control module (pcm) had been selectively erased and reprogrammed…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10136856: “FLASH: MIL Illumination For P0730 - Incorrect Gear Ratio This bulletin involves updating the Transmission Control…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Dodge Durango reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 469; 100 is average, lower is better). The Dodge Durango year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2020.

What are the most common 2012 Dodge Durango problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (51%), brakes (21%) and fuel system (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Dodge Durango have?

10 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Dodge Durango get?

15 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Dodge Durango safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Dodge Durango problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 43,000 and 100,000 miles (the middle half of the 471 complaints, of all 640 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Dodge Durango have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 6% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Dodge Durango cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $4,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Dodge Durango have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Durango year is better than the 2012?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest (3.2× fewer complaints than average).
