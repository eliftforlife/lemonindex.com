2002 Dodge Durango: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Dodge Durango drew 1.9× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 224 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 106,925 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Durango complaints that listed a mileage came before 58,000 miles. Most came between 30,000 miles and 107,000 miles.

The top problem area, suspension, usually showed up between 26,500 miles and 70,400 miles.
