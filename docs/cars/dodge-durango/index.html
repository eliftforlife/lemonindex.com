Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Durango reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.9× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Dodge Durango a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 194; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best Dodge Durango years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020, 2022, 2019. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Durango years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 493), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 469), 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 269). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Durango have?

138 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2013 (11), 2014 (11), 2006 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Durango problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, suspension and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
