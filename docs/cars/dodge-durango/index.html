Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Durango a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 194; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2011.

What are the best Dodge Durango years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2022, 2019.

What Dodge Durango years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2005.
