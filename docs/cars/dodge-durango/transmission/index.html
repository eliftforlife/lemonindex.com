Dodge Durango transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2005 (140 complaints), 2001 (88 complaints), 2011 (29 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2024, 2023.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The shifter may experience an interference with the shifter boot and come out of gear while driving, which can result in a sudden loss of drive power.

  • 2022 2022

    The shifter assembly was not properly heat-treated, which can prevent the shifter from properly engaging in the "drive" position.

  • 2019 2020

    The front differential may have been assembled with pinion gears that are insufficiently hardened, which can lead to the gear teeth wearing down.

  • 2018 2018

    An incorrect transmission park lock rod may have been installed in the transmission.

Quick answers

Which Dodge Durango years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Dodge Durango years are 2005, 2001, 2011.

What are common Dodge Durango transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, rolled away or park trouble, slipping.

Which Dodge Durango years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Dodge Durango years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2024, 2023.
