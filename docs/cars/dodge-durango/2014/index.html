Quick answers

Is the 2014 Dodge Durango reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 197; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Durango year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2014 Dodge Durango problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (29%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Dodge Durango have?

11 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Dodge Durango get?

16 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Dodge Durango safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Dodge Durango problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,800 and 85,000 miles. The middle report was at 63,000 miles, from 534 complaints.

Does the 2014 Dodge Durango have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Dodge Durango cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Dodge Durango have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Durango year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2022 and 2019.
