When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Durango complaints that listed a mileage came before 63,000 miles. Most came between 39,800 miles and 85,000 miles.

The top problem area, lights, usually showed up between 44,800 miles and 75,000 miles.

Recalls: 11

  • electrical 2018
    These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging. NHTSA 18V332000
  • electrical 2017
    In the affected vehicles, the alternator may suddenly fail. NHTSA 17V741000
  • brakes 2017
    The affected vehicles had brake booster shields installed under a previous campaign to prevent water from entering the brake booster and limiting braking ability. NHTSA 17V572000
  • electrical 2017
    The affected vehicles have electro-hydraulic power steering (EHPS) and are equipped with a 5.7L or a 3.6L engine and a 160, 180 or 220 amp alternator. NHTSA 17V435000
  • equipment 2015
    The affected vehicles are equipped with radios that have software vulnerabilities that can allow third-party access to certain networked vehicle control systems. NHTSA 15V461000
  • seat belts 2014
    Due to a fault within the Occupant Restraint Control (OCR) module, the frontal air bags, seat belt pretensioners, and side air bags may be disabled. NHTSA 14V643000

Recall work is free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. See all 11 at nhtsa.gov.
