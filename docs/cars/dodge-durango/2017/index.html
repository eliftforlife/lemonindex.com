Quick answers

Is the 2017 Dodge Durango reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 123; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Durango year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2017 Dodge Durango problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (37%), electrical (25%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Dodge Durango have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Dodge Durango get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
