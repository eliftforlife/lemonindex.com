Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Atlas reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Best year: 2020. Avoid 2018, 2019 and 2021.

Is the Volkswagen Atlas a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2018-2022: Lemon Index 241; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2021.

What are the best Volkswagen Atlas years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2022, 2019.

What Volkswagen Atlas years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2021, 2018, 2019.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Atlas have?

83 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2018 (17), 2021 (17), 2019 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Volkswagen Atlas problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
