Quick answers

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 187; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Atlas year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2022 Volkswagen Atlas problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (32%), electrical (25%) and engine (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas get?

19 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
