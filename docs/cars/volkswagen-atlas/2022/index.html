Quick answers

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 187; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Atlas year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2022 Volkswagen Atlas problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (32%), electrical (25%) and engine (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas get?

19 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2022 Volkswagen Atlas problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,400 and 56,100 miles. The middle report was at 42,000 miles, from 21 complaints.

Does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Volkswagen Atlas have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Volkswagen Atlas year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2022 and 2019.
