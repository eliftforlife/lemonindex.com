Quick answers

Is the 2023 Volkswagen Atlas reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.4× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 137; 100 is average). The best Volkswagen Atlas year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2023 Volkswagen Atlas problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), electrical (23%) and airbags (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Volkswagen Atlas have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Volkswagen Atlas get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
