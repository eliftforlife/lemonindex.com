Volkswagen Atlas transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (20 complaints), 2024 (21 complaints), 2025 (16 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2020, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Volkswagen Atlas years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Volkswagen Atlas years are 2019, 2024, 2025.

What are common Volkswagen Atlas transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, rolled away or park trouble, delay or hesitation.

Which Volkswagen Atlas years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Volkswagen Atlas years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2020, 2022.
