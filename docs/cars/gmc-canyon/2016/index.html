How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 GMC Canyon reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 165; 100 is average, lower is better). The GMC Canyon year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2016 GMC Canyon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (35%), steering (27%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 GMC Canyon have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 GMC Canyon get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 GMC Canyon safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2016 GMC Canyon problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,900 and 27,000 miles (4,700–44,000 km) (the middle half of the 57 complaints, of all 62 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2016 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2016 GMC Canyon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 35% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 GMC Canyon cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $4,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 GMC Canyon have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 2.8L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What GMC Canyon year is better than the 2016?

Nearby, the 2019 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 18V358000 (fuel system, gasoline), 17V621000 (airbags) and 16V125000 (airbags) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. GMC sent dealers 1577 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information on Transmission Adaptive Functions and Correcting Low Mileage Harsh Shifts, Slips,…”?
