Chevrolet Traverse transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2018 (175 complaints), 2019 (157 complaints), 2009 (47 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2021, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The left-side half-shaft assemblies may be missing a retaining ring.

  • 2023 2023

    The transmission may have been built with an incorrect sun gear, which can result in the driver-side half-shaft disengaging from the transmission.

  • 2021 2021

    The screw and clip that hold the inner wheel-well plastic shroud in place may chafe against the transmission case, possibly resulting in a transmission fluid leak.

  • 2020 2020

    The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing bolts.

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the transmission shift cable adjuster may disengage from the transmission shift lever.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Traverse years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Traverse years are 2018, 2019, 2009.

What are common Chevrolet Traverse transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, shudder, lost power while driving.

Which Chevrolet Traverse years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Traverse years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2021, 2017.
