How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 36; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Traverse year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2021 Chevrolet Traverse problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (27%), engine (22%) and brakes (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse get?

20 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $3,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Chevrolet Traverse have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Chevrolet Traverse year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Chevrolet Traverse years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22V210000 (airbags), 21V817000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 21V472000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (16 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $7,900.)
  6. Chevy sent dealers 666 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Preliminary information communicates that a dealer may encounter a vehicle that is making…”?
