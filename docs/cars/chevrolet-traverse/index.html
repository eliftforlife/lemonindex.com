Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Mostly. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2013. Most complaints: 2011 and 2018.

Is the Chevrolet Traverse a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Traverse years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2021, 2017. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Traverse years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (worse than average, Lemon Index 133), 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 119, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (about average, Lemon Index 96). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Traverse have?

66 safety recalls across the 17 model years we track. Most: 2009 (8), 2020 (8), 2014 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Traverse recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Traverse problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Chevrolet Traverse repairs cost?

The repairs Chevrolet Traverse owners reported paying for most: steering rack replacement (middle bill $1,500, 39 reports), timing chain replacement (middle bill $2,600, 18 reports), power steering pump replacement (middle bill $1,500, 11 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 17 Chevrolet Traverse years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 596 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Traverse, 2009 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
