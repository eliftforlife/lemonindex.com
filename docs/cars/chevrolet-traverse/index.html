Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.3× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2013. Avoid 2011 and 2018.

Is the Chevrolet Traverse a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2011.

What are the best Chevrolet Traverse years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2021, 2017.

What Chevrolet Traverse years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2011, 2018.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Traverse have?

66 safety recalls across the 17 model years we track. Most: 2009 (8), 2020 (8), 2014 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Traverse problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
