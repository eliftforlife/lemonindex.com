Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 60; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Traverse year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Traverse problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (20%), transmission & drivetrain (20%) and stability control (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Traverse have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Traverse get?

17 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
