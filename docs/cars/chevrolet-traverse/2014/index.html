When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 37,000 and 92,000 miles (59,000–149,000 km) (the middle half of the 282 complaints, of all 548 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles (161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: airbags mostly 44,000–102,000 miles (70,000–165,000 km); electrical mostly 40,000–98,000 miles (64,000–158,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V334000 (airbags), 21V782000 (airbags) and 21V246000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the stability-control light off on a test drive?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (90 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $1,500.)
  6. Chevy sent dealers 1550 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Cadillac/buick/saturn/pontiac/chevrolet/ gmc: due to an abnormal engine noise or internal by…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 96; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Traverse year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Traverse problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are stability control (30%), engine (24%) and airbags (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Chevrolet Traverse problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 37,000 and 92,000 miles (59,000–149,000 km) (the middle half of the 282 complaints, of all 548 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2014 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Chevrolet Traverse have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Traverse year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
