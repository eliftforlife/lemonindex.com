Quick answers

Is the 2022 Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 58; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Traverse year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2022 Chevrolet Traverse problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (41%), electrical (17%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Chevrolet Traverse have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Chevrolet Traverse get?

20 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
