Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Traverse year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Traverse problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (20%), engine (18%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse get?

17 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Chevrolet Traverse problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,200 and 51,500 miles. The middle report was at 38,000 miles, from 65 complaints.

Does the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Chevrolet Traverse have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Traverse year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2021 and 2017.
