Quick answers

Is the 2016 Jeep Cherokee reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 280; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Cherokee year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2016 Jeep Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (36%), engine (29%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Jeep Cherokee have?

9 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Jeep Cherokee get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
