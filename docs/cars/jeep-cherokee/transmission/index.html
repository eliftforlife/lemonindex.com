Jeep Cherokee transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2014 (1,237 complaints), 2015 (809 complaints), 2017 (471 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2026

    The power transfer unit (PTU) may fail.

  • 2021 2021

    The transmission oil cooler hose may have been incorrectly cured, allowing transmission fluid to leak.

  • 2019 2018

    A component in the transmission may not have been welded properly, possibly causing the transmission to not transmit engine power to the wheels.

  • 2019 2018

    Improper transmission calibration may result in a stall.

  • 2019 2025

    The power transfer unit (PTU) may become damaged and disengage the transmission and differential, resulting in a loss of drive power and/or loss of park function.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Jeep Cherokee years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Jeep Cherokee years are 2014, 2015, 2017.

What are common Jeep Cherokee transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, delay or hesitation.

Which Jeep Cherokee years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Jeep Cherokee years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2021, 2020.
