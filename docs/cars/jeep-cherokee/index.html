Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Cherokee a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2014-2022: Lemon Index 307; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Jeep Cherokee years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2020, 2021.

What Jeep Cherokee years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2015, 2017.
