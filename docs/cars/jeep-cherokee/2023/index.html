Quick answers

Is the 2023 Jeep Cherokee reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 3 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Jeep Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (67%), airbags (33%) and parking brake (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Jeep Cherokee have?

1 safety recall (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Jeep Cherokee get?

22 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Jeep Cherokee safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Jeep Cherokee have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 67% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Jeep Cherokee cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Jeep Cherokee have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Cherokee year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2021.
