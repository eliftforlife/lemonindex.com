How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Cadillac SRX reliable?

We can't score it: only 16,156 were sold in the U.S. in 2008 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 17 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2008 Cadillac SRX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (24%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and visibility (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Cadillac SRX have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Cadillac SRX get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2008 Cadillac SRX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 24% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2008 Cadillac SRX cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 to $5,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Cadillac SRX have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac SRX year is better than the 2008?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  4. Cadillac sent dealers 1001 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “2-3 upshift slip or flare”?
