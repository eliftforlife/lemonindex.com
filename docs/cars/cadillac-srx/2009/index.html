Quick answers

Is the 2009 Cadillac SRX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 6 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Cadillac SRX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are traction control system (33%), visibility (17%) and seats (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Cadillac SRX have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Cadillac SRX get?

15 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2009 Cadillac SRX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Cadillac SRX cost in fuel a year?

About $3350 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Cadillac SRX have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.
