Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Buick LeSabre reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2004. Most complaints: 2000.

Is the Buick LeSabre a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2005: Lemon Index 78; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2004 drew the fewest complaints and 2000 the most.

What are the best Buick LeSabre years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004, 2003, 2005. 2004 drew the fewest.

What Buick LeSabre years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2000 (worse than average, Lemon Index 159), 2001 (better than average, Lemon Index 70), 2002 (better than average, Lemon Index 56). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Buick LeSabre have?

8 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2000 (5), 2002 (2), 2003 (1). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Buick LeSabre problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, visibility and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 14 Buick LeSabre years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 394 YouTube videos about the Buick LeSabre, 1992 to 2005, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 1996. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
