2005 Ford Taurus: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Ford Taurus drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 62 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 196,919 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Taurus complaints that listed a mileage came before 90,000 miles. Most came between 65,000 miles and 110,900 miles.

The top problem area, speed control, usually showed up between 71,000 miles and 118,300 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
