Quick answers

Is the 2009 Ford Taurus reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 104; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Taurus year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2009 Ford Taurus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (47%), speed control (11%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Ford Taurus have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Ford Taurus get?

18 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Ford Taurus problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 3,900 and 75,300 miles. The middle report was at 31,400 miles, from 53 complaints.

Does the 2009 Ford Taurus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Ford Taurus cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Ford Taurus have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Taurus year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2017.
