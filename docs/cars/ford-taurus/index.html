Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Ford Taurus reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2004. Most complaints: 2000, 2013 and 2015.

Is the Ford Taurus a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2019: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2004 drew the fewest complaints and 2013 the most.

What are the best Ford Taurus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004, 2018, 2007. 2004 drew the fewest.

What Ford Taurus years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 117), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 116), 2000 (about average, Lemon Index 111). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Taurus have?

80 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2001 (10), 2013 (9), 2000 (8). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Taurus recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Taurus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, speed control and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Taurus repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Taurus owners reported paying for most: strut and shock replacement (middle bill $760, 49 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $2,600, 19 reports), torque converter replacement (middle bill $1,300, 14 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 18 Ford Taurus years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 594 YouTube videos about the Ford Taurus, 2000 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2010. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
