Quick answers.

Is the Ford Taurus reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2013 and 2015.

Is the Ford Taurus a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2019: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Ford Taurus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018, 2016, 2017. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Ford Taurus years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 117), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 116), 2010 (about average, Lemon Index 98, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Taurus have?

80 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2001 (10), 2013 (9), 2000 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Taurus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, brakes and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
