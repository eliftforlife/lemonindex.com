Quick answers.

Is the Ford Taurus reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.2× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2004. Avoid 2000, 2013 and 2015.

Is the Ford Taurus a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2019: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2004 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Ford Taurus years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004, 2018, 2007.

What Ford Taurus years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2015, 2000.

How many recalls does the Ford Taurus have?

78 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2001 (10), 2013 (9), 2000 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Taurus problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, brakes and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
