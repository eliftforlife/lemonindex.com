Recalls 5

  • transmission & drivetrain 2020

    The support bracket for the front drive axle halfshaft may fail.

  • suspension 2020

    The rear suspension toe links may fracture due to stress on the rear suspension.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2016

    A component within the fuel pump electric module (PEM) may overheat causing a loss of electrical power to the fuel pump.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the halfshaft retention circlip may not have been properly installed, and as a result, the halfshaft may move outward and disengage from the linkshaft while driving and without prior warning.

  • lights 2014

    When used in areas that use road salt, the license plate lamp assembly may experience an electro-chemical reaction and corrosion as a result of water intrusion.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Ford Taurus reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 88; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Taurus year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Ford Taurus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (18%), steering (16%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Ford Taurus have?

5 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Ford Taurus get?

20 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Ford Taurus safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Ford Taurus problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 41,000 and 99,000 miles. The middle report was at 73,000 miles, from 163 complaints.

Does the 2014 Ford Taurus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Ford Taurus cost in fuel a year?

About $2450 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Ford Taurus have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Taurus year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2017.
