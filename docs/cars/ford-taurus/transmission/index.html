Ford Taurus transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2003 (300 complaints), 2008 (41 complaints), 2005 (107 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2017, 2016, 2014.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2015 2020

    The support bracket for the front drive axle halfshaft may fail.

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the halfshaft retention circlip may not have been properly installed, and as a result, the halfshaft may move outward and disengage from the linkshaft while driving and without prior warning.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Taurus years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Taurus years are 2003, 2008, 2005.

What are common Ford Taurus transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, transmission replaced, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Ford Taurus years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Taurus years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2017, 2016, 2014.
