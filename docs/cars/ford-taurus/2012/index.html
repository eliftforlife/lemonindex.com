Quick answers

Is the 2012 Ford Taurus reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 51; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Taurus year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2012 Ford Taurus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (35%), lights (24%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Ford Taurus have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Ford Taurus get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
