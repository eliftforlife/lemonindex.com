Quick answers

Is the 2000 Ford Taurus reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 111; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Taurus year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2000 Ford Taurus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (15%), engine (13%) and lights (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Ford Taurus have?

8 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Ford Taurus get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Ford Taurus problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 49,500 and 99,000 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 615 complaints.

Does the 2000 Ford Taurus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 Ford Taurus cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Ford Taurus have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What Ford Taurus year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2017.
