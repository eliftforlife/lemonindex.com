Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Passat reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020. Most complaints: 2013 and 2014.

Is the Volkswagen Passat a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2012-2021: Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best Volkswagen Passat years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2018, 2021. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Volkswagen Passat years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 291), 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 209), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 109). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Passat have?

57 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2012 (11), 2014 (10), 2013 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Volkswagen Passat problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
