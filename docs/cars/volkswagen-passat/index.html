Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Passat a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2012-2021: Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Volkswagen Passat years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2018, 2021.

What Volkswagen Passat years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2013.
