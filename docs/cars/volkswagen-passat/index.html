Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Passat reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020. Most complaints: 2013 and 2014.

Is the Volkswagen Passat a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2012-2021: Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best Volkswagen Passat years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2018, 2021. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Volkswagen Passat years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 291), 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 209), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 109). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Passat have?

57 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2012 (11), 2014 (10), 2013 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Volkswagen Passat recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Volkswagen Passat problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Volkswagen Passat repairs cost?

The repairs Volkswagen Passat owners reported paying for most: ABS module replacement (middle bill $1,300, 52 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $7,000, 27 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $1,600, 24 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Volkswagen Passat years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 388 YouTube videos about the Volkswagen Passat, 2012 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
