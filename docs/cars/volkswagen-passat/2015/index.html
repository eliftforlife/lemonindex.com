2015 Volkswagen Passat.

The 2015 Volkswagen Passat is about average by owner complaints: 91 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 109, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about airbags, it has 7 recalls, and it gets 23 to 33 mpg combined. The best Volkswagen Passat year is 2020.

Recalls 7

  • airbags 2019

    The driver's side air bag inflator may explode due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.

  • lights 2019

    The blocking cap disabling the adjustment of the headlight's horizontal aim may not have been installed.

  • airbags 2019

    Upon deployment of the driver's frontal air bag, excessive internal pressure may cause the inflator to explode.

  • engine 2015

    In the affected vehicles, the camshaft lobe that drives the brake vacuum pump may shear off, resulting in a loss of brake assist.

    The affected vehicles have a brake line at the left rear wheel area that may not have been properly tightened.

    A sealing cap at the fuel rail may fail, allowing fuel to leak into the engine compartment.

    In the affected vehicles, the seatback recliner retaining bracket may not engage correctly, resulting in unexpected movement of the seatback.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Volkswagen Passat reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 109; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Passat year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2015 Volkswagen Passat problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (33%), engine (25%) and brakes (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Volkswagen Passat have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Volkswagen Passat get?

23 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Volkswagen Passat safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Volkswagen Passat problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 13,200 and 67,800 miles. The middle report was at 47,400 miles, from 132 complaints.

Does the 2015 Volkswagen Passat have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Volkswagen Passat cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $3450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Volkswagen Passat have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Volkswagen Passat year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2018 and 2021.
