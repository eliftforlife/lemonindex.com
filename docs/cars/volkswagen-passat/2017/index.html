Quick answers

Is the 2017 Volkswagen Passat reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 63; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Passat year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2017 Volkswagen Passat problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (23%), electrical (19%) and steering (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat get?

23 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Volkswagen Passat safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Volkswagen Passat problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,000 and 50,000 miles. The middle report was at 32,000 miles, from 51 complaints.

Does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Volkswagen Passat year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2018 and 2021.
