Quick answers

Is the 2017 Volkswagen Passat reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 63; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Passat year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2017 Volkswagen Passat problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (23%), electrical (19%) and airbags (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Volkswagen Passat get?

23 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
