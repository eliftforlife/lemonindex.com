How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Chrysler 300M reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 112; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chrysler 300M year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2001.

What are the most common 2002 Chrysler 300M problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (19%), electrical (16%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Chrysler 300M have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Chrysler 300M get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2002 Chrysler 300M have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2002 Chrysler 300M cost in fuel a year?

About $3,150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Chrysler 300M have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Chrysler 300M year is better than the 2002?

Nearby, the 2001 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 09E012000 (lights) and 03V035000 (seats) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Chrysler sent dealers 78 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Subject regarding front door speaker wiring incorrectly installed”?
