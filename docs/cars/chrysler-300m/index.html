Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 300M reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2001.

Is the Chrysler 300M a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2003: Lemon Index 116; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2001 drew the fewest complaints and 2000 the most.

What are the best Chrysler 300M years to buy?

Only 3 Chrysler 300M years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2001 drew the fewest complaints (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car).

What Chrysler 300M years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Chrysler 300M years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 300M have?

14 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2000 (7), 2001 (4), 2002 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler 300M problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, airbags and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Chrysler 300M years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Chrysler 300M, 1999 to 2004, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2003. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
