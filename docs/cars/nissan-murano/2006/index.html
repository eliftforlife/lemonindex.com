2006 Nissan Murano: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Nissan Murano drew 2.2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 123 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 81,362 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Murano complaints that listed a mileage came before 65,500 miles. Most came between 49,000 miles and 96,000 miles.

The top problem area, seats, usually showed up between 50,600 miles and 94,400 miles.
