Problems Nissan told dealers about

Nissan sent dealers 79 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Drivers seat noise and/or rocks back and forth
  • Squeak noise when brake pedal is pressed
  • Srs precautions during service;improper maintenance, including incorrect removal and installation

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.
