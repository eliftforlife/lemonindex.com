Quick answers

Is the 2019 Nissan Murano reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 32; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Murano year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Nissan Murano problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (38%), speed control (21%) and wipers & visibility (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Nissan Murano have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Nissan Murano get?

23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
