Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Murano reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2015.

Is the Nissan Murano a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best Nissan Murano years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2011. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Murano years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 149, the first year of a new generation), 2018 (about average, Lemon Index 92), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 82). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Murano have?

50 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2003 (6), 2005 (6), 2015 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Murano problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, seats and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
