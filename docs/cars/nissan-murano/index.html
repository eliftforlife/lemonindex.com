Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Murano reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2003, 2004 and 2005.

Is the Nissan Murano a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2003 the most.

What are the best Nissan Murano years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2011. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Murano years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2003 (avoid, Complaint Score 466), 2005 (avoid, Complaint Score 284), 2004 (avoid, Complaint Score 233). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Murano have?

50 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2003 (6), 2005 (6), 2015 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Nissan Murano recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Nissan Murano problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, seats and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Nissan Murano repairs cost?

The repairs Nissan Murano owners reported paying for most: transfer case replacement (middle bill $3,500, 42 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $5,000, 39 reports), ABS module replacement (middle bill $2,500, 15 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 19 Nissan Murano years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 761 YouTube videos about the Nissan Murano, 2003 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
