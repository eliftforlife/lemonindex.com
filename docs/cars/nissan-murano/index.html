Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Murano a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Nissan Murano years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2021, 2011.

What Nissan Murano years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003, 2005, 2004.

How many recalls does the Nissan Murano have?

49 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2003 (6), 2005 (6), 2015 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Murano problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, seats and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
