Quick answers

Is the 2011 Nissan Murano reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 22; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Murano year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2011 Nissan Murano problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (50%), body structure (21%) and wipers & visibility (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Nissan Murano have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Nissan Murano get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Nissan Murano safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2011 Nissan Murano problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 59,500 and 106,200 miles. The middle report was at 85,000 miles, from 106 complaints.

Does the 2011 Nissan Murano have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2011 Nissan Murano cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Nissan Murano have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Murano year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2021 and 2011.
