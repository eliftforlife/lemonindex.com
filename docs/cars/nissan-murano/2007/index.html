Quick answers

Is the 2007 Nissan Murano reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 148; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Murano year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2007 Nissan Murano problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seats (45%), visibility (19%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Nissan Murano have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Nissan Murano get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
