Honda Civic transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2001 (394 complaints), 2002 (179 complaints), 2015 (164 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2021, 2011, 2023.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Honda Civic years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Civic years are 2001, 2002, 2015.

What are common Honda Civic transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, lost power while driving, transmission replaced.

Which Honda Civic years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Civic years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2021, 2011, 2023.
