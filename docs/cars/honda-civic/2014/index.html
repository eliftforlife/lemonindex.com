Recalls 2

  • transmission & drivetrain 2015

    The software settings that control the transmission operation may result in damage to the transmission drive pulley shaft.

  • tires 2014

    In the affected vehicles, during mounting of the tires, the tire bead may have gotten pinched between the assembly equipment and the steel wheel rims, resulting in damage to the tire.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
