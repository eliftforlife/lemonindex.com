2006 Honda Civic: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Honda Civic drew 2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 445 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 316,638 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Civic complaints that listed a mileage came before 60,900 miles. Most came between 30,000 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 85,800 miles and 149,700 miles.
