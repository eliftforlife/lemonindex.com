Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 168 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Manual transmission grinds shifting to 3rd gear
  • Automatic transmission fluid (atf) leaks
  • Ecm update to prevent momentary engine stall during gear shifts

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Honda Civic reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Civic year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2005 Honda Civic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (24%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and speed control (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Honda Civic have?

15 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Honda Civic get?

28 to 41 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Honda Civic problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 28,600 and 123,200 miles. The middle report was at 75,000 miles, from 255 complaints.

Does the 2005 Honda Civic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Honda Civic cost in fuel a year?

About $1550 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Honda Civic have?

1.3L 4-cyl, 1.7L 4-cyl.

What Honda Civic year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2015.
