2005 Honda Civic: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Honda Civic drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 86 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 308,415 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Civic complaints that listed a mileage came before 75,000 miles. Most came between 28,600 miles and 123,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 35,500 miles and 150,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
