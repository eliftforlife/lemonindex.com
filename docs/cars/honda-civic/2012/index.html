Quick answers

Is the 2012 Honda Civic reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 57; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Civic year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2012 Honda Civic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (26%), speed control (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Honda Civic have?

3 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Honda Civic get?

25 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Honda Civic safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Honda Civic problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,600 and 73,000 miles. The middle report was at 30,200 miles, from 333 complaints.

Does the 2012 Honda Civic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Honda Civic cost in fuel a year?

About $1450 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Honda Civic have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Honda Civic year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2015.
