Quick answers

Is the 2007 Honda Civic reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 184; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Civic year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2007 Honda Civic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (26%), visibility (23%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Honda Civic have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Honda Civic get?

23 to 42 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Honda Civic problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 22,000 and 82,500 miles. The middle report was at 47,200 miles, from 944 complaints.

Does the 2007 Honda Civic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Honda Civic cost in fuel a year?

About $1550 to $3450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Honda Civic have?

1.3L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Civic year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2015.
