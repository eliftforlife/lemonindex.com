Recalls 3

  • seats 2024

    The driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, which can result in an unsecured seat.

  • steering 2024

    The steering gearbox assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause excessive internal friction and lead to difficulty steering the vehicle.

  • steering 2023

    The steering rack may have been incorrectly assembled, which can allow the tire to chafe against the lower suspension or tie rod end, possibly resulting in tire damage.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Honda Civic reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.6× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). The best Honda Civic year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2024 Honda Civic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (50%), automatic emergency braking (20%) and lane assist (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Honda Civic have?

3 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Honda Civic get?

32 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Honda Civic safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Honda Civic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Honda Civic cost in fuel a year?

About $1800 to $2000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Honda Civic have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Civic year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2015.
