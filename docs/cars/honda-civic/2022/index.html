Quick answers

Is the 2022 Honda Civic reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 437; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Civic year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2022 Honda Civic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (85%), automatic emergency braking (10%) and lane assist (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Honda Civic have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Honda Civic get?

32 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Honda Civic safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2022 Honda Civic problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 14,000 and 46,600 miles. The middle report was at 25,500 miles, from 51 complaints.

Does the 2022 Honda Civic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Honda Civic cost in fuel a year?

About $1800 to $2000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Honda Civic have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Civic year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2015.
