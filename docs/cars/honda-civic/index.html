Quick answers.

Is the Honda Civic reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2022.

Is the Honda Civic a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 67; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2022 the most.

What are the best Honda Civic years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2013, 2021. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Honda Civic years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022 (avoid, Lemon Index 437, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (about average, Lemon Index 106, the first year of a new generation), 2012 (better than average, Lemon Index 57, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Civic have?

228 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2001 (27), 2002 (24), 2003 (24). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Civic problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, airbags and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
