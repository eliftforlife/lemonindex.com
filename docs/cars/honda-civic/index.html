Quick answers.

Is the Honda Civic reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.5× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2015. Avoid 2006, 2007 and 2022.

Is the Honda Civic a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 67; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2022.

What are the best Honda Civic years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2013, 2015.

What Honda Civic years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2022, 2006, 2007.

How many recalls does the Honda Civic have?

228 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2001 (27), 2002 (24), 2003 (24). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Civic problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, airbags and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
