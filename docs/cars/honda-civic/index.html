Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Honda Civic reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2006, 2007 and 2022.

Is the Honda Civic a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 67; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2022 the most.

What are the best Honda Civic years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2013, 2021. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Honda Civic years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022 (avoid, Lemon Index 437, the first year of a new generation), 2006 (avoid, Lemon Index 202, the first year of a new generation), 2007 (worse than average, Lemon Index 184). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Civic have?

228 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2001 (27), 2002 (24), 2003 (24). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda Civic recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda Civic problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, engine and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Honda Civic repairs cost?

The repairs Honda Civic owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $3,000, 53 reports), steering rack replacement (middle bill $4,000, 41 reports), AC condenser replacement (middle bill $700, 37 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 52 Honda Civic years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 2,162 YouTube videos about the Honda Civic, 1973 to 2026, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
