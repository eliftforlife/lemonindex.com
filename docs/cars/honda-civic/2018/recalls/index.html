2018 Honda Civic

2018 Honda Civic recalls and safety

Recalls 6

  • Airbags · recall issued 2026 New: most likely still open

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2023

    The fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2020

    The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

  • Equipment · recall issued 2018

    The owners guide in these vehicles may not have been included or if included, the owner's guide may not have been properly provided required information.

  • Steering · recall issued 2018

    The magnet that controls the torque sensor output signal for the electronic power steering system may not be properly secured, allowing the magnet to become dislodged.

  • Equipment · recall issued 2018

    The manufacturing date area of the certification labels may have random characters which can affect the owner's ability to determine if a safety recall includes their vehicle.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.
