Questions to ask the seller

  1. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (13 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  5. Jeep sent dealers 63 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Water Leaks At The Front And/Or Rear Roof Area This bulletin involves inspecting the front…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Jeep Compass reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better). 2008 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Jeep Compass year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2008 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (50%), steering (35%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Jeep Compass have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Jeep Compass get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Jeep Compass problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 54,000 and 111,000 miles (87,000–179,000 km) (the middle half of the 146 complaints, of all 172 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2008 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2008 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2008 Jeep Compass have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Jeep Compass cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Jeep Compass have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Jeep Compass year than the 2008?

Not nearby: the 2008 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Jeep Compass years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest.
