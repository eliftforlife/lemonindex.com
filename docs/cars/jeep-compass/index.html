Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Compass reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2013. Most complaints: 2014, 2016 and 2017.

Is the Jeep Compass a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Jeep Compass years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2015, 2021. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Jeep Compass years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 300), 2014 (worse than average, Lemon Index 197), 2022 (worse than average, Lemon Index 190). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Jeep Compass have?

37 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2018 (6), 2007 (4), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Compass problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
