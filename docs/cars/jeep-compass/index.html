Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Compass reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2013. Most complaints: 2014, 2018 and 2022.

Is the Jeep Compass a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Jeep Compass years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2015, 2021. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Jeep Compass years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (avoid, Complaint Score 300), 2014 (worse than average, Complaint Score 197), 2022 (worse than average, Complaint Score 190). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Jeep Compass have?

37 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2018 (6), 2007 (4), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Jeep Compass recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Jeep Compass problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 18 Jeep Compass years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 385 YouTube videos about the Jeep Compass, 2007 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
