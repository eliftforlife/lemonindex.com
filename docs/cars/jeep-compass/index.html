Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Compass reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2013. Avoid 2014, 2018 and 2022.

Is the Jeep Compass a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2018.

What are the best Jeep Compass years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2015, 2021.

What Jeep Compass years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2018, 2014, 2022.

How many recalls does the Jeep Compass have?

35 safety recalls across the 17 model years we track. Most: 2018 (6), 2007 (4), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Compass problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
