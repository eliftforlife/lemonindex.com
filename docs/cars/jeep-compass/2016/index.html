Quick answers

Is the 2016 Jeep Compass reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 116; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Compass year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2016 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (47%), electrical (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Jeep Compass have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Jeep Compass get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2016 Jeep Compass problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 37,800 and 71,000 miles. The middle report was at 55,000 miles, from 181 complaints.

Does the 2016 Jeep Compass have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Jeep Compass cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Jeep Compass have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Compass year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2015 and 2021.
