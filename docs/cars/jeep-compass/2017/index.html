Quick answers

Is the 2017 Jeep Compass reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 189; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Compass year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), engine (32%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Jeep Compass have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Jeep Compass get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Jeep Compass safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Jeep Compass problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 12,000 and 48,000 miles. The middle report was at 28,000 miles, from 159 complaints.

Does the 2017 Jeep Compass have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Jeep Compass cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Jeep Compass have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Compass year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2015 and 2021.
