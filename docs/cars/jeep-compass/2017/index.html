What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10231800: “2.4L Excessive Oil Consumption XB1 Warranty Extension The customer may describe one or more of the following: Oil…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10121812: “Flash: Transmission Diagnostic and Shift Enhancements AN-M4-21-20170929 Refer to China Joint Venture (JV) released…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Jeep Compass reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 189; 100 is average, lower is better). The Jeep Compass year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), engine (32%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Jeep Compass get?

At what mileage do 2017 Jeep Compass problems show up?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 17V824000 (equipment), 17V740000 (electrical) and 17V433000 (equipment) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Does the screen respond, and does the backup camera come on every time? (7 owners named a screen, radio & camera repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  6. Jeep sent dealers 259 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Power Transfer Unit (PTU) If you have a leak at the transmission to Power Transfer Unit (PTU)…”?
