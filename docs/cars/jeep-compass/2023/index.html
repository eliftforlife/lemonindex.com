Quick answers

Is the 2023 Jeep Compass reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.4× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 41; 100 is average). The best Jeep Compass year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2023 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), automatic emergency braking (21%) and steering (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Jeep Compass have?

2 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Jeep Compass get?

27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
