Quick answers

Is the 2022 Jeep Compass reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 190; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Compass year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2022 Jeep Compass problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (48%), automatic emergency braking (28%) and lights (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Jeep Compass have?

4 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Jeep Compass get?

25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
