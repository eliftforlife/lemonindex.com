2006 Mazda3: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Mazda3 drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 46 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 94,438 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Mazda3 complaints that listed a mileage came before 71,000 miles. Most came between 35,000 miles and 105,600 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 39,400 miles and 107,100 miles.
