Quick answers

Is the 2009 Mazda3 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 89; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda3 year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2009 Mazda3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (35%), tires (25%) and wheels (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Mazda3 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Mazda3 get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Mazda3 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,400 and 57,000 miles. The middle report was at 33,700 miles, from 97 complaints.

Does the 2009 Mazda3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 4% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Mazda3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Mazda3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.3L 4-cyl.

What Mazda3 year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2017.
