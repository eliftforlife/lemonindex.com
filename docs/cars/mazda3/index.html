Quick answers.

Is the Mazda3 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.8× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2017. Avoid 2008 and 2021.

Is the Mazda3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 56; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best Mazda3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2013, 2017.

What Mazda3 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008, 2021.

How many recalls does the Mazda3 have?

58 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2016 (6), 2019 (6), 2009 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
