Quick answers.

Is the Mazda3 reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2008 and 2021.

Is the Mazda3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 56; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2008 the most.

What are the best Mazda3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2013, 2017. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Mazda3 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2008 (worse than average, Complaint Score 151), 2021 (about average, Complaint Score 113), 2010 (about average, Complaint Score 107, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Mazda3 have?

58 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2016 (6), 2019 (6), 2009 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Mazda3 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Mazda3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Mazda3 repairs cost?

The repairs Mazda3 owners reported paying for most: clutch replacement (middle bill $1,000, 9 reports), ABS module replacement (middle bill $1,500, 5 reports), power steering pump replacement (middle bill $950, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 19 Mazda3 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 785 YouTube videos about the Mazda3, 2004 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
