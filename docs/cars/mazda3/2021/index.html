Quick answers

Is the 2021 Mazda3 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 113; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda3 year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2021 Mazda3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (39%), automatic emergency braking (17%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Mazda3 have?

1 safety recall (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Mazda3 get?

26 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Mazda3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Mazda3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Mazda3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2050 to $2450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Mazda3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Mazda3 year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2017.
