When problems show up

Half of the Mazda3 complaints that listed a mileage came before 5,000 miles. Most came between 2,000 miles and 10,500 miles.

The top problem area, brakes, usually showed up between 1,500 miles and 9,200 miles.

Recalls: 6

  • seats 2019
    The seat cover material on both front seats may interfere with the head restraint lock release button, causing it to stick in the release position and not allow the head restraint to lock into place. NHTSA 19V514000
  • wheels 2019
    The lug nuts on all four wheels may loosen, potentially resulting in a wheel detaching from the vehicle. NHTSA 19V425000
  • electrical 2019
    Due to incorrect programing, the passenger air bag deactivation indicator light may erroneously turn off although the passenger air bag is deactivated. NHTSA 19V363000
  • automatic emergency braking 2019
    The Smart Brake System (SBS) can falsely detect an obstacle while driving, activating the automatic emergency braking system and suddenly stopping the vehicle. NHTSA 19V907000
  • engine 2019
    A software error in the powertrain control module (PCM) may cause the engine to stall. NHTSA 19V497000
  • visibility 2019
    The adhesive between the interior rearview mirror and the housing may not be properly bonded, allowing the mirror to detach from the housing. NHTSA 19V558000
