2005 Mazda3 Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 22 yearsCompare

Is the 2005 Mazda3 reliable? Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mazda3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Mazda3 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mazda3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2005 Mazda3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (28%), airbags (17%) and equipment & accessories (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Mazda3 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Mazda3 get?

23 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Mazda3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 40,000 and 109,000 miles (64,000–175,000 km) (the middle half of the 77 complaints, of all 97 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2005 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2005 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2005 Mazda3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Mazda3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,400 to $2,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Mazda3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.3L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Mazda3 year than the 2005?

Not nearby: the 2005 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Mazda3 years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V494000 (airbags), 09E011000 (equipment) and 04V559000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (6 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $834.)
  5. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (6 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  6. Mazda sent dealers 211 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Engine vibration/ knocking noise/ no”?
