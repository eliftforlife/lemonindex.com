Quick answers

Is the 2022 Mazda3 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda3 year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2022 Mazda3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (61%), electrical (17%) and seat belts (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Mazda3 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Mazda3 get?

26 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
