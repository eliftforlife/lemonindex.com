Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mazda3 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 36; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda3 year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2018 Mazda3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (28%), engine (16%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mazda3 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mazda3 get?

28 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Mazda3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Mazda3 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 3,000 and 79,000 miles. The middle report was at 29,800 miles, from 37 complaints.

Does the 2018 Mazda3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Mazda3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Mazda3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Mazda3 year is better than the 2018?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2013 and 2017.
