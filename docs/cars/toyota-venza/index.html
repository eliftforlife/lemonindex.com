Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Venza reliable?

About average. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2021.

Is the Toyota Venza a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 86; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2021 the most.

What are the best Toyota Venza years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2011, 2014. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Venza years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2021 (avoid, Lemon Index 220), 2022 (better than average, Lemon Index 66), 2015 (better than average, Lemon Index 60). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Venza have?

42 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2010 (9), 2009 (8), 2011 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Venza recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Venza problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, speed control and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Toyota Venza years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the Toyota Venza, 2009 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
