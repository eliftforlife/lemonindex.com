How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Toyota Venza reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 60; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Venza year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 Toyota Venza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (38%), seats (23%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Toyota Venza have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Toyota Venza get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Toyota Venza safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2015 Toyota Venza have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Toyota Venza cost in fuel a year?

About $2,600 to $2,850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Toyota Venza have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Venza year is better than the 2015?

Nearby, the 2012 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 21V257000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do the seats adjust, heat and lock in place properly?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Toyota sent dealers 205 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Front Brake Vibration Key Points: * Issue can occur at any time if improper torque is applied…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the Toyota Venza, 2009 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
