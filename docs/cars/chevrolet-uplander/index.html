Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Uplander reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2007.

Is the Chevrolet Uplander a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2005-2008: Lemon Index 134; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2007 drew the fewest complaints and 2006 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Uplander years to buy?

Only 4 Chevrolet Uplander years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2007 drew the fewest complaints (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car).

What Chevrolet Uplander years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Chevrolet Uplander years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Uplander have?

11 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2005 (4), 2006 (4), 2007 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Uplander problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, doors, locks & latches and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Chevrolet Uplander years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Uplander, 2005 to 2009, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2009. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
