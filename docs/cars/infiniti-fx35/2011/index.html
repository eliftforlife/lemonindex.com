Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 11V592000 (engine) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Infiniti FX35 reliable?

We can't score it: only 9,939 were sold in the U.S. in 2011 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 3 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2011 Infiniti FX35 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (33%), brakes (33%) and suspension (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Infiniti FX35 have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Infiniti FX35 get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2011 Infiniti FX35 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 33% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Infiniti FX35 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,950 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Infiniti FX35 have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Infiniti FX35 year is better than the 2011?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2003 drew the fewest (about average).
