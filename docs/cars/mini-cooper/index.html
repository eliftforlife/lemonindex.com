Quick answers.

Is the Mini Cooper reliable?

Yes, very. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car. Fewest complaints: 2013.

Is the Mini Cooper a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 43; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Mini Cooper years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2012, 2022. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Mini Cooper years should you stay away from?

No Mini Cooper year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2014 (better than average, Lemon Index 72, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 67), 2015 (better than average, Lemon Index 59).

How many recalls does the Mini Cooper have?

56 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2014 (7), 2015 (6), 2005 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mini Cooper problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
