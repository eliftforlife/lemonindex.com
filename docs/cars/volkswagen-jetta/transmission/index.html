Volkswagen Jetta transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2006 (232 complaints), 2019 (124 complaints), 2009 (121 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2021, 2020, 2018.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the O-ring seals between the oil cooler and the transmission may leak fluid.

  • 2013 2013

    Due to gearbox fluid additives, the control module within the gearbox may corrode.

  • 2013 2019

    Hairline cracks can develop within the transmission electronic gearbox (mechatronic unit), causing an internal transmission oil leak and a decrease in oil pressure.

  • 2010 2009

    VOLKSWAGEN IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2009 AND 2010 PASSENGER CARS MANUFACTURED BETWEEN SEPTEMBER 2008 AND AUGUST 2009.

  • 2009 2009

    VOLKSWAGEN IS RECALLING 25,594 MY 2006-2009 JETTA 5TH GENERATION EQUIPPED WITH 2.01 ENGINE.

Quick answers

Which Volkswagen Jetta years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Volkswagen Jetta years are 2006, 2019, 2009.

What are common Volkswagen Jetta transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation.

Which Volkswagen Jetta years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Volkswagen Jetta years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2021, 2020, 2018.
