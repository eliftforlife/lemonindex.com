Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the O-ring seals between the oil cooler and the transmission may leak fluid.

  • 2013 2013

    Due to gearbox fluid additives, the control module within the gearbox may corrode.

  • 2013 2019

    Hairline cracks can develop within the transmission electronic gearbox (mechatronic unit), causing an internal transmission oil leak and a decrease in oil pressure.

Quick answers

Which Volkswagen Jetta years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Volkswagen Jetta years are 2006, 2019, 2009.

What are common Volkswagen Jetta transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation.

Which Volkswagen Jetta years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Volkswagen Jetta years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2021, 2020.
