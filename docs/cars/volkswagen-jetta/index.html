Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

About average. It draws 1.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2010, 2011 and 2019.

Is the Volkswagen Jetta a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 111; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Volkswagen Jetta years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018, 2020, 2015. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Volkswagen Jetta years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019 (avoid, Lemon Index 310, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (avoid, Lemon Index 286), 2011 (worse than average, Lemon Index 133, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Jetta have?

143 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2019 (11), 2011 (10), 2012 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Volkswagen Jetta problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
