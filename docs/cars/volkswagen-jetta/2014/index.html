Recalls 5

  • brakes 2020

    The fluid seal crimp on the master cylinder may be insufficient, causing brake fluid to leak or the cylinder to come apart.

  • electrical 2019

    A build-up of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may enable the key to be removed from the ignition when the transmission shift lever is not in "Park.".

  • airbags 2015

    In the affected vehicles, debris may contaminate the air bag clock spring, a spiral wound, flat cable that keeps the air bag powered while the steering wheel is being turned.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2015

    A sealing cap at the fuel rail may fail, allowing fuel to leak into the engine compartment.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the O-ring seals between the oil cooler and the transmission may leak fluid.

Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 1597 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Diagnosis for high pressure fuel pump where no start, engine crank, without driving, after long
  • When vehicle is at idle or the oil pressure light is on, there is no oil or have low oil pressure
  • During cold weather, engine fails to start, after standing, and if moisture, from intake air

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 78; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (25%), engine (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Volkswagen Jetta have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Volkswagen Jetta get?

25 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
