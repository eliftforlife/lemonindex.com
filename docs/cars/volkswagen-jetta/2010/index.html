2010 Volkswagen Jetta.

The 2010 Volkswagen Jetta is one of the worst years by owner complaints: 388 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 286, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about fuel system, diesel, it has 5 recalls, and it gets 25 to 32 mpg combined. The best Volkswagen Jetta year is 2018.

Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 1130 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Gff test plan, ground connection verification
  • Malfunction light is on and there is noise with the engine running rough
  • In some vehicles, the warning light for engine may be on and the engine power has failed

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 286; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2010 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, diesel (36%), engine (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Volkswagen Jetta problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 87,900 miles. The middle report was at 55,000 miles, from 796 complaints.

Does the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Volkswagen Jetta have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 5-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2020 and 2015.
