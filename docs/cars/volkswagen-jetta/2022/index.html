Quick answers

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 60; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2022 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (33%), engine (30%) and electrical (27%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta get?

33 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $1950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Volkswagen Jetta have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2020 and 2015.
