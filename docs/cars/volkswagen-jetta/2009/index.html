2009 Volkswagen Jetta: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Volkswagen Jetta drew 4.4× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 380 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 108,427 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Jetta complaints that listed a mileage came before 61,000 miles. Most came between 29,900 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, fuel system, diesel, usually showed up between 27,800 miles and 73,600 miles.
