Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 1058 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Concerns regarding fluid leaking from the transmission area, inspect the transmission oil pan's
  • Gff test plan, ground connection verification
  • Malfunction light is on and there is noise with the engine running rough

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 438; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2009 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, diesel (38%), transmission & drivetrain (26%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta get?

24 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Volkswagen Jetta problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 29,900 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 61,000 miles, from 818 complaints.

Does the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 26% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Volkswagen Jetta have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 5-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2020 and 2015.
