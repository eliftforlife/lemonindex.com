Quick answers

Is the 2003 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 225; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2003 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seats (26%), airbags (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Volkswagen Jetta have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Volkswagen Jetta get?

22 to 39 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
