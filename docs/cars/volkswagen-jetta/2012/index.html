This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20E064000 (brakes), 19V679000 (unknown or other) and 19V615000 (electrical) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into air bag clockspring failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  6. Volkswagen sent dealers 1543 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “In some vehicles, the warning light for engine may be on and the engine power has failed”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10253483: “Engine Exhaust Flap Limited Warranty Extension”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10050979: “Volkswagen: depending on the driving conditions, a slight hesitation, related to concerns of dsg in vehicles ,with…”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10125617: “Technical Bulletin (Update Programming - MIL ON, DTC P240F Stored in ECM Fault Memory): The following fault code…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 1543 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • In some vehicles, the warning light for engine may be on and the engine power has failed
  • All models experience the timing chain slipping, rattling noises of the engine after starting
  • Possible oil leak from oil filter housing

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 93; 100 is average, lower is better). The Volkswagen Jetta year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2012 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), engine (21%) and fuel or ev drive system (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta have?

8 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Volkswagen Jetta problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 34,000 and 80,000 miles (the middle half of the 428 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $2450 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Volkswagen Jetta have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 5-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (1.8× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest.
