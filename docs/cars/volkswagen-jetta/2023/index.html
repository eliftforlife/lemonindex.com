Quick answers

Is the 2023 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.3× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2023 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (26%), brakes (23%) and electrical (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Volkswagen Jetta have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Volkswagen Jetta get?

33 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
