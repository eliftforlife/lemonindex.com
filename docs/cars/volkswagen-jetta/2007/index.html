2007 Volkswagen Jetta: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Volkswagen Jetta drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 81 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 98,951 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Jetta complaints that listed a mileage came before 53,700 miles. Most came between 26,700 miles and 90,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 47,600 miles and 98,500 miles.
