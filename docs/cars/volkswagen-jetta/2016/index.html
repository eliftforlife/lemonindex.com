Recalls 5

  • fuel system, gasoline 2020

    The fuel rail bolts may become loose over time, allowing fuel to leak.

  • brakes 2020

    The fluid seal crimp on the master cylinder may be insufficient, causing brake fluid to leak or the cylinder to come apart.

  • electrical 2019

    A build-up of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may enable the key to be removed from the ignition when the transmission shift lever is not in "Park.".

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2016 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (26%), electrical (25%) and engine (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta have?

5 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta get?

27 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Volkswagen Jetta problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 17,000 and 69,000 miles. The middle report was at 41,600 miles, from 95 complaints.

Does the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $1800 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Volkswagen Jetta have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2020 and 2015.
