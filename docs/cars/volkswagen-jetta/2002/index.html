What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10000581: “Misfire, incorrect cylinder fault codes p0301, p0302, p0303 or p0304 identified by ecm software”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10156129: “Transmission Control Module Updates: This document informs of the software, and technical bulletins that service…”)

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 282; 100 is average, lower is better). The Volkswagen Jetta year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2002 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), seats (18%) and airbags (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta get?

19 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Volkswagen Jetta problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 26,000 and 87,000 miles (42,000–140,000 km) (the middle half of the 487 complaints, of all 702 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2002 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2002 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $3,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 1.9L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.8L 6-cyl.

What Volkswagen Jetta year is better than the 2002?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14E007000 (engine), 07E023000 (brakes) and 07V063000 (lights) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the seats adjust, heat and lock in place properly?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (55 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $2,200.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (37 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $1,100.)
  7. Volkswagen sent dealers 242 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Tech Tips (Air Conditioning Does not Operate): This document informs of concerns of no or…”?
