Quick answers

Is the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 282; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2002 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), seats (18%) and airbags (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Volkswagen Jetta get?

19 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
