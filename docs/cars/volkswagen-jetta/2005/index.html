Problems Volkswagen told dealers about

Volkswagen sent dealers 393 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Engine pre-heater installation
  • Engine cranks intermittently or has no crank
  • Gff test plan, ground connection verification

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Volkswagen Jetta reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 122; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Jetta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2005 Volkswagen Jetta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (31%), engine (25%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Volkswagen Jetta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Volkswagen Jetta get?

22 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
