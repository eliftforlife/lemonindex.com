2001 Volkswagen Jetta: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Volkswagen Jetta drew 2.5× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 386 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 145,221 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Volkswagen Jetta complaints that listed a mileage came before 50,000 miles. Most came between 30,000 miles and 87,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 25,500 miles and 68,200 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
