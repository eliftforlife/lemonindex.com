  • 17V821000 Dec 2017 Power Train

    Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2016-2017 Ram 3500 Cab Chassis with a Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) of less than 10,000 lbs, 2011-2017 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 Cab Chassis vehicles, 2009-2017 Ram 1500 and 2010-2017 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickup trucks, all equipped with a column shifter. Pushing the brake pedal for prolonged periods when the vehicle is running and in PARK may cause the Brake Transmission Shift Interlock (BTSI) pin to stick in the open position. With the pin in the open position, the transmission can be shifted out of PARK into any gear without pushing the brake pedal or having the key in the ignition.

    Fix. Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will update the vehicle software, inspect the BTSI, and, as necessary, replace it, free of charge. The recall began February 26, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is T79.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Ram 2500/3500 reliable?

We can't score it per truck (heavy-duty sales aren't reported apart from the half-ton). Owners filed 293 complaints about it, most often about engine (26% of them). Compare the diesel generations on their engine pages.

What engine does the 2013 Ram 2500/3500 have?

By the VINs in owner complaints: 6.7 Cummins diesel (91%), 5.7 V8 gas (9%).

How many recalls does the 2013 Ram 2500/3500 (Heavy Duty) have?

17. Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check the truck's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

The whole-line score for 2013, mostly half-tons: 2013 Ram pickup.

No Lemon Index score: automakers report heavy-duty pickup sales together with the half-ton (one F-Series, Ram or Silverado number), so there is no honest per-truck denominator. These are counts and shares of owner complaints, not failure rates. Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under the truck's 2500/3500 (F-250/F-350) names, all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN (position 8 is the engine code) with NHTSA's vPIC decoder. Complaints filed as just “Silverado” or “Sierra” are left out (they mix 1500s and HDs). Older trucks have had more years to collect complaints, and popular years draw more because more were sold.
