Quick answers

Is the 2007 Ram 2500/3500 reliable?

We can't score it per truck (heavy-duty sales aren't reported apart from the half-ton). Owners filed 578 complaints about it, most often about steering (50% of them). Compare the diesel generations on their engine pages.

What engine does the 2007 Ram 2500/3500 have?

By the VINs in owner complaints: 6.7 Cummins diesel (50%), 5.9 Cummins diesel (35%), 5.7 V8 gas (15%).

How many recalls does the 2007 Ram 2500/3500 (Heavy Duty) have?

14. Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check the truck's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

The whole-line score for 2007, mostly half-tons: 2007 Ram pickup.

No Lemon Index score: automakers report heavy-duty pickup sales together with the half-ton (one F-Series, Ram or Silverado number), so there is no honest per-truck denominator. These are counts and shares of owner complaints, not failure rates. Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under the truck's 2500/3500 (F-250/F-350) names, all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN (position 8 is the engine code) with NHTSA's vPIC decoder. Complaints filed as just “Silverado” or “Sierra” are left out (they mix 1500s and HDs). Older trucks have had more years to collect complaints, and popular years draw more because more were sold.
