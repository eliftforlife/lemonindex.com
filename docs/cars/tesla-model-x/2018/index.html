Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V376000 (seat belts), 24V051000 (electrical) and 23V838000 (electrical) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Tesla Model X reliable?

We can't score it: Tesla doesn't report U.S. sales by model, and no estimate covers this model year on its own, so there is no per-car rate. Owners filed 74 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2018 Tesla Model X problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (26%), electrical (23%) and speed control (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Tesla Model X have?

9 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2018 Tesla Model X safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Tesla Model X problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,200 and 21,000 miles (6,700–34,000 km) (the middle half of the 58 complaints, of all 74 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Tesla Model X have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Tesla Model X cost in fuel a year?

About $800 to $900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Tesla Model X year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit more than the average car's complaints).
