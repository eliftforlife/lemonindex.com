Quick answers.

Is the Ford Ranger reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011.

Is the Ford Ranger a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 48; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Ford Ranger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2010, 2022. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Ford Ranger years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019 (about average, Lemon Index 106), 2021 (better than average, Lemon Index 61), 2020 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 49). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Ranger have?

117 safety recalls across the 14 model years we track. Most: 2024 (13), 2025 (13), 2006 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Ranger problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
