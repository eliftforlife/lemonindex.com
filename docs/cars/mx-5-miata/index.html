Known issues

  • Radiator plastic tanks crackParts seller

    The stock radiator has plastic end tanks. Flyin' Miata says they last about a decade and should be replaced once they discolor.

    Fix: Replace the radiator before the tanks crack.

  • Ignition coil faults (2000)Owner-reported

    Several 1999–2000 owners told NHTSA of failing ignition coils that set the check-engine light or caused rough running.

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2005)
  • Mazdaspeed fuel pump failuresOwner-reported

    Two 2004 Mazdaspeed owners told NHTSA of repeat fuel pump failures causing stalls and no-starts. Too few reports to call a pattern.

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2005)

    Fix: Replace an aging tank, or fit an aluminum one.

Known issues

  • No known engine patternOwner-reported

    Few NHTSA complaints for 2016–2025 MX-5s involve the engine; most are about the manual gearbox and an air bag recall.

  • Fuel pump recallRecall

    On some 2018–2019 MX-5s, the low-pressure fuel pump impeller can crack and deform, so the pump can fail and the engine stall.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-875).

    8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2025)
  • Manual gearbox failures (transmission)Owner-reported

    Owners report third gear that grinds or won't engage, and some outright failures, mostly 2016 and 2022–2024 cars. No recall; rates unverified.

    8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2025)
  • Automatic downshift recall (transmission)Recall

    On some 2016–2019 automatics, transmission software could cause an unexpected downshift and sudden slowing.

    Fix: Dealer reprograms the transmission computer for free (NHTSA 19V-072).

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2025)

Quick answers

How fast is the Mazda MX-5 Club?

The ND MX-5 Club makes 181 hp and 151 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.4 seconds.

What engine does the Mazda MX-5 Miata have?

The ND Miata (2016–2025) uses the Mazda Skyactiv-G 2.0, a 2.0-liter inline-4, direct injection. Older generations: NB: Mazda BP 1.8; NC: Mazda MZR 2.0.
