2005 Toyota Tundra: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Toyota Tundra drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 58 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 126,529 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tundra complaints that listed a mileage came before 82,200 miles. Most came between 43,100 miles and 132,500 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 77,000 miles and 154,400 miles.
