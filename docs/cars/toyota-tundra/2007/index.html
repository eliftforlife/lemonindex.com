2007 Toyota Tundra: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Toyota Tundra drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 177 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 196,555 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tundra complaints that listed a mileage came before 48,200 miles. Most came between 13,000 miles and 85,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 22,000 miles and 97,600 miles.
