Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Tundra reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.8× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2015. Avoid 2000 and 2022.

Is the Toyota Tundra a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 55; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2015 and the worst is 2022.

What are the best Toyota Tundra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2011, 2013.

What Toyota Tundra years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2022, 2000.

How many recalls does the Toyota Tundra have?

235 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2010 (16), 2005 (14), 2007 (14). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Tundra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
