Quick answers

Is the 2001 Toyota Tundra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 69; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tundra year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2001 Toyota Tundra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (71%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and engine (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Toyota Tundra have?

5 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Toyota Tundra get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
