2001 Toyota Tundra: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Toyota Tundra drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 79 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 108,863 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tundra complaints that listed a mileage came before 102,000 miles. Most came between 63,200 miles and 148,500 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 90,000 miles and 153,800 miles.
