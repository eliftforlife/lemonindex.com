2003 Toyota Tundra: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Toyota Tundra drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 51 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 101,316 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tundra complaints that listed a mileage came before 98,600 miles. Most came between 47,000 miles and 137,400 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 79,800 miles and 153,200 miles.
