Quick answers

Is the 2009 Toyota Tundra reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 18 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Toyota Tundra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (56%), speed control (28%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Toyota Tundra have?

13 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Toyota Tundra get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
