Quick answers

Is the 2018 Toyota Tundra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tundra year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2018 Toyota Tundra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (37%), electrical (33%) and speed control (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Toyota Tundra have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Toyota Tundra get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Toyota Tundra safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Toyota Tundra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,600 and 25,800 miles. The middle report was at 7,900 miles, from 48 complaints.

Does the 2018 Toyota Tundra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Toyota Tundra cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Toyota Tundra have?

4.6L 8-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Toyota Tundra year is better than the 2018?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2011 and 2013.
