Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the Audi RS 7 performance?

The C8 RS 7 performance makes 621 hp and 627 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 2.9 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (limited in the U.S. pricing story; Audi's reveal listed 174 mph with the RS Dynamic package and 190 mph with Dynamic plus).

What engine does the Audi RS 7 have?

The C8 RS 7 (2021–) uses the VW Group 4.0 TFSI V8 (EA825), a 4.0-liter V-8, twin-turbo, 48-volt mild hybrid. Older generations: C7: Audi 4.0 TFSI V8 (EA824).

Which Audi RS 7 engine do YouTube owners like most?

VW Group 4.0 TFSI V8 (EA825) 54/100, Audi 4.0 TFSI V8 (EA824) 53/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
