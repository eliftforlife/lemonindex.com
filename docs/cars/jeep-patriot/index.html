Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Patriot reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2009, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Jeep Patriot a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 126; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Jeep Patriot years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2013, 2017.

What Jeep Patriot years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2009, 2012.

How many recalls does the Jeep Patriot have?

17 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2011 (4), 2012 (4), 2010 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Patriot problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
