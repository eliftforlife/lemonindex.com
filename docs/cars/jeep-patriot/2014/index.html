Quick answers

Is the 2014 Jeep Patriot reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 261; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Patriot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2014 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (44%), seat belts (34%) and engine (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Jeep Patriot have?

2 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
