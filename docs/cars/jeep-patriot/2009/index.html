Quick answers

Is the 2009 Jeep Patriot reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 174; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Patriot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2009 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (33%), tires (19%) and suspension (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Jeep Patriot have?

1 safety recall (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
