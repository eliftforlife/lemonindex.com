Quick answers

Is the 2009 Jeep Patriot reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 174; 100 is average, lower is better). The Jeep Patriot year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2009 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (31%), suspension (17%) and tires (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Jeep Patriot have?

1 safety recall (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Jeep Patriot problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 39,000 and 112,000 miles (63,000–179,000 km) (the middle half of the 229 complaints, of all 275 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2009 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2009 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2009 Jeep Patriot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Jeep Patriot cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Jeep Patriot have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Patriot year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 08V528000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (19 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $4,000.)
  5. Jeep sent dealers 39 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Water Leaks At The Front And/Or Rear Roof Area This bulletin involves inspecting the front…”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Jeep Patriot years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Jeep Patriot, 2007 to 2017, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2012. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Complaint Score counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
