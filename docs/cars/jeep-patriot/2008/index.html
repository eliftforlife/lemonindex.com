Quick answers

Is the 2008 Jeep Patriot reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 153; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Patriot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2008 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (49%), body structure (23%) and steering (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Jeep Patriot have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Jeep Patriot problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 43,400 and 107,200 miles. The middle report was at 74,200 miles, from 382 complaints.

Does the 2008 Jeep Patriot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Jeep Patriot cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Jeep Patriot have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Patriot year is better than the 2008?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2013 and 2017.
