Quick answers

Is the 2013 Jeep Patriot reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 41; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Patriot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2013 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (29%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and airbags (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Jeep Patriot have?

1 safety recall (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 Jeep Patriot safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 Jeep Patriot problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 20,200 and 80,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 77 complaints.

Does the 2013 Jeep Patriot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Jeep Patriot cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Jeep Patriot have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Patriot year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2013 and 2017.
