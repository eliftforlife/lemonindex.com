Quick answers

Is the 2017 Jeep Patriot reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 65; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Patriot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2017 Jeep Patriot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (25%), seat belts (14%) and steering (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Jeep Patriot have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Jeep Patriot get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
