Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22V925000 (fuel system, gasoline), 22V792000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 22V384000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Ford sent dealers 29 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some 2021-2023 F-Super Duty/E-Series vehicles equipped with a 6.2L, 6.8L or 7.3L gasoline…”?

Recalls 3

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2022

    The paint along the weld seam of the fuel tank may peel and lead to corrosion of the exposed metal, which can result in a fuel leak.

  • Transmission & drivetrain · recall issued 2022

    The bolt that secures the rear axle rotor to the wheel-hub may be improperly tightened, possibly resulting in wheel separation.

  • Equipment · recall issued 2022

    In addition, certain 2016 E-Series, 2017 and 2019 F-450, 2021 F-350, and 2021 Transit vehicles that received a reprinted label are also included in this recall.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2023 Ford E-Series reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 6 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Ford E-Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), suspension (33%) and body structure (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Ford E-Series have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2023 Ford E-Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

What Ford E-Series year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.
