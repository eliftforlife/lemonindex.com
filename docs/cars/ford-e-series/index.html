Quick answers.

Is the Ford E-Series reliable?

Mostly. It draws about a quarter as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010.

Is the Ford E-Series a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 23; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2000 the most.

What are the best Ford E-Series years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2019, 2018. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Ford E-Series years should you stay away from?

No Ford E-Series year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2000 (better than average, Complaint Score 54), 2021 (better than average, Complaint Score 51), 2016 (better than average, Complaint Score 50).

How many recalls does the Ford E-Series have?

298 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2006 (43), 2007 (36), 2008 (34). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford E-Series recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford E-Series problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, tires and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford E-Series repairs cost?

The repairs Ford E-Series owners reported paying for most: spark plug replacement (middle bill $2,000, 8 reports), fuel injector replacement (middle bill $3,000, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Ford E-Series years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 280 YouTube videos about the Ford E-Series, 1992 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 1999. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
