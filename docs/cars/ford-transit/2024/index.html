Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 26V061000 (body structure), 25V876000 (equipment adaptive/mobility) and 25V623000 (steering) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Ford sent dealers 86 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “During service and repair requiring removal and replacement of a crankshaft bolt and/or…”?

Questions6 quick answers

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Ford Transit reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about a fifth as many complaints as the average car per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 21; 100 is average). The Ford Transit year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2019.

What are the most common 2024 Ford Transit problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (21%), electrical (18%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Ford Transit have?

10 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2024 Ford Transit safe?

NHTSA gave it 3 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Ford Transit have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

What Ford Transit year is better than the 2024?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest.
