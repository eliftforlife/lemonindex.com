Known issues

  • Coolant pump noise and hard starts (service action)Maker bulletin

    VW found that the coolant pump caused engine-bay noise and hard starts on some 2012–2013 Golf Rs.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the coolant pump for free (Service Action 19J5).

  • No other known patternOwner-reported

    Owners filed only five NHTSA complaints for 2012–2013 Golf Rs, and none shows a repeating engine failure.

Known issues

  • Turbo wastegate sticks (class settlement)Maker bulletin

    Rust can seize the turbo wastegate rod on 2015–2019 Golf Rs, setting code P2563 or P00AF. VW denies a defect but settled a class action.

    Fix: VW pays 50% of a turbo repair for 8.5 years or 85,000 miles (VWP-25-16); TSB 21-25-01 first tries freeing the rod.

  • Fuel hose recall (2018)Recall

    On some 2018 Golf Rs, the fuel hose connector in the engine bay can come loose and leak fuel, raising fire risk.

    Fix: Dealer secures the fuel line connection for free (NHTSA 18V-864).

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2019)
  • Coolant leaks at the water pumpOwner-reported

    A few owners told NHTSA of coolant leaks that dealers traced to the water pump and thermostat housing.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2019)

Known issues

  • Engine cover recall (2022)Recall

    Hard acceleration could loosen the engine cover on 2022 Golf Rs; it can touch hot parts and melt, raising fire risk.

    Fix: Dealer removes the cover, then fits an updated one for free (NHTSA 22V-163).

  • Coolant hose recall (2022–2023)Recall

    The radiator may not be fixed to the intercooler correctly, which can damage a coolant hose and let the engine overheat.

    Fix: Dealer inspects the radiator and replaces damaged parts for free (NHTSA 22V-707).

  • Thermostat fault code P00B7Maker bulletin

    Some 2022–2024 Golf Rs set P00B7 (low coolant flow) and the check-engine light. VW says not to replace parts first.

    Fix: Dealer updates the engine computer software (TSB 01-25-05).

Quick answers

How fast is the Volkswagen Golf R (2025 update)?

The Mk8 Golf R (2025 update) makes 328 hp and 295 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.1 seconds.

What engine does the Volkswagen Golf R have?

The Mk8 Golf R (2022–2025) uses the VW EA888 evo4 2.0 TSI (Golf R), a 2.0-liter inline-4, turbo. Older generations: Mk6: VW EA113 2.0 TSI (Golf R); Mk7 / Mk7.5: VW EA888 Gen 3 2.0 TSI (Golf R).
