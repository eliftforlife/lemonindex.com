Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Volvo S60 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014.

Is the Volvo S60 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2012-2014: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints and 2012 the most.

What are the best Volvo S60 years to buy?

Only 3 Volvo S60 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2014 drew the fewest complaints (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

What Volvo S60 years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Volvo S60 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Volvo S60 have?

51 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2012 (8), 2023 (6), 2017 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Volvo S60 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Volvo S60 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 12 Volvo S60 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 596 YouTube videos about the Volvo S60, 2001 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2012. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
