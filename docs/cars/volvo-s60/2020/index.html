Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 25V179000 (electrical), 23V780000 (airbags) and 21V414000 (electrical) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Volvo S60 reliable?

We can't score it: only 11,946 were sold in the U.S. in 2020 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2020 Volvo S60 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (43%), electrical (29%) and steering (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Volvo S60 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Volvo S60 get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Volvo S60 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2020 Volvo S60 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Volvo S60 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,500 to $3,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Volvo S60 have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volvo S60 year is better than the 2020?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
