Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.5× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2013, 2014 and 2015.

Is the Nissan Pathfinder a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 150; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Nissan Pathfinder years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2010, 2020. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Pathfinder years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 478, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 239), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 162). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Pathfinder have?

74 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2013 (11), 2014 (11), 2006 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Pathfinder problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
