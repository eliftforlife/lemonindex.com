Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.5× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2013, 2014 and 2015.

Is the Nissan Pathfinder a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 150; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Nissan Pathfinder years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2010, 2020.

What Nissan Pathfinder years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2014, 2015.

How many recalls does the Nissan Pathfinder have?

70 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2013 (11), 2014 (11), 2006 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Pathfinder problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
