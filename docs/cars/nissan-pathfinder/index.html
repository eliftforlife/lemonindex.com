Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Pathfinder a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 150; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Nissan Pathfinder years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2010, 2020.

What Nissan Pathfinder years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2014, 2015.
