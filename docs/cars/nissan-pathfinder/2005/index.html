2005 Nissan Pathfinder.

The 2005 Nissan Pathfinder is worse than average by owner complaints: 61 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 116, where 100 is the average car. Quick answers

Is the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 116; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Pathfinder year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2005 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (33%), engine (16%) and fuel system, other (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder get?

16 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Nissan Pathfinder problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 70,000 and 115,000 miles. The middle report was at 90,000 miles, from 954 complaints.

Does the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Nissan Pathfinder have?

4.0L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Pathfinder year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2020.
