Nissan Pathfinder transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2005 (566 complaints), 2006 (437 complaints), 2007 (331 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2012, 2016.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Nissan Pathfinder years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Nissan Pathfinder years are 2005, 2006, 2007.

What are common Nissan Pathfinder transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Nissan Pathfinder years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Nissan Pathfinder years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2012, 2016.
