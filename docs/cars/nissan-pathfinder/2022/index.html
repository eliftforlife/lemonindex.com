Quick answers

Is the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 96; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Pathfinder year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2022 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder have?

5 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Pathfinder year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2020.
