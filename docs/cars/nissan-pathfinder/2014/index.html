Recalls 11

  • latches/locks/linkages 2022

    On certain Pathfinder vehicles, the secondary hood latch may malfunction and remain in the unlatched, unsecured, position when the hood is closed.

  • lights 2021

    The location of the brake light switch may cause the brake light switch relay to repeatedly open and close.

  • lights 2016

    The brake light switch may have been installed incorrectly causing the relay to make intermittent contact and repeatedly cycle.

Show all 11 recalls
  • airbags 2016

    In these vehicles, the front seat passenger Occupant Classification System (OCS) may incorrectly classify an adult passenger as a child or classify the seat as empty despite it being occupied.

  • airbags 2015

    The affected vehicles received an incorrect occupant classification system (OCS) control unit during a repair.

  • body structure 2015

    In the affected vehicles the hood release cable assembly may have been installed incorrectly preventing the latching.

  • brakes 2015

    During the assembly process, the front wheel hub assembly fasteners may not have been properly torqued.

  • wheels 2014

    The right side wheels of the affected vehicles may have one lug nut each that was not properly tightened.

  • electrical 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the occupant classification system (OCS) software may incorrectly classify the passenger seat as empty, when it is occupied by an adult.

  • brakes 2013

    In the affected vehicles, during light braking on rough roads, the antilock brake system (ABS) brake pressure output software may lead to an increase in stopping distance.

  • airbags 2013

    In the event of a crash necessitating deployment of the passenger's frontal air bag, the improper air bag deployment may increase the risk of personal injury.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 239; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Pathfinder year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2014 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (36%), transmission & drivetrain (35%) and wipers & visibility (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Nissan Pathfinder have?

11 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Nissan Pathfinder get?

21 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
