Quick answers

Is the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 7.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 13; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2012 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (17%), engine (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have?

2 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder cost in fuel a year?

About $4700 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 5.6L 8-cyl.
