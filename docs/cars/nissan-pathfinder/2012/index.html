What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10192821: “SERVICE INFORMATION Replacement 5-speed rear wheel drive Automatic Transmission (A/T) assemblies and replacement…”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10192591: “SERVICE INFORMATION After ECM reprogramming, the Calibration Verification Number (CVN) needs to be automatically…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 13; 100 is average, lower is better). 2012 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.5× the average car's complaints.

What are the most common 2012 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), airbags (17%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have?

2 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder cost in fuel a year?

About $4,700 to $5,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Nissan Pathfinder have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 5.6L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Nissan Pathfinder year than the 2012?

Not nearby: the 2012 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Nissan Pathfinder years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 12V462000 (suspension) and 11V592000 (engine) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (249 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $5,000.)
  6. Any overheating, coolant smell or leaks? Ask whether the water pump or radiator was replaced. (121 owners named a cooling repair bill; the middle one was $5,000.)
  7. Nissan sent dealers 106 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “CLICKING NOISE FROM FRONT OR REAR AXLE DURING TAKE-OFF/ACCELERATION This bulletin has been…”?
