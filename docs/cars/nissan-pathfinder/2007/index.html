Quick answers

Is the 2007 Nissan Pathfinder reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 134; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Pathfinder year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2007 Nissan Pathfinder problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (60%), fuel system, other (17%) and electrical (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Nissan Pathfinder have?

2 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Nissan Pathfinder get?

16 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
