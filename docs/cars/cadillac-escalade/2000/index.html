Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Cadillac Escalade reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 167; 100 is average, lower is better). 2000 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. The Cadillac Escalade year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2000 Cadillac Escalade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (16%), brakes (16%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Cadillac Escalade have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Cadillac Escalade get?

13 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2000 Cadillac Escalade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Cadillac Escalade cost in fuel a year?

About $4,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Cadillac Escalade have?

5.7L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac Escalade year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2002 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07E088000 (equipment), 06E043000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 06E026000 (lights) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 334 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Left front axle shaft difficult to remove”?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
