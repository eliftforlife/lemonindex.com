Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Cadillac Escalade reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2022.

Is the Cadillac Escalade a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 65; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2022 the most.

What are the best Cadillac Escalade years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2019, 2018. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Cadillac Escalade years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022 (worse than average, Lemon Index 172), 2016 (about average, Lemon Index 104), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 91, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Cadillac Escalade have?

91 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2021 (11), 2015 (9), 2003 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Cadillac Escalade recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Cadillac Escalade problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 22 Cadillac Escalade years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 796 YouTube videos about the Cadillac Escalade, 2002 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
