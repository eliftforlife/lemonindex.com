How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Cadillac Escalade reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac Escalade year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2012 Cadillac Escalade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (78%), brakes (33%) and speed control (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Cadillac Escalade have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Cadillac Escalade get?

14 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Cadillac Escalade safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2012 Cadillac Escalade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Cadillac Escalade cost in fuel a year?

About $2,600 to $3,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Cadillac Escalade have?

6.0L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac Escalade year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V054000 (airbags), 21V051000 (airbags) and 14V827000 (electrical) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Cadillac sent dealers 1677 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some trucks and passenger vehicles, accessory power outlets (apo) and trailer lighting are”?
