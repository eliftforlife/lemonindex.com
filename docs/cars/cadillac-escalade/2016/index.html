Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V645000 (brakes), 17V437000 (airbags) and 16V651000 (airbags) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 1826 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information on Transmission Adaptive Functions and Correcting Low Mileage Harsh Shifts, Slips,…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Cadillac Escalade reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 104; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac Escalade year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2016 Cadillac Escalade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (34%), suspension (22%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Cadillac Escalade have?

4 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Cadillac Escalade get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2016 Cadillac Escalade problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,600 and 47,000 miles (9,000–75,000 km) (the middle half of the 34 complaints, of all 41 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2016 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2016 Cadillac Escalade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 34% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Cadillac Escalade cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Cadillac Escalade have?

6.2L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac Escalade year is better than the 2016?

Nearby, the 2018 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.
