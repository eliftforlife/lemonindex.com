Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V619000 (fuel system, diesel), 25V274000 (engine) and 24V797000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 11 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into loss of motive power due to engine failure post recall remedy. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Cadillac Escalade reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 51; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac Escalade year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2021 Cadillac Escalade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (55%), transmission & drivetrain (30%) and automatic emergency braking (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Cadillac Escalade have?

11 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, diesel). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Cadillac Escalade get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Cadillac Escalade safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Cadillac Escalade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 30% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Cadillac Escalade cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $4,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Cadillac Escalade have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Cadillac Escalade year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Cadillac Escalade years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.
