How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Cadillac Escalade reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 7 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2005 Cadillac Escalade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), equipment & accessories (43%) and airbags (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Cadillac Escalade have?

4 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Cadillac Escalade get?

13 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2005 Cadillac Escalade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Cadillac Escalade cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $5,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Cadillac Escalade have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac Escalade year is better than the 2005?

Nearby, the 2004 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07E106000 (wheels), 06E043000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 05V163000 (seat belts) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Cadillac sent dealers 760 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Intermittent no start, service stability message, anti-lock brake system (abs) light…”?
