Known issues

  • Brake-assist recall (engine software)Recall

    On about 13,000 2020–2021 Supras, engine software could damage the part that powers brake assist on certain starts, lengthening stops.

    Fix: Dealer updates the engine computer for free (NHTSA 21V-598).

  • Oil filter housing leaksParts seller

    The plastic housing and its gaskets can warp with heat and leak oil or coolant, often around 60,000–100,000 miles.

    Fix: Replace the housing and gaskets.

  • Coolant lossOwner-reported

    Owners report coolant leaks. A parts seller names the water pump seal and the heat management module as common sources.

  • Valve cover and PCVOwner-reported

    Valve cover gasket leaks and PCV valve faults are the most-reported B58 problems on owner forums, per SlashGear.

Quick answers

How fast is the Toyota GR Supra 3.0?

The A90 (J29) GR Supra 3.0 makes 382 hp and 368 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.7 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (limited).

What engine does the Toyota GR Supra have?

The A90 (J29) GR Supra (2020–2025) uses the BMW B58, a 3.0-liter inline-6, turbo.
