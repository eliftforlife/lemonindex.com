Quick answers.

Is the Ford Bronco Sport reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.9× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2022, still drew 2.2× the average.

Is the Ford Bronco Sport a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2021-2022: Lemon Index 287; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2021 the most.

What are the best Ford Bronco Sport years to buy?

Only 2 Ford Bronco Sport years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2022 drew the fewest complaints (about twice the average car's complaints).

What Ford Bronco Sport years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Ford Bronco Sport years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Ford Bronco Sport have?

60 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2021 (16), 2022 (15), 2023 (14). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Bronco Sport recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Bronco Sport problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
