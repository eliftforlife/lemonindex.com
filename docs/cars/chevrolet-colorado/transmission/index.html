Chevrolet Colorado transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (84 complaints), 2016 (68 complaints), 2018 (62 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2012, 2011.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Colorado years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Colorado years are 2017, 2016, 2018.

What are common Chevrolet Colorado transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation.

Which Chevrolet Colorado years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Colorado years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2012, 2011.
