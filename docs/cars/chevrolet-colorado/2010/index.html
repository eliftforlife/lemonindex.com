Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chevrolet Colorado reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 19; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Chevrolet Colorado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), equipment & accessories (33%) and transmission & drivetrain (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chevrolet Colorado have?

2 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chevrolet Colorado get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
