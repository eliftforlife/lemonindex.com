Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Colorado reliable?

Mostly. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2015 and 2016.

Is the Chevrolet Colorado a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Colorado years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2020, 2022. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Colorado years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 150), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 148), 2017 (about average, Lemon Index 90). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Colorado have?

49 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2011 (5), 2006 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Colorado problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
