2005 Chevrolet Colorado: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Chevrolet Colorado drew 1.5× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 135 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 128,359 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Colorado complaints that listed a mileage came before 51,000 miles. Most came between 22,000 miles and 86,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 35,000 miles and 95,000 miles.
