Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Colorado reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 150; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Colorado year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Colorado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (34%), steering (27%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Colorado have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Colorado get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
