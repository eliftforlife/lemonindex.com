2017 Ford F-150.

The 2017 Ford F-150 is worse than average by owner complaints: 1,516 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 160, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about steering, it has 28 recalls, and it gets 16 to 21 mpg combined. The best Ford F-150 year is 2007.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Ford F-150 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 160; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2017 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (54%), suspension (42%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Ford F-150 have?

28 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Ford F-150 get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Ford F-150 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 19,000 and 55,000 miles. The middle report was at 32,000 miles, from 1,989 complaints.

Does the 2017 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Ford F-150 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
