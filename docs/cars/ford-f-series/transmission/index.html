Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2025 2025

    The transmission valve body may have been machined incorrectly, which can cause reverse gear failure, or unexpected forward movement in reverse or neutral gear.

  • 2025 2025

    The rear axle hub bolt may fatigue and break, which can result in damage to the axle hub splines.

  • 2023 2023

    The transmission may contain a loose bolt which could prevent the transmission from engaging the park gear, although the gear shifter position may indicate that the vehicle has been shifted to "PARK.".

  • 2023 2023

    The front axle pinion may not have been heat-treated, which can cause it to fracture.

  • 2023 2025

    The integrated park module may fail to lock into the park position when the driver shifts into park.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford F-150 years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford F-150 years are 2013, 2011, 2018.

What are common Ford F-150 transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Ford F-150 years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford F-150 years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2024, 2023.
