Quick answers

Is the 2021 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 111; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2021 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (30%), electrical (16%) and visibility (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Ford F-150 have?

29 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Ford F-150 get?

19 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Ford F-150 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 19,600 and 79,000 miles. The middle report was at 46,000 miles, from 355 complaints.

Does the 2021 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 30% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $4100 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Ford F-150 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
