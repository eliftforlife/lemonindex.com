Quick answers

Is the 2006 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 88; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2006 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (35%), steering (26%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Ford F-150 have?

17 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Ford F-150 get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Ford F-150 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 92,000 miles. The middle report was at 53,000 miles, from 1,217 complaints.

Does the 2006 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2006 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Ford F-150 have?

4.2L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl, 5.4L 8-cyl.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
