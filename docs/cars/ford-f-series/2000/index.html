Quick answers

Is the 2000 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 94; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2000 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (29%), steering (15%) and brakes (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Ford F-150 have?

21 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Ford F-150 get?

13 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
