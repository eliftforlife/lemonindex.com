How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Ford F-150 reliable?

We can't score it: Ford reports F-150 and Super Duty sales together as one F-Series number (there is no F-150-only sales figure), so there is no per-car rate. Owners filed 630 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

Does this include the 2018 F-250 and F-350?

No. These are the 630 complaints NHTSA files as F-150 or F-150 Hybrid. The 2018 Super Duty drew 437 of its own in its first 3 years; they are on its own page.

What are the most common 2018 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (26%), body structure (21%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Ford F-150 have?

19 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Ford F-150 get?

18 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2018 Ford F-150 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 11,000 and 80,000 miles (18,000–129,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,035 complaints, of all 2,339 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2018 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 26% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

  1. Were recalls 26V373000 (lights), 26V375000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 25V236000 (brakes) done? It has 19 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.

  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 1,751 YouTube videos about the Ford F-Series, 1975 to 2026, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
