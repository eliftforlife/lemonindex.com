Quick answers

Is the 2018 Ford F-150 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 116; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2018 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (32%), suspension (22%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Ford F-150 have?

27 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Ford F-150 get?

18 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
