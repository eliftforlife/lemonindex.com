Quick answers

Is the 2025 Ford F-150 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.9× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 34; 100 is average).

What are the most common 2025 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (22%), automatic emergency braking (22%) and electrical (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Ford F-150 have?

16 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2025 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $1050 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
