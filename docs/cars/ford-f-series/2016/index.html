    Water and contaminants may get into the block heater cable's splice connector causing corrosion and damage.

  • seat belts 2018

    If a front seat belt pretensioner deploys as the result of a crash, the sparks may ignite materials such as carpeting or insulation within the B-pillar area.

  • seats 2017

    Loose power seat adjuster pivot bolts may cause the front seat cushions to detach and not properly restrain the seat occupant in the event of a crash.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2017

    The left rear inflatable seat belt buckle assembly may be inadequately attached to its mounting bracket.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2017

    The seat back frame may have insufficient welds which may not meet the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 202, "Head Restraints" and 207, "Seating Systems.".

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), body structure (17%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford F-150 have?

18 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Ford F-150 get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
