2016 Ford F-150.

The 2016 Ford F-150 is about average by owner complaints: 809 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 99, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about brakes, it has 18 recalls, and it gets 16 to 21 mpg combined. The best Ford F-150 year is 2007.

Most owners who filed a complaint with NHTSA had 20K to 80K miles on the car (1,398 complaints listed the miles). This shows when people who had a problem reported it, not the odds that your car will have it.

  • transmission & drivetrain complaints mostly came at 50K–130K miles
  • engine complaints mostly came at 22K–80K miles
  • brakes complaints mostly came at 9K–52K miles

Recalls 18

  • transmission & drivetrain 2026

    A loss of signal between the transmission range sensor and the powertrain control module can cause the transmission to unexpectedly downshift into second gear.

  • back over prevention 2025

    The rearview camera may display a distorted, inverted, or blank image when the vehicle is in reverse.

  • airbags 2022

    Dust may accumulate in the steering wheel clock spring, causing a disconnection in the electrical connection resulting in the driver's air bag not deploying as intended.

  • brakes 2022

    The brake master cylinder may allow brake fluid to leak from the front wheel circuit into the brake booster.

  • suspension 2021

    The ball joint may have a misaligned dust boot and may be missing alignment hole and installation warning tag, which may lead to improper alignment of the ball joint.

  • steering 2020

    The foam ring inside the stabilizer "through shaft" may block the piston ports, possibly causing the steering to lock up.

  • brakes 2020

    The brake master cylinder may leak brake fluid into the brake booster.

  • engine 2020

    The incorrect location could put the heater too close to hot engine components, possibly resulting in a damaged cable.

  • engine 2019

    During the previous repair, the block heater cable's splice connector may have been damaged.

  • engine 2018

    Water and contaminants may get into the block heater cable's splice connector causing corrosion and damage.

  • seat belts 2018

    If a front seat belt pretensioner deploys as the result of a crash, the sparks may ignite materials such as carpeting or insulation within the B-pillar area.

  • seats 2017

    Loose power seat adjuster pivot bolts may cause the front seat cushions to detach and not properly restrain the seat occupant in the event of a crash.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2017

    The doors on these vehicles may have a bent or kinked actuation cable or the door latches may freeze after water gets into the latch.

  • seat belts 2017

    The left rear inflatable seat belt buckle assembly may be inadequately attached to its mounting bracket.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2017

    These accessory door handle covers may cause the doors to unlatch in a side impact collision.

  • seats 2016

    The seat back frame may have insufficient welds which may not meet the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 202, "Head Restraints" and 207, "Seating Systems.".

  • tires 2016

    The tires installed during assembly of the affected vehicles may have sustained damage to the inner sidewall, possibly resulting in a loss of air or a tire rupture.

  • airbags 2016

    The front passenger seat Occupant Classification System (OCS) that calibrates the air bag deployment level may incorrectly classify an adult as a child when the seat massage feature is activated.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2016 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), body structure (17%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford F-150 have?

18 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Ford F-150 get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Ford F-150 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 20,000 and 80,100 miles. The middle report was at 48,000 miles, from 1,398 complaints.

Does the 2016 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Ford F-150 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
