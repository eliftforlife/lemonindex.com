Questions to ask the seller

7 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 26V237000 (transmission & drivetrain), 21E027000 (suspension) and 20V332000 (brakes) done? It has 14 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into unintended transmission downshift and rear wheel lock-up. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (154 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $8,100.)
  7. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (82 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $4,200.)

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford F-150 reliable?

We can't score it: Ford reports F-150 and Super Duty sales together as one F-Series number (there is no F-150-only sales figure), so there is no per-car rate. Owners filed 561 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

Does this include the 2015 F-250 and F-350?

No. These are the 561 complaints NHTSA files as F-150 or F-150 Hybrid. The 2015 Super Duty drew 112 of its own in its first 3 years; they are on its own page.

What are the most common 2015 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), body structure (17%) and speed control (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford F-150 have?

14 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford F-150 get?

17 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Ford F-150 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Ford F-150 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 24,000 and 80,000 miles (39,000–128,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,108 complaints, of all 1,752 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2015 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2015 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2015 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.
