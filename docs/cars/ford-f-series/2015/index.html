Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford F-150 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 89; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2015 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (22%), body structure (15%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford F-150 have?

17 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford F-150 get?

17 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
