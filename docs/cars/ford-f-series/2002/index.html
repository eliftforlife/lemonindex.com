Quick answers

Is the 2002 Ford F-150 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2002 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (18%), tires (13%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Ford F-150 have?

15 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Ford F-150 get?

11 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
