Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Ford F-150 reliable?

We can't score it per truck sold: Ford reports F-150 and Super Duty sales together as one F-Series number (there is no F-150-only sales figure). The F-150's own owner complaints are on every year's page: 6,190 in the first 3 years of model years 2010–2022.

What are the best Ford F-150 years to buy?

We don't rank F-150 years by complaints: without F-150 sales, a year that sold more would look worse just for selling more. Each year's YouTube rank (how positively owners and reviewers talk about it) is in the chart above.

Does the F-150 score include the F-250 and F-350?

No. These pages count only complaints NHTSA files as F-150 or F-150 Hybrid. The F-250/F-350 Super Duty has its own page with its complaints by year and engine.

How many recalls does the Ford F-150 have?

310 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (29), 2022 (23), 2001 (19). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford F-Series recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford F-150 problems?

Across every year, F-150 owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most.
