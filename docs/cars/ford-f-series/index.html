Ford F-⁠150 reliability.

Is it reliable? It's mixed. About as many complaints as the average car.

Mostly half-ton trucks: not valid for 2500/3500 diesels

Raptor, Hybrid, Lightning) and 40% are F-250/F-350 Super Duty trucks, gas and diesel mixed, because Ford reports F-Series sales as one line. So the score and the best and worst years mostly reflect the half-ton and can't tell one diesel from another.

Quick answers.

Is the Ford F-150 reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2017, 2018 and 2019. This score is the whole line: 40% of its complaints are F-250/F-350 Super Duty, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Super Duty page, by engine.

Is the Ford F-150 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 101; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Ford F-150 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2010, 2020. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Ford F-150 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 170), 2017 (worse than average, Lemon Index 160), 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 116). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford F-150 have?

310 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (29), 2022 (23), 2001 (19). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford F-150 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
