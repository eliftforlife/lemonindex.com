Quick answers.

Is the Ford F-150 reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2007. Avoid 2017, 2018 and 2019.

Is the Ford F-150 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 101; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2007 and the worst is 2019.

What are the best Ford F-150 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2007, 2014, 2009.

What Ford F-150 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2019, 2017, 2018.

How many recalls does the Ford F-150 have?

401 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (29), 2017 (28), 2018 (27). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford F-150 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
