Quick answers

Is the 2009 Ford F-150 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 47; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford F-150 year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2009 Ford F-150 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (17%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and steering (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Ford F-150 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Ford F-150 get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Ford F-150 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 19,900 and 98,000 miles. The middle report was at 54,000 miles, from 394 complaints.

Does the 2009 Ford F-150 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Ford F-150 cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Ford F-150 have?

4.6L 8-cyl, 5.4L 8-cyl.

What Ford F-150 year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
