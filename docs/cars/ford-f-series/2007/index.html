2007 Ford F-Series: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Ford F-Series drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 254 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 690,589 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford F-Series complaints that listed a mileage came before 51,500 miles. Most came between 21,100 miles and 93,100 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 64,400 miles and 135,800 miles.
