Quick answers

Is the 2025 Kia Optima reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.2× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 120; 100 is average). The best Kia Optima year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2025 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (28%), electrical (21%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Kia Optima have?

2 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2025 Kia Optima safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Kia Optima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

What Kia Optima year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2019 and 2020.
