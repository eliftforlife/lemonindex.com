Quick answers.

Is the Kia Optima reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2001, 2006 and 2007.

Is the Kia Optima a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Complaint Score 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2001 the most.

What are the best Kia Optima years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2020, 2019. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Kia Optima years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2001 (avoid, Complaint Score 233), 2006 (worse than average, Complaint Score 160), 2007 (worse than average, Complaint Score 119, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Optima have?

60 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2013 (9), 2014 (7), 2012 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Kia Optima recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Kia Optima problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Kia Optima repairs cost?

The repairs Kia Optima owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $6,400, 48 reports), brake repair (middle bill $695, 7 reports), steering column repair (middle bill $1,200, 7 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 20 Kia Optima years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 790 YouTube videos about the Kia Optima, 2001 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
