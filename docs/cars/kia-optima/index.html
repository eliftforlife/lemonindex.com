Quick answers.

Is the Kia Optima a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 84; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2001.

What are the best Kia Optima years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2019, 2020.

What Kia Optima years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2001, 2006, 2007.
