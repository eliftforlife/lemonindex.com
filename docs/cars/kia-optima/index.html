Quick answers.

Is the Kia Optima reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2022.

Is the Kia Optima a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 84; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2022 the most.

What are the best Kia Optima years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2020, 2019. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Kia Optima years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022 (about average, Lemon Index 112), 2013 (about average, Lemon Index 107), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 98). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Optima have?

79 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2013 (9), 2014 (7), 2012 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Optima problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
