When problems show up

Half of the Kia Optima complaints that listed a mileage came before 57,000 miles. Most came between 22,000 miles and 83,000 miles.

The top problem area, fuel system, gasoline, usually showed up between 73,800 miles and 90,800 miles.

Recalls: 5

  • fuel system, gasoline 2025
    A damaged check valve can allow air to enter the fuel tank, causing it to expand and contact hot exhaust components, melting the tank. NHTSA 25V794000
  • airbags 2023
    The side curtain air bags may have been installed incorrectly, causing them not to deploy properly in the event of a crash. NHTSA 23V149000
  • transmission & drivetrain 2022
    The vehicle's "fail-safe" limited-mobility drive mode may be impaired, when prompted by a transmission oil pump malfunction, which can result in a complete loss of drive power. NHTSA 22V760000
  • fuel system, gasoline 2021
    Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and fuel rail. NHTSA 21V519000
  • lane assist 2021
    The Rack-Mounted Motor Driven Power Steering (R-MDPS) system lacks the ability to diagnose communication faults. NHTSA 21V447000
