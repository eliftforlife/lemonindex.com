Quick answers

Is the 2004 Kia Optima reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Optima year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2004 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), engine (21%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Kia Optima have?

1 safety recall (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Kia Optima get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Kia Optima problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 57,000 and 105,000 miles. The middle report was at 82,500 miles, from 187 complaints.

Does the 2004 Kia Optima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Kia Optima cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Kia Optima have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

What Kia Optima year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2019 and 2020.
