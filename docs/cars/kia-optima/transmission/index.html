Kia Optima transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2009 (32 complaints), 2010 (23 complaints), 2001 (12 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2014, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Kia Optima years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Optima years are 2009, 2010, 2001.

What are common Kia Optima transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, transmission replaced.

Which Kia Optima years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Optima years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2014, 2015.
