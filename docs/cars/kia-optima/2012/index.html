What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10094572: “Warranty bulletin: wty004 - 2011-2014 my optima (tf/qf) engine short block warranty extension (wty004) - this warranty…”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10053708: “Kia: due to some vehicles experiencing an illumination malfunction indicator lamp (mil), the electronic control module…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10089421: “Hcu/tcu upgrade - for 100 mesh solenoid equipped hybrid transmissions -this bulletin provides information related to a…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Recalls & safety6 recalls · Free fix at any dealer

Recalls 6

  • Brakes · recall issued 2023

    The Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may experience an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

  • Body structure · recall issued 2022

    The right and left headliner plates may not be secured properly, which can result in the plates detaching in the event of a side curtain air bag deployment.

  • Engine · recall issued 2020

    An engine compartment fire can occur while driving.

Show all 6 recalls
  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2018

    The high pressure fuel pipe that connects to the fuel pump outlet may have been damaged, misaligned or improperly torqued during the engine replacement procedure, possibly causing a fuel leak.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2018

    In the event of a crash, the air bag control unit (ACU) may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags and seat belt pretensioners from deploying.

  • Engine · recall issued 2017

    Machining errors during the engine manufacturing process may cause premature bearing wear within the engine.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Questions to ask the seller

6 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 23V652000 (brakes), 22V560000 (body structure) and 20V750000 (engine) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into hyundai and kia abs module fires. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Kia sent dealers 294 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some vehicles warning light for the engine may illuminate”?

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Kia Optima reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 95; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Optima year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2012 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (32%), engine (15%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Kia Optima have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Kia Optima get?

26 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Kia Optima safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Kia Optima problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 48,000 and 117,000 miles (78,000–188,000 km) (the middle half of the 739 complaints, of all 1,029 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Kia Optima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Kia Optima cost in fuel a year?

About $1,800 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Kia Optima have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Optima year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2014 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.
