How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Kia Optima reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 55; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Optima year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2019 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (15%), electrical (15%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Kia Optima have?

1 safety recall (latest: automatic emergency braking). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Kia Optima get?

24 to 41 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Kia Optima safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2019 Kia Optima problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 9,500 and 74,000 miles (15,000–118,000 km) (the middle half of the 52 complaints, of all 212 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2019 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Kia Optima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Kia Optima cost in fuel a year?

About $1,550 to $2,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Kia Optima have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Optima year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 19V539000 (automatic emergency braking) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Kia sent dealers 130 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “LOWER ENGINE SERVICE COVER BOLT LOCATIONS - This Pitstop provides information on the correct bolt”?
