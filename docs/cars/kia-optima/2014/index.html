Quick answers

Is the 2014 Kia Optima reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 66; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Optima year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2014 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (41%), steering (34%) and speed control (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Kia Optima have?

7 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Kia Optima get?

24 to 37 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Kia Optima safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Kia Optima problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,500 and 109,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,000 miles, from 363 complaints.

Does the 2014 Kia Optima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Kia Optima cost in fuel a year?

About $1750 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Kia Optima have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Optima year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2019 and 2020.
