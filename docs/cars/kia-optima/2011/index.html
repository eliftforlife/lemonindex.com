Quick answers

Is the 2011 Kia Optima reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 85; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Optima year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2011 Kia Optima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (26%), electrical (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Kia Optima have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Kia Optima get?

26 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
