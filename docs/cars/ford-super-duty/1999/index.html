During the subsequent static rollover test, fuel spillage exceeded the specified rate.

    Fix. Dealers will install a stop plate assembly on the vehicle frame to prevent recovery valve contact with a frame crossmember during the impact sequence.

  • 99V219000 Aug 1999 Fuel System, Gasoline

    Vehicle description: gasoline and diesel engine pickup trucks equipped with 8 foot beds, do not fully satisfy the requirements of fmvss no. 301, "fuel system integrity." the fuel tank front strap isolator may not be properly positioned which could result in the strap cutting the tank during a 30 mph front barrier test, resulting in fuel spillage.

    Fix. Dealers will reposition the front strap isolator.

  • 99V093002 Apr 1999 Fuel System, Gasoline

    Vehicle description: pickup trucks equipped with 6" beds manufactured from january 5, 1998 through june 4, 1999, do not not fully satisfy the post crash test static rollover requirements of fmvss no. 301, "fuel system integrity." contact between the fuel tank and a fuel tank locator bracket during a front barrier impact test resulted in a cut in the tank, resulting in fuel leakage.

    Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank locator bracket with a similar bracket whose design has been revised to incorporate more rounded surfaces where contact with the tank occurs.

Quick answers

Is the 1999 Super Duty reliable?

We can't score it per truck (heavy-duty sales aren't reported apart from the half-ton). Owners filed 1,383 complaints about it, most often about tires (18% of them). Compare the diesel generations on their engine pages.

What engine does the 1999 Super Duty have?

By the VINs in owner complaints: 7.3 Power Stroke diesel (63%), 6.8 V10 gas (19%), 5.4 V8 gas (16%), 4.6 V8 gas (1%), gas (1%).

How many recalls does the 1999 Ford Super Duty (F-250/F-350) have?

19. Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check the truck's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

No Lemon Index score: automakers report heavy-duty pickup sales together with the half-ton (one F-Series, Ram or Silverado number), so there is no honest per-truck denominator. These are counts and shares of owner complaints, not failure rates. Complaints: NHTSA owner complaints filed under the truck's 2500/3500 (F-250/F-350) names, all years filed, one per complaint number. Engine: decoded from the complaint's VIN (position 8 is the engine code) with NHTSA's vPIC decoder. Complaints filed as just “Silverado” or “Sierra” are left out (they mix 1500s and HDs). Older trucks have had more years to collect complaints, and popular years draw more because more were sold.
