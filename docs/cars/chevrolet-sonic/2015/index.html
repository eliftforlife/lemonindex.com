Recalls 3

  • electrical 2016

    The affected radios may fail to provide an audible warning chime when the driver fails to fasten their seatbelt or if the driver leaves the key in the ignition.

  • equipment 2016

    The affected radios may fail to provide an audible warning chime when the driver, after turning off the ignition and leaving the key in the cylinder, waits 10 minutes or longer to open the door.

  • equipment 2015

    In the affected vehicles, equipped with the base radio and OnStar, the radio may lock up due to a software issue, causing the display to go blank and all audio functions to cease.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chevrolet Sonic reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 68; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Sonic year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2015 Chevrolet Sonic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (32%), electrical (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chevrolet Sonic have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chevrolet Sonic get?

28 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
