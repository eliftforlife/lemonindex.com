Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Sonic reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 53; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Sonic year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Sonic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), engine (17%) and airbags (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Sonic have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Sonic get?

28 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
