Quick answers

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 37; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2018 Chevrolet Sonic problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (44%), seats (22%) and wheels (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic have?

1 safety recall (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic get?

28 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Chevrolet Sonic have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl.
