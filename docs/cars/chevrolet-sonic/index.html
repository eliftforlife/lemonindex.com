Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Sonic reliable?

About average. It draws 1.2× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2018. Avoid 2012.

Is the Chevrolet Sonic a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2012-2020: Lemon Index 85; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Chevrolet Sonic years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2016, 2017.

What Chevrolet Sonic years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Sonic have?

23 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2012 (7), 2013 (6), 2014 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Sonic problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
