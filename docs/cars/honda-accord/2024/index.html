Quick answers

Is the 2024 Honda Accord reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.5× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 152; 100 is average). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2024 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (32%), electrical (18%) and automatic emergency braking (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Honda Accord have?

6 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Honda Accord get?

32 to 48 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Honda Accord safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2024 Honda Accord problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 4,000 and 22,600 miles. The middle report was at 10,500 miles, from 26 complaints.

Does the 2024 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $1350 to $2000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Honda Accord have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
