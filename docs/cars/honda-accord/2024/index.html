Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 97 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Multiple warning lights on and the vehicle may go into fail-safe mode; DTC P060A
  • Customer complains of a clicking, light ticking, or binding sound while turning
  • A complaint of a creaking or squeaking noise coming from the passenger side dash area

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Honda Accord reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about one and a half times the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 152; 100 is average). 2024 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car. The Honda Accord year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2024 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (32%), electrical (18%) and automatic emergency braking (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Honda Accord have?

6 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Honda Accord get?

32 to 48 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Honda Accord safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2024 Honda Accord problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,000 and 23,000 miles (6,400–36,000 km) (the middle half of the 26 complaints, of all 230 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2024 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2024 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $1,350 to $2,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Honda Accord have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2024?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V663000 (tires), 26V366000 (tires) and 25V785000 (electrical) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  5. Honda sent dealers 97 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Multiple warning lights on and the vehicle may go into fail-safe mode; DTC P060A”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 1,187 YouTube videos about the Honda Accord, 1998 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
