Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 187 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Low mileage a/ts can leak from axle seals
  • Automatic transmission fluid (atf) leaks
  • 1st to 2nd upshift shudder, judder, vibration at 2k rpm

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 50; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2005 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (16%), airbags (15%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Honda Accord have?

24 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Honda Accord get?

21 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
