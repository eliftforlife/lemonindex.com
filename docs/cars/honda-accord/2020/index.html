Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 77; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Accord year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2020 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (24%), brakes (24%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Honda Accord have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Honda Accord get?

26 to 48 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Honda Accord safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Honda Accord problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,300 and 54,000 miles (3,700–87,000 km) (the middle half of the 99 complaints, of all 403 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $1,350 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Honda Accord have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags), 24V064000 (airbags) and 23V858000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into inadvertent automatic emergency braking. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (97 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $5,000.)
  7. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work? (54 owners named a fuel system repair bill; the middle one was $1,800.)

How the YouTube ranks work
