Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 298 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Stalls with electrical load after body shop repair
  • Audio-hvac display module is dim or dark
  • Update replacement control unit/module software

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.
