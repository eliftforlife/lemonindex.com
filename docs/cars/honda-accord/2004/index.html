2004 Honda Accord: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Honda Accord drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 206 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 386,770 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Accord complaints that listed a mileage came before 90,000 miles. Most came between 50,000 miles and 122,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 77,800 miles and 123,000 miles.
