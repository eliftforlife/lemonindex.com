Quick answers

Is the 2014 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 69; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2014 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), engine (15%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Honda Accord have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Honda Accord get?

24 to 47 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Honda Accord safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Honda Accord problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,000 and 72,000 miles. The middle report was at 45,100 miles, from 600 complaints.

Does the 2014 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $1350 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Honda Accord have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
