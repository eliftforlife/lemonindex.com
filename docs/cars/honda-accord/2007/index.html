Quick answers

Is the 2007 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 37; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2007 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (18%), speed control (14%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Honda Accord have?

10 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Honda Accord get?

21 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Honda Accord problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 27,100 and 115,800 miles. The middle report was at 67,500 miles, from 371 complaints.

Does the 2007 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Honda Accord have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
