Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 382 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Automatic transmission leaks on the cooler lines
  • Manual transmission hard to shift into 1st gear, grinds into reverse
  • Wheel bearing noise after transmission removed and replaced

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.
