Honda Accord transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2003 (901 complaints), 2002 (466 complaints), 2004 (333 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2022, 2021.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Honda Accord years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Accord years are 2003, 2002, 2004.

What are common Honda Accord transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, transmission replaced, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Honda Accord years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Accord years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2022, 2021.
