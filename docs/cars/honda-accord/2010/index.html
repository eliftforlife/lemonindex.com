Quick answers

Is the 2010 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2010 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (31%), seats (14%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Honda Accord have?

13 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Honda Accord get?

20 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Honda Accord problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 20,000 and 89,000 miles. The middle report was at 42,600 miles, from 414 complaints.

Does the 2010 Honda Accord have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Honda Accord cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Honda Accord have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Accord year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
