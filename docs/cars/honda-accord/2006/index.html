2006 Honda Accord: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Honda Accord drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 108 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 354,441 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Accord complaints that listed a mileage came before 77,300 miles. Most came between 27,700 miles and 124,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 65,000 miles and 136,500 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
