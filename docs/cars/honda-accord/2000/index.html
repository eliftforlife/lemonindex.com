Quick answers

Is the 2000 Honda Accord reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Accord year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2000 Honda Accord problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (26%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and speed control (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Honda Accord have?

21 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Honda Accord get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
