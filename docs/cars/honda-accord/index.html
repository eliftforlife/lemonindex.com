Quick answers.

Is the Honda Accord reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2011. Avoid 2003, 2008 and 2018.

Is the Honda Accord a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 82; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best Honda Accord years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2007, 2006.

What Honda Accord years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008, 2018, 2003.

How many recalls does the Honda Accord have?

267 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2003 (24), 2005 (24), 2004 (22). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Accord problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
