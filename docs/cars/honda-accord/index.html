Quick answers.

Is the Honda Accord reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2018.

Is the Honda Accord a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 82; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Honda Accord years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2012, 2010. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Honda Accord years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 196, the first year of a new generation), 2013 (about average, Lemon Index 104, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (about average, Lemon Index 94). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Accord have?

267 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2003 (24), 2005 (24), 2004 (22). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Accord problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
