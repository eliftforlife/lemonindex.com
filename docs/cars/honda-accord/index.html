Quick answers.

Is the Honda Accord reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2003, 2008 and 2018.

Is the Honda Accord a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 82; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2008 the most.

What are the best Honda Accord years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2007, 2006. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Honda Accord years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2008 (avoid, Complaint Score 210, the first year of a new generation), 2018 (worse than average, Complaint Score 196, the first year of a new generation), 2003 (worse than average, Complaint Score 150, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Accord have?

267 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2003 (24), 2005 (24), 2004 (22). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda Accord recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda Accord problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Honda Accord repairs cost?

The repairs Honda Accord owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $3,600, 181 reports), brake repair (middle bill $250, 100 reports), starter replacement (middle bill $800, 67 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 28 Honda Accord years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 1,187 YouTube videos about the Honda Accord, 1998 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
