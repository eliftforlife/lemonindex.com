Known issues

  • High-pressure fuel pump recallRecall

    On some 2015–2018 RC Fs (and GS F, 2018 LC 500), a damper in one of the two high-pressure fuel pumps could crack the pump cover and leak fuel.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the high-pressure fuel pumps for free (NHTSA 18V-107).

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2025)
  • In-tank fuel pump recallRecall

    On 2015–2022 RC Fs (plus GS F and 2022 IS 500), the in-tank pump’s resin impeller can swell with hot fuel and stop the pump, which can stall the engine.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump assembly for free (NHTSA 26V-222, Lexus 26LA05).

    8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2025)
  • Valley plate coolant leakOwner-reported

    Sealant under the valley plate can fail and leak coolant. 8020 Automotive ties it mainly to the older IS F and says it hasn’t heard of it on the RC F.

    Fix: Reseal the valley plate; Jalopnik puts it at about $1,500 to over $3,000.

Quick answers

How fast is the Lexus RC F?

The USC10 RC F makes 472 hp and 395 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.1 seconds. Top speed: 168 mph (Lexus top track speed).

What engine does the Lexus RC F have?

The USC10 RC F (2015–2025) uses the Lexus 2UR-GSE, a 5.0-liter V-8, naturally aspirated.
