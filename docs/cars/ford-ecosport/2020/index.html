Quick answers

Is the 2020 Ford EcoSport reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 152; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford EcoSport year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Ford EcoSport problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (67%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and engine (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Ford EcoSport have?

3 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Ford EcoSport get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Ford EcoSport safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Ford EcoSport problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 53,000 and 81,000 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 139 complaints.

Does the 2020 Ford EcoSport have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 24% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Ford EcoSport cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Ford EcoSport have?

1.0L 3-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Ford EcoSport year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2019 and 2018.
