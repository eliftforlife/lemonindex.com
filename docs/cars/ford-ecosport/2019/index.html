Quick answers

Is the 2019 Ford EcoSport reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford EcoSport year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Ford EcoSport problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (52%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Ford EcoSport have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Ford EcoSport get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
