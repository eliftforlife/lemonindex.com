Quick answers

Is the 2000 Audi A6 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 134; 100 is average, lower is better). 2000 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. The Audi A6 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2000 Audi A6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (22%), electrical (16%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Audi A6 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Audi A6 get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2000 Audi A6 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Audi A6 cost in fuel a year?

About $4,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Audi A6 have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 2.8L 6-cyl, 4.2L 8-cyl.

What Audi A6 year is better than the 2000?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V056000 (airbags), 06V198000 (electrical) and 04V133000 (electrical) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Audi sent dealers 99 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Oil leak in area of turbocharger”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 592 YouTube videos about the Audi A6, 2005 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2019. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
