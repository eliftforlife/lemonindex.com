Estimate: labor hours × your shop rate + parts. Owner prices are mostly dealer bills from NHTSA complaints.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2023 Audi A6 reliable?

We can't score it: only 8,317 were sold in the U.S. in 2023 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 3 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Audi A6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), backup camera (33%) and automatic emergency braking (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Audi A6 have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Audi A6 get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2023 Audi A6 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Audi A6 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,950 to $3,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Audi A6 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Audi A6 year is better than the 2023?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 25V900000 (back over prevention) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Audi sent dealers 1005 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This bulletin provides instructions for when the head up display does not work and/or the 12v…”?

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
