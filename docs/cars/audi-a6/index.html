Quick answers.

Is the Audi A6 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. Fewest complaints: 2015. Most complaints: 2019.

Is the Audi A6 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2019: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2015 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Audi A6 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015, 2013, 2014. 2015 drew the fewest.

What Audi A6 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 132, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Audi A6 have?

96 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2021 (12), 2020 (9), 2012 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi A6 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
