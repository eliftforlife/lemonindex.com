Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V900000 (back over prevention), 22V861000 (electrical) and 22V806000 (equipment) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Audi sent dealers 1132 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This bulletin contains instructions for using the UV additive to find engine oil leaks”?

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Audi A6 reliable?

We can't score it: only 6,005 were sold in the U.S. in 2022 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 3 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2022 Audi A6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are backup camera (67%), engine (33%) and transmission & drivetrain (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Audi A6 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Audi A6 get?

23 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2022 Audi A6 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 33% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Audi A6 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $3,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Audi A6 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Audi A6 year is better than the 2022?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
