Known issues

  • Second-gear grind, early manual cars (transmission)Maker bulletin

    In late 2022 Nissan held all manual 2023 Zs at dealers over second-gear shifting, then replaced the gearbox on 174 cars with a manufacturing fault.

    Fix: Fixed before sale; Nissan says the fault was corrected in production.

  • Check-engine light, MAF codes P0101 / P010BMaker bulletin

    On the 2023 Z, a leaking or loose intake air duct can set airflow sensor codes and turn on the check-engine light.

    Fix: Dealer checks and reseats the intake ducts (Nissan bulletin NTB23-006).

  • Stall after hard acceleration (Infiniti recall)Recall

    On 2021 Infiniti Q50 and Q60, engine software could cut fuel after rapid acceleration and stall the engine.

    Fix: Dealer reprograms the engine computer for free (NHTSA 21V-234); the Z is not included.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2023–2025)
  • No engine recall on the Z so farNHTSA records

    NHTSA lists two Z recalls, both for the pop-up hood, and no engine recall for 2023–2025 Zs. Few owner complaints are on file.

Quick answers

How fast is the Nissan Z NISMO?

The RZ34 Z NISMO makes 420 hp and 384 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.9 seconds.

What engine does the Nissan Z have?

The RZ34 Z (2023–2025) uses the Nissan VR30DDTT, a 3.0-liter V6, twin-turbo.
