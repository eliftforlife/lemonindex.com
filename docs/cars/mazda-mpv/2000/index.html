How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Mazda MPV reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 193; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mazda MPV year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2001.

What are the most common 2000 Mazda MPV problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (13%), seat belts (13%) and tires (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Mazda MPV have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Mazda MPV get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2000 Mazda MPV have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Mazda MPV cost in fuel a year?

About $3,350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Mazda MPV have?

2.5L 6-cyl.

What Mazda MPV year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2001 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 01V382000 (brakes), 00V113001 (equipment) and 00V113002 (equipment) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the seat belts latch and retract?
  4. Mazda sent dealers 136 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information concerning oil drain plug”?
