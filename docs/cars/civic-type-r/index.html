Known issues

  • Fuel pump recallRecall

    2018–2020 Type Rs are in NHTSA recall 23V858: the in-tank fuel pump may fail, which can stall the engine while driving.

    Fix: Dealers replace the fuel pump module free of charge.

    6 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2017–2021)
  • Heat in long track sessionsOwner-reported

    Track-day owners report coolant and oil temperatures climbing and power dropping in long sessions. Honda enlarged the grille and radiator core for 2020 and the FL5 grille opening by 48%.

    Fix: Owners who track often add a bigger radiator or oil cooler.

  • Low fuel-pressure warning on track (unconfirmed)Owner-reported

    Owners in one CivicXI thread report a low fuel-pressure warning and limp mode on track, some with a low tank. The cause is not confirmed.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2017–2021)

Quick answers

How fast is the Honda Civic Type R?

The FL5 Civic Type R makes 315 hp and 310 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.9 seconds.

What engine does the Honda Civic Type R have?

The FL5 Civic Type R (2023–2025) uses the Honda K20C1, a 2.0-liter inline-4, turbo. Older generations: FK8: Honda K20C1.
