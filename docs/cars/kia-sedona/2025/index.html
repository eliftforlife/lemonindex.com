Quick answers

Is the 2025 Kia Sedona reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.9× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 289; 100 is average). The best Kia Sedona year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2025 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (67%), engine (12%) and fuel or ev drive system (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Kia Sedona have?

1 safety recall (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2025 Kia Sedona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

What Kia Sedona year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2014 and 2011.
