Quick answers

Is the 2010 Kia Sedona reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 26; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (29%), brakes (29%) and body structure (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Kia Sedona have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Kia Sedona get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Kia Sedona problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 44,600 and 98,800 miles. The middle report was at 79,800 miles, from 25 complaints.

Does the 2010 Kia Sedona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 29% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Kia Sedona cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Kia Sedona have?

3.8L 6-cyl.
