Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Kia Sedona reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Sedona year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2015 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), body structure (22%) and airbags (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Kia Sedona have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Kia Sedona get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Kia Sedona safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2015 Kia Sedona problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 23,000 and 88,000 miles (37,000–141,000 km) (the middle half of the 65 complaints, of all 139 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2015 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2015 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2015 Kia Sedona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Kia Sedona cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $3,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Kia Sedona have?

3.3L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Kia Sedona year than the 2015?

Not nearby: the 2015 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Kia Sedona years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V725000 (lights), 19V109000 (electrical) and 18V338000 (body structure) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (24 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $8,400.)
  6. Kia sent dealers 146 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some vehicles, power sliding door release actuators fails to operate properly during severe…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Kia Sedona, 2002 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
