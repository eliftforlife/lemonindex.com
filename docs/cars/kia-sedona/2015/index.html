Quick answers

Is the 2015 Kia Sedona reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sedona year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2015 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), body structure (22%) and airbags (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Kia Sedona have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Kia Sedona get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Kia Sedona safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Kia Sedona problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 23,000 and 87,800 miles. The middle report was at 55,000 miles, from 65 complaints.

Does the 2015 Kia Sedona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Kia Sedona cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Kia Sedona have?

3.3L 6-cyl.

What Kia Sedona year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2014 and 2011.
