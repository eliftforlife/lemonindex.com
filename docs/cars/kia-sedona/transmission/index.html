Quick answers

Which Kia Sedona years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Sedona years are 2002, 2006, 2016.

What are common Kia Sedona transmission problems?

Owners most often describe rolled away or park trouble, shudder, lost power while driving.

Which Kia Sedona years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Sedona years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2017, 2022.
