Kia Sedona transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2002 (19 complaints), 2006 (19 complaints), 2016 (4 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2017, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Kia Sedona years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Sedona years are 2002, 2006, 2016.

What are common Kia Sedona transmission problems?

Owners most often describe rolled away or park trouble, shudder, lost power while driving.

Which Kia Sedona years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Sedona years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2017, 2022.
