Quick answers

Is the 2007 Kia Sedona reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 97; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sedona year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2007 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (23%), speed control (15%) and body structure (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Kia Sedona have?

6 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Kia Sedona get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
