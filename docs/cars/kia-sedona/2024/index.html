Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V232000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 24V025000 (body structure) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the seats adjust, heat and lock in place properly?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10252112: “SERVICE ACTION: ECM LOGIC UPDATE FOR DTC P2118 AND THROTTLE BODY FREEZING (SA514) - This…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11033217: “INFOTAINMENT SYSTEM SOFTWARE UPDATE USING UPDATE.KIA.COM - This bulletin provides…”)

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Kia Sedona reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about twice the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 175; 100 is average). The Kia Sedona year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2024 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), engine (11%) and seats (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Kia Sedona have?

2 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2024 Kia Sedona safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Kia Sedona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

What Kia Sedona year is better than the 2024?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).
