Quick answers.

Is the Kia Sedona reliable?

About average. It draws 1.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2016 and 2022.

Is the Kia Sedona a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 114; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2022 the most.

What are the best Kia Sedona years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2014, 2011. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Kia Sedona years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022 (avoid, Lemon Index 220), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 161), 2017 (about average, Lemon Index 102). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Sedona have?

73 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2006 (7), 2007 (6), 2011 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Sedona problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
