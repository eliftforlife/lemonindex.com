Quick answers

Is the 2008 Kia Sedona reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 188; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sedona year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2008 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (55%), electrical (13%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Kia Sedona have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Kia Sedona get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
