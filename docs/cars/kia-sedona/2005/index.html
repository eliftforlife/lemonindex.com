Quick answers

Is the 2005 Kia Sedona reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 258; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sedona year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2005 Kia Sedona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (74%), electrical (13%) and suspension (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Kia Sedona have?

2 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Kia Sedona get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
