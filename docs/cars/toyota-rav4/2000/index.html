Quick answers

Is the 2000 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 10 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2000 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (30%), transmission & drivetrain (20%) and tires (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Toyota RAV4 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2000 Toyota RAV4 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 Toyota RAV4 cost in fuel a year?

About $1050 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Toyota RAV4 year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2022 and 2015.
