Quick answers

Is the 2022 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 26; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota RAV4 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2022 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), engine (17%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Toyota RAV4 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Toyota RAV4 get?

28 to 40 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
