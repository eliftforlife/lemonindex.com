Quick answers

Is the 2003 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 35; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota RAV4 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2003 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (23%), airbags (18%) and electrical (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Toyota RAV4 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.
