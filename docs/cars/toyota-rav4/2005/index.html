Quick answers

Is the 2005 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 16 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2005 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (38%), visibility (19%) and airbags (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Toyota RAV4 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Toyota RAV4 get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
