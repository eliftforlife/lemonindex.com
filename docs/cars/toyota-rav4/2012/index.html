Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V096000 (seat belts), 15V577000 (wipers & visibility) and 14V054000 (brakes) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (79 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $688.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (49 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,300.)
  7. Toyota sent dealers 332 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Squeak noise which is heard coming from the front”?

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota RAV4 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2012 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (30%), brakes (20%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Toyota RAV4 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2012 Toyota RAV4 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Toyota RAV4 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 8,000 and 55,000 miles (13,000–89,000 km) (the middle half of the 168 complaints, of all 266 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Toyota RAV4 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Toyota RAV4 cost in fuel a year?

About $1,000 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Toyota RAV4 year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.
