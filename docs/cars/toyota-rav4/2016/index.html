Recalls 3

  • electrical 2023

    Certain replacement 12-volt batteries may not fit properly in the battery tray, allowing the battery to move and contact the hold-down bracket, possibly causing a short circuit.

  • tires 2016

    The Load Carrying Capacity Modification Label may not reflect the correct added weight of the installed accessories.

  • brakes 2016

    These vehicles are equipped with an ABS actuator that may have a damaged O-ring which could result in improper brake fluid pressure control during ABS, Traction Control, or Stability Control activation.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 37; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota RAV4 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), lights (14%) and wipers & visibility (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Toyota RAV4 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Toyota RAV4 get?

24 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Toyota RAV4 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Toyota RAV4 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 3,500 and 44,400 miles. The middle report was at 14,800 miles, from 166 complaints.

Does the 2016 Toyota RAV4 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Toyota RAV4 cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Toyota RAV4 have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Toyota RAV4 year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2022 and 2015.
