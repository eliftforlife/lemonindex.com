Quick answers

Is the 2002 Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 35; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota RAV4 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2002 Toyota RAV4 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (23%), electrical (19%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Toyota RAV4 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

At what mileage do 2002 Toyota RAV4 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 73,900 and 116,500 miles. The middle report was at 94,000 miles, from 474 complaints.

Does the 2002 Toyota RAV4 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2002 Toyota RAV4 cost in fuel a year?

About $950 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Toyota RAV4 year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2022 and 2015.
