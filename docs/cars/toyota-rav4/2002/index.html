2002 Toyota RAV4: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Toyota RAV4 drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 31 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 86,601 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota RAV4 complaints that listed a mileage came before 94,000 miles. Most came between 73,900 miles and 116,500 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 79,600 miles and 118,200 miles.
