Quick answers.

Is the Toyota RAV4 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.9× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2022. Avoid 2006, 2007 and 2009.

Is the Toyota RAV4 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2007.

What are the best Toyota RAV4 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2022, 2015.

What Toyota RAV4 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2007, 2006, 2009.

How many recalls does the Toyota RAV4 have?

143 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2009 (15), 2007 (14), 2008 (14). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota RAV4 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, speed control and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
