Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Volvo XC90 reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2004, 2005 and 2016.

Is the Volvo XC90 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 58; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2004 the most.

What are the best Volvo XC90 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2020, 2011. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Volvo XC90 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2004 (worse than average, Lemon Index 138), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 134), 2005 (worse than average, Lemon Index 120). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Volvo XC90 have?

79 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2016 (8), 2017 (7), 2020 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Volvo XC90 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Volvo XC90 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Volvo XC90 repairs cost?

The repairs Volvo XC90 owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $6,000, 16 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $1,100, 7 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $14,500, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 17 Volvo XC90 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Volvo XC90, 2003 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
