Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Volvo XC90 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 28; 100 is average, lower is better). The Volvo XC90 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2011 Volvo XC90 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (43%), transmission & drivetrain (43%) and body structure (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Volvo XC90 have?

1 safety recall (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Volvo XC90 get?

16 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2011 Volvo XC90 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 43% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Volvo XC90 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,350 to $3,750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Volvo XC90 have?

3.2L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Volvo XC90 year than the 2011?

Not nearby: the 2011 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Volvo XC90 years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 11V286000 (steering) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Volvo sent dealers 221 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Shifting rough from 5th to 4th gear, and after standing experience shift shock when brake…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Volvo XC90, 2003 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
