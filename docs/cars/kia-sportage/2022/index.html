How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Kia Sportage reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 23; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Sportage year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2022 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (40%), automatic emergency braking (14%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Kia Sportage have?

1 safety recall (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Kia Sportage get?

21 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Kia Sportage safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2022 Kia Sportage have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Kia Sportage cost in fuel a year?

About $2,450 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Kia Sportage have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Kia Sportage year than the 2022?

Not nearby: the 2022 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Kia Sportage years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 22V703000 (trailer hitches) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Kia sent dealers 52 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Front view camera replacement and software upgrade installation - this bulletin”?
