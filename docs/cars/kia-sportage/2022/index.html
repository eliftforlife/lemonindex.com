Quick answers

Is the 2022 Kia Sportage reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 23; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sportage year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2022 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (40%), automatic emergency braking (23%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Kia Sportage have?

1 safety recall (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Kia Sportage get?

21 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Kia Sportage safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Kia Sportage have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Kia Sportage cost in fuel a year?

About $2450 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Kia Sportage have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Sportage year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2022 and 2021.
