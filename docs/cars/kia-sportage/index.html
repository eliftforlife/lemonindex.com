Quick answers.

Is the Kia Sportage a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 51; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2000.

What are the best Kia Sportage years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2022, 2021.

What Kia Sportage years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2000, 2002, 2001.

How many recalls does the Kia Sportage have?

68 safety recalls across the 24 model years we track. Most: 2011 (6), 2023 (6), 2012 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Sportage problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
