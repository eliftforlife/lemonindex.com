Recalls 2

  • trailer hitches 2022

    Debris and moisture accumulation on the tow hitch harness module printed circuit board (PCB) may cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.

  • brakes 2021

    The electrical circuit in the Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may short-circuit, which can cause a fire in the engine compartment.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Kia Sportage reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 25; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sportage year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2019 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (30%), electrical (22%) and lights (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Kia Sportage have?

2 safety recalls (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Kia Sportage get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Kia Sportage safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Kia Sportage problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 5,000 and 74,000 miles. The middle report was at 32,000 miles, from 23 complaints.

Does the 2019 Kia Sportage have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Kia Sportage cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Kia Sportage have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Sportage year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2022 and 2021.
