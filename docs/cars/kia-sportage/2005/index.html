Quick answers

Is the 2005 Kia Sportage reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sportage year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2005 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), fuel system, gasoline (15%) and visibility (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Kia Sportage have?

3 safety recalls (latest: parking brake). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Kia Sportage get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
