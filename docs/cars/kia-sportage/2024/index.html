Quick answers

Is the 2024 Kia Sportage reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.9× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 52; 100 is average). The best Kia Sportage year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2024 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), automatic emergency braking (18%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Kia Sportage have?

3 safety recalls (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Kia Sportage get?

25 to 43 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Kia Sportage safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Kia Sportage have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Kia Sportage cost in fuel a year?

About $1500 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Kia Sportage have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Kia Sportage year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2022 and 2021.
