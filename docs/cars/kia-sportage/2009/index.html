Quick answers

Is the 2009 Kia Sportage reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 11 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (27%), fuel system, gasoline (18%) and traction control system (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Kia Sportage have?

3 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
