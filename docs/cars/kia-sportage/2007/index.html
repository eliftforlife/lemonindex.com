Quick answers

Is the 2007 Kia Sportage reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 19 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (32%), brakes (16%) and tires (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Kia Sportage have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Kia Sportage get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Kia Sportage problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,000 and 121,800 miles. The middle report was at 69,000 miles, from 57 complaints.

Does the 2007 Kia Sportage have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Kia Sportage cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Kia Sportage have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

What Kia Sportage year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2022 and 2021.
