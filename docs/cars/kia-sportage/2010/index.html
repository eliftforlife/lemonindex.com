Quick answers

Is the 2010 Kia Sportage reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 11.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 9; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Kia Sportage problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seats (33%), transmission & drivetrain (33%) and airbags (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Kia Sportage have?

2 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Kia Sportage get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
