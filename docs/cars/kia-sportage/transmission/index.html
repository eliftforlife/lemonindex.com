Kia Sportage transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2000 (21 complaints), 2011 (15 complaints), 2002 (14 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2014, 2016.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Kia Sportage years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Sportage years are 2000, 2011, 2002.

What are common Kia Sportage transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, slipping, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Kia Sportage years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Sportage years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2014, 2016.
