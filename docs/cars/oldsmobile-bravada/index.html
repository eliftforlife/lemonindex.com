Quick answers.

Is the Oldsmobile Bravada reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2000, still drew 1.3× the average.

Is the Oldsmobile Bravada a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2001: Complaint Score 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2000 drew the fewest complaints.

What are the best Oldsmobile Bravada years to buy?

Only 1 Oldsmobile Bravada years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2000 drew the fewest complaints (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

What Oldsmobile Bravada years should you stay away from?

Only 1 Oldsmobile Bravada years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Oldsmobile Bravada have?

23 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2002 (10), 2003 (7), 2000 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Oldsmobile Bravada recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Oldsmobile Bravada problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Oldsmobile Bravada years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Oldsmobile Bravada, 1996 to 2004, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2001. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

Looking at a 2001 or newer?

Those are too new for a full complaint score (any score shown is partial, in gray), but YouTube owners like the 2001 best.
