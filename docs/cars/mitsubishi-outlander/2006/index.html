Quick answers

Is the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander reliable?

We can't score it: only 11,493 were sold in the U.S. in 2006 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 2 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (50%) and speed control (50%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander have?

1 safety recall (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $3,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Mitsubishi Outlander have?

2.4L 4-cyl.

What Mitsubishi Outlander year is better than the 2006?

Nearby, the 2003 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average). Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 16V458000 (suspension) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Mitsubishi sent dealers 35 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Technical Service Bulletin was sent informing dealers an abnormal rubbing noise is heard…”?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
