Mercedes-Benz M/GLE transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2006 (31 complaints), 2007 (17 complaints), 2016 (9 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2019, 2012.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years are 2006, 2007, 2016.

What are common Mercedes-Benz M/GLE transmission problems?

Owners most often describe stuck in gear or limp mode, lost power while driving, rolled away or park trouble.

Which Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2019, 2012.
