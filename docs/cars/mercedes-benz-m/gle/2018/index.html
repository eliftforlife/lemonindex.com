How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), lights (14%) and speed control (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

How much does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE cost in fuel a year?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V854000 (body structure), 22V955000 (fuel system, diesel) and 22V365000 (electrical) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (7 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $4,500.)
  6. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 180 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Complaint To insure vehicle down time is kept at a minimum and all required information is…”?
