Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V854000 (body structure), 22V955000 (fuel system, diesel) and 22V365000 (electrical) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  5. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 180 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Complaint To insure vehicle down time is kept at a minimum and all required information is…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), speed control (14%) and lights (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 8,000 and 45,000 miles (the middle half of the 29 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2019 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (5.6× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.
