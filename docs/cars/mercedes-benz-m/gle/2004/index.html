Quick answers

Is the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 16 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (31%), visibility (19%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

14 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 50,000 and 118,500 miles. The middle report was at 71,500 miles, from 42 complaints.

Does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE cost in fuel a year?

About $5300 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2019 and 2011.
