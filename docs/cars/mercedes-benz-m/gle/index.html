Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

Yes. It draws 2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2002. Avoid 2006 and 2020.

Is the Mercedes-Benz M/GLE a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 51; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2006.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2019, 2011.

What Mercedes-Benz M/GLE years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2006, 2020.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

97 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2020 (24), 2021 (19), 2016 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
