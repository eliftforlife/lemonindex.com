Quick answers

Is the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 12 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (25%), fuel system, diesel (17%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

3 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

15 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,000 and 95,000 miles. The middle report was at 67,900 miles, from 75 complaints.

Does the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE cost in fuel a year?

About $4400 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

3.5L 6-cyl, 5.5L 8-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2019 and 2011.
