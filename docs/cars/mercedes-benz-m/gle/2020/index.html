Quick answers

Is the 2020 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 129; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2020 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (29%), speed control (22%) and engine (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

24 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
