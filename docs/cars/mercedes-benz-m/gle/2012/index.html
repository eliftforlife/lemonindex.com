Quick answers

Is the 2012 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz M/GLE year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (37%), fuel or ev drive system (16%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz M/GLE get?

17 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
