Quick answers

Is the 2024 Audi Q5 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.4× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 74; 100 is average). The best Audi Q5 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2024 Audi Q5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (62%), electrical (25%) and brakes (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Audi Q5 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Audi Q5 get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
