Quick answers

Is the 2017 Audi Q5 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 41; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q5 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2017 Audi Q5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (32%), speed control (12%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Audi Q5 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Audi Q5 get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Audi Q5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2017 Audi Q5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Audi Q5 cost in fuel a year?

About $3600 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Audi Q5 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Audi Q5 year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2017 and 2020.
