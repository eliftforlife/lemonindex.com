Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Audi Q5 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2019 Audi Q5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (27%), brakes (23%) and electrical (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Audi Q5 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Audi Q5 get?

24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Audi Q5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2019 Audi Q5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Audi Q5 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Audi Q5 have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Audi Q5 year than the 2019?

Not nearby: the 2019 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Audi Q5 years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V947000 (electrical), 19V737000 (body structure) and 19V473000 (airbags) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (6 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $7,000.)
  6. Audi sent dealers 1091 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Steering wheel shimmy, vibration and/or oscillation”?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
