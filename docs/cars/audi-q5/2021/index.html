Quick answers

Is the 2021 Audi Q5 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 120; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q5 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2021 Audi Q5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (34%), engine (21%) and automatic emergency braking (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Audi Q5 have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Audi Q5 get?

25 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
