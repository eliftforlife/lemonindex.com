Recalls 7

  • suspension 2019

    A joint in one or both of the front lower control arms may have been improperly welded.

  • airbags 2017

    While being previously remedied for recall 16V-651, the affected vehicles may not have received the complete software update necessary to remedy the recall condition.

  • airbags 2016

    In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test.

  • electrical 2016

    The affected radios may fail to provide an audible warning chime when the driver fails to fasten their seatbelt or if the driver leaves the key in the ignition.

  • equipment 2016

    The affected radios may fail to provide an audible warning chime when the driver, after turning off the ignition and leaving the key in the cylinder, waits 10 minutes or longer to open the door.

  • equipment 2015

    The affected vehicles have tire placards that do not provide the rim size information for the front or rear wheels.

  • steering 2015

    The steering column assembly housing may contact the power steering printed circuit board causing the circuit board to wear.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chevrolet Trax reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Trax year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2015 Chevrolet Trax problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (22%), electrical (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chevrolet Trax have?

7 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chevrolet Trax get?

27 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Chevrolet Trax safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Chevrolet Trax problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,200 and 74,800 miles. The middle report was at 35,700 miles, from 64 complaints.

Does the 2015 Chevrolet Trax have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Chevrolet Trax cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Chevrolet Trax have?

1.4L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Trax year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2020.
