Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Trax reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2021.

Is the Chevrolet Trax a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2022: Lemon Index 52; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints and 2015 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Trax years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2022, 2020. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Trax years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Trax year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2015 (better than average, Lemon Index 64), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 63), 2016 (better than average, Lemon Index 61).

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Trax have?

21 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2016 (5), 2017 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Trax problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and suspension most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
