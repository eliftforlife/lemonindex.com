Quick answers

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Trax reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 18; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2021 Chevrolet Trax problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), airbags (18%) and electrical (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Chevrolet Trax have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Chevrolet Trax get?

26 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
