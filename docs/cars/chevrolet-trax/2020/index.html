Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Trax reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Trax year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2020 Chevrolet Trax problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (27%), electrical (21%) and brakes (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chevrolet Trax have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Chevrolet Trax get?

26 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
