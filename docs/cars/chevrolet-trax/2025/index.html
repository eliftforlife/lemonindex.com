Quick answers

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Trax reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.6× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). The best Chevrolet Trax year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2025 Chevrolet Trax problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (40%), automatic emergency braking (17%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Chevrolet Trax have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Chevrolet Trax get?

29 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Trax safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Chevrolet Trax have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2025 Chevrolet Trax cost in fuel a year?

About $2150 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Chevrolet Trax have?

1.2L 3-cyl.

What Chevrolet Trax year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2020.
