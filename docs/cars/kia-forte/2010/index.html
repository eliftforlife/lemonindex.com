    In the event of a crash, the air bag control unit (ACU) may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags and seat belt pretensioners from deploying.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Kia Forte reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 65; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Forte year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2010 Kia Forte problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (31%), wheels (20%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Kia Forte have?

3 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Kia Forte get?

26 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
