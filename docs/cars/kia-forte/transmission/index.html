Kia Forte transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (41 complaints), 2010 (15 complaints), 2020 (12 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2017, 2018.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2019 2020

    The left front axle driveshaft may not have been heat-treated, which can cause it to break.

  • 2011 2016

    The affected vehicles have a transmission fluid cooler hose that may have been improperly manufactured, resulting in a transmission fluid leak.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Kia Forte years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Forte years are 2019, 2010, 2020.

What are common Kia Forte transmission problems?

Owners most often describe transmission replaced, lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Kia Forte years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Forte years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2017, 2018.
