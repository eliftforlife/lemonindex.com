Recalls 3

  • brakes 2023

    The Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may experience an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

  • airbags 2018

    In the event of a crash, the air bag control unit (ACU) may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags and seat belt pretensioners from deploying.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2016

    The affected vehicles have a transmission fluid cooler hose that may have been improperly manufactured, resulting in a transmission fluid leak.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Kia Forte reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 28; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Forte year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2011 Kia Forte problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (28%), airbags (24%) and speed control (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Kia Forte have?

3 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Kia Forte get?

26 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
