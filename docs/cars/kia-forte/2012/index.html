How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Kia Forte reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Forte year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Kia Forte problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (18%), lights (18%) and speed control (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Kia Forte have?

4 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Kia Forte get?

26 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Kia Forte safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Kia Forte problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 50,000 and 116,000 miles (81,000–186,000 km) (the middle half of the 192 complaints, of all 306 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Kia Forte have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Kia Forte cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Kia Forte have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Kia Forte year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V652000 (brakes), 20V750000 (engine) and 18V363000 (airbags) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into hyundai and kia abs module fires. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (22 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $5,000.)
  7. Kia sent dealers 71 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “When engine is at 2000 rpm and operating temperature, it may experience, from the balance…”?
