Quick answers

Is the 2012 Kia Forte reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Forte year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Kia Forte problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (18%), lights (18%) and speed control (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Kia Forte have?

4 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Kia Forte get?

26 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
