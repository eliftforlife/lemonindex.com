Quick answers.

Is the Kia Forte reliable?

Yes. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2022.

Is the Kia Forte a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 50; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2019 the most.

What are the best Kia Forte years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2011, 2013. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Kia Forte years should you stay away from?

No Kia Forte year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2019 (better than average, Complaint Score 84, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (better than average, Complaint Score 82, the first year of a new generation), 2017 (better than average, Complaint Score 80).

How many recalls does the Kia Forte have?

34 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2013 (5), 2012 (4), 2010 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Kia Forte recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Kia Forte problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 15 Kia Forte years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 591 YouTube videos about the Kia Forte, 2010 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
