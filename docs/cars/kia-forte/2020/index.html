Quick answers

Is the 2020 Kia Forte reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Forte year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Kia Forte problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (22%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and speed control (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Kia Forte have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Kia Forte get?

28 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
