Known issues

  • Supercharger bearing wearOwner-reported

    Worn supercharger front bearings and drive coupler parts cause noise and can damage the rotors. Motor1 lists supercharger drive wear as a Trackhawk check item.

    Fix: Replacing the front bearings and coupler fixes most supercharger noise.

  • Intercooler pumpOwner-reported

    Motor1 says specialist guides flag the intercooler circulation pump as a possible weak point.

  • Fuel line may separate (2018)Recall

    Some early 2018 Trackhawks had a fuel line that could separate, risking a stall or an engine fire.

    Fix: Dealers inspect and replace the fuel line free under recall U02.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2018–2021)

Quick answers

How fast is the Jeep Trackhawk?

The WK2 Trackhawk makes 707 hp and 645 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.5 seconds. Top speed: 180 mph (maker claim).

What engine does the Jeep Grand Cherokee Trackhawk have?

The WK2 Trackhawk (2018–2021) uses the Hellcat 6.2 supercharged HEMI, a 6.2-liter V-8, supercharged.
