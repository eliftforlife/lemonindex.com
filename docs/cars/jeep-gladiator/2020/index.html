Quick answers

Is the 2020 Jeep Gladiator reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 235; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Gladiator year by this measure since 2010 is 2024.

What are the most common 2020 Jeep Gladiator problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (61%), transmission & drivetrain (12%) and suspension (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Jeep Gladiator have?

7 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Jeep Gladiator get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2020 Jeep Gladiator problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 300 and 26,200 miles. The middle report was at 3,000 miles, from 212 complaints.

Does the 2020 Jeep Gladiator have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Jeep Gladiator cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Jeep Gladiator have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Gladiator year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2021 and 2020.
