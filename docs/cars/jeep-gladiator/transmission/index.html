Jeep Gladiator transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2021 (36 complaints), 2020 (33 complaints), 2022 (13 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2023, 2024.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Jeep Gladiator years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Jeep Gladiator years are 2021, 2020, 2022.

What are common Jeep Gladiator transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, lost power while driving, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Jeep Gladiator years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Jeep Gladiator years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2023, 2024.
