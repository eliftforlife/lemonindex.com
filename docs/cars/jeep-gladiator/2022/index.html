Quick answers

Is the 2022 Jeep Gladiator reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 98; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Jeep Gladiator problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), steering (29%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Jeep Gladiator have?

8 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Jeep Gladiator get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
