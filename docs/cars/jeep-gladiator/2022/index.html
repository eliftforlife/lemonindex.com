Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V363000 (steering), 24V652000 (electrical) and 23V641000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into high pressure fuel pump failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Jeep sent dealers 458 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “No Start, ABS Module Has No Communication, Stop/Start Inoperative”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10234448: “Customer Satisfaction Notification ZD2 Engine Misfire”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10221448: “While performing the PCM reprogramming portion of Recall Z37, the process fails at approximately 20% progress”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Jeep Gladiator reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 98; 100 is average, lower is better). 2022 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.9× the average car's complaints.

What are the most common 2022 Jeep Gladiator problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), steering (29%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Jeep Gladiator have?

8 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
