Quick answers

Is the 2015 Kia Rio reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Rio year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2015 Kia Rio problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (42%), electrical (25%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Kia Rio have?

1 safety recall (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Kia Rio get?

30 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Kia Rio safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Kia Rio problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,700 and 94,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 29 complaints.

Does the 2015 Kia Rio have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Kia Rio cost in fuel a year?

About $2050 to $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Kia Rio have?

1.6L 4-cyl.

What Kia Rio year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2022 and 2012.
