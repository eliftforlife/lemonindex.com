Quick answers

Is the 2004 Kia Rio reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 74; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Rio year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2004 Kia Rio problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (44%), fuel system, gasoline (20%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Kia Rio have?

2 safety recalls (latest: child seat). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Kia Rio get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
