Is the 2009 Kia Rio reliable?

What are the most common 2009 Kia Rio problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (45%), steering (18%) and brakes (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Kia Rio have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Kia Rio get?

30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Kia Rio problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 18,800 and 85,000 miles. The middle report was at 48,200 miles, from 30 complaints.

Does the 2009 Kia Rio have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Kia Rio cost in fuel a year?
