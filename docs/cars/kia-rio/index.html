Quick answers

Is the Kia Rio a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2001.

What are the best Kia Rio years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2022, 2012.
