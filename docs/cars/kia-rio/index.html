Quick answers.

Is the Kia Rio reliable?

Yes. It draws 2.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2012. Avoid 2001, 2002 and 2003.

Is the Kia Rio a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2001.

What are the best Kia Rio years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2022, 2012.

What Kia Rio years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2001, 2002, 2003.

How many recalls does the Kia Rio have?

25 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2001 (3), 2003 (3), 2004 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Rio problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, airbags and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
