Quick answers

Is the 2002 Kia Rio reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 187; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Rio year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2002 Kia Rio problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (29%), airbags (19%) and brakes (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Kia Rio have?

1 safety recall (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Kia Rio get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
