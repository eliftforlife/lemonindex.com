Quick answers

Is the 2001 Kia Rio reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 232; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Rio year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2001 Kia Rio problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (25%), airbags (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Kia Rio have?

3 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Kia Rio get?

25 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Kia Rio problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 26,500 and 66,000 miles. The middle report was at 48,000 miles, from 75 complaints.

Does the 2001 Kia Rio have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Kia Rio cost in fuel a year?

About $2450 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Kia Rio have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Kia Rio year is better than the 2001?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2022 and 2012.
