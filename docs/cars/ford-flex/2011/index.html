Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Flex reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 89; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Flex year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Flex problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (19%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Flex have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Flex get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2011 Ford Flex problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 5,200 and 36,000 miles. The middle report was at 16,439 miles, from 30 complaints.

Does the 2011 Ford Flex have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Ford Flex cost in fuel a year?

About $3150 to $3350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Ford Flex have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Flex year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2017 and 2012.
