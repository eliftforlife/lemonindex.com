2006 Chevrolet Impala: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Chevrolet Impala drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 124 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 289,868 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Impala complaints that listed a mileage came before 74,900 miles. Most came between 40,000 miles and 114,000 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 33,500 miles and 96,800 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
