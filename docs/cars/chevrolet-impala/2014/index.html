Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Impala reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 107; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Impala year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Impala problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), lights (15%) and stability control (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Impala have?

12 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Impala get?

21 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Impala safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Chevrolet Impala problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 29,900 and 89,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 461 complaints.

Does the 2014 Chevrolet Impala have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Chevrolet Impala cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Chevrolet Impala have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Impala year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2010 and 2016.
