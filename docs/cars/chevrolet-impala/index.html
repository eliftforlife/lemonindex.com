Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Impala a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2000.

What are the best Chevrolet Impala years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2010, 2016.

What Chevrolet Impala years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2000, 2011.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Impala have?

85 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2014 (12), 2000 (10), 2001 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Impala problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, steering and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
