2002 Chevrolet Impala: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Chevrolet Impala drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 188 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 198,918 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Impala complaints that listed a mileage came before 78,000 miles. Most came between 46,000 miles and 115,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 54,000 miles and 120,000 miles.
