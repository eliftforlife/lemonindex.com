Quick answers

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Impala reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Impala year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2004 Chevrolet Impala problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), brakes (16%) and steering (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Chevrolet Impala have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Chevrolet Impala get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Chevrolet Impala problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,000 and 101,800 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 859 complaints.

Does the 2004 Chevrolet Impala have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Chevrolet Impala cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Chevrolet Impala have?

3.4L 6-cyl, 3.8L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Impala year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2010 and 2016.
