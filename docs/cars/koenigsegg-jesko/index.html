Known issues: Koenigsegg 5.0 twin-turbo V8 (Jesko)

No known pattern · Hydraulic hose leak (2024 fire)
  • No known patternOwner-reported

    No recurring engine failure is documented for this V8, and only 125 cars are being built. The one known Jesko fire was traced to a hydraulic hose, not the engine.

  • Hydraulic hose leak (2024 fire)Maker statement

    A Jesko burned out in Greece in June 2024. Koenigsegg blamed a leaking pressurized hydraulic hose and asked owners to wait for a hose check before driving.

    Fix: Service centers inspect the hoses, and Koenigsegg said it would add software that shuts the hydraulic system down when pressure drops.
