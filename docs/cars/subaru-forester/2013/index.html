Quick answers

Is the 2013 Subaru Forester reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 16; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2013 Subaru Forester problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (17%), brakes (17%) and speed control (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Subaru Forester have?

12 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Subaru Forester get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
