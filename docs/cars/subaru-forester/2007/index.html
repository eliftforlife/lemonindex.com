2007 Subaru Forester: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Subaru Forester drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 24 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 44,530 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Subaru Forester complaints that listed a mileage came before 56,800 miles. Most came between 35,900 miles and 94,700 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 5,300 miles and 95,500 miles.
