Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Forester reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2013. Most complaints: 2015, 2017 and 2018.

Is the Subaru Forester a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 133; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Subaru Forester years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2011, 2012. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Forester years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019 (avoid, Lemon Index 349, the first year of a new generation), 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 297), 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 166). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Forester have?

105 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2009 (18), 2010 (17), 2011 (16). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Forester problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
