How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Subaru Forester reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 297; 100 is average, lower is better). The Subaru Forester year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2020 Subaru Forester problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (49%), electrical (12%) and visibility (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Subaru Forester have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Subaru Forester get?

29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Subaru Forester safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2020 Subaru Forester problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,900 and 11,000 miles (3,100–18,000 km) (the middle half of the 272 complaints, of all 603 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Subaru Forester have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Subaru Forester cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Subaru Forester have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Forester year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest.
