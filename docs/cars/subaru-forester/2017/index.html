Quick answers

Is the 2017 Subaru Forester reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 158; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Forester year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Subaru Forester problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (39%), wipers & visibility (24%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Subaru Forester have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Subaru Forester get?

24 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Subaru Forester safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Subaru Forester problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,100 and 48,000 miles. The middle report was at 30,000 miles, from 321 complaints.

Does the 2017 Subaru Forester have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Subaru Forester cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Subaru Forester have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Forester year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2011 and 2012.
