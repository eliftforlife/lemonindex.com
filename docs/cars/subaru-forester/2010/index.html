Quick answers

Is the 2010 Subaru Forester reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 63; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Forester year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2010 Subaru Forester problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (20%), brakes (12%) and engine (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Subaru Forester have?

17 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Subaru Forester get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Subaru Forester problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 31,100 and 89,800 miles. The middle report was at 65,000 miles, from 200 complaints.

Does the 2010 Subaru Forester have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Subaru Forester cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Subaru Forester have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Forester year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2011 and 2012.
