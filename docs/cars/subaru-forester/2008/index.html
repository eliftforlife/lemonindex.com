Quick answers

Is the 2008 Subaru Forester reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 10 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2008 Subaru Forester problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (40%), doors, locks & latches (20%) and brakes (electric) (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.
