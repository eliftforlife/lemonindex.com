Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Nissan Quest reliable?

We can't score it: only 9,833 were sold in the U.S. in 2014 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2014 Nissan Quest problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), body structure (14%) and fuel system (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Nissan Quest have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Nissan Quest get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2014 Nissan Quest have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Nissan Quest cost in fuel a year?

About $2,850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Nissan Quest have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Quest year is better than the 2014?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2007 drew the fewest (about average).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 10 Nissan Quest years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 552 YouTube videos about the Nissan Quest, 1999 to 2017, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2000. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
