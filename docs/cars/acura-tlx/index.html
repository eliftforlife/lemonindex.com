Quick answers.

Is the Acura TLX reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2015 and 2021.

Is the Acura TLX a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2021: Lemon Index 124; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints and 2015 the most.

What are the best Acura TLX years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2017, 2020. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Acura TLX years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 238), 2021 (worse than average, Lemon Index 119, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (about average, Lemon Index 89). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Acura TLX have?

47 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2015 (6), 2019 (6), 2021 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Acura TLX recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Acura TLX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 8 Acura TLX years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Acura TLX, 2015 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
