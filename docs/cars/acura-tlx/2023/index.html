Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V721000 (steering), 26V332000 (airbags) and 25V391000 (brakes) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into no restart after auto start/stop engages. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  6. Acura sent dealers 49 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Dealer Message - American Honda Motor Co., Inc”?

Recalls 5

  • Steering · recall issued 2026

    The software for the electric power steering (EPS) assist may cause the EPS system to enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2026

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

  • Brakes · recall issued 2025

    The brake pedal pivot pin was not secured properly, which can cause the pedal to shift out of position.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2025

    A software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit (FI-ECU) may cause an engine stall or a loss of power.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2024

    The fuel filler neck and fuel tank may have been improperly welded, which can result in a fuel leak.

Quick answers

Is the 2023 Acura TLX reliable?

We can't score it: only 16,731 were sold in the U.S. in 2023 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 19 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Acura TLX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (47%), electrical (11%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Acura TLX have?

5 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Acura TLX get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Acura TLX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Acura TLX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 47% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Acura TLX cost in fuel a year?

About $3000 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Acura TLX have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Acura TLX year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (1.4× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest.
