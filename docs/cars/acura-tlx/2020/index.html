How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Acura TLX reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 71; 100 is average, lower is better). The Acura TLX year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2018.

What are the most common 2020 Acura TLX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (40%), brakes (20%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Acura TLX have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Acura TLX get?

23 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Acura TLX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2020 Acura TLX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Acura TLX cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $3,250 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Acura TLX have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Acura TLX year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2018 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags), 24V064000 (airbags) and 23V858000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into no restart after auto start/stop engages. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Acura sent dealers 50 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The AWD warning light is on with one or more AWD DTCs having been set due to water”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Acura TLX, 2015 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
