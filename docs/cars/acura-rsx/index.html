Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Acura RSX reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2002.

Is the Acura RSX a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2002-2005: Lemon Index 94; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2002 drew the fewest complaints and 2005 the most.

What are the best Acura RSX years to buy?

Only 2 Acura RSX years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2002 drew the fewest complaints (about average).

What Acura RSX years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Acura RSX years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Acura RSX have?

16 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2002 (4), 2003 (4), 2004 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Acura RSX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, suspension and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Acura RSX years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 196 YouTube videos about the Acura RSX, 2002 to 2006, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2003. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
