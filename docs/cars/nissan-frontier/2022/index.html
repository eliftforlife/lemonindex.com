Quick answers

Is the 2022 Nissan Frontier reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 109; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Frontier year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2022 Nissan Frontier problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (35%), electrical (25%) and transmission & drivetrain (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Nissan Frontier have?

6 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Nissan Frontier get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
