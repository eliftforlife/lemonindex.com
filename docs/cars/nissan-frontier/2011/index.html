Quick answers

Is the 2011 Nissan Frontier reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 30; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Frontier year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2011 Nissan Frontier problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (22%), airbags (22%) and body structure (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Nissan Frontier have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Nissan Frontier get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
