Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Frontier a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 37; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2007.

What are the best Nissan Frontier years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2018, 2014.

What Nissan Frontier years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2007, 2006.
