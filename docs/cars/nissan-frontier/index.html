Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Frontier reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2006 and 2007.

Is the Nissan Frontier a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 37; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2007 the most.

What are the best Nissan Frontier years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2018, 2014. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Frontier years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2007 (worse than average, Lemon Index 181), 2006 (worse than average, Lemon Index 150), 2022 (about average, Lemon Index 109, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Frontier have?

61 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2006 (7), 2005 (6), 2008 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Nissan Frontier recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Nissan Frontier problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name fuel system, gasoline, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Nissan Frontier repairs cost?

The repairs Nissan Frontier owners reported paying for most: radiator replacement (middle bill $4,400, 36 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $5,000, 30 reports), timing chain replacement (middle bill $1,600, 10 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 12 Nissan Frontier years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Nissan Frontier, 2005 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
