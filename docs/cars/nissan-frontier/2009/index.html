Quick answers

Is the 2009 Nissan Frontier reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 14 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Nissan Frontier problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (29%), brakes (29%) and body structure (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Nissan Frontier have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Nissan Frontier get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
