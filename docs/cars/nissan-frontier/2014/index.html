Recalls 2

  • seat belts 2014

    The affected vehicles were manufactured with an out of specification anchor nut installed on the weld nut plate where the rear seat belt anchor is attached.

  • electrical 2014

    In the affected vehicles, a circuit breaker may have been installed incorrectly causing the main wire harness connected to the circuit breaker to face outward, potentially contacting a metal bolt located on the A-pillar.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Nissan Frontier reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 23; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Frontier year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2014 Nissan Frontier problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (26%), electrical (11%) and speed control (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Nissan Frontier have?

2 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Nissan Frontier get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
