Quick answers

Is the 2015 Nissan Frontier reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 27; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Frontier year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2015 Nissan Frontier problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (28%), airbags (22%) and seats (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Nissan Frontier have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Nissan Frontier get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2015 Nissan Frontier have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Nissan Frontier cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Nissan Frontier have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Frontier year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2018 and 2014.
