Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Ford Windstar reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2002.

Is the Ford Windstar a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2003: Lemon Index 116; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2002 drew the fewest complaints and 2000 the most.

What are the best Ford Windstar years to buy?

Only 4 Ford Windstar years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2002 drew the fewest complaints (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car).

What Ford Windstar years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Ford Windstar years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Ford Windstar have?

50 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2000 (16), 2001 (16), 2002 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Windstar recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Windstar problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, body structure and visibility most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Ford Windstar years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Ford Windstar, 1995 to 2003, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 1995. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
