Quick answers

Is the 2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 82; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz E-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (27%), electrical (20%) and brakes (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class have?

3 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class get?

16 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
