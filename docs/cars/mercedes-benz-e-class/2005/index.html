2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Mercedes-Benz E-Class drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 30 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 50,383 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Mercedes-Benz E-Class complaints that listed a mileage came before 91,800 miles. Most came between 60,000 miles and 121,500 miles.

The top problem area, fuel or EV drive system, usually showed up between 69,800 miles and 123,000 miles.
