The 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class is better than average by owner complaints: 22 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 52, where 100 is the average car. Quick answers

Is the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz E-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seat belts (32%), steering (14%) and lane assist (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class have?

9 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz E-Class have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz E-Class year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2013.
