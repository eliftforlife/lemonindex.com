Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz E-Class a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 45; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz E-Class years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2013.

What Mercedes-Benz E-Class years should you stay away from?
