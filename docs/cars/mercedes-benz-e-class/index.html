Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz E-Class reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 2.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2011. Avoid 2003.

Is the Mercedes-Benz E-Class a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 45; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz E-Class years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2013.

What Mercedes-Benz E-Class years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz E-Class have?

119 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2017 (20), 2018 (10), 2019 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz E-Class problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
