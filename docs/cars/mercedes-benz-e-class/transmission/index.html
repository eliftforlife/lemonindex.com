Mercedes-Benz E-Class transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2006 (26 complaints), 2003 (25 complaints), 2007 (21 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2013, 2011.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Mercedes-Benz E-Class years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Mercedes-Benz E-Class years are 2006, 2003, 2007.

What are common Mercedes-Benz E-Class transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, stuck in gear or limp mode, lost power while driving.

Which Mercedes-Benz E-Class years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Mercedes-Benz E-Class years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2013, 2011.
