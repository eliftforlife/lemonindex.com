Quick answers.

Is the Infiniti Q50 reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2016. Most complaints: 2014.

Is the Infiniti Q50 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2014-2019: Complaint Score 78; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2016 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best Infiniti Q50 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2016, 2019, 2017. 2016 drew the fewest.

What Infiniti Q50 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014 (worse than average, Complaint Score 182), 2015 (better than average, Complaint Score 81), 2018 (one of the most reliable, Complaint Score 43). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Infiniti Q50 have?

26 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2014 (8), 2016 (4), 2017 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Infiniti Q50 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Infiniti Q50 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, electrical and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 8 Infiniti Q50 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Infiniti Q50, 2014 to 2023, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2018. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
