Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Journey reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2011. Avoid 2009, 2010 and 2015.

Is the Dodge Journey a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 129; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2009.

What are the best Dodge Journey years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2011.

What Dodge Journey years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2009, 2010, 2015.

How many recalls does the Dodge Journey have?

52 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2009 (8), 2014 (7), 2012 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Journey problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
