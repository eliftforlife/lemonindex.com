When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Journey complaints that listed a mileage came before 56,000 miles. Most came between 29,500 miles and 80,000 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 30,000 miles and 82,800 miles.

Recalls: 4

  • electrical 2018
    These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging. NHTSA 18V332000
  • engine 2016
    The crankshaft or camshaft sensor may only work intermittently, causing the engine to stall. NHTSA 16V907000
  • transmission & drivetrain 2016
    The transmission pump may seize causing a loss of hydraulic pressure. NHTSA 16V461000
  • steering 2016
    After exposure to cold temperatures, the power steering return hose may rupture at engine start-up causing a loss of power steering assist. NHTSA 16V273000

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
