  • airbags 2017

    In the affected vehicles, the driver's frontal air bag may unexpectedly deploy if the air bag wiring harness gets chafed within the steering wheel and short circuits.

  • steering 2016

    After exposure to cold temperatures, the power steering return hose may rupture at engine start-up causing a loss of power steering assist.

  • stability control 2015

    In the affected vehicles, water may enter the wiring harness for the Anti-lock Brake System (ABS) module and leak into the module, disabling the ABS and/or the Electronic Stability Control (ESC) system.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Dodge Journey reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 125; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Journey year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2012 Dodge Journey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), engine (25%) and electrical (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Dodge Journey have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Dodge Journey get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
