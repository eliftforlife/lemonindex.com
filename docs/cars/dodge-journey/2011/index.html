Quick answers

Is the 2011 Dodge Journey reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 92; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Journey year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2011 Dodge Journey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (42%), transmission & drivetrain (12%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Dodge Journey have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Dodge Journey get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
