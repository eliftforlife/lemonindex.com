Quick answers

Is the 2009 Dodge Journey reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 561; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Journey year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2009 Dodge Journey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (51%), electrical (20%) and tires (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Dodge Journey have?

8 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Dodge Journey get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
