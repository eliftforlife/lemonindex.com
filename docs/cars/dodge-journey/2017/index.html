Quick answers

Is the 2017 Dodge Journey reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 96; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Journey year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2017 Dodge Journey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (26%), engine (24%) and airbags (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Dodge Journey have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Dodge Journey get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Dodge Journey safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Dodge Journey problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,000 and 63,000 miles. The middle report was at 35,000 miles, from 107 complaints.

Does the 2017 Dodge Journey have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Dodge Journey cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Dodge Journey have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Dodge Journey year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2019 and 2011.
