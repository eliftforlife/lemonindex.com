When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Journey complaints that listed a mileage came before 56,900 miles. Most came between 26,800 miles and 82,200 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 18,200 miles and 85,300 miles.

Recalls: 6

  • electrical 2018
    These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging. NHTSA 18V332000
  • airbags 2017
    In the affected vehicles, the driver's frontal air bag may unexpectedly deploy if the air bag wiring harness gets chafed within the steering wheel and short circuits. NHTSA 17V432000
  • steering 2016
    After exposure to cold temperatures, the power steering return hose may rupture at engine start-up causing a loss of power steering assist. NHTSA 16V273000
  • stability control 2015
    In the affected vehicles, water may enter the wiring harness for the Anti-lock Brake System (ABS) module and leak into the module, disabling the ABS and/or the Electronic Stability Control (ESC) system. NHTSA 15V675000
  • engine 2015
    In the affected vehicles, the engine cover may detach from the engine and contact the exhaust manifold. NHTSA 15V431000
  • equipment 2014
    The affected vehicles may be equipped with tire placard labels which contain inaccurate seating capacity and occupant/cargo combined weight information. NHTSA 14V711000

Recall work is free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Details at nhtsa.gov.
