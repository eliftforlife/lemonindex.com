Quick answers

Is the 2020 Dodge Journey reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 22; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2020 Dodge Journey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (18%), tires (18%) and airbags (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Dodge Journey have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Dodge Journey get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
