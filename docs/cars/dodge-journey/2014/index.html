Recalls 7

  • electrical 2018

    These vehicles are being recalled to address a defect that could prevent the cruise control system from disengaging.

  • equipment 2017

    A list of the affected trailer models is available at: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2017/RMISC-17V824-0103.pdf.

  • airbags 2017

    In the affected vehicles, the driver's frontal air bag may unexpectedly deploy if the air bag wiring harness gets chafed within the steering wheel and short circuits.

Show all 7 recalls
  • steering 2016

    After exposure to cold temperatures, the power steering return hose may rupture at engine start-up causing a loss of power steering assist.

  • stability control 2015

    In the affected vehicles, water may enter the wiring harness for the Anti-lock Brake System (ABS) module and leak into the module, disabling the ABS and/or the Electronic Stability Control (ESC) system.

  • engine 2015

    In the affected vehicles, the engine cover may detach from the engine and contact the exhaust manifold.

  • equipment 2014

    The affected vehicles may be equipped with tire placard labels which contain inaccurate seating capacity and occupant/cargo combined weight information.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
