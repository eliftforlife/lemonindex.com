Quick answers

Is the 2020 Kia Telluride reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 317; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Telluride year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2020 Kia Telluride problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (38%), wipers & visibility (31%) and electrical (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Kia Telluride have?

7 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Kia Telluride get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
