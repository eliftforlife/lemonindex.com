2022 Kia Telluride Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 6 yearsCompare

Is the 2022 Kia Telluride reliable? No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 218; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Telluride year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2021.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11033994: “WARRANTY EXTENSION CAMPAIGN: OIL FILTER HOUSING GASKET REPLACEMENT (WTY045) - This bulletin…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10253811: “SERVICE ACTION: DISPLAY AUDIO 2.0/AVN5W SOFTWARE UPDATE (SA569) - This bulletin provides…”)

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Kia Telluride reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 218; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Telluride year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2021.

What are the most common 2022 Kia Telluride problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (25%), electrical (19%) and engine (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Kia Telluride have?

7 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Kia Telluride get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Kia Telluride safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2022 Kia Telluride problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 18,000 and 64,000 miles (29,000–104,000 km) (the middle half of the 25 complaints, of all 327 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2022 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2022 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2022 Kia Telluride have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 6% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Kia Telluride cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Kia Telluride have?

3.8L 6-cyl.

What Kia Telluride year is better than the 2022?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about twice the average car's complaints).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V430000 (seats), 24V407000 (seats) and 24V214000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (21 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $5,000.)
  6. Kia sent dealers 96 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “BATTERY DEALER (SHIPPING)/CUSTOMER MODE SWITCH - This bulletin has been revised to include”?
