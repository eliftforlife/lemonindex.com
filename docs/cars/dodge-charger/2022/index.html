Quick answers

Is the 2022 Dodge Charger reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 12.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 8; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (38%), airbags (25%) and transmission & drivetrain (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Dodge Charger have?

3 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Dodge Charger get?

15 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
