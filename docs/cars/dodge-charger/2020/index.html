Quick answers

Is the 2020 Dodge Charger reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 26; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Charger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (32%), electrical (21%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Dodge Charger have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Dodge Charger get?

15 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
