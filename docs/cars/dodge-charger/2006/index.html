Quick answers

Is the 2006 Dodge Charger reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 145; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Charger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2006 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (70%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Dodge Charger have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Dodge Charger get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Dodge Charger problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 50,000 and 108,000 miles. The middle report was at 80,500 miles, from 1,079 complaints.

Does the 2006 Dodge Charger have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Dodge Charger cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Dodge Charger have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Charger year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2021.
