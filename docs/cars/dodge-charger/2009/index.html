2009 Dodge Charger: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Dodge Charger drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 36 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 60,651 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Charger complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 51,000 miles and 117,700 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 55,000 miles and 124,800 miles.
