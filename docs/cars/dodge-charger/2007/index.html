When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 44,000 and 110,000 miles (71,000–177,000 km) (the middle half of the 441 complaints, of all 570 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles (241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: transmission & drivetrain mostly 51,000–100,000 miles (83,000–161,000 km); engine mostly 66,000–130,000 miles (105,000–209,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V352000 (airbags), 15V313000 (airbags) and 08V583000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work? (16 owners named a fuel system repair bill; the middle one was $1,600.)
  7. Dodge sent dealers 47 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Torque converter shudder / shift quality improvements / dtc p0732”?

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Dodge Charger reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The Dodge Charger year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2007 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (40%), brakes (18%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Dodge Charger have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Dodge Charger get?

15 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Dodge Charger problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 44,000 and 110,000 miles (71,000–177,000 km) (the middle half of the 441 complaints, of all 570 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Dodge Charger have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 40% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Dodge Charger cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $5,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Dodge Charger have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Charger year is better than the 2007?

Nearby, the 2008 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.
