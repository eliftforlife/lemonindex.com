2007 Dodge Charger: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Dodge Charger drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 115 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 119,289 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Charger complaints that listed a mileage came before 78,000 miles. Most came between 44,000 miles and 110,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 51,400 miles and 100,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
