Dodge Charger transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2007 (208 complaints), 2006 (171 complaints), 2012 (35 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2022, 2021.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2022 2022

    The shifter assembly was not properly heat-treated, which can prevent the shifter from properly engaging in the "drive" position.

  • 2018 2018

    An incorrect transmission park lock rod may have been installed in the transmission.

  • 2018 2018

    The front driveshaft universal joint may seize or fracture causing the shaft to detach from the vehicle.

  • 2017 2017

    The front driveshaft bolts may loosen and allow the front driveshaft to disconnect, potentially causing a loss of motive power.

  • 2016 2018

    If the vehicle maintains its speed or accelerates despite attempts to deactivate the cruise control, there would be an increased risk of a crash.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Dodge Charger years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Dodge Charger years are 2007, 2006, 2012.

What are common Dodge Charger transmission problems?

Owners most often describe rolled away or park trouble, stuck in gear or limp mode, lost power while driving.

Which Dodge Charger years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Dodge Charger years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2022, 2021.
