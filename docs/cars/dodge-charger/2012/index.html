Quick answers

Is the 2012 Dodge Charger reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 311; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Charger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (63%), airbags (13%) and lights (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Dodge Charger have?

14 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Dodge Charger get?

17 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Dodge Charger safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Dodge Charger problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 44,000 and 86,000 miles. The middle report was at 64,000 miles, from 487 complaints.

Does the 2012 Dodge Charger have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Dodge Charger cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Dodge Charger have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Charger year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2021.
