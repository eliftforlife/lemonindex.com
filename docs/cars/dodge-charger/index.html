Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Charger reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Dodge Charger a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best Dodge Charger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2020. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Charger years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 357, the first year of a new generation), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 311), 2014 (worse than average, Lemon Index 133). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Charger have?

109 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2012 (14), 2011 (11), 2014 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Charger problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
