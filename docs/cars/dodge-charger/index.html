Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Charger a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2011.

What are the best Dodge Charger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2020, 2021.

What Dodge Charger years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2006.

How many recalls does the Dodge Charger have?

109 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2012 (14), 2011 (11), 2014 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Charger problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
