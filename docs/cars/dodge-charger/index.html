Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Charger reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2006, 2011 and 2012.

Is the Dodge Charger a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best Dodge Charger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2020. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Charger years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 357, the first year of a new generation), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 311), 2006 (worse than average, Lemon Index 145). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Charger have?

109 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2012 (14), 2011 (11), 2014 (10). Each year's page lists them, and every Dodge Charger recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Dodge Charger problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Dodge Charger repairs cost?

The repairs Dodge Charger owners reported paying for most: alternator replacement (middle bill $832, 36 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $8,000, 6 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $1,100, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 15 Dodge Charger years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Dodge Charger, 2006 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
