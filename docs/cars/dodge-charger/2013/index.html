2013 Dodge Charger.

The 2013 Dodge Charger is worse than average by owner complaints: 145 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 122, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about electrical, it has 8 recalls, and it gets 17 to 23 mpg combined. The best Dodge Charger year is 2022.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Dodge Charger reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 122; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Charger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2013 Dodge Charger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (52%), airbags (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Dodge Charger have?

8 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Dodge Charger get?

17 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 Dodge Charger safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 Dodge Charger problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 86,100 miles. The middle report was at 58,000 miles, from 266 complaints.

Does the 2013 Dodge Charger have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Dodge Charger cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Dodge Charger have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Charger year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2020 and 2021.
