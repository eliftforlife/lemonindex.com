Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Sonata reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2004. Avoid 2006, 2007 and 2011.

Is the Hyundai Sonata a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 129; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2004 and the worst is 2011.

What are the best Hyundai Sonata years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004, 2005, 2010.

What Hyundai Sonata years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2011, 2006, 2007.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Sonata have?

122 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2011 (14), 2006 (11), 2013 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Sonata problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
