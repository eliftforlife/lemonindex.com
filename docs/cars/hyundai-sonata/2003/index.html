2003 Hyundai Sonata: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Hyundai Sonata drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 66 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 82,330 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Hyundai Sonata complaints that listed a mileage came before 65,300 miles. Most came between 30,800 miles and 106,800 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 49,200 miles and 97,100 miles.
