What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10006734: “Transmission control module (tcm) reprogramming instructions”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10008730: “Check engine light illumination with diagnostic trouble code (dtc) p0133/p0153”)

Questions to ask the seller

6 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 09V317000 (latches/locks/linkages), 09V171000 (latches/locks/linkages) and 09V124000 (suspension) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (6 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $950.)
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 78 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This SKU is an Air Conditioning Bypass Pulley”?

Quick answers

Is the 2003 Hyundai Sonata reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 98; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Sonata year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2003 Hyundai Sonata problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (15%), engine (14%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Hyundai Sonata have?

6 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Hyundai Sonata get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Hyundai Sonata problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 31,000 and 107,000 miles (50,000–172,000 km) (the middle half of the 148 complaints, of all 199 filed so far, that listed a mileage). How much does the 2003 Hyundai Sonata cost in fuel a year?
