Quick answers

Is the 2000 Hyundai Sonata reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 136; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Sonata year by this measure since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2000 Hyundai Sonata problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (35%), electrical (22%) and engine (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Hyundai Sonata have?

4 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Hyundai Sonata get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
