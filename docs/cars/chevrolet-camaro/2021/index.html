How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 25; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2021 Chevrolet Camaro problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), airbags (17%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $5,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Chevrolet Camaro have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Chevrolet Camaro year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Chevrolet Camaro years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V148000 (transmission & drivetrain), 21V211000 (airbags) and 21V188000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Chevy sent dealers 878 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Preliminary information communicates the use of an alternate adhesive to complete the…”?
