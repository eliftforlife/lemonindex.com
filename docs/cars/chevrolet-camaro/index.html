Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2021. Most complaints: 2017.

Is the Chevrolet Camaro a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Camaro years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2022, 2019. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Camaro years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017 (worse than average, Lemon Index 129), 2016 (about average, Lemon Index 96, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (about average, Lemon Index 87). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Camaro have?

36 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2012 (5), 2013 (4), 2014 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Camaro problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
