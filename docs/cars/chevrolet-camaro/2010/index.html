Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Camaro year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2010 Chevrolet Camaro problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (32%), electrical (17%) and suspension (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chevrolet Camaro have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chevrolet Camaro get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
