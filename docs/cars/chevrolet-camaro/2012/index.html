When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Camaro complaints that listed a mileage came before 34,000 miles. Most came between 8,000 miles and 72,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 20,500 miles and 76,400 miles.

    In the affected vehicles, the bolt that secures the driver's and passenger's power front seat height adjuster may fall out causing the seat to drop suddenly to the lowest vertical position. NHTSA 14V447000

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
