Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Camaro year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2012 Chevrolet Camaro problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (26%), transmission & drivetrain (22%) and airbags (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro get?

14 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Chevrolet Camaro problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 8,000 and 72,000 miles. The middle report was at 34,000 miles, from 83 complaints.

Does the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Chevrolet Camaro have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Camaro year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2019.
