Recalls 3

  • electrical 2019

    GM dealers may have inadvertently sold these assemblies as replacement keys despite them having been recalled under NHTSA recall number 14V-346 (GM number 14294) when they were the original equipment assemblies.

  • seats 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the bolt that secures the driver's and passenger's power front seat height adjuster may fall out causing the seat to drop suddenly to the lowest vertical position.

  • electrical 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the driver may accidentally hit the ignition key with their knee, unintentionally knocking the key out of the run position, turning off the engine.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 76; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Camaro year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2011 Chevrolet Camaro problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (32%), electrical (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2011 Chevrolet Camaro problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 12,400 and 68,200 miles. The middle report was at 40,000 miles, from 146 complaints.

Does the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Chevrolet Camaro have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Camaro year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2019.
