Quick answers

Is the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 15 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Chevrolet Camaro problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (40%), electrical (33%) and fuel or ev drive system (27%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Chevrolet Camaro have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Camaro year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2022 and 2019.
