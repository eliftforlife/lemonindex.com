GMC Acadia transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2008 (254 complaints), 2007 (167 complaints), 2017 (213 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which GMC Acadia years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst GMC Acadia years are 2008, 2007, 2017.

What are common GMC Acadia transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving.

Which GMC Acadia years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the GMC Acadia years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2021, 2015.
