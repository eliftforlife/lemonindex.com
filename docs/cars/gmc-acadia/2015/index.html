Quick answers

Is the 2015 GMC Acadia reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 47; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Acadia year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2015 GMC Acadia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (18%), engine (14%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 GMC Acadia have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 GMC Acadia get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
