Quick answers

Is the 2014 GMC Acadia reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 101; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Acadia year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2014 GMC Acadia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), airbags (16%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 GMC Acadia have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 GMC Acadia get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 GMC Acadia safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 GMC Acadia problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,800 and 90,200 miles. The middle report was at 59,500 miles, from 206 complaints.

Does the 2014 GMC Acadia have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 GMC Acadia cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 GMC Acadia have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What GMC Acadia year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2016 and 2015.
