Quick answers

Is the 2007 GMC Acadia reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 120; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Acadia year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2007 GMC Acadia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (31%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and visibility (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 GMC Acadia have?

5 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 GMC Acadia get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 GMC Acadia problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 52,800 and 105,500 miles. The middle report was at 77,500 miles, from 372 complaints.

Does the 2007 GMC Acadia have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 GMC Acadia cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 GMC Acadia have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What GMC Acadia year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2016 and 2015.
