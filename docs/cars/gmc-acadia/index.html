Quick answers.

Is the GMC Acadia reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car. Best year: 2013. Avoid 2008, 2017 and 2018.

Is the GMC Acadia a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 103; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best GMC Acadia years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2016, 2015.

What GMC Acadia years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017, 2018, 2008.

How many recalls does the GMC Acadia have?

71 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2009 (7), 2014 (6), 2020 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Acadia problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
