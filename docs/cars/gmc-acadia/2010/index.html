Quick answers

Is the 2010 GMC Acadia reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 92; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Acadia year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2010 GMC Acadia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (43%), electrical (22%) and transmission & drivetrain (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 GMC Acadia have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 GMC Acadia get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
