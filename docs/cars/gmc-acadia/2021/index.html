Quick answers

Is the 2021 GMC Acadia reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 124; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Acadia year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2021 GMC Acadia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), fuel or ev drive system (19%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 GMC Acadia have?

2 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 GMC Acadia get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 GMC Acadia safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 GMC Acadia problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 4,800 and 40,000 miles. The middle report was at 27,800 miles, from 27 complaints.

Does the 2021 GMC Acadia have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 GMC Acadia cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 GMC Acadia have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What GMC Acadia year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2016 and 2015.
