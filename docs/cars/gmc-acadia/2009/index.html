2009 GMC Acadia: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 GMC Acadia drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 46 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 53,820 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

Half of the GMC Acadia complaints that listed a mileage came before 75,400 miles. Most came between 55,500 miles and 105,000 miles.
