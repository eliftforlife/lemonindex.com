2016 Ford Fiesta Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

Is the 2016 Ford Fiesta reliable? About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 102; 100 is average, lower is better). 2016 is one of the better years of a model that draws 2.4× the average car's complaints. The Ford Fiesta year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2018.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11036546: “Customer Satisfaction Program 26L02 Certain 2014-2021 Model Year Various Vehicles 1.0L Fox Classic Engine Timing Belt…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10075274: “DPS6 - NO START AND/OR TRANSMISSION ENGAGEMENT OR LOSS OF POWER WITH DTCS U0100, U0101, U1013, U3003 AND/OR P0606 ISSUE…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10092556: “Some 2016 focus, c-max, fiesta, expedition, and navigator vehicles equipped with sync 3 may exhibit a condition in…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford Fiesta reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 102; 100 is average, lower is better). 2016 is one of the better years of a model that draws 2.4× the average car's complaints. The Ford Fiesta year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2018.

What are the most common 2016 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (45%), engine (29%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford Fiesta have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Ford Fiesta safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. NHTSA has an open investigation into timing belt failure. Has this car had that problem?
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (30 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $419.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 110 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Dps6 - no start and/or transmission engagement or loss of power with dtcs u0100, u0101, u1013,…”?
