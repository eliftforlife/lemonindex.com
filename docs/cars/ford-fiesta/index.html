Quick answers.

Is the Ford Fiesta reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2012, 2013 and 2014.

Is the Ford Fiesta a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 237; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints and 2013 the most.

What are the best Ford Fiesta years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2019, 2017. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Ford Fiesta years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 452), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 397), 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 335). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Fiesta have?

23 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2014 (7), 2015 (4), 2011 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Fiesta recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Fiesta problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Fiesta repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Fiesta owners reported paying for most: clutch replacement (middle bill $3,700, 13 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $4,400, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 8 Ford Fiesta years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 197 YouTube videos about the Ford Fiesta, 2011 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2019. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
