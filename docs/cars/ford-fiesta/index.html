Quick answers.

Is the Ford Fiesta reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2019. Avoid 2012, 2013 and 2014.

Is the Ford Fiesta a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 237; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Ford Fiesta years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2019, 2017.

What Ford Fiesta years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2012, 2014.

How many recalls does the Ford Fiesta have?

23 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2014 (7), 2015 (4), 2011 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Fiesta problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, body structure and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
