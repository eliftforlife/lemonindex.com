When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 8,800 and 63,000 miles (14,000–101,000 km) (the middle half of the 547 complaints, of all 668 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles (161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: transmission & drivetrain mostly 4,000–53,000 miles (6,400–85,000 km); engine mostly 5,000–54,000 miles (8,000–87,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V331000 (latches/locks/linkages), 15V246000 (latches/locks/linkages) and 12V488000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (30 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $419.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 89 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some vehicles, experiencing, no start, crank, intermittent start, hard start or noise during…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Ford Fiesta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 5 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 452; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Fiesta year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2018.

What are the most common 2013 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (64%), engine (18%) and body structure (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Ford Fiesta have?

3 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Ford Fiesta get?

32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 Ford Fiesta safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 Ford Fiesta problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 8,800 and 63,000 miles (14,000–101,000 km) (the middle half of the 547 complaints, of all 668 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2013 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2013 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2013 Ford Fiesta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 64% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Ford Fiesta cost in fuel a year?

About $2,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Ford Fiesta have?

1.6L 4-cyl.

What Ford Fiesta year is better than the 2013?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average). Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest.
