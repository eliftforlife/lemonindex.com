When problems show up

Half of the Ford Fiesta complaints that listed a mileage came before 31,500 miles. Most came between 8,800 miles and 63,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 4,000 miles and 52,900 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
