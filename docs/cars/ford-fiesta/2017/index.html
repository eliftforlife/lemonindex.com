Quick answers

Is the 2017 Ford Fiesta reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 63; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fiesta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2017 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (35%), engine (19%) and fuel or ev drive system (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Ford Fiesta have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
