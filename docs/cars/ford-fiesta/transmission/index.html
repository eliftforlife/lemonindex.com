Ford Fiesta transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2013 (357 complaints), 2012 (257 complaints), 2011 (326 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2019, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Ford Fiesta years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Fiesta years are 2013, 2012, 2011.

What are common Ford Fiesta transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, shudder.

Which Ford Fiesta years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Fiesta years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2019, 2017.
