Quick answers

Is the 2018 Ford Fiesta reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 27; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2018 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (36%), electrical (29%) and engine (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Ford Fiesta have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
