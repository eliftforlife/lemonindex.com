How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford Fiesta reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 133; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Fiesta year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2018.

What are the most common 2015 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (38%), engine (32%) and fuel system (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford Fiesta have?

4 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Ford Fiesta safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2015 Ford Fiesta problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 80,000 miles (32,000–130,000 km) (the middle half of the 178 complaints, of all 258 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2015 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2015 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2015 Ford Fiesta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 38% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Ford Fiesta cost in fuel a year?

About $1,850 to $2,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Ford Fiesta have?

1.0L 3-cyl, 1.6L 4-cyl.

What Ford Fiesta year is better than the 2015?

Nearby, the 2018 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V775000 (latches/locks/linkages), 20V177000 (latches/locks/linkages) and 17V209000 (engine) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into timing belt failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  6. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (30 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $419.)
  7. Ford sent dealers 94 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Transmission fluid leakage and excessive transmission”?
