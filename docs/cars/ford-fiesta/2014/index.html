Quick answers

Is the 2014 Ford Fiesta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 335; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fiesta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (46%), engine (13%) and body structure (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Ford Fiesta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
