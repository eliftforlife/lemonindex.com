Quick answers

Is the 2014 Ford Fiesta reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 335; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fiesta year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Ford Fiesta problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (46%), engine (13%) and body structure (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Ford Fiesta have?

7 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Ford Fiesta get?

29 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Ford Fiesta safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Ford Fiesta problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 15,000 and 63,600 miles. The middle report was at 40,000 miles, from 393 complaints.

Does the 2014 Ford Fiesta have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 46% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Ford Fiesta cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Ford Fiesta have?

1.0L 3-cyl, 1.6L 4-cyl.

What Ford Fiesta year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2019 and 2017.
