Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Cadillac DeVille reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 76; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac DeVille year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2004.

What are the most common 2000 Cadillac DeVille problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), electrical (22%) and airbags (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Cadillac DeVille have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Cadillac DeVille get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2000 Cadillac DeVille have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Cadillac DeVille cost in fuel a year?

About $3,950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Cadillac DeVille have?

4.6L 8-cyl.

What Cadillac DeVille year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2003 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2004 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V355000 (electrical), 02V227000 (seat belts) and 00V347000 (seat belts) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 375 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Inoperative climate control / display”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 12 Cadillac DeVille years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Cadillac DeVille, 1994 to 2005, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 1994. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
