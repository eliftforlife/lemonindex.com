Quick answers

Is the 2020 GMC Terrain reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Terrain year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2020 GMC Terrain problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (28%), brakes (19%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 GMC Terrain have?

2 safety recalls (latest: child seat). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 GMC Terrain get?

23 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 GMC Terrain safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 GMC Terrain problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 6,300 and 91,000 miles. The middle report was at 47,700 miles, from 40 complaints.

Does the 2020 GMC Terrain have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 GMC Terrain cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $3450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 GMC Terrain have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What GMC Terrain year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2016.
