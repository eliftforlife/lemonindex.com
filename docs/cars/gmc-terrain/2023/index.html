Quick answers

Is the 2023 GMC Terrain reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.3× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 43; 100 is average). The best GMC Terrain year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2023 GMC Terrain problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (39%), engine (37%) and transmission & drivetrain (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 GMC Terrain have?

2 safety recalls (latest: child seat). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 GMC Terrain get?

26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
