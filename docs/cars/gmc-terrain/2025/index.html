What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11007116: “This bulletin provides information on identifying Non-GM Engine Calibrations for Gasoline Engines using the Tech 2 or…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11017093: “This technical bulletin provides repair information to correct a customer concern of Radio Software Update…”)

Quick answers

Is the 2025 GMC Terrain reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 13 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 GMC Terrain problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), transmission & drivetrain (31%) and steering (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 GMC Terrain have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2025 GMC Terrain get?

25 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2025 GMC Terrain have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 31% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 GMC Terrain cost in fuel a year?

About $2,400 to $2,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 GMC Terrain have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What GMC Terrain year is better than the 2025?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. GMC sent dealers 342 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Preliminary information communicates possible transmission fluid leak in the torque…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the GMC Terrain, 2010 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
