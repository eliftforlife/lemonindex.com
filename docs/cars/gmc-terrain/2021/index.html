What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10186106: “This bulletin provides information on identifying Non-GM Engine Calibrations for Gasoline Engines using the Tech 2 or…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether a transmission special coverage or warranty extension applies to this car. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10187229: “Customer Satisfaction Program - certain vehicles equipped with a 9-speed automatic transmission may contain…”)

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 GMC Terrain reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 29; 100 is average, lower is better). The GMC Terrain year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2017.

What are the most common 2021 GMC Terrain problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (29%), brakes (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 GMC Terrain have?

2 safety recalls (latest: child seat). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 GMC Terrain get?

26 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 GMC Terrain safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2021 GMC Terrain have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 24% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 GMC Terrain cost in fuel a year?

About $2,400 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 GMC Terrain have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

Is there a better GMC Terrain year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the GMC Terrain years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V339000 (child seat) and 21V612000 (tires) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (14 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,600.)
  6. GMC sent dealers 697 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This informational bulletin provides information on Transmission Adaptive Functions and…”?
