Quick answers.

Is the GMC Terrain reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017.

Is the GMC Terrain a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints and 2011 the most.

What are the best GMC Terrain years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2021, 2016. 2017 drew the fewest.

What GMC Terrain years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2011 (about average, Lemon Index 87), 2020 (better than average, Lemon Index 83), 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 75). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the GMC Terrain have?

39 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2018 (5), 2011 (4), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every GMC Terrain recall is in one list here.

What are the most common GMC Terrain problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do GMC Terrain repairs cost?

The repairs GMC Terrain owners reported paying for most: timing chain replacement (middle bill $1,800, 19 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $6,700, 13 reports), brake booster replacement (middle bill $900, 6 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 15 GMC Terrain years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the GMC Terrain, 2010 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
