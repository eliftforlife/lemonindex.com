Quick answers.

Is the GMC Terrain reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.9× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2017.

Is the GMC Terrain a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2011.

What are the best GMC Terrain years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2021, 2016.

What GMC Terrain years should you stay away from?

No GMC Terrain year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the GMC Terrain have?

39 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2018 (5), 2011 (4), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Terrain problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
