Chevrolet Bolt transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (9 complaints), 2021 (4 complaints), 2020 (2 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2021, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Bolt years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Bolt years are 2017, 2021, 2020.

What are common Chevrolet Bolt transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, rolled away or park trouble.

Which Chevrolet Bolt years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Bolt years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2021, 2017.
