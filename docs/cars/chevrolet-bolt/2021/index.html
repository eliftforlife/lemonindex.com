Quick answers

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 272; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Bolt year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2021 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (52%), fuel or ev drive system (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Chevrolet Bolt have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
