Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 571; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Bolt year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2020 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (65%), fuel or ev drive system (15%) and speed control (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Chevrolet Bolt problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,500 and 35,200 miles. The middle report was at 5,800 miles, from 22 complaints.

Does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 5% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt cost in fuel a year?

About $650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Chevrolet Bolt year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2020.
