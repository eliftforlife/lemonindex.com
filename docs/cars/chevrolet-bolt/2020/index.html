Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 571; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Bolt year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2020 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (65%), fuel or ev drive system (15%) and speed control (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
