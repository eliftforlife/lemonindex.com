2020 Chevrolet Bolt Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

Battery replacement recall (fire risk): check yours by VIN

GM recalled the Bolt EV and Bolt EUV (2020–2022) because the high-voltage battery can catch fire. The fix replaces the battery modules (on many cars the whole pack), free at any Chevy dealer. A used Bolt should have it done: ask for the paperwork.

NHTSA recalls: 21V650000 · 24V481000 · 24V812000

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 6 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 571; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Bolt year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2017.

What are the most common 2020 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (64%), electric drive system (15%) and seat belts (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2020 Chevrolet Bolt problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,500 and 35,000 miles (2,400–57,000 km) (the middle half of the 22 complaints, of all 174 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 5% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt cost in fuel a year?

About $650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Chevrolet Bolt year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about 3 times the average car's complaints).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V812000 (electrical), 24V481000 (electrical) and 23V845000 (body structure) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do all the seat belts latch and retract?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (11 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
