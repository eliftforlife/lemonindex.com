Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

Not really. It draws 4.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Even its best year, 2017, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2020 and 2021.

Is the Chevrolet Bolt a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Lemon Index 410; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Chevrolet Bolt years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2021, 2020.

What Chevrolet Bolt years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2021, 2017.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Bolt have?

20 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2020 (8), 2021 (5), 2017 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Bolt problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, fuel or ev drive system and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
