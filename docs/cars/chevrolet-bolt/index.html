Chevrolet Bolt reliability.

Is it reliable? Not really. 4.1× the average car's complaints.

Battery replacement recall (fire risk): check yours by VIN

GM recalled the Bolt EV and Bolt EUV (2017–2022) because the high-voltage battery can catch fire. The fix replaces the battery modules (on many cars the whole pack), free at any Chevy dealer. A used Bolt should have it done: ask for the paperwork.

NHTSA recalls: 20V701000 · 21V560000 · 21V650000 · 24V481000 · 24V812000

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

Not really. It draws 4.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2017, still drew 2.5× the average.

Is the Chevrolet Bolt a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Lemon Index 410; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Bolt years to buy?

Only 3 Chevrolet Bolt years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2017 drew the fewest complaints (about 3 times the average car's complaints).

What Chevrolet Bolt years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Chevrolet Bolt years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Bolt have?

38 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2020 (8), 2022 (7), 2019 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Bolt problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, electric drive system and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
