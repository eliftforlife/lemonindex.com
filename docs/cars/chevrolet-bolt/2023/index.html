Quick answers

Is the 2023 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 4.7× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 465; 100 is average). The best Chevrolet Bolt year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2023 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (25%), automatic emergency braking (24%) and steering (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Chevrolet Bolt have?

3 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
