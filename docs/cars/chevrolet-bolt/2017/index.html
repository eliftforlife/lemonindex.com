Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Bolt reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 254; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Bolt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), fuel or ev drive system (28%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Bolt have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
