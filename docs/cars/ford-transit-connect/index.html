Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Ford Transit Connect reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2018.

Is the Ford Transit Connect a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 40; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best Ford Transit Connect years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2017, 2013. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Ford Transit Connect years should you stay away from?

No Ford Transit Connect year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2014 (better than average, Lemon Index 67, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (better than average, Lemon Index 61), 2010 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 48).

How many recalls does the Ford Transit Connect have?

72 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2014 (15), 2015 (11), 2016 (10). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Transit Connect recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Transit Connect problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, body structure and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Ford Transit Connect years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Ford Transit Connect, 2010 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2015. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
