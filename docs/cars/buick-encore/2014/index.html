How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Buick Encore reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better). The Buick Encore year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2020.

What are the most common 2014 Buick Encore problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), brakes (28%) and airbags (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Buick Encore have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Buick Encore get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Buick Encore safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2014 Buick Encore have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Buick Encore cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $2,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Buick Encore have?

1.4L 4-cyl.

What Buick Encore year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 17V437000 (airbags), 16V651000 (airbags) and 14V446000 (seats) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
