How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Tesla Model S reliable?

We can't score it: only 17,653 were sold in the U.S. in 2021 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 155 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2021 Tesla Model S problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (28%), steering (16%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Tesla Model S have?

24 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2021 Tesla Model S safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2021 Tesla Model S have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Tesla Model S cost in fuel a year?

About $650 to $800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Tesla Model S year is better than the 2021?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about 3 times the average car's complaints).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V283000 (back over prevention), 25V172000 (airbags) and 24V967000 (airbags) done? It has 24 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
