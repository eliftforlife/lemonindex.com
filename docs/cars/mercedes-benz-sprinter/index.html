Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2015. Most complaints: 2016, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2015 drew the fewest complaints and 2019 the most.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz Sprinter years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2013, 2018. 2015 drew the fewest.

What Mercedes-Benz Sprinter years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2019 (avoid, Lemon Index 261, the first year of a new generation), 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 229), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 147). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter have?

176 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2019 (42), 2020 (22), 2021 (18). Each year's page lists them, and every Mercedes-Benz Sprinter recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz Sprinter problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 13 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, 2007 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
