Mazda6 transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2003 (33 complaints), 2006 (19 complaints), 2005 (18 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2010, 2011, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Mazda6 years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Mazda6 years are 2003, 2006, 2005.

What are common Mazda6 transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Mazda6 years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Mazda6 years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2010, 2011, 2017.
