Quick answers

Is the 2016 Mazda6 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda6 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2016 Mazda6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (17%), wheels (10%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Mazda6 have?

1 safety recall (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Mazda6 get?

29 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
