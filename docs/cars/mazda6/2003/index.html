Quick answers

Is the 2003 Mazda6 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 161; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda6 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2003 Mazda6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (28%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and brakes (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Mazda6 have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Mazda6 get?

20 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
