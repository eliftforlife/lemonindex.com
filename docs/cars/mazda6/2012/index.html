Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V782000 (airbags), 19V323000 (body structure) and 18V717000 (airbags) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Mazda sent dealers 247 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Mil on with dtc's p0123 and / or p0222”?

Problems Mazda told dealers about

Mazda sent dealers 247 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Mil on with dtc's p0123 and / or p0222
  • In certain vehicles, within the engine harness, there are some abnormal electrical noises
  • Due to an abnormal electrical noise, within the engine harness, certain vehicles experience mil

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Mazda6 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mazda6 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2012 Mazda6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (67%), electrical (25%) and brakes (4%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Mazda6 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Mazda6 get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Mazda6 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Mazda6 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 40,000 and 96,000 miles (65,000–154,000 km) (the middle half of the 51 complaints, of all 99 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Mazda6 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Mazda6 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Mazda6 have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.7L 6-cyl.

What Mazda6 year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest.
