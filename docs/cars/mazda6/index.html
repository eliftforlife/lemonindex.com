Quick answers.

Is the Mazda6 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.8× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2017. Avoid 2003.

Is the Mazda6 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2021: Lemon Index 55; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Mazda6 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2011, 2017.

What Mazda6 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003.

How many recalls does the Mazda6 have?

82 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2009 (10), 2003 (9), 2010 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda6 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
