Quick answers

Is the 2004 Mazda6 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda6 year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2004 Mazda6 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (19%), airbags (14%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Mazda6 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Mazda6 get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Mazda6 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 22,200 and 114,000 miles. The middle report was at 66,100 miles, from 93 complaints.

Does the 2004 Mazda6 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Mazda6 cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Mazda6 have?

2.3L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Mazda6 year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2011 and 2017.
