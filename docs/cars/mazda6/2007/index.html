2007 Mazda6: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Mazda6 drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 28 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 57,575 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Mazda6 complaints that listed a mileage came before 77,800 miles. Most came between 44,500 miles and 110,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 53,200 miles and 127,500 miles.
