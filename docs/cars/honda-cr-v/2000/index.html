Quick answers

Is the 2000 Honda CR-V reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 32; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda CR-V year by this measure since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2000 Honda CR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (17%), electrical (14%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Honda CR-V have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Honda CR-V get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Honda CR-V problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 53,000 and 137,000 miles. The middle report was at 92,800 miles, from 47 complaints.

Does the 2000 Honda CR-V have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 Honda CR-V cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Honda CR-V have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda CR-V year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2010 and 2013.
