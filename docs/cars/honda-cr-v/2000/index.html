2000 Honda CR-V: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Honda CR-V drew 3.1× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 42 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 118,260 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda CR-V complaints that listed a mileage came before 92,800 miles. Most came between 53,000 miles and 137,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 12,800 miles and 192,200 miles.
