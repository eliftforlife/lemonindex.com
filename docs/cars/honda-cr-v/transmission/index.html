Honda CR-V transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (87 complaints), 2015 (78 complaints), 2002 (22 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2021, 2025, 2019.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The electronic transmission may have been improperly manufactured, which can result in a loss of drive power.

  • 2022 2021

    A component in the right or left front driveshaft may have been improperly heat-treated, which could result in driveshaft failure.

  • 2013 2013

    During sub-freezing temperatures, the brake-shift interlock blocking mechanism may become slow and allow the gear selector to be moved from the Park position without pressing the brake pedal.

  • 2010 2011

    HONDA IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2005-2010 ACCORD, 2007-2010 CR-V, AND 2005-2008 ELEMENT PASSENGER CARS MANUFACTURED FROM JULY 1, 2004, THROUGH SEPTEMBER 3, 2010.

  • 2003 2003

    ON CERTAIN SPORT UTILITY VEHICLES EQUIPPED WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSIONS, EXCESSIVE CORROSION OF THE SHIFT CABLE LINKAGE COULD PREVENT THE DRIVER FROM SHIFTING THE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION INTO PARK.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Honda CR-V years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda CR-V years are 2017, 2015, 2002.

What are common Honda CR-V transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, lost power while driving, delay or hesitation.

Which Honda CR-V years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda CR-V years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2021, 2025, 2019.
