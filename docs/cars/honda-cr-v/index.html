Quick answers.

Is the Honda CR-V reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2015, 2017 and 2018.

Is the Honda CR-V a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2017 the most.

What are the best Honda CR-V years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2010, 2013. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Honda CR-V years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017 (avoid, Lemon Index 237, the first year of a new generation), 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 209), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 135). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda CR-V have?

206 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2002 (16), 2010 (15), 2003 (14). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda CR-V problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
