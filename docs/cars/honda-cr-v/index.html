Quick answers.

Is the Honda CR-V reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2001. Most complaints: 2015, 2017 and 2018.

Is the Honda CR-V a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2001 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Honda CR-V years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2001, 2011, 2010. 2001 drew the fewest.

What Honda CR-V years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017 (avoid, Lemon Index 237, the first year of a new generation), 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 209), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 135). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda CR-V have?

206 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2002 (16), 2010 (15), 2003 (14). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda CR-V recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda CR-V problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Honda CR-V repairs cost?

The repairs Honda CR-V owners reported paying for most: fuel injector replacement (middle bill $1,700, 61 reports), steering rack replacement (middle bill $3,600, 52 reports), AC compressor replacement (middle bill $1,800, 44 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 29 Honda CR-V years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 1,179 YouTube videos about the Honda CR-V, 1997 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
