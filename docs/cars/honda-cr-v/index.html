Quick answers.

Is the Honda CR-V a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2001 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Honda CR-V years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2001, 2011, 2010.

What Honda CR-V years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017, 2018, 2015.
