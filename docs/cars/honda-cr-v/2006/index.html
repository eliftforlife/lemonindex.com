How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2006 Honda CR-V reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda CR-V year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2001.

What are the most common 2006 Honda CR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (15%), electrical (15%) and tires (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Honda CR-V have?

13 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Honda CR-V get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Honda CR-V problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 41,000 and 108,000 miles (66,000–173,000 km) (the middle half of the 285 complaints, of all 398 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2006 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2006 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2006 Honda CR-V have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2006 Honda CR-V cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Honda CR-V have?

2.4L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Honda CR-V year than the 2006?

Not nearby: the 2006 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Honda CR-V years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2001 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V768000 (visibility), 19E068000 (speed control) and 19V499000 (airbags) done? It has 13 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Does the A/C blow cold and the heat work? Ask whether the A/C compressor or condenser was replaced. (97 owners named a a/c & heat repair bill; the middle one was $2,500.)
  6. Honda sent dealers 55 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some model vehicles, there is an alternator/starter tester (ast) module capable of testing”?
