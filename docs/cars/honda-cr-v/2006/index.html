2006 Honda CR-V: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Honda CR-V drew 3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 39 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 170,028 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda CR-V complaints that listed a mileage came before 74,000 miles. Most came between 41,000 miles and 107,500 miles.

The top problem area, lights, usually showed up between 45,200 miles and 116,000 miles.

Before you buy one
