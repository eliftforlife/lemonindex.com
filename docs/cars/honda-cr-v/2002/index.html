Quick answers

Is the 2002 Honda CR-V reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 91; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda CR-V year by this measure since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2002 Honda CR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (18%), airbags (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Honda CR-V have?

16 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Honda CR-V get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
