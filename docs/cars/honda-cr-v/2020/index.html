Quick answers

Is the 2020 Honda CR-V reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda CR-V year by this measure since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2020 Honda CR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (36%), electrical (14%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Honda CR-V have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Honda CR-V get?

29 to 38 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
