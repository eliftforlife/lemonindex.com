  • airbags 2026

  • airbags 2024

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, failing to suppress the air bag as intended.

Show all 9 recalls
  • electrical 2023

    The 12-Volt battery cable routed outside of the body frame is missing a fuse on the power circuit, which can allow the battery cable to short circuit or overheat during a crash.

  • equipment 2023

    The certification label on the driver's side door states incorrect Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR), Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR), and tire size information.

  • seat belts 2023

    A manufacturing issue with the front seat belts may cause the seat belt buckle channel to interfere with the release button, preventing the seat belt buckle from latching.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2022

    The clip for the absorber inside the fuel tank was not secured properly and may detach, which can cause an inaccurate fuel gauge reading.

  • electrical 2020

    The DC-DC converter may shut down and prevent the 12-volt battery from recharging, possibly resulting in a loss of drive power.

  • body structure 2019

    The rear subframe bolts were improperly manufactured and may loosen, allowing the rear subframe to separate.
