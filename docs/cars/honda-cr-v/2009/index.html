When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 32,000 and 100,000 miles (51,000–161,000 km) (the middle half of the 182 complaints, of all 386 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles (241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: airbags mostly 50,000–103,000 miles (80,000–166,000 km); electrical mostly 48,000–95,000 miles (76,000–153,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V228000 (body structure), 19V500000 (airbags) and 19V502000 (airbags) done? It has 12 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (34 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $319.)
  5. Does the A/C blow cold and the heat work? Ask whether the A/C compressor or condenser was replaced. (18 owners named a a/c & heat repair bill; the middle one was $900.)
  6. Honda sent dealers 89 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Engine has a loud rattling noise at a cold start up”?

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Honda CR-V reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda CR-V year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2009 Honda CR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (20%), airbags (12%) and steering (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Honda CR-V have?

12 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Honda CR-V get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Honda CR-V problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 32,000 and 100,000 miles (51,000–161,000 km) (the middle half of the 182 complaints, of all 386 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2009 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2009 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2009 Honda CR-V have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Honda CR-V cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Honda CR-V have?

2.4L 4-cyl.

What Honda CR-V year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).
