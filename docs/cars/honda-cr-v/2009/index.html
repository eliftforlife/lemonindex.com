2009 Honda CR-V: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Honda CR-V drew 3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 51 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 191,214 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda CR-V complaints that listed a mileage came before 65,000 miles. Most came between 31,800 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 49,800 miles and 103,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
