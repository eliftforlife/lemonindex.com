Quick answers

Is the 2006 Hyundai Accent reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 13 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 Hyundai Accent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (46%), lights (15%) and visibility (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Hyundai Accent have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Hyundai Accent get?

28 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
