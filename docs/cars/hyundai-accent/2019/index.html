Quick answers

Is the 2019 Hyundai Accent reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 22; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Accent year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2019 Hyundai Accent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), suspension (17%) and airbags (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Hyundai Accent have?

1 safety recall (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Hyundai Accent get?

31 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
