Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Accent reliable?

Yes. It draws 2.4× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2014.

Is the Hyundai Accent a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 42; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Accent years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2018, 2019. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Hyundai Accent years should you stay away from?

No Hyundai Accent year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Accent have?

31 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2020 (4), 2021 (4), 2006 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Accent problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
