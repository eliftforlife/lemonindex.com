Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Accent a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 42; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2002.

What are the best Hyundai Accent years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2018, 2019.
