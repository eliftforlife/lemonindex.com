Quick answers

Is the 2003 Hyundai Accent reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 46; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Accent year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2003 Hyundai Accent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (43%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and electrical (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Hyundai Accent have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Hyundai Accent get?

26 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Hyundai Accent problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,400 and 61,900 miles. The middle report was at 35,000 miles, from 21 complaints.

Does the 2003 Hyundai Accent have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 Hyundai Accent cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $2450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 Hyundai Accent have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 1.6L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Accent year is better than the 2003?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2018 and 2019.
