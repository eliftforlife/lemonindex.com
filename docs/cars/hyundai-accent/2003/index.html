2003 Hyundai Accent: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Hyundai Accent drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 23 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 56,585 were sold in the U.S.

Half of the Hyundai Accent complaints that listed a mileage came before 35,000 miles. Most came between 16,400 miles and 61,900 miles.
