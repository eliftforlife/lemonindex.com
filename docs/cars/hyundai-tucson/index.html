Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Tucson reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2021. Most complaints: 2016, 2017 and 2019.

Is the Hyundai Tucson a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 145; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Tucson years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2021, 2013, 2014. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Hyundai Tucson years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 626, the first year of a new generation), 2017 (avoid, Lemon Index 245), 2022 (worse than average, Lemon Index 195, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Tucson have?

66 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2005 (8), 2025 (7), 2006 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Tucson problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
