Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Tucson a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 145; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2021 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best Hyundai Tucson years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2013, 2014.

What Hyundai Tucson years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016, 2017, 2022.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Tucson have?

64 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2005 (8), 2025 (7), 2006 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Tucson problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and automatic emergency braking most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
