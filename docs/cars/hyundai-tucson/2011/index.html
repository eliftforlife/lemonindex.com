Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V651000 (brakes), 19V063000 (engine) and 16V057000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into hyundai and kia abs module fires. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Is the stability-control light off on a test drive?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (35 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $6,000.)
  6. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (5 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,100.)
  7. Hyundai sent dealers 127 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Check engine light on, transaxle held in third gear, and trouble codes stored”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10057711: “Hyundai 2010-2011 my tucson 2.4l: provided information regarding the ecm software update”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10082455: “The pcm or tcm contains logic to adjust solenoid duty and line pressure as needed to compensate for normal clutch wear…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Problems Hyundai told dealers about

Hyundai sent dealers 127 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Check engine light on, transaxle held in third gear, and trouble codes stored
  • Information provided regarding installation of inhibitor switch bracket to prevent shock
  • The shift lever on some 6-speed vehicles may intermittently not shift out of park

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Recalls 5

  • Brakes · recall issued 2023

    The Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) module may leak brake fluid internally and cause an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

  • Engine · recall issued 2019

    The engine oil pan may leak and, if not addressed, the loss of oil may result in engine damage.

  • Transmission & drivetrain · recall issued 2016

    The affected vehicles have a transmission fluid cooler hose that may have been improperly manufactured, resulting in a transmission fluid leak.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 51; 100 is average, lower is better). 2011 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Hyundai Tucson year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2021.

What are the most common 2011 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (39%), stability control (32%) and tires (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Hyundai Tucson have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Hyundai Tucson get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2011 Hyundai Tucson problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 30,000 and 110,000 miles (48,000–178,000 km) (the middle half of the 70 complaints, of all 124 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2011 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2011 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2011 Hyundai Tucson have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 39% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Hyundai Tucson cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Hyundai Tucson have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Tucson year is better than the 2011?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2021 drew the fewest.
