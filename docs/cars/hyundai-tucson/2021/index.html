Quick answers

Is the 2021 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 24; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2021 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (37%), brakes (23%) and electrical (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Hyundai Tucson have?

1 safety recall (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Hyundai Tucson get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
