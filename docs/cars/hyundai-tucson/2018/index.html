Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 20V543000 (brakes) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (13 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $885.)
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 170 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The shift lever on some 6-speed vehicles may intermittently not shift out of Park”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 95; 100 is average, lower is better). 2018 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Hyundai Tucson year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2021.

What are the most common 2018 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (37%), transmission & drivetrain (34%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Hyundai Tucson have?

1 safety recall (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Hyundai Tucson get?

22 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Hyundai Tucson safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Hyundai Tucson problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 15,000 and 74,000 miles (24,000–120,000 km) (the middle half of the 112 complaints, of all 399 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2018 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Hyundai Tucson have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 34% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Hyundai Tucson cost in fuel a year?

About $2,400 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Hyundai Tucson have?
