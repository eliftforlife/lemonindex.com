Questions to ask the seller

6 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recall 20V543000 (brakes) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into hyundai and kia abs module fires. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the doors lock from the key fob, and do all the windows go up and down? (13 owners named a doors, locks & windows repair bill; the middle one was $885.)
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 146 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “If you are servicing an applicable vehicle with a ?Check Engine?”?

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 132; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Tucson year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2021.

What are the most common 2019 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (32%), brakes (24%) and engine (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Hyundai Tucson have?

1 safety recall (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Hyundai Tucson get?

23 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Hyundai Tucson safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Hyundai Tucson problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,000 and 59,000 miles (8,000–95,000 km) (the middle half of the 95 complaints, of all 385 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Hyundai Tucson have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Hyundai Tucson cost in fuel a year?

About $2,450 to $2,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Hyundai Tucson have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Tucson year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).
