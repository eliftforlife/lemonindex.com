  • brakes 2023

    The Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) module may leak brake fluid internally and cause an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.

  • engine 2019

    The engine oil pan may leak and, if not addressed, the loss of oil may result in engine damage.

    In the affected vehicles, the air bag assembly installed in the steering wheel may come loose from its mounting.
