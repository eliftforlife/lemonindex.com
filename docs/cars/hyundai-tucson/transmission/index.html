Hyundai Tucson transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2016 (374 complaints), 2017 (191 complaints), 2010 (19 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2024, 2020, 2021.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2024 2023

    The electronic controller for the Idle Stop & Go oil pump assembly may contain damaged electrical components that can cause the pump controller to overheat.

  • 2016 2016

    Certain driving conditions and high temperatures may cause the vehicle to not move if the accelerator pedal is pressed and released repeatedly.

  • 2011 2016

    The affected vehicles have a transmission fluid cooler hose that may have been improperly manufactured, resulting in a transmission fluid leak.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Hyundai Tucson years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Hyundai Tucson years are 2016, 2017, 2010.

What are common Hyundai Tucson transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Hyundai Tucson years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Hyundai Tucson years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2020, 2021.
