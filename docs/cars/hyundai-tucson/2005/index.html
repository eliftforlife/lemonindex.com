Quick answers

Is the 2005 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 136; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Tucson year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2005 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (20%), airbags (19%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Hyundai Tucson have?

8 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Hyundai Tucson get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
