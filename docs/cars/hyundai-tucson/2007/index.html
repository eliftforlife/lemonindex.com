Quick answers

Is the 2007 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 13 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (38%), seats (15%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson have?

5 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Hyundai Tucson problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,000 and 129,000 miles. The middle report was at 88,000 miles, from 47 complaints.

Does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

What Hyundai Tucson year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2013 and 2014.
