Quick answers

Is the 2007 Hyundai Tucson reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 13 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Hyundai Tucson problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (38%), seats (15%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson have?

5 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Hyundai Tucson get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
