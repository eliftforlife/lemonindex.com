Audi S4

Should you buy an Audi S4?

  • New, the S4 sedan cost $37,900 (2000, plus $525 destination).
  • YouTube likes the 2001 best (47/100).
  • Most owner complaints: the 2000 (143). Fewest: the 2002 (22). Raw counts, not per car sold, for every version NHTSA files under this name.
  • Budget for turbo failure: 3 owner complaints mention it. Ask whether the turbos were ever replaced. Owners often do them with the engine out.
  • Budget for valve cover gasket oil leaks: 5 owner complaints mention it. Replace the valve cover gaskets with a full kit, ideally while the engine is out.
  • 1 recall campaign across 2000–2002 (all S4 versions). Check a VIN before you buy.
Sources
  1. Audi of America: Audi's broadest-ever offering, 2000 model-year pricing (Sep 1999, via The Auto Channel)
