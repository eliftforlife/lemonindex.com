Questions

Quick answers

How fast is the Audi S4?

The B9 S4 makes 354 hp and 369 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.3 seconds. Top speed: 155 mph (electronically limited).

What engine does the Audi S4 have?

The B9 S4 (2018–2025) uses the Audi 3.0 TFSI V6 (EA839), a 3.0-liter V-6, single twin-scroll turbo. Older generations: B5: Audi 2.7T V6 biturbo; B6: Audi 4.2 V8 (40-valve); B7: Audi 4.2 V8 (40-valve); B8: Audi 3.0 TFSI V6 (supercharged).
