Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Saturn Vue reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 15 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Saturn Vue problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (40%), body structure (20%) and equipment & accessories (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Saturn Vue have?

2 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Saturn Vue get?

18 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2009 Saturn Vue have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Saturn Vue cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $3,350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Saturn Vue have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Saturn Vue year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2008 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average). Across every year, 2005 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 08V616000 (steering) and 08V470000 (steering) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Saturn sent dealers 1023 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Low tire pressure, leaking cast aluminum wheels (repair with adhesive sealant)”?

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
