Quick answers.

Is the Saturn Vue reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car. Fewest complaints: 2005. Most complaints: 2003.

Is the Saturn Vue a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2002-2009: Lemon Index 109; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2005 drew the fewest complaints and 2003 the most.

What are the best Saturn Vue years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2005, 2002, 2007. 2005 drew the fewest.

What Saturn Vue years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003.

How many recalls does the Saturn Vue have?

10 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2008 (3), 2002 (2), 2004 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Saturn Vue problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
