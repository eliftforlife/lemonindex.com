Quick answers.

Is the Toyota C-HR reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020.

Is the Toyota C-HR a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2018-2021: Complaint Score 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2018 the most.

What are the best Toyota C-HR years to buy?

Only 4 Toyota C-HR years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2020 drew the fewest complaints (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

What Toyota C-HR years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Toyota C-HR years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Toyota C-HR have?

6 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2019 (3), 2018 (1), 2020 (1). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota C-HR problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Toyota C-HR years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 197 YouTube videos about the Toyota C-HR, 2018 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
