Quick answers.

Is the Toyota C-HR reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020.

Is the Toyota C-HR a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2018-2021: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2018 the most.

What are the best Toyota C-HR years to buy?

Only 4 Toyota C-HR years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2020 drew the fewest complaints (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

What Toyota C-HR years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Toyota C-HR years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Toyota C-HR have?

6 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2019 (3), 2018 (1), 2020 (1). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota C-HR problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
