Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2007 GMC Envoy reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 79; 100 is average, lower is better). 2007 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.5× the average car's complaints.

What are the most common 2007 GMC Envoy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (41%), electrical (18%) and fuel system, other (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 GMC Envoy have?

5 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 GMC Envoy get?

16 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 GMC Envoy problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 33,000 and 48,000 miles (53,000–77,000 km) (the middle half of the 32 complaints, of all 34 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 GMC Envoy have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 GMC Envoy cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 to $3,750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 GMC Envoy have?

4.2L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl.

Is there a better GMC Envoy year than the 2007?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 15V599000 (visibility), 15V519000 (lights) and 14V404000 (visibility) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. GMC sent dealers 908 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Gm/hummer/saab: intermittent illumination of mil, service lamps, service messages, or dtc set…”?

How to read this chart

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the GMC Envoy, 2002 to 2009, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2003. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
