Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Prius reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2021. Most complaints: 2010.

Is the Toyota Prius a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 179; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Toyota Prius years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2021, 2019, 2020. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Prius years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010 (avoid, Lemon Index 1107, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (about average, Lemon Index 98, the first year of a new generation), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 84). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Prius have?

90 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2010 (10), 2004 (6), 2005 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Prius recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Prius problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name lights, brakes and brakes (electric) most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 24 Toyota Prius years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 988 YouTube videos about the Toyota Prius, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
