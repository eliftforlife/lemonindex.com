Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Toyota Prius reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 16 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 Toyota Prius problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), automatic emergency braking (19%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Toyota Prius have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Toyota Prius get?

48 to 57 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Toyota Prius safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Toyota Prius have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Toyota Prius cost in fuel a year?

About $1,150 to $1,350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Toyota Prius have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Prius year is better than the 2025?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2021 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V049000 (electrical) and 25V744000 (back over prevention) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Toyota sent dealers 40 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “A battery in a stored vehicle is subject to conditions that can reduce its performance and…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 988 YouTube videos about the Toyota Prius, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
