Known issues

  • Head gasket coolant leakMaker bulletin

    On 2016–2017 cars built through July 6, 2017, the head gasket can leak coolant into the cylinders, showing as white smoke or a dropping coolant level with no outside leak.

    Fix: Ford program 17B32 pressure-tested and replaced the gasket (or the head) free, with no mileage limit, through May 31, 2019.

    33 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2018)
  • Oil use in hard drivingMaker bulletin

    Ford says track use and long stints at high rpm can raise oil use on early cars, and running low on oil can destroy the engine.

    Fix: Program 16B43 reprogrammed the engine computer to call for oil changes sooner; Ford tells owners to check the oil more often.

    57 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2018)
  • Oil seep at the head jointMaker bulletin

    Some 2016–2018 engines seep oil where the cylinder head meets the block.

    Fix: Dealers reseal it using a revised sealant procedure (Ford SSM 47443).

    4 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2018)
  • Block heater recallRecall

    On 438 Focus RS cars with a factory block heater, the heater can leak coolant and short out when plugged in, a fire risk.

    Fix: Don't plug it in; dealers replace the heater or fit a blanking plug free (Ford 25SA4, NHTSA 26V-012).

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2018)

Should you buy a Focus RS?

  • New, the Focus RS cost $36,605 (2016, including destination).
  • Most owner complaints: the 2016 (1183). Fewest: the 2018 (837). Raw counts, not per car sold, for every version NHTSA files under this name.
  • Budget for head gasket coolant leak: 33 owner complaints mention it. Ford program 17B32 pressure-tested and replaced the gasket (or the head) free, with no mileage limit, through May 31, 2019.
  • Budget for oil use in hard driving: 57 owner complaints mention it. Program 16B43 reprogrammed the engine computer to call for oil changes sooner; Ford tells owners to check the oil more often.
  • 12 recall campaigns across 2016–2018 (all Focus versions). Check a VIN before you buy.
  • Repair costs: we don't have owner-reported repair costs for the Focus RS yet.

Quick answers

How fast is the Ford Focus RS?

The Mk3 Focus RS makes 350 hp and 350 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.6 seconds. Top speed: 165 mph.

What engine does the Ford Focus RS have?

The Mk3 Focus RS (2016–2018) uses the Ford 2.3 EcoBoost (Focus RS), a 2.3-liter inline-4, turbo.
