When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 50,000 and 115,000 miles (80,000–185,000 km) (the middle half of the 64 complaints, of all 87 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07E106000 (wheels), 06E043000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 05V379000 (brakes) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (11 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  5. GMC sent dealers 283 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some customers may comment on inoperative rear power window condition”?

Quick answers

Is the 2000 GMC Yukon XL reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 17 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2000 GMC Yukon XL problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are parking brake (24%), fuel system, gasoline (18%) and tires (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 GMC Yukon XL have?

8 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 GMC Yukon XL get?
