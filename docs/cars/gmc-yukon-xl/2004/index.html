  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10004370: “Higher than expected oil consumption <8500 gvw”)
  • Transmission: ask whether a transmission special coverage or warranty extension applies to this car. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10006811: “4l80e / 4l85e transmission may have a condition in which the transmission overdrive gear set may fail due to inadequate…”)

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2004 GMC Yukon XL reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 84; 100 is average, lower is better). The GMC Yukon XL year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2004 GMC Yukon XL problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (51%), engine (12%) and airbags (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 GMC Yukon XL have?

4 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 GMC Yukon XL get?

13 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 GMC Yukon XL problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 42,000 and 116,000 miles (67,000–187,000 km) (the middle half of the 147 complaints, of all 178 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2004 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2004 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2004 GMC Yukon XL have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2004 GMC Yukon XL cost in fuel a year?

About $4,300 to $4,950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 GMC Yukon XL have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What GMC Yukon XL year is better than the 2004?

Nearby, the 2005 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07E106000 (wheels), 06V289000 (engine) and 05V043000 (brakes) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done? (18 owners named a brakes repair bill; the middle one was $1,200.)
  6. GMC sent dealers 515 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Higher than expected oil consumption <8500 gvw”?
