2005 GMC Yukon XL: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 GMC Yukon XL drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 21 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 53,652 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Yukon XL complaints that listed a mileage came before 73,000 miles. Most came between 42,900 miles and 114,200 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 48,500 miles and 101,500 miles.
