Quick answers.

Is the GMC Yukon XL reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2015, 2016 and 2017.

Is the GMC Yukon XL a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 121; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best GMC Yukon XL years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2012, 2013. 2010 drew the fewest.

What GMC Yukon XL years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 271), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 181), 2017 (worse than average, Lemon Index 139). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the GMC Yukon XL have?

62 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2000 (8), 2001 (6), 2005 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Yukon XL problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name lights, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
