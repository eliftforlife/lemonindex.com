Quick answers.

Is the GMC Yukon XL reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2015, 2016 and 2017.

Is the GMC Yukon XL a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Complaint Score 121; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best GMC Yukon XL years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2012, 2013. 2010 drew the fewest.

What GMC Yukon XL years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (avoid, Complaint Score 271), 2015 (worse than average, Complaint Score 181), 2017 (worse than average, Complaint Score 139). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the GMC Yukon XL have?

62 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2000 (8), 2001 (6), 2005 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every GMC Yukon XL recall is in one list here.

What are the most common GMC Yukon XL problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name lights, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do GMC Yukon XL repairs cost?

The repairs GMC Yukon XL owners reported paying for most: brake repair (middle bill $1,200, 14 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $11,300, 9 reports), ABS module replacement (middle bill $1,200, 8 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 17 GMC Yukon XL years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 565 YouTube videos about the GMC Yukon XL, 2000 to 2017, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2016. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
