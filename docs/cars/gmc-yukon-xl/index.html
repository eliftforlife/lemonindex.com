Quick answers

Is the GMC Yukon XL a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 121; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best GMC Yukon XL years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2012, 2011.

What GMC Yukon XL years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016, 2015, 2017.
