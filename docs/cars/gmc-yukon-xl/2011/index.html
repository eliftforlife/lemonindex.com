How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2011 GMC Yukon XL reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 24; 100 is average, lower is better). The GMC Yukon XL year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 GMC Yukon XL problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (57%), doors, locks & latches (14%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 GMC Yukon XL have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 GMC Yukon XL get?

12 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 GMC Yukon XL safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2011 GMC Yukon XL problems show up?

How much does the 2011 GMC Yukon XL cost in fuel a year?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V050000 (airbags), 21V054000 (airbags) and 14V827000 (electrical) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (6 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,100.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (5 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  7. GMC sent dealers 1517 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Chevrolet/gmc/cadillac: rear axle pinion bearing noise, gear noise and/or pinion bearing…”?
