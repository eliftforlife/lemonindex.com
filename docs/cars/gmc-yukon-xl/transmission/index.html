GMC Yukon XL transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2016 (34 complaints), 2017 (32 complaints), 2015 (36 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2012, 2011, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which GMC Yukon XL years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst GMC Yukon XL years are 2016, 2017, 2015.

What are common GMC Yukon XL transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, shudder, lost power while driving.

Which GMC Yukon XL years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the GMC Yukon XL years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2012, 2011, 2015.
