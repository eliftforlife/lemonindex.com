Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 08V231000 (lights), 01V364000 (lights) and 00V228003 (seat belts) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 115; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Blazer year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2020.

What are the most common 2000 Chevrolet Blazer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (18%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and lights (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer have?

4 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer get?

15 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Chevrolet Blazer problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 37,000 and 58,000 miles (60,000–93,000 km) (the middle half of the 57 complaints, of all 288 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2000 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer cost in fuel a year?

About $3,350 to $4,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Chevrolet Blazer have?

4.3L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Blazer year is better than the 2000?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
