Is the 2006 Chevrolet Blazer reliable?

We can't score it: only 114 were sold in the U.S. in 2006 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 2 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 Chevrolet Blazer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (100%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Chevrolet Blazer have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

Does the 2006 Chevrolet Blazer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

What Chevrolet Blazer year is better than the 2006?

Nearby, the 2004 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest.

How to read this chart

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 389 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Blazer, 1995 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
