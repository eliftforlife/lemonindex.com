When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 13,000 and 45,000 miles (21,000–72,000 km) (the middle half of the 26 complaints, of all 49 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K, 100K and 150K miles (97K, 161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 04V132000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 408 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Water leak from sunroof into headliner”?

Quick answers

Is the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 113; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Blazer year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2020.

What are the most common 2003 Chevrolet Blazer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (22%), airbags (14%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Chevrolet Blazer problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 13,000 and 45,000 miles (21,000–72,000 km) (the middle half of the 26 complaints, of all 49 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2003 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 to $4,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 Chevrolet Blazer have?

4.3L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Blazer year is better than the 2003?

Nearby, the 2004 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2020 drew the fewest.
