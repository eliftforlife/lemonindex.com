How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 58; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Blazer year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2020.

What are the most common 2019 Chevrolet Blazer problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (29%), transmission & drivetrain (29%) and brakes (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer have?

2 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 29% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer cost in fuel a year?

About $2,500 to $2,850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Chevrolet Blazer have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Blazer year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V115000 (tires) and 20V668000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 850 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Preliminary information communicates to perform a transmission fluid drain and fill to…”?
