Quick answers

Is the 2018 Nissan Sentra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Sentra year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2018 Nissan Sentra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (34%), electrical (23%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Nissan Sentra have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Nissan Sentra get?

27 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
