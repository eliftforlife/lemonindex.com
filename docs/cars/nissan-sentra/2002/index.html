2002 Nissan Sentra: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Nissan Sentra drew 1.1× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 123 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 106,060 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Sentra complaints that listed a mileage came before 71,000 miles. Most came between 28,800 miles and 112,900 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 31,500 miles and 109,000 miles.
