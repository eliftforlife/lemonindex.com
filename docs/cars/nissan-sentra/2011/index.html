Quick answers

Is the 2011 Nissan Sentra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 35; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Sentra year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2011 Nissan Sentra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (19%), electrical (17%) and brakes (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Nissan Sentra have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Nissan Sentra get?

24 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Nissan Sentra safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2011 Nissan Sentra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 18,100 and 109,200 miles. The middle report was at 71,000 miles, from 116 complaints.

Does the 2011 Nissan Sentra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Nissan Sentra cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $3300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Nissan Sentra have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.
