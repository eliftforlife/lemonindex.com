2005 Nissan Sentra: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Nissan Sentra drew 2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 41 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 119,489 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Sentra complaints that listed a mileage came before 76,200 miles. Most came between 49,800 miles and 109,500 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 46,100 miles and 86,400 miles.
