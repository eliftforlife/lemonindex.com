Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Sentra reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.7× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2001, 2002 and 2013.

Is the Nissan Sentra a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Nissan Sentra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2017, 2006.

What Nissan Sentra years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2001, 2002.

How many recalls does the Nissan Sentra have?

110 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2002 (14), 2003 (10), 2001 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Sentra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
