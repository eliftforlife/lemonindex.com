Quick answers

Is the 2019 Nissan Sentra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 61; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Sentra year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2019 Nissan Sentra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), automatic emergency braking (19%) and brakes (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Nissan Sentra have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Nissan Sentra get?

27 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Nissan Sentra safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Nissan Sentra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 5,700 and 58,200 miles. The middle report was at 34,500 miles, from 90 complaints.

Does the 2019 Nissan Sentra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Nissan Sentra cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Nissan Sentra have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Sentra year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2017 and 2010.
