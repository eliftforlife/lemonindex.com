Recall campaigns 2

  • 21V135000 Mar 2021 Exterior Lighting / Brake Lights / Switch

    Defect. Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2016-2019 Nissan Sentra vehicles. The brake light switch may become contaminated, preventing the circuit from closing and the brake lights from illuminating.

    Risk. The brake lights may not illuminate, increasing the risk of a crash.

    Remedy. Dealers will replace the brake light switch and install a protective grommet, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 1, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800867-7769. Nissan's number for this recall is PM971.

  • 19V654000 Sep 2019 Back Over Prevention / Sensing System / Camera

Additionally included are 2019 Nissan GT-R and Taxi and Infiniti QX50, QX60, Q70, Q70L vehicles.
