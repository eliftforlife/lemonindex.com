Nissan Sentra transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2013 (288 complaints), 2014 (164 complaints), 2015 (153 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2021, 2022, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Nissan Sentra years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Nissan Sentra years are 2013, 2014, 2015.

What are common Nissan Sentra transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Nissan Sentra years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Nissan Sentra years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2021, 2022, 2020.
