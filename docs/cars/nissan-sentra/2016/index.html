Quick answers

Is the 2016 Nissan Sentra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 47; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Sentra year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2016 Nissan Sentra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (35%), airbags (17%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Nissan Sentra have?

6 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Nissan Sentra get?

30 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Nissan Sentra safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Nissan Sentra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 29,500 and 95,200 miles. The middle report was at 60,300 miles, from 146 complaints.

Does the 2016 Nissan Sentra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 35% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Nissan Sentra cost in fuel a year?

About $1950 to $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Nissan Sentra have?

1.8L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Sentra year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2017 and 2010.
