Quick answers.

Is the Porsche Macan reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 3.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2015.

Is the Porsche Macan a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2022: Lemon Index 31; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Porsche Macan years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2015, 2021.

What Porsche Macan years should you stay away from?

No Porsche Macan year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Porsche Macan have?

28 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2016 (6), 2017 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Porsche Macan problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, airbags and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
