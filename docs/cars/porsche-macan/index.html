Quick answers.

Is the Porsche Macan reliable?

Yes, very. It draws about a third as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022.

Is the Porsche Macan a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2022: Lemon Index 31; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Porsche Macan years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2015, 2021. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Porsche Macan years should you stay away from?

No Porsche Macan year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 62), 2018 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 34), 2019 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 26).

How many recalls does the Porsche Macan have?

28 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2016 (6), 2017 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Porsche Macan problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, airbags and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
