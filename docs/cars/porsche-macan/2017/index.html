Recalls 6

  • seat belts 2023

    The screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame on the outboard rear seats may not have been tightened properly.

  • airbags 2021

    The front passenger seat occupancy sensor mat may degrade and fail to detect a passenger in the front seat.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2020

    The fuel pump service cover may contact the flow nozzle on the filter flange of the fuel pump, potentially causing cracks and a fuel leak.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Porsche Macan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Porsche Macan year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2017 Porsche Macan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (64%), speed control (21%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Porsche Macan have?

6 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Porsche Macan get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2017 Porsche Macan have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Porsche Macan cost in fuel a year?

About $3600 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Porsche Macan have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Porsche Macan year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2015 and 2021.
