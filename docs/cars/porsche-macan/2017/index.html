Quick answers

Is the 2017 Porsche Macan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Porsche Macan year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2017 Porsche Macan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (64%), speed control (21%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Porsche Macan have?

6 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Porsche Macan get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
