Quick answers

Is the 2022 Porsche Macan reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 10× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 10; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Porsche Macan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (67%), engine (33%) and transmission & drivetrain (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Porsche Macan have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Porsche Macan get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
