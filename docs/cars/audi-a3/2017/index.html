Recalls 5

  • fuel system, gasoline 2024

    Due to a problem with a suction jet pump seal inside the fuel tank, fuel may flow into the evaporative emissions (EVAP) system and possibly leak out of the charcoal canister.

  • airbags 2021

    The passenger occupant detection system (PODS) may malfunction and switch off the passenger air bag even when the seat is occupied.

  • airbags 2019

    An improper electrical contact may cause the Passenger Occupant Detection System (PODS) to falsely detect a malfunction and deactivate the passenger air bag.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Audi A3 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi A3 year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2017 Audi A3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (30%), airbags (26%) and fuel or ev drive system (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Audi A3 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Audi A3 get?

25 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Audi A3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Audi A3 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,300 and 53,000 miles. The middle report was at 37,500 miles, from 32 complaints.

Does the 2017 Audi A3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Audi A3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Audi A3 have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Audi A3 year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2017.
