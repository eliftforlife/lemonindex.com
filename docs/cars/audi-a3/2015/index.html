Quick answers

Is the 2015 Audi A3 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 82; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi A3 year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2015 Audi A3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (17%), fuel or ev drive system (17%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Audi A3 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Audi A3 get?

26 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
