How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Recalls & safety

Recalls 7

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2024

    Due to a problem with a suction jet pump seal inside the fuel tank, fuel may flow into the evaporative emissions (EVAP) system and possibly leak out of the charcoal canister.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2021

    The passenger occupant detection system (PODS) may malfunction and switch off the passenger air bag even when the seat is occupied.

  • Airbags · recall issued 2019

    An improper electrical contact may cause the Passenger Occupant Detection System (PODS) to falsely detect a malfunction and deactivate the passenger air bag.

Show all 7 recalls
  • Equipment · recall issued 2017

    The safety certification label has an incorrect vehicle production date.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2016

    Due to a problem with the suction pump inside the fuel tank, fuel may flow into the evaporative emissions (EVAP) system.

  • Seats · recall issued 2015

    The affected vehicles have a driver or front passenger seat whose cover may be incorrectly stitched, preventing the side air bag from properly deploying in the event of a crash.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2015

    If the fuel pump fails, the vehicle will not start, or if the engine is running, it will stop and the vehicle will stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Audi A3 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 82; 100 is average, lower is better). The Audi A3 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2016.

What are the most common 2015 Audi A3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (17%), engine (17%) and fuel system (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Audi A3 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Audi A3 get?

26 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Audi A3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2015 Audi A3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 22,000 and 68,000 miles (35,000–109,000 km) (the middle half of the 119 complaints, of all 233 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2015 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2015 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2015 Audi A3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Audi A3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2,700 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Audi A3 have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Audi A3 year is better than the 2015?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V110000 (fuel system, gasoline), 21V198000 (airbags) and 19V474000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Audi sent dealers 1141 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On Audi A3 vehicles manufactured within a specific period the turbocharger may fail causing…”?
