Quick answers.

Is the Audi A3 reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car. Best year: 2016.

Is the Audi A3 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2016 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Audi A3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2016, 2015, 2017.

What Audi A3 years should you stay away from?

No Audi A3 year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Audi A3 have?

18 safety recalls across the 3 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2016 (6), 2017 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi A3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and fuel or ev drive system most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
