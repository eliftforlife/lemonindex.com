Quick answers.

Is the Audi A3 reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2016.

Is the Audi A3 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 107; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2016 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Audi A3 years to buy?

Only 3 Audi A3 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2016 drew the fewest complaints (about half as many complaints as the average car).

What Audi A3 years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Audi A3 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Audi A3 have?

70 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2015 (7), 2022 (7), 2016 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Audi A3 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Audi A3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Audi A3 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 189 YouTube videos about the Audi A3, 2015 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2019. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
