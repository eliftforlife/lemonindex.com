Quick answers

Is the 2024 Honda HR-V reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.2× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 84; 100 is average). The best Honda HR-V year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2024 Honda HR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (47%), automatic emergency braking (12%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Honda HR-V have?

4 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Honda HR-V get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Honda HR-V safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Honda HR-V have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Honda HR-V cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Honda HR-V have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda HR-V year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2021 and 2019.
