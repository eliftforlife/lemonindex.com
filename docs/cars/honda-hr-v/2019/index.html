Quick answers

Is the 2019 Honda HR-V reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 41; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda HR-V year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2019 Honda HR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (24%), electrical (20%) and fuel or ev drive system (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Honda HR-V have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Honda HR-V get?

28 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
