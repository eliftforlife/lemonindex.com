Quick answers.

Is the Honda HR-V reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020.

Is the Honda HR-V a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2016-2022: Lemon Index 41; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2016 the most.

What are the best Honda HR-V years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2021, 2022. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Honda HR-V years should you stay away from?

No Honda HR-V year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2016 (better than average, Lemon Index 58), 2018 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 47), 2017 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 45).

How many recalls does the Honda HR-V have?

32 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2019 (7), 2020 (5), 2021 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda HR-V recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda HR-V problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, steering and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 10 Honda HR-V years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Honda HR-V, 2016 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
