Quick answers

Is the 2023 Honda HR-V reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 4.7× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 468; 100 is average). The best Honda HR-V year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2023 Honda HR-V problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (46%), visibility (12%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Honda HR-V have?

3 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Honda HR-V get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Honda HR-V safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 Honda HR-V problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 9,500 and 22,900 miles. The middle report was at 13,500 miles, from 69 complaints.

Does the 2023 Honda HR-V have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Honda HR-V cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Honda HR-V have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Honda HR-V year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2021 and 2019.
