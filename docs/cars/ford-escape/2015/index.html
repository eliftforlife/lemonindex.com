Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford Escape reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 57; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Escape year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2015 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (18%), electrical (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford Escape have?

9 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford Escape get?

23 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
