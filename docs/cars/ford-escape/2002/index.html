How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Ford Escape reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 281; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Escape year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2002 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (50%), electrical (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Ford Escape have?

10 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Ford Escape get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 15V606000 (transmission & drivetrain), 15E045000 (suspension) and 14V165000 (body structure) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Ford sent dealers 109 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some engine assemblies with aluminum cylinder heads repaired in-vehile have been found to leak…”?
