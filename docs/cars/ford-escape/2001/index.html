Problems Ford told dealers about

Ford sent dealers 158 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Manual transmission shifter rattle or buzz
  • Rear axle pinion seal leak at the rotary blade coupling (rbc)
  • Intermittent loss of both

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2001 Ford Escape reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 263; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Escape year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2001 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (42%), electrical (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Ford Escape have?

15 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Ford Escape get?

18 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Ford Escape problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 42,100 and 121,900 miles. The middle report was at 75,400 miles, from 478 complaints.

Does the 2001 Ford Escape have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Ford Escape cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Ford Escape have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Ford Escape year is better than the 2001?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2015 and 2018.
