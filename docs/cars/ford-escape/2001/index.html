2001 Ford Escape: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Ford Escape drew 2.6× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 577 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 206,819 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Escape complaints that listed a mileage came before 75,400 miles. Most came between 42,100 miles and 121,900 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 24,100 miles and 76,600 miles.
