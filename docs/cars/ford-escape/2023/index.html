Quick answers

Is the 2023 Ford Escape reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.2× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 80; 100 is average). The best Ford Escape year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2023 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (45%), engine (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Ford Escape have?

11 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Ford Escape get?

26 to 40 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Ford Escape safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 Ford Escape problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,700 and 35,000 miles. The middle report was at 15,500 miles, from 33 complaints.

Does the 2023 Ford Escape have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Ford Escape cost in fuel a year?

About $1600 to $2450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Ford Escape have?

1.5L 3-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Ford Escape year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2015 and 2018.
