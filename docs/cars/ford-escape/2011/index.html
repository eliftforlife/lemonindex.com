When problems show up

Half of the Ford Escape complaints that listed a mileage came before 57,000 miles. Most came between 39,700 miles and 96,000 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 78,000 miles and 144,000 miles.

Recalls: 3

  • fuel system, gasoline 2016
    On vehicles with a 3.0L engine, the Fuel Delivery Module (FDM) may crack, causing a fuel leak. NHTSA 16V777000
  • suspension 2015
    The affected wheel bearings have incorrect dimensions of a 42mm bore, 76mm outer dimension, and 39mm width, instead of a 42mm bore, 78mm outer dimension and a 45mm width. NHTSA 15E045000
  • steering 2014
    The affected vehicles have a steering torque sensor that may not be able to properly detect driver steering input. NHTSA 14V284000

Before you buy one
