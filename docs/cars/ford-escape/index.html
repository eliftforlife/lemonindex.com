Quick answers.

Is the Ford Escape reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.5× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2010, 2011 and 2013.

Is the Ford Escape a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 153; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Ford Escape years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2018, 2015. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Ford Escape years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2020, 2021.

How many recalls does the Ford Escape have?

226 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2020 (24), 2022 (21), 2021 (20). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Escape problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and speed control most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
