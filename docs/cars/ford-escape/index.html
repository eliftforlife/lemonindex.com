Quick answers.

Is the Ford Escape a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 153; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2010.

What are the best Ford Escape years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2004, 2015.

What Ford Escape years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2010, 2020, 2002.
