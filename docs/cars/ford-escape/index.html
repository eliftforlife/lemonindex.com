Quick answers.

Is the Ford Escape reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.5× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2002, 2010 and 2020.

Is the Ford Escape a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 153; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Ford Escape years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2004, 2018. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Ford Escape years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010 (avoid, Lemon Index 516), 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 495, the first year of a new generation), 2002 (avoid, Lemon Index 281). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Escape have?

226 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2020 (24), 2022 (21), 2021 (20). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Escape recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Escape problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and speed control most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Escape repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Escape owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $9,000, 248 reports), throttle body replacement (middle bill $474, 138 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $5,100, 91 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 24 Ford Escape years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 792 YouTube videos about the Ford Escape, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
