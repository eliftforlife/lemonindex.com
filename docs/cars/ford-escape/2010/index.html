Problems Ford told dealers about

Ford sent dealers 49 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • The transmission may leak fluid from a seal
  • Illuminated mil or safety stop now message, or a no
  • On the left hand half shaft seal, there is a fluid leak from the transmission because of wear…

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ford Escape reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 516; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Escape year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2010 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (43%), transmission & drivetrain (36%) and engine (34%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ford Escape have?

5 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ford Escape get?

20 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Ford Escape problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 43,000 and 89,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 1,818 complaints.

Does the 2010 Ford Escape have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 36% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Ford Escape cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Ford Escape have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Ford Escape year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2015 and 2018.
