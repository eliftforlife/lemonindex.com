Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ford Escape reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 5.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 516; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Escape year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2010 Ford Escape problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (43%), transmission & drivetrain (36%) and engine (34%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ford Escape have?

5 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ford Escape get?

20 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
