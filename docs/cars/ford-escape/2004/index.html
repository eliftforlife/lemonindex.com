2004 Ford Escape: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Ford Escape drew 2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 80 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 183,430 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Escape complaints that listed a mileage came before 107,700 miles. Most came between 70,300 miles and 140,000 miles.

The top problem area, suspension, usually showed up between 100,700 miles and 155,000 miles.
