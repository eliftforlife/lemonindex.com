Ford Escape transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2008 (285 complaints), 2014 (413 complaints), 2013 (275 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2023, 2012.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2022 2024

    The Hybrid Powertrain Control Module (HPCM) software may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, causing a loss of drive power.

  • 2020 2020

    The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing or loose bolts.

  • 2020 2021

    An inadequate amount of lubricant in the rear drive unit may cause seizure of the rear drive axle.

  • 2019 2022

    The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

  • 2014 2018

    The bushing that attaches the transmission shifter cable to the transmission may degrade over time and cause the bushing to detach from the transmission.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Escape years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Escape years are 2008, 2014, 2013.

What are common Ford Escape transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, transmission replaced.

Which Ford Escape years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Escape years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2023, 2012.
