Known issues

  • Random misfire code (2003)Owner-reported (NHTSA)

    Eight 2003 Evo complaints to NHTSA describe a recurring P0300 random-misfire code that dealers could not fix. One owner says Mitsubishi blamed a faulty input shaft in a service bulletin.

    9 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2003–2005)
  • Turbo coolant hose recallRecall

    On some 2005 cars the turbocharger coolant hose was made wrong and can leak. Coolant on the hot manifold can catch fire.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the hose for free (NHTSA 05V-335).

Mitsubishi 4B11T engine page →

Why the engine page shows fewer: it counts only the 116 owner complaints and 10 recalls for 2008–2015 that name this engine or a version that only had it. This page counts all 93 complaints and 10 recall campaigns NHTSA files under "Lancer Evolution" for those years, other engines included.

Known issues

  • Drive belt and pulley recallRecall

    On 2008-2011 cars with 4B1 engines, the accessory pulleys can wear and let the drive belt come off. That can drain the battery and stall the engine.

    Fix: Dealer fits a rubber belt and replaces worn pulleys for free (NHTSA 14V-562).

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2015)
  • Fuel return pipe recallRecall

    On 2008-2009 Evos, vibration at certain engine speeds can crack the fuel return pipe where it is fixed, which can leak fuel and start a fire.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the pipe and adds two brackets for free (NHTSA 09V-349).

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2008–2015)
  • No engine failure patternOwner-reported (NHTSA)

    NHTSA complaints on 2008-2015 Evos center on the AYC/ACD hydraulic pump and the clutch, not the engine. Only a handful report engine failures.

Quick answers

How fast is the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution Final Edition?

The CZ4A (Evo X) Lancer Evolution Final Edition makes 303 hp and 305 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.4 seconds.

What engine does the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution have?

The CZ4A (Evo X) Evo (2008–2015) uses the Mitsubishi 4B11T, a 2.0-liter inline-4, turbo. Older generations: CT9A (Evo VIII): Mitsubishi 4G63T; CT9A (Evo IX): Mitsubishi 4G63T.

Which Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution engine do YouTube owners like most?

Mitsubishi 4G63T 48/100, Mitsubishi 4B11T 45/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
