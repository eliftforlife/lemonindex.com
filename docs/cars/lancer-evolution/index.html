Quick answers

How fast is the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution Final Edition?

The CZ4A (Evo X) Lancer Evolution Final Edition makes 303 hp and 305 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.4 seconds.

What engine does the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution have?

The CZ4A (Evo X) Evo (2008–2015) uses the Mitsubishi 4B11T, a 2.0-liter inline-4, turbo. Older generations: CT9A (Evo VIII): Mitsubishi 4G63T; CT9A (Evo IX): Mitsubishi 4G63T.

Which Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution engine do YouTube owners like most?

Mitsubishi 4G63T 48/100, Mitsubishi 4B11T 45/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.

Why is the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution famous?

Tommi Mäkinen's four titles: Tommi Mäkinen won the World Rally drivers' title four years in a row, 1996 to 1999, every time in a Lancer Evolution (the Evo III through Evo VI). He also helped Mitsubishi win the 1998 makers' title. A road-going Evo VI was later named after him. Initial D: In Initial D, Takumi faces the Emperors, a team that only drives Lancer Evolutions. Their leader, Kyoichi Sudo, beats him in an Evo III when Takumi's engine blows. Another Emperor driver, Seiji Iwaki, races an Evo IV. Evo vs. STI: The Evo and the Subaru Impreza WRX STI both started as rally homologation cars, and they have been compared ever since. MotorWeek tested a 2003 Evo against a 2004 STI and said it had never tested two cars this close to equal.
