How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Lexus IS reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 75; 100 is average, lower is better). The Lexus IS year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2009 Lexus IS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (57%), fuel system, gasoline (9%) and doors, locks & latches (4%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Lexus IS have?

8 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Lexus IS get?

18 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Lexus IS problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,700 and 25,000 miles (7,600–40,000 km) (the middle half of the 20 complaints, of all 23 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2009 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2009 Lexus IS have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Lexus IS cost in fuel a year?

About $2,950 to $3,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Lexus IS have?

2.5L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Lexus IS year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2012 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V741000 (airbags), 18V024000 (airbags) and 17V006000 (airbags) done? It has 8 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Lexus sent dealers 174 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Rear suspension”?
