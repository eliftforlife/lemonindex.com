How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Is the 2009 Chevrolet Express reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 7 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Chevrolet Express problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (43%), airbags (14%) and fuel system, diesel (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Chevrolet Express have?

9 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Chevrolet Express get?

14 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2009 Chevrolet Express have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Chevrolet Express cost in fuel a year?

About $4,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Chevrolet Express have?

4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Express year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 18V267000 (equipment), 14V706000 (equipment adaptive/mobility) and 14V117000 (airbags) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  4. Chevy sent dealers 1159 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Steering noise when turning left”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Express, 2002 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2003. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
