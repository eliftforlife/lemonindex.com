Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Express reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 8.3× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2011.

Is the Chevrolet Express a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2021: Lemon Index 12; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2010.

What are the best Chevrolet Express years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2015, 2018.

What Chevrolet Express years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Express year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Express have?

92 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2008 (11), 2005 (10), 2009 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Express problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, electrical and tires most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
