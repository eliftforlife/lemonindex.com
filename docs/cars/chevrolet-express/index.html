Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Express reliable?

Mostly. It draws less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011.

Is the Chevrolet Express a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2021: Lemon Index 12; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints and 2010 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Express years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2018, 2015. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Express years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Express year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2010 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 17), 2021 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 17), 2016 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 16).

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Express have?

92 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2008 (11), 2005 (10), 2009 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Express recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Express problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, electrical and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 3 Chevrolet Express years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Express, 2002 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2003. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
