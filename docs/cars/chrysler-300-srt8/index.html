Known issues

  • Lifter and camshaft failure ("HEMI tick")Owner-reported

    Owners report a tick from failed roller lifters that then wear the camshaft. SlashGear links most cases to the cylinder-deactivation (MDS) lifters and low oil flow.

    Fix: Replacing the lifters and camshaft fixes it; on-time oil changes with the right oil help prevent it.

    12 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2012–2014)
  • No engine failure pattern foundOwner-reported

    The sources checked here document no recurring 6.4 HEMI failure in the 300. Any recall for these model years is listed under Problems & recalls.

Quick answers

How fast is the Chrysler 300 SRT8?

The LD 300 SRT8 makes 470 hp and 470 lb-ft. MotorTrend measured 0–60 mph in 4.3 seconds. Top speed: 175 mph (maker claim).

What engine does the Chrysler 300C SRT8 / 300 SRT have?

The LD 300 SRT8 (2012–2014) uses the 392 HEMI 6.4, a 6.4-liter V-8. Older generations: LX: 6.1 SRT8 HEMI.

Which Chrysler 300C SRT8 / 300 SRT engine do YouTube owners like most?

392 HEMI 6.4 53/100, 6.1 SRT8 HEMI 50/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
