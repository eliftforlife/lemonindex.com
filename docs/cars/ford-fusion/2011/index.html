2011 Ford Fusion Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 15 yearsCompare

Is the 2011 Ford Fusion reliable? No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 298; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Fusion year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2008.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Fusion reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 298; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Fusion year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2008.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (36%), engine (27%) and speed control (26%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Fusion have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Fusion get?

19 to 38 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Ford Fusion safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2011 Ford Fusion problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 45,000 and 110,000 miles (72,000–177,000 km) (the middle half of the 2,180 complaints, of all 2,926 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2011 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2011 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2011 Ford Fusion have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 24% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Ford Fusion cost in fuel a year?

About $1,700 to $3,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Ford Fusion have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Fusion year is better than the 2011?

Nearby, the 2008 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V158000 (airbags), 21V081000 (airbags) and 21E013000 (airbags) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (99 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,500.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 55 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Illuminated mil or safety stop now message, or a no”?
