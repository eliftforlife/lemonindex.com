Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Fusion reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 298; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fusion year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (36%), speed control (27%) and engine (26%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Fusion have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Fusion get?

19 to 38 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
