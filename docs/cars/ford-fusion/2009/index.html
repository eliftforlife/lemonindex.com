The 2009 Ford Fusion drew 3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 48 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 180,671 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Fusion complaints that listed a mileage came before 97,000 miles. Most came between 72,700 miles and 125,000 miles.

The top problem area, brakes, usually showed up between 81,000 miles and 130,000 miles.
