Quick answers

Is the 2020 Ford Fusion reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 73; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fusion year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2020 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (43%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and automatic emergency braking (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Ford Fusion have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Ford Fusion get?

23 to 42 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Ford Fusion safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Ford Fusion problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 96,800 miles. The middle report was at 67,000 miles, from 66 complaints.

Does the 2020 Ford Fusion have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 21% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Ford Fusion cost in fuel a year?

About $1550 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Ford Fusion have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Ford Fusion year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2019 and 2020.
