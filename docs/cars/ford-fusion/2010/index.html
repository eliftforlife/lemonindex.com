Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ford Fusion reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 7.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 729; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fusion year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2010 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (39%), speed control (38%) and engine (28%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ford Fusion have?

13 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ford Fusion get?

19 to 39 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Ford Fusion problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,000 and 110,000 miles. The middle report was at 74,000 miles, from 4,381 complaints.

Does the 2010 Ford Fusion have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 39% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Ford Fusion cost in fuel a year?

About $1650 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Ford Fusion have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Fusion year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2019 and 2020.
