Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford Fusion reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 148; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fusion year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2016 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (31%), engine (23%) and steering (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford Fusion have?

13 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Ford Fusion get?

25 to 41 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Ford Fusion safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Ford Fusion problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 50,000 and 104,400 miles. The middle report was at 77,000 miles, from 960 complaints.

Does the 2016 Ford Fusion have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 31% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Ford Fusion cost in fuel a year?

About $1550 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Ford Fusion have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Ford Fusion year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2019 and 2020.
