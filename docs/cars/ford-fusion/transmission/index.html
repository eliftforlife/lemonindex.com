Ford Fusion transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2010 (433 complaints), 2017 (278 complaints), 2016 (239 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2020, 2012.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2017 2017

    On vehicles with 2.0L gas engines and six-speed automatic transmissions, the torque converter weld studs may have been inadequately welded.

  • 2016 2018

    The bushing that attaches the transmission shifter cable to the transmission may degrade over time and cause the bushing to detach from the transmission.

  • 2016 2022

    The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

  • 2016 2026

    The clutch may fracture, resulting in damage to the transmission assembly and possibly a transmission fluid leak.

  • 2015 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the key may be able to be removed even while the transmission is not in Park.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Fusion years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Fusion years are 2010, 2017, 2016.

What are common Ford Fusion transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Ford Fusion years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Fusion years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2020, 2012.
