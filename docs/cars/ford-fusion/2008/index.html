Quick answers

Is the 2008 Ford Fusion reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 25; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Fusion year by this measure since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2008 Ford Fusion problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (22%), doors, locks & latches (16%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Ford Fusion have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Ford Fusion get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
