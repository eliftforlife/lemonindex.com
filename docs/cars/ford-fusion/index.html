Quick answers.

Is the Ford Fusion reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.9× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2008. Most complaints: 2010, 2011 and 2012.

Is the Ford Fusion a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 193; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2008 drew the fewest complaints and 2010 the most.

What are the best Ford Fusion years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2008, 2009, 2018. 2008 drew the fewest.

What Ford Fusion years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2010 (avoid, Lemon Index 729), 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 298), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 236). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Fusion have?

128 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2013 (19), 2014 (19), 2010 (13). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Fusion recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Fusion problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Fusion repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Fusion owners reported paying for most: steering rack replacement (middle bill $1,800, 148 reports), throttle body replacement (middle bill $500, 120 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $8,500, 88 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 15 Ford Fusion years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 398 YouTube videos about the Ford Fusion, 2006 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2019. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
