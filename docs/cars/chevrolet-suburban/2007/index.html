Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V050000 (airbags), 21V052000 (airbags) and 10V240000 (electrical) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (6 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,400.)
  7. Chevy sent dealers 1290 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Sag or hesitation on acceleration (normal operating characteristics)”?

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 115; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Suburban year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2007 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (21%), body structure (15%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Chevrolet Suburban problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 42,000 and 126,000 miles (68,000–202,000 km) (the middle half of the 273 complaints, of all 482 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $4,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2007?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.
