Quick answers

Is the 2008 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2008 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (53%), body structure (21%) and electrical (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Chevrolet Suburban have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Chevrolet Suburban get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
