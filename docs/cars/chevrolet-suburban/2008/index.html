2008 Chevrolet Suburban: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Chevrolet Suburban drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 43 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 54,058 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Suburban complaints that listed a mileage came before 79,000 miles. Most came between 47,600 miles and 119,800 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 62,300 miles and 128,200 miles.
