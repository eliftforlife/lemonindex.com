When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Suburban complaints that listed a mileage came before 62,000 miles. Most came between 43,200 miles and 90,000 miles.

The top problem area, brakes, usually showed up between 51,000 miles and 90,000 miles.

Recalls: 11

  • steering 2014
    Due to a problem with the EPS module, the affected vehicles may experience a sudden loss of power steering assist during vehicle operation. NHTSA 14V719000
  • airbags 2014
    The affected vehicles were assembled with a roof rail air bags (RRAB) that were not reinforced at the points were the bags contact the hardware used to attach the roof carrier. NHTSA 14V451000
  • transmission & drivetrain 2014
    In the affected vehicles, an electrical signal short may cause the transfer case to shift to neutral without the driver's input. NHTSA 14V374000
  • transmission & drivetrain 2014
    The subject vehicles may have a transmission oil cooler line that is not securely seated in the fitting. NHTSA 14V152000
  • electrical 2015
    In the affected vehicles, the ignition lock actuator may bind, making turning the key difficult or causing the ignition to get stuck in the "Start" position. NHTSA 15V640000
  • airbags 2016
    In the affected vehicles, certain driving conditions may cause the air bag sensing and diagnostic module (SDM) software to activate a diagnostic test. NHTSA 16V651000
