Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07E106000 (wheels), 06V289000 (engine) and 05V552000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the seat belts latch and retract?
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10022369: “Service update for inventory and customer vehicles e85 virtual flex fuel sensor (vffs) update- reprogram ecm”)
  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10036464: “Buick/cadillac/chevrolet/gmc/hummer/pontiac/saab: oil consumption and/or exhaust smoke-possible pcv baffle or lifter…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10134662: “This informational bulletin provides information of suspected tampering or vehicle modifications for non-GM…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.
