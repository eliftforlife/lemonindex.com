Quick answers

Is the 2006 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 9 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (44%), seat belts (22%) and suspension (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Chevrolet Suburban have?

3 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Chevrolet Suburban get?

13 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
