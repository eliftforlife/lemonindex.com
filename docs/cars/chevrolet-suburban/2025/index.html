Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 26V304000 (wheels) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into loss of motive power due to engine failure post recall remedy. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11013069: “This bulletin provides information on identifying Non-GM Engine Calibrations for Gasoline Engines using the Tech 2 or…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11014592: “This technical bulletin provides repair information to correct a customer concern of Radio Software Update…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn somewhat fewer complaints than the average car per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 87; 100 is average). The Chevrolet Suburban year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2025 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (45%), electrical (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban have?

1 safety recall (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3,800 to $5,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Chevrolet Suburban have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2025?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
