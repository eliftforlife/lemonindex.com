Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2015, 2016 and 2021.

Is the Chevrolet Suburban a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 91; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Suburban years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2010, 2020. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Suburban years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 355, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 158), 2021 (worse than average, Lemon Index 142, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Suburban have?

140 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2000 (12), 2021 (12), 2015 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Suburban problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
