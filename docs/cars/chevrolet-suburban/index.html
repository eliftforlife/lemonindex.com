Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2015, 2016 and 2021.

Is the Chevrolet Suburban a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 91; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Suburban years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2010, 2020. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Suburban years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (avoid, Complaint Score 355, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (worse than average, Complaint Score 158), 2021 (worse than average, Complaint Score 142, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Suburban have?

140 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2000 (12), 2021 (12), 2015 (11). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Suburban recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Suburban problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Chevrolet Suburban repairs cost?

The repairs Chevrolet Suburban owners reported paying for most: brake repair (middle bill $1,000, 44 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $12,000, 14 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $6,000, 14 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 26 Chevrolet Suburban years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 788 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Suburban, 2000 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
