Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Suburban a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 91; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best Chevrolet Suburban years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2010, 2020.

What Chevrolet Suburban years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015, 2016, 2021.
