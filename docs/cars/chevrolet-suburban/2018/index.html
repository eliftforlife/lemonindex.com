When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 10,000 and 88,000 miles (16,000–142,000 km) (the middle half of the 27 complaints, of all 84 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles (161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V289000 (transmission & drivetrain), 19V761000 (brakes) and 19V645000 (brakes) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (18 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $6,000.)
  6. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced? (7 owners named a suspension repair bill; the middle one was $1,800.)

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 41; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Suburban year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2018 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (28%), electrical (16%) and fuel system (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Chevrolet Suburban problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 10,000 and 88,000 miles (16,000–142,000 km) (the middle half of the 27 complaints, of all 84 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2018 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5.3L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.
