Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2010 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (24%), speed control (18%) and wheels (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban get?

14 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Chevrolet Suburban problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 51,500 and 117,200 miles. The middle report was at 80,200 miles, from 113 complaints.

Does the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
