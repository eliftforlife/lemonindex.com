Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (15%), body structure (15%) and seats (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Chevrolet Suburban problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 43,000 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 55,000 miles, from 35 complaints.

Does the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5.3L 8-cyl.
