Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 158; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (48%), body structure (8%) and engine (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
