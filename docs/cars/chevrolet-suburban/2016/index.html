Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 158; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (48%), body structure (8%) and engine (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Chevrolet Suburban problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 29,000 and 88,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 131 complaints.

Does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5.3L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
