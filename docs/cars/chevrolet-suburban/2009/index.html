Quick answers

Is the 2009 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 14 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are stability control (29%), electrical (21%) and doors, locks & latches (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Chevrolet Suburban have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Chevrolet Suburban get?

16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
