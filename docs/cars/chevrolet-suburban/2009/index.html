Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V052000 (airbags), 21V053000 (airbags) and 21V050000 (airbags) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Is the stability-control light off on a test drive?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (17 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $6,700.)
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (6 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,400.)
  7. Chevy sent dealers 1375 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “No start, no crank, intermittent electrical operations (check for poor connections within…”?
