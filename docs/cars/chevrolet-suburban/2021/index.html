Quick answers

Is the 2021 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 142; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2021 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (26%), automatic emergency braking (15%) and fuel or ev drive system (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Chevrolet Suburban have?

12 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Chevrolet Suburban get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
