Quick answers

Is the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 108; 100 is average). The best Chevrolet Suburban year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2024 Chevrolet Suburban problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (55%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Chevrolet Suburban have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Suburban year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2010 and 2020.
