2002 Chevrolet Suburban: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Chevrolet Suburban drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 163 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 151,056 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Suburban complaints that listed a mileage came before 77,000 miles. Most came between 45,400 miles and 124,000 miles.

The top problem area, service brakes, hydraulic, usually showed up between 54,500 miles and 112,800 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
