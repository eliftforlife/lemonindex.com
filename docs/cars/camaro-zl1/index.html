Known issues

  • Supercharger rattle at idleMaker bulletin

    GM found the supercharger on 2012–2013 ZL1s (and 2009–2013 CTS-Vs) can rattle at idle. Left alone, it can ruin the bearing grease and, in the worst case, stop the engine.

    Fix: Dealers replace the supercharger free under a special coverage of 10 years or 120,000 miles.

  • Low-rpm buzz on replacement superchargersMaker bulletin

    On cars with a replacement supercharger (solid coupling), a buzz at 800–900 rpm in second or third gear under load is normal, GM told dealers.

    Fix: No repair; GM said not to replace parts for this noise.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2012–2015)

Known issues

  • Track misfire after supercharger cooling serviceMaker bulletin

    In high heat above 100 mph on a track, the engine can misfire (code P0300) if air was left in the supercharger cooling loop after a repair.

    Fix: Dealer drains and vacuum-fills the loop with GM's tools (bulletin 17-NA-408).

    6 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2017–2024)
  • 10-speed automatic recall (transmission)Recall

    On 2020–2022 Camaros with the 10-speed, valve body wear can cause harsh downshifts and, rarely, a brief wheel lock-up.

    Fix: Dealer installs software that spots the wear and holds fifth gear before a lock-up (NHTSA 25V-148).

    11 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2017–2024)

Quick answers

How fast is the Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 1LE?

The 6th gen Camaro ZL1 1LE makes 650 hp and 650 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.5 seconds.

What engine does the Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 have?

The 6th gen Camaro ZL1 (2017–2024) uses the GM LT4, a 6.2-liter V-8, supercharged. Older generations: 5th gen: GM LSA.

Which Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 engine do YouTube owners like most?

GM LSA 52/100, GM LT4 52/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
