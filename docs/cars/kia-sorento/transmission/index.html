Kia Sorento transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2021 (111 complaints), 2011 (204 complaints), 2016 (66 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2014, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Kia Sorento years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Kia Sorento years are 2021, 2011, 2016.

What are common Kia Sorento transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, transmission replaced.

Which Kia Sorento years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Kia Sorento years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2014, 2015.
