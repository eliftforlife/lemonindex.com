Quick answers.

Is the Kia Sorento reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2020. Avoid 2005, 2008 and 2021.

Is the Kia Sorento a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 128; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2021.

What are the best Kia Sorento years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2020, 2004.

What Kia Sorento years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2021, 2008, 2005.

How many recalls does the Kia Sorento have?

76 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2011 (8), 2016 (8), 2014 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Sorento problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
