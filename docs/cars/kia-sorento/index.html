Quick answers.

Is the Kia Sorento reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2011, 2013 and 2016.

Is the Kia Sorento a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 128; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2021 the most.

What are the best Kia Sorento years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018, 2020, 2012. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Kia Sorento years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2021 (avoid, Lemon Index 209, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 173, the first year of a new generation), 2017 (worse than average, Lemon Index 168). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Sorento have?

76 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2011 (8), 2016 (8), 2014 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Sorento problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
