Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Kia Sorento reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2005, 2008 and 2021.

Is the Kia Sorento a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 128; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2021 the most.

What are the best Kia Sorento years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018, 2020, 2004. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Kia Sorento years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2021 (avoid, Lemon Index 209, the first year of a new generation), 2008 (worse than average, Lemon Index 186), 2005 (worse than average, Lemon Index 179). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Sorento have?

76 safety recalls across the 22 model years we track. Most: 2011 (8), 2016 (8), 2014 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Kia Sorento recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Kia Sorento problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Kia Sorento repairs cost?

The repairs Kia Sorento owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $7,500, 98 reports), airbag module and clock spring replacement (middle bill $700, 10 reports), fuel pump replacement (middle bill $1,200, 7 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 22 Kia Sorento years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Kia Sorento, 2003 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
