Recalls 8

  • trailer hitches 2022

    Debris and moisture accumulation on the tow hitch harness module printed circuit board (PCB) may cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2022

    The vehicles may have been manufactured with faulty automatic transmission shift lever assembly components, preventing the shift lever from locking, which can allow the vehicle to rollaway.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2018

    The high pressure fuel pipe that connects to the fuel pump outlet may have been damaged, misaligned or improperly torqued during the engine replacement procedure, possibly causing a fuel leak.

  • trailer hitches 2016

    The affected vehicles are equipped with an accessory trailer hitch wiring harness that, due to a malfunction of the tow hitch module, may result in the trailer brake lights being constantly illuminated.

  • seats 2016

    The driver's seatback frame may have been produced with inadequate weld joints and, as a result, the seatback may not function as designed in the event of a rear-end collision.

  • suspension 2016

    A component of the rear suspension may not be properly welded.

  • seat belts 2015

    The affected vehicles have a front passenger seat belt whose buckle latch assembly may prevent the front passenger from fastening the seat belt.

  • speed control 2015

    Due to an improperly soldered component in the accelerator pedal assembly, the driver may not be able to accelerate the vehicle.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Kia Sorento reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 173; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Sorento year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2016 Kia Sorento problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), speed control (13%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Kia Sorento have?

8 safety recalls (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Kia Sorento get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Kia Sorento safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Kia Sorento problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 31,000 and 95,000 miles. The middle report was at 66,000 miles, from 424 complaints.

Does the 2016 Kia Sorento have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Kia Sorento cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Kia Sorento have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl.

What Kia Sorento year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2020 and 2012.
