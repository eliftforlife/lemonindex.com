Quick answers

Is the 2018 Kia Sorento reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2018 Kia Sorento problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (23%), steering (18%) and lights (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Kia Sorento have?

3 safety recalls (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Kia Sorento get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
