Quick answers

Is the 2023 Toyota Corolla reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 3.8× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 26; 100 is average). The best Toyota Corolla year by this measure since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2023 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (22%), electrical (20%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Toyota Corolla have?

3 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Toyota Corolla get?

30 to 50 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
