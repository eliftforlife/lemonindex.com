Quick answers

Is the 2018 Toyota Corolla reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 31; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Corolla year by this measure since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2018 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (31%), fuel or ev drive system (18%) and fuel system, gasoline (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Toyota Corolla have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Toyota Corolla get?

30 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
