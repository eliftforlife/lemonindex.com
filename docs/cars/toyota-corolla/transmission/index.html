Quick answers

Which Toyota Corolla years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Toyota Corolla years are 2003, 2019, 2010.

What are common Toyota Corolla transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, transmission replaced, lost power while driving.

Which Toyota Corolla years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Toyota Corolla years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2013, 2023.
