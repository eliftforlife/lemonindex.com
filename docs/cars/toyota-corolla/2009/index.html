2009 Toyota Corolla: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Toyota Corolla drew 2.8× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 674 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 296,874 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Corolla complaints that listed a mileage came before 23,000 miles. Most came between 5,300 miles and 61,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 29,000 miles and 116,500 miles.
