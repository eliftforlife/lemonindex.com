Quick answers

Is the 2015 Toyota Corolla reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Corolla year by this measure since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (21%), speed control (13%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Toyota Corolla have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Toyota Corolla get?

30 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Toyota Corolla safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Toyota Corolla problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,200 and 58,000 miles. The middle report was at 30,000 miles, from 133 complaints.

Does the 2015 Toyota Corolla have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Toyota Corolla cost in fuel a year?

About $1900 to $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Toyota Corolla have?

1.8L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Corolla year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2013 and 2022.
