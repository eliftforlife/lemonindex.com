2010 Toyota Corolla Problems, recalls and years to avoid

All 26 yearsCompare

Is the 2010 Toyota Corolla reliable? No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 199; 100 is average, lower is better). 2010 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about half as many complaints as the average car. The Toyota Corolla year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2002.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Toyota Corolla reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 199; 100 is average, lower is better). 2010 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about half as many complaints as the average car. The Toyota Corolla year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2002.

What are the most common 2010 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (50%), speed control (24%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Toyota Corolla have?

17 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Toyota Corolla get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Toyota Corolla problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 600 and 50,000 miles (965–80,000 km) (the middle half of the 767 complaints, of all 1,244 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Toyota Corolla have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Toyota Corolla cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $2,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Toyota Corolla have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Corolla year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2012 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2002 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V741000 (airbags), 19V005000 (airbags) and 18V024000 (airbags) done? It has 17 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (13 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,200.)
  7. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (11 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $2,000.)
