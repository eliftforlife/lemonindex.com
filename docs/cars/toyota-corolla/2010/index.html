Quick answers

Is the 2010 Toyota Corolla reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 199; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Corolla year by this measure since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2010 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (50%), speed control (24%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Toyota Corolla have?

17 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Toyota Corolla get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
