Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Corolla reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 2.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2002. Avoid 2009 and 2010.

Is the Toyota Corolla a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2002 and the worst is 2009.

What are the best Toyota Corolla years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2002, 2000, 2012.

What Toyota Corolla years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2009, 2010.

How many recalls does the Toyota Corolla have?

145 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2010 (17), 2003 (13), 2004 (13). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Corolla problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, speed control and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
