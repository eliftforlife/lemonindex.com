Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Corolla reliable?

Yes. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2010.

Is the Toyota Corolla a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Toyota Corolla years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2013, 2022. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Corolla years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010 (worse than average, Lemon Index 199), 2020 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 49, the first year of a new generation), 2021 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 42). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Corolla have?

145 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2010 (17), 2003 (13), 2004 (13). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Corolla problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, speed control and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
