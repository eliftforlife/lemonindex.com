How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Toyota Corolla reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about a third as many complaints as the average car per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 33; 100 is average). The Toyota Corolla year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2002.

What are the most common 2024 Toyota Corolla problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (24%), steering (18%) and brakes (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Toyota Corolla have?

2 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Toyota Corolla get?

33 to 50 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Toyota Corolla safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2024 Toyota Corolla have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 Toyota Corolla cost in fuel a year?

About $1,250 to $1,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Toyota Corolla have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Corolla year is better than the 2024?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2002 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V878000 (steering) and 24V548000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Any overheating, coolant smell or leaks? Ask whether the water pump or radiator was replaced. (21 owners named a cooling repair bill; the middle one was $800.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (5 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $800.)
  7. Toyota sent dealers 78 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “To prevent brake rotor rust from forming during transportation and storage, wheel film will be…”?

How the YouTube ranks work
