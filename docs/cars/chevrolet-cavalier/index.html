Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Cavalier reliable?

Mostly. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2004.

Is the Chevrolet Cavalier a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2004: Lemon Index 63; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2004 drew the fewest complaints and 2003 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Cavalier years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004, 2002, 2001. 2004 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Cavalier years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Cavalier year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2003 (better than average, Lemon Index 80), 2000 (better than average, Lemon Index 66).

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Cavalier have?

23 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2000 (7), 2001 (6), 2002 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Cavalier recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Cavalier problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Chevrolet Cavalier repairs cost?

The repairs Chevrolet Cavalier owners reported paying for most: fuel pump replacement (middle bill $700, 73 reports), brake repair (middle bill $348, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Chevrolet Cavalier years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 198 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Cavalier, 1996 to 2005, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 1998. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
