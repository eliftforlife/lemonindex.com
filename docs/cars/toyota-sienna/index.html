Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Sienna reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.8× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2012. Avoid 2000, 2004 and 2005.

Is the Toyota Sienna a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 57; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2004.

What are the best Toyota Sienna years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2013, 2012.

What Toyota Sienna years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2004, 2005, 2000.

How many recalls does the Toyota Sienna have?

147 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2011 (14), 2005 (12), 2006 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Sienna problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name body structure, tires and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
