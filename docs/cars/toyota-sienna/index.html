Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Sienna reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020. Most complaints: 2011.

Is the Toyota Sienna a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 57; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best Toyota Sienna years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020, 2013, 2012. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Sienna years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (worse than average, Lemon Index 132, the first year of a new generation), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 99), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 60). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Sienna have?

147 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2011 (14), 2005 (12), 2006 (11). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Sienna recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Sienna problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name body structure, tires and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 23 Toyota Sienna years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Toyota Sienna, 1998 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
