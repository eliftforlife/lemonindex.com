Quick answers

Is the 2019 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2019 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (19%), fuel system, gasoline (19%) and body structure (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Toyota Sienna have?

7 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Toyota Sienna get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
