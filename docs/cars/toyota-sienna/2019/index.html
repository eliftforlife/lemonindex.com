How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Sienna year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2020.

What are the most common 2019 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system (19%), fuel system, gasoline (19%) and body structure (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Toyota Sienna have?

7 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Toyota Sienna get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Toyota Sienna safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2019 Toyota Sienna have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Toyota Sienna cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Toyota Sienna have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Sienna year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V682000 (fuel system, gasoline), 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 19V532000 (steering) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Does 4WD engage cleanly, with no clunks or grinding? Ask for transfer-case and differential fluid records. (8 owners named a 4x4 & axles repair bill; the middle one was $2,600.)
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (7 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $6,500.)
  6. Toyota sent dealers 191 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “All grades of 2018-2019 model year Mirai and Sienna and 2019 model year CH-R vehicles are not…”?
