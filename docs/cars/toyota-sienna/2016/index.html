Quick answers

Is the 2016 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2016 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (37%), electrical (14%) and airbags (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Toyota Sienna have?

5 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Toyota Sienna get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
