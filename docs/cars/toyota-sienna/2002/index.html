Quick answers

Is the 2002 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2002 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (19%), brakes (19%) and tires (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Toyota Sienna have?

1 safety recall (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Toyota Sienna get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Toyota Sienna problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 98,000 miles. The middle report was at 66,800 miles, from 118 complaints.

Does the 2002 Toyota Sienna have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2002 Toyota Sienna cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Toyota Sienna have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Sienna year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2013 and 2012.
