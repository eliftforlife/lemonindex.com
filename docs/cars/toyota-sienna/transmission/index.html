Toyota Sienna transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2000 (67 complaints), 2004 (72 complaints), 2017 (33 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2016, 2012, 2014.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2014 2014

    The affected vehicles have a transmission shift control cable whose protective cable sleeve may have been damaged during assembly.

  • 2011 2011

    TOYOTA IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2011 VENZA AND SIENNA PASSENGER CARS MANUFACTURED FROM MARCH 7, THROUGH MARCH 10, 2011.

  • 2010 2017

    In the affected vehicles, due to a problem within the shift lock assembly, the shift lever may be moved out of the 'Park' position without depressing the brake pedal.

  • 2009 2013

    In the affected vehicles, due to a problem within the shift lock solenoid, there is a possibility that the shift lever could be moved out of Park position without depressing the brake pedal.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Toyota Sienna years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Toyota Sienna years are 2000, 2004, 2017.

What are common Toyota Sienna transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, rolled away or park trouble, transmission replaced.

Which Toyota Sienna years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Toyota Sienna years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2016, 2012, 2014.
