2001 Toyota Sienna: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Toyota Sienna drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 76 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 88,469 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Sienna complaints that listed a mileage came before 66,300 miles. Most came between 34,200 miles and 110,000 miles.
