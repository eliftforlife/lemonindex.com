Quick answers

Is the 2004 Toyota Sienna reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 390; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2004 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (29%), tires (19%) and doors, locks & latches (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Toyota Sienna have?

10 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Toyota Sienna get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
