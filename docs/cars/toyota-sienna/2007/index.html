Quick answers

Is the 2007 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 68; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2007 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (21%), body structure (18%) and tires (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Toyota Sienna have?

10 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Toyota Sienna get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Toyota Sienna problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 36,000 and 106,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,000 miles, from 295 complaints.

Does the 2007 Toyota Sienna have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Toyota Sienna cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Toyota Sienna have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Sienna year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2013 and 2012.
