Quick answers

Is the 2000 Toyota Sienna reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 171; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2000 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (29%), engine (23%) and tires (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Toyota Sienna have?

1 safety recall (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Toyota Sienna get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
