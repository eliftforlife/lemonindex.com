Quick answers

Is the 2010 Toyota Sienna reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 50; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Sienna year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2010 Toyota Sienna problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (28%), speed control (20%) and doors, locks & latches (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Toyota Sienna have?

8 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Toyota Sienna get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
