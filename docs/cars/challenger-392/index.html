Known issues

  • Lifter and camshaft failure ("HEMI tick")Owner-reported

    Owners report a tick from failed roller lifters that then wear the camshaft. SlashGear ties most cases to the cylinder-deactivation lifters, used on automatics only.

    Fix: The repair is new lifters and a camshaft; catch it before metal debris spreads.

    13 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2011–2014)
  • No engine recallRecall

    NHTSA lists no recall for the 6.4 engine on 2011–2023 Challengers. The 2015 and 2017 engine recalls cover the supercharged Hellcat only.

Quick answers

How fast is the Dodge R/T Scat Pack?

The LC (2015–2023) R/T Scat Pack makes 485 hp and 475 lb-ft. Car and Driver (2015 automatic) measured 0–60 mph in 4.2 seconds.

What engine does the Dodge Challenger SRT8 / Scat Pack 392 have?

The LC (2015–2023) Challenger Scat Pack (2015–2023) uses the 392 HEMI 6.4, a 6.4-liter V-8. Older generations: LC (2008–2010): 6.1 SRT8 HEMI; LC (2011–2014): 392 HEMI 6.4.

Which Dodge Challenger SRT8 / Scat Pack 392 engine do YouTube owners like most?

392 HEMI 6.4 53/100, 6.1 SRT8 HEMI 50/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
