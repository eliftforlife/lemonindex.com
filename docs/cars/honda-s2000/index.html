Known issues

  • Timing chain tensioner rattleOwner-reported

    A rattle at startup usually means the timing chain tensioner needs replacing, per SlashGear and PistonHeads buyer's guides.

    Fix: Replace the timing chain tensioner.

  • Oil useOwner-reported

    Some engines use up to a liter of oil every 1,000 miles while others use almost none. If the level runs low, the computer blocks VTEC.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2003)
  • Early oiling (banjo bolts)Outlet report

    Honda updated the oil banjo bolts in mid-2002 for better engine oiling. Hagerty says later engines are thought to be a bit more durable.

  • Cracked valve retainersOutlet report

    Hagerty says valve retainers can occasionally crack. It is not a widespread pattern.

Quick answers

How fast is the Honda S2000 CR?

The AP2 S2000 CR makes 237 hp and 162 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.7 seconds.

What engine does the Honda S2000 have?

The AP2 S2000 (2004–2009) uses the Honda F22C1, a 2.2-liter inline-4, VTEC, naturally aspirated. Older generations: AP1: Honda F20C.

Which Honda S2000 engine do YouTube owners like most?

Honda F20C 48/100, Honda F22C1 48/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
