What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10006736: “Ecm (engine control module) reprogramming for warm ambient cold start stumble (wacss) and engine hesitation at…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10008731: “Engine control module (ecm) reprogramming to reduce the delay in rpm drop between shifts with the air conditioner on”)
  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11038456: “Certain vehicles equipped with internal combustion engines may exhibit higher-than-expected engine oil consumption”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 09V125000 (suspension), 04V369000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 04V178000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Hyundai sent dealers 70 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “High pitched wind noise related to front door weatherstrip”?

Quick answers

Is the 2003 Hyundai Elantra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Elantra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2003 Hyundai Elantra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (28%), brakes (20%) and body structure (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Hyundai Elantra have?

5 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Hyundai Elantra get?

25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Hyundai Elantra problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 32,000 and 95,000 miles (52,000–153,000 km) (the middle half of the 168 complaints, of all 234 filed so far, that listed a mileage). Does the 2003 Hyundai Elantra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2003 Hyundai Elantra cost in fuel a year?

What Hyundai Elantra year is better than the 2003?
