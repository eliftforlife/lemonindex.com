Quick answers

Is the 2025 Hyundai Elantra reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.6× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 38; 100 is average). The best Hyundai Elantra year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2025 Hyundai Elantra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lane assist (29%), engine (15%) and automatic emergency braking (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Hyundai Elantra have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Hyundai Elantra get?

23 to 54 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Hyundai Elantra safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Hyundai Elantra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Hyundai Elantra cost in fuel a year?

About $1200 to $3450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Hyundai Elantra have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Elantra year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2014 and 2016.
