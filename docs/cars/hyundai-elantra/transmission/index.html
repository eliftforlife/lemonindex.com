A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Hyundai Elantra years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Hyundai Elantra years are 2020, 2008, 2010.

What are common Hyundai Elantra transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Hyundai Elantra years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Hyundai Elantra years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2021, 2014.
