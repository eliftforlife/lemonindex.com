Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Elantra reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2015. Most complaints: 2004, 2008 and 2013.

Is the Hyundai Elantra a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 74; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2015 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Elantra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015, 2014, 2016. 2015 drew the fewest.

What Hyundai Elantra years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 141), 2008 (worse than average, Lemon Index 124), 2004 (worse than average, Lemon Index 120). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Elantra have?

86 safety recalls across the 24 model years we track. Most: 2008 (7), 2002 (6), 2007 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Hyundai Elantra recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Hyundai Elantra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Hyundai Elantra repairs cost?

The repairs Hyundai Elantra owners reported paying for most: steering column repair (middle bill $2,200, 31 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $7,000, 30 reports), airbag module and clock spring replacement (middle bill $594, 10 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 25 Hyundai Elantra years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 991 YouTube videos about the Hyundai Elantra, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
