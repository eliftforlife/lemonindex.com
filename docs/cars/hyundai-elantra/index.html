Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Elantra reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.4× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2016. Avoid 2004, 2008 and 2013.

Is the Hyundai Elantra a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 74; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2015 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Hyundai Elantra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2014, 2016.

What Hyundai Elantra years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2008, 2004.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Elantra have?

86 safety recalls across the 24 model years we track. Most: 2008 (7), 2002 (6), 2007 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Elantra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
