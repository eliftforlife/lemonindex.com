What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10127002: “This bulletin provides instructions related to an Electronic Control Module (ECM) software update to revise the fuel…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10082455: “The pcm or tcm contains logic to adjust solenoid duty and line pressure as needed to compensate for normal clutch wear…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Hyundai Elantra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Elantra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2016 Hyundai Elantra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (23%), airbags (15%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Hyundai Elantra have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V307000 (airbags) and 24V146000 (latches/locks/linkages) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into no restart after auto start/stop engages. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 134 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Before installing an automatic transmission, check the depth of the torque converter in the…”?
