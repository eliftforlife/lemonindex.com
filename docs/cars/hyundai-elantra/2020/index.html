Quick answers

Is the 2020 Hyundai Elantra reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 119; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Elantra year by this measure is 2015.

What are the most common 2020 Hyundai Elantra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (37%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and steering (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Hyundai Elantra have?

3 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Hyundai Elantra get?

26 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
