Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V061000 (brakes), 15V100000 (steering) and 14V310000 (body structure) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Hyundai sent dealers 49 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Slight rattle noise from the steering while at low speeds driving over rough roads or turning…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10033665: “Hyundai: tcm calibration update (service campaign 795)”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10041637: “Hyundai: information provided for ecm upgrade software”)
  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11038456: “Certain vehicles equipped with internal combustion engines may exhibit higher-than-expected engine oil consumption”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Problems Hyundai told dealers about

Hyundai sent dealers 49 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Slight rattle noise from the steering while at low speeds driving over rough roads or turning…
  • The MDPS (motor driven power steering) assembly contains a flexible rubber coupling which may…
  • Electronic power steering (eps) warning indicator lamp will illuminate when sensing discrepancy

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Recalls 4

  • Brakes · recall issued 2020

    On February 21, 2020 Hyundai expanded the recall to include 46,425 model year 2007-2008 Entourage and 2007 Santa Fe vehicles.

  • Steering · recall issued 2015

    The electronic power steering (EPS) electronic control unit (ECU) may sense a discrepancy in the steering input signals and, as a result, disable the steering power assist.

  • Body structure · recall issued 2014

    The affected vehicles have a support bracket attached to the headliner that may become displaced during a side curtain airbag deployment.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Hyundai Elantra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 58; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Elantra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2010 Hyundai Elantra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (24%), airbags (19%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Hyundai Elantra have?

4 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Hyundai Elantra get?

26 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Hyundai Elantra problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 33,000 and 98,000 miles (53,000–157,000 km) (the middle half of the 325 complaints, of all 455 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Hyundai Elantra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Hyundai Elantra cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Hyundai Elantra have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Elantra year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2009 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2015 drew the fewest.
