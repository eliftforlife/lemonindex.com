How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Honda Fit reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Fit year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2012 Honda Fit problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (23%), speed control (19%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Honda Fit have?

11 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Honda Fit get?

29 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Honda Fit safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2012 Honda Fit problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 15,000 and 79,000 miles (24,000–127,000 km) (the middle half of the 71 complaints, of all 113 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Honda Fit have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Honda Fit cost in fuel a year?

About $2,050 to $2,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Honda Fit have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Honda Fit year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V770000 (transmission & drivetrain), 19V500000 (airbags) and 19V502000 (airbags) done? It has 11 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Does 4WD engage cleanly, with no clunks or grinding? Ask for transfer-case and differential fluid records. (5 owners named a 4x4 & axles repair bill; the middle one was $740.)
  6. Honda sent dealers 44 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some model vehicles, there is an alternator/starter tester (ast) module capable of testing”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 518 YouTube videos about the Honda Fit, 2007 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2018. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
