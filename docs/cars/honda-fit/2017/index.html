Quick answers

Is the 2017 Honda Fit reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 18; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2017 Honda Fit problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (30%), electrical (20%) and wheels (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Honda Fit have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Honda Fit get?

32 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
