2009 Honda Fit: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Honda Fit drew 1.1× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 60 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 67,315 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Fit complaints that listed a mileage came before 60,200 miles. Most came between 20,100 miles and 103,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 51,200 miles and 88,400 miles.
