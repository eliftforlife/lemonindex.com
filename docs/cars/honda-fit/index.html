Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Honda Fit reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2007, 2013 and 2019.

Is the Honda Fit a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Honda Fit years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2011, 2020. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Honda Fit years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 177), 2007 (worse than average, Lemon Index 153), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 122). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Fit have?

93 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2010 (12), 2007 (11), 2009 (11). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda Fit recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda Fit problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, electrical and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 13 Honda Fit years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 518 YouTube videos about the Honda Fit, 2007 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2018. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
