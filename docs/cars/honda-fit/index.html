Quick answers.

Is the Honda Fit reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.3× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2012. Avoid 2007, 2013 and 2019.

Is the Honda Fit a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Honda Fit years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2011, 2020.

What Honda Fit years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2007, 2019.

How many recalls does the Honda Fit have?

93 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2010 (12), 2007 (11), 2009 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Fit problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, electrical and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
