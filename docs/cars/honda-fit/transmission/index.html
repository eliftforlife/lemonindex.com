Honda Fit transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2015 (30 complaints), 2008 (14 complaints), 2009 (10 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2012, 2018, 2019.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2015 2015

    The software settings that control the transmission operation may result in damage to the transmission drive pulley shaft.

  • 2013 2014

    The passenger side driveshaft may break while driving due to an improper heat treatment application during manufacturing.

  • 2013 2020

    The drive shafts' protective coating may not have been applied properly during m.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Honda Fit years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Fit years are 2015, 2008, 2009.

What are common Honda Fit transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, slipping.

Which Honda Fit years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Fit years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2012, 2018, 2019.
