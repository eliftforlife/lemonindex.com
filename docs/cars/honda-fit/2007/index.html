Quick answers

Is the 2007 Honda Fit reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 153; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Fit year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2007 Honda Fit problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (30%), steering (25%) and body structure (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Honda Fit have?

11 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Honda Fit get?

29 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Honda Fit problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 35,000 and 89,300 miles. The middle report was at 60,700 miles, from 281 complaints.

Does the 2007 Honda Fit have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Honda Fit cost in fuel a year?

About $2050 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Honda Fit have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Honda Fit year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2011 and 2020.
