Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags), 24V384000 (back over prevention) and 23V858000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  4. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  5. Honda sent dealers 48 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Dealer message - American Honda (AHM) is investigating certain 2018-2019 Fits with a customer…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Honda Fit reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 74; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Fit year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2018 Honda Fit problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (23%), fuel or ev drive system (20%) and body structure (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Honda Fit have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Honda Fit get?

31 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Honda Fit safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Honda Fit problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 13,000 and 60,000 miles (the middle half of the 31 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2018 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Honda Fit have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Honda Fit cost in fuel a year?

About $1800 to $2050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Honda Fit have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Honda Fit year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (5.6× fewer complaints than average).
