Recalls 4

  • body structure 2015

    In the affected vehicles, the rear grab handle brackets were not manufactured properly, and as a result, the brackets may puncture the side curtain air bags upon their deployment.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2015

    The software settings that control the transmission operation may result in damage to the transmission drive pulley shaft.

  • electrical 2015

    The affected vehicles may experience damage to the wires inside the Plug Top Ignition Coils (PTC) due to improper protection against electrical noise.
