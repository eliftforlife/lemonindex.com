Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The front axle pinion may not have been heat-treated, which can cause it to fracture.

  • 2021 2026

    The transmission park pawl may engage while the vehicle is in motion, resulting in park system damage.

  • 2020 2020

    The transmission shift cable lock clip may not be fully seated, allowing the transmission to actually be in a different gear than the gear shift position selected by the driver.

  • 2019 2020

    The "Transmission Not In Park" warning message and associated chime duration are shorter than intended.

  • 2018 2018

    A roll pin may not have been installed in the transmission, potentially causing a loss of the "Park" function.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Expedition years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Expedition years are 2020, 2018, 2019.

What are common Ford Expedition transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Ford Expedition years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Expedition years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2023, 2016.
