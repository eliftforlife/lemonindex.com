Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Expedition reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 51; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (38%), seats (33%) and engine (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Expedition have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Expedition get?

13 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
