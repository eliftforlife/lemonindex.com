2003 Ford Expedition Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 26 yearsCompare

Is the 2003 Ford Expedition reliable? No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 172; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Expedition year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2003 Ford Expedition reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 172; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Expedition year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2003 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (21%), tires (12%) and visibility (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Ford Expedition have?

6 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Ford Expedition get?

14 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Ford Expedition problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 33,000 and 120,000 miles (53,000–193,000 km) (the middle half of the 463 complaints, of all 615 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2003 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2003 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2003 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $4,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 Ford Expedition have?

4.6L 8-cyl, 5.4L 8-cyl.

What Ford Expedition year is better than the 2003?

Nearby, the 2005 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 09E012000 (lights), 06E064000 (lights) and 06E026000 (lights) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (28 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $1,800.)
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (12 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $500.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 152 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Power steering noise / vibration from the area of the power steering pump”?
