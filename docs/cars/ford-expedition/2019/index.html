How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Ford Expedition reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 68; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Expedition year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2019 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (30%), engine (22%) and suspension (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Ford Expedition have?

11 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Ford Expedition get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Ford Expedition safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2019 Ford Expedition problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 52,000 and 95,000 miles (84,000–153,000 km) (the middle half of the 191 complaints, of all 517 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2019 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 30% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $3,200 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Ford Expedition have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Ford Expedition year than the 2019?

Not nearby: the 2019 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ford Expedition years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V402000 (transmission & drivetrain), 26V368000 (body structure) and 26V344000 (seat belts) done? It has 11 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  5. Ford sent dealers 150 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some 2017-2019 F-150, 2018-2019 Expedition/Navigator/Mustang, 2019 Ranger vehicles equipped…”?
