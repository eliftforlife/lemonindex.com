2001 Ford Expedition: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Ford Expedition drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 187 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 178,045 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Expedition complaints that listed a mileage came before 90,500 miles. Most came between 59,000 miles and 127,600 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 75,000 miles and 130,000 miles.
