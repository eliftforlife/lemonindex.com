Quick answers

Is the 2001 Ford Expedition reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 93; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2001 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (25%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and speed control (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Ford Expedition have?

10 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Ford Expedition get?

13 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Ford Expedition problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 59,000 and 127,600 miles. The middle report was at 90,500 miles, from 374 complaints.

Does the 2001 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Ford Expedition have?

4.6L 8-cyl, 5.4L 8-cyl.

What Ford Expedition year is better than the 2001?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2012 and 2013.
