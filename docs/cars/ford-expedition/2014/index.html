How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Ford Expedition reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a quarter as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 23; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (45%), electrical (18%) and seats (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Ford Expedition have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Ford Expedition get?

15 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Ford Expedition safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Ford Expedition problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 53,000 and 140,000 miles (85,000–225,000 km) (the middle half of the 42 complaints, of all 72 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2014 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $4,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Ford Expedition have?

5.4L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Ford Expedition year than the 2014?

Not nearby: the 2014 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ford Expedition years within 3 model years of it.

  1. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
