Quick answers

Is the 2022 Ford Expedition reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 104; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2022 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (18%), automatic emergency braking (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Ford Expedition have?

12 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Ford Expedition get?

17 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
