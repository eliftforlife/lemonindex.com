Quick answers

Is the 2009 Ford Expedition reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 9 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seats (33%), electrical (33%) and lights (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Ford Expedition have?

1 safety recall (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
