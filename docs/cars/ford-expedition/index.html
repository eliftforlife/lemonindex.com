Quick answers.

Is the Ford Expedition reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2011. Avoid 2003, 2020 and 2021.

Is the Ford Expedition a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 98; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Ford Expedition years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2012, 2013.

What Ford Expedition years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003, 2021, 2020.

How many recalls does the Ford Expedition have?

154 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2020 (18), 2018 (14), 2021 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Expedition problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, tires and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
