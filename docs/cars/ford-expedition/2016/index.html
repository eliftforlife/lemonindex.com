Quick answers

Is the 2016 Ford Expedition reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 100; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (32%), engine (23%) and speed control (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Ford Expedition have?

3 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Ford Expedition get?

16 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Ford Expedition safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Ford Expedition problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 49,200 and 100,200 miles. The middle report was at 66,800 miles, from 128 complaints.

Does the 2016 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Ford Expedition have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Expedition year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2012 and 2013.
