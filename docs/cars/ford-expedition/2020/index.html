2020 Ford Expedition Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 26 yearsCompare

Is the 2020 Ford Expedition reliable? No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 154; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Expedition year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10180531: “Some 2017-2020 F-150 2018-2020 Expedition/Navigator vehicles built on or before 31-Oct-2019 and equipped with a 3.5L…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10201905: “Some 2017-2020 F-150, 2018-2021 Expedition/Navigator/Mustang and 2019-2021 Ranger vehicles equipped with a 10R80…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Ford Expedition reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 154; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Expedition year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2020 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (56%), engine (15%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Ford Expedition have?

18 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Ford Expedition get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Ford Expedition safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2020 Ford Expedition problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 40,000 and 88,000 miles (64,000–142,000 km) (the middle half of the 100 complaints, of all 425 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2020 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 56% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Ford Expedition have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Expedition year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2019 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V402000 (transmission & drivetrain), 26V368000 (body structure) and 26V344000 (seat belts) done? It has 18 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into rearview camera fmvss 111 noncompliance. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Ford sent dealers 177 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some 2020 F150/Nautilus/Expedition/Transit/Edge/Escape/Mustang vehicles built on or before…”?
