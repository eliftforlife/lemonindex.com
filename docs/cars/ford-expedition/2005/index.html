Quick answers

Is the 2005 Ford Expedition reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 58; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2005 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (25%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Ford Expedition have?

5 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Ford Expedition get?

14 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
