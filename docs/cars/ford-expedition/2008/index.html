Quick answers

Is the 2008 Ford Expedition reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Expedition year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2008 Ford Expedition problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (20%), body structure (16%) and speed control (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Ford Expedition have?

4 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Ford Expedition get?

14 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Ford Expedition problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,500 and 96,500 miles. The middle report was at 62,000 miles, from 117 complaints.

Does the 2008 Ford Expedition have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2008 Ford Expedition cost in fuel a year?

About $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Ford Expedition have?

5.4L 8-cyl.

What Ford Expedition year is better than the 2008?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2012 and 2013.
