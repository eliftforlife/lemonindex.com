Quick answers.

Is the Cadillac XT5 reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2021.

Is the Cadillac XT5 a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Complaint Score 38; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Cadillac XT5 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2022, 2019. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Cadillac XT5 years should you stay away from?

No Cadillac XT5 year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2017 (better than average, Complaint Score 51), 2018 (one of the most reliable, Complaint Score 35), 2020 (one of the most reliable, Complaint Score 25).

How many recalls does the Cadillac XT5 have?

19 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2020 (5), 2023 (4), 2022 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Cadillac XT5 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Cadillac XT5 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Cadillac XT5 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Cadillac XT5, 2017 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2018. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
