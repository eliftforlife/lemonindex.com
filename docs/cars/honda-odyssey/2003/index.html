Quick answers

Is the 2003 Honda Odyssey reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 97; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Odyssey year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2003 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (35%), engine (11%) and speed control (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Honda Odyssey have?

15 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Honda Odyssey get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
