Honda Odyssey transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2002 (598 complaints), 2003 (519 complaints), 2001 (329 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2013, 2023, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Honda Odyssey years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Odyssey years are 2002, 2003, 2001.

What are common Honda Odyssey transmission problems?

Owners most often describe transmission replaced, slipping, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Honda Odyssey years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Odyssey years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2013, 2023, 2017.
