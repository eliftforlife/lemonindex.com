  • fuel system, gasoline 2023

    The fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

  • engine 2023

    Due to a manufacturing error, the connecting rod bearing in the engine may wear and seize, damaging the engine.

  • back over prevention 2023

    Due to a faulty Media Oriented Systems Transport (MOST) communication coaxial cable connector, the rearview camera image may not appear on the display.

  • seat belts 2023

    A manufacturing issue with the front seat belts may cause the seat belt buckle channel to interfere with the release button, preventing the seat belt buckle from latching.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2021

    The fuel tank assembly installed by Braun may be susceptible to the fuel tank overfilling into the charcoal canister.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2020

    Water may enter the outer door handle cables for the sliding doors.

  • back over prevention 2020

    Incorrect instrument panel control module software can cause the instrument panel to not display critical information, such as engine oil pressure, speedometer, and gear selector position until the next ignition cycle.

  • electrical 2020

    The wire harness for the third row seat accessory power outlet may get pinched between the unibody and rear trim panel, possibly damaging the wires and causing an electrical short.

  • transmission & drivetrain 2019

    Loose battery terminal connections or a degraded battery may cause the Transmission Control Unit (TCU) to unexpectedly reboot.

  • service brakes, air 2019

    The rear wheel speed sensor harnesses may have been routed to the incorrect rear wheels, resulting in the possibility that the anti-lock brake system will not recognize each rear wheel speed properly.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2018

    The latch assemblies for the power sliding doors may stick and not properly latch to the door strikers.

  • brakes 2018

    The rear brake caliper pistons may have an insufficient coating, causing gas pockets to form, reducing brake performance.

  • seat belts 2017

    The seat belt tongue for the third row center seating position may not be compatible with the latch, preventing the seat belt from latching securely.

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Honda Odyssey reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 213; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Odyssey year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2018 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (32%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and body structure (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Honda Odyssey have?

16 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Honda Odyssey get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Honda Odyssey safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Honda Odyssey problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 6,000 and 78,000 miles. The middle report was at 24,000 miles, from 271 complaints.

Does the 2018 Honda Odyssey have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 21% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Honda Odyssey cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Honda Odyssey have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Odyssey year is better than the 2018?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2013 and 2010.
