Quick answers

Is the 2005 Honda Odyssey reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 192; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Odyssey year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2005 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (19%), equipment & accessories (14%) and body structure (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Honda Odyssey have?

9 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Honda Odyssey get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Honda Odyssey problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 37,000 and 112,200 miles. The middle report was at 74,200 miles, from 673 complaints.

Does the 2005 Honda Odyssey have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Honda Odyssey cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Honda Odyssey have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Odyssey year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2013 and 2010.
