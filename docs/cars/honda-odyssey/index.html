Quick answers.

Is the Honda Odyssey a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2018.

What are the best Honda Odyssey years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2013, 2010.

What Honda Odyssey years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2018, 2000, 2007.
