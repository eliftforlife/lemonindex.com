Quick answers.

Is the Honda Odyssey reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2014, 2018 and 2019.

Is the Honda Odyssey a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Honda Odyssey years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2013, 2010. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Honda Odyssey years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 213, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 192), 2022 (worse than average, Lemon Index 157). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Odyssey have?

186 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2019 (20), 2018 (16), 2003 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Odyssey problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
