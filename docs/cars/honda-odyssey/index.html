Quick answers.

Is the Honda Odyssey reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2000, 2007 and 2018.

Is the Honda Odyssey a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Honda Odyssey years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2013, 2010. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Honda Odyssey years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (avoid, Complaint Score 213, the first year of a new generation), 2000 (avoid, Complaint Score 200), 2007 (worse than average, Complaint Score 199). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Honda Odyssey have?

186 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2019 (20), 2018 (16), 2003 (15). Each year's page lists them, and every Honda Odyssey recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Honda Odyssey problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Honda Odyssey repairs cost?

The repairs Honda Odyssey owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $4,000, 152 reports), engine mount replacement (middle bill $925, 57 reports), AC condenser replacement (middle bill $800, 49 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 27 Honda Odyssey years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 789 YouTube videos about the Honda Odyssey, 1999 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
