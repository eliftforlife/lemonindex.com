2000 Honda Odyssey: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Honda Odyssey drew 2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 280 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 126,705 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Odyssey complaints that listed a mileage came before 97,600 miles. Most came between 62,800 miles and 134,200 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 75,500 miles and 140,000 miles.
