Quick answers

Is the 2022 Honda Odyssey reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 157; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Odyssey year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2022 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (32%), electrical (26%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Honda Odyssey have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Honda Odyssey get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
