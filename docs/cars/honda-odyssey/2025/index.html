Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags) and 25V877000 (equipment adaptive/mobility) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into no restart after auto start/stop engages. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Honda Odyssey reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about 8 times the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 799; 100 is average). The Honda Odyssey year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2025 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (55%), electrical (52%) and transmission & drivetrain (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Honda Odyssey have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Honda Odyssey get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Honda Odyssey safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2025 Honda Odyssey problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 10,000 and 22,000 miles (16,000–36,000 km) (the middle half of the 26 complaints, of all 428 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2025 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2025 Honda Odyssey have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Honda Odyssey cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Honda Odyssey have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Odyssey year is better than the 2025?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).
