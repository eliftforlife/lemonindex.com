Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 17V725000 (seats), 16V933000 (seats) and 13V016000 (airbags) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (31 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $4,000.)
  6. Honda sent dealers 124 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some model vehicles, there is an alternator/starter tester (ast) module capable of testing”?

Problems Honda told dealers about

Honda sent dealers 124 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • On some model vehicles, there is an alternator/starter tester (ast) module capable of testing
  • After shifting into 2nd, 3rd, or 4th gear, vehicle would surge or judder or would have hesitation
  • See document search button for owner letter

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

    One or more rivets that attach the airbag module to the airbag cover may be missing.

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Honda Odyssey reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 94; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Odyssey year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2011 Honda Odyssey problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (36%), brakes (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Honda Odyssey have?

5 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Honda Odyssey get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Honda Odyssey safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2011 Honda Odyssey problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,500 and 58,000 miles (2,400–93,000 km) (the middle half of the 181 complaints, of all 235 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2011 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2011 Honda Odyssey have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Honda Odyssey cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Honda Odyssey have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Odyssey year is better than the 2011?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.
