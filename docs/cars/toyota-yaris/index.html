Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Yaris reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 2.6× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2012.

Is the Toyota Yaris a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2013: Lemon Index 39; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2009.

What are the best Toyota Yaris years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2013, 2010.

What Toyota Yaris years should you stay away from?

No Toyota Yaris year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Toyota Yaris have?

56 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2007 (10), 2008 (10), 2009 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Yaris problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, speed control and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
