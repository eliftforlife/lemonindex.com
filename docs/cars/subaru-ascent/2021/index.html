Quick answers

Is the 2021 Subaru Ascent reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 127; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Ascent year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2021 Subaru Ascent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lane assist (19%), electrical (18%) and wipers & visibility (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Subaru Ascent have?

2 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Subaru Ascent get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Subaru Ascent safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Subaru Ascent problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,500 and 9,000 miles. The middle report was at 3,200 miles, from 20 complaints.

Does the 2021 Subaru Ascent have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Subaru Ascent cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Subaru Ascent have?

2.4L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Ascent year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2021 and 2019.
