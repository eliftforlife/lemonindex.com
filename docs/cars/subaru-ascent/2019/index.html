Quick answers

Is the 2019 Subaru Ascent reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 299; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Ascent year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Subaru Ascent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), transmission & drivetrain (17%) and wipers & visibility (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Subaru Ascent have?

9 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Subaru Ascent get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
