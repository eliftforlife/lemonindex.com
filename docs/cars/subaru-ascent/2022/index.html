Quick answers

Is the 2022 Subaru Ascent reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Subaru Ascent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (24%), brakes (22%) and wipers & visibility (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Subaru Ascent have?

2 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Subaru Ascent get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
