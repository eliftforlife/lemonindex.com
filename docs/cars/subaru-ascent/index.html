Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Ascent reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Best year: 2022. Avoid 2019, 2020 and 2021.

Is the Subaru Ascent a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2019-2022: Lemon Index 233; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Subaru Ascent years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2021, 2019.

What Subaru Ascent years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2021.

How many recalls does the Subaru Ascent have?

24 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2019 (9), 2020 (5), 2023 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Ascent problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, wipers & visibility and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
