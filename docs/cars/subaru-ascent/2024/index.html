How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Subaru Ascent reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 100; 100 is average). 2024 is one of the better years of a model that draws 2.3× the average car's complaints. The Subaru Ascent year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2024 Subaru Ascent problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (46%), wipers & visibility (16%) and automatic emergency braking (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Subaru Ascent have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Subaru Ascent get?

Is the 2024 Subaru Ascent safe?

Does the 2024 Subaru Ascent have transmission problems?

How much does the 2024 Subaru Ascent cost in fuel a year?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 26V436000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (7 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $500.)
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (6 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $9,500.)
  6. Subaru sent dealers 229 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Bulletin provides guidance for diagnosing a leaking front or rear suspension strut”?
