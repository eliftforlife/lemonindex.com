Subaru Ascent transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2020 (43 complaints), 2019 (59 complaints), 2021 (17 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2024, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The center support bolts for the driveshaft may loosen, resulting in the disconnection of the front end of the driveshaft.

  • 2020 2021

    A programming error in the Transmission Control Unit (TCU) may allow the clutch to engage before the drive chain is completely clamped.

  • 2019 2019

    The center support bolts for the driveshaft may loosen, possibly resulting in the front portion of the driveshaft disconnecting.

  • 2019 2019

    The transmission hydraulic sensor may inaccurately measure the hydraulic fluid pressure.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Subaru Ascent years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Subaru Ascent years are 2020, 2019, 2021.

What are common Subaru Ascent transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, transmission replaced, delay or hesitation.

Which Subaru Ascent years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Subaru Ascent years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2024, 2022.
