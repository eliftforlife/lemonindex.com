Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Legacy reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2015, 2018 and 2019.

Is the Subaru Legacy a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 108; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Subaru Legacy years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2014, 2013. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Legacy years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 404, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 186), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 153, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Legacy have?

130 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2010 (18), 2011 (16), 2012 (16). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Legacy problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
