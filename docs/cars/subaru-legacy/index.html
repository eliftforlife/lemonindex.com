Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Legacy a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 108; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Subaru Legacy years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2014, 2013.

What Subaru Legacy years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2015.

How many recalls does the Subaru Legacy have?

130 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2010 (18), 2011 (16), 2012 (16). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Legacy problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
