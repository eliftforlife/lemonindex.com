Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Legacy reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2015, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Subaru Legacy a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 108; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Subaru Legacy years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2014, 2013. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Legacy years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 404, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 186), 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 153, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Legacy have?

130 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2010 (18), 2011 (16), 2012 (16). Each year's page lists them, and every Subaru Legacy recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Subaru Legacy problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Subaru Legacy repairs cost?

The repairs Subaru Legacy owners reported paying for most: torque converter replacement (middle bill $2,000, 6 reports), transmission replacement (middle bill $8,200, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 18 Subaru Legacy years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 782 YouTube videos about the Subaru Legacy, 2005 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
