Quick answers

Is the 2015 Subaru Legacy reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 153; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Legacy year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 Subaru Legacy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (30%), electrical (19%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Subaru Legacy have?

3 safety recalls (latest: stability control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Subaru Legacy get?

23 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Subaru Legacy safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Subaru Legacy problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 2,500 and 43,400 miles. The middle report was at 16,100 miles, from 104 complaints.

Does the 2015 Subaru Legacy have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2015 Subaru Legacy cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Subaru Legacy have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Subaru Legacy year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2014 and 2013.
