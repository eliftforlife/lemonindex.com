Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V002000 (airbags), 20V001000 (airbags) and 20V003000 (airbags) done? It has 18 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any shudder, whine or slipping from the CVT? Ask for CVT fluid service records.
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Subaru sent dealers 321 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Steering wheel vibration (shake, shimmy) at highway speeds”?

What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether a transmission special coverage or warranty extension applies to this car. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10117538: “Cvt models only”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10038970: “Subaru service campaign: see document search button for owner letter”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Subaru Legacy reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The Subaru Legacy year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2010 Subaru Legacy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (29%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and steering (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Subaru Legacy have?

18 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Subaru Legacy get?

20 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
