Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V227000 (airbags), 23V755000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 22V485000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  5. Subaru sent dealers 460 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “If you encounter a customer complaint of a white residue build up on the rear door partition…”?

Recalls 3

  • Airbags · recall issued 2024

    A capacitor in the sensors for the Occupant Detection System (ODS) may crack and short circuit, which can prevent the front passenger air bag from deploying in a crash.

  • Transmission & drivetrain · recall issued 2023

    An insufficient weld may allow water to enter the inhibitor switch, causing it to fail.

  • Transmission & drivetrain · recall issued 2022

    A programming error in the Transmission Control Unit (TCU) may allow the clutch to engage before the drive chain is completely clamped.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Subaru Legacy reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 102; 100 is average, lower is better). The Subaru Legacy year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2021 Subaru Legacy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (21%), airbags (21%) and automatic emergency braking (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Subaru Legacy have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Subaru Legacy get?

27 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Subaru Legacy safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Subaru Legacy have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Subaru Legacy cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $2,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Subaru Legacy have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Subaru Legacy year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Subaru Legacy years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.
