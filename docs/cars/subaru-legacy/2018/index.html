Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Recalls 3

  • fuel system, gasoline 2021

    The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

  • visibility 2018

    In the affected vehicles, a software issue may cause the audio display system to not initialize, resulting in the camera display remaining blank.

  • electrical 2018

    Due to a software error, the low fuel warning light may not illuminate at the intended remaining fuel level and the miles-to-empty display may incorrectly indicate a positive number despite the tank being empty.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Subaru Legacy reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 141; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Legacy year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2018 Subaru Legacy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (35%), electrical (24%) and visibility (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Subaru Legacy have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Subaru Legacy get?

23 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Subaru Legacy safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Subaru Legacy problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 6,900 and 35,000 miles. The middle report was at 20,400 miles, from 54 complaints.

Does the 2018 Subaru Legacy have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Subaru Legacy cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Subaru Legacy have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Subaru Legacy year is better than the 2018?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2014 and 2013.
