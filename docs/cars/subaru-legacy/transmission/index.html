Subaru Legacy transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2010 (41 complaints), 2011 (33 complaints), 2012 (27 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2019, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2023

    The center support bolts for the driveshaft may loosen, resulting in the disconnection of the front end of the driveshaft.

  • 2023 2023

    An insufficient weld may allow water to enter the inhibitor switch, causing it to fail.

  • 2021 2022

    A programming error in the Transmission Control Unit (TCU) may allow the clutch to engage before the drive chain is completely clamped.

  • 2016 2015

    Transmission oil may leak from a deformed seal cap on the propeller shaft yoke.

  • 2016 2015

    Due to the improper tightening of the securing nuts, the drive shaft may separate from the rear differential.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Subaru Legacy years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Subaru Legacy years are 2010, 2011, 2012.

What are common Subaru Legacy transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, shudder, delay or hesitation.

Which Subaru Legacy years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Subaru Legacy years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2019, 2022.
