Quick answers

Is the 2007 Subaru Legacy reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 4 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Subaru Legacy problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (75%), airbags (25%) and transmission & drivetrain (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Subaru Legacy have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Subaru Legacy get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Subaru Legacy problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,500 and 93,600 miles. The middle report was at 65,400 miles, from 20 complaints.

Does the 2007 Subaru Legacy have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 25% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Subaru Legacy cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Subaru Legacy have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Legacy year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2014 and 2013.
