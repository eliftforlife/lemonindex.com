Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Buick Regal reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the Buick Regal a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2014: Lemon Index 64; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints and 2011 the most.

What are the best Buick Regal years to buy?

Only 3 Buick Regal years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2012 drew the fewest complaints (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car).

What Buick Regal years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Buick Regal years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Buick Regal have?

28 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2012 (7), 2013 (6), 2011 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Buick Regal recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Buick Regal problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Buick Regal years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 588 YouTube videos about the Buick Regal, 1997 to 2020, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2012. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
