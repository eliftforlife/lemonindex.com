Chevrolet Volt transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (146 complaints), 2016 (37 complaints), 2013 (8 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2012, 2013, 2016.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Volt years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Volt years are 2017, 2016, 2013.

What are common Chevrolet Volt transmission problems?

Owners most often describe rolled away or park trouble, lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Chevrolet Volt years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Volt years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2012, 2013, 2016.
