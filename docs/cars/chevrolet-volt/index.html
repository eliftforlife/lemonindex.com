Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Volt reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2016.

Is the Chevrolet Volt a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 261; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2016 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Volt years to buy?

Only 4 Chevrolet Volt years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2016 drew the fewest complaints (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car).

What Chevrolet Volt years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Chevrolet Volt years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Volt have?

12 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2013 (3), 2012 (2), 2018 (2). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Volt recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Volt problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 4 Chevrolet Volt years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Volt, 2011 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2016. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
