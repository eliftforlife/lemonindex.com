Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Volt reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2016.

Is the Chevrolet Volt a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 261; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2016 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Volt years to buy?

Only 4 Chevrolet Volt years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2016 drew the fewest complaints (1.1× fewer complaints than average).

What Chevrolet Volt years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Chevrolet Volt years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Volt have?

12 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2013 (3), 2012 (2), 2018 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Volt problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
