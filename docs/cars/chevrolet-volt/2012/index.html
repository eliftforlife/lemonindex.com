Recalls 2

  • electrical 2015

    If the driver exits the vehicle without turning off the electrical system, the battery may drain low enough that the gasoline engine will automatically start itself to recharge the electric battery.

  • stability control 2013

    Due to a brake pressure modulator valve problem, the vehicles do not relieve brake pressure from the front brakes during an anti-lock brake system (ABS), electronic stability control (ESC), or traction control event.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Volt reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 177; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Volt year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2012 Chevrolet Volt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (36%), engine (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chevrolet Volt have?

2 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chevrolet Volt get?

37 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Volt safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Chevrolet Volt problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 42,000 and 103,500 miles. The middle report was at 80,000 miles, from 185 complaints.

Does the 2012 Chevrolet Volt have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Chevrolet Volt cost in fuel a year?

About $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Chevrolet Volt have?

1.4L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Volt year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2013 and 2012.
