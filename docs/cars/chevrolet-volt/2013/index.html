When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Volt complaints that listed a mileage came before 67,000 miles. Most came between 38,000 miles and 89,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 58,000 miles and 97,000 miles.

Recalls: 3

  • electrical 2015
    If the driver exits the vehicle without turning off the electrical system, the battery may drain low enough that the gasoline engine will automatically start itself to recharge the electric battery. NHTSA 15V145000
  • hybrid propulsion system 2018
    An update to the Hybrid Powertrain Control Module 2 (HPCM2) may have disabled the cell balancing function causing a low-voltage condition. NHTSA 18V397000
  • airbags 2023
    The driver-side air bag inflator may explode during deployment due to a manufacturing defect. NHTSA 23V516000
