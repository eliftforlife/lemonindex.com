Recalls 3

  • airbags 2023

    The driver-side air bag inflator may explode during deployment due to a manufacturing defect.

  • hybrid propulsion system 2018

    An update to the Hybrid Powertrain Control Module 2 (HPCM2) may have disabled the cell balancing function causing a low-voltage condition.

  • electrical 2015

    If the driver exits the vehicle without turning off the electrical system, the battery may drain low enough that the gasoline engine will automatically start itself to recharge the electric battery.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Chevrolet Volt reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 146; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Volt year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2013 Chevrolet Volt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), engine (31%) and transmission & drivetrain (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Chevrolet Volt have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Chevrolet Volt get?

37 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
