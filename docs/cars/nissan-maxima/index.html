Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Maxima a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 50; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2004.

What are the best Nissan Maxima years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2019, 2011.

What Nissan Maxima years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2004, 2005, 2009.
