2009 Nissan Maxima: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Nissan Maxima drew 1.2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 53 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 53,351 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Maxima complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,500 miles. Most came between 45,000 miles and 103,800 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 46,400 miles and 92,000 miles.
