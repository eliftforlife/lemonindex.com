Quick answers

Is the 2019 Nissan Maxima reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 29; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Maxima year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2019 Nissan Maxima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), body structure (18%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Nissan Maxima have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Nissan Maxima get?

24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2019 Nissan Maxima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Nissan Maxima cost in fuel a year?

About $3300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Nissan Maxima have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Maxima year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2019 and 2011.
