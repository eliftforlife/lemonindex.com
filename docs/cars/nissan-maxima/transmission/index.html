Nissan Maxima transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2004 (924 complaints), 2005 (769 complaints), 2006 (382 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2019, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Nissan Maxima years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Nissan Maxima years are 2004, 2005, 2006.

What are common Nissan Maxima transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, slipping, transmission replaced.

Which Nissan Maxima years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Nissan Maxima years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2019, 2017.
