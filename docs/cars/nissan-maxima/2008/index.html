Quick answers

Is the 2008 Nissan Maxima reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Maxima year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2008 Nissan Maxima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (40%), airbags (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Nissan Maxima have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Nissan Maxima get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
