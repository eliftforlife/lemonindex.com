2004 Nissan Maxima: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Nissan Maxima drew 4.1× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 281 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 76,367 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Maxima complaints that listed a mileage came before 80,000 miles. Most came between 56,100 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 67,000 miles and 101,000 miles.
