Quick answers

Is the 2000 Nissan Maxima reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Maxima year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2000 Nissan Maxima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), electrical (20%) and engine (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Nissan Maxima have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Nissan Maxima get?

20 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
