Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Liberty reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2005, 2006 and 2007.

Is the Jeep Liberty a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2012: Complaint Score 104; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2006 the most.

What are the best Jeep Liberty years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2010. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Jeep Liberty years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2006 (avoid, Complaint Score 305), 2007 (avoid, Complaint Score 271), 2005 (avoid, Complaint Score 217). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Jeep Liberty have?

38 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2006 (10), 2005 (8), 2007 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Jeep Liberty recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Jeep Liberty problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name visibility, suspension and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Jeep Liberty repairs cost?

The repairs Jeep Liberty owners reported paying for most: window regulator replacement (middle bill $425, 38 reports), ball joint replacement (middle bill $1,000, 11 reports), brake repair (middle bill $446, 10 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Jeep Liberty years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 395 YouTube videos about the Jeep Liberty, 2002 to 2012, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2011. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
