When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 7,000 and 40,000 miles (11,000–64,000 km) (the middle half of the 204 complaints, of all 257 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K, 100K and 150K miles (97K, 161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 18V278000 (suspension), 15V222000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 13V252000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Jeep sent dealers 62 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Transmission torque converter may fail”?

Quick answers

Is the 2006 Jeep Liberty reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 305; 100 is average, lower is better). The Jeep Liberty year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2006 Jeep Liberty problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (33%), electrical (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Jeep Liberty have?

10 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Jeep Liberty get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Jeep Liberty problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 7,000 and 40,000 miles (11,000–64,000 km) (the middle half of the 204 complaints, of all 257 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2006 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2006 Jeep Liberty have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Jeep Liberty cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 to $4,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Jeep Liberty have?

2.8L 4-cyl, 3.7L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Liberty year is better than the 2006?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest (about half as many complaints as the average car).
