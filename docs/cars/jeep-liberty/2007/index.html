How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Jeep Liberty reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 271; 100 is average, lower is better). The Jeep Liberty year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2007 Jeep Liberty problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (74%), transmission & drivetrain (8%) and fuel system, gasoline (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Jeep Liberty have?

7 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Jeep Liberty get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Jeep Liberty problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 13,000 and 51,000 miles (22,000–82,000 km) (the middle half of the 194 complaints, of all 221 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Jeep Liberty have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Jeep Liberty cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Jeep Liberty have?

3.7L 6-cyl.

What Jeep Liberty year is better than the 2007?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 18V278000 (suspension), 13V252000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 12V085000 (suspension) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Jeep sent dealers 51 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Torque converter shudder / shift quality improvements / dtc p0732”?
