Known issues

  • Sudden downshift to first gear (transmission)Recall

    2011–2013 F-150s with the 6-speed automatic can suddenly downshift into first at any speed. Ford also recalled certain 2014 F-150s for the same symptom.

    Fix: Dealers reprogram the powertrain control module; 2013 trucks with the 6.2 needed a second update (19V-433).

    733 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2010–2014)
  • No engine recallRecall

    NHTSA lists no recall on the engine itself for 2010–2014 F-150s. No 6.2 failure pattern is documented in the sources used here.

Known issues

  • 10-speed shift linkage and park pin recalls (2017–2018)Recall

    On some 2017 F-150s with the 10-speed, the pin holding the shift linkage can fall out. A few 2017–2018 trucks were built without a park roll pin. Either can let the truck roll in Park.

    Fix: Dealers replace or install the pin (17V-671, 18V-213).

    11 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2017–2020)
  • High-pressure fuel pump welds (2018)Recall

    Welds on the high-pressure fuel pump can crack and leak fuel or oil on 5,645 2018 trucks with the 3.5 GTDI. NHTSA's summary does not say whether Raptors are included.

    Fix: Dealers replace the pump (18V-392). Check the VIN on nhtsa.gov.

    10 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2017–2020)

Known issues

  • Shift to neutral (2021)Recall

    On some 2021 F-150s a false low-fluid-pressure code can drop the 10-speed into neutral while driving.

    Fix: Dealers update the powertrain software (22V-188; some trucks redone under 25V-169).

    23 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2021–2025)
  • Engine cup plug oil leak (2024–2025)Recall

    A misaligned engine cup plug can cause a fast oil leak on some 2024–2025 F-150s with the 3.5 GTDI. NHTSA's summaries do not say whether Raptors are included.

    Fix: Dealers check the plug and replace it if needed (24V-851, 25V-198). Check the VIN on nhtsa.gov.

    6 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2021–2025)

Quick answers

How fast is the Ford Raptor 37 (37-inch tires)?

The 3rd gen Raptor 37 (37-inch tires) makes 450 hp and 510 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.2 seconds.

What engine does the Ford F-150 Raptor have?

The 3rd gen Raptor (2021–2025) uses the Ford 3.5 EcoBoost HO, a 3.5-liter V-6, twin-turbo. Older generations: 1st gen: Ford 6.2 Boss V8; 2nd gen: Ford 3.5 EcoBoost HO.
