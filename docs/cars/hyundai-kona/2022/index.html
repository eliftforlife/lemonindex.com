Quick answers

Is the 2022 Hyundai Kona reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 100; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Kona year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2022 Hyundai Kona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (31%), electrical (23%) and automatic emergency braking (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Hyundai Kona have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.
