Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Kona reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2018. Most complaints: 2020.

Is the Hyundai Kona a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2018-2022: Lemon Index 96; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2018 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Kona years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2021, 2019. 2018 drew the fewest.

What Hyundai Kona years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020 (worse than average, Lemon Index 140), 2022 (about average, Lemon Index 100). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Kona have?

10 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2024 (4), 2020 (2), 2019 (1). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Kona problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and automatic emergency braking most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
