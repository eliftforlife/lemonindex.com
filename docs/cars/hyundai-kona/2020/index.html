Quick answers

Is the 2020 Hyundai Kona reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 140; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Kona year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2020 Hyundai Kona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (66%), electrical (7%) and transmission & drivetrain (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Hyundai Kona have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2020 Hyundai Kona safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Hyundai Kona problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 5,000 and 28,800 miles. The middle report was at 12,000 miles, from 48 complaints.

Does the 2020 Hyundai Kona have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Hyundai Kona cost in fuel a year?

About $600 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Hyundai Kona year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2021 and 2019.
