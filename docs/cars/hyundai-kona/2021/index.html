Quick answers

Is the 2021 Hyundai Kona reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 81; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Kona year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2021 Hyundai Kona problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (61%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Hyundai Kona have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
