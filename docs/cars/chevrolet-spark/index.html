Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Spark reliable?

About average. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2021. Most complaints: 2013 and 2014.

Is the Chevrolet Spark a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 89; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Spark years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2018, 2019. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Spark years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014 (worse than average, Lemon Index 181), 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 166), 2016 (better than average, Lemon Index 79, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Spark have?

21 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2014 (6), 2013 (5), 2015 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Spark recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Spark problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Chevrolet Spark years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 394 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Spark, 2013 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
