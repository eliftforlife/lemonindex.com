Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Spark reliable?

About average. It draws 1.1× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2021. Avoid 2013 and 2014.

Is the Chevrolet Spark a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 89; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2021 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Chevrolet Spark years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2018, 2019.

What Chevrolet Spark years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2013.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Spark have?

21 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2014 (6), 2013 (5), 2015 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Spark problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
