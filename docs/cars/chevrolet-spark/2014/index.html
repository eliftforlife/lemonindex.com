Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Spark reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 181; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Spark year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Spark problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (36%), transmission & drivetrain (23%) and electrical (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Spark have?

6 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
