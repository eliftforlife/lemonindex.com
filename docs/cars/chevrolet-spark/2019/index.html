Quick answers

Is the 2019 Chevrolet Spark reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 25; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Spark year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2019 Chevrolet Spark problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (62%), brakes (25%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Chevrolet Spark have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Chevrolet Spark get?

32 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2019 Chevrolet Spark have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Chevrolet Spark cost in fuel a year?

About $1950 to $2000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Chevrolet Spark have?

1.4L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Spark year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2018 and 2019.
