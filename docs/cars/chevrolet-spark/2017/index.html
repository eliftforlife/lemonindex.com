Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Spark reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Spark year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Spark problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (26%), brakes (26%) and transmission & drivetrain (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Spark have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chevrolet Spark get?

32 to 33 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
