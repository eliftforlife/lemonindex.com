Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Spark reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 79; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Spark year by this measure is 2021.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Spark problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (54%), airbags (11%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Spark have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

At what mileage do 2016 Chevrolet Spark problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 2,700 and 44,800 miles. The middle report was at 11,000 miles, from 35 complaints.

Does the 2016 Chevrolet Spark have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Chevrolet Spark cost in fuel a year?

About $650 to $1950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Chevrolet Spark year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2021, 2018 and 2019.
