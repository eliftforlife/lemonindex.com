Chevrolet Spark transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2014 (40 complaints), 2015 (20 complaints), 2013 (27 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2016, 2017.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Spark years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Spark years are 2014, 2015, 2013.

What are common Chevrolet Spark transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Chevrolet Spark years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Spark years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2016, 2017.
