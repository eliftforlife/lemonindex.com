Quick answers.

Is the Mazda CX-7 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.6× the average car's complaints. Fewest complaints: 2011.

Is the Mazda CX-7 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2007-2011: Lemon Index 161; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Mazda CX-7 years to buy?

Only 4 Mazda CX-7 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2011 drew the fewest complaints (3.2× fewer complaints than average).

What Mazda CX-7 years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Mazda CX-7 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-7 have?

29 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2009 (6), 2007 (5), 2008 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda CX-7 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, equipment & accessories and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
