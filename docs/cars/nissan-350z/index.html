Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Nissan 350Z reliable?

Not really. It draws 3.3× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2006, still drew 1.5× the average.

Is the Nissan 350Z a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2003-2006: Lemon Index 330; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2006 drew the fewest complaints and 2003 the most.

What are the best Nissan 350Z years to buy?

Only 4 Nissan 350Z years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2006 drew the fewest complaints (about one and a half times the average car's complaints).

What Nissan 350Z years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Nissan 350Z years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Nissan 350Z have?

12 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2003 (4), 2004 (3), 2005 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan 350Z problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name suspension, tires and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Nissan 350Z years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 198 YouTube videos about the Nissan 350Z, 2003 to 2009, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2007. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
