When problems show up

Half of the Chrysler Town & Country complaints that listed a mileage came before 74,000 miles. Most came between 47,500 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 54,100 miles and 101,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
