Quick answers

Is the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 119; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Town & Country year by this measure since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2009 Chrysler Town & Country problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (31%), electrical (14%) and wheels (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Chrysler Town & Country problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 45,000 and 101,300 miles. The middle report was at 75,000 miles, from 412 complaints.

Does the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Chrysler Town & Country have?

3.3L 6-cyl, 3.8L 6-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Chrysler Town & Country year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2014 and 2011.
