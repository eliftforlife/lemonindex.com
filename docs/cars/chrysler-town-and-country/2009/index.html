When problems show up

Half of the Chrysler Town & Country complaints that listed a mileage came before 75,000 miles. Most came between 45,000 miles and 101,300 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 60,500 miles and 106,400 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
