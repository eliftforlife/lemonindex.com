When problems show up

Half of the Chrysler Town & Country complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 55,000 miles and 93,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 67,000 miles and 105,000 miles.
