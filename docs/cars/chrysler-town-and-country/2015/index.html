Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 71; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2015 Chrysler Town & Country problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and airbags (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Chrysler Town & Country problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 43,000 and 89,300 miles. The middle report was at 68,800 miles, from 228 complaints.

Does the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Chrysler Town & Country have?

3.6L 6-cyl.
