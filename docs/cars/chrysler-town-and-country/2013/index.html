Quick answers

Is the 2013 Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 229; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Town & Country year by this measure since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2013 Chrysler Town & Country problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (77%), engine (7%) and brakes (4%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Chrysler Town & Country have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Chrysler Town & Country get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
