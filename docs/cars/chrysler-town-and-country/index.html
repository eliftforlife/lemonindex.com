Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2007. Most complaints: 2005, 2008 and 2009.

Is the Chrysler Town & Country a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2016: Lemon Index 177; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2007 drew the fewest complaints and 2008 the most.

What are the best Chrysler Town & Country years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2007, 2015, 2006. 2007 drew the fewest.

What Chrysler Town & Country years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008 (avoid, Lemon Index 303, the first year of a new generation), 2005 (avoid, Lemon Index 254), 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 242). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chrysler Town & Country have?

56 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2005 (7), 2008 (7), 2009 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Chrysler Town & Country recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chrysler Town & Country problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 16 Chrysler Town & Country years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 392 YouTube videos about the Chrysler Town & Country, 2001 to 2016, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2015. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
