Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2015. Avoid 2005, 2008 and 2016.

Is the Chrysler Town & Country a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2016: Lemon Index 177; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2007 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best Chrysler Town & Country years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2007, 2015, 2006.

What Chrysler Town & Country years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008, 2005, 2016.

How many recalls does the Chrysler Town & Country have?

56 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2005 (7), 2008 (7), 2009 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler Town & Country problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
