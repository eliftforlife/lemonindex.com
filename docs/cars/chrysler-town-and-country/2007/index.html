Quick answers

Is the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Town & Country year by this measure since 2010 is 2015.

What are the most common 2007 Chrysler Town & Country problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), engine (17%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country have?

2 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Chrysler Town & Country problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 41,400 and 105,700 miles. The middle report was at 69,500 miles, from 188 complaints.

Does the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Chrysler Town & Country have?

3.3L 6-cyl, 3.8L 6-cyl.

What Chrysler Town & Country year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2015, 2014 and 2011.
