2007 Chrysler Town & Country: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Chrysler Town & Country drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 48 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 138,151 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chrysler Town & Country complaints that listed a mileage came before 69,500 miles. Most came between 41,400 miles and 105,700 miles.

The top problem area, fuel or EV drive system, usually showed up between 65,000 miles and 118,100 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
