How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Volvo S40 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 160; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2005 Volvo S40 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (22%), brakes (22%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Volvo S40 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Volvo S40 get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2005 Volvo S40 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Volvo S40 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $3,750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Volvo S40 have?

2.4L 5-cyl, 2.5L 5-cyl.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 10V566000 (equipment), 08V033000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 06V137000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Volvo sent dealers 177 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Vibration during acceleration front wheel drive (fwd) only”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 11 Volvo S40 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 359 YouTube videos about the Volvo S40, 2000 to 2011, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2011. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
