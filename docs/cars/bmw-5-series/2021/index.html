Quick answers

Is the 2021 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 65; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 5 Series year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2021 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), fuel or ev drive system (19%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 BMW 5 Series have?

8 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 BMW 5 Series get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2021 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2850 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 BMW 5 Series have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW 5 Series year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2020.
