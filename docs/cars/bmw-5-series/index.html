Quick answers.

Is the BMW 5 Series a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 58; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2016 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best BMW 5 Series years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2016, 2015, 2020.

What BMW 5 Series years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008, 2011.
