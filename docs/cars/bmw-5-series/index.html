Quick answers.

Is the BMW 5 Series reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2016. Most complaints: 2011.

Is the BMW 5 Series a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 58; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2016 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best BMW 5 Series years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016, 2015, 2020. 2016 drew the fewest.

What BMW 5 Series years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (worse than average, Lemon Index 190, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 75), 2021 (better than average, Lemon Index 65). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the BMW 5 Series have?

94 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2006 (11), 2021 (8), 2008 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common BMW 5 Series problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, tires and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
