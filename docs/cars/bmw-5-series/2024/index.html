Quick answers

Is the 2024 BMW 5 Series reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 18 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2024 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (44%), stability control (44%) and brakes (28%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 BMW 5 Series have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 BMW 5 Series get?

28 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2024 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2650 to $2850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 BMW 5 Series have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW 5 Series year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2020.
