Quick answers

Is the 2004 BMW 5 Series reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 91; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 5 Series year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2004 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (19%), tires (17%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 BMW 5 Series have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2004 BMW 5 Series get?

17 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
