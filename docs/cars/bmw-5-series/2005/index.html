When problems show up

Half of the BMW 5 Series complaints that listed a mileage came before 87,600 miles. Most came between 60,000 miles and 114,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 65,100 miles and 111,500 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
