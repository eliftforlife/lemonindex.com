Quick answers

Is the 2016 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 7.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 14; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2016 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (40%), tires (40%) and brakes (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 BMW 5 Series have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 BMW 5 Series get?

19 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 BMW 5 Series safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 BMW 5 Series problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 36,000 and 94,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,000 miles, from 27 complaints.

Does the 2016 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2950 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 BMW 5 Series have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.
