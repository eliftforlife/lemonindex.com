What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10146812: “Check control message: no oil pressure”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10151197: “NBT and Entry Head Unit Software Update for Improved Satellite Radio Reception”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10151404: “Vibration or Shudder from Driveline when Accelerating from a Stop”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2016 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 14; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2016 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (40%), tires (40%) and brakes (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 BMW 5 Series have?

5 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 BMW 5 Series get?

19 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 BMW 5 Series safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 BMW 5 Series problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 36,000 and 94,000 miles (58,000–151,000 km) (the middle half of the 27 complaints, of all 81 filed so far, that listed a mileage). This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2016 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2,950 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

Is there a better BMW 5 Series year than the 2016?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V733000 (engine), 24V608000 (engine) and 16V914000 (airbags) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into high pressure fuel pump failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (22 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $11,000.)
  6. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (10 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $2,300.)
  7. BMW sent dealers 257 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Cbs brake pad calculation and correction”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 939 YouTube videos about the BMW 5 Series, 1997 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
