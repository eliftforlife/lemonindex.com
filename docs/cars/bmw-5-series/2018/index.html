Quick answers

Is the 2018 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 61; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 5 Series year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2018 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seat belts (33%), airbags (26%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 BMW 5 Series have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 BMW 5 Series get?

23 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
