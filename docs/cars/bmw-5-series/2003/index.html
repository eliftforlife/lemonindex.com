Quick answers

Is the 2003 BMW 5 Series reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 17 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2003 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (41%), electrical (24%) and seat belts (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 BMW 5 Series have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 BMW 5 Series get?

16 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 BMW 5 Series problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 26,000 and 100,800 miles. The middle report was at 76,800 miles, from 59 complaints.

Does the 2003 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2003 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $3600 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 BMW 5 Series have?

2.5L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW 5 Series year is better than the 2003?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2020.
