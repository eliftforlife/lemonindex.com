How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2007 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The BMW 5 Series year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2016.

What are the most common 2007 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (17%), airbags (13%) and tires (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 BMW 5 Series have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 BMW 5 Series get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 BMW 5 Series problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 56,000 and 110,000 miles (91,000–177,000 km) (the middle half of the 217 complaints, of all 290 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $3,600 to $4,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 BMW 5 Series have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl.

Is there a better BMW 5 Series year than the 2007?

Not nearby: the 2007 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the BMW 5 Series years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2016 drew the fewest.

  1. Were recalls 17V683000 (engine) and 13V564000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
