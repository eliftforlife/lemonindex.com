Quick answers

Is the 2013 BMW 5 Series reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 30; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 5 Series year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2013 BMW 5 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), electrical (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 BMW 5 Series have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 BMW 5 Series get?

17 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 BMW 5 Series safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 BMW 5 Series problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 44,500 and 98,000 miles. The middle report was at 75,000 miles, from 117 complaints.

Does the 2013 BMW 5 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 BMW 5 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2950 to $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 BMW 5 Series have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW 5 Series year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2020.
