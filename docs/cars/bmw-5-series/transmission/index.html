BMW 5 Series transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2004 (31 complaints), 2008 (31 complaints), 2011 (31 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2016, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2023 2026

    The connection between the driveshaft and the rear differential may become damaged, possibly resulting in a loss of power to the rear wheels or a vehicle rollaway.

  • 2022 2022

    The mechatronics unit inside the transmission may not have been assembled properly, which could result in an internal leak.

  • 2021 2021

    The output shafts (left/right) which transfer power from the driveshaft to the rear wheels may break due to a heat treatment error during supplier production.

  • 2011 2017

    The rear driveshaft constant velocity (CV) joint on the affected vehicles may fatigue and break.

  • 2006 2005

    CERTAIN PASSENGER VEHICLES MAY HAVE BEEN BUILT WITH AN INCORRECTLY MANUFACTURED AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION PARKING PAWL GUIDE PLATE.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which BMW 5 Series years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst BMW 5 Series years are 2004, 2008, 2011.

What are common BMW 5 Series transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, delay or hesitation.

Which BMW 5 Series years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the BMW 5 Series years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2016, 2020.
