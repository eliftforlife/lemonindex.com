Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Fiat 500 reliable?

We can't score it: only 970 were sold in the U.S. in 2024 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 5 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2024 Fiat 500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (40%), automatic emergency braking (20%) and fuel or electric drive system (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Fiat 500 have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2024 Fiat 500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 Fiat 500 cost in fuel a year?

About $650 to $700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Fiat 500 year is better than the 2024?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V715000 (lights) and 24V510000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Fiat sent dealers 16 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Start Stop Charging Warning Lamp Illuminated On, Intelligent Battery Sensor (IBS) State of…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 196 YouTube videos about the Fiat 500, 2012 to 2019, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2012. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
