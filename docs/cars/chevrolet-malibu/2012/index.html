Recalls 3

  • seat belts 2015

    In the affected vehicles, the flexible steel cables that connect the seat belts to the vehicle at the outside of the driver seat and the front passenger seat may be bent from being sat on while entering the vehicle.

  • steering 2014

    The affected steering shafts may have a yoke that inadequately supports the u-joint bearing resulting in a premature failure.

  • electrical 2014

    In the affected vehicles, increased resistance in the Body Control Module (BCM) connection may result in voltage fluctuations in the Brake Apply Sensor (BAS) circuit.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Malibu year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (26%), airbags (20%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chevrolet Malibu have?

3 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chevrolet Malibu get?

20 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
