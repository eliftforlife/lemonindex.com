Quick answers

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 221; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Malibu year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2004 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (115%), electrical (12%) and brakes (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
