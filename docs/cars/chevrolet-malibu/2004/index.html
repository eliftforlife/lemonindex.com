2004 Chevy Malibu Reliability, recalls and years to avoid

All 26 yearsCompare

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu reliable? No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 221; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Malibu year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 221; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Malibu year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2004 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (69%), electrical (11%) and brakes (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Chevrolet Malibu problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 40,000 and 116,000 miles (64,000–187,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,413 complaints, of all 1,697 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2004 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2004 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Chevrolet Malibu have?

2.2L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Malibu year is better than the 2004?

Nearby, the 2007 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14E044000 (steering), 14V252000 (electrical) and 14V224000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 646 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Inoperative park lamps, tail lamps, interior lamps, backup lamps, remote keyless entry, rear…”?
