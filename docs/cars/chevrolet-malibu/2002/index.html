2002 Chevrolet Malibu: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Chevrolet Malibu drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 142 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 169,000 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.
