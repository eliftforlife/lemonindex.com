Quick answers

Is the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 165; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Malibu year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2000 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (62%), engine (19%) and lights (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Chevrolet Malibu problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 47,000 and 105,000 miles. The middle report was at 69,900 miles, from 463 complaints.

Does the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Chevrolet Malibu have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.1L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Malibu year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2015.
