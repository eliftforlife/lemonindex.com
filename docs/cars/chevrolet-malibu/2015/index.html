Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 46; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Malibu year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2015 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (27%), engine (15%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu get?

24 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Chevrolet Malibu problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 23,200 and 86,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 160 complaints.

Does the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Chevrolet Malibu have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Malibu year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2015.
