Chevrolet Malibu transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2018 (114 complaints), 2021 (26 complaints), 2009 (80 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2024, 2023, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Malibu years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Malibu years are 2018, 2021, 2009.

What are common Chevrolet Malibu transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, rolled away or park trouble.

Which Chevrolet Malibu years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Malibu years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2023, 2015.
