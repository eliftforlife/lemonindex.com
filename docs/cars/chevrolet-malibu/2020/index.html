Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 81; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Malibu year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2020 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (49%), electrical (28%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chevrolet Malibu have?

1 safety recall (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Chevrolet Malibu get?

26 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
