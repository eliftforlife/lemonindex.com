When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 51,000 and 128,000 miles (82,000–206,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,091 complaints, of all 1,393 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14E044000 (steering), 14V252000 (electrical) and 14V224000 (transmission & drivetrain) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 643 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Intermittent steering wheel dither after front end alignment was performed”?

Problems Chevy told dealers about

Chevy sent dealers 643 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Intermittent steering wheel dither after front end alignment was performed
  • Electric steering is inoperative with c0545
  • Loss of power steering assist at high engine rpm

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 151; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Malibu year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2005 Chevrolet Malibu problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (52%), electrical (14%) and lights (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu have?

6 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Chevrolet Malibu problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 51,000 and 128,000 miles (82,000–206,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,091 complaints, of all 1,393 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2005 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2005 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $2,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Chevrolet Malibu have?

2.2L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Malibu year is better than the 2005?

Nearby, the 2007 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.
