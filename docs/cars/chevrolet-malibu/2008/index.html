2008 Chevrolet Malibu: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Chevrolet Malibu drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 186 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 178,253 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Malibu complaints that listed a mileage came before 78,800 miles. Most came between 49,000 miles and 118,000 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 50,000 miles and 124,900 miles.
