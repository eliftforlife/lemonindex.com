Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2016, 2017 and 2018.

Is the Chevrolet Malibu a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 99; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Malibu years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2014, 2015. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Malibu years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 217), 2017 (worse than average, Lemon Index 169), 2016 (worse than average, Lemon Index 156, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Malibu have?

116 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2013 (10), 2004 (9), 2006 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every Chevrolet Malibu recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chevrolet Malibu problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, steering and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 27 Chevrolet Malibu years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 990 YouTube videos about the Chevrolet Malibu, 1997 to 2023, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
