Quick answers

Is the 2003 Nissan Altima reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 116; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Altima year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2003 Nissan Altima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (53%), electrical (14%) and transmission & drivetrain (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Nissan Altima have?

10 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Nissan Altima get?

19 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Nissan Altima problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 57,000 and 120,000 miles. The middle report was at 84,900 miles, from 725 complaints.

Does the 2003 Nissan Altima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 Nissan Altima cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 Nissan Altima have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Nissan Altima year is better than the 2003?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2011 and 2021.
