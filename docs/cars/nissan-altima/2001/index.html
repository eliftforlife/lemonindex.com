Quick answers

Is the 2001 Nissan Altima reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Altima year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2001 Nissan Altima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (25%), electrical (16%) and speed control (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Nissan Altima have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Nissan Altima get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
