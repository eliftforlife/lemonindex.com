Nissan Altima transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2013 (440 complaints), 2016 (179 complaints), 2014 (185 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2019.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Nissan Altima years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Nissan Altima years are 2013, 2016, 2014.

What are common Nissan Altima transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, transmission replaced.

Which Nissan Altima years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Nissan Altima years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2021, 2019.
