Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Altima reliable?

Mostly. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2013.

Is the Nissan Altima a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 70; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Nissan Altima years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2011, 2021. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Altima years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (worse than average, Lemon Index 194, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (about average, Lemon Index 90), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 86). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Altima have?

113 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2002 (12), 2013 (12), 2003 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Altima problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, airbags and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
