Quick answers

Is the 2024 Nissan Altima reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.5× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 40; 100 is average). The best Nissan Altima year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2024 Nissan Altima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (12%), brakes (12%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Nissan Altima have?

1 safety recall (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Nissan Altima get?

29 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Nissan Altima safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Nissan Altima have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Nissan Altima cost in fuel a year?

About $2050 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Nissan Altima have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Altima year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2011 and 2021.
