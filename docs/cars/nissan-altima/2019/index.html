Quick answers

Is the 2019 Nissan Altima reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 50; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Altima year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Nissan Altima problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (21%), automatic emergency braking (21%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Nissan Altima have?

7 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Nissan Altima get?

29 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
