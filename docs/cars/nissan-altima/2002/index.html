2002 Nissan Altima: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Nissan Altima drew 1.2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 253 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 201,822 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Nissan Altima complaints that listed a mileage came before 88,200 miles. Most came between 64,100 miles and 112,100 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 69,000 miles and 108,000 miles.
