Known issues

  • Valve spring recallRecall

    On early 2013 FR-S cars, valve springs can fracture, which can make the engine run badly or stall.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the valve springs for free (NHTSA 18V-772).

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2013–2020)
  • Engine failures after the valve spring fixOwner-reported, disputed

    Some owners reported power loss, knocking or a dead engine after the recall repair. Excess sealant getting into the oil is a theory, not a confirmed cause.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2013–2020)
  • Fuel pump recallRecall

    On some 2018–2019 Toyota 86s, the low-pressure fuel pump in the tank can fail and stall the engine while driving.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-587).

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2013–2020)

Known issues

  • Oil starvation and bearing failureOwner-reported, disputed

    Some early GR86s lost rod bearings; one dealer teardown blamed excess factory sealant blocking the oil pickup. How many engines are affected is not known.

    Fix: Toyota rebuilt that car under warranty after first refusing.

    14 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2022–2025)
  • Oil pressure drops on trackOwner-reported

    An owner's data logging on one BRZ and one GR86 showed big oil pressure drops in hard right-hand corners on track.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2022–2025)

Quick answers

How fast is the Toyota GR86 manual?

The ZN8 GR86 manual makes 228 hp and 184 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.4 seconds.

What engine does the Toyota GR86 have?

The ZN8 GR86 (2022–2025) uses the Subaru FA24, a 2.4-liter flat-4, naturally aspirated. Older generations: ZN6: Subaru FA20.

Which Toyota GR86 engine do YouTube owners like most?

Subaru FA20 46/100, Subaru FA24 46/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
