Quick answers

Is the 2017 Mazda CX-9 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 15; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2017 Mazda CX-9 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (50%), seats (25%) and body structure (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Mazda CX-9 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Mazda CX-9 get?

23 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2017 Mazda CX-9 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Mazda CX-9 cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Mazda CX-9 have?

2.5L 4-cyl.
