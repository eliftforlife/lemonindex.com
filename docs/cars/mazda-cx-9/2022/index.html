Quick answers

Is the 2022 Mazda CX-9 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 16; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mazda CX-9 year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2022 Mazda CX-9 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (43%), lights (29%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Mazda CX-9 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Mazda CX-9 get?

23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
