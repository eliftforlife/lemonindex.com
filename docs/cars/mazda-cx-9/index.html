Quick answers.

Is the Mazda CX-9 reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2010 and 2012.

Is the Mazda CX-9 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 79; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints and 2012 the most.

What are the best Mazda CX-9 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2022, 2020. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Mazda CX-9 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 201), 2010 (worse than average, Lemon Index 120), 2021 (about average, Lemon Index 93). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-9 have?

48 safety recalls across the 17 model years we track. Most: 2009 (7), 2007 (6), 2008 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda CX-9 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
