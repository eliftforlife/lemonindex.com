Quick answers.

Is the Mazda CX-9 reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2010 and 2012.

Is the Mazda CX-9 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Complaint Score 79; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints and 2012 the most.

What are the best Mazda CX-9 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2022, 2020. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Mazda CX-9 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012 (avoid, Complaint Score 201), 2010 (worse than average, Complaint Score 120), 2021 (about average, Complaint Score 93). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-9 have?

48 safety recalls across the 17 model years we track. Most: 2009 (7), 2007 (6), 2008 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Mazda CX-9 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Mazda CX-9 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Mazda CX-9 repairs cost?

The repairs Mazda CX-9 owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $9,000, 11 reports), water pump replacement (middle bill $3,700, 6 reports), brake booster replacement (middle bill $1,000, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 16 Mazda CX-9 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Mazda CX-9, 2007 to 2023, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2018. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
