Known issues

  • Intercooler pump failureEnthusiast outlet

    The pump that cools the supercharger's intercooler fails often. Intake air heats up and the engine cuts boost.

    Fix: Replace it with the updated Bosch 010 pump.

  • Supercharger couplerParts seller

    The plastic coupler that drives the supercharger can crack and break, usually around 80,000–120,000 miles, causing a sudden loss of boost.

    Fix: Replace it with a billet aluminum coupler.

  • Oil leaksEnthusiast outlet

    The rear main seal and valve cover gaskets harden with age and leak.

    Fix: Replace the rear main seal before the leak gets bad.

Known issues

  • Timing chain stretchEnthusiast outlet

    The chain can stretch when its tensioner briefly runs short of oil, mainly on 2011–2012 engines. Long cranks, misfires and a check-engine light are the signs.

    Fix: Replace the chain with the revised tensioner, guides and check valves.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2013–2018)
  • Spark plug wearEnthusiast outlet

    The twin turbos wear spark plugs quickly, causing misfires and rough idle.

    Fix: Replace all eight plugs about every 40,000–50,000 miles.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2013–2018)
  • Valve timing cover leakEnthusiast outlet

    Oil or coolant can leak from a drain hole on the front valve timing cover when it isn't seated right.

    Fix: Reseal the cover following Mercedes' service bulletin.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2013–2018)
  • Coolant hose leaksEnthusiast outlet

    Coolant hoses split, crack or pop loose with age, which can overheat the engine quickly.

    Fix: Replace the leaking hose and check the ones next to it.

Known issues

  • Turbo oil feed line leak (recall)Recall

    NHTSA recall 20V048000 covers some 2019 G63s: the oil feed line to the turbocharger may leak, which raises fire risk.

    Fix: Dealers replace the oil feed line for free.

  • Cam adjuster wearParts seller

    Old or poor oil can leave varnish that slows the cam adjusters, causing a rough cold idle and P0011/P0021 codes.

    Fix: Change the oil on time with an approved full synthetic.

  • Spark plugs and coilsParts seller

    High boost wears spark plugs fast. Misfires and hesitation under throttle are the signs.

    Fix: Replace plugs on schedule, and coils as a set if one fails.

Quick answers

How fast is the Mercedes-Benz AMG G63?

The W463A AMG G63 makes 577 hp and 627 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.9 seconds. Top speed: 137 mph (limited; 149 with AMG Driver’s Package).

What engine does the Mercedes-AMG G63 have?

The W463A G63 (2019–2025) uses the Mercedes-AMG M177, a 4.0-liter V8, twin-turbo. Older generations: W463: Mercedes-AMG M113K; W463: Mercedes-AMG M157.

Which Mercedes-AMG G63 engine do YouTube owners like most?

Mercedes-AMG M177 56/100, Mercedes-AMG M157 46/100, Mercedes-AMG M113K 45/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
