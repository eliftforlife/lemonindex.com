Known issues

  • Valve spring recall (2013)Recall

    On 2013 BRZs, valve springs could break, making the engine run badly or stall. Some owners told The Drive their engines failed soon after the recall work.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the valve springs for free (NHTSA 18V-772).

    32 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2013–2020)
  • Fuel pump recall (2018–2019)Recall

    On some 2018–2019 BRZs, the low-pressure fuel pump can fail, which can stall the engine while driving.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the fuel pump for free (NHTSA 21V-587).

    27 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2013–2020)
  • Coil packs overheat (early cars)Parts seller

    A parts seller says coil packs on early BRZs tend to overheat, causing misfires and faster spark plug wear.

    Fix: Replace with the newest coil pack revision.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2013–2020)
  • RTV sealant and oil starvation (disputed)Lawsuit claim, disputed

    A 2024 class action claims excess factory RTV sealant can flake into the oil and starve 2013–2023 BRZ engines. It is an unproven claim; no bulletin or recall has been issued.

    6 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2013–2020)

Known issues

  • Oil pressure drops on track (disputed)Owner-reported

    One owner's logging on a 2022 BRZ and GR86 showed oil pressure falling from 62 to 28 psi in hard right-hand track corners. Subaru and Toyota did not comment.

    Fix: Owners suggest a full oil level, an oil cooler or a pan baffle for track use.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2022–2025)
  • RTV sealant in the oil pickup (disputed)Owner-reported / lawsuit claim

    In 2022 a GR86 owner found RTV sealant in the oil pickup after a bearing failure; BRZ owners reported similar buildup. A 2024 class action makes the same claim. No bulletin or recall followed.

    7 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2022–2025)
  • No engine recallRecall

    NHTSA lists no engine recall for 2022–2025 BRZs; the one recall (2022) covers rear turn signals.

Quick answers

How fast is the Subaru BRZ tS?

The ZD8 BRZ tS makes 228 hp and 184 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.5 seconds.

What engine does the Subaru BRZ have?

The ZD8 BRZ (2022–2025) uses the Subaru FA24, a 2.4-liter flat-4, naturally aspirated. Older generations: ZC6: Subaru FA20.

Which Subaru BRZ engine do YouTube owners like most?

Subaru FA20 46/100, Subaru FA24 46/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
