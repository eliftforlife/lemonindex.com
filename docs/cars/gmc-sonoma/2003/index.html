How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2003 GMC Sonoma reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2003 GMC Sonoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (19%), brakes (14%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 GMC Sonoma have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 GMC Sonoma get?

14 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2003 GMC Sonoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 GMC Sonoma cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $4,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 GMC Sonoma have?

2.2L 4-cyl, 4.3L 6-cyl.

Is there a better GMC Sonoma year than the 2003?

Not nearby: the 2003 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the GMC Sonoma years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 04V132000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. GMC sent dealers 411 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Malfunction indicator lamp (mil) is on; the transmission is stuck in third gear; and / or the…”?

How the YouTube ranks work
