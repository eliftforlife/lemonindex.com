2009 Hyundai Santa Fe: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Hyundai Santa Fe drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 59 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 80,343 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Hyundai Santa Fe complaints that listed a mileage came before 77,000 miles. Most came between 37,400 miles and 98,300 miles.

The top problem area, fuel or EV drive system, usually showed up between 70,000 miles and 104,400 miles.
