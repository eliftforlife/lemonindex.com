Quick answers

Is the 2014 Hyundai Santa Fe reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 106; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Santa Fe year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2014 Hyundai Santa Fe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (41%), steering (19%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

7 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Hyundai Santa Fe get?

19 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
