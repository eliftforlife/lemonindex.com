Quick answers

Is the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.7× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 172; 100 is average). The best Hyundai Santa Fe year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (23%), engine (22%) and automatic emergency braking (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

1 safety recall (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe get?

24 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,400 and 27,400 miles. The middle report was at 9,100 miles, from 21 complaints.

Does the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 23% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe cost in fuel a year?

About $1900 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Hyundai Santa Fe year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2018.
