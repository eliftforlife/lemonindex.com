When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 65,000 and 135,000 miles (104,000–218,000 km) (the middle half of the 130 complaints, of all 209 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Still under the range where most problems showed up, so they may be ahead of it.
  • At 100K miles (161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles (241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: suspension mostly 97,000–149,000 miles (156,000–240,000 km); engine mostly 50,000–82,000 miles (80,000–132,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V435000 (suspension), 09V123000 (suspension) and 03V520000 (equipment) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (14 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $950.)
  6. Hyundai sent dealers 135 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Subject regarding automatic transaxle oil drain plug”?

What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 623008: “Information on ecm/tcm reprogramming instructions for p0128 and minor engine stumble conditions”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10000714: “Engine control module (ecm) reprogramming instructions for p0139”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 105; 100 is average, lower is better). The Hyundai Santa Fe year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2016.

What are the most common 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), engine (38%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

4 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe get?

18 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 65,000 and 135,000 miles (104,000–218,000 km) (the middle half of the 130 complaints, of all 209 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2001 is still under the range where most problems showed up, so they may be ahead of it. At 100K miles, a 2001 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2001 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

What Hyundai Santa Fe year is better than the 2001?

Nearby, the 2004 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2016 drew the fewest.
