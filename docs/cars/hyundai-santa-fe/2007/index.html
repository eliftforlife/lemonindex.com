Quick answers

Is the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 164; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Hyundai Santa Fe year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (34%), brakes (20%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

9 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 43,000 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 74,000 miles, from 563 complaints.

Does the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Hyundai Santa Fe have?

2.7L 6-cyl, 3.3L 6-cyl.

What Hyundai Santa Fe year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2016, 2015 and 2018.
