Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Santa Fe reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2016. Avoid 2007, 2013 and 2017.

Is the Hyundai Santa Fe a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 110; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2016 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Hyundai Santa Fe years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2016, 2006, 2015.

What Hyundai Santa Fe years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017, 2007, 2013.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Santa Fe have?

110 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2007 (9), 2021 (8), 2013 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Santa Fe problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
