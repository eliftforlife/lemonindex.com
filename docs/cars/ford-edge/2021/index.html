How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Ford Edge reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 53; 100 is average, lower is better). 2021 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.8× the average car's complaints. The Ford Edge year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2021 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (17%), backup camera (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Ford Edge have?

7 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Ford Edge get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Ford Edge safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Ford Edge problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 16,000 and 66,000 miles (26,000–106,000 km) (the middle half of the 24 complaints, of all 112 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2021 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2021 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2021 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $2,700 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Ford Edge have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Ford Edge year than the 2021?

Not nearby: the 2021 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ford Edge years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V124000 (back over prevention), 25V315000 (back over prevention) and 25V160000 (back over prevention) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (276 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $8,000.)
  5. Ford sent dealers 148 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Noise from the rear”?
