Quick answers

Is the 2021 Ford Edge reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 53; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2021 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), backup camera (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Ford Edge have?

7 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Ford Edge get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
