Quick answers

Is the 2013 Ford Edge reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 6.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 610; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2013 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (55%), body structure (20%) and lights (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Ford Edge have?

2 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Ford Edge get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 Ford Edge safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 Ford Edge problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,000 and 79,500 miles. The middle report was at 54,000 miles, from 1,785 complaints.

Does the 2013 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2013 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Ford Edge have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl, 3.7L 6-cyl.

What Ford Edge year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2021 and 2014.
