Ford Edge transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (410 complaints), 2008 (218 complaints), 2020 (168 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2024, 2012.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2021 2021

    An inadequate amount of lubricant in the rear drive unit may cause seizure of the rear drive axle.

  • 2020 2020

    The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing or loose bolts.

  • 2018 2018

    On vehicles with 2.0L gas engines and six-speed automatic transmissions, the torque converter weld studs may have been inadequately welded.

  • 2018 2022

    The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the halfshaft retention circlip may not have been properly installed, and as a result, the halfshaft may move outward and disengage from the linkshaft while driving and without prior warning.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Edge years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Edge years are 2019, 2008, 2020.

What are common Ford Edge transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, shudder, delay or hesitation.

Which Ford Edge years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Edge years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2024, 2012.
