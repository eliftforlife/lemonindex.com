Quick answers

Is the 2017 Ford Edge reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 112; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2017 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (41%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Ford Edge have?

8 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Ford Edge get?

19 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Ford Edge safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Ford Edge problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 51,100 and 90,000 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 616 complaints.

Does the 2017 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Ford Edge have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Edge year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2021 and 2014.
