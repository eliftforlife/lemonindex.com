Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ford Edge reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2010 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (26%), electrical (16%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ford Edge have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ford Edge get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Ford Edge problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 47,500 and 118,800 miles. The middle report was at 85,000 miles, from 356 complaints.

Does the 2010 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 26% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Ford Edge have?

3.5L 6-cyl.
