Quick answers

Is the 2007 Ford Edge reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 90; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2007 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (26%), visibility (20%) and transmission & drivetrain (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Ford Edge have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Ford Edge get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Ford Edge problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 53,900 and 118,800 miles. The middle report was at 90,000 miles, from 600 complaints.

Does the 2007 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Ford Edge have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Ford Edge year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2021 and 2014.
