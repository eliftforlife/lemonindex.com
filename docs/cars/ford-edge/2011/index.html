Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Edge reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 282; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Edge year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (41%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and body structure (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Edge have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Edge get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
