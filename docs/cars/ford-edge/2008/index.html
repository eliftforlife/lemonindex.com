2008 Ford Edge: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Ford Edge drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 69 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 110,798 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ford Edge complaints that listed a mileage came before 85,600 miles. Most came between 60,000 miles and 120,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 58,000 miles and 130,600 miles.
