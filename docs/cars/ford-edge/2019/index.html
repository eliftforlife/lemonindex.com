What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10155555: “Some 2019 Edge/Nautilus vehicles equipped with a 2.0L EcoBoost engine, built on or before 23-Jan-2019 may exhibit an…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10159459: “Some 2019 Edge/Nautilus vehicles equipped with a 8F57 transmission and built on or before 01-Mar-2019 may exhibit…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10156564: “Some 2019 Mustang, Continental, Fusion, MKZ, F150, Transit Connect, Ranger, Edge or Nautilus with SYNC 3 may have the…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

    A software error may cause the rearview camera to display a blank image, or the image may remain on the display after the backing event has ended.

    The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing or loose bolts.

  • Seat belts · recall issued 2019

    The driver-side seat belt pretensioner anchor may have been improperly crimped, possibly resulting in the seat belt webbing detaching from the anchor in the event of a crash.

Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 25V442000 (back over prevention), 20V550000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 19V766000 (seat belts) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Ford sent dealers 203 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “For 2010-2019 vehicles equipped with turbocharged engines, when diagnosing and/or replacing…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Ford Edge reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 74; 100 is average, lower is better). 2019 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.8× the average car's complaints. The Ford Edge year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2019 Ford Edge problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (23%), engine (16%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Ford Edge have?

3 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Ford Edge get?

21 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Ford Edge safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Ford Edge problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 26,000 and 80,000 miles (43,000–129,000 km) (the middle half of the 219 complaints, of all 770 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2019 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Ford Edge have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 23% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Ford Edge cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Ford Edge have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl.

What Ford Edge year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.
