Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Ford Edge reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Ford Edge a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 176; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best Ford Edge years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2021, 2014. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Ford Edge years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013 (avoid, Lemon Index 610), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 283), 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 282). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Edge have?

92 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2017 (8), 2008 (7), 2009 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Edge recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Edge problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, body structure and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 17 Ford Edge years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Ford Edge, 2007 to 2024, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
