Quick answers.

Is the Ford Edge reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2008. Avoid 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Ford Edge a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 176; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Ford Edge years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2021, 2008.

What Ford Edge years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2012, 2011.

How many recalls does the Ford Edge have?

92 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2017 (8), 2008 (7), 2009 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Edge problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, body structure and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
