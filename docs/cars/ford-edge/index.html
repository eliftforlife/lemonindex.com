Quick answers.

Is the Ford Edge a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 176; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Ford Edge years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2021, 2008.

What Ford Edge years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2012, 2011.
