Quick answers

Is the 2022 Dodge Challenger reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 10× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 10; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Dodge Challenger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are seat belts (29%), engine (14%) and wipers & visibility (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Dodge Challenger have?

2 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Dodge Challenger get?

15 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Dodge Challenger safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Dodge Challenger have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Dodge Challenger cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Dodge Challenger have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.
