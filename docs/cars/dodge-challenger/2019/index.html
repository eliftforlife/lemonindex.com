Quick answers

Is the 2019 Dodge Challenger reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 49; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Challenger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2019 Dodge Challenger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wheels (26%), electrical (23%) and steering (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Dodge Challenger have?

3 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Dodge Challenger get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
