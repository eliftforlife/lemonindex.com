Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Challenger a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2010.

What are the best Dodge Challenger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2020, 2021.

What Dodge Challenger years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2010, 2012, 2009.
