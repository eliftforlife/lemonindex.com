Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Challenger reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2009, 2010 and 2012.

Is the Dodge Challenger a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Dodge Challenger years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2020, 2021. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Challenger years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010 (avoid, Lemon Index 255), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 205), 2009 (about average, Lemon Index 115). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Challenger have?

61 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2009 (7), 2010 (7), 2011 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Dodge Challenger recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Dodge Challenger problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 7 Dodge Challenger years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Dodge Challenger, 2008 to 2021, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
