Dodge Challenger transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2010 (21 complaints), 2009 (20 complaints), 2012 (12 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2020, 2017, 2021.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Dodge Challenger years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Dodge Challenger years are 2010, 2009, 2012.

What are common Dodge Challenger transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Dodge Challenger years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Dodge Challenger years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2020, 2017, 2021.
