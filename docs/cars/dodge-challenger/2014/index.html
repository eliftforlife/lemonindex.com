How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Dodge Challenger reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 107; 100 is average, lower is better). The Dodge Challenger year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2014 Dodge Challenger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (51%), engine (23%) and airbags (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Dodge Challenger have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Dodge Challenger get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Dodge Challenger safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V018000 (airbags), 18E053000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 17V741000 (electrical) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  6. Dodge sent dealers 200 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Various vehicles are experiencing loss of oil, and possibly an oil leakage pooling in engine…”?
