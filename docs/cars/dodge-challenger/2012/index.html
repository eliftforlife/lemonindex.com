Quick answers

Is the 2012 Dodge Challenger reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 205; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Challenger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Dodge Challenger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (59%), airbags (17%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Dodge Challenger have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Dodge Challenger get?

17 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
