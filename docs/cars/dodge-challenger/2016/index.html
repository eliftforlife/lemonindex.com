Quick answers

Is the 2016 Dodge Challenger reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 57; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Challenger year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2016 Dodge Challenger problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (22%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Dodge Challenger have?

2 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Dodge Challenger get?

16 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
