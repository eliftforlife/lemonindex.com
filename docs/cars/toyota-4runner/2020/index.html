Quick answers

Is the 2020 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 22; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota 4Runner year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2020 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (19%), brakes (19%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Toyota 4Runner have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Toyota 4Runner get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
