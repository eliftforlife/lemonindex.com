Quick answers

Is the 2005 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 53; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota 4Runner year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2005 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (21%), seats (13%) and suspension (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Toyota 4Runner have?

5 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Toyota 4Runner get?

16 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Toyota 4Runner problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 59,100 and 157,000 miles. The middle report was at 104,800 miles, from 301 complaints.

Does the 2005 Toyota 4Runner have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Toyota 4Runner cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Toyota 4Runner have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl.

What Toyota 4Runner year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2017 and 2021.
