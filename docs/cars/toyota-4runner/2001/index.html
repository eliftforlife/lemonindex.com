Quick answers

Is the 2001 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 23; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota 4Runner year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2001 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (25%), brakes (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Toyota 4Runner have?

3 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Toyota 4Runner get?

16 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
