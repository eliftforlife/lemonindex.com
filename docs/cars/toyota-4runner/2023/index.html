Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 23V680000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (8 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (5 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  6. Toyota sent dealers 96 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “2023 model year Toyota vehicles are equipped with driver side floor mat retainers to secure…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2023 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about a quarter as many complaints as the average car per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 22; 100 is average). The Toyota 4Runner year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2023 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (12%), lights (9%) and automatic emergency braking (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Toyota 4Runner have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Toyota 4Runner get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Toyota 4Runner safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Toyota 4Runner have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Toyota 4Runner cost in fuel a year?

About $3,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Toyota 4Runner have?

4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota 4Runner year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest.

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
