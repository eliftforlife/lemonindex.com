Questions to ask the seller

7 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 19V741000 (airbags), 19V005000 (airbags) and 18V024000 (airbags) done? It has 12 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (8 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (5 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  7. Toyota sent dealers 288 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information on installing manual transmission shift lever or park neutral position switch”?

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota 4Runner year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2010 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), speed control (20%) and traction control system (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Toyota 4Runner have?

12 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Toyota 4Runner get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Toyota 4Runner problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 12,000 and 100,000 miles (20,000–161,000 km) (the middle half of the 51 complaints, of all 146 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Toyota 4Runner have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Toyota 4Runner cost in fuel a year?

About $3,200 to $3,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Toyota 4Runner have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota 4Runner year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).
