Quick answers

Is the 2017 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 18; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota 4Runner year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (28%), electrical (16%) and airbags (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Toyota 4Runner have?

5 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Toyota 4Runner get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Toyota 4Runner safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Toyota 4Runner problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 7,000 and 33,000 miles. The middle report was at 12,900 miles, from 23 complaints.

Does the 2017 Toyota 4Runner have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Toyota 4Runner cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Toyota 4Runner have?

4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota 4Runner year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2017 and 2021.
