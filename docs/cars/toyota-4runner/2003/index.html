Quick answers

Is the 2003 Toyota 4Runner reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 193; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota 4Runner year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2003 Toyota 4Runner problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (47%), transmission & drivetrain (11%) and seats (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Toyota 4Runner have?

3 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Toyota 4Runner get?

15 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
