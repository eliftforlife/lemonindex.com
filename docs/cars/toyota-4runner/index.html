Quick answers.

Is the Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Yes, very. It draws 3.8× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2020. Avoid 2003.

Is the Toyota 4Runner a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 26; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2003.

What are the best Toyota 4Runner years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2017, 2021.

What Toyota 4Runner years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2003.

How many recalls does the Toyota 4Runner have?

115 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2010 (12), 2011 (10), 2015 (9). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota 4Runner problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
