2004 Toyota 4Runner: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Toyota 4Runner drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 80 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 114,212 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota 4Runner complaints that listed a mileage came before 116,000 miles. Most came between 63,000 miles and 155,000 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 98,000 miles and 166,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
