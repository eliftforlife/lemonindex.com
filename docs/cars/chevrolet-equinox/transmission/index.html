Chevrolet Equinox transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2010 (143 complaints), 2011 (75 complaints), 2005 (48 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2024, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Equinox years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Equinox years are 2010, 2011, 2005.

What are common Chevrolet Equinox transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, delay or hesitation.

Which Chevrolet Equinox years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Equinox years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2024, 2022.
