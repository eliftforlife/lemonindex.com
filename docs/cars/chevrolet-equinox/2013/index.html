Quick answers

Is the 2013 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 54; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Equinox year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2013 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (41%), electrical (18%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Chevrolet Equinox have?

2 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Chevrolet Equinox get?

19 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
