Quick answers

Is the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 100; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Equinox year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2006 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (46%), electrical (14%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
