When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 50,000 and 108,000 miles (80,000–173,000 km) (the middle half of the 601 complaints, of all 695 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K and 100K miles (97K and 161K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 150K miles (241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: visibility mostly 48,000–82,000 miles (78,000–131,000 km); electrical mostly 54,000–124,000 miles (88,000–199,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Does the A/C blow cold and the heat work? Ask whether the A/C compressor or condenser was replaced. (50 owners named a a/c & heat repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)

Quick answers

Is the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 100; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Equinox year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2006 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (46%), electrical (14%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Chevrolet Equinox problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 50,000 and 108,000 miles (80,000–173,000 km) (the middle half of the 601 complaints, of all 695 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2006 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2006 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Chevrolet Equinox have?

3.4L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2006?

Nearby, the 2008 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.
