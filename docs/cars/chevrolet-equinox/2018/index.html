Quick answers

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 54; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Equinox year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2018 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (18%), engine (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox have?

5 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox get?

24 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Chevrolet Equinox problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 8,000 and 65,000 miles. The middle report was at 22,200 miles, from 188 complaints.

Does the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $2300 to $3300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox have?

1.5L 4-cyl, 1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2018?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2014.
