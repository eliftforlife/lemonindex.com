Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 23V869000 (body structure) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10240359: “This bulletin provides information on identifying Non-GM Engine Calibrations for Gasoline Engines using the Tech 2 or…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11010465: “A new radio software update, version C157/C158, was released to service vehicles equipped with Infotainment system RPO…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.3× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 44; 100 is average). The Chevrolet Equinox year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2024 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (29%), electrical (21%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox have?

1 safety recall (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $700 to $2450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2024?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (3.8× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.
