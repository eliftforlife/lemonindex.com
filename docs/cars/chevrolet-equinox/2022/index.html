Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Equinox year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2022 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system (39%), fuel system, gasoline (19%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox have?

5 safety recalls (latest: child seat). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2022 Chevrolet Equinox problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,000 and 49,000 miles (8,000–78,000 km) (the middle half of the 42 complaints, of all 184 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2022 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $2,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Chevrolet Equinox have?

1.5L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2022?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V339000 (child seat), 23V013000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 22V724000 (seats) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (10 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $725.)

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
