Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  2. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11007116: “This bulletin provides information on identifying Non-GM Engine Calibrations for Gasoline Engines using the Tech 2 or…”)

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about one and a half times the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 141; 100 is average). 2025 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws about half as many complaints as the average car. The Chevrolet Equinox year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2025 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), automatic emergency braking (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Chevrolet Equinox have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

Is the 2025 Chevrolet Equinox safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2025 Chevrolet Equinox problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,600 and 14,000 miles (4,200–22,000 km) (the middle half of the 27 complaints, of all 234 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2025 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2025 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $700 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2025?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.
