Quick answers

Is the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 11 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2009 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (18%), airbags (18%) and brakes (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Chevrolet Equinox problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 56,400 and 104,000 miles. The middle report was at 79,000 miles, from 77 complaints.

Does the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Chevrolet Equinox have?

3.4L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2014.
