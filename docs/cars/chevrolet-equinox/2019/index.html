Quick answers

Is the 2019 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Equinox year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2019 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (43%), fuel or ev drive system (16%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Chevrolet Equinox have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Chevrolet Equinox get?

24 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
