When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Equinox complaints that listed a mileage came before 14,000 miles. Most came between 2,800 miles and 71,500 miles.

The top problem area, brakes, usually showed up between 800 miles and 53,000 miles.

Recalls: 4

  • brakes 2019
    The rear brake calipers may have been produced with an incorrect lubricant, potentially causing the rubber seals to swell and the brakes to drag. NHTSA 19V704000
  • fuel system, gasoline 2019
    The fuel tanks may have been manufactured with an improperly sealed seam, possibly allowing fuel to leak along the seam near the fuel inlet. NHTSA 19V667000
  • transmission & drivetrain 2020
    The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing bolts. NHTSA 20V668000
  • child seat 2023
    The four rear-seat lower child seat LATCH anchorage bars may have excessive powder coating, which could prevent a child seat from being installed with the anchorage bars. NHTSA 23V339000

Recall work is free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Details at nhtsa.gov.
