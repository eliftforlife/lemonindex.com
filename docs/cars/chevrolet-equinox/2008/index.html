Quick answers

Is the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Equinox year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2008 Chevrolet Equinox problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (30%), electrical (16%) and steering (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Chevrolet Equinox problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,400 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 75,000 miles, from 296 complaints.

Does the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Chevrolet Equinox have?

3.4L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chevrolet Equinox year is better than the 2008?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2014.
