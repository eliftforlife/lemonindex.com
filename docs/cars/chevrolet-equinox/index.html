Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2017.

Is the Chevrolet Equinox a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 46; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Equinox years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2021, 2014. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Equinox years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Equinox year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 84, the first year of a new generation), 2011 (better than average, Lemon Index 76), 2013 (better than average, Lemon Index 54).

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Equinox have?

46 safety recalls across the 21 model years we track. Most: 2018 (5), 2022 (5), 2007 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Equinox problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
