Known issues

  • Intake-valve carbon buildupTuning shop

    Direct injection means fuel never washes the backs of the intake valves, so oil-vapor deposits build up. Symptoms: rough idle, cold-start misfire, hesitation.

    Fix: Physical cleaning (walnut blasting) once an inspection confirms deposits; fuel additives don’t reach the valves.

  • Oil level and oil consumptionTuning shop

    The engine holds relatively little oil, so leaks, PCV faults, turbo seal wear or worn rings drop the level faster. Check the dipstick often.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2021)
  • Turbo air intake duct recall (2015–2016)Recall

    On WRXs built April to October 2015, the turbocharger air intake duct may crack, which can stall the engine.

    Fix: Dealer inspects the duct and replaces it for free, depending on its lot number (NHTSA 16V-162, Subaru WTA-62).

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2015–2021)

Quick answers

How fast is the Subaru WRX (6-speed manual)?

The VB WRX (6-speed manual) makes 271 hp and 258 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 5.5 seconds.

What engine does the Subaru WRX have?

The VB WRX (2022–) uses the Subaru FA24F 2.4 turbo, a 2.4-liter flat-4, turbo, direct injection. Older generations: VA: Subaru FA20F (FA20DIT).
