Quick answers

Is the 2021 Nissan Versa reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 36; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Versa year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2021 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (19%), engine (19%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Nissan Versa have?

3 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Nissan Versa get?

30 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Nissan Versa safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Nissan Versa have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Nissan Versa cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Nissan Versa have?

1.6L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Versa year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2010 and 2011.
