Quick answers

Is the 2007 Nissan Versa reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Versa year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2007 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (19%), engine (17%) and tires (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Nissan Versa have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Nissan Versa get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
