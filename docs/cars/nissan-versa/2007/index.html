Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22E066000 (airbags), 20V008000 (airbags) and 18V452000 (airbags) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced? (22 owners named a suspension repair bill; the middle one was $724.)
  6. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (16 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $4,000.)
  7. Nissan sent dealers 89 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “SERVICE INFORMATION This bulletin provides information to identify: - A leaking strut or a…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Nissan Versa reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The Nissan Versa year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2019.

What are the most common 2007 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (19%), engine (17%) and tires (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Nissan Versa have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Nissan Versa get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Nissan Versa problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 28,000 and 90,000 miles (46,000–145,000 km) (the middle half of the 202 complaints, of all 354 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Nissan Versa have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Nissan Versa cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $2,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Nissan Versa have?

1.8L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Versa year is better than the 2007?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest.
