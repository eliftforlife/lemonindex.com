Quick answers

Is the 2016 Nissan Versa reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Versa year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2016 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (27%), brakes (19%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Nissan Versa have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Nissan Versa get?

29 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
