Quick answers

Is the 2016 Nissan Versa reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Nissan Versa year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2016 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (27%), brakes (19%) and steering (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Nissan Versa have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Nissan Versa get?

29 to 34 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Nissan Versa safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Nissan Versa problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 9,800 and 62,000 miles. The middle report was at 34,300 miles, from 66 complaints.

Does the 2016 Nissan Versa have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Nissan Versa cost in fuel a year?

About $1900 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Nissan Versa have?

1.6L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Versa year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2010 and 2011.
