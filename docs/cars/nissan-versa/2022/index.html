Questions to ask the seller

  1. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Nissan Versa reliable?

We can't score it: only 13,396 were sold in the U.S. in 2022 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 14 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2022 Nissan Versa problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (36%), engine (29%) and automatic emergency braking (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Nissan Versa have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Nissan Versa get?

30 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Nissan Versa safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Nissan Versa have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 36% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Nissan Versa cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $2150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Nissan Versa have?

1.6L 4-cyl.

What Nissan Versa year is better than the 2022?

Nearby, the 2019 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (5.9× fewer complaints than average).

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
