Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Versa reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.7× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2012.

Is the Nissan Versa a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Nissan Versa years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2010, 2011.

What Nissan Versa years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012.

How many recalls does the Nissan Versa have?

71 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2012 (11), 2007 (9), 2008 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Nissan Versa problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
