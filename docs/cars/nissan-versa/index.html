Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Nissan Versa reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2012.

Is the Nissan Versa a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 59; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2012 the most.

What are the best Nissan Versa years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2019, 2010, 2011. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Nissan Versa years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012 (worse than average, Lemon Index 118, the first year of a new generation), 2014 (about average, Lemon Index 99), 2015 (about average, Lemon Index 90). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Nissan Versa have?

71 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2012 (11), 2007 (9), 2008 (8). Each year's page lists them, and every Nissan Versa recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Nissan Versa problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 17 Nissan Versa years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 539 YouTube videos about the Nissan Versa, 2007 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
