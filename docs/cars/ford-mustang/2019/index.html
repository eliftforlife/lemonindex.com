Quick answers

Is the 2019 Ford Mustang reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 65; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Mustang year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2019 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (35%), transmission & drivetrain (17%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Ford Mustang have?

6 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Ford Mustang get?

18 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
