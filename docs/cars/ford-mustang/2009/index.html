When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 67,000 miles (32,000–108,000 km) (the middle half of the 72 complaints, of all 181 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles (161K and 241K km) Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: airbags mostly 30,000–69,000 miles (48,000–111,000 km); speed control mostly 13,000–100,000 miles (21,000–161,000 km).

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21E013000 (airbags), 21V081000 (airbags) and 18V046000 (airbags) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (17 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $895.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 27 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Due to vehicles, with carbon fiber clutches, a chatter or moan/groan noise is heard while…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Ford Mustang reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). 2009 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Ford Mustang year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2009 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (38%), airbags (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Ford Mustang have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Ford Mustang get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Ford Mustang problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 67,000 miles (32,000–108,000 km) (the middle half of the 72 complaints, of all 181 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2009 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2009 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2009 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $3,200 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Ford Mustang have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

What Ford Mustang year is better than the 2009?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).
