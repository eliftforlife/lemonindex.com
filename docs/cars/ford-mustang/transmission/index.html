Ford Mustang transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2011 (180 complaints), 2012 (154 complaints), 2013 (42 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2022, 2025.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2025 2025

    The transmission valve body may have been machined incorrectly, which can cause reverse gear failure, or unexpected forward movement in reverse or neutral gear.

  • 2024 2024

    The clutch pressure line may be improperly secured and contact hot exhaust components, which can result in melting of the clutch pressure line and cause a brake fluid leak.

  • 2023 2023

    The transmission may contain a loose bolt which could prevent the transmission from engaging the park gear, although the gear shifter position may indicate that the vehicle has been shifted to "PARK.".

  • 2020 2020

    The "Transmission Not In Park" warning message and associated chime duration are shorter than intended.

  • 2020 2022

    The Powertrain Control Module (PCM) may broadcast an unintended faulted signal to the modules controlling the reverse camera, reverse light, and driver assist features, which may disable all of these functions.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ford Mustang years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ford Mustang years are 2011, 2012, 2013.

What are common Ford Mustang transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Ford Mustang years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ford Mustang years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2022, 2025.
