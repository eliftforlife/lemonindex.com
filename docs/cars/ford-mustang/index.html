Quick answers

Is the Ford Mustang a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 136; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Ford Mustang years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2008, 2021.

What Ford Mustang years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2011, 2012.
