Quick answers.

Is the Ford Mustang reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Ford Mustang a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 136; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Ford Mustang years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2008, 2021. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Ford Mustang years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 301), 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 276), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 247). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Mustang have?

186 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2024 (12), 2015 (11), 2000 (10). Each year's page lists them, and every Ford Mustang recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Ford Mustang problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Ford Mustang repairs cost?

The repairs Ford Mustang owners reported paying for most: engine replacement (middle bill $9,200, 14 reports), AC evaporator replacement (middle bill $1,900, 10 reports), throttle body replacement (middle bill $467, 10 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 28 Ford Mustang years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 795 YouTube videos about the Ford Mustang, 1994 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
