Quick answers.

Is the Ford Mustang reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Ford Mustang a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 136; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Ford Mustang years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2021, 2022. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Ford Mustang years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 301), 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 276), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 247). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Ford Mustang have?

186 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2024 (12), 2015 (11), 2000 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Mustang problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
