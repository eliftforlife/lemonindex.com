  • seat belts 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the passenger safety belt tension sensor may misclassify the size of the front passenger seat occupant.

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford Mustang reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 107; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Mustang year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2015 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (18%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford Mustang have?

11 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford Mustang get?

18 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Ford Mustang safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Ford Mustang problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,000 and 58,800 miles. The middle report was at 34,500 miles, from 264 complaints.

Does the 2015 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Ford Mustang have?

2.3L 4-cyl, 3.7L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford Mustang year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2021 and 2022.
