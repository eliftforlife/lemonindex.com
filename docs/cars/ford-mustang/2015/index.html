Quick answers

Is the 2015 Ford Mustang reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 107; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Mustang year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2015 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (18%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Ford Mustang have?

11 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Ford Mustang get?

18 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
