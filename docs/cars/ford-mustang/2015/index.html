  • brakes 2022

    Exposure to certain environmental conditions, such as high temperatures, high humidity, and salt air can cause the brake pedal bumper to corrode and separate from the brake pedal.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2020

    These vehicles were previously recalled and repaired under recalls 15V-246, 16V-643 or 17V-210, however the repair may not have been completed properly.

  • engine 2016

    A hose may separate from the engine oil cooler tube assembly causing an oil leak.

  • latches/locks/linkages 2016

    A component within the door latches may break, preventing the doors from latching and/or leading the driver or a passenger to believe a door is securely closed when, in fact, it is not.

  • brakes 2015

    The affected vehicles have a brake caliper guide pin that may not have been sufficiently tightened during installation of the half shafts.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2015

    Prolonged exposure to elevated underbody temperatures can cause degradation of the fuel tank and/or fuel vapor lines, which may eventually result in a fuel leak.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2014

    The fuel pressure sensor may not have been seated properly to the fuel jumper line, which may cause a fuel leak.

  • seat belts 2014

    In the affected vehicles, the passenger safety belt tension sensor may misclassify the size of the front passenger seat occupant.
