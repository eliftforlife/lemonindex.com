What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10211909: “Some 2021-2022 Mustang vehicles built on or before 21-Apr-2022 and equipped with a 2.3L EcoBoost engine may exhibit an…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10210471: “Some 2017-2020 F-150, 2018-2022 Expedition/Navigator/Mustang and 2019-2022 Ranger vehicles equipped with a 10R80…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Ford Mustang reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 62; 100 is average, lower is better). 2022 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. The Ford Mustang year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2022 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), engine (14%) and steering (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Ford Mustang have?

9 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Ford Mustang get?

18 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Ford Mustang safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Ford Mustang have?

2.3L 4-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford Mustang year is better than the 2022?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V689000 (electrical), 25V455000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 25V442000 (back over prevention) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Does the A/C blow cold and the heat work? Ask whether the A/C compressor or condenser was replaced. (12 owners named a a/c & heat repair bill; the middle one was $2,200.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 119 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Clunk/pop type”?

How to read this chart

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 795 YouTube videos about the Ford Mustang, 1994 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
