Questions to ask the seller

6 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 21V081000 (airbags), 21E013000 (airbags) and 19V001000 (airbags) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (17 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $895.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 35 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some 2010 models there may be many symptoms such as an engine rattle, malfunction light, lack”?

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ford Mustang reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). 2010 is one of the better years of a model that draws 1.4× the average car's complaints.

What are the most common 2010 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (22%), airbags (16%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ford Mustang have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ford Mustang get?

19 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Ford Mustang problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 16,000 and 66,000 miles (27,000–107,000 km) (the middle half of the 93 complaints, of all 261 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2010 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $3,050 to $3,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Ford Mustang have?

4.0L 6-cyl, 4.6L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Ford Mustang year than the 2010?

Not nearby: the 2010 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ford Mustang years within 3 model years of it.
