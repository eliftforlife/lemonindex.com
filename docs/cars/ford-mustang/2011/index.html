Quick answers

Is the 2011 Ford Mustang reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 276; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Mustang year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (78%), engine (8%) and speed control (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Ford Mustang have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Ford Mustang get?

17 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
