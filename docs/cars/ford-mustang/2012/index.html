How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Ford Mustang reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 247; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ford Mustang year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (53%), airbags (11%) and engine (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Ford Mustang have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Ford Mustang get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Ford Mustang safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2012 Ford Mustang problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,100 and 56,000 miles (6,600–90,000 km) (the middle half of the 307 complaints, of all 469 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 53% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 to $3,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Ford Mustang have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford Mustang year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V081000 (airbags), 21E013000 (airbags) and 19V001000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (17 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $895.)
  6. Ford sent dealers 62 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Illuminated mil or safety stop now message, or a no”?
