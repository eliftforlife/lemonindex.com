Quick answers

Is the 2012 Ford Mustang reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 247; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ford Mustang year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Ford Mustang problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (54%), airbags (11%) and engine (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Ford Mustang have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Ford Mustang get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Ford Mustang safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Ford Mustang problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 4,100 and 56,000 miles. The middle report was at 23,000 miles, from 307 complaints.

Does the 2012 Ford Mustang have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 54% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Ford Mustang cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Ford Mustang have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 5.0L 8-cyl.

What Ford Mustang year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2021 and 2022.
