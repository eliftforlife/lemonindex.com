Quick answers.

Is the Mazda 626 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. Fewest complaints: 2000.

Is the Mazda 626 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2001: Lemon Index 138; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2000 drew the fewest complaints and 2001 the most.

What are the best Mazda 626 years to buy?

Only 2 Mazda 626 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2000 drew the fewest complaints (about average).

What Mazda 626 years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Mazda 626 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Mazda 626 have?

3 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2000 (2), 2001 (1). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda 626 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Mazda 626 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Mazda 626, 1998 to 2002, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2001. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
