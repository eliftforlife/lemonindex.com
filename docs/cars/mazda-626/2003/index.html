Quick answers

Is the 2003 Mazda 626 reliable?

We can't score it: only 329 were sold in the U.S. in 2003 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 6 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2003 Mazda 626 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (17%), engine (17%) and brakes (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Mazda 626 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

Does the 2003 Mazda 626 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

What Mazda 626 year is better than the 2003?

Nearby, the 2000 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about average).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Mazda sent dealers 22 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some customers may complain of signs of oil on the struts or shock absorbers”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 5 Mazda 626 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Mazda 626, 1998 to 2002, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2001. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
