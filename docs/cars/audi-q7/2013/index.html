Recalls 3

  • equipment 2023

    The Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) listed on the certification label is incorrect, which can allow the vehicle to be overloaded.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2017

    These gasoline-powered vehicles have a fuel pump flange that may crack, allowing fuel to leak.

  • engine 2014

    In the affected vehicles, a check valve in the engine may become contaminated with plastic debris and allow engine oil to leak into the brake booster.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 15; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2013 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (20%), lights (20%) and engine (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Audi Q7 get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
