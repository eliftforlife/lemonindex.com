Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V086000 (equipment), 17V298000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 14V516000 (engine) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Audi sent dealers 1239 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “When selecting reverse gear, the picture of rearview camera fails to appear, sporadically”?

Problems Audi told dealers about

Audi sent dealers 1239 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • When selecting reverse gear, the picture of rearview camera fails to appear, sporadically
  • Presence of coolant loss inside electrical connector, causes engine warning light to illuminate
  • The oil filter, for some vehicles, are too short, (should be 155 (=/-1) mm long) for installation

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Recalls 3

  • Equipment · recall issued 2023

    The Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) listed on the certification label is incorrect, which can allow the vehicle to be overloaded.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2017

    These gasoline-powered vehicles have a fuel pump flange that may crack, allowing fuel to leak.

  • Engine · recall issued 2014 3.0L engines only

    In the affected vehicles, a check valve in the engine may become contaminated with plastic debris and allow engine oil to leak into the brake booster.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a fifth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 15; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2013 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (20%), lights (20%) and engine (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Audi Q7 get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2013 Audi Q7 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 51,000 and 102,000 miles (82,000–164,000 km) (the middle half of the 24 complaints, of all 43 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2013 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2013 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2013 Audi Q7 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2013 Audi Q7 cost in fuel a year?

About $4,300 to $4,400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Audi Q7 have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Audi Q7 year than the 2013?

Not nearby: the 2013 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Audi Q7 years within 3 model years of it.
