Problems Audi told dealers about

Audi sent dealers 1144 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Steering wheel shimmy, vibration and/or oscillation
  • The customer has various complaints about the RSE system and would like to update the software…
  • This bulletin contains repair instructions for an oil leak on the rear differential

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q7 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2019 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (26%), electrical (22%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Audi Q7 have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Audi Q7 get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Audi Q7 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2019 Audi Q7 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Audi Q7 cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Audi Q7 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Audi Q7 year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2019 and 2017.
