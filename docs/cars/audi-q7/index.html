Quick answers.

Is the Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.6× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2019.

Is the Audi Q7 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 63; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2018.

What are the best Audi Q7 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2019, 2017.

What Audi Q7 years should you stay away from?

No Audi Q7 year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Audi Q7 have?

26 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2017 (7), 2019 (4), 2007 (3). Each year's page lists them.

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
