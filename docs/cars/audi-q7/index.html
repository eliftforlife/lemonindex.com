Quick answers.

Is the Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2013.

Is the Audi Q7 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 63; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2018 the most.

What are the best Audi Q7 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2013, 2019, 2017. 2013 drew the fewest.

What Audi Q7 years should you stay away from?

No Audi Q7 year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2018 (better than average, Lemon Index 80).

How many recalls does the Audi Q7 have?

54 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2017 (7), 2020 (5), 2011 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi Q7 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
