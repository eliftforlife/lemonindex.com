How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Recalls 3

  • Equipment · recall issued 2023

    The Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) listed on the certification label is incorrect, which can allow the vehicle to be overloaded.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2018

    These vehicles were previously repaired with an interim repair of installing butyl tape to the fuel pump flange, until replacement remedy parts became available.

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2016

    The fuel pump flange on the affected vehicles may crack, allowing fuel to leak.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Audi Q7 reliable?

We can't score it: only 7,976 were sold in the U.S. in 2010 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2010 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, diesel (57%), fuel system (43%) and engine (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Audi Q7 get?

15 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2010 Audi Q7 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Audi Q7 cost in fuel a year?

About $4,700 to $5,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Audi Q7 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 4.2L 8-cyl.

What Audi Q7 year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V086000 (equipment), 18V818000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 16V660000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
