Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V086000 (equipment), 18V818000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 16V660000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Audi Q7 reliable?

We can't score it: only 7,976 were sold in the U.S. in 2010 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2010 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, diesel (57%), fuel or ev drive system (43%) and engine (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Audi Q7 get?

15 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2010 Audi Q7 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Audi Q7 cost in fuel a year?

About $4700 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Audi Q7 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 4.2L 8-cyl.

What Audi Q7 year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (6.7× fewer complaints than average).

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
