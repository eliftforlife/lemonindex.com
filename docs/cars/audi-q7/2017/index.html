  • steering 2016

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 69; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q7 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (16%), steering (14%) and fuel or ev drive system (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Audi Q7 have?

7 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Audi Q7 get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
