Quick answers

Is the 2018 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q7 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2018 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (21%), electrical (15%) and wheels (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Audi Q7 get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
