Quick answers

Which Audi Q7 years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Audi Q7 years are 2013, 2019, 2018.

What are common Audi Q7 transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, shudder, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Audi Q7 years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Audi Q7 years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2017, 2018, 2019.
