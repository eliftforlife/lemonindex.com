Quick answers

Is the 2007 Audi Q7 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 74; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q7 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2007 Audi Q7 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), stability control (15%) and seats (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Audi Q7 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Audi Q7 get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Audi Q7 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 38,800 and 116,200 miles. The middle report was at 77,400 miles, from 70 complaints.

Does the 2007 Audi Q7 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Audi Q7 cost in fuel a year?

About $5000 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Audi Q7 have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 4.2L 8-cyl.

What Audi Q7 year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2019 and 2017.
