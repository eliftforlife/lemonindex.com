Quick answers

Is the 2017 GMC Sierra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 76; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Sierra year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2017 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (31%), transmission & drivetrain (31%) and engine (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 GMC Sierra have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 GMC Sierra get?

17 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 GMC Sierra safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 GMC Sierra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 13,300 and 65,400 miles. The middle report was at 37,000 miles, from 222 complaints.

Does the 2017 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 31% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 GMC Sierra have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Sierra year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
