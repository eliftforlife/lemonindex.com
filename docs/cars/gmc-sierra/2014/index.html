What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10112819: “This informational bulletin provides information on Identifying Non-GM (Aftermarket) Engine Calibrations for Gasoline…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10113482: “This preliminary information bulletin provides a procedure to reprogram the ECM/TCM due to upshifts that clunk when the…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 GMC Sierra reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 166; 100 is average, lower is better). 2014 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car. The GMC Sierra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010. This score is the whole line: 4% of its complaints that year are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Sierra HD page, by engine.

Is this score valid for a 2014 Sierra HD or a diesel?

No. Automakers report 2500/3500 sales together with the half-ton, so we can't give heavy-duty trucks their own per-truck score. The 2014 Sierra HD page shows their owner complaints split by engine (from each complaint's VIN), and each diesel has its own page.

What are the most common 2014 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (47%), steering (13%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 GMC Sierra have?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V245000 (seat belts), 19V761000 (brakes) and 19V645000 (brakes) done? It has 21 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (30 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $4,500.)
  7. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (21 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
