2009 GMC Sierra: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 GMC Sierra drew 2.8× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 35 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 111,842 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Sierra complaints that listed a mileage came before 62,000 miles. Most came between 35,000 miles and 91,400 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 49,900 miles and 120,500 miles.
