GMC Sierra transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (123 complaints), 2018 (114 complaints), 2019 (100 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2010, 2025, 2013.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2022 2026

    The transmission control valve may fail and cause the rear wheels to lock up.

  • 2020 2020

    One of the driveshaft joints may not have been properly welded, causing the driveshaft to separate while the vehicle is moving.

  • 2014 2014

    The subject vehicles may have a transmission oil cooler line that is not securely seated in the fitting.

  • 2014 2014

    In the affected vehicles, an electrical signal short may cause the transfer case to shift to neutral without the driver's input.

Quick answers

Which GMC Sierra years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst GMC Sierra years are 2017, 2018, 2019.

What are common GMC Sierra transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, shudder, lost power while driving.

Which GMC Sierra years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the GMC Sierra years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2010, 2025, 2013.
