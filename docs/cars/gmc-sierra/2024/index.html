Quick answers

Is the 2024 GMC Sierra reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.9× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 190; 100 is average). The best GMC Sierra year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2024 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (40%), transmission & drivetrain (22%) and electrical (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 GMC Sierra have?

5 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 GMC Sierra get?

15 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 GMC Sierra safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2024 GMC Sierra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 5,800 and 41,000 miles. The middle report was at 18,900 miles, from 88 complaints.

Does the 2024 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 GMC Sierra have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Sierra year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
