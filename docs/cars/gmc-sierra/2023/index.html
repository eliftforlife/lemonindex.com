How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2023 GMC Sierra reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about one and a half times the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 158; 100 is average). 2023 is one of the weaker years of a model that usually draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car. The GMC Sierra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010. This score is the whole line: 11% of its complaints that year are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Sierra HD page, by engine.

Is this score valid for a 2023 Sierra HD or a diesel?

No. Automakers report 2500/3500 sales together with the half-ton, so we can't give heavy-duty trucks their own per-truck score. The 2023 Sierra HD page shows their owner complaints split by engine (from each complaint's VIN), and each diesel has its own page.

What are the most common 2023 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (39%), transmission & drivetrain (23%) and body structure (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 GMC Sierra have?

3 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 GMC Sierra get?

15 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 GMC Sierra safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2023 GMC Sierra problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 12,000 and 49,000 miles (20,000–79,000 km) (the middle half of the 57 complaints, of all 553 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2023 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2023 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 23% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $3,550 to $5,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 GMC Sierra have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Sierra year is better than the 2023?

Nearby, the 2020 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V274000 (engine), 24V674000 (brakes) and 22V903000 (lights) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into loss of motive power due to engine failure post recall remedy. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
