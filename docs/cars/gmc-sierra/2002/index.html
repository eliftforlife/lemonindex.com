2002 GMC Sierra: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 GMC Sierra drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 192 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 202,045 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Sierra complaints that listed a mileage came before 66,600 miles. Most came between 38,000 miles and 106,000 miles.

The top problem area, service brakes, hydraulic, usually showed up between 35,000 miles and 86,000 miles.
