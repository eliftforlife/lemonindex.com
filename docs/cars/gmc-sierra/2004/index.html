Quick answers

Is the 2004 GMC Sierra reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 114; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Sierra year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2004 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (40%), brakes (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 GMC Sierra have?

15 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 GMC Sierra get?

13 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 GMC Sierra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 37,200 and 90,000 miles. The middle report was at 58,100 miles, from 492 complaints.

Does the 2004 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $4950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 GMC Sierra have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What GMC Sierra year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
