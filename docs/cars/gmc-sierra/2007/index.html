2007 GMC Sierra: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 GMC Sierra drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 68 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 208,243 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the GMC Sierra complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 26,800 miles and 104,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 39,700 miles and 115,000 miles.
