Quick answers

Is the 2010 GMC Sierra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 15; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (19%), brakes (14%) and body structure (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 GMC Sierra have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 GMC Sierra get?

14 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 GMC Sierra problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,500 and 90,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 145 complaints.

Does the 2010 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 GMC Sierra have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.
