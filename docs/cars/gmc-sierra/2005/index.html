Quick answers

Is the 2005 GMC Sierra reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 66; 100 is average, lower is better). The best GMC Sierra year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2005 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (25%), steering (24%) and engine (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 GMC Sierra have?

14 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 GMC Sierra get?

11 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
