Quick answers.

Is the GMC Sierra a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 87; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best GMC Sierra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2012, 2013.

What GMC Sierra years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015, 2014, 2022.

How many recalls does the GMC Sierra have?

218 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2014 (21), 2020 (16), 2000 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common GMC Sierra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
