Questions

Quick answers.

Is the GMC Sierra reliable?

It&#39;s mixed. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2014, 2015 and 2022. This score is the whole line: 14% of its complaints are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Sierra HD page, by engine.

Is the GMC Sierra a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 87; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best GMC Sierra years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2012, 2013. 2010 drew the fewest.

What GMC Sierra years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (worse than average, Lemon Index 167), 2014 (worse than average, Lemon Index 166, the first year of a new generation), 2022 (worse than average, Lemon Index 161). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the GMC Sierra have?

201 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2014 (21), 2000 (15), 2004 (15). Each year's page lists them, and every GMC Sierra recall is in one list here.

What are the most common GMC Sierra problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 27 GMC Sierra years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 771 YouTube videos about the GMC Sierra, 1999 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
