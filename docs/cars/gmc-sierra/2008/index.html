How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2008 GMC Sierra reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 36; 100 is average, lower is better). The GMC Sierra year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010. This score is the whole line: 22% of its complaints that year are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Sierra HD page, by engine.

Is this score valid for a 2008 Sierra HD or a diesel?

No. Automakers report 2500/3500 sales together with the half-ton, so we can't give heavy-duty trucks their own per-truck score. The 2008 Sierra HD page shows their owner complaints split by engine (from each complaint's VIN), and each diesel has its own page.

What are the most common 2008 GMC Sierra problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (22%), brakes (13%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 GMC Sierra have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 GMC Sierra get?

14 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 GMC Sierra problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 37,000 and 110,000 miles (60,000–177,000 km) (the middle half of the 300 complaints, of all 555 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2008 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2008 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2008 GMC Sierra have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 GMC Sierra cost in fuel a year?

About $3,800 to $5,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 GMC Sierra have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What GMC Sierra year is better than the 2008?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V052000 (airbags), 21V050000 (airbags) and 10V240000 (electrical) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (12 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $1,000.)
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (11 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  7. GMC sent dealers 947 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Rear leaf spring slap or clunk noise (replace spring inserts) *tt updated 10/18/07”?
