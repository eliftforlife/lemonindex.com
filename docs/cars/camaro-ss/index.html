Known issues

  • Piston slap (cold-start knock)Enthusiast outlet

    A knock or tick on cold starts that fades as the engine warms is common on the LS1. The outlet calls it noisy but not harmful when it goes away warm.

    Fix: Usually left alone; a noise that stays when warm needs a leak-down test.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2000–2002)
  • Oil consumptionEnthusiast outlet

    Worn piston ring seals and piston-to-wall clearance can lead to high oil use and blow-by.

    Fix: Check the level between changes; a ring job is the real fix.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2002)
  • Water pump leaksEnthusiast outlet

    The water pump gasket and shaft can leak or fail, often around 100,000 miles.

    Fix: Replace the gasket or the pump.

Known issues

  • AFM lifter failure (L99, disputed)Class action (alleged)

    A pending class action says GM's AFM lifters fail early and names 2014-and-later Camaro SS. Only the automatic L99 has AFM in this generation; no court has found a defect.

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2010–2015)
  • Rocker arm trunnion bearingsTrade outlet

    The factory rocker trunnion needle bearings can walk out and drop needles into the oil, mainly on high-rpm or modified engines with stiffer valve springs.

    Fix: Fit an upgraded trunnion kit when modifying the engine.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2010–2015)

Quick answers

How fast is the Chevrolet Camaro SS 1LE?

The 6th gen (Alpha) Camaro SS 1LE makes 455 hp and 455 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.1 seconds.

What engine does the Chevrolet Camaro SS have?

The 6th gen (Alpha) Camaro SS (2016–2024) uses the GM LT1, a 6.2-liter V8, pushrod, direct injection. Older generations: 4th gen (F-body): GM LS1; 5th gen (Zeta): GM LS3 / L99.

Which Chevrolet Camaro SS engine do YouTube owners like most?

GM LS3 / L99 51/100, GM LS1 46/100, GM LT1 45/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
