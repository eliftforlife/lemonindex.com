Known issues

  • Fuel pump module can overheatRecall

    On 2004–2007 CTS-Vs the fuel pump module's electrical terminals can overheat and melt the flange. Fuel can then leak, and the car can stall.

    Fix: Dealers replace the fuel module and tank jumper harness free (NHTSA 14V-542).

    65 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2004–2007)
  • No mechanical engine pattern foundOwner-reported

    The sources used here document no recurring LS6 or LS2 mechanical failure in the CTS-V. For 2006 GM did fit a stronger rear differential and half-shafts.

Known issues

  • Supercharger rattle at idleMaker bulletin

    GM found the supercharger on 2009–2013 CTS-Vs (and 2012–2013 Camaro ZL1s) can rattle at idle. Left alone, it can ruin the bearing grease and, in the worst case, stop the engine.

    Fix: Dealers replace the supercharger free under a special coverage of 10 years or 120,000 miles.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mention it (2009–2015)
  • Low-rpm buzz on replacement superchargersMaker bulletin

    On manual 2014 CTS-Vs, and earlier ones with a replacement supercharger (solid coupling), a buzz at 800–900 rpm in second or third gear under load is normal, GM told dealers.

    Fix: No repair; GM said not to replace parts for this noise.

Known issues

  • 8-speed automatic shudder and harsh shifts (disputed)Lawsuit

    A class action says GM 8L90 and 8L45 automatics built 2015–2019 can shudder when the fluid absorbs moisture and can shift harshly. GM disputes it; in 2025 an appeals court vacated the class certification.

    Fix: No recall; the court record says dealer workarounds improved drive quality on some cars.

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2016–2019)

Quick answers

How fast is the Cadillac CTS-V?

The 3rd gen CTS-V makes 640 hp and 630 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.6 seconds. Top speed: 200 mph (Cadillac claim).

What engine does the Cadillac CTS-V have?

The 3rd gen CTS-V (2016–2019) uses the GM LT4, a 6.2-liter V-8, supercharged. Older generations: 1st gen: GM LS6 / LS2; 2nd gen: GM LSA.

Which Cadillac CTS-V engine do YouTube owners like most?

GM LSA 52/100, GM LT4 52/100, GM LS6 / LS2 49/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
