Questions6 quick answers

Quick answers.

Is the Mazda Tribute reliable?

Not really. It draws 3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2004. Most complaints: 2001, 2002 and 2003.

Is the Mazda Tribute a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2001-2008: Lemon Index 301; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2004 drew the fewest complaints and 2001 the most.

What are the best Mazda Tribute years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2004. 2004 drew the fewest.

What Mazda Tribute years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2001 (avoid, Lemon Index 505), 2002 (avoid, Lemon Index 449), 2005 (avoid, Lemon Index 200). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Mazda Tribute have?

60 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2001 (15), 2002 (9), 2003 (8). Each year's page lists them, and every Mazda Tribute recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Mazda Tribute problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, transmission & drivetrain and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
