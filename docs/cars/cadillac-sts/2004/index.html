Quick answers

Is the 2004 Cadillac STS reliable?

We can't score it: only 9,484 were sold in the U.S. in 2004 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 5 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2004 Cadillac STS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (20%), electrical (20%) and engine (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Cadillac STS have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

Does the 2004 Cadillac STS have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Cadillac sent dealers 106 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Engine oil leak from front of vehicle”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 7 Cadillac STS years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Cadillac STS, 2005 to 2011, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2009. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Complaint Score counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
