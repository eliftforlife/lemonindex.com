Quick answers

Is the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 5.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 17; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Tahoe year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (47%), electrical (20%) and visibility (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe get?

17 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 50,000 and 96,200 miles. The middle report was at 74,200 miles, from 202 complaints.

Does the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Tahoe year is better than the 2010?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2020.
