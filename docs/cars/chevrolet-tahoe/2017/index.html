Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 48; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Tahoe year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), electrical (14%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
