Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 48; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Tahoe year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), electrical (14%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 84,500 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 75 complaints.

Does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

5.3L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Tahoe year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2020.
