2007 Chevrolet Tahoe: problems and owner complaints

The 2007 Chevrolet Tahoe drew 1.1× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 148 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 146,259 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Tahoe complaints that listed a mileage came before 77,000 miles. Most came between 48,000 miles and 112,500 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 63,800 miles and 130,000 miles.
