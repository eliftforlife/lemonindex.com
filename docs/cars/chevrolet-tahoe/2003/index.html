2003 Chevrolet Tahoe: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Chevrolet Tahoe drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 120 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 199,065 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Tahoe complaints that listed a mileage came before 74,000 miles. Most came between 44,000 miles and 110,400 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 52,200 miles and 93,800 miles.
