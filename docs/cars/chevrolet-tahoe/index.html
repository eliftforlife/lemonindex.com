Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

Mostly. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2015, 2021 and 2022.

Is the Chevrolet Tahoe a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2015 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Tahoe years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2010, 2020. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Tahoe years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 267, the first year of a new generation), 2021 (worse than average, Lemon Index 179, the first year of a new generation), 2022 (about average, Lemon Index 112). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Tahoe have?

142 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2021 (16), 2015 (14), 2000 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Tahoe problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
