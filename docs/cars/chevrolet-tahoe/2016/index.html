Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 106; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Tahoe year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Tahoe problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (39%), steering (11%) and suspension (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Tahoe have?

8 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Tahoe get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
