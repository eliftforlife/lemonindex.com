Quick answers.

Is the Kia Niro reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2021. Most complaints: 2019 and 2022.

Is the Kia Niro a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Complaint Score 104; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2021 drew the fewest complaints and 2022 the most.

What are the best Kia Niro years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2021, 2018, 2017. 2021 drew the fewest.

What Kia Niro years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2022 (worse than average, Complaint Score 165), 2019 (worse than average, Complaint Score 135). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Niro have?

24 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2017 (5), 2023 (4), 2018 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Kia Niro recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Kia Niro problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 8 Kia Niro years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Kia Niro, 2017 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
