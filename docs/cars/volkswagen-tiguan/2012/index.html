Recalls 3

  • unknown or other 2018

    Modifications made while the vehicles were in an internal evaluation period may cause the affected vehicles to not comply with all of the applicable regulatory requirements.

  • airbags 2015

    In the affected vehicles, debris may contaminate the air bag clock spring, a spiral wound, flat cable that keeps the air bag powered while the steering wheel is being turned.

  • fuel system, gasoline 2014

    When using winterized fuel, in certain conditions bubbles may form in the fuel system which could result in the vehicle stalling.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 131; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Tiguan year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (42%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

3 safety recalls (latest: unknown or other). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

21 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 27,400 and 80,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 116 complaints.

Does the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan cost in fuel a year?

About $3300 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Tiguan year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2015 and 2021.
