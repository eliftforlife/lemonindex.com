Quick answers

Is the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 69; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Tiguan year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (33%), electrical (23%) and steering (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 59,000 miles. The middle report was at 45,000 miles, from 39 complaints.

Does the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan cost in fuel a year?

About $3450 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Tiguan year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2015 and 2021.
