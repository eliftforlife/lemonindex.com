Quick answers

Is the 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 189; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Tiguan year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (42%), electrical (23%) and transmission & drivetrain (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

5 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
