Quick answers

Is the 2013 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Volkswagen Tiguan year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2013 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and fuel or ev drive system (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

3 safety recalls (latest: unknown or other). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
