What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10182515: “Oil consumption test updated to include additional models and model year applicability”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10180053: “Volkswagen is providing an Engine and Transmission Control Module (ECM/TCM) software update to address elevated…”)

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 66; 100 is average, lower is better). The Volkswagen Tiguan year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2014.

What are the most common 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (23%), automatic emergency braking (19%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

3 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,200 and 66,000 miles (6,800–106,000 km) (the middle half of the 28 complaints, of all 141 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2020 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan cost in fuel a year?

About $2,550 to $2,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Volkswagen Tiguan year is better than the 2020?

Nearby, the 2021 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 22V226000 (body structure), 20V579000 (seat belts) and 20V505000 (seats) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Volkswagen sent dealers 521 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “No Crank/No Start with P06EF and/or P06E9 Customer states MIL-on with P0463 Customer states…”?
