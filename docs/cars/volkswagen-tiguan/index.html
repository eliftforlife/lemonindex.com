Quick answers.

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

About average. It draws 1.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2018.

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2022: Lemon Index 114; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints and 2018 the most.

What are the best Volkswagen Tiguan years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2015, 2021. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Volkswagen Tiguan years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2018, 2022, 2011.

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Tiguan have?

60 safety recalls across the 15 model years we track. Most: 2018 (14), 2019 (5), 2021 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
