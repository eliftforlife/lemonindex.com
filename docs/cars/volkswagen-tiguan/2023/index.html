Quick answers

Is the 2023 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.3× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 77; 100 is average). The best Volkswagen Tiguan year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2023 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (36%), electrical (19%) and automatic emergency braking (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

1 safety recall (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
