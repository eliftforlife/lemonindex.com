Quick answers

Is the 2014 Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 50; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Volkswagen Tiguan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (47%), steering (20%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Volkswagen Tiguan have?

3 safety recalls (latest: unknown or other). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Volkswagen Tiguan get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
