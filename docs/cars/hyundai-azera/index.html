Quick answers.

Is the Hyundai Azera reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2007, still drew 1.3× the average.

Is the Hyundai Azera a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2006-2007: Lemon Index 143; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2007 drew the fewest complaints and 2006 the most.

What are the best Hyundai Azera years to buy?

Only 2 Hyundai Azera years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2007 drew the fewest complaints (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

What Hyundai Azera years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Hyundai Azera years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Hyundai Azera have?

41 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2007 (9), 2008 (7), 2006 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hyundai Azera problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, lights and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
