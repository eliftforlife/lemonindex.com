How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Hyundai Azera reliable?

We can't score it: only 3,060 were sold in the U.S. in 2017 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 7 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2017 Hyundai Azera problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (43%), body structure (29%) and electrical (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Hyundai Azera have?

1 safety recall (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Hyundai Azera get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2017 Hyundai Azera have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Hyundai Azera cost in fuel a year?

About $2,600 to $2,750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Hyundai Azera have?

3.3L 6-cyl.

What Hyundai Azera year is better than the 2017?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2007 drew the fewest (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 21V619000 (latches/locks/linkages) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Hyundai sent dealers 70 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some Sonata (YF, LF), Sonata Hybrid (YF HEV, LF HEV), Santa Fe (NC), and Azera (HG)…”?
