Quick answers

Is the 2000 Lexus ES reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 59; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus ES year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2000 Lexus ES problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (41%), brakes (19%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Lexus ES have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Lexus ES get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
