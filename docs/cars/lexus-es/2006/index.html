When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 15,000 and 76,000 miles (24,000–122,000 km) (the middle half of the 59 complaints, of all 79 filed so far, that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles (97K km) Right in the range where most owners reported problems.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (5 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $3,500.)
  5. Lexus sent dealers 75 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The purpose of this service bulletin is to provide information regarding the installation of…”?
