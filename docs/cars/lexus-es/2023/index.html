Quick answers

Is the 2023 Lexus ES reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 12 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2023 Lexus ES problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (17%), tires (8%) and visibility (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Lexus ES have?

2 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Lexus ES get?

25 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Lexus ES safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 Lexus ES have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2023 Lexus ES cost in fuel a year?

About $1450 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Lexus ES have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Lexus ES year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2012 and 2018.
