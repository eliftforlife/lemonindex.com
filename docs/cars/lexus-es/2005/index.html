Quick answers

Is the 2005 Lexus ES reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 163; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus ES year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2005 Lexus ES problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (53%), speed control (25%) and engine (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Lexus ES have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Lexus ES get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
