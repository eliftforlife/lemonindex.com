Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2005 (64 complaints), 2004 (60 complaints), 2002 (49 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2016, 2017, 2018.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Lexus ES years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Lexus ES years are 2005, 2004, 2002.

What are common Lexus ES transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, hard or jerky shifting, slipping.

Which Lexus ES years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Lexus ES years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2016, 2017, 2018.
