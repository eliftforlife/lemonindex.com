Quick answers

Is the 2020 Lexus ES reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 8.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 12; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2020 Lexus ES problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (40%), electrical (40%) and automatic emergency braking (40%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Lexus ES have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Lexus ES get?

25 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
