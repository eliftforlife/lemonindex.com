Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Lexus ES reliable?

Yes, very. It draws about a quarter as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2020.

Is the Lexus ES a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 27; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2019 the most.

What are the best Lexus ES years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020, 2012, 2018. 2020 drew the fewest.

What Lexus ES years should you stay away from?

No Lexus ES year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2019 (better than average, Lemon Index 50, the first year of a new generation), 2021 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 46), 2010 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 38).

How many recalls does the Lexus ES have?

51 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2007 (5), 2008 (5), 2009 (5). Each year's page lists them, and every Lexus ES recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Lexus ES problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, transmission & drivetrain and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 26 Lexus ES years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 985 YouTube videos about the Lexus ES, 1997 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
