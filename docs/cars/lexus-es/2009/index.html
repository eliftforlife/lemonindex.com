Quick answers

Is the 2009 Lexus ES reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus ES year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2009 Lexus ES problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (55%), electrical (12%) and brakes (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Lexus ES have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Lexus ES get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
