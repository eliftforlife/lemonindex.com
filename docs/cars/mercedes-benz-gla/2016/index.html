Quick answers

Is the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (33%), engine (22%) and electrical (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA get?

27 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA cost in fuel a year?

About $2850 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

2.0L 4-cyl.
