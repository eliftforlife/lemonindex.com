Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mercedes-Benz GLA reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 37; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz GLA year by this measure is 2016.

What are the most common 2018 Mercedes-Benz GLA problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wheels (33%), airbags (33%) and engine (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

6 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz GLA get?

26 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
