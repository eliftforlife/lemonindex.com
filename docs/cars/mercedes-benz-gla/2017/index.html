Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V854000 (body structure), 22V365000 (electrical) and 21V229000 (steering) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 133 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “7G-DCT: Scratching noise when changing gear from D to R when transmission cold”?

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 35; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz GLA year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2016.

What are the most common 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (56%), engine (56%) and transmission & drivetrain (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

7 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA get?

26 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 22% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Mercedes-Benz GLA cost in fuel a year?
