How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz GLA year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2016.

What are the most common 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (44%), airbags (22%) and brakes (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

6 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA get?

26 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 44% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA cost in fuel a year?

About $2,950 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz GLA year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V854000 (body structure), 22V365000 (electrical) and 21V961000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 110 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “7G-DCT: Scratching noise when changing gear from D to R when transmission cold”?
