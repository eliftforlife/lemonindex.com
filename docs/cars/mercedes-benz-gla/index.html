Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz GLA a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2021: Lemon Index 50; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2016 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz GLA years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2016, 2017, 2018.

What Mercedes-Benz GLA years should you stay away from?

No Mercedes-Benz GLA year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz GLA have?

27 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2017 (7), 2018 (6), 2019 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz GLA problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
