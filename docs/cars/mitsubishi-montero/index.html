Quick answers.

Is the Mitsubishi Montero reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.1× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2000, still drew 1.7× the average.

Is the Mitsubishi Montero a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2001: Lemon Index 210; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2000 drew the fewest complaints and 2001 the most.

What are the best Mitsubishi Montero years to buy?

Only 2 Mitsubishi Montero years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2000 drew the fewest complaints (about one and a half times the average car's complaints).

What Mitsubishi Montero years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Mitsubishi Montero years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Mitsubishi Montero have?

11 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2001 (4), 2004 (3), 2000 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mitsubishi Montero problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
