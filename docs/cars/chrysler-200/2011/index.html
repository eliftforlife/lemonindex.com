What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10170408: “No Start After Powertrain Control Module (PCM) Replacement and/or Reprogramming”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10139594: “Part Description: RHR Radio Issue Description: Issue 1: While performing RHR radio reflash (software update), on rare…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V668000 (seat belts), 13V282000 (electrical) and 11V315000 (steering) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  5. Does the A/C blow cold and the heat work? Ask whether the A/C compressor or condenser was replaced. (11 owners named a a/c & heat repair bill; the middle one was $1,200.)
  6. Chrysler sent dealers 136 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “MIL Illumination: P0300, P0302, P0304 or P0306 (X56 Warranty Extension) This bulletin involves…”?
