Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 200 reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2013.

Is the Chrysler 200 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2018: Lemon Index 261; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints and 2015 the most.

What are the best Chrysler 200 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Chrysler 200 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015 (avoid, Lemon Index 655, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (avoid, Lemon Index 224), 2012 (worse than average, Lemon Index 145). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 200 have?

24 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2015 (8), 2013 (5), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler 200 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
