Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler 200 reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.6× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Best year: 2014. Avoid 2012, 2015 and 2016.

Is the Chrysler 200 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2018: Lemon Index 261; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best Chrysler 200 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2011, 2013.

What Chrysler 200 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015, 2016, 2012.

How many recalls does the Chrysler 200 have?

23 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2015 (8), 2013 (5), 2012 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler 200 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
