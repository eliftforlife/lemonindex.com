Quick answers

Which Chrysler 200 years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chrysler 200 years are 2015, 2016, 2013.

What are common Chrysler 200 transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Chrysler 200 years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chrysler 200 years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2014, 2012, 2011.
