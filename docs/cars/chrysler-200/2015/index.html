Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chrysler 200 reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 6.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 655; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 200 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2015 Chrysler 200 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (37%), electrical (36%) and engine (27%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chrysler 200 have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chrysler 200 get?

22 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2015 Chrysler 200 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2015 Chrysler 200 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 13,900 and 71,000 miles. The middle report was at 44,000 miles, from 1,440 complaints.

Does the 2015 Chrysler 200 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 37% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Chrysler 200 cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Chrysler 200 have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chrysler 200 year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2011 and 2013.
