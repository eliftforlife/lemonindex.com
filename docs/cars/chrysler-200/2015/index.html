Quick answers

Is the 2015 Chrysler 200 reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 6.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 655; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 200 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2015 Chrysler 200 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (37%), electrical (36%) and engine (27%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Chrysler 200 have?

8 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Chrysler 200 get?

22 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
