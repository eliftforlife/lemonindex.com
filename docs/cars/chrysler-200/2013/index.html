  • seats 2017

    A component within the occupant restraint controller (ORC) may fail and prevent the active headrests from deploying in the event of a rear-end crash.

  • seat belts 2016

    The Occupant Restraint Control (OCR) module may short circuit, preventing the frontal air bags, seat belt pretensioners, and side air bags from deploying in the event of a crash.

  • engine 2013

    Due to abrasive debris in the balance shaft bearings, these engines may have a loss of engine oil pressure, possibly resulting in an engine stall or engine failure.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Chrysler 200 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 118; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler 200 year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2013 Chrysler 200 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (17%), engine (16%) and airbags (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Chrysler 200 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Chrysler 200 get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
