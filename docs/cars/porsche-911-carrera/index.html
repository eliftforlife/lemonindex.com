The engine: Porsche M96

3.4- and 3.6-liter flat-6, water-cooled

Output300–345 hp
Displacement3.4 liters (to 2001), 3.6 liters (2002–2004)
CoolingFirst water-cooled 911 engine, 4 valves per cylinder
LubricationIntegrated dry sump
Performance engines →

Known issues

  • IMS bearing failureClass action settlement

    A U.S. class action (Eisen v. Porsche) covered 2001–2005 911s whose intermediate shaft bearing failed, which can wreck the engine. LN Engineering cites about 8% failures for the single-row bearing used 2002–2004.

    Fix: Aftermarket retrofit bearings exist; LN Engineering puts the job at about $2,000–3,500.

    13 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2004)
  • Rear main seal leaksOwner-reported

    The rear main seal often weeps oil on M96 and M97 engines. Wikipedia ties some cases to machining and says a later seal fixed most.

    2 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2000–2004)

The engine: Porsche M97

3.6- and 3.8-liter flat-6, water-cooled

Output325–355 hp (SAE)
Torque273–295 lb-ft
Displacement3.6 liters (Carrera), 3.8 liters (Carrera S)

Performance engines →

Known issues

  • Bore scoringOwner-reported

    Scored cylinder walls are most linked to the 3.8-liter Carrera S engine. Renndriver puts a top-end repair at $8,000–15,000.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2005–2008)
  • IMS bearing failureParts seller

    From 2006 the 997 used a larger IMS bearing that fails far less often (about 1% per LN Engineering) but cannot be replaced without splitting the engine. 2005 cars built before Feb. 20, 2005 fell inside the Eisen settlement class.

  • Rear main seal leaksOwner-reported

    Renndriver says the rear main seal often weeps oil on both M96 and M97 engines.

Known issues

    Renndriver reports no widespread engine problems on the 991.2 or the 992.

    REREV says coolant lines to the turbos on early 991.2 cars could leak at the fittings and Porsche updated the lines.

Quick answers

How fast is the Porsche Carrera S?

The 992 Carrera S makes 443 hp and 390 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 2.9 seconds. Top speed: 191 mph.

What engine does the Porsche 911 Carrera have?

The 992 911 Carrera (2020–2025) uses the Porsche 9A2 / 9A2 Evo, a 3.0-liter flat-6, twin-turbo. Older generations: 996: Porsche M96; 997.1: Porsche M97; 997.2: Porsche 9A1; 991.1: Porsche 9A1; 991.2: Porsche 9A2 / 9A2 Evo.
