Quick answers.

Is the Hummer H2 reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2003.

Is the Hummer H2 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2003-2005: Lemon Index 75; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2003 drew the fewest complaints.

What are the best Hummer H2 years to buy?

Only 1 Hummer H2 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2003 drew the fewest complaints (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car).

What Hummer H2 years should you stay away from?

Only 1 Hummer H2 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Hummer H2 have?

23 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2003 (5), 2006 (5), 2004 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Hummer H2 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, visibility and suspension most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
