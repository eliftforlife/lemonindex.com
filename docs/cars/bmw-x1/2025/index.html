Quick answers

Is the 2025 BMW X1 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 17 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 BMW X1 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (53%), transmission & drivetrain (35%) and brakes (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 BMW X1 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 BMW X1 get?

26 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 BMW X1 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 BMW X1 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 35% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 BMW X1 cost in fuel a year?

About $2850 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 BMW X1 have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What BMW X1 year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2017 and 2016.
