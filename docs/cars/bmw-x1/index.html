Quick answers.

Is the BMW X1 reliable?

About average. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2013. Most complaints: 2014 and 2018.

Is the BMW X1 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 89; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2013 drew the fewest complaints and 2014 the most.

What are the best BMW X1 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2017, 2016. 2013 drew the fewest.

What BMW X1 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014 (worse than average, Lemon Index 162), 2018 (worse than average, Lemon Index 117). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the BMW X1 have?

43 safety recalls across the 13 model years we track. Most: 2014 (6), 2023 (6), 2013 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common BMW X1 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, brakes and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
