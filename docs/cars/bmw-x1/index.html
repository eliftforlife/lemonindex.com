Quick answers.

Is the BMW X1 reliable?

About average. It draws 1.1× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2013. Avoid 2014 and 2018.

Is the BMW X1 a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2022: Lemon Index 89; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2013 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best BMW X1 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2013, 2017, 2016.

What BMW X1 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2018.

How many recalls does the BMW X1 have?

32 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2014 (6), 2023 (6), 2013 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common BMW X1 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, brakes and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
