Quick answers

Is the 2013 BMW X1 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 57; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2013 BMW X1 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (56%), brakes (12%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 BMW X1 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 BMW X1 get?

21 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
