Quick answers

Is the 2014 BMW X1 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 162; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X1 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2014 BMW X1 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (73%), electrical (8%) and body structure (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 BMW X1 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 BMW X1 get?

21 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2014 BMW X1 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 103,000 miles. The middle report was at 69,400 miles, from 50 complaints.

Does the 2014 BMW X1 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 BMW X1 cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 BMW X1 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW X1 year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2017 and 2016.
