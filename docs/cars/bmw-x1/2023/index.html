Quick answers

Is the 2023 BMW X1 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.8× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 280; 100 is average). The best BMW X1 year by this measure is 2013.

What are the most common 2023 BMW X1 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (44%), stability control (44%) and brakes (31%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 BMW X1 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 BMW X1 get?

28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 BMW X1 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2023 BMW X1 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 BMW X1 cost in fuel a year?

About $2850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 BMW X1 have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What BMW X1 year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2017 and 2016.
