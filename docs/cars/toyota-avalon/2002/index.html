2002 Toyota Avalon: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Toyota Avalon drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 31 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 69,029 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Avalon complaints that listed a mileage came before 91,000 miles. Most came between 46,200 miles and 148,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 64,500 miles and 172,500 miles.
