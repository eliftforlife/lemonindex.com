Quick answers

Is the 2005 Toyota Avalon reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 100; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Avalon year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2005 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (32%), transmission & drivetrain (32%) and steering (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Toyota Avalon have?

7 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Toyota Avalon get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Toyota Avalon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 18,300 and 85,000 miles. The middle report was at 47,800 miles, from 180 complaints.

Does the 2005 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 32% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Toyota Avalon have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2016 and 2015.
