Quick answers

Is the 2015 Toyota Avalon reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 42; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Avalon year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2015 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), automatic emergency braking (27%) and electrical (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Toyota Avalon have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Toyota Avalon get?

24 to 40 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
