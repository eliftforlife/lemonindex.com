Quick answers

Is the 2004 Toyota Avalon reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 18 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2004 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (33%), speed control (28%) and transmission & drivetrain (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Toyota Avalon have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Toyota Avalon get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Toyota Avalon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 36,000 and 110,700 miles. The middle report was at 67,900 miles, from 114 complaints.

Does the 2004 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Toyota Avalon have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2016 and 2015.
