Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V024000 (airbags), 15V728000 (automatic emergency braking) and 14V715000 (suspension) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into brake actuator valve wear. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  6. Toyota sent dealers 462 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “While a vehicle is in inventory, the battery is subject to conditions that can reduce its…”?

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Toyota Avalon reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 104; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Avalon year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2014 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (24%), electrical (10%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Toyota Avalon have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Toyota Avalon get?

24 to 40 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Toyota Avalon safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Toyota Avalon problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 5,000 and 45,000 miles (8,000–72,000 km) (the middle half of the 75 complaints, of all 114 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $1,600 to $2,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Toyota Avalon have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
