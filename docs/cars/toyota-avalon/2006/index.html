Quick answers

Is the 2006 Toyota Avalon reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 200; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Avalon year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2006 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (39%), transmission & drivetrain (26%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Toyota Avalon have?

7 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Toyota Avalon get?

23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
