How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Toyota Avalon reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 88; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Avalon year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2017.

What are the most common 2010 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (43%), electrical (11%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Toyota Avalon have?

7 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Toyota Avalon get?

23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Toyota Avalon problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,000 and 58,000 miles (1,600–93,000 km) (the middle half of the 35 complaints, of all 46 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $2,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Toyota Avalon have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2012 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2017 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V396000 (seats), 14V743000 (seats) and 13V014000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (10 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $2,500.)
  6. Toyota sent dealers 319 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some customers may encounter bluetooth connectivity concerns such as: difficulty pairing the…”?
