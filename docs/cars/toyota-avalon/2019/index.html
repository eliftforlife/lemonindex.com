Quick answers

Is the 2019 Toyota Avalon reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 153; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Avalon year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2019 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (30%), engine (13%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Toyota Avalon have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Toyota Avalon get?

25 to 44 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Toyota Avalon safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Toyota Avalon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 2,400 and 12,000 miles. The middle report was at 5,200 miles, from 31 complaints.

Does the 2019 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $1450 to $2550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Toyota Avalon have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2016 and 2015.
