Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Avalon reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.2× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2012. Avoid 2006, 2007 and 2008.

Is the Toyota Avalon a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best Toyota Avalon years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2016, 2002.

What Toyota Avalon years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008, 2006, 2007.

How many recalls does the Toyota Avalon have?

76 safety recalls across the 20 model years we track. Most: 2005 (7), 2006 (7), 2009 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Avalon problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
