Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Avalon reliable?

Yes. It draws somewhat fewer complaints than the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2017. Most complaints: 2006, 2007 and 2008.

Is the Toyota Avalon a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 81; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2008 the most.

What are the best Toyota Avalon years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2017, 2016, 2002. 2017 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Avalon years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2008 (avoid, Lemon Index 287), 2006 (avoid, Lemon Index 200), 2007 (worse than average, Lemon Index 177). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Avalon have?

81 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2005 (7), 2006 (7), 2009 (7). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Avalon recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Avalon problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 20 Toyota Avalon years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 793 YouTube videos about the Toyota Avalon, 2000 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2019. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
