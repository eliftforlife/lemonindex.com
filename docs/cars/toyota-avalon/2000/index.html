Quick answers

Is the 2000 Toyota Avalon reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Avalon year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2000 Toyota Avalon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (20%), brakes (17%) and airbags (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Toyota Avalon have?

2 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Toyota Avalon get?

22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Toyota Avalon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 41,900 and 114,200 miles. The middle report was at 83,800 miles, from 82 complaints.

Does the 2000 Toyota Avalon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Toyota Avalon cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Toyota Avalon have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Avalon year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2016 and 2015.
