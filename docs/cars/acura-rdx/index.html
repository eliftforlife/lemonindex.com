Quick answers.

Is the Acura RDX reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2018. Avoid 2019, 2020 and 2021.

Is the Acura RDX a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 105; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2018 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Acura RDX years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2018, 2016, 2013.

What Acura RDX years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2021.

How many recalls does the Acura RDX have?

36 safety recalls across the 14 model years we track. Most: 2019 (5), 2020 (5), 2007 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Acura RDX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, airbags and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
