Acura RDX transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2017 (18 complaints), 2019 (13 complaints), 2020 (8 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2014, 2015, 2013.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2021 2021

    A component in the right or left front driveshaft may have been improperly heat-treated, which could result in driveshaft failure.

  • 2013 2013

    During sub-freezing temperatures, the brake-shift interlock blocking mechanism may become slow and allow the gear selector to be moved from the Park position without pressing the brake pedal.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Acura RDX years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Acura RDX years are 2017, 2019, 2020.

What are common Acura RDX transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, delay or hesitation, shudder.

Which Acura RDX years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Acura RDX years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2014, 2015, 2013.
