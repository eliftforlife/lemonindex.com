Quick answers

Is the 2014 Acura RDX reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura RDX year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2014 Acura RDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (70%), suspension (7%) and speed control (5%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Acura RDX have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Acura RDX get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Acura RDX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Acura RDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,000 and 88,800 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 56 complaints.

Does the 2014 Acura RDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Acura RDX cost in fuel a year?

About $3450 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Acura RDX have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Acura RDX year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2013.
