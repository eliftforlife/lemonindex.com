Quick answers

Is the 2007 Acura RDX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 10 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Acura RDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (30%), equipment & accessories (30%) and visibility (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Acura RDX have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Acura RDX get?

19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Acura RDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 36,100 and 130,500 miles. The middle report was at 68,100 miles, from 33 complaints.

Does the 2007 Acura RDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Acura RDX cost in fuel a year?

About $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Acura RDX have?

2.3L 4-cyl.

What Acura RDX year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2013.
