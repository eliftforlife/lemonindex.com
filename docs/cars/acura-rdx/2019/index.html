Quick answers

Is the 2019 Acura RDX reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 274; 100 is average, lower is better). The Acura RDX year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2019 Acura RDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (38%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Acura RDX have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Acura RDX get?

23 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Acura RDX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Acura RDX problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 3,200 and 30,000 miles (5,100–49,000 km) (the middle half of the 129 complaints, of all 440 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Acura RDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Acura RDX cost in fuel a year?

About $3,300 to $3,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Acura RDX have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Acura RDX year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V332000 (airbags), 23V858000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 23V158000 (seat belts) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Acura sent dealers 223 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Engineering request for investigation - American Honda Motor Co., Inc”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 592 YouTube videos about the Acura RDX, 2007 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
