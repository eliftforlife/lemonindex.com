    During sub-freezing temperatures, the brake-shift interlock blocking mechanism may become slow and allow the gear selector to be moved from the Park position without pressing the brake pedal.

Quick answers

Is the 2013 Acura RDX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura RDX year by this measure is 2018.

What are the most common 2013 Acura RDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (62%), electrical (8%) and steering (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Acura RDX have?

3 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Acura RDX get?

22 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2013 Acura RDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 13,000 and 76,000 miles. The middle report was at 47,500 miles, from 39 complaints.

Does the 2013 Acura RDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 6% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Acura RDX cost in fuel a year?

About $3450 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Acura RDX have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Acura RDX year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2016 and 2013.
