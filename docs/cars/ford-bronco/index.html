Quick answers.

Is the Ford Bronco reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.3× the average car's complaints. Even its best year, 2022, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2021.

Is the Ford Bronco a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2021-2022: Lemon Index 227; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2022 and the worst is 2021.

What are the best Ford Bronco years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2022, 2021.

What Ford Bronco years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2021, 2022.

How many recalls does the Ford Bronco have?

96 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2022 (23), 2021 (20), 2025 (19). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ford Bronco problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
