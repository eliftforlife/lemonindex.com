Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Ford Bronco reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 189; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Ford Bronco problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (23%), engine (13%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Ford Bronco have?

23 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Ford Bronco get?

15 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Ford Bronco safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2022 Ford Bronco problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 6,000 and 39,000 miles (9,700–63,000 km) (the middle half of the 49 complaints, of all 275 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2022 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2022 Ford Bronco have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Ford Bronco cost in fuel a year?

About $3,000 to $4,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Ford Bronco have?

2.3L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Ford Bronco year than the 2022?

Not nearby: the 2022 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ford Bronco years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V468000 (engine), 26V403000 (body structure) and 26V299000 (body structure) done? It has 23 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Ford sent dealers 281 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Customers who neutral (4-wheel flat) tow 2021-2022 Broncos equipped with an electronic brake…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Ford Bronco, 2021 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
