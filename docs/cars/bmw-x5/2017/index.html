Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V608000 (engine), 21V907000 (fuel system, diesel) and 21V586000 (fuel system, diesel) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into high pressure fuel pump failure. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  6. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work? (7 owners named a fuel system repair bill; the middle one was $12,000.)
  7. BMW sent dealers 300 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “TICKING NOISE FROM THE FRONT AXLE WHEN CORNERING: The customer may complain of a metallic…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10163991: “N63T ENGINE FUEL INJECTORS: LIMITED WARRANTY EXTENSION TO 10 YEARS/120000 MILES This Service Information Bulletin…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10146536: “?automatic transmission driveability complaints”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10146812: “Check control message: no oil pressure”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 BMW X5 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 50; 100 is average, lower is better). The BMW X5 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2017 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (19%), engine (19%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 BMW X5 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 BMW X5 get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 BMW X5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 BMW X5 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 16,000 and 57,000 miles (26,000–92,000 km) (the middle half of the 34 complaints, of all 75 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2017 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2017 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $3,300 to $5,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 BMW X5 have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2017?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.
