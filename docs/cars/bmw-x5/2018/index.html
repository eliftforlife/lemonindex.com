Quick answers

Is the 2018 BMW X5 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X5 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2018 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel or ev drive system (21%), transmission & drivetrain (16%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 BMW X5 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 BMW X5 get?

16 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
