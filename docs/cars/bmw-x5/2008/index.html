Quick answers

Is the 2008 BMW X5 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 19 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2008 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (37%), fuel system, gasoline (37%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 BMW X5 have?

10 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 BMW X5 get?

16 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
