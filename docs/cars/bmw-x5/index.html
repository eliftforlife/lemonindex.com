Questions

Quick answers.

Is the BMW X5 reliable?

Yes. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2010.

Is the BMW X5 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2011 the most.

What are the best BMW X5 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2016, 2015. 2010 drew the fewest.

What BMW X5 years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011 (about average, Lemon Index 95), 2012 (better than average, Lemon Index 81), 2019 (better than average, Lemon Index 74, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the BMW X5 have?

197 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2010 (19), 2009 (16), 2011 (15). Each year's page lists them, and every BMW X5 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common BMW X5 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 24 BMW X5 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 781 YouTube videos about the BMW X5, 2000 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
