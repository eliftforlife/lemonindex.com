Quick answers

Is the 2012 BMW X5 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 81; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X5 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (36%), transmission & drivetrain (26%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 BMW X5 have?

10 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 BMW X5 get?

14 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2012 BMW X5 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 48,600 and 90,000 miles. The middle report was at 67,800 miles, from 173 complaints.

Does the 2012 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 26% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $4200 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 BMW X5 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2015.
