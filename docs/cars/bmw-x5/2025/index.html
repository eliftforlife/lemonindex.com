Quick answers

Is the 2025 BMW X5 reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.4× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 136; 100 is average). The best BMW X5 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2025 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are stability control (29%), brakes (27%) and brakes (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 BMW X5 have?

5 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 BMW X5 get?

15 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 BMW X5 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2025 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 BMW X5 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2015.
