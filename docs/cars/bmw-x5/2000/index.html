Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V748000 (airbags), 03V001000 (brakes) and 02V194000 (brakes) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (11 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $3,000.)
  6. BMW sent dealers 78 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Customers may complain about erratic/harsh shifting and the transmission light is illuminated…”?

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2000 BMW X5 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 14 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2000 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (21%), transmission & drivetrain (14%) and visibility (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 BMW X5 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 BMW X5 get?

14 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2000 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $5,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 BMW X5 have?

4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2002 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better).
