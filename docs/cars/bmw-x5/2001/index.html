Quick answers

Is the 2001 BMW X5 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 142; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW X5 year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2001 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (30%), body structure (13%) and electrical (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 BMW X5 have?

12 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 BMW X5 get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 BMW X5 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 56,700 and 115,000 miles. The middle report was at 80,200 miles, from 95 complaints.

Does the 2001 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2001 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $5000 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 BMW X5 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 4.4L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2001?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2015.
