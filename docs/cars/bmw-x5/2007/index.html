Quick answers

Is the 2007 BMW X5 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 10 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 BMW X5 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (30%), parking brake (20%) and electrical (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 BMW X5 have?

13 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 BMW X5 get?

16 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 BMW X5 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 49,200 and 105,500 miles. The middle report was at 81,700 miles, from 80 complaints.

Does the 2007 BMW X5 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 30% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 BMW X5 cost in fuel a year?

About $4400 to $5000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 BMW X5 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl.

What BMW X5 year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2016 and 2015.
