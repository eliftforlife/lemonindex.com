Quick answers

Is the 2021 Jeep Renegade reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Renegade year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2021 Jeep Renegade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (35%), automatic emergency braking (21%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Jeep Renegade have?

2 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Jeep Renegade get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Jeep Renegade safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Jeep Renegade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Jeep Renegade cost in fuel a year?

About $2400 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Jeep Renegade have?

1.3L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Renegade year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2022 and 2020.
