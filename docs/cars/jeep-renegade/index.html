Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Renegade reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2015, 2016 and 2017.

Is the Jeep Renegade a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2015-2022: Complaint Score 210; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints and 2015 the most.

What are the best Jeep Renegade years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2022, 2020. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Jeep Renegade years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2015 (avoid, Complaint Score 428), 2017 (avoid, Complaint Score 328), 2016 (avoid, Complaint Score 246). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Jeep Renegade have?

24 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2015 (4), 2019 (4), 2017 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Jeep Renegade recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Jeep Renegade problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 7 Jeep Renegade years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Jeep Renegade, 2015 to 2022, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2016. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
