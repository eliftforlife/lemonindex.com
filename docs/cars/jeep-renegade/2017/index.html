Quick answers

Is the 2017 Jeep Renegade reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 328; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Renegade year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2017 Jeep Renegade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (49%), electrical (24%) and fuel or ev drive system (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Jeep Renegade have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Jeep Renegade get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
