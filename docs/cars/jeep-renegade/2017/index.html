Most owners who filed a complaint with NHTSA had 16K to 45K miles on the car (376 complaints listed the miles). This shows when people who had a problem reported it, not the odds that your car will have it.

Recalls 3

  • fuel system, gasoline 2018

    In certain operating conditions such as high power demand in conjunction with high altitude and/or high ambient temperatures, a manufacturing error in the fuel pump assembly may result in a loss of fuel pressure.

  • engine 2018

    These vehicles may be equipped with a cracked oil pump housing/balance shaft module (BSM), which can cause the oil pump to fail and the engine to stall.

  • electrical 2017

    Due to a software issue, multiple malfunction indicator lamps may illuminate on the instrument panel when no malfunction exists.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Jeep Renegade reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 328; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Renegade year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2017 Jeep Renegade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (49%), electrical (24%) and fuel or ev drive system (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Jeep Renegade have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Jeep Renegade get?

24 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Jeep Renegade safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Jeep Renegade problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,000 and 45,800 miles. The middle report was at 28,300 miles, from 376 complaints.

Does the 2017 Jeep Renegade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Jeep Renegade cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Jeep Renegade have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Renegade year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2022 and 2020.
