Quick answers

Is the 2015 Jeep Renegade reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 428; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Renegade year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2015 Jeep Renegade problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), transmission & drivetrain (32%) and electrical (26%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Jeep Renegade have?

4 safety recalls (latest: trailer hitches). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Jeep Renegade get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2015 Jeep Renegade problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,900 and 54,700 miles. The middle report was at 35,000 miles, from 398 complaints.

Does the 2015 Jeep Renegade have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 32% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2015 Jeep Renegade cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2015 Jeep Renegade have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Jeep Renegade year is better than the 2015?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2022 and 2020.
