How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Cadillac DTS reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 44; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2007 Cadillac DTS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (20%), brakes (20%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Cadillac DTS have?

3 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Cadillac DTS get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2007 Cadillac DTS have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2007 Cadillac DTS cost in fuel a year?

About $3,350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Cadillac DTS have?

4.6L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Cadillac DTS year than the 2007?

Not nearby: the 2007 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Cadillac DTS years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V355000 (electrical), 10V240000 (electrical) and 08V441000 (electrical) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 844 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Check engine light on, traction control systems (tcs) warning light on, service traction…”?
