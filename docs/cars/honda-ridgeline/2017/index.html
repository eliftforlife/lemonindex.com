Quick answers

Is the 2017 Honda Ridgeline reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 170; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Ridgeline year by this measure since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2017 Honda Ridgeline problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), fuel or ev drive system (24%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Honda Ridgeline have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Honda Ridgeline get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
