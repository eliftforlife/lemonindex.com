2008 Honda Ridgeline: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Honda Ridgeline drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 27 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 33,875 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Honda Ridgeline complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 39,000 miles and 108,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 57,800 miles and 107,500 miles.
