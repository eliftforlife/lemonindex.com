Recalls 7

  • body structure 2026

    The rear subframe may corrode at the suspension mounting points, which can result in rear suspension component failure.

  • airbags 2026

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

  • back over prevention 2024

    The rearview camera (RVC) tailgate wire harness may fatigue and break, which can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying.

Show all 7 recalls
  • airbags 2024

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, failing to suppress the air bag as intended.

  • brakes 2023

    The tie rod fastener that connects the brake booster and the brake master cylinder may have been improperly assembled during manufacturing, which can cause the brake master cylinder to separate from the booster assembly.

  • visibility 2023

    The heating pads behind both side-view mirrors may not be bonded properly, allowing the mirror glass to detach.

  • seat belts 2021

    The automatic locking retractor on the second-row center seat belt assembly may deactivate improperly, which can result in an unsecured child restraint system.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Honda Ridgeline reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 173; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Ridgeline year by this measure since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2021 Honda Ridgeline problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (25%), lights (17%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Honda Ridgeline have?

7 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Honda Ridgeline get?

21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Honda Ridgeline safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2021 Honda Ridgeline have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Honda Ridgeline cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Honda Ridgeline have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Ridgeline year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2018 and 2017.
