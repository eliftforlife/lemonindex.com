Recalls: 7

  • body structure 2026
    The rear subframe may corrode at the suspension mounting points, which can result in rear suspension component failure. NHTSA 26V365000
  • airbags 2026
    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash. NHTSA 26V332000
  • back over prevention 2024
    The rearview camera (RVC) tailgate wire harness may fatigue and break, which can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying. NHTSA 24V321000
  • airbags 2024
    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, failing to suppress the air bag as intended. NHTSA 24V064000
  • brakes 2023
    The tie rod fastener that connects the brake booster and the brake master cylinder may have been improperly assembled during manufacturing, which can cause the brake master cylinder to separate from the booster assembly. NHTSA 23V458000
  • visibility 2023
    The heating pads behind both side-view mirrors may not be bonded properly, allowing the mirror glass to detach. NHTSA 23V174000
