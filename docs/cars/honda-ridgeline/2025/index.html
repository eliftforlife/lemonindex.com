Quick answers

Is the 2025 Honda Ridgeline reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 3.4× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 341; 100 is average). The best Honda Ridgeline year by this measure since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2025 Honda Ridgeline problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (28%), lights (27%) and engine (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Honda Ridgeline have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Honda Ridgeline get?

20 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Honda Ridgeline safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Honda Ridgeline have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2025 Honda Ridgeline cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Honda Ridgeline have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Ridgeline year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2013, 2018 and 2017.
