Quick answers

Is the 2006 Honda Ridgeline reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 107; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Ridgeline year by this measure since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2006 Honda Ridgeline problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (25%), visibility (14%) and body structure (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Honda Ridgeline have?

14 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Honda Ridgeline get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
