Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V365000 (body structure), 26V332000 (airbags) and 24V321000 (back over prevention) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into no restart after auto start/stop engages. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Do all the headlights, taillights and turn signals work?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (12 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $5,500.)
  7. Honda sent dealers 76 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Video Message - It concerns how to replace a leaky front rocker arm oil control valve”?

Quick answers

Is the 2020 Honda Ridgeline reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 212; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Ridgeline year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2013.

What are the most common 2020 Honda Ridgeline problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (27%), lights (16%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Honda Ridgeline have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Honda Ridgeline get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Honda Ridgeline safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Honda Ridgeline problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 2,000 and 55,000 miles (3,200–89,000 km) (the middle half of the 25 complaints, of all 169 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2020 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2020 Honda Ridgeline have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Honda Ridgeline cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Honda Ridgeline have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Ridgeline year is better than the 2020?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2013 drew the fewest (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car).
