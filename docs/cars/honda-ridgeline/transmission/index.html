Quick answers

Which Honda Ridgeline years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Ridgeline years are 2019, 2017, 2018.

What are common Honda Ridgeline transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Honda Ridgeline years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Ridgeline years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2013, 2025, 2024.
