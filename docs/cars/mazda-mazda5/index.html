Quick answers.

Is the Mazda5 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.3× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the Mazda5 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2006-2012: Complaint Score 133; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2006 the most.

What are the best Mazda5 years to buy?

Only 3 Mazda5 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2012 drew the fewest complaints (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car).

What Mazda5 years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Mazda5 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Mazda5 have?

15 safety recalls across the 10 model years we track. Most: 2006 (3), 2009 (3), 2007 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mazda5 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, tires and suspension most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Mazda5 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 297 YouTube videos about the Mazda5, 2006 to 2015, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2014. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
