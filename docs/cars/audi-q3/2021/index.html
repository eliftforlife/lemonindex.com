Quick answers

Is the 2021 Audi Q3 reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 27; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Audi Q3 year by this measure is 2017.

What are the most common 2021 Audi Q3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (22%), fuel or ev drive system (22%) and seat belts (22%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Audi Q3 have?

3 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Audi Q3 get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
