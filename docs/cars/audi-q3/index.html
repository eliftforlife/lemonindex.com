Quick answers.

Is the Audi Q3 reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.5× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Best year: 2017. Avoid 2020.

Is the Audi Q3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2021: Lemon Index 66; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2017 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Audi Q3 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2017, 2021, 2020.

What Audi Q3 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020.

How many recalls does the Audi Q3 have?

13 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2017 (3), 2020 (3), 2021 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi Q3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and fuel or ev drive system most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
