Quick answers.

Is the Audi Q3 reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2017.

Is the Audi Q3 a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2021: Lemon Index 66; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2017 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Audi Q3 years to buy?

Only 3 Audi Q3 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2017 drew the fewest complaints (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).

What Audi Q3 years should you stay away from?

Only 3 Audi Q3 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Audi Q3 have?

18 safety recalls across the 8 model years we track. Most: 2019 (4), 2017 (3), 2020 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Audi Q3 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and fuel system most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
