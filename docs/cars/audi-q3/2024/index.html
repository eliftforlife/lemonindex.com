Quick answers

Is the 2024 Audi Q3 reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 18 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2024 Audi Q3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (33%), brakes (28%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Audi Q3 have?

2 safety recalls (latest: back over prevention). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Audi Q3 get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Audi Q3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Audi Q3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 Audi Q3 cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $2800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Audi Q3 have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Audi Q3 year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2017, 2021 and 2020.
