Recalls 3

  • lights 2019

    An inoperable front turn signal may not illuminate an indicator, failing to alert the driver to the issue.

  • lights 2017

    If the parking brake is used during emergency braking, the brake lights on the vehicle will not activate.

  • electrical 2016

    The affected vehicles may have been programmed with new software that inadvertently makes the side marker lights inoperative in conjunction with the parking lights or the headlights.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. A recall only matters if it's still open on that exact car, so check its VIN.
