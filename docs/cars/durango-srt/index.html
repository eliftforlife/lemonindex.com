Known issues

  • Lifter and camshaft failure ("HEMI tick")Owner-reported

    Owners report a tick from failed roller lifters that then wear the camshaft. SlashGear links most cases to the cylinder-deactivation (MDS) lifters and low oil flow.

    Fix: Replacing the lifters and camshaft fixes it; on-time oil changes with the right oil help prevent it.

    8 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2018–2025)
  • No engine recall on the DurangoRecall

    NHTSA lists no recall for the 6.4 engine on 2018–2024 Durangos. The recalls that hit SRT models are for other parts, such as the rear spoiler.

Quick answers

How fast is the Dodge Durango SRT Hellcat?

The WD Durango SRT Hellcat makes 710 hp and 645 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 3.6 seconds. Top speed: 180 mph (maker claim).

What engine does the Dodge Durango SRT have?

The WD Durango SRT (2018–2025) uses the 392 HEMI 6.4, a 6.4-liter V-8.

Which Dodge Durango SRT engine do YouTube owners like most?

392 HEMI 6.4 54/100, Hellcat 6.2 supercharged HEMI 52/100. That's the tone of the most-watched videos about each engine, praise words against trouble words.
