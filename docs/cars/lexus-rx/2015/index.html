Quick answers

Is the 2015 Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 31; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus RX year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2015 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (23%), engine (19%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Lexus RX have?

1 safety recall (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Lexus RX get?

20 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
