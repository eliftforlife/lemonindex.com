Quick answers.

Is the Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, very. It draws about a third as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2014.

Is the Lexus RX a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 31; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Lexus RX years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2012, 2011. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Lexus RX years should you stay away from?

No Lexus RX year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 81, the first year of a new generation), 2016 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 44, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (one of the most reliable, Lemon Index 41).

How many recalls does the Lexus RX have?

60 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2004 (6), 2000 (5), 2001 (5). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Lexus RX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, transmission & drivetrain and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
