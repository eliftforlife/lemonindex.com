How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a fifth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 17; 100 is average, lower is better). The Lexus RX year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2012 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (56%), electrical (11%) and fuel system, gasoline (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Lexus RX have?

1 safety recall (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Lexus RX get?

20 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Lexus RX safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2012 Lexus RX problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 11,000 and 77,000 miles (18,000–123,000 km) (the middle half of the 34 complaints, of all 48 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Lexus RX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Lexus RX cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $3,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Lexus RX have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Lexus RX year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2014 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (less than a sixth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 14V054000 (brakes) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Lexus sent dealers 267 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The purpose of this service bulletin is to provide information regarding the installation of…”?
