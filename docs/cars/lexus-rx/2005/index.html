Quick answers

Is the 2005 Lexus RX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 16 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2005 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (25%), transmission & drivetrain (25%) and body structure (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Lexus RX have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Lexus RX get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Lexus RX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 45,400 and 109,000 miles. The middle report was at 80,000 miles, from 142 complaints.

Does the 2005 Lexus RX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 25% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Lexus RX cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Lexus RX have?

3.3L 6-cyl.

What Lexus RX year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2011 and 2012.
