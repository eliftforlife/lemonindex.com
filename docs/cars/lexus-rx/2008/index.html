2008 Lexus RX: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Lexus RX drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 51 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 84,181 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Lexus RX complaints that listed a mileage came before 72,200 miles. Most came between 35,000 miles and 108,800 miles.

The top problem area, speed control, usually showed up between 15,600 miles and 69,000 miles.
