Quick answers

Is the 2022 Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.7× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 27; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus RX year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2022 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (15%), fuel or ev drive system (12%) and tires (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Lexus RX have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Lexus RX get?

21 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
