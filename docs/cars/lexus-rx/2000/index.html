Quick answers

Is the 2000 Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Lexus RX year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2000 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (24%), brakes (24%) and steering (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Lexus RX have?

5 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Lexus RX get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Lexus RX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 79,800 and 122,000 miles. The middle report was at 101,000 miles, from 187 complaints.

Does the 2000 Lexus RX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Lexus RX cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Lexus RX have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What Lexus RX year is better than the 2000?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2011 and 2012.
