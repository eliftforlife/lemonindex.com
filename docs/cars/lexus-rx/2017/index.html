How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Lexus RX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew a bit under half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The Lexus RX year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2017 Lexus RX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (22%), engine (16%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Lexus RX have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Lexus RX get?

22 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Lexus RX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2017 Lexus RX problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,400 and 30,000 miles (7,100–49,000 km) (the middle half of the 54 complaints, of all 103 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2017 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2017 Lexus RX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2017 Lexus RX cost in fuel a year?

About $2,650 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Lexus RX have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Lexus RX year is better than the 2017?

Nearby, the 2018 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V682000 (fuel system, gasoline), 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 18V211000 (brakes) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Lexus sent dealers 237 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “To minimize battery discharge during transportation and storage, the D/C Cut Fuse (10A) has…”?
