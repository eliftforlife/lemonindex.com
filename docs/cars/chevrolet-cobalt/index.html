Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Cobalt reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.5× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2010, still drew 1.5× the average.

Is the Chevrolet Cobalt a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2005-2010: Lemon Index 249; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010.

What are the best Chevrolet Cobalt years to buy?

No Chevrolet Cobalt year beat the average car. 2010 drew the fewest complaints (about one and a half times the average car's complaints).

What Chevrolet Cobalt years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Cobalt year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Cobalt have?

35 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2005 (6), 2006 (6), 2007 (6). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Cobalt problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name steering, electrical and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
