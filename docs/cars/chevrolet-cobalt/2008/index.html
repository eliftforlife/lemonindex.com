Quick answers

Is the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 134; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Cobalt year by this measure since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (43%), brakes (29%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt get?

25 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 13,209 and 46,500 miles. The middle report was at 25,692 miles, from 221 complaints.

Does the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt cost in fuel a year?

About $2150 to $3100 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Chevrolet Cobalt have?

2.2L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.
