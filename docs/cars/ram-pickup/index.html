Quick answers.

Is the Ram 1500 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 120; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2000 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Ram 1500 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2000, 2002, 2007.

What Ram 1500 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012, 2011, 2014.
