Quick answers.

Is the Ram 1500 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2000. Avoid 2011, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Ram 1500 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 120; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2000 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Ram 1500 years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2000, 2002, 2007.

What Ram 1500 years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012, 2011, 2014.

How many recalls does the Ram 1500 have?

344 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2019 (37), 2014 (24), 2015 (23). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Ram 1500 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
