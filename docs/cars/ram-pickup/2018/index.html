Quick answers

Is the 2018 Ram 1500 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 78; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2018 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (24%), steering (14%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Ram 1500 have?

11 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Ram 1500 get?

14 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
