Quick answers

Is the 2017 Ram 1500 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2017 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (19%), engine (19%) and steering (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Ram 1500 have?

19 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Ram 1500 get?

15 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
