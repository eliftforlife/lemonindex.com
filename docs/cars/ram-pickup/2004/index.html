2004 Ram pickup: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Ram pickup drew 1.2× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 466 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 426,289 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ram pickup complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 31,900 miles and 120,000 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 63,700 miles and 150,000 miles.
