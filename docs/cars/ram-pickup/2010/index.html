Quick answers

Is the 2010 Ram 1500 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 122; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2010 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (49%), steering (25%) and suspension (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Ram 1500 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Ram 1500 get?

15 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
