Quick answers

Is the 2012 Ram 1500 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 190; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2012 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (53%), engine (10%) and transmission & drivetrain (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Ram 1500 have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Ram 1500 get?

15 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2012 Ram 1500 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 63,500 miles, from 815 complaints.

Does the 2012 Ram 1500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Ram 1500 cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Ram 1500 have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Ram 1500 year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2017 and 2013.
