Quick answers

Is the 2021 Ram 1500 reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 102; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2021 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (41%), electrical (28%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Ram 1500 have?

20 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Ram 1500 get?

17 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Ram 1500 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Ram 1500 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,200 and 60,000 miles. The middle report was at 35,000 miles, from 135 complaints.

Does the 2021 Ram 1500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2021 Ram 1500 cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Ram 1500 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Ram 1500 year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2017 and 2013.
