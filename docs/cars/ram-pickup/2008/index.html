2008 Ram pickup: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 Ram pickup drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 251 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 245,840 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ram pickup complaints that listed a mileage came before 63,000 miles. Most came between 32,800 miles and 101,900 miles.

The top problem area, steering, usually showed up between 38,900 miles and 95,900 miles.
