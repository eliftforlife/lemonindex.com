Ram 1500 transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2004 (208 complaints), 2005 (165 complaints), 2022 (173 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2024, 2023, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2025 2025

    A software error may cause the gear selection display to be inaccurate.

  • 2025 2026

    A software error in the powertrain control module may improperly allow the vehicle speed to exceed the tire speed rating, which can result in a loss of vehicle control.

  • 2023 2022

    A build-up of pressure and heat inside the transmission may result in a transmission fluid leak from the dipstick tube.

  • 2023 2024

    An insufficient weld in the transmission control unit can allow transmission fluid to leak on to electrical components, resulting in a loss of drive power or loss of park function.

  • 2021 2023

    The powertrain control module software may cause an incorrect fuel mixture condition in the engine, and result in an engine stall.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which Ram 1500 years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Ram 1500 years are 2004, 2005, 2022.

What are common Ram 1500 transmission problems?

Owners most often describe rolled away or park trouble, lost power while driving, slipping.

Which Ram 1500 years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Ram 1500 years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2024, 2023, 2020.
