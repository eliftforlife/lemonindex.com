Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V352000 (airbags), 15V313000 (airbags) and 14V795000 (electrical) done? It has 12 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  6. Ram sent dealers 94 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “2003-2008 (dr) ram truck 1500-steering shudder during parking lot or low vehicle speed maneuvers”?

What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10020946: “5.9l dtc p0191 - fuel pressure sensor rationality”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10022531: “Emissions recall reprogram tipm - transmission default”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Ram 1500 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 75; 100 is average, lower is better). The Ram 1500 year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2018.

What are the most common 2007 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (26%), steering (24%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Ram 1500 have?

12 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Ram 1500 get?

14 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Ram 1500 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 32,000 and 108,000 miles (the middle half of the 774 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2007 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2007 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2007 Ram 1500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Ram 1500 cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Ram 1500 have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

Is there a better Ram 1500 year than the 2007?

Not nearby: the 2007 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Ram 1500 years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2018 drew the fewest.
