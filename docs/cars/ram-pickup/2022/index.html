Quick answers

Is the 2022 Ram 1500 reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 137; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2022 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (31%), engine (31%) and electrical (27%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Ram 1500 have?

23 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Ram 1500 get?

17 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Ram 1500 safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2022 Ram 1500 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 8,700 and 45,800 miles. The middle report was at 23,400 miles, from 148 complaints.

Does the 2022 Ram 1500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 31% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Ram 1500 cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Ram 1500 have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Ram 1500 year is better than the 2022?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2017 and 2013.
