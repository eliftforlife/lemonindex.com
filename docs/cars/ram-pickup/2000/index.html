2000 Ram pickup: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Ram pickup drew 2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 210 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 380,874 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Ram pickup complaints that listed a mileage came before 80,000 miles. Most came between 50,000 miles and 109,800 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 54,000 miles and 110,000 miles.
