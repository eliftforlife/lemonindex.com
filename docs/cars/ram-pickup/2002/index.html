Quick answers

Is the 2002 Ram 1500 reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Ram 1500 year by this measure since 2010 is 2023.

What are the most common 2002 Ram 1500 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (21%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Ram 1500 have?

6 safety recalls (latest: wheels). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Ram 1500 get?

12 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Ram 1500 problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 40,000 and 102,600 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 726 complaints.

Does the 2002 Ram 1500 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2002 Ram 1500 cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $5350 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Ram 1500 have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl, 5.9L 8-cyl.

What Ram 1500 year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2018, 2017 and 2013.
