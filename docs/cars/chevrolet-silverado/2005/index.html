Quick answers

Is the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Silverado year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2005 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), steering (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado have?

14 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado get?

11 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Chevrolet Silverado problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 35,000 and 92,000 miles. The middle report was at 62,000 miles, from 1,039 complaints.

Does the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $5850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Silverado year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2011.
