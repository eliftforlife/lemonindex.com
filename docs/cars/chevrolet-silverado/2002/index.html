2002 Chevrolet Silverado: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Chevrolet Silverado drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 599 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 652,646 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Silverado complaints that listed a mileage came before 68,000 miles. Most came between 40,000 miles and 110,000 miles.

The top problem area, service brakes, hydraulic, usually showed up between 37,300 miles and 89,500 miles.
