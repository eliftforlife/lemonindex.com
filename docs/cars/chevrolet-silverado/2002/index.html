Quick answers

Is the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 88; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Silverado year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2002 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (37%), transmission & drivetrain (11%) and engine (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado have?

15 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado get?

11 to 16 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Chevrolet Silverado problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 40,000 and 110,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,000 miles, from 1,303 complaints.

Does the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $5850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 4.8L 8-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.0L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Silverado year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2011.
