When problems show up

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 68,000 miles (the middle half of the 594 complaints that listed a mileage).

  • At 60K miles Right in the range where most owners reported problems.
  • At 100K and 150K miles Past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most.

By area: brakes mostly 37,000–65,000 miles; transmission & drivetrain mostly 18,000–89,000 miles.

This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not the odds that one car will have it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V611000 (airbags), 21V504000 (airbags) and 21V245000 (seat belts) done? It has 12 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Chevy sent dealers 2224 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information on Transmission Adaptive Functions and Correcting Low Mileage Harsh Shifts, Slips,…”?

What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether a transmission special coverage or warranty extension applies to this car. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10078762: “16999 - customer satisfaction program (stop delivery order)”)
  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10078770: “15801 customer satisfaction program”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Quick answers

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 70; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Silverado year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2016 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (36%), steering (21%) and transmission & drivetrain (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado have?

12 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado get?

17 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Chevrolet Silverado problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 68,000 miles (the middle half of the 594 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2016 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2016 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 12% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Silverado year is better than the 2016?

Nearby, the 2018 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (2.7× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.
