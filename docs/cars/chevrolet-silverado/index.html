Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.9× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2012.

Is the Chevrolet Silverado a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2000.

What are the best Chevrolet Silverado years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2010, 2011.

What Chevrolet Silverado years should you stay away from?

No Chevrolet Silverado year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Silverado have?

232 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2000 (20), 2020 (16), 2001 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Silverado problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
