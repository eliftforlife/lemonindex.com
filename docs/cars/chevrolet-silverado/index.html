Chevrolet Silverado reliability.

Is it reliable? Mostly. About half as many complaints as the average car.

Mostly half-ton trucks: not valid for 2500/3500 diesels

Of the 3,863 owner complaints behind this score (model years 2010–2022, first 3 years), only 15% are filed as 2500HD/3500HD trucks; the rest are filed as 1500s or just “Silverado”, because GM reports Silverado sales as one line. So the score and the best and worst years mostly reflect the half-ton and can't tell one diesel from another.

Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Mostly. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. This score is the whole line: 15% of its complaints are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Silverado HD page, by engine.

Is the Chevrolet Silverado a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 54; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Silverado years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2010, 2013. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Silverado years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014 (about average, Lemon Index 87, the first year of a new generation), 2015 (better than average, Lemon Index 80), 2022 (better than average, Lemon Index 76). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Silverado have?

212 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2000 (20), 2001 (15), 2002 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Silverado problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
