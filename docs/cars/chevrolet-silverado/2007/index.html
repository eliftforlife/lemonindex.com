Quick answers

Is the 2007 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 44; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Silverado year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2007 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (18%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and brakes (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Chevrolet Silverado have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Chevrolet Silverado get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
