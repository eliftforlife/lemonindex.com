What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10112819: “This informational bulletin provides information on Identifying Non-GM (Aftermarket) Engine Calibrations for Gasoline…”)
  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10113482: “This preliminary information bulletin provides a procedure to reprogram the ECM/TCM due to upshifts that clunk when the…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet Silverado year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012. This score is the whole line: 3% of its complaints that year are 2500HD/3500HD, gas and diesel mixed in. For a 2500/3500 or a diesel, see the Silverado HD page, by engine.

Is this score valid for a 2014 Silverado HD or a diesel?

No. Automakers report 2500/3500 sales together with the half-ton, so we can't give heavy-duty trucks their own per-truck score. The 2014 Silverado HD page shows their owner complaints split by engine (from each complaint's VIN), and each diesel has its own page.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (25%), electrical (19%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado have?

6 safety recalls (latest: seat belts). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado get?

17 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Chevrolet Silverado problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 28,000 and 85,000 miles (45,000–137,000 km) (the middle half of the 1,169 complaints, of all 1,610 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2014 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado cost in fuel a year?

About $3,200 to $3,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4.3L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Silverado year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2017 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V245000 (seat belts), 19V761000 (brakes) and 19V645000 (brakes) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (35 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $5,600.)
  7. Chevy sent dealers 3098 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Chevrolet/gmc: see document search button for owner letter”?
