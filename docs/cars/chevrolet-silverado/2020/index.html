When problems show up

Half of the Chevrolet Silverado complaints that listed a mileage came before 28,000 miles. Most came between 3,500 miles and 77,200 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 17,500 miles and 94,000 miles.

Recalls: 16

  • electrical 2019
    The battery positive (B+) cable rings may have been manufactured with excessive glue, potentially causing an intermittent electrical connection between the B+ cable and alternator. NHTSA 19V888000
  • seat belts 2019
    When the front seat belt pretensioners deploy, hot gas may vent through an opening in the pretensioner bracket, possibly igniting the carpet. NHTSA 19V814000
  • fuel system, gasoline 2019
    The fuel pump may be missing a pressure regulator, allowing for over-pressurization of the fuel system. NHTSA 19V837000
  • body structure 2020
    Some of the vehicles in 20V-324 were not part of Recall 20V-142 but received defect parts during another service (typically during collision repair). NHTSA 20V324000
  • tires 2020
    The vehicles may have been manufactured with Continental tires that were overcured. NHTSA 20V684000
  • latches/locks/linkages 2020
    The hood-latch striker wires may not have been heat-treated properly, possibly causing them to fracture. NHTSA 20V142000

Recall work is free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. See all 16 at nhtsa.gov.
