Chevrolet Silverado transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (277 complaints), 2017 (213 complaints), 2020 (212 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2025, 2012, 2013.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Chevrolet Silverado years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Chevrolet Silverado years are 2019, 2017, 2020.

What are common Chevrolet Silverado transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, shudder, slipping.

Which Chevrolet Silverado years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Chevrolet Silverado years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2012, 2013.
