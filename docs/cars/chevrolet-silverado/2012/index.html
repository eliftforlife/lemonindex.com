Quick answers

Is the 2012 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 7.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 14; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2012 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (17%), body structure (15%) and tires (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Chevrolet Silverado get?

14 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
