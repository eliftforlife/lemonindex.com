Quick answers

Is the 2022 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 76; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Silverado year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2022 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (30%), transmission & drivetrain (25%) and electrical (24%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Chevrolet Silverado have?

8 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Chevrolet Silverado get?

15 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
