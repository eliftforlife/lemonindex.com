Quick answers

Is the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.5× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 68; 100 is average). The best Chevrolet Silverado year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2023 Chevrolet Silverado problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (36%), transmission & drivetrain (30%) and electrical (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado get?

15 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2023 Chevrolet Silverado problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 16,000 and 49,500 miles. The middle report was at 34,000 miles, from 63 complaints.

Does the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 30% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $5300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 5.3L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl.

What Chevrolet Silverado year is better than the 2023?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2010 and 2011.
