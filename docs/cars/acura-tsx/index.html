Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Acura TSX reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012.

Is the Acura TSX a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2004-2012: Lemon Index 66; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2010 the most.

What are the best Acura TSX years to buy?

Only 4 Acura TSX years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2012 drew the fewest complaints (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

What Acura TSX years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Acura TSX years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Acura TSX have?

41 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2010 (7), 2009 (6), 2012 (6). Each year's page lists them, and every Acura TSX recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Acura TSX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name brakes, seats and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 8 Acura TSX years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the Acura TSX, 2004 to 2014, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2008. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
