Quick answers.

Is the Buick Rendezvous reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints. Best year: 2005. Avoid 2002, 2003 and 2004.

Is the Buick Rendezvous a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2002-2006: Lemon Index 143; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2005 and the worst is 2002.

What are the best Buick Rendezvous years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2005, 2003, 2004.

What Buick Rendezvous years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2002, 2004, 2003.

How many recalls does the Buick Rendezvous have?

18 safety recalls across the 6 model years we track. Most: 2003 (7), 2004 (5), 2002 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Buick Rendezvous problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and suspension most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
