Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 07V349000 (fuel system, gasoline), 07E021000 (fuel system, other) and 02V222000 (airbags) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Buick sent dealers 391 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Condition of electrically”?

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Buick Rendezvous reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 170; 100 is average, lower is better). The Buick Rendezvous year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2005.

What are the most common 2002 Buick Rendezvous problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (19%), engine (16%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Buick Rendezvous have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Buick Rendezvous get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Buick Rendezvous problems show up?

At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2002 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.
