Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Scion tC reliable?

About average. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2011. Most complaints: 2005 and 2006.

Is the Scion tC a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2005-2011: Lemon Index 94; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2005 the most.

What are the best Scion tC years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2011, 2008, 2007. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Scion tC years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2005 (about average, Lemon Index 113), 2006 (about average, Lemon Index 112). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Scion tC have?

28 safety recalls across the 11 model years we track. Most: 2006 (5), 2005 (4), 2008 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Scion tC recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Scion tC problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name visibility, airbags and body structure most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Scion tC years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 396 YouTube videos about the Scion tC, 2005 to 2016, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2011. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
