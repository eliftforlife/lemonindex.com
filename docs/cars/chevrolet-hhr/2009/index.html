Questions to ask the seller

5 questions to ask before you buy
  1. Were recalls 14E021000 (electrical), 14V171000 (electrical) and 14V153000 (steering) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Chevy sent dealers 1003 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “The power steering is inoperative or the vehicle is hard to turn”?

Quick answers

Is the 2009 Chevrolet HHR reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 265; 100 is average, lower is better). The Chevrolet HHR year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2009 Chevrolet HHR problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (42%), steering (28%) and airbags (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Chevrolet HHR have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Chevrolet HHR get?

23 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Chevrolet HHR problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 27,000 and 60,000 miles (44,000–97,000 km) (the middle half of the 130 complaints, of all 151 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2009 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2009 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2009 Chevrolet HHR have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2009 Chevrolet HHR cost in fuel a year?
