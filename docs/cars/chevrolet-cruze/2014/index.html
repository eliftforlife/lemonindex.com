Quick answers

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 82; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Cruze year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2014 Chevrolet Cruze problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (22%), steering (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze have?

10 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze get?

26 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2014 Chevrolet Cruze problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,800 and 80,000 miles. The middle report was at 53,000 miles, from 483 complaints.

Does the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Chevrolet Cruze have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 1.8L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Chevrolet Cruze year is better than the 2014?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2019, 2018 and 2015.
