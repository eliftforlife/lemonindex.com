Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chevrolet Cruze reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 51; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chevrolet Cruze year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2017 Chevrolet Cruze problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), electrical (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chevrolet Cruze have?

2 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chevrolet Cruze get?

31 to 37 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
