Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Cruze a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 79; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2012.

What are the best Chevrolet Cruze years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2018, 2015.
