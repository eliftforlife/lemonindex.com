Quick answers.

Is the Chevrolet Cruze reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.3× fewer complaints than average, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2012.

Is the Chevrolet Cruze a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2011-2019: Lemon Index 79; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints and 2012 the most.

What are the best Chevrolet Cruze years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2018, 2015. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Chevrolet Cruze years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2012.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Cruze have?

44 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2014 (10), 2011 (7), 2012 (7). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chevrolet Cruze problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
