Problems Chevy told dealers about

Chevy sent dealers 1658 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Information on checking, adjusting, or installing of the timing chain
  • There may be a lack of power or a rattle noise from the engine with stored trouble codes
  • Check engine light is on with multiple trouble codes possibly stored

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.
