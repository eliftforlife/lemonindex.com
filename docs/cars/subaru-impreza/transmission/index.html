Subaru Impreza transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2008 (116 complaints), 2009 (60 complaints), 2013 (24 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2019, 2020, 2025.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2024 2023

    The front driveshaft assemblies' outer race may develop cracks and break.

  • 2023 2023

    The center support bolts for the driveshaft may loosen, resulting in the disconnection of the front end of the driveshaft.

  • 2021 2021

    The continuously variable transmission (CVT) select lever cable nut may not have been tightened properly, allowing the nut to loosen.

Quick answers

Which Subaru Impreza years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Subaru Impreza years are 2008, 2009, 2013.

What are common Subaru Impreza transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, slipping, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Subaru Impreza years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Subaru Impreza years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2019, 2020, 2025.
