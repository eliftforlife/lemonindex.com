Quick answers

Is the 2009 Subaru Impreza reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 99; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Impreza year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2009 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (43%), transmission & drivetrain (24%) and speed control (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Subaru Impreza have?

15 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Subaru Impreza get?

21 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 Subaru Impreza problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 38,000 and 91,000 miles. The middle report was at 65,000 miles, from 131 complaints.

Does the 2009 Subaru Impreza have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 24% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 Subaru Impreza cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 Subaru Impreza have?

2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Impreza year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2014 and 2011.
