Quick answers

Is the 2022 Subaru Impreza reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 87; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Impreza year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2022 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (48%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and automatic emergency braking (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Subaru Impreza have?

2 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Subaru Impreza get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
