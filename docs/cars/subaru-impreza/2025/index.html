Quick answers

Is the 2025 Subaru Impreza reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 8 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (25%), backup camera (25%) and electrical (25%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Subaru Impreza get?

29 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Subaru Impreza safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 25% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Subaru Impreza cost in fuel a year?

About $2150 to $2200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Impreza year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2014 and 2011.
