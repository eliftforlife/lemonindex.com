How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2025 Subaru Impreza reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 8 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (25%), transmission & drivetrain (25%) and automatic emergency braking (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Subaru Impreza get?

29 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Subaru Impreza safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

Does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 25% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2025 Subaru Impreza cost in fuel a year?

About $2,150 to $2,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Subaru Impreza have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Impreza year is better than the 2025?

Nearby, the 2022 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (somewhat fewer complaints than the average car). Across every year, 2010 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  2. Any shudder, whine or slipping from the CVT? Ask for CVT fluid service records.
  3. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  4. Subaru sent dealers 149 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This bulletin provides guidance when diagnosing chain slip, judder, shudder, and hesitation…”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 974 YouTube videos about the Subaru Impreza, 2002 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
