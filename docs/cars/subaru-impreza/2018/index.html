Quick answers

Is the 2018 Subaru Impreza reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 96; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Impreza year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2018 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (38%), electrical (16%) and engine (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Subaru Impreza have?

6 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Subaru Impreza get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
