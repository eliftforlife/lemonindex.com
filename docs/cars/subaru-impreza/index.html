Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Impreza reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2008, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Subaru Impreza a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 100; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2020 the most.

What are the best Subaru Impreza years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2014, 2011. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Impreza years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020 (worse than average, Lemon Index 182), 2008 (worse than average, Lemon Index 177, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 160). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Impreza have?

122 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2008 (15), 2009 (15), 2010 (15). Each year's page lists them, and every Subaru Impreza recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Subaru Impreza problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, wipers & visibility and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Subaru Impreza repairs cost?

The repairs Subaru Impreza owners reported paying for most: clutch replacement (middle bill $1,600, 10 reports), engine replacement (middle bill $6,500, 5 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 22 Subaru Impreza years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 974 YouTube videos about the Subaru Impreza, 2002 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
