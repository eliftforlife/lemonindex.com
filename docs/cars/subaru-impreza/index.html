Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Impreza reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2012, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Subaru Impreza a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 100; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2020 the most.

What are the best Subaru Impreza years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2014, 2011. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Impreza years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020 (worse than average, Lemon Index 182), 2019 (worse than average, Lemon Index 160), 2012 (worse than average, Lemon Index 137, the first year of a new generation). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Impreza have?

122 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2008 (15), 2009 (15), 2010 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Impreza problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
