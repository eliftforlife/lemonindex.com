Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Impreza reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2008, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Subaru Impreza a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 100; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Subaru Impreza years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2014, 2011.

What Subaru Impreza years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2008, 2019.

How many recalls does the Subaru Impreza have?

122 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2008 (15), 2009 (15), 2010 (15). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Impreza problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, airbags and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
