Quick answers

Is the 2007 Subaru Impreza reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 18 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Subaru Impreza problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (33%), tires (17%) and steering (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Subaru Impreza have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Subaru Impreza get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
