2017 Chrysler Pacifica.

The 2017 Chrysler Pacifica is one of the worst years by owner complaints: 780 complaints in its first 3 years and a Lemon Index of 409, where 100 is the average car. Owners complain most about transmission & drivetrain, it has 11 recalls, and it gets 22 to 32 mpg combined. The best Chrysler Pacifica year is 2020.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 4.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 409; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Pacifica year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2017 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (38%), engine (31%) and electrical (28%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica have?

11 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica get?

22 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Chrysler Pacifica problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,000 and 47,900 miles. The middle report was at 25,000 miles, from 821 complaints.

Does the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 38% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica cost in fuel a year?

About $2000 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica have?

3.6L 6-cyl.

What Chrysler Pacifica year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2020, 2019 and 2022.
