Quick answers

Is the 2023 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.4× the average car's complaints per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 136; 100 is average). The best Chrysler Pacifica year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2023 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), electrical (29%) and transmission & drivetrain (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Chrysler Pacifica have?

14 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Chrysler Pacifica get?

20 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
