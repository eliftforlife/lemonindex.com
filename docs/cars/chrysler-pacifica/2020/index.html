Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 114; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2020 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (38%), electrical (37%) and transmission & drivetrain (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica have?

6 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica get?

20 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
