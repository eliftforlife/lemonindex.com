Quick answers

Is the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 114; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Pacifica year by this measure since 2010 is 2025.

What are the most common 2020 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (38%), electrical (37%) and transmission & drivetrain (29%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica have?

6 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica get?

20 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Chrysler Pacifica problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 2,900 and 57,500 miles. The middle report was at 33,000 miles, from 37 complaints.

Does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 29% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica cost in fuel a year?

About $2150 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Chrysler Pacifica have?

3.6L 6-cyl.
