Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2020, still drew 1.1× the average. Most complaints: 2017, 2018 and 2021.

Is the Chrysler Pacifica a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Lemon Index 279; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2020 drew the fewest complaints and 2017 the most.

What are the best Chrysler Pacifica years to buy?

No Chrysler Pacifica year beat the average car. 2020 drew the fewest complaints (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

What Chrysler Pacifica years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017 (avoid, Lemon Index 409), 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 300), 2021 (avoid, Lemon Index 294). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Chrysler Pacifica have?

89 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2023 (14), 2018 (13), 2017 (11). Each year's page lists them, and every Chrysler Pacifica recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Chrysler Pacifica problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 9 Chrysler Pacifica years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Chrysler Pacifica, 2017 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
