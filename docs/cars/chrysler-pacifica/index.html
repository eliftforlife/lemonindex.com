Quick answers.

Is the Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.8× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Even its best year, 2020, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2017, 2018 and 2021.

Is the Chrysler Pacifica a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2017-2022: Lemon Index 279; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2017.

What are the best Chrysler Pacifica years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2022.

What Chrysler Pacifica years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2017, 2018, 2021.

How many recalls does the Chrysler Pacifica have?

89 safety recalls across the 9 model years we track. Most: 2023 (14), 2018 (13), 2017 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Chrysler Pacifica problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
