Quick answers

Is the 2022 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 225; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Chrysler Pacifica year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2022 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (42%), transmission & drivetrain (40%) and electrical (31%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Chrysler Pacifica have?

10 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Chrysler Pacifica get?

20 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
