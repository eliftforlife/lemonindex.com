Quick answers

Is the 2025 Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 2.6× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 39; 100 is average).

What are the most common 2025 Chrysler Pacifica problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (30%), automatic emergency braking (23%) and speed control (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Chrysler Pacifica have?

9 safety recalls (latest: stability control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Chrysler Pacifica get?

20 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
