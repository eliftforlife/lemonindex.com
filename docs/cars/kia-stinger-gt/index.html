Known issues

  • Turbo oil feed line recallRecall

    On 2018–2023 Stingers and 2019–2020 K900s with the 3.3T, the left turbo oil feed pipe and hose can wear out and leak oil onto hot parts, a fire risk.

    Fix: Dealer replaces the left turbo oil feed pipe and hose for free (NHTSA 24V169, Kia SC300).

    5 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2018–2023)
  • Intake valve carbonParts seller

    Direct injection never washes the intake valves, so carbon builds up. A parts seller writing about this engine in the Genesis G70 plans for cleaning at 60,000–80,000 miles.

    Fix: Walnut-blast the intake valves.

  • Timing chain tensioner (disputed)Owner-reported

    Forum posts blame timing chain tensioners, but the same parts seller says no widespread defect is documented. Treat it as unconfirmed.

    1 owner complaint to NHTSA mentions it (2018–2023)

Quick answers

How fast is the Kia Stinger GT1 / GT2?

The CK Stinger GT1 / GT2 makes 368 hp and 376 lb-ft. Car and Driver measured 0–60 mph in 4.6 seconds. Top speed: 167 mph (on summer tires; 149 mph on all-season tires).

What engine does the Kia Stinger GT have?

The CK Stinger GT (2018–2023) uses the Hyundai Lambda II 3.3 T-GDI (G6DP), a 3.3-liter V6, twin-turbo.
