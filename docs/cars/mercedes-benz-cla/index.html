Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz CLA reliable?

Mostly. It draws 1.1× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Safest year: 2018. Avoid 2014.

Is the Mercedes-Benz CLA a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2014-2021: Lemon Index 112; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2015 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz CLA years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2015, 2018, 2017.

What Mercedes-Benz CLA years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz CLA have?

40 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2017 (10), 2018 (9), 2016 (8). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz CLA problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and speed control most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
