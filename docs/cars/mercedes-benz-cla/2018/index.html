Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 23V854000 (body structure), 22V365000 (electrical) and 21V229000 (steering) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 119 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Transmission intermittently fails to shift, vehicle jolts in higher gears”?

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 57; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz CLA year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2015.

What are the most common 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (38%), engine (31%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA get?

27 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA cost in fuel a year?

About $2,750 to $2,950 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz CLA have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz CLA year is better than the 2018?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).
