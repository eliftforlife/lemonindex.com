Quick answers.

Is the Suzuki SX4 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2008, still drew 1.3× the average.

Is the Suzuki SX4 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2008-2009: Complaint Score 135; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2008 drew the fewest complaints and 2009 the most.

What are the best Suzuki SX4 years to buy?

Only 2 Suzuki SX4 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2008 drew the fewest complaints (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

What Suzuki SX4 years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Suzuki SX4 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Suzuki SX4 have?

24 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2010 (5), 2013 (5), 2008 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Suzuki SX4 recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Suzuki SX4 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, electrical and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 7 Suzuki SX4 years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 196 YouTube videos about the Suzuki SX4, 2007 to 2013, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2010. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
