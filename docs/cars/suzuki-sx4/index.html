Quick answers.

Is the Suzuki SX4 reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, but YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2008, still drew 1.3× the average.

Is the Suzuki SX4 a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2008-2009: Lemon Index 135; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2008 drew the fewest complaints and 2009 the most.

What are the best Suzuki SX4 years to buy?

Only 2 Suzuki SX4 years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2008 drew the fewest complaints (a bit more than the average car's complaints).

What Suzuki SX4 years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Suzuki SX4 years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Suzuki SX4 have?

24 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2010 (5), 2013 (5), 2008 (3). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Suzuki SX4 problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name airbags, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
