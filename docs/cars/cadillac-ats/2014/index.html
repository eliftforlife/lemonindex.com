How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Cadillac ATS reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 94; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac ATS year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2015.

What are the most common 2014 Cadillac ATS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), electrical (16%) and steering (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Cadillac ATS have?

4 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Cadillac ATS get?

21 to 26 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Cadillac ATS safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Cadillac ATS problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,400 and 14,000 miles (7,200–23,000 km) (the middle half of the 34 complaints, of all 37 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Cadillac ATS have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2014 Cadillac ATS cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $2,850 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Cadillac ATS have?

2.0L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl.

What Cadillac ATS year is better than the 2014?

Nearby, the 2015 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 15V558000 (visibility), 15V463000 (visibility) and 14V446000 (seats) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 1991 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some trucks and passenger vehicles, accessory power outlets (apo) and trailer lighting are”?
