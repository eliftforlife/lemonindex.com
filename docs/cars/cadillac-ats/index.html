Quick answers.

Is the Cadillac ATS reliable?

Mostly. It draws about as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2015.

Is the Cadillac ATS a good car?

It is about average, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2016: Lemon Index 93; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2015 drew the fewest complaints and 2013 the most.

What are the best Cadillac ATS years to buy?

Only 4 Cadillac ATS years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2015 drew the fewest complaints (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

What Cadillac ATS years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Cadillac ATS years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Cadillac ATS have?

21 safety recalls across the 7 model years we track. Most: 2013 (6), 2014 (4), 2015 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Cadillac ATS recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Cadillac ATS problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and steering most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 6 Cadillac ATS years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 200 YouTube videos about the Cadillac ATS, 2013 to 2018, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2017. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
