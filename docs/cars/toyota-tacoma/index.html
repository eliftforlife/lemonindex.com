Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2022. Most complaints: 2016.

Is the Toyota Tacoma a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 44; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2016 the most.

What are the best Toyota Tacoma years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2021, 2014. 2022 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Tacoma years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2016 (about average, Lemon Index 114, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (better than average, Lemon Index 78), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 70). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Tacoma have?

158 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2009 (13), 2010 (13), 2006 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Tacoma problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, speed control and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
