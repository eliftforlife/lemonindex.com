Toyota Tacoma transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2009 (66 complaints), 2016 (57 complaints), 2005 (44 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2023, 2014, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Toyota Tacoma years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Toyota Tacoma years are 2009, 2016, 2005.

What are common Toyota Tacoma transmission problems?

Owners most often describe slipping, shudder, delay or hesitation.

Which Toyota Tacoma years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Toyota Tacoma years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2023, 2014, 2020.
