Quick answers

Is the 2011 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 28; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2011 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (19%), speed control (16%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Toyota Tacoma have?

9 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Toyota Tacoma safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2011 Toyota Tacoma problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 32,600 and 95,000 miles. The middle report was at 65,100 miles, from 130 complaints.

Does the 2011 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2021.
