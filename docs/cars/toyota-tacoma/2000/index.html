Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 09E025000 (lights), 09E012000 (lights) and 06E049000 (lights) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Toyota sent dealers 89 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Toyota suspension and axle - differential locking system repair manual correction”?

Quick answers

Is the 2000 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 43; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Tacoma year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2000 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (24%), airbags (20%) and suspension (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Toyota Tacoma have?

5 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Toyota Tacoma problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 80,000 and 180,000 miles (the middle half of the 103 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2000 is still under the range where most problems showed up, so they may be ahead of it. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2000 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2000 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2000 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.7L 4-cyl, 3.4L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2000?

Nearby, the 2001 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (4.5× fewer complaints than average). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.
