2000 Toyota Tacoma: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Toyota Tacoma drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 70 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 147,295 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tacoma complaints that listed a mileage came before 130,300 miles. Most came between 80,000 miles and 180,000 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 107,000 miles and 179,500 miles.
