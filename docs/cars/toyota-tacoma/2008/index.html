Problems Toyota told dealers about

Toyota sent dealers 330 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Rear differential whine or howling noise at highway speeds
  • Automatic transmission/transaxle
  • Information on installing manual transmission shift lever or park neutral position switch

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 94; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2008 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (50%), transmission & drivetrain (13%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Toyota Tacoma have?

11 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
