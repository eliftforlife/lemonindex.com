Quick answers

Is the 2024 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.2× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 85; 100 is average). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2024 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (36%), brakes (12%) and electrical (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Toyota Tacoma have?

5 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Toyota Tacoma get?

20 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2024 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 36% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2024 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $2700 to $3200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.4L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2024?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2021.
