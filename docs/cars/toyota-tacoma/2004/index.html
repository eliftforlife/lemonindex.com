2004 Toyota Tacoma: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Toyota Tacoma drew 2.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 58 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 152,932 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Tacoma complaints that listed a mileage came before 108,000 miles. Most came between 55,000 miles and 147,000 miles.

The top problem area, body structure, usually showed up between 90,000 miles and 151,500 miles.
