Quick answers

Is the 2009 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 210; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2009 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (31%), speed control (25%) and lights (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 Toyota Tacoma have?

13 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
