Quick answers

Is the 2025 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.5× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2025 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 66; 100 is average). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2025 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (39%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and steering (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Toyota Tacoma have?

5 safety recalls (latest: suspension). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Toyota Tacoma get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
