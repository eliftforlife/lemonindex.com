Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Tacoma year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2022.

What are the most common 2012 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (17%), transmission & drivetrain (17%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Toyota Tacoma have?

7 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Toyota Tacoma get?

17 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Toyota Tacoma safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2012 Toyota Tacoma problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 36,000 and 80,000 miles (58,000–128,000 km) (the middle half of the 261 complaints, of all 295 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $3,800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2014 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a quarter as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2022 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 17V425000 (body structure), 14V475000 (equipment) and 14V054000 (brakes) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (28 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $518.)
  6. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (27 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $550.)
  7. Toyota sent dealers 464 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Information on installing manual transmission shift lever or park neutral position switch”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 29 Toyota Tacoma years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Toyota Tacoma, 1995 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
