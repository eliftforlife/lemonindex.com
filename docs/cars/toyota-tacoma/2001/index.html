Quick answers

Is the 2001 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 22; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2001 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (21%), brakes (21%) and suspension (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Toyota Tacoma have?

6 safety recalls (latest: tires). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Toyota Tacoma problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 97,800 and 178,600 miles. The middle report was at 144,000 miles, from 295 complaints.

Does the 2001 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 13% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.7L 4-cyl, 3.4L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2001?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2021.
