Quick answers

Is the 2022 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 6.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 16; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (17%), airbags (17%) and engine (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Toyota Tacoma have?

3 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Toyota Tacoma get?

18 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Toyota Tacoma safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2022 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2022 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.
