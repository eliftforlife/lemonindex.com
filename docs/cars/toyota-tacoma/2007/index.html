Problems Toyota told dealers about

Toyota sent dealers 326 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Rear differential whine or howling noise at highway speeds
  • Automatic transmission/transaxle
  • Information on installing manual transmission shift lever or park neutral position switch

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2007 Toyota Tacoma reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew 1.1× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 112; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Tacoma year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2007 Toyota Tacoma problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (49%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and brakes (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Toyota Tacoma have?

10 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Toyota Tacoma get?

16 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Toyota Tacoma problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,900 and 102,100 miles. The middle report was at 62,000 miles, from 386 complaints.

Does the 2007 Toyota Tacoma have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Toyota Tacoma cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Toyota Tacoma have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 4.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Tacoma year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2014 and 2021.
