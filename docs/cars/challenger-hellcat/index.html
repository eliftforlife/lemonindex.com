Known issues

  • Supercharger bearing wearOwner-reported

    Worn supercharger front bearings cause noise and can damage the rotors and the drive coupler pins, an enthusiast magazine reports.

    Fix: Replacing the four front bearings fixes nearly all of the noise.

  • Fuel rail crossover hose leak (2015)Recall

    On early 2015 Hellcats the fuel rail crossover hose and its O-ring could be damaged and leak fuel near hot engine parts, a fire risk.

    Fix: Dealers replace the crossover hose free under recall R07 (NHTSA 15V-114).

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2023)
  • Oil cooler hoses can fail (2017)Recall

    On some 2017 Hellcats the engine oil cooler hoses can fail and dump oil fast, possibly onto the windshield or hot parts.

    Fix: Dealers replace the oil cooler lines free under recall T48 (NHTSA 17V-496).

    3 owner complaints to NHTSA mention it (2015–2023)

Quick answers

How fast is the Dodge SRT Hellcat Redeye?

The LC (3rd gen) SRT Hellcat Redeye makes 797 hp and 707 lb-ft. Car and Driver (Redeye Widebody) measured 0–60 mph in 3.7 seconds. Top speed: 203 mph.

What engine does the Dodge Challenger SRT Hellcat have?

The LC (3rd gen) Challenger Hellcat (2015–2023) uses the Hellcat 6.2 supercharged HEMI, a 6.2-liter V-8, supercharged.
