Quick answers

Is the 2000 Dodge Neon reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 131; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Neon year by this measure is 2005.

What are the most common 2000 Dodge Neon problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (27%), airbags (22%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2000 Dodge Neon have?

11 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2000 Dodge Neon get?

24 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2000 Dodge Neon problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,000 and 60,000 miles. The middle report was at 38,511 miles, from 28 complaints.

Does the 2000 Dodge Neon have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2000 Dodge Neon cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2500 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2000 Dodge Neon have?

2.0L 4-cyl.
