How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2008 Toyota Sequoia reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Sequoia year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2008 Toyota Sequoia problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), speed control (18%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Toyota Sequoia have?

9 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Toyota Sequoia get?

14 to 15 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2008 Toyota Sequoia have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Toyota Sequoia cost in fuel a year?

About $4,000 to $4,300 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Toyota Sequoia have?

4.7L 8-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl.

What Toyota Sequoia year is better than the 2008?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a third as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21E103000 (steering), 21V920000 (steering) and 16V396000 (seats) done? It has 9 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Toyota sent dealers 241 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Accelerator pedal sensor assembly information regarding the inspection, removal, and…”?
