Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Outback reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2012. Most complaints: 2010, 2011 and 2015.

Is the Subaru Outback a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 207; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2012 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2020 the most.

What are the best Subaru Outback years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2012, 2014, 2013. 2012 drew the fewest.

What Subaru Outback years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 551, the first year of a new generation), 2019 (avoid, Lemon Index 391), 2018 (avoid, Lemon Index 316). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Subaru Outback have?

189 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2010 (18), 2011 (16), 2012 (16). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Subaru Outback problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name wipers & visibility, electrical and automatic emergency braking most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
