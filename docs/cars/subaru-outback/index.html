Quick answers.

Is the Subaru Outback a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 207; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2012 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Subaru Outback years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2012, 2004, 2007.

What Subaru Outback years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020, 2019, 2018.
