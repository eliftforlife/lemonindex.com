Recalls 3

  • fuel system, gasoline 2021

    The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

  • visibility 2018

    In the affected vehicles, a software issue may cause the audio display system to not initialize, resulting in the camera display remaining blank.

  • electrical 2018

    Due to a software error, the low fuel warning light may not illuminate at the intended remaining fuel level and the miles-to-empty display may incorrectly indicate a positive number despite the tank being empty.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Subaru Outback reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 316; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Outback year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2018 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (35%), electrical (32%) and fuel or ev drive system (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Subaru Outback have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Subaru Outback get?

22 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
