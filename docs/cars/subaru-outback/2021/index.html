Quick answers

Is the 2021 Subaru Outback reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.8× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 176; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Outback year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2021 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (37%), electrical (26%) and automatic emergency braking (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Subaru Outback have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Subaru Outback get?

26 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Subaru Outback safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2021 Subaru Outback problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,200 and 14,000 miles. The middle report was at 3,600 miles, from 61 complaints.

Does the 2021 Subaru Outback have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2021 Subaru Outback cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Subaru Outback have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Outback year is better than the 2021?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2014 and 2013.
