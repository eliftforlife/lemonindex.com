2002 Subaru Outback: problems and owner complaints

The 2002 Subaru Outback drew about average, per car sold. Owners filed 68 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 60,974 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Subaru Outback complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,500 miles. Most came between 37,800 miles and 102,800 miles.

The top problem area, service brakes, hydraulic, usually showed up between 8,000 miles and 48,200 miles.
