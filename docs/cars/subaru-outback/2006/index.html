2006 Subaru Outback: problems and owner complaints

The 2006 Subaru Outback drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 34 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 59,109 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Subaru Outback complaints that listed a mileage came before 95,300 miles. Most came between 60,000 miles and 134,700 miles.

The top problem area, speed control, usually showed up between 76,800 miles and 134,700 miles.
