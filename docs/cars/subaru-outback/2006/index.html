Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V003000 (airbags), 20V002000 (airbags) and 20V001000 (airbags) done? It has 7 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (15 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $300.)
  5. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced? (12 owners named a electrical repair bill; the middle one was $381.)
  6. Subaru sent dealers 209 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “This Bulletin provides guidance for diagnosing a leaking front or rear suspension strut”?

Quick answers

Is the 2006 Subaru Outback reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew somewhat fewer complaints than the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 83; 100 is average, lower is better). 2006 is one of the better years of a model that draws 2.1× the average car's complaints. The Subaru Outback year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2006 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (41%), tires (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Subaru Outback have?

7 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Subaru Outback get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Subaru Outback problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 60,000 and 135,000 miles (97,000–217,000 km) (the middle half of the 222 complaints, of all 277 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2006 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2006 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2006 Subaru Outback have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 15% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Subaru Outback cost in fuel a year?

About $2,900 to $4,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Subaru Outback have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Subaru Outback year is better than the 2006?

Nearby, the 2007 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest.
