Quick answers

Is the 2015 Subaru Outback reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 171; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Outback year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2015 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (39%), electrical (12%) and transmission & drivetrain (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Subaru Outback have?

5 safety recalls (latest: steering). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 Subaru Outback get?

22 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
