What to ask the shop

  • Transmission: ask whether the latest transmission software update was done, and ask for it before paying for parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10203874: “This bulletin announces the availability of a Transmission Control Module (TCM) reprogramming file to address DTC P0871”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10207180: “The following information announces reprogramming file availability and provides a software update installation…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Subaru Outback reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about twice the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 239; 100 is average, lower is better). The Subaru Outback year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2012.

What are the most common 2022 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are wipers & visibility (58%), electrical (9%) and visibility (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Subaru Outback have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2022 Subaru Outback get?

24 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2022 Subaru Outback safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2022 Subaru Outback problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 3,800 and 36,000 miles (6,100–58,000 km) (the middle half of the 39 complaints, of all 465 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2022 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2022 Subaru Outback have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Subaru Outback cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 to $2,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2022 Subaru Outback have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Subaru Outback year is better than the 2022?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2012 drew the fewest (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V227000 (airbags), 23V755000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 22V712000 (visibility) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Subaru sent dealers 405 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Event Data Recorder (EDR) Function of Airbag Module”?
