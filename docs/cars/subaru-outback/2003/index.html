Quick answers

Is the 2003 Subaru Outback reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Subaru Outback year by this measure is 2012.

What are the most common 2003 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (19%), speed control (19%) and transmission & drivetrain (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Subaru Outback have?

15 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Subaru Outback get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2003 Subaru Outback problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 21,100 and 89,200 miles. The middle report was at 73,800 miles, from 64 complaints.

Does the 2003 Subaru Outback have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2003 Subaru Outback cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2003 Subaru Outback have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Subaru Outback year is better than the 2003?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2012, 2014 and 2013.
