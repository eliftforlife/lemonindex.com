Quick answers

Is the 2012 Subaru Outback reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 38; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2012 Subaru Outback problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (29%), steering (15%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Subaru Outback have?

16 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Subaru Outback get?

20 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
