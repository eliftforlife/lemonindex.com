Quick answers

Is the 2014 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (25%), electrical (17%) and transmission & drivetrain (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Toyota Highlander have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Toyota Highlander get?

20 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
