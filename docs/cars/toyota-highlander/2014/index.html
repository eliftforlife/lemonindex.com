How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a third as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 33; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (21%), electrical (15%) and transmission & drivetrain (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Toyota Highlander have?

5 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Toyota Highlander get?

20 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Toyota Highlander safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Toyota Highlander problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 13,000 and 55,000 miles (20,000–89,000 km) (the middle half of the 95 complaints, of all 138 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Toyota Highlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 8% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Toyota Highlander cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $3,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Toyota Highlander have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Toyota Highlander year than the 2014?

Not nearby: the 2014 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Toyota Highlander years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 18E107000 (electrical), 14V576000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 14V272000 (electrical) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Toyota sent dealers 418 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Revision notice august 3, 2016 rev1: warranty information section has been updated with a new…”?
