Toyota Highlander transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2019 (195 complaints), 2021 (211 complaints), 2020 (122 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2016, 2024, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Toyota Highlander years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Toyota Highlander years are 2019, 2021, 2020.

What are common Toyota Highlander transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, transmission replaced, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Toyota Highlander years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Toyota Highlander years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2016, 2024, 2015.
