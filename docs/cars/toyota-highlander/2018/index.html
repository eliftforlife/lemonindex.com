When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Highlander complaints that listed a mileage came before 16,000 miles. Most came between 5,900 miles and 37,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 15,500 miles and 107,400 miles.

  • electrical 2018
    The windshields have an attached wire harness that water may leak into, possibly causing damage to the vehicle's Engine Control Module (ECM). NHTSA 18E107000
  • brakes 2018
    During the manufacturing process, the oil galley in the rotor for the brake booster vacuum pump assembly may have been improperly machined possibly resulting in a sudden loss of brake assist. NHTSA 18V211000
  • equipment 2017
    These vehicles may have incorrect load carrying capacity modification labels. NHTSA 17V831000
