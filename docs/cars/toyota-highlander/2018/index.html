Quick answers

Is the 2018 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Highlander year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2018 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (19%), engine (14%) and fuel or ev drive system (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Toyota Highlander have?

5 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Toyota Highlander get?

22 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
