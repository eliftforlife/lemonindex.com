Quick answers

Is the 2017 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 46; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Highlander year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2017 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (20%), transmission & drivetrain (19%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Toyota Highlander have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Toyota Highlander get?

22 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Toyota Highlander safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Toyota Highlander problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 6,900 and 40,000 miles. The middle report was at 20,000 miles, from 126 complaints.

Does the 2017 Toyota Highlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Toyota Highlander cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Toyota Highlander have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Highlander year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2016 and 2017.
