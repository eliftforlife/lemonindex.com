Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.7× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2002. Avoid 2008.

Is the Toyota Highlander a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 58; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2008.

What are the best Toyota Highlander years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2016, 2017.

What Toyota Highlander years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2008.

How many recalls does the Toyota Highlander have?

134 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2008 (14), 2006 (11), 2009 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Toyota Highlander problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, brakes and speed control most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
