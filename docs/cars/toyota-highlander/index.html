Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes. It draws a bit over half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2014. Most complaints: 2008.

Is the Toyota Highlander a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 58; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2008 the most.

What are the best Toyota Highlander years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014, 2016, 2017. 2014 drew the fewest.

What Toyota Highlander years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2008 (worse than average, Lemon Index 158, the first year of a new generation), 2004 (about average, Lemon Index 100), 2021 (about average, Lemon Index 86). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Toyota Highlander have?

134 safety recalls across the 25 model years we track. Most: 2008 (14), 2006 (11), 2009 (10). Each year's page lists them, and every Toyota Highlander recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Toyota Highlander problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, engine and brakes most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Toyota Highlander repairs cost?

The repairs Toyota Highlander owners reported paying for most: transmission replacement (middle bill $9,400, 67 reports), ABS module replacement (middle bill $3,000, 53 reports), brake repair (middle bill $2,000, 19 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 24 Toyota Highlander years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 787 YouTube videos about the Toyota Highlander, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2023. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
