2005 Toyota Highlander: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Toyota Highlander drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 62 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 137,409 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Toyota Highlander complaints that listed a mileage came before 56,000 miles. Most came between 29,500 miles and 90,000 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 4,500 miles and 89,800 miles.
