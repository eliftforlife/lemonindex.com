Quick answers

Is the 2025 Toyota Highlander reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 10 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (30%), speed control (30%) and brakes (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 Toyota Highlander have?

4 safety recalls (latest: seats). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 Toyota Highlander get?

24 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 Toyota Highlander safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 Toyota Highlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2025 Toyota Highlander cost in fuel a year?

About $1850 to $2700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 Toyota Highlander have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.5L 4-cyl.

What Toyota Highlander year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2016 and 2017.
