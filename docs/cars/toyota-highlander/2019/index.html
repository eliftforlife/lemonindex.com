How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 72; 100 is average, lower is better). The Toyota Highlander year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2014.

What are the most common 2019 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system (39%), engine (21%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Toyota Highlander have?

3 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Toyota Highlander get?

22 to 29 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Toyota Highlander safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2019 Toyota Highlander problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,200 and 41,000 miles (6,800–66,000 km) (the middle half of the 148 complaints, of all 503 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2019 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2019 Toyota Highlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 7% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Toyota Highlander cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 to $2,900 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Toyota Highlander have?

2.7L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Highlander year is better than the 2019?

Nearby, the 2016 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit under half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2014 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 20V682000 (fuel system, gasoline), 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 19V244000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records. (40 owners named a transmission repair bill; the middle one was $9,700.)
  6. Toyota sent dealers 162 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “REVISION NOTICE November 14, 2019 Rev2: ?”?
