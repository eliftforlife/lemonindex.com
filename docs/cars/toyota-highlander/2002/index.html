Quick answers

Is the 2002 Toyota Highlander reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.1× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 47; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Toyota Highlander year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2002 Toyota Highlander problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (20%), engine (13%) and body structure (11%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Toyota Highlander have?

2 safety recalls (latest: latches/locks/linkages). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Toyota Highlander get?

18 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2002 Toyota Highlander problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 42,300 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 70,000 miles, from 204 complaints.

Does the 2002 Toyota Highlander have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2002 Toyota Highlander cost in fuel a year?

About $2900 to $3550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2002 Toyota Highlander have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Toyota Highlander year is better than the 2002?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2016 and 2017.
