Quick answers

Is the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 15 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are visibility (40%), tires (20%) and seats (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

3 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

18 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 41,400 and 96,800 miles. The middle report was at 61,500 miles, from 50 complaints.

Does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost in fuel a year?

About $3450 to $4400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2004 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.6L 6-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz C-Class year is better than the 2004?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
