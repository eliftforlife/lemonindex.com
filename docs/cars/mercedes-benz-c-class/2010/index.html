Quick answers

Is the 2010 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.8× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 26; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz C-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2010 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are lights (41%), electrical (24%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

20 to 21 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
