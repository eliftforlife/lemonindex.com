Quick answers

Is the 2007 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 13 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (15%), airbags (15%) and suspension (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

5 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?
