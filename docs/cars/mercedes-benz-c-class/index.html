Quick answers.

Is the Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes. It draws 2.1× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2012.

Is the Mercedes-Benz C-Class a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2018: Lemon Index 48; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2011 and the worst is 2015.

What are the best Mercedes-Benz C-Class years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2012, 2010.

What Mercedes-Benz C-Class years should you stay away from?

No Mercedes-Benz C-Class year drew much more than an average car's complaints.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

85 safety recalls across the 18 model years we track. Most: 2018 (19), 2016 (13), 2015 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and airbags most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
