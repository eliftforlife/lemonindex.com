Mercedes-Benz C-Class transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2007 (44 complaints), 2006 (23 complaints), 2005 (11 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2013, 2018, 2015.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Mercedes-Benz C-Class years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Mercedes-Benz C-Class years are 2007, 2006, 2005.

What are common Mercedes-Benz C-Class transmission problems?

Owners most often describe hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving, stuck in gear or limp mode.

Which Mercedes-Benz C-Class years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Mercedes-Benz C-Class years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2013, 2018, 2015.
