Quick answers

Is the 2018 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 54; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz C-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2018 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (31%), airbags (14%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

19 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2018 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

25 to 30 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
