2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class: problems and owner complaints

The 2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 24 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 65,982 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class complaints that listed a mileage came before 65,400 miles. Most came between 20,200 miles and 101,500 miles.

The top problem area, engine, usually showed up between 46,600 miles and 75,000 miles.
