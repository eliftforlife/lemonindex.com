Problems Mercedes-Benz told dealers about

Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 58 repair notes for this car. The ones about its biggest problems:

  • Engine oil leaks
  • Engine ticking (piston slap like sound) noise when accelerating between 1200 and 3000 rpm
  • Check engine light on / fault codes fc1200 / fc1208 stored in me-sfi control unit

Ask a seller or mechanic if these were done. Some are free under warranty or a special program.

Quick answers

Is the 2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 45; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz C-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (42%), engine (29%) and speed control (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

3 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2003 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

18 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
