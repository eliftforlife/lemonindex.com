Questions to ask the seller

  1. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (76 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $1,300.)
  5. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 129 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Xentry (mercedes): check engine light (cel) is activated; stored in engine control unit is…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a fifth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 19; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz C-Class year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (24%), body structure (12%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

20 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 48,000 and 96,000 miles (77,000–154,000 km) (the middle half of the 167 complaints, of all 579 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2012 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2012 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost in fuel a year?
