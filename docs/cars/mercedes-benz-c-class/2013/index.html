Quick answers

Is the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 39; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz C-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (26%), speed control (24%) and engine (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

22 to 25 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 34,600 and 90,000 miles. The middle report was at 63,000 miles, from 189 complaints.

Does the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 17% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost in fuel a year?

About $3200 to $3600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2013 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz C-Class year is better than the 2013?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
