2009 Mercedes-Benz C-Class: problems and owner complaints

The 2009 Mercedes-Benz C-Class drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 20 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 52,427 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class complaints that listed a mileage came before 70,000 miles. Most came between 50,400 miles and 88,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 52,100 miles and 87,500 miles.
