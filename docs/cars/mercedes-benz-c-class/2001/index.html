Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V874000 (visibility), 22V954000 (visibility) and 19V918000 (visibility) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Mercedes-Benz sent dealers 45 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Engine noises after engine start”?

Quick answers

Is the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 40; 100 is average, lower is better). The Mercedes-Benz C-Class year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (45%), visibility (23%) and engine (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

3 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

19 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 45,000 and 106,000 miles (the middle half of the 64 complaints that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2001 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2001 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost in fuel a year?

About $4000 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

2.6L 6-cyl, 3.2L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Mercedes-Benz C-Class year than the 2001?

Not nearby: the 2001 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class years within 3 model years of it. Across every year, 2011 drew the fewest.
