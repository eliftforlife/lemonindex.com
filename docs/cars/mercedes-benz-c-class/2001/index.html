2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 Mercedes-Benz C-Class drew 2.5× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 22 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 51,210 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Mercedes-Benz C-Class complaints that listed a mileage came before 73,600 miles. Most came between 45,200 miles and 105,800 miles.
