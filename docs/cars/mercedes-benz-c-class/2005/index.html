Quick answers

Is the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 52; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Mercedes-Benz C-Class year by this measure is 2011.

What are the most common 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (39%), engine (35%) and tires (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

4 safety recalls (latest: visibility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class get?

18 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 35,600 and 110,500 miles. The middle report was at 78,000 miles, from 81 complaints.

Does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 9% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class cost in fuel a year?

About $3300 to $4400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 Mercedes-Benz C-Class have?

1.8L 4-cyl, 2.6L 6-cyl, 3.2L 6-cyl.

What Mercedes-Benz C-Class year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2011, 2012 and 2010.
