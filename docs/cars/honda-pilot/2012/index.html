Quick answers

Is the 2012 Honda Pilot reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 64; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (23%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and brakes (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Honda Pilot have?

9 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Honda Pilot get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
