Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2004 Honda Pilot reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 56; 100 is average, lower is better). The Honda Pilot year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2004 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (12%), electrical (10%) and transmission & drivetrain (10%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2004 Honda Pilot have?

13 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2004 Honda Pilot get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2004 Honda Pilot problems show up?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V501000 (airbags), 19V499000 (airbags) and 19V182000 (airbags) done? It has 13 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  5. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  6. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done? (24 owners named a airbags repair bill; the middle one was $348.)
  7. Honda sent dealers 235 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Rear differential noise”?
