Quick answers

Is the 2020 Honda Pilot reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 233; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2020 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (57%), engine (17%) and automatic emergency braking (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Honda Pilot have?

11 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2020 Honda Pilot get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2020 Honda Pilot safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2020 Honda Pilot problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 1,000 and 15,000 miles. The middle report was at 4,000 miles, from 147 complaints.

Does the 2020 Honda Pilot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2020 Honda Pilot cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2020 Honda Pilot have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Pilot year is better than the 2020?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2015 and 2014.
