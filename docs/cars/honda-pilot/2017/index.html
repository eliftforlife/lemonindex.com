Quick answers

Is the 2017 Honda Pilot reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 132; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2017 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (25%), electrical (23%) and transmission & drivetrain (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Honda Pilot have?

4 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Honda Pilot get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
