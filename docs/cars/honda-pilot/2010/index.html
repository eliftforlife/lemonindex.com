How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Honda Pilot reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew about a fifth as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 15; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2010 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (17%), suspension (17%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Honda Pilot have?

10 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Honda Pilot get?

18 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Honda Pilot problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 30,000 and 100,000 miles (48,000–161,000 km) (the middle half of the 71 complaints, of all 111 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K and 100K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Honda Pilot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Honda Pilot cost in fuel a year?

About $3,400 to $3,550 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Honda Pilot have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

Is there a better Honda Pilot year than the 2010?

Not nearby: the 2010 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Honda Pilot years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V502000 (airbags), 19V378000 (airbags) and 18V661000 (airbags) done? It has 10 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Honda sent dealers 83 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “On some model vehicles, there is an alternator/starter tester (ast) module capable of testing”?
