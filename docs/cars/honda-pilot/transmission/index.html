Honda Pilot transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2003 (209 complaints), 2016 (145 complaints), 2005 (151 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2010, 2015, 2014.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Honda Pilot years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Honda Pilot years are 2003, 2016, 2005.

What are common Honda Pilot transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, hard or jerky shifting, lost power while driving.

Which Honda Pilot years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Honda Pilot years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2010, 2015, 2014.
