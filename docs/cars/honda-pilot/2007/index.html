Quick answers

Is the 2007 Honda Pilot reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 46; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2007 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are tires (23%), speed control (13%) and brakes (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Honda Pilot have?

9 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Honda Pilot get?

17 to 18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Honda Pilot problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 25,900 and 133,900 miles. The middle report was at 82,200 miles, from 124 complaints.

Does the 2007 Honda Pilot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 10% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Honda Pilot cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Honda Pilot have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Pilot year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2015 and 2014.
