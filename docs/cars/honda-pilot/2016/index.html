Quick answers

Is the 2016 Honda Pilot reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 340; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2016 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (41%), engine (20%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Honda Pilot have?

8 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Honda Pilot get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
