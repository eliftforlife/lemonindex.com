Quick answers

Is the 2016 Honda Pilot reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.4× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 340; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Honda Pilot year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2016 Honda Pilot problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (41%), engine (20%) and transmission & drivetrain (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Honda Pilot have?

8 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Honda Pilot get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Honda Pilot safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Honda Pilot problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 77,000 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 735 complaints.

Does the 2016 Honda Pilot have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Honda Pilot cost in fuel a year?

About $2800 to $3050 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Honda Pilot have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Honda Pilot year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2015 and 2014.
