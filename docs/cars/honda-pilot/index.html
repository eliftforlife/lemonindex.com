Quick answers.

Is the Honda Pilot reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2008. Avoid 2016, 2019 and 2020.

Is the Honda Pilot a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 117; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best Honda Pilot years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2009, 2008.

What Honda Pilot years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016, 2020, 2019.

How many recalls does the Honda Pilot have?

195 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2005 (15), 2003 (14), 2004 (13). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Honda Pilot problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
