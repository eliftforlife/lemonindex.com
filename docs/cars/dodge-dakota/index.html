Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Dakota reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.1× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2006. Most complaints: 2000, 2001 and 2002.

Is the Dodge Dakota a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2008: Lemon Index 213; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2006 drew the fewest complaints and 2002 the most.

What are the best Dodge Dakota years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2006, 2007, 2004. 2006 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Dakota years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2002 (avoid, Lemon Index 338), 2001 (avoid, Lemon Index 271), 2000 (avoid, Lemon Index 227). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Dakota have?

91 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2001 (14), 2000 (12), 2002 (12). Each year's page lists them, and every Dodge Dakota recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Dodge Dakota problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name suspension, brakes and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Dodge Dakota repairs cost?

The repairs Dodge Dakota owners reported paying for most: brake repair (middle bill $400, 28 reports), ball joint replacement (middle bill $700, 23 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 13 Dodge Dakota years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 397 YouTube videos about the Dodge Dakota, 1997 to 2011, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2008. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
