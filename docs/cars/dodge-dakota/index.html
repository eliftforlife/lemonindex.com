Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Dakota reliable?

Not really. It draws 2.1× the average car's complaints. Fewest complaints: 2006. Most complaints: 2000, 2001 and 2002.

Is the Dodge Dakota a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2000-2008: Lemon Index 213; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2006 drew the fewest complaints and 2002 the most.

What are the best Dodge Dakota years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2006, 2007, 2004. 2006 drew the fewest.

What Dodge Dakota years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2002 (avoid, Lemon Index 338), 2001 (avoid, Lemon Index 271), 2000 (avoid, Lemon Index 227). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Dodge Dakota have?

91 safety recalls across the 12 model years we track. Most: 2001 (14), 2000 (12), 2002 (12). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Dakota problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name suspension, brakes and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
