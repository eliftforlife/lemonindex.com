Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 19V018000 (airbags), 18V021000 (airbags) and 16V352000 (airbags) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  5. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  6. Dodge sent dealers 68 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Advanced Field Action Communication ?”?

Quick answers

Is the 2011 Dodge Dakota reliable?

We can't score it: only 12,156 were sold in the U.S. in 2011 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 15 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2011 Dodge Dakota problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (27%), fuel or ev drive system (13%) and brakes (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Dodge Dakota have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Dodge Dakota get?

15 to 17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2011 Dodge Dakota have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 27% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Dodge Dakota cost in fuel a year?

About $3550 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Dodge Dakota have?

3.7L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl.

What Dodge Dakota year is better than the 2011?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2006 drew the fewest (about average).
