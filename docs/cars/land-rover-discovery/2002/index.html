Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 12E010000 (transmission & drivetrain), 04V006000 (engine) and 04V005000 (brakes) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.

Quick answers

Is the 2002 Land Rover Discovery reliable?

We can't score it: only 17,417 were sold in the U.S. in 2002 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 14 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2002 Land Rover Discovery problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (43%), airbags (14%) and visibility (7%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Land Rover Discovery have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2002 Land Rover Discovery have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.
