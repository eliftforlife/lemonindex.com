Recalls & safety

Recalls 4

  • Fuel system, gasoline · recall issued 2021

    The fuel outlet flange mounted on the fuel tank may crack, possibly resulting in a fuel leak.

  • Stability control · recall issued 2015

    Due to an Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS) software error, the Dynamic Stability Control (DSC), Roll Stability Control (RSC), Traction Control (TC) and Hill Decent Control (HDC) systems may be disabled.

  • Wheels · recall issued 2015

    The affected vehicles have wheel lug nuts that may crack and detach from the wheel hub stud.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2015 Land Rover Discovery reliable?

We can't score it: only 9,031 were sold in the U.S. in 2015 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 3 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2015 Land Rover Discovery problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (67%), airbags (33%) and engine (33%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Land Rover Discovery have?

4 safety recalls (latest: fuel system, gasoline). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Does the 2015 Land Rover Discovery have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V635000 (fuel system, gasoline), 15V214000 (stability control) and 15V092000 (wheels) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  3. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Land Rover sent dealers 235 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Vehicle repaired but timing chain system is still making noise from engine”?

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 786 YouTube videos about the Land Rover Discovery, 1999 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2004. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
