How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Is the 2009 Cadillac CTS reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew a bit more than the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 122; 100 is average, lower is better). The Cadillac CTS year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2010.

What are the most common 2009 Cadillac CTS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (21%), electrical (16%) and speed control (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V394000 (electrical), 11V089000 (suspension) and 10V414000 (equipment) done? It has 5 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  3. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  5. Cadillac sent dealers 1248 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “One or more of the electrical concerns: abs, stability indicator/warning messages; compass inop”?
