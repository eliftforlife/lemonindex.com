Quick answers

Is the 2016 Acura MDX reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 217; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura MDX year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2016 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (48%), engine (20%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Acura MDX have?

7 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Acura MDX get?

21 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
