Recalls 8

  • body structure 2026

    The rear subframe may corrode at the suspension mounting points, which can result in rear suspension component failure.

  • airbags 2026

    The front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit, which can cause the air bags to deploy unintentionally during a crash.

  • lights 2025

    Moisture may enter the tailgate lid light assembly and wiring harness, which can cause the interior and exterior lights to fail, including the lid lights, position lights, license plate lights and taillights.

Quick answers

Is the 2017 Acura MDX reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 102; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura MDX year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2017 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (32%), electrical (24%) and engine (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2017 Acura MDX have?

8 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2017 Acura MDX get?

21 to 27 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2017 Acura MDX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2017 Acura MDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,500 and 89,000 miles. The middle report was at 37,000 miles, from 96 complaints.

Does the 2017 Acura MDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 32% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2017 Acura MDX cost in fuel a year?

About $2950 to $3800 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2017 Acura MDX have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Acura MDX year is better than the 2017?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
