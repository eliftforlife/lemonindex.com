Acura MDX transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2001 (127 complaints), 2002 (122 complaints), 2003 (101 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2013, 2024, 2025.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Acura MDX years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Acura MDX years are 2001, 2002, 2003.

What are common Acura MDX transmission problems?

Owners most often describe shudder, transmission replaced, lost power while driving.

Which Acura MDX years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Acura MDX years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2013, 2024, 2025.
