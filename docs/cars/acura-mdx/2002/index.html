Quick answers

Is the 2002 Acura MDX reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.6× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 63; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura MDX year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2002 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (38%), brakes (12%) and steering (9%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2002 Acura MDX have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2002 Acura MDX get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
