Quick answers.

Is the Acura MDX reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.3× fewer complaints than average. Best year: 2010. Avoid 2014 and 2016.

Is the Acura MDX a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 77; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2016.

What are the best Acura MDX years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2012, 2013.

What Acura MDX years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2016, 2014.

How many recalls does the Acura MDX have?

128 safety recalls across the 24 model years we track. Most: 2003 (13), 2005 (13), 2006 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Acura MDX problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, engine and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
