Quick answers

Is the 2006 Acura MDX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 12 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2006 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (58%), lights (8%) and visibility (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2006 Acura MDX have?

11 safety recalls (latest: speed control). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2006 Acura MDX get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2006 Acura MDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 55,000 and 105,000 miles. The middle report was at 76,000 miles, from 63 complaints.

Does the 2006 Acura MDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 58% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2006 Acura MDX cost in fuel a year?

About $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2006 Acura MDX have?

3.5L 6-cyl.

What Acura MDX year is better than the 2006?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
