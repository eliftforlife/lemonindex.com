Quick answers

Is the 2007 Acura MDX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 14 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2007 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (21%), seat belts (14%) and transmission & drivetrain (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Acura MDX have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Acura MDX get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Acura MDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 59,500 and 122,800 miles. The middle report was at 92,500 miles, from 90 complaints.

Does the 2007 Acura MDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 14% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Acura MDX cost in fuel a year?

About $4700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Acura MDX have?

3.7L 6-cyl.

What Acura MDX year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
