Quick answers

Is the 2008 Acura MDX reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 16 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2008 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (19%), body structure (12%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2008 Acura MDX have?

1 safety recall (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2008 Acura MDX get?

17 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2008 Acura MDX problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 23,000 and 88,000 miles (37,000–142,000 km) (the middle half of the 35 complaints, of all 49 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2008 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2008 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2008 Acura MDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2008 Acura MDX cost in fuel a year?

About $4,700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2008 Acura MDX have?

3.7L 6-cyl.

What Acura MDX year is better than the 2008?

Nearby, the 2010 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about a fifth as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recall 08E013000 (equipment) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  3. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced? (6 owners named a suspension repair bill; the middle one was $1,300.)
  6. Acura sent dealers 41 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “In cold areas, the front active dampers may be susceptible to leaking”?

How to read this chart

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Acura MDX, 2001 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2025. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
