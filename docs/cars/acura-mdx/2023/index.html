Quick answers

Is the 2023 Acura MDX reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn 1.6× fewer complaints than average per car sold, against other 2023 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 63; 100 is average). The best Acura MDX year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2023 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (26%), automatic emergency braking (23%) and electrical (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2023 Acura MDX have?

2 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2023 Acura MDX get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
