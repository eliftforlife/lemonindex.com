Quick answers

Is the 2011 Acura MDX reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 3.3× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 30; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Acura MDX year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2011 Acura MDX problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are transmission & drivetrain (19%), electrical (19%) and fuel system, gasoline (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Acura MDX have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Acura MDX get?

18 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2011 Acura MDX safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2011 Acura MDX problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 30,000 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 66,000 miles, from 44 complaints.

Does the 2011 Acura MDX have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 19% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2011 Acura MDX cost in fuel a year?

About $4400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2011 Acura MDX have?

3.7L 6-cyl.

What Acura MDX year is better than the 2011?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2012 and 2013.
