Quick answers.

Is the Lincoln MKZ reliable?

Yes. It draws about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car. Fewest complaints: 2011.

Is the Lincoln MKZ a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2017: Lemon Index 67; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2011 drew the fewest complaints and 2013 the most.

What are the best Lincoln MKZ years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2011, 2010, 2012. 2011 drew the fewest.

What Lincoln MKZ years should you stay away from?

No Lincoln MKZ year stands out as bad. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2013 (better than average, Lemon Index 83, the first year of a new generation), 2015 (better than average, Lemon Index 82), 2017 (better than average, Lemon Index 70).

How many recalls does the Lincoln MKZ have?

92 safety recalls across the 14 model years we track. Most: 2014 (14), 2013 (13), 2015 (11). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Lincoln MKZ problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, steering and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
