Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 21V158000 (airbags), 21E013000 (airbags) and 19V001000 (airbags) done? It has 6 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. NHTSA has an open investigation into desiccated air bag inflator rupture. Has this car had that problem?
  3. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced?
  4. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  5. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  6. Lincoln sent dealers 45 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “To help differentiate engine oil leaks from other possible sources, a new white-colored…”?

Quick answers

Is the 2012 Lincoln MKZ reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew a bit over half as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 61; 100 is average, lower is better). The Lincoln MKZ year with the fewest complaints per car sold since 2010 is 2011.

What are the most common 2012 Lincoln MKZ problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are steering (42%), engine (32%) and speed control (21%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Lincoln MKZ have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Lincoln MKZ get?

19 to 38 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Lincoln MKZ safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2012 Lincoln MKZ have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 16% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2012 Lincoln MKZ cost in fuel a year?

About $1,600 to $3,150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Lincoln MKZ have?

2.5L 4-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Lincoln MKZ year is better than the 2012?

Nearby, the 2011 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car).
