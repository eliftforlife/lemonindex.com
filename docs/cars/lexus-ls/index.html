Questions6 quick answers

Quick answers.

Is the Lexus LS reliable?

Yes. It draws about half as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners sound happier with it than with most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2007.

Is the Lexus LS a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2001-2008: Lemon Index 52; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2007 drew the fewest complaints and 2008 the most.

What are the best Lexus LS years to buy?

Only 2 Lexus LS years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2007 drew the fewest complaints (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car).

What Lexus LS years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Lexus LS years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Lexus LS have?

34 safety recalls across the 23 model years we track. Most: 2018 (4), 2019 (4), 2007 (3). Each year's page lists them, and every Lexus LS recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Lexus LS problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name speed control, engine and electrical most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
