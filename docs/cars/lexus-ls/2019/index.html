Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Lexus LS reliable?

We can't score it: only 5,528 were sold in the U.S. in 2019 (calendar-year sales), under the 20,000 we need for a fair per-car score. Owners filed 5 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2019 Lexus LS problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system (40%), brakes (20%) and transmission & drivetrain (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Lexus LS have?

4 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Lexus LS get?

21 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Does the 2019 Lexus LS have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 20% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Lexus LS cost in fuel a year?

About $2,700 to $3,600 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Lexus LS have?

3.4L 6-cyl, 3.5L 6-cyl.

What Lexus LS year is better than the 2019?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2007 drew the fewest (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car).

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 24V275000 (airbags), 20V682000 (fuel system, gasoline) and 20V012000 (fuel system, gasoline) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any fuel smell, stalling or hard starts? On a hybrid or EV, any reduced-power warning or battery work?
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  5. Lexus sent dealers 167 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Some 2018 2023 model year LC 500, and LC 500h, 2021 2023 model year LC 500c, 2018 2023 model…”?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 15 Lexus LS years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 794 YouTube videos about the Lexus LS, 1995 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2004. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.

How to read these numbers

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect. How it works
