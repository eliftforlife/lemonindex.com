What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an engine special coverage or warranty extension applies. Covered repairs can be free. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10231800: “2.4L Excessive Oil Consumption XB1 Warranty Extension The customer may describe one or more of the following: Oil…”)
  • Engine: ask whether the engine computer (PCM) update was done before chasing parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 10074916: “Flash: 2.4l powertrain diagnostic and system improvements”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2014 Dodge Dart reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 142; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2014 Dodge Dart problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (32%), transmission & drivetrain (23%) and brakes (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2014 Dodge Dart have?

4 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2014 Dodge Dart get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2014 Dodge Dart safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2014 Dodge Dart problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 20,000 and 72,000 miles (33,000–116,000 km) (the middle half of the 260 complaints, of all 372 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2014 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2014 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2014 Dodge Dart have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 23% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2014 Dodge Dart cost in fuel a year?

About $2,200 to $2,650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2014 Dodge Dart have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

Is there a better Dodge Dart year than the 2014?

Not nearby: the 2014 drew the fewest complaints per car sold of the Dodge Dart years within 3 model years of it.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V674000 (transmission & drivetrain), 19V293000 (transmission & drivetrain) and 15V800000 (brakes) done? It has 4 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  5. Dodge sent dealers 268 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Right Rear Spring Shifted In Upper Isolator And Possible Clunk Noise This bulletin involves…”?
