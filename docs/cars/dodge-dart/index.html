Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Dart reliable?

Not really. It draws 3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. No year beat the average car. The year with the fewest complaints, 2014, still drew 1.4× the average.

Is the Dodge Dart a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2016: Lemon Index 305; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2014 drew the fewest complaints and 2013 the most.

What are the best Dodge Dart years to buy?

Only 4 Dodge Dart years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2014 drew the fewest complaints (about one and a half times the average car's complaints).

What Dodge Dart years should you stay away from?

Only 4 Dodge Dart years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Dodge Dart have?

16 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2015 (6), 2016 (5), 2014 (4). Each year's page lists them, and every Dodge Dart recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Dodge Dart problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 4 Dodge Dart years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 199 YouTube videos about the Dodge Dart, 2013 to 2016, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2013. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
