Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Dart reliable?

Not really. It draws 3× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Even its best year, 2014, draws more complaints than average. Avoid 2013, 2015 and 2016.

Is the Dodge Dart a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2013-2016: Lemon Index 305; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2014 and the worst is 2013.

What are the best Dodge Dart years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2014, 2015, 2016.

What Dodge Dart years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2013, 2016, 2015.

How many recalls does the Dodge Dart have?

16 safety recalls across the 4 model years we track. Most: 2015 (6), 2016 (5), 2014 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Dart problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name transmission & drivetrain, brakes and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
