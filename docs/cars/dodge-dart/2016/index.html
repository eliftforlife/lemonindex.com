Quick answers

Is the 2016 Dodge Dart reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 247; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Dart year by this measure is 2014.

What are the most common 2016 Dodge Dart problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (33%), transmission & drivetrain (25%) and electrical (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 Dodge Dart have?

5 safety recalls (latest: transmission & drivetrain). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 Dodge Dart get?

25 to 32 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2016 Dodge Dart safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2016 Dodge Dart problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 31,400 and 70,000 miles. The middle report was at 49,000 miles, from 155 complaints.

Does the 2016 Dodge Dart have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 25% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2016 Dodge Dart cost in fuel a year?

About $2200 to $2650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2016 Dodge Dart have?

1.4L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl, 2.4L 4-cyl.

What Dodge Dart year is better than the 2016?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2014, 2015 and 2016.
