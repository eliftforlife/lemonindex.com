Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 16V401000 (airbags), 15V338000 (airbags) and 10E059000 (suspension) done? It has 14 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  5. Dodge sent dealers 91 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “Concerns with popping or squawking noise from rear suspension while driving vehicle”?

Quick answers

Is the 2001 Dodge Stratus reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 73; 100 is average, lower is better). The Dodge Stratus year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2006.

What are the most common 2001 Dodge Stratus problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (28%), transmission & drivetrain (21%) and suspension (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2001 Dodge Stratus have?

14 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2001 Dodge Stratus get?

20 to 23 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2001 Dodge Stratus problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 35,000 and 58,000 miles (56,000–93,000 km) (the middle half of the 33 complaints, of all 86 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2001 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2001 Dodge Stratus have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 21% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2001 Dodge Stratus cost in fuel a year?

About $2,600 to $3,000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2001 Dodge Stratus have?

2.4L 4-cyl, 2.7L 6-cyl, 3.0L 6-cyl.

What Dodge Stratus year is better than the 2001?

Nearby, the 2002 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (a bit over half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2006 drew the fewest.
