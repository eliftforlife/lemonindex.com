How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2018 Tesla Model 3 reliable?

We can't score it: Tesla doesn't report U.S. sales by model, and no estimate covers this model year on its own, so there is no per-car rate. Owners filed 228 complaints with NHTSA in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2018 Tesla Model 3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are suspension (21%), speed control (18%) and electrical (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2018 Tesla Model 3 have?

19 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2018 Tesla Model 3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2018 Tesla Model 3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 4,400 and 22,000 miles (7,100–35,000 km) (the middle half of the 167 complaints, of all 228 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2018 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2018 Tesla Model 3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2018 Tesla Model 3 cost in fuel a year?

About $600 to $650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V507000 (lights), 24V935000 (tires) and 24V376000 (seat belts) done? It has 19 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any clunks over bumps or uneven tire wear? Were struts, shocks or control arms replaced?
  3. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
