How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2022 Tesla Model 3 reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew about 3 times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 276; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2022 Tesla Model 3 problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are automatic emergency braking (72%), brakes (23%) and speed control (23%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2022 Tesla Model 3 have?

18 safety recalls (latest: lights). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2022 Tesla Model 3 safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2022 Tesla Model 3 problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 1,000 and 20,000 miles (1,600–32,000 km) (the middle half of the 55 complaints, of all 727 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2022 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2022 Tesla Model 3 have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2022 Tesla Model 3 cost in fuel a year?

About $550 to $650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 26V507000 (lights), 26V283000 (back over prevention) and 24V935000 (tires) done? It has 18 recalls in all. Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Has it ever braked hard for no reason? Ask whether the safety-system software was updated.
  3. Do the brakes feel even, with no ABS or brake warning light? When were the pads and brake fluid last done?
  4. Does it accelerate and hold speed smoothly, with no surging or stalling?

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Two windows: the 2022 scores 276 here (complaints in its first 3 years) and 490 on its EV page (complaints filed by the end of 2023). Different windows, so the numbers differ.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 394 YouTube videos about the Tesla Model 3, 2018 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2020. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
