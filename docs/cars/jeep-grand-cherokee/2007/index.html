The 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee drew 1.3× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 147 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 120,937 were sold in the U.S.

When problems show up

Half of the Jeep Grand Cherokee complaints that listed a mileage came before 68,800 miles. Most came between 39,100 miles and 100,000 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 50,000 miles and 110,000 miles.
