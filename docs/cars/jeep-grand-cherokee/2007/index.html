Quick answers

Is the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 132; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Grand Cherokee year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (27%), transmission & drivetrain (26%) and electrical (20%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

7 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee get?

12 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 39,100 and 100,000 miles. The middle report was at 68,800 miles, from 740 complaints.

Does the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 26% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee cost in fuel a year?

About $3800 to $6650 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2007 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.7L 6-cyl, 4.7L 8-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.1L 8-cyl.

What Jeep Grand Cherokee year is better than the 2007?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2019 and 2020.
