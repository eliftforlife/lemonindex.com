Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.7× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2010. Avoid 2011, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Jeep Grand Cherokee a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 170; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2010 and the worst is 2014.

What are the best Jeep Grand Cherokee years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2010, 2019, 2020.

What Jeep Grand Cherokee years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2014, 2011, 2012.

How many recalls does the Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

268 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2022 (24), 2023 (23), 2014 (19). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
