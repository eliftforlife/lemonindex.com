Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.7× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2010. Most complaints: 2011, 2012 and 2014.

Is the Jeep Grand Cherokee a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 170; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2010 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2014 the most.

What are the best Jeep Grand Cherokee years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2010, 2020, 2019. 2010 drew the fewest.

What Jeep Grand Cherokee years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold since 2010: 2014 (avoid, Lemon Index 476), 2011 (avoid, Lemon Index 453, the first year of a new generation), 2012 (avoid, Lemon Index 374). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

268 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2022 (24), 2023 (23), 2014 (19). Each year's page lists them, and every Jeep Grand Cherokee recall is in one list here.

What are the most common Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, transmission & drivetrain and engine most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

What do Jeep Grand Cherokee repairs cost?

The repairs Jeep Grand Cherokee owners reported paying for most: blend door actuator replacement (middle bill $1,200, 69 reports), brake repair (middle bill $420, 41 reports), TIPM replacement (middle bill $1,200, 34 reports). Those are bills owners named in complaints to NHTSA and in YouTube videos, mostly at dealers. Each year's page has an estimate at your own shop rate.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 27 Jeep Grand Cherokee years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 792 YouTube videos about the Jeep Grand Cherokee, 1999 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2022. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
