2000 Jeep Grand Cherokee: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 Jeep Grand Cherokee drew 1.9× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 574 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 271,723 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Jeep Grand Cherokee complaints that listed a mileage came before 88,200 miles. Most came between 53,000 miles and 123,000 miles.

The top problem area, service brakes, hydraulic, usually showed up between 11,800 miles and 52,500 miles.
