Quick answers

Is the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 3.7× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 374; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Grand Cherokee year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (51%), brakes (32%) and engine (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

11 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee get?

14 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 40,000 and 94,900 miles. The middle report was at 65,000 miles, from 1,017 complaints.

Does the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee cost in fuel a year?

About $3400 to $5700 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2012 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Jeep Grand Cherokee year is better than the 2012?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2019 and 2020.
