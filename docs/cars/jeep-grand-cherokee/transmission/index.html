Jeep Grand Cherokee transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2014 (335 complaints), 2015 (266 complaints), 2007 (148 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2022, 2020, 2010.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Jeep Grand Cherokee years are 2014, 2015, 2007.

What are common Jeep Grand Cherokee transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, rolled away or park trouble, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Jeep Grand Cherokee years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2022, 2020, 2010.
