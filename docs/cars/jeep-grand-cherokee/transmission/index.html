Quick answers

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Jeep Grand Cherokee years are 2014, 2015, 2007.

What are common Jeep Grand Cherokee transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, rolled away or park trouble, hard or jerky shifting.

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Jeep Grand Cherokee years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2025, 2022, 2024.
