Recalls 7

  • brakes 2024

    A malfunction in the antilock-brake system (ABS) module may incorrectly illuminate the brake lights and disable the ABS and electronic stability control (ESC).

  • engine 2023

    The crankshaft position sensor tone wheel may delaminate, causing the engine to lose its ability to synchronize the fuel injector pulses and cam shaft timing, possibly resulting in an engine stall.

  • fuel system, diesel 2022

    A high pressure fuel pump (HPFP) failure may introduce internal debris into the fuel system, potentially resulting in fuel starvation.

Quick answers

Is the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 49; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Grand Cherokee year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (31%), transmission & drivetrain (18%) and engine (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

7 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee get?

13 to 24 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 10,000 and 66,600 miles. The middle report was at 36,000 miles, from 137 complaints.

Does the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 18% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee cost in fuel a year?

About $3050 to $6150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

3.0L 6-cyl, 3.6L 6-cyl, 5.7L 8-cyl, 6.2L 8-cyl, 6.4L 8-cyl.

What Jeep Grand Cherokee year is better than the 2019?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2010, 2019 and 2020.
