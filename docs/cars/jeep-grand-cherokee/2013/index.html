Quick answers

Is the 2013 Jeep Grand Cherokee reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 153; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Jeep Grand Cherokee year by this measure is 2010.

What are the most common 2013 Jeep Grand Cherokee problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (52%), brakes (35%) and engine (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Jeep Grand Cherokee have?

10 safety recalls (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Jeep Grand Cherokee get?

14 to 19 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
