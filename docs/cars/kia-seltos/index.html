Questions

Quick answers.

Is the Kia Seltos reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.4× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2022.

Is the Kia Seltos a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2021-2022: Lemon Index 139; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints and 2021 the most.

What are the best Kia Seltos years to buy?

Only 2 Kia Seltos years have enough data to score, so we don't list best years. 2022 drew the fewest complaints (about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car).

What Kia Seltos years should you stay away from?

Only 2 Kia Seltos years have enough data to score, too few to single out years to avoid.

How many recalls does the Kia Seltos have?

7 safety recalls across the 5 model years we track. Most: 2021 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (2). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Seltos problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 198 YouTube videos about the Kia Seltos, 2021 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2024. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
