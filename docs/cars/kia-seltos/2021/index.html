How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2021 Kia Seltos reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew about one and a half times the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 164; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Seltos year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2022.

What are the most common 2021 Kia Seltos problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (56%), transmission & drivetrain (22%) and electrical (18%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2021 Kia Seltos have?

2 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2021 Kia Seltos get?

27 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2021 Kia Seltos safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style.

At what mileage do 2021 Kia Seltos problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 3,400 and 21,000 miles (5,500–34,000 km) (the middle half of the 44 complaints, of all 155 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K, 100K and 150K miles, a 2021 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2021 Kia Seltos have transmission problems?

How much does the 2021 Kia Seltos cost in fuel a year?

About $1,950 to $2,200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2021 Kia Seltos have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Kia Seltos year is better than the 2021?

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 25V099000 (engine) and 21V259000 (engine) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records.
  3. Any slipping, hard or late shifts, or a transmission warning light? Ask for transmission fluid service records.
  4. Do all the lights, screens and power features work? Has the battery, alternator or any wiring been replaced?
