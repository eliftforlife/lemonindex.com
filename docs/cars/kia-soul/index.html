Quick answers.

Is the Kia Soul reliable?

Yes. It draws 1.3× fewer complaints than average, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Safest year: 2012. Avoid 2020.

Is the Kia Soul a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2019 and the worst is 2020.

What are the best Kia Soul years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2013, 2017.

What Kia Soul years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020.

How many recalls does the Kia Soul have?

34 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2014 (5), 2015 (4), 2023 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Soul problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
