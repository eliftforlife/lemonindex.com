Quick answers.

Is the Kia Soul reliable?

Yes. It draws about three-quarters as many complaints as the average car, and YouTube owners rate it about average for its age. Fewest complaints: 2019. Most complaints: 2020.

Is the Kia Soul a good car?

Yes, it draws fewer complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 76; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2019 drew the fewest complaints and 2020 the most.

What are the best Kia Soul years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2019, 2013, 2017. 2019 drew the fewest.

What Kia Soul years should you stay away from?

The 3 years with the most complaints per car sold: 2020 (avoid, Lemon Index 293, the first year of a new generation), 2010 (about average, Lemon Index 104), 2021 (about average, Lemon Index 95). 100 is the average car; lower is better.

How many recalls does the Kia Soul have?

34 safety recalls across the 16 model years we track. Most: 2014 (5), 2015 (4), 2023 (4). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Kia Soul problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, electrical and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
