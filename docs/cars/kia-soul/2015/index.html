Quick answers

Is the 2015 Kia Soul reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 66; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Soul year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2015 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (24%), electrical (19%) and steering (19%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 Kia Soul have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
