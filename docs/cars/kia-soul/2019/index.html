Quick answers

Is the 2019 Kia Soul reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 34; 100 is average, lower is better).

What are the most common 2019 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (35%), airbags (15%) and electrical (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2019 Kia Soul have?

1 safety recall (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Is the 2019 Kia Soul safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

At what mileage do 2019 Kia Soul problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 8,800 and 99,500 miles. The middle report was at 50,000 miles, from 45 complaints.

Does the 2019 Kia Soul have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2019 Kia Soul cost in fuel a year?

About $700 to $2400 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.
