Quick answers

Is the 2012 Kia Soul reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 49; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Soul year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2012 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are airbags (16%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and electrical (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2012 Kia Soul have?

3 safety recalls (latest: brakes). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2012 Kia Soul get?

25 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
