How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Quick answers

Is the 2010 Kia Soul reliable?

About average by government complaints. It drew about the average number of complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Complaint Score 104; 100 is average, lower is better). The Kia Soul year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2019.

What are the most common 2010 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are body structure (23%), visibility (13%) and airbags (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2010 Kia Soul have?

3 safety recalls (latest: body structure). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2010 Kia Soul get?

26 to 28 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2010 Kia Soul problems show up?

Most problems on this year showed up between 3,000 and 73,000 miles (4,800–117,000 km) (the middle half of the 105 complaints, of all 135 filed so far, that listed a mileage). At 60K miles, a 2010 is right in the range where most owners reported problems. At 100K and 150K miles, a 2010 is past the range where most problems showed up: it has likely had them or dodged them, so repair records matter most. This is when owners who had a problem reported it, not a prediction for one car.

Does the 2010 Kia Soul have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2010 Kia Soul cost in fuel a year?

About $2,300 to $2,450 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2010 Kia Soul have?

1.6L 4-cyl, 2.0L 4-cyl.

What Kia Soul year is better than the 2010?

Nearby, the 2013 drew fewer owner complaints per car sold (about half as many complaints as the average car). Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest.

Questions to ask the seller

  1. Were recalls 14V822000 (body structure), 13V114000 (lights) and 10V388000 (electrical) done? Ask for the dealer printout, or check the VIN.
  2. Any rust or damage underneath, or doors and hatches that don't close right?
  3. Do the wipers, washers, defrost and mirrors all work?
  4. Is the airbag light off, and were any airbag recalls done?
  5. Any oil burning, leaks or check-engine lights? Ask for oil-change records. (18 owners named a engine repair bill; the middle one was $4,600.)
  6. Any steering noise, wander or power-steering warning? Has the steering rack or pump been replaced? (18 owners named a steering repair bill; the middle one was $475.)
  7. Kia sent dealers 74 repair notes for this car. Was this one done: “CYLINDER HEAD STEEL BALL INSPECTION AND INSTALLATION - This Pitstop provides information…”?
