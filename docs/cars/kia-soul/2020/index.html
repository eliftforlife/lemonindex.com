Quick answers

Is the 2020 Kia Soul reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 293; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Kia Soul year by this measure is 2019.

What are the most common 2020 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (51%), transmission & drivetrain (36%) and electrical (8%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2020 Kia Soul have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
