What to ask the shop

  • Engine: ask whether an oil consumption test was done, and what it showed. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11014912: “EXCESSIVE OIL CONSUMPTION NU/GAMMA/THETA/KAPPA ENGINES - This bulletin has been revised to…”)
  • Screen, radio & camera: ask for the radio / screen software update first; it often fixes freezes and reboots without new parts. (automaker note to dealers, NHTSA document 11033217: “INFOTAINMENT SYSTEM SOFTWARE UPDATE USING UPDATE.KIA.COM - This bulletin provides…”)

Each range is the middle half of the bills owners wrote down (one repair, parts and labor as they said it), not a yearly or total cost. Owners who complain often had expensive repairs, so typical bills can be lower. Prices depend on the shop, the miles and where you live.

How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each generation, for an independent shop with quality aftermarket parts), checked against the repair bills owners reported. Confidence says how well owner bills back the number: High = many owner bills that agree, Medium = some owner bills, Low = an estimate with few or no owner bills behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Where this generation's owner bills are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number: those are mostly dealer bills that owners named in complaints to NHTSA, so they often run higher than an independent shop. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and miles.

Only the repair, the price and the first 3 digits of your ZIP are kept. No name or email. One per repair per car.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the 2024 Kia Soul reliable?

It's too early to be sure. So far it has drawn about two-thirds as many complaints as the average car per car sold, against other 2024 cars after the same time on the road (early Lemon Index 63; 100 is average). The Kia Soul year with the fewest complaints per car sold is 2019.

What are the most common 2024 Kia Soul problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (30%), engine (17%) and automatic emergency braking (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2024 Kia Soul have?

None: NHTSA has no safety recalls on record for it.

What gas mileage does the 2024 Kia Soul get?

30 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2024 Kia Soul safe?

NHTSA gave it 4 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2024 Kia Soul have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2024 Kia Soul cost in fuel a year?

About $2,050 to $2,150 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2024 Kia Soul have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What Kia Soul year is better than the 2024?

No year within 3 model years of it drew fewer complaints. Across every year, 2019 drew the fewest (about a third as many complaints as the average car).
