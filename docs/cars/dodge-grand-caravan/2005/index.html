2005 Dodge Grand Caravan: problems and owner complaints

The 2005 Dodge Grand Caravan drew 2.9× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 468 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 226,771 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Grand Caravan complaints that listed a mileage came before 65,000 miles. Most came between 42,900 miles and 94,000 miles.

The top problem area, doors, locks & latches, usually showed up between 46,800 miles and 82,200 miles.
