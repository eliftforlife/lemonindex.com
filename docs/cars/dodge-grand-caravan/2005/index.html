Quick answers

Is the 2005 Dodge Grand Caravan reliable?

No, it is one of the worst by government complaints. It drew 2.9× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 293; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Grand Caravan year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2005 Dodge Grand Caravan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are doors, locks & latches (31%), airbags (16%) and steering (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 Dodge Grand Caravan have?

11 safety recalls (latest: equipment). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 Dodge Grand Caravan get?

18 to 20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
