Quick answers

Is the 2011 Dodge Grand Caravan reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 80; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Grand Caravan year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2011 Dodge Grand Caravan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (39%), transmission & drivetrain (15%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 Dodge Grand Caravan have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 Dodge Grand Caravan get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
