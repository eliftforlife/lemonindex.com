Quick answers.

Is the Dodge Grand Caravan reliable?

Not really. It draws 1.2× the average car's complaints, and YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Safest year: 2004. Avoid 2005, 2008 and 2016.

Is the Dodge Grand Caravan a good car?

Not overall: it draws more complaints than the average car, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2020: Lemon Index 120; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: the best is 2020 and the worst is 2005.

What are the best Dodge Grand Caravan years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold: 2020, 2004, 2007.

What Dodge Grand Caravan years should you stay away from?

The years with the most complaints per car sold: 2005, 2008, 2016.

How many recalls does the Dodge Grand Caravan have?

120 safety recalls across the 19 model years we track. Most: 2002 (11), 2005 (11), 2008 (10). Each year's page lists them.

What are the most common Dodge Grand Caravan problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name electrical, brakes and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.
