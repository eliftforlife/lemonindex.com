Quick answers

Is the 2013 Dodge Grand Caravan reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.3× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 134; 100 is average, lower is better). The best Dodge Grand Caravan year by this measure is 2020.

What are the most common 2013 Dodge Grand Caravan problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are electrical (75%), transmission & drivetrain (6%) and speed control (6%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2013 Dodge Grand Caravan have?

4 safety recalls (latest: equipment adaptive/mobility). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2013 Dodge Grand Caravan get?

20 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
