2004 Dodge Grand Caravan: problems and owner complaints

The 2004 Dodge Grand Caravan drew 3.3× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 62 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 242,307 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the Dodge Grand Caravan complaints that listed a mileage came before 75,000 miles. Most came between 35,000 miles and 112,000 miles.

The top problem area, fuel or EV drive system, usually showed up between 70,500 miles and 122,500 miles.
