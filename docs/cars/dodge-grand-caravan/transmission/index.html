Dodge Grand Caravan transmission problems by year.

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2016 (192 complaints), 2015 (68 complaints), 2008 (58 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2018, 2014, 2020.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Quick answers

Which Dodge Grand Caravan years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst Dodge Grand Caravan years are 2016, 2015, 2008.

What are common Dodge Grand Caravan transmission problems?

Owners most often describe lost power while driving, hard or jerky shifting, transmission replaced.

Which Dodge Grand Caravan years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the Dodge Grand Caravan years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2018, 2014, 2020.
