2008 BMW 3 Series: problems and owner complaints

The 2008 BMW 3 Series drew 1.4× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 163 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 112,464 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the BMW 3 Series complaints that listed a mileage came before 58,200 miles. Most came between 27,800 miles and 92,900 miles.

The top problem area, electrical, usually showed up between 52,000 miles and 110,000 miles.
