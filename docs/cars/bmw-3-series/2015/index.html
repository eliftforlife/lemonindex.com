Quick answers

Is the 2015 BMW 3 Series reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 4× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 25; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 3 Series year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2015 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (14%), fuel or ev drive system (14%) and engine (14%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2015 BMW 3 Series have?

4 safety recalls (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2015 BMW 3 Series get?

25 to 36 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
