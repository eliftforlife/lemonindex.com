BMW 3 Series transmission problems by year

Worst years for transmission complaints per car sold: 2000 (285 complaints), 2001 (59 complaints), 2008 (47 complaints). Fewest since 2010: 2017, 2019, 2022.

Transmission complaints owners filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) for each model year, per 100,000 cars sold. A complaint counts when it's filed under the transmission, or under the power train and the owner describes the transmission (shifting, slipping, gears, CVT). It's an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and older years have had longer to collect complaints.

Transmission and power train recalls

  • 2022 2022

    The mechatronics unit inside the transmission may not have been assembled properly, which could result in an internal leak.

  • 2014 2020

    The constant-velocity joint which transfers power between the transfer case and the rear drive shaft, may become damaged, possibly resulting in loss of power to the rear wheels.

  • 2011 2011

    BMW IS RECALLING CERTAIN MODEL YEAR 2011 328I XDRIVE AND 335I XDRIVE VEHICLES MANUFACTURED FROM FEBRUARY 8, 2011, THROUGH JULY 29, 2011.

  • 2011 2017

    The rear driveshaft constant velocity (CV) joint on the affected vehicles may fatigue and break.

Recall repairs are free at any dealer. Check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Quick answers

Which BMW 3 Series years have transmission problems?

By transmission complaints filed with NHTSA per car sold, the worst BMW 3 Series years are 2000, 2001, 2008.

What are common BMW 3 Series transmission problems?

Owners most often describe delay or hesitation, transmission replaced, lost power while driving.

Which BMW 3 Series years have the fewest transmission complaints?

Since 2010, the BMW 3 Series years with the fewest transmission complaints per car sold are 2017, 2019, 2022.
