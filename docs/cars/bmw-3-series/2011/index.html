Quick answers

Is the 2011 BMW 3 Series reliable?

Yes, better than average by government complaints. It drew 1.5× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 67; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 3 Series year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2011 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (21%), speed control (18%) and transmission & drivetrain (17%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2011 BMW 3 Series have?

8 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2011 BMW 3 Series get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
