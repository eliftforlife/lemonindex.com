Quick answers

Is the 2016 BMW 3 Series reliable?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable by government complaints. It drew 2.9× fewer complaints than average filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 35; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 3 Series year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2016 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are engine (24%), brakes (20%) and airbags (15%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2016 BMW 3 Series have?

1 safety recall (latest: engine). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2016 BMW 3 Series get?

26 to 35 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).
