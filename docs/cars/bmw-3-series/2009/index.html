Quick answers

Is the 2009 BMW 3 Series reliable?

No, it is worse than average by government complaints. It drew 1.2× the average car's complaints filed with NHTSA in its first 3 years, per car sold (Lemon Index 122; 100 is average, lower is better). The best BMW 3 Series year by this measure is 2022.

What are the most common 2009 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are fuel system, gasoline (39%), transmission & drivetrain (32%) and engine (13%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2009 BMW 3 Series have?

6 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2009 BMW 3 Series get?

20 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2009 BMW 3 Series problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 18,400 and 98,000 miles. The middle report was at 60,000 miles, from 279 complaints.

Does the 2009 BMW 3 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 32% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2009 BMW 3 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $3600 to $4000 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2009 BMW 3 Series have?

3.0L 6-cyl.

What BMW 3 Series year is better than the 2009?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2019 and 2017.
