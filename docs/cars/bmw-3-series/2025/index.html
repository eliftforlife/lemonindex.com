Quick answers

Is the 2025 BMW 3 Series reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 8 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2025 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are brakes (25%), lane assist (12%) and electrical (12%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2025 BMW 3 Series have?

1 safety recall (latest: electrical). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2025 BMW 3 Series get?

29 to 31 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

Is the 2025 BMW 3 Series safe?

NHTSA gave it 5 out of 5 stars overall in its best tested version. Ratings differ by body style, and the full table is on the advanced page.

Does the 2025 BMW 3 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues aren't among its top 5 complaint areas.

How much does the 2025 BMW 3 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $2550 to $2750 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2025 BMW 3 Series have?

2.0L 4-cyl.

What BMW 3 Series year is better than the 2025?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2019 and 2017.
