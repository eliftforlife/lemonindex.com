2001 BMW 3 Series: problems and owner complaints

The 2001 BMW 3 Series drew 1.3× the average car's complaints, per car sold. Owners filed 139 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 103,227 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the BMW 3 Series complaints that listed a mileage came before 104,000 miles. Most came between 64,800 miles and 146,200 miles.

The top problem area, airbags, usually showed up between 59,700 miles and 152,000 miles.

Before you buy one

Run the car's VIN through the free recall check at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and ask for records of any recall work. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a repair-cost rating.
