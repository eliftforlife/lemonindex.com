2000 BMW 3 Series: problems and owner complaints

The 2000 BMW 3 Series drew 1.4× fewer complaints than average, per car sold. Owners filed 69 safety complaints with the government within 3 years. 89,681 were sold in the U.S.

Older model year: fewer owners filed complaints with the government back then, and a new model's first cars often sold the year before, so treat the score as rough. The problems and owner complaints below are exactly as filed.

When problems show up

Half of the BMW 3 Series complaints that listed a mileage came before 95,000 miles. Most came between 68,000 miles and 128,500 miles.

The top problem area, transmission & drivetrain, usually showed up between 72,200 miles and 120,000 miles.
