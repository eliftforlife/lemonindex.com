Questions

Quick answers.

Is the BMW 3 Series reliable?

Mostly. It draws a bit under half as many complaints as the average car, but YouTube owners complain about it more than about most cars its age. Fewest complaints: 2022.

Is the BMW 3 Series a good car?

Yes, it is one of the most reliable models we track, by owner complaints to the government per car sold (2010-2022: Lemon Index 37; 100 is average). Some years are much better than others: 2022 drew the fewest complaints since 2010 and 2013 the most.

What are the best BMW 3 Series years to buy?

By owner complaints per car sold since 2010: 2022, 2019, 2017. 2022 drew the fewest.

What BMW 3 Series years should you stay away from?

No BMW 3 Series year stands out as bad since 2010. Even the years with the most complaints per car sold beat the average car: 2013 (better than average, Lemon Index 68), 2011 (better than average, Lemon Index 67), 2014 (better than average, Lemon Index 53).

How many recalls does the BMW 3 Series have?

157 safety recalls across the 26 model years we track. Most: 2020 (14), 2014 (13), 2019 (9). Each year's page lists them, and every BMW 3 Series recall is in one list here.

What are the most common BMW 3 Series problems?

Across every year, owners' complaints to the government name engine, fuel system, gasoline and transmission & drivetrain most. Some years are much worse than others: see the chart above.

How to read this chart

Newest year first. Red = worse than average, gray = about average, too new (2023+: a partial score, marked “partial”) or too few complaints to score. #1, #2… = that year's YouTube rank among the 24 BMW 3 Series years with enough videos (#1 = the year owners and reviewers talk about most positively). It is not a reliability rank.

Scores before 2010 are rough: fewer owners filed complaints back then.

How the YouTube ranks work

YouTube rank: we read 778 YouTube videos about the BMW 3 Series, 1999 to 2025, and ranked every year by how much they praise it vs. how much they talk about problems. YouTube's favorite: 2021. Old cars rank lower partly because most of their videos are repair and restoration videos. A gray dot means not enough data yet: under 10 videos, or a new year with no owner complaints to compare.
