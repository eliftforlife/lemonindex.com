Quick answers

Is the 2005 BMW 3 Series reliable?

There isn't enough data to score it: owners filed only 19 complaints in its first 3 years.

What are the most common 2005 BMW 3 Series problems?

In owner complaints to the government, the parts named most are speed control (21%), brakes (16%) and electrical (16%). That is the share of complaints, not the share of cars that break.

How many recalls does the 2005 BMW 3 Series have?

5 safety recalls (latest: airbags). Recall repairs are free at any dealer; check a specific car's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

What gas mileage does the 2005 BMW 3 Series get?

19 to 22 mpg combined, depending on the engine (EPA estimate).

At what mileage do 2005 BMW 3 Series problems start?

Half of the owners who gave a mileage reported the problem between 51,400 and 109,400 miles. The middle report was at 71,100 miles, from 118 complaints.

Does the 2005 BMW 3 Series have transmission problems?

Transmission and drivetrain issues make up 11% of its owner complaints.

How much does the 2005 BMW 3 Series cost in fuel a year?

About $3600 to $4200 a year, by the EPA's current estimate.

What engines does the 2005 BMW 3 Series have?

2.5L 6-cyl.

What BMW 3 Series year is better than the 2005?

By owner complaints per car sold, the best recent years are 2022, 2019 and 2017.
