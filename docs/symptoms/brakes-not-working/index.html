Brakes not working.

Brakes · owner complaints29,505
What it usually means

Brakes that fail or barely stop the car need help before the car moves again.

How serious

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it. Have it towed.

Where a shop starts

A brake fluid leak or a burst brake line. Then: a failed master cylinder or brake booster; an ABS or electronic brake fault.

Owners describe this in 29,505 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Chevrolet Silverado.

Common causes of brakes not working
  • A brake fluid leak or a burst brake line
  • A failed master cylinder or brake booster
  • An ABS or electronic brake fault
  • Pads or shoes worn out completely
  • Overheated brakes (fade) after long downhill braking

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive if the brakes are not working?

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it. Have it towed.

Get help right away if you notice it at all.

Of the 29,505 complaints describing it, 20% mention a crash, 5% say the car was towed, 1% mention a fire and 8% report an injury.

What the complaints show

The parts owners filed these complaints under: service brakes, hydraulic (49%), service brakes (37%), electrical system (8%), vehicle speed control (7%). A complaint can list more than one.

Questions

What causes brakes not working?

Brakes that fail or barely stop the car need help before the car moves again. Common causes: a brake fluid leak or a burst brake line; a failed master cylinder or brake booster; an ABS or electronic brake fault; pads or shoes worn out completely; overheated brakes (fade) after long downhill braking.

Is it safe to drive if the brakes are not working?

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it. Have it towed.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Chevrolet Silverado, Toyota Prius and Ford F-Series. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 493 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $631 to $2,300. The right repair depends on the cause.
