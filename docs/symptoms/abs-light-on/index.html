ABS light on.

Brakes · owner complaints19,928
What it usually means

An ABS light means the anti-lock system has switched itself off. Normal braking usually still works.

How serious

Fix soon. Normal brakes usually still work, but the wheels can lock in a hard stop. Get it checked soon.

Where a shop starts

A dirty or failed wheel speed sensor. Then: a damaged sensor wire or tone ring; low brake fluid.

Common causes of ABS light on
  • A dirty or failed wheel speed sensor
  • A damaged sensor wire or tone ring
  • Low brake fluid
  • A failed ABS module or pump
  • A blown fuse or low battery voltage

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive with the ABS light on?

Fix soon. Normal brakes usually still work, but the wheels can lock in a hard stop. Get it checked soon.

Get help right away if the red brake light is on too; the pedal feels different.

Of the 19,928 complaints describing it, 3% mention a crash, 2% say the car was towed, 2% mention a fire and 1% report an injury.

Questions

What causes ABS light on?

An ABS light means the anti-lock system has switched itself off. Normal braking usually still works. Common causes: a dirty or failed wheel speed sensor; a damaged sensor wire or tone ring; low brake fluid; a failed ABS module or pump; a blown fuse or low battery voltage.

Is it safe to drive with the ABS light on?

Fix soon. Normal brakes usually still work, but the wheels can lock in a hard stop. Get it checked soon.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Ram pickup, Ford Windstar and Toyota Prius. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 324 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $1,000 to $3,300. The right repair depends on the cause.
