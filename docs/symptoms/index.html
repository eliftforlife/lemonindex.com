Quick answers

What is the most common car symptom in complaints?

Among the symptoms we track, owners describe car loses power while driving most (60,729 complaints), then steering wheel hard to turn and car won't start.

Which symptoms mean I should stop driving?

Brakes that fail or a pedal that sinks, steering that locks or goes heavy, overheating, smoke, a fuel smell, a flashing check engine light, a car that stalls or speeds up on its own, and exhaust fumes inside the car. Pull over safely and get help.

Is a symptom proof that my car is defective?

No. These pages count owner complaints to NHTSA. They show which model years owners report a symptom on, not what is wrong with any one car. A shop has to diagnose your car.

How we count: we matched owners' own wording in NHTSA complaint text (for example “shakes when braking”), then read samples of each match to check them. A complaint can describe several symptoms. Pages with fewer than 200 matching complaints stay out of search results. General information, not repair advice for your car.
