Engine misfire.

Engine · owner complaints9,466
What it usually means

A misfire means one or more cylinders isn’t firing properly, so the engine runs rough and loses power.

How serious

Fix soon. Keep trips short and gentle. A flashing check engine light means stop soon.

Where a shop starts

Worn spark plugs. Then: a failing ignition coil or plug wire; a clogged or leaking fuel injector.

Owners describe this in 9,466 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Ford Escape.

Common causes of engine misfire
  • Worn spark plugs
  • A failing ignition coil or plug wire
  • A clogged or leaking fuel injector
  • A vacuum leak
  • Low compression from engine wear or a head gasket problem

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive with an engine misfire?

Fix soon. Keep trips short and gentle. A flashing check engine light means stop soon.

Get help right away if the check engine light is flashing; the car shakes badly or smells of fuel.

Of the 9,466 complaints describing it, 0.5% mention a crash, 3% say the car was towed and 0.9% mention a fire.

Questions

What causes engine misfire?

A misfire means one or more cylinders isn’t firing properly, so the engine runs rough and loses power. Common causes: worn spark plugs; a failing ignition coil or plug wire; a clogged or leaking fuel injector; a vacuum leak; low compression from engine wear or a head gasket problem.

Is it safe to drive with an engine misfire?

Fix soon. Keep trips short and gentle. A flashing check engine light means stop soon.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Ford Escape, Ford Edge and Ford Fusion. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 684 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $2,000 to $8,700. The right repair depends on the cause.
