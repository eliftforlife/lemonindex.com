Engine sputtering.

Engine · owner complaints4,763
What it usually means

Sputtering means the engine isn’t getting steady fuel or spark.

How serious

Fix soon. Get it checked soon. It can turn into stalling.

Where a shop starts

A clogged fuel filter or weak fuel pump. Then: dirty fuel injectors; worn spark plugs or coils.

Owners describe this in 4,763 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Ford Escape.

Common causes of engine sputtering
  • A clogged fuel filter or weak fuel pump
  • Dirty fuel injectors
  • Worn spark plugs or coils
  • A vacuum leak
  • A dirty mass air flow sensor

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive with a sputtering engine?

Fix soon. Get it checked soon. It can turn into stalling.

Get help right away if the car stalls or loses power; the check engine light flashes.

Of the 4,763 complaints describing it, 0.8% mention a crash, 2% say the car was towed and 2% mention a fire.

The parts owners filed these complaints under: engine (43%), power train (25%), fuel/propulsion system (22%), electrical system (18%). A complaint can list more than one.

Questions

What causes engine sputtering?

Sputtering means the engine isn’t getting steady fuel or spark. Common causes: a clogged fuel filter or weak fuel pump; dirty fuel injectors; worn spark plugs or coils; a vacuum leak; a dirty mass air flow sensor.

Is it safe to drive with a sputtering engine?

Fix soon. Get it checked soon. It can turn into stalling.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Ford Escape, Ford Focus and Ford Fusion. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 119 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $686 to $6,500. The right repair depends on the cause.
