Exhaust smell inside car.

Engine · owner complaints2,298
What it usually means

Exhaust fumes inside the car can carry carbon monoxide, which is dangerous to breathe.

How serious

Stop and get help. Open the windows and get out if you feel dizzy or sick. Get it checked before driving with the windows up.

Where a shop starts

A leak in the exhaust manifold or pipes. Then: rusted floor pans or gaps in body seals; rear hatch or trunk seals that let fumes in.

Owners describe this in 2,298 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Ford Explorer.

Common causes of exhaust smell inside car
  • A leak in the exhaust manifold or pipes
  • Rusted floor pans or gaps in body seals
  • Rear hatch or trunk seals that let fumes in
  • A cracked exhaust manifold
  • Aftermarket parts that broke a seal

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive with an exhaust smell inside the car?

Stop and get help. Open the windows and get out if you feel dizzy or sick. Get it checked before driving with the windows up.

Get help right away if anyone feels dizzy, sleepy or gets a headache.

Questions

What causes exhaust smell inside car?

Exhaust fumes inside the car can carry carbon monoxide, which is dangerous to breathe. Common causes: a leak in the exhaust manifold or pipes; rusted floor pans or gaps in body seals; rear hatch or trunk seals that let fumes in; a cracked exhaust manifold; aftermarket parts that broke a seal.

Is it safe to drive with an exhaust smell inside the car?

Stop and get help. Open the windows and get out if you feel dizzy or sick. Get it checked before driving with the windows up.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Ford Explorer, Ram pickup and Nissan Titan. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 38 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $1,000 to $3,000. The right repair depends on the cause.
