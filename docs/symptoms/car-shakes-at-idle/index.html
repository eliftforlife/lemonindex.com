The details

Common causes of car shakes at idle
  • Worn spark plugs or a failing ignition coil
  • A vacuum leak
  • Worn engine mounts
  • A dirty throttle body or fuel injectors
  • A faulty EGR valve

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Quick answers

What causes car shakes at idle?

A shake or rough idle while stopped is most often a misfire or a worn engine mount. Common causes: worn spark plugs or a failing ignition coil; a vacuum leak; worn engine mounts; a dirty throttle body or fuel injectors; a faulty EGR valve.

Is it safe to drive if your car shakes at idle?

Fix soon. Usually OK to drive to a shop. Get it checked soon.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Ford Escape, Honda CR-V and Ford Fusion. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 174 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $1,700 to $8,000. The right repair depends on the cause.
