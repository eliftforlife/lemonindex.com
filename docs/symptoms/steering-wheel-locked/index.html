Steering wheel locked up.

Steering and suspension · owner complaints10,461
What it usually means

A steering wheel that locks or seizes while driving is a safety problem that needs a shop before you drive again.

How serious

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it until it is checked.

Where a shop starts

A failed electric steering motor or control unit. Then: a seized steering column joint; a failed steering rack.

Owners describe this in 10,461 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Chevrolet Malibu.

Common causes of steering wheel locked up
  • A failed electric steering motor or control unit
  • A seized steering column joint
  • A failed steering rack
  • An ignition or steering lock fault
  • A power steering pump failure

General causes, not a diagnosis. A shop should confirm the cause with tests before replacing parts.

Is it safe to drive if the steering wheel locked up?

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it until it is checked.

Get help right away if you notice it at all.

Of the 10,461 complaints describing it, 12% mention a crash, 8% say the car was towed, 0.8% mention a fire and 6% report an injury.

Questions

What causes steering wheel locked up?

A steering wheel that locks or seizes while driving is a safety problem that needs a shop before you drive again. Common causes: a failed electric steering motor or control unit; a seized steering column joint; a failed steering rack; an ignition or steering lock fault; a power steering pump failure.

Is it safe to drive if the steering wheel locked up?

Stop and get help. Don’t drive it until it is checked.

Which cars have this problem most?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners describe it most on the Chevrolet Malibu, Ford Fusion and Ford Explorer. These are raw counts, so popular models rank higher; complaints are owner reports, not proof of a defect.

How much does it cost to fix?

In 151 repair bills owners named in complaints describing it, the middle half ran $1,300 to $2,400. The right repair depends on the cause.
