Privacy policy

Westbrook Software LLC ("we") runs lemonindex.com. This page says what we collect and why.

Visiting the site

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. We don't run ad trackers.

If you create an account or buy a report

We store your email address to sign you in and to remember that you unlocked the reports. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. If you opt in, we email you car-buying tips and new studies, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the services that run the site (hosting, sign-in, email, Stripe) and when the law requires it.
