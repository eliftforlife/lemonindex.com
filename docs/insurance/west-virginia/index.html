Questions

Which company has the cheapest car insurance in West Virginia?

In the West Virginia Offices of the Insurance Commissioner's 2024 West Virginia Automobile Survey, the cheapest for its sample driver (age 35, clean record, 2022 Toyota Camry XLE, state minimum coverage) were PURE ($24/mo), Horace Mann ($26/mo), Farmers ($29/mo). USAA was $38/mo, for military families only. Minimum coverage only (no collision or comprehensive), so full coverage costs more.

How much is car insurance in West Virginia per month?

The NAIC's 2024 average for West Virginia was $114/mo (liability, collision and comprehensive, across all drivers). In the state's own guide, that sample driver paid $24 to $173 a month depending on the company.

How this works

Official prices. 11 state insurance departments publish what each company charges sample drivers: DC, Florida, Hawaii, Kentucky, Maryland, Nebraska, Nevada, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Utah and West Virginia. We show their numbers per month (yearly or 6-month premiums divided down), each company at the middle of its prices across the state's sample places, and pick the sample driver closest to your age and record. Brands with several companies show their cheapest one (hover a row for the company's legal name). Old or minimum-coverage sources carry a note.

Estimates. Where a state publishes nothing by company, we show a typical price: the official NAIC 2024 average premium for that state (liability, collision and comprehensive), scaled to one driver with full coverage and adjusted by an AI-estimated age and record curve. Tested against 22 published sample drivers in 6 states (each state held out in turn), it was off by 21% on average (worst 53%). The AI's own prices were off by 44%. It runs low for teens and young drivers. We never use AI to rank companies: it picked the cheapest one in 1 state out of 8.

Car type. No official source prices car types for these drivers, so this part is always our estimate: trucks ×1 (in Nevada's guide a Ram 1500 cost about 4% less than a Corolla), sports cars ×1.5, supercars ×3. It changes the price, never the order of the companies.

What we promise. Nothing to sign up for, no agents, we don't sell your info, and insurers can't pay to rank higher. There are no affiliate links on these pages. Prices people add go to a review list first and are not shown yet.
