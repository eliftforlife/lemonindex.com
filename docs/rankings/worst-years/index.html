How the score works

Lemon Index = NHTSA owner complaints filed in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model year. 100 is average. Only model years with 20,000+ sold are scored. 2023–2025 are early reads (complaints filed so far). A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a safety rating.
