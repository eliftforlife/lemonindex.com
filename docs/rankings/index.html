How the score works

The Lemon Score rates each car out of 100: reliability and safety (40%), cost to own (25%), what YouTube owners and reviewers say (20%) and how well it holds its value (15%). Higher is better; 50 is the middle car. Reliability counts owner complaints per car sold (the Complaint Score), how serious they are, recalls and NHTSA crash tests.

* Tesla doesn't publish U.S. sales by model, so its numbers use published estimates. How the Lemon Score works · All free data
