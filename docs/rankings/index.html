The worst model years
How the score works

Lemon Index = NHTSA owner complaints filed in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years. 100 is average; 25 is a quarter of the complaints, 300 is three times as many. A complaint is an owner's report, not a confirmed defect, and this is not a safety rating.

* Tesla doesn't publish U.S. sales by model, so its numbers use published estimates. How the index works · All free data
