The details

What P0627 means

The fuel pump control circuit is open. The engine may not start or may stall.

Can I drive with P0627?

Fix soon. Fix it soon. Stop driving if the car slips, shakes, overheats or warns you.

73% of the owner complaints that name P0627 also say the car stalls or shuts off. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P0627
  • Failed fuel pump relay or module
  • Wiring fault
  • Failed fuel pump

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
