U0129: Lost Communication With Brake System Control Module.

Network · GenericU0129
What it means

The other computers in the car stopped hearing from the brake system control module.

How serious

Stop and fix now. Don't put it off. This can affect safety, stall the car or cause expensive damage.

Typical fix

Wiring or connector fault on the data network

Owners named U0129 in 8 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Toyota.

The details

What U0129 means

The other computers in the car stopped hearing from the brake system control module. Features that depend on it can stop working, and several warning lights often come on together.

Can I drive with U0129?

Stop and fix now. Don't put it off. This can affect safety, stall the car or cause expensive damage.

75% of the owner complaints that name U0129 also say the car brakes or ABS act up. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of U0129
  • Wiring or connector fault on the data network
  • Power or ground problem at the brake system control module
  • Failed brake system control module
  • Weak battery

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.

What owners describe
  • Brakes or ABS act up: 75% of complaints
  • Warning lights and electrical faults: 38% of complaints
