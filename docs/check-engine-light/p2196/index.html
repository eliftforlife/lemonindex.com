Check engine light / Engine codes

P2196: O2 Sensor Signal Biased/Stuck Rich (Bank 1 Sensor 1)

P2196 means: O2 Sensor Signal Biased/Stuck Rich (Bank 1 Sensor 1). Fix it soon. Waiting can lead to a bigger repair or a breakdown. Owners named P2196 in 24 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Ford Focus.

Powertrain · GenericP2196

What P2196 means

The upstream O2 sensor keeps reading rich.

Can I drive with P2196?

Get it fixed soon. Fix it soon. Waiting can lead to a bigger repair or a breakdown.

33% of the owner complaints that name P2196 also say the car stalls or shuts off. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P2196

  • Failing O2 sensor
  • Leaking injector
  • Fuel pressure too high

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
