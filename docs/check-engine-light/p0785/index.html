P0785: Shift/Timing Solenoid

P0785 means: Shift/Timing Solenoid. Fix it soon. Stop driving if the car slips, shakes, overheats or warns you. Owners named P0785 in 5 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Chevrolet.

Powertrain · GenericP0785

What P0785 means

The shift timing solenoid has a fault.

Can I drive with P0785?

Fix soon. Fix it soon. Stop driving if the car slips, shakes, overheats or warns you.

40% of the owner complaints that name P0785 also say the car warning lights and electrical faults. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P0785

  • Failed solenoid
  • Wiring fault

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
