The details

What C1250 means

C1250 is a manufacturer-specific code: each carmaker decides what it means, so the same code can point to different problems on different brands. We haven't verified its meaning yet. A dealer or a scanner with the maker's software can tell you exactly what it means on your car.

Owners who reported it drive: Ford (31), Honda (3).

What owners describe
  • Brakes or ABS act up: 100% of complaints
  • Warning lights and electrical faults: 12% of complaints
  • Stalls or shuts off: 6% of complaints
