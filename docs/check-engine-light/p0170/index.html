Check engine light · Engine codes

P0170: Fuel Trim (Bank 1)

Powertrain · GenericP0170
What it means

The computer had to adjust fuel on bank 1 beyond its normal range.

How serious

Get it fixed soon. A few more days of normal driving is usually OK, but waiting can turn a small repair into a bigger one or a breakdown.

Where a shop starts

Vacuum leak. Then: fuel delivery problem; faulty MAF or O2 sensor.

Owners named P0170 in 5 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Kia.

Symptoms owners describe with P0170: Car stalls while driving · Engine misfire · ABS light on

The details

What P0170 means

Can I drive with P0170?

Get it fixed soon. A few more days of normal driving is usually OK, but waiting can turn a small repair into a bigger one or a breakdown.

40% of the owner complaints that name P0170 also say the car stalls or shuts off. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P0170
  • Vacuum leak
  • Fuel delivery problem
  • Faulty MAF or O2 sensor

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.

What owners describe
