P0101: Mass or Volume Air Flow Sensor A Range/Performance.

Powertrain · GenericP0101
What it means

The air flow reading doesn't match what the computer expects.

How serious

Get it fixed soon. A few more days of normal driving is usually OK, but waiting can turn a small repair into a bigger one or a breakdown.

Where a shop starts

Dirty MAF sensor. Then: air leak after the sensor; clogged air filter.

The details

What P0101 means

The air flow reading doesn't match what the computer expects. The engine may hesitate, stall or idle rough.

Can I drive with P0101?

Get it fixed soon. A few more days of normal driving is usually OK, but waiting can turn a small repair into a bigger one or a breakdown.

19% of the owner complaints that name P0101 also say the car stalls or shuts off. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P0101
  • Dirty MAF sensor
  • Air leak after the sensor
  • Clogged air filter
  • Failing MAF sensor

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.

What owners describe
  • Stalls or shuts off: 19% of complaints
  • Loses power or goes into limp mode: 18% of complaints
  • Transmission slips or shifts hard: 18% of complaints
  • Warning lights and electrical faults: 14% of complaints
