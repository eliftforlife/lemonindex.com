What to do

  • Solid light: tighten the gas cap, then book a check. Most causes aren't emergencies, but don't ignore it.
  • Flashing light: ease off the gas and get to a shop now. It usually means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter.
  • Light plus other warnings: if the temperature gauge is high, oil light is on, or the car shakes, stop somewhere safe.
  • Read the code: a cheap OBD-II scanner works on any car from 1996 on, and many parts stores read codes for free.

Quick answers

What does a solid check engine light mean?

The car's computer found a fault it wants checked. The car is usually safe to drive to a shop soon. Start with the gas cap, because a loose one can trigger the light.

What does a flashing check engine light mean?

A flashing light usually means the engine is misfiring badly enough to damage the catalytic converter. Slow down and get it looked at right away. If the car shakes or loses power, pull over somewhere safe.

Can I read the code myself?

Yes. Any car from 1996 on has an OBD-II port under the dash. A cheap scanner reads the code, and many parts stores will read it for free.

Which cars get the check engine light most?

In owner complaints to the government, Ford EcoSport, Ford Focus and Ford Edge mention it most per car sold, model years 2010 to 2022.
