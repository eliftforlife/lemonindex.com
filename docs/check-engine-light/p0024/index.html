Check engine light / Engine codes

P0024: B Camshaft Position Timing Over-Advanced or System Performance (Exhaust, Bank 2)

P0024 means: B Camshaft Position Timing Over-Advanced or System Performance (Exhaust, Bank 2). Fix it soon. Waiting can lead to a bigger repair or a breakdown. Owners named P0024 in 25 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Chevrolet Traverse.

Powertrain · GenericP0024

What P0024 means

The variable valve timing for the exhaust camshaft on bank 2 isn't where the computer asked it to be. The engine can idle rough, lose power or use more fuel. Low or dirty oil is a common trigger, because oil pressure moves the timing.

Can I drive with P0024?

Get it fixed soon. Fix it soon. Waiting can lead to a bigger repair or a breakdown.

48% of the owner complaints that name P0024 also say the car stalls or shuts off. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

Common causes of P0024

  • Low, old or wrong-grade engine oil
  • Sticking oil control (VVT) solenoid
  • Worn or stretched timing chain
  • Faulty cam phaser

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
