How we count

We read every owner complaint in the NHTSA file whose text names this trouble code, and count the ones filed about this model (matched by the make, model and model year on the complaint). Mileage is what the owner entered on the form.

Parts: a complaint counts for a part when one of the owner's own sentences names that part together with a cause word (failed, bad, diagnosed, caused by, leaking ...) or a repair word (replaced, repaired, new, reprogrammed ...). "Came back" means the same complaint also says the problem returned or the repair didn't fix it. These are counts of what owners wrote, not a diagnosis, and one complaint can name several parts.

Complaints are reports, not proof of a defect, and most owners never file one. Popular models get more complaints, so the counts are not a failure rate. The code meaning is the standard SAE J2012 definition in our own words, or the carmaker's meaning where we verified it for that make.

What P0420 means

The catalytic converter on bank 1 isn't cleaning the exhaust as well as it should. The car usually drives normally, but it can fail an emissions test.

Drive on, get it checked. You can usually keep driving. Have the code read at your next service or within a few weeks.

  • Worn-out catalytic converter
  • Faulty downstream O2 sensor
  • Exhaust leak
  • Misfires or oil burning that damaged the converter

Questions

What does P0420 mean on a Ford Focus?

Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1). The catalytic converter on bank 1 isn't cleaning the exhaust as well as it should. The car usually drives normally, but it can fail an emissions test.

Which Ford Focus years have the most P0420 complaints?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners of the 2013 (4), 2012 (4), 2014 (3) Ford Focus named P0420 most (16 complaints in all, 2012–2017). Raw counts, not a failure rate.

What fixes P0420 on a Ford Focus?

Owners most often name the EVAP purge or vent valve (7 of 16 complaints), then catalytic converter and fuel pump. That's what owners wrote, not a diagnosis: have a shop confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.

Can I drive my Ford Focus with P0420?
