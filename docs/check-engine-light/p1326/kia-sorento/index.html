How we count

We read every owner complaint in the NHTSA file whose text names this trouble code, and count the ones filed about this model (matched by the make, model and model year on the complaint). Mileage is what the owner entered on the form.

Parts: a complaint counts for a part when one of the owner's own sentences names that part together with a cause word (failed, bad, diagnosed, caused by, leaking ...) or a repair word (replaced, repaired, new, reprogrammed ...). "Came back" means the same complaint also says the problem returned or the repair didn't fix it. These are counts of what owners wrote, not a diagnosis, and one complaint can name several parts.

Complaints are reports, not proof of a defect, and most owners never file one. Popular models get more complaints, so the counts are not a failure rate. The code meaning is the standard SAE J2012 definition in our own words, or the carmaker's meaning where we verified it for that make.

What P1326 means

On Hyundai and Kia engines, the knock sensor detection system picked up vibration that can point to wearing connecting rod bearings. The car may go into limp mode. Owners are usually told to see a dealer, because this system was added during the brands' engine bearing campaigns.

Stop and fix now. Don't put it off. This can affect safety, stall the car or cause expensive damage.

  • Early connecting rod bearing wear
  • Faulty knock sensor or wiring
  • Software (KSDS update)

Questions

What does P1326 mean on a Kia Sorento?

Knock Sensor Detection System (KSDS). On Hyundai and Kia engines, the knock sensor detection system picked up vibration that can point to wearing connecting rod bearings. The car may go into limp mode. Owners are usually told to see a dealer, because this system was added during the brands' engine bearing campaigns.

Which Kia Sorento years have the most P1326 complaints?

In complaints to NHTSA, owners of the 2013 (6), 2018 (5), 2017 (5) Kia Sorento named P1326 most (31 complaints in all, 2011–2019). Raw counts, not a failure rate.

What fixes P1326 on a Kia Sorento?

Owners most often name the engine (replaced or rebuilt) (8 of 31 complaints), then engine software (a reflash) and knock sensor. That's what owners wrote, not a diagnosis: have a shop confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
