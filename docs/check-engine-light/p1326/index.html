Check engine light / Engine codes

P1326: Knock Sensor Detection System (KSDS)

P1326 means: Knock Sensor Detection System (KSDS). Don't put it off. This can affect safety, stall the car or cause expensive damage. Owners named P1326 in 393 complaints to the U.S. government, most often on the Hyundai Sonata.

Powertrain · ManufacturerP1326

What P1326 means

On Hyundai and Kia engines, the knock sensor detection system picked up vibration that can point to wearing connecting rod bearings. The car may go into limp mode. Owners are usually told to see a dealer, because this system was added during the brands' engine bearing campaigns.

This meaning applies to Hyundai, Kia. On other makes P1326 can mean something different.

Can I drive with P1326?

Stop and fix now. Don't put it off. This can affect safety, stall the car or cause expensive damage.

58% of the owner complaints that name P1326 also say the car rattles or knocks. A complaint can describe more than one problem, so this doesn't prove the code caused it.

5% of those owners said the car had to be towed.

Common causes of P1326

  • Early connecting rod bearing wear
  • Faulty knock sensor or wiring
  • Software (KSDS update)

A shop should confirm the cause with a scan and tests before replacing parts.
