Honda VFR800 / VFR1200F years to avoid. The model years with the most complaints filed with NHTSA.

200312 complaints

11 of 12 describe an electrical failure (wiring, fuses or charging) that killed the lights or the engine.

  • Recall 07V359000 (electrical system): On certain motorcycles, the front fairing sub-harness (section of the wiring harness) transfers the electrical grounding load of the headlights, front turn signals, instrument panel, and various relays to the main wiring…
  • Recall 05V010000 (service brakes, hydraulic): Certain motorcycles equipped with combined-brake systems have a proportioning control valve (pcv) that mechanically proportions brake force when the rear brake is applied.
201012 complaints

3 of 12 describe the new VFR1200F surging, stumbling or losing power.

6 mention a brake light that stops working.

  • Recall 24V864000 (fuel system, gasoline): The fuel pump impellers may have been improperly molded, which can cause them to deform and result in fuel pump failure.
  • Recall 15V821000 (power train): Manufacturing or assembly errors may result in the affected motorcycles having driveshaft universal joint bearings that may prematurely wear which can cause the universal joint to separate or break.
20029 complaints

9 of 9 describe an electrical failure (wiring, fuses or charging) that killed the lights or the engine.

  • Recall 07V359000 (electrical system): On certain motorcycles, the front fairing sub-harness (section of the wiring harness) transfers the electrical grounding load of the headlights, front turn signals, instrument panel, and various relays to the main wiring…
  • Recall 05V010000 (service brakes, hydraulic): Certain motorcycles equipped with combined-brake systems have a proportioning control valve (pcv) that mechanically proportions brake force when the rear brake is applied.

After 2010, no model year drew more than 2.

Fewest complaints on file: 2007 (1), 2012 (2), 2017 (2). Few complaints is not a clean bill of health, just fewer owners filing.

What each year's complaints describe comes from reading their text. These are counts, not rates: there are no sales figures per motorcycle model year. A complaint is not proof of a defect.
