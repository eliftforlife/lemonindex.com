How this score works

YouTube buyer score only. We have no owner-complaint data for the BMW G 310 GS / G 310 R yet, so it has no Lemon Index score. We read 176 YouTube videos about it (2017–2025): reviews, owner reports and problem videos. Each model year is scored by how much its videos praise it vs. how much they talk about problems, and ranked against the other years: #1 is the year YouTube likes most. A year needs 10 videos to be ranked. This is how people talk about the bike, not a failure rate. Viewers asked us for this bike, so it has a page before our usual data arrives.
