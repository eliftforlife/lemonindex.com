BMW F 900 R / XR (and F 800 S/ST/GT/R) years to avoid. The model years with the most complaints filed with NHTSA.

200722 complaints

5 of 22 describe the engine stalling, often when downshifting.

7 mention the rear wheel bearing or a wobble.

7 mention a fuel leak or the fuel pump.

1 reported a fire.

  • Recall 13V549000 (wheels): Due to incorrect tolerances, the rear wheel drive bearing and rear axle may wear.
  • Recall 08V342000 (steering): It is possible for the coating to separate from the inside face of the steering damper housing.
200815 complaints

5 of 15 describe the engine stalling or shutting off.

2 reported a crash and 1 a fire.

  • Recall 13V549000 (wheels): Due to incorrect tolerances, the rear wheel drive bearing and rear axle may wear.
  • Recall 08V241000 (fuel system, gasoline): Due to the routing of the fuel tank breather hose, pressure equalization inside the fuel tank is possible only to a limited extent.

After 2008, no model year drew more than 9.

Fewest complaints on file: 2010 (1), 2013 (3), 2015 (1). Few complaints is not a clean bill of health, just fewer owners filing.

What each year's complaints describe comes from reading their text. These are counts, not rates: there are no sales figures per motorcycle model year. A complaint is not proof of a defect.
