Specs & engines

BMW R 1200 R / R 1250 R / RS (boxer roadsters) years to avoid. The model years with the most complaints filed with NHTSA.

200714 complaints

5 of 14 describe the fuel gauge reading wrong, some until the bike ran dry.

5 mention a fuel pump problem or a fuel leak.

  • Recall 20V471000 (fuel system, gasoline): This recall involves the fuel pump, and also the auxiliary fuel pump as applicable.
  • Recall 15V141000 (wheels): In the affected motorcycles, the rear wheel mounting flange may crack if the rear wheel mounting bolts are over tightened.
201312 complaints

9 of 12 describe the fuel gauge (the tank's fuel level strip) reading wrong, some until the bike ran dry.

  • Recall 21V060000 (fuel system, gasoline): The fuel pump, and auxiliary fuel pump in certain models, may crack and leak fuel during vehicle operation.
  • Recall 14V065000 (electrical system): Water may enter the side-stand switch preventing the motorcycle from starting or potentially shutting off the motorcycle while it is being ridden.
200811 complaints

6 of 11 describe the fuel gauge reading wrong, some until the bike ran dry.

4 mention the ABS.

  • Recall 20V471000 (fuel system, gasoline): This recall involves the fuel pump, and also the auxiliary fuel pump as applicable.
  • Recall 15V141000 (wheels): In the affected motorcycles, the rear wheel mounting flange may crack if the rear wheel mounting bolts are over tightened.

After 2013, no model year drew more than 4.

Fewest complaints on file: 2015 (1), 2020 (1), 2021 (1). Few complaints is not a clean bill of health, just fewer owners filing.

What each year's complaints describe comes from reading their text. These are counts, not rates: there are no sales figures per motorcycle model year. A complaint is not proof of a defect.
