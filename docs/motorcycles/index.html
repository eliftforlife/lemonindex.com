How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each bike and version, for an independent motorcycle shop or dealer at $120/hour unless you change the rate), checked against what owners reported paying. Overhauls, paint and other big jobs are one all-in price. Confidence says how well owner prices back the number: High = many owner prices that agree, Medium = some owner prices, Low = an estimate with few or no owner prices behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Owner bills come mostly from repair costs owners named in complaints to NHTSA (often dealer bills), plus prices said in owner YouTube videos. Where this bike's own owner prices are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and condition.

There are no free U.S. sales figures by motorcycle model, so this section shows counts and shares, not a per-bike score like our cars. Source: NHTSA owner complaints and recalls.
