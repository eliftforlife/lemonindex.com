How these estimates work

Labor hours and parts prices are AI estimates (written by Claude for each car and version, for a marque dealer or specialist at $200/hour unless you change the rate), checked against what owners reported paying. Overhauls, paint and other big jobs are one all-in price. Confidence says how well owner prices back the number: High = many owner prices that agree, Medium = some owner prices, Low = an estimate with few or no owner prices behind it. With too little to go on we show no number. Supercar owners rarely share their bills, so almost every number here is an estimate with Low confidence. Where this car's own owner prices are strong (or far from the estimate), what owners paid is the main number. Not a quote: prices vary by shop, region, parts and condition.

Owner complaints are too rare for supercars to score (Ferrari has about 70 in total), so this section shows EPA and recall data. Sources: EPA fueleconomy.gov, NHTSA.
