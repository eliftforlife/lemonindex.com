Quick answers

What MPG does the Mercedes-AMG GT get?

The EPA rates the 2025 Mercedes-AMG GT at 14–22 mpg combined (12–19 city, 19–27 highway).

How much does the Mercedes-AMG GT cost in fuel?

About $3,600–5,700 a year for the 2025 model, the EPA estimate for 15,000 miles at national average prices.

How many recalls does the Mercedes-AMG GT have?

7 recalls on record with NHTSA for model years 2011–2025. Recall repairs are free at any dealer.
