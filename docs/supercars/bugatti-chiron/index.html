Quick answers

What MPG does the Bugatti Chiron get?

The EPA rates the 2024 Bugatti Chiron at 9 mpg combined (8 city, 11 highway).

How much does the Bugatti Chiron cost in fuel?

About $8,850 a year for the 2024 model, the EPA estimate for 15,000 miles at national average prices.

How many recalls does the Bugatti Chiron have?

8 recalls on record with NHTSA for model years 2018–2024. Recall repairs are free at any dealer.
