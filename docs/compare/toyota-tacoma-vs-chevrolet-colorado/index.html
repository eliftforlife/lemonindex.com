Where this data comes from

Toyota Tacoma or Chevrolet Colorado? Here is the Colorado vs Tacoma question answered from owner complaints filed with the government, for every model year both were sold.

The winner is the car with the higher Lemon Score (out of 100: reliability and safety, cost to own, YouTube owners and resale); within 2 points is too close to call.

The Complaint Score counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A year counts toward the head-to-head only when both cars have 3 full years of complaints, so 2023 and newer show as partial. Recalls are NHTSA campaigns, counted once each. Fuel costs are the EPA's estimates (15,000 miles a year). YouTube: the most-watched owner reviews and buyer guides for each generation.
