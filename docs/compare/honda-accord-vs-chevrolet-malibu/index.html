Where this comes from

Honda Accord or Chevy Malibu? Here is the Malibu vs Accord question answered from owner complaints filed with the government, for every model year both were sold.

The Lemon Index counts owner complaints filed with the U.S. government (NHTSA) in a car's first 3 years, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). It measures complaints, not crash safety. A year counts toward the head-to-head only when both cars have 3 full years of complaints, so 2023 and newer show as partial. Recalls are NHTSA campaigns, counted once each. Fuel costs are the EPA's estimates (15,000 miles a year). YouTube: the most-watched owner reviews and buyer guides for each generation.
