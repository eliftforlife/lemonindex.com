What the score is, and isn't

Owner complaints to NHTSA filed within 3 years of each model year, per car sold, against the average car of the same model years (100 = average, lower is better). Complaints are owners' reports, not confirmed defects, and most owners never file one, so read scores as a comparison between cars, not a failure rate. It is not a repair-cost or a safety rating.
