How dealers make money on your loan

When a dealer arranges your loan, the lender approves a rate (the “buy rate”) and the dealer can add points on top. Federal settlements with several big lenders capped that markup at about 1 to 1.25 points, and lenders commonly cap it at 2 to 3. That markup is negotiable.

  1. Get pre-approved first. A quote from your bank or a credit union is your leverage.
  2. Ask the dealer to beat it. If they can't, use your own loan.
  3. Compare the total cost, not the monthly payment. A longer loan lowers the payment and raises what you pay overall.
